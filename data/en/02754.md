### TITLE

Applying henna is not obligatory for a woman

### QUESTION

It comes in some narrations that a woman made a request for the pledge of allegiance and stretched out her hand, whereupon the Prophet ﷺ declined and said that if she were a woman she would have dyed her hand with henna. From this it appears that not applying henna is a fault. Is this ḥadīth authentic, and is it a fault for a woman not to apply henna, or is applying henna obligatory and binding? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER

For a woman to dye her hands with henna is recommended; it is not obligatory or binding, nor is not applying it any kind of fault. The narration mentioned above is weak. It is far removed from the dignity of the Prophet ﷺ that he should decline the pledge of allegiance because dye had not been applied; rather, had the applying of dye been required, he would have taken her pledge upon that very thing. Then the fact is that the Prophet ﷺ took the pledge of women verbally; the shaking of hands is not established.
The critical study of the aḥādīth has already been given above.
Some scholars of ḥadīth have said that no narration concerning henna is established with a sound chain. Consider:

قال في الجامع الصغير: وقال في المعارضة: أحاديث الحناء كلها ضعيفة أو مجهولة. (الجامع الصغير، رقم: ۷٤۸۵). (وكذا في الآثار المرفوعة، ص ۱۰۲، والأسرار المرفوعة، ص ٤۸٦، والمنار المنيف، ص ۱۳۱).
