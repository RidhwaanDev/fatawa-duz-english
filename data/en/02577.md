### TITLE
Using parts of an insect as medicine

### QUESTION
There is a kind of creature among the crawling creatures of the earth that is called a ghonghā (snail). Is it permissible to use parts of this creature as medicine or not?

### ANSWER
Eating the crawling creatures of the earth (insects and vermin) is not permissible or sound. Using them externally for medical treatment is, however, sound. It is therefore permissible to apply externally a cream or an ointment into which parts of the creature mentioned have been incorporated.

Note what Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ states:

وأما الذي يعيش في البر فأنواع ثلاثة ما ليس له دم أصلاً وما ليس له دم سائل وما له دم سائل مثل الجراد والزنبور والذباب والعنكبوت

ونحوها لا يحل أكله إلا الجراد خاصة لا نها من الخبائث لاستبعاد الطباع السليمة إياها وقد قال الله تبارك وتعالى: ﴿ويحرم عليهم الخبائث﴾

وكذلك ما ليس له دم سائل مثل الحية والوزغ وسام أبرص وجميع الحشرات وهوام الأرض من الفار والقراد والقنافذ والضب واليربوع وابن عرس ونحوها ولا خلاف في حرمة هذه الأشياء إلا في الضب فإنه حلال عند الشافعي. (بدائع الصنائع: ۵/۳٦، سعيد).

(وكذا في الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۲۸۹، والطحطاوي على الدر المختار: ٤/۱۵۵).

وفي رد المحتار: لم يذكروا حكم دودة القرمز

قلت: وفيه أنها من أعز الأموال اليوم، ويصدق عليها تعريف المال المتقوم ويحتاج إليها الناس كثيراً في الصباغ وغيره، فينبغي جواز بيعها

مع أن هذه الدودة إن لم يكن لها نفس سائلة تكون ميتتها طاهرة كالذباب والبعوض وإن لم يجز أكلها. (رد المحتار: ۵/۵۱، سعيد).

قال في الدر المختار: فلو تفتت فيه نحو ضفدع جاز الوضوء به لا شربه لحرمة لحمه. (الدر المختار: ۱/۱۸۵، سعيد).

From the wording of the Durr al-Mukhtār we learn that external use is sound; internal use, however, is not sound.

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

There is no flowing blood in the fly and the ant, so they are pure, but eating them is not lawful. External use is sound, internal use is not sound. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ٦/۲۵) ۔

Bihishtī Zewar states:

Insects and vermin, and all land animals that have no flowing blood, are pure — as most of the crawling creatures of the earth are. Using them externally is sound in every way, and internally they are all forbidden except the locust. (بہشتی زیور، نواں حصہ، ص: ۷۷۸) ۔

ʿIlāj o Muʿālajah ke Sharʿī Aḥkām states:

If a medicine is prepared from such insects and vermin, or they are burnt in oil and made into a liniment, then preparing it is also permissible, and the external use of the medicine, oil and so on prepared from it is likewise permissible; nor is there any harm in performing the prayer while such an ointment or oil is applied. Its internal use, however, will not be permissible. (علاج و معالجہ کے شرعی احکام، ص ۹۵), by Mufti Inʿām al-Ḥaqq Qāsimī ۔

An explanation of external and internal use:

It should be known that there are two ways of using these things, and the sharia ruling for each is separate: one is internal use and the other external. Internal use refers only to a thing reaching the throat and the stomach — that is, internal use is the name for eating and drinking. Every other way of using a thing is external. Even istinshāq, that is, drawing a moist substance up into the nose; saʿūṭ, that is, dripping a moist medicine into the nose; nafūkh, that is, blowing medicine into the nose; sanūn, that is, rubbing on tooth-powder; shamūm, that is, smelling some medicine, whether moist or dry; ʿaṭūs, that is, taking snuff; maḍgh, that is, chewing; and maḍmaḍah, that is, rinsing the mouth — all of these too are external use, provided the medicine does not reach the throat. But in all of them except shamūm there is the risk that the medicine will reach the throat; indeed it is more likely than not that it does reach it. Therefore, if not in themselves then by reason of what happens in most cases, all of these too come under the ruling of internal use. Care is certainly needed that a thing whose internal use is not sound should not be used in these ways; for if even a little of it reaches the throat there will be the sin of eating a forbidden thing, like carrion. And if someone is able to take precautions, there is scope in the fatwa. (بہشتی زیور، نواں حصہ، ص: ۷۷۲، دار الاشاعت) ۔
