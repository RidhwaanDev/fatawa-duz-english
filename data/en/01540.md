### TITLE
Ruling on a husband and wife signing an agreement

### QUESTION
If a husband and wife sign an agreement which, among other things, contains the words, "It has become impossible for us as husband and wife to live together," does a ṭalāq take effect in the Sharia by the signing of such an agreement or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question no ṭalāq takes effect by signing such an agreement, because these are not words of ṭalāq at all. If, however, the husband said something further afterwards or in addition to this, then the question should be put again.

Mufti ʿAzīz al-Raḥmān says:

Question: A husband wrote a note to his mother-in-law saying that it was difficult for him and her daughter to get along together in this world. He now says that he wrote the note without meaning anything by it and had no intention of ending the relationship. Has an irrevocable ṭalāq taken effect by this sentence or not?

الجواب: In this case no ṭalāq has taken effect, because saying that getting along together is difficult is neither an explicit ṭalāq nor an allusive one. (فتاوی دار العلوم دیوبند: ۹/۳۸۱، مدلل مکمل) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
