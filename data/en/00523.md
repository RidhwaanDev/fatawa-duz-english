### TITLE
Holding a second congregation in the courtyard of a mosque

### QUESTION
Some men arrived at the neighbourhood mosque after the congregation was over. If they pray in the courtyard of the mosque, should they pray in congregation or each on his own? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
Holding a second congregation in a neighbourhood mosque is not correct; it is strictly disliked. If the manner is changed, it is permissible with a mild dislike. The reason is that if repeating the congregation were permitted generally, the importance of the first congregation would no longer remain. So it is far better for them to pray individually. There is no harm in holding a congregation in the courtyard of the mosque, where that courtyard lies outside the mosque proper — provided they do not make a habit of it.

Consider what Shams al-Aʾimmah ʿAllāmah al-Sarakhsī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

قال وإذا دخل القوم مسجد اً قد صلى فيه أهله كرهت لهم أن يصلوا جماعة بأذان وإقامة ولكنهم يصلون وحداناً بغير أذان ولا إقامة لحديث الحسن رحمه الله قال: كانت الصحابة رضي الله تعالى عنهم إذا فاتتهم الجماعة فمنهم من اتبع الجماعات ومنهم من صلى في مسجد ه بغير أذان ولا إقامة، وفي الحديث أن النبي ﷺ خرج ليصلح بين الأنصار فاستخلف عبد الرحمن بن عوف رضي الله عنه فرجع بعد ماصلي فدخل رسول الله ﷺ بيته وجمع أهله فصلى بهم بأذان وإقامة فلوكان يجوز إعادة الجماعة في المسجد لما ترك الصلاة في المسجد والصلاة فيه أفضل ووهذا عندنا. (المبسوط للسرخسي: ۱/۱۳٥، باب الاذان)

Imdād al-Aḥkām states:

In a neighbourhood mosque that has an appointed imam and muezzin, a second congregation is disliked; on Imam Abū Yūsuf's view (may Allah have mercy on him) there is room for it if the manner is changed. Our own scholars, however, have not given a fatwa to that effect, for the sake of orderliness among the public; rather they have ruled that in a neighbourhood mosque with an appointed imam and muezzin it is disliked without qualification. (امداد الاحکام : ۱/٤۹۷)

Islāmī Fiqh states:

In any mosque that has an appointed imam and muezzin and where the five daily prayers are regularly held in congregation, if the congregation has finished and two or three of that mosque's worshippers then arrive, they should not hold the congregation again but should each pray on his own. If, however, the prayer is performed in congregation again a little away from where the first congregation was held, there is no harm in it. (اسلامی فقہ: ۱/۲٤٥)

Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

If the second congregation is held in the same manner as the first, and in a mosque where a fixed congregation is held, it is strictly disliked; if it is held with a change of manner, it is mildly disliked. The initial use of the expression

"لا بأس"

or of the word "permissible" does not conflict with its being mildly disliked. (کفایت المفتی: ۳/۱۳٤)

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

They should pray individually, without the call to prayer or the iqāmah, since a second congregation in the mosque is disliked. On the authority of al-Mabsūṭ. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۳/۲۷، کتاب الصلاۃ)
