### TITLE
The ruling on acting upon a bequest concerning who is to lead the funeral prayer

### QUESTION
If a person makes a bequest that such-and-such a religious scholar should lead his funeral prayer, will it be necessary to act upon this bequest, and will that religious scholar be entitled to lead the funeral prayer or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
It is not binding to act upon a bequest concerning who is to lead the funeral prayer; rather the jurists have written that such a bequest is void. Consider what ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

قوله والفتوى على بطلان الوصية

عزاه في الهندية إلى المضمرات أي لو أوصى بأن يصلي عليه غير من له حق التقدم أو بأن يغسله فلان لا يلزم تنفيذ وصيته ولا يبطل حق الولي بذلك. (فتاوى الشامي: ۲/۲۲۱، سعيد).

It is mentioned in ʿUmdat al-Fiqh:

If the deceased made a bequest that such-and-such a person should lead the prayer over him, or that such-and-such a person should bathe him, that bequest is void; carrying it out is not binding, and the right of the guardian is not lost by such a bequest. This is the position upon which the fatwa is given. The guardian does, however, have the choice not to lead it himself but to have that person lead it. Likewise, if he bequeathed that he should be shrouded in a particular cloth, or buried in a particular place, this bequest is void and acting upon it is not binding. (ʿUmdat al-Fiqh: 2/527).

For more, see: (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 4/91, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
