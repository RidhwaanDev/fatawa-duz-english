### TITLE
An elderly woman travelling for Hajj without a maḥram

### QUESTION
A woman is about 75 years old. She has the money for Hajj, but she has no maḥram. She wants to go for Hajj with a group in which there are a good number of women, and her cousin — her paternal uncle's son — is also in that group. She is extremely eager to perform Hajj. Can this Hanafi woman go for Hajj or not?

### ANSWER
The jurists generally write that it is not permissible to make the journey for Hajj without a maḥram.

However, Muftī Walī Ḥasan (may Allah have mercy on him) was once asked whether a 75-year-old woman could go for Hajj with a group of trustworthy women.

The Muftī (may Allah have mercy on him) allowed it, and as evidence he cited this passage of al-Durr al-Mukhtār:

أما العجوز التي لا تشتهي فلا بأس بمصافحتها ومس يدها إذا أمن، ومتي جاز المس جاز سفره لها، ويخلو إذا أمن عليه وعليها، وإلا لا. (الدر المختار: ٦/۳٦۸، سعيد).

Fayḍ al-Bārī states:

وفي كتب الحنفية عامة عدم جواز السفر إلا مع محرم قلت: ويجوز عندي مع غير محرم أيضاً بشرط الاعتماد والأمن عن الفتنة، وقد وجدت له مادة كثيرة في الأحاديث، أما في الفقه، فهو مسائل الفتن. (فيض الباري: ۲/۳۹۷).

In the footnote the compiler writes:

منها: أمر النبي ﷺ أبا العاص أن يرسل زينب رضي الله عنه ا مع رجل لم يكن لها محرماً، ومجيء عائشة رضي الله عنها في قصة الإفك. (حاشية فيض الباري: ۲/۳۹۷).

Mawlānā Ashraf ʿAlī Thānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) wrote in answer to an enquiry:

In the Hanafi school there is no permission even in this case. In the Shāfiʿī school there is permission if trustworthy women are travelling with her, and it is permissible for a Hanafi, when there is a genuine need, to follow the Shāfiʿī school in a particular question. Whether such a need exists is not something I can decide. — Ashraf ʿAlī, 23 Ramadan 1357 AH. (Majālis Ḥakīm al-Ummat: p. 290, compiled by Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ, may Allah have mercy on him).

Some scholars, in the light of the hadith

"لترين الظعينة ترتحل من الحيرة حتى تطوف بالكعبة لا تخاف الا الله" (بخاري: ۱/۵۰۷)

have said that there is room for a woman to travel alone where there is no fear of temptation.

Note: bear in mind that this is according to one way of explaining the hadith. Otherwise the argument drawn from this hadith, and the answer to it, have already been given; so this should not be taken as a contradiction.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
