### TITLE
Continuing to talk with one's fiancée after the engagement

### QUESTION
What do the scholars of the religion and the muftis of the firm sharia say about the following matter:

Zayd sent a proposal of marriage to a young woman and it was accepted. May Zayd now speak with his fiancée before the marriage, or not?

### ANSWER
The sacred sharia has permitted speaking when there is a need, and once the match has been settled that need has been met; there is now no need to keep the conversation going. Moreover, talking with one's fiancée for pleasure before the marriage is not something the sharia regards with approval. More dangerous still is what has been seen in many places: before the wedding a man takes his fiancée out driving in a car, which also means being alone with her. This is not permissible at all. It should be firmly borne in mind that a fiancée has the status of a woman unrelated to him.

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

ولا يكلم الأجنبية. (الدر المختار: ٦/۳٦۹، سعيد).

Majmūʿah Qawānīn-e-Islāmī states:

It is unlawful for the two of them to be alone together before the wedding.

وفي الأشباه: الخلوة بالأجنبية حرام. "الدر المختار: ۳/۳۲۳". (مجموعہ قوانین اسلامی: ص۳۷، دفعہ ۱۱مع الحاشیہ).

Āp ke Masāʾil states:

Before the marriage the fiancé is a stranger, so before the marriage the ruling on the fiancé is the same as that on any unrelated man — it is not permissible for the woman to mix with him. That something has become customary in society is no proof; a wrong custom that goes against the sharia itself stands in need of reform. Nor is there any permission for close ties before the marriage: neither mixing together nor being alone together is allowed. Meeting and mingling before the marriage is in itself an immoral act. (آپ کے مسائل اور ان کا حل: ۵/۳٤، ۳۵).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
