### TITLE
Using zakat to repay a loan taken by a madrasah's fundraiser

### QUESTION
A man is the safīr (travelling fundraiser) of a madrasah. He took a loan from someone for the madrasah in order to spend it on the students. He has now received zakat. Can he use it to pay off that loan or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, once the students' agent has taken possession of the money, its only proper recipients are the poor and the needy. It is therefore necessary to spend this money on deserving students by way of transfer of ownership. Since the loan was taken by the safīr, he cannot spend the students' zakat money on it. He may, however, give the zakat money to the students as stipends, then collect from them the madrasah's charges for tuition, food and the like, after which the madrasah may spend it as it wishes. If, on the other hand, someone gave zakat to the safīr himself, for his own use, then he may use it to pay off his debt.

Consider what Īḍāḥ al-Nawādir states:

Most of our muftis have accepted that the muhtamim (the principal) is the agent both of the students and of the donors; and because he is the agent of the students, the donors' zakat is discharged as soon as the muhtamim or those working under him take possession. Ḥaḍrat al-Aqdas Mawlānā Khalīl Aḥmad Sahāranpūrī, the hadith scholar (may his secret be sanctified), says that with respect to the donors the people of the madrasah are like the officers of the public treasury, and that they are agents on behalf of the students and recipients — فتاوی خلیلیہ: ۱/۳۱۹. Ḥaḍrat Muftī Maḥmūd Ḥasan Gangohī (may Allah have mercy on him) has quoted the same point in Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: when students take admission and accept the muhtamim's management, administration and rules, it is as though they have said, "You are my agent" — فتاوی محمودیہ: ۲/۲۱۸. And Ḥaḍrat Quṭb-i ʿĀlam Mawlānā Rashīd Aḥmad Gangohī (may his secret be sanctified) declared in plain and explicit words that the muhtamim is the agent of the students — تذکرۃ الرشید: ۱/۱٦٤، حاشیۃ فتاوی خلیلیہ: ۱/۳۲۰. Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ (may his secret be sanctified) differed to some extent, but in the last part of his life he withdrew from that ruling — امداد المفتین: جلد دوم: ۱۰۸۵. Even so, the people of the madrasah must spend it on a valid recipient. (ملخص از ایضاح النوادر: حصہ دوم ۵۰ ۔ ۵۳، نعیمیہ) ۔

Jawāhir al-Fatāwā states:

Once zakat funds have come into the hands of the principals and administrators of religious madrasahs, these men may not spend them — beyond providing food, medicine and the like to deserving students — on other needs such as the salaries of teachers and employees, construction work, or buying books for the library, without the device of transferring ownership. They have absolutely no authority to spend zakat funds on any purpose other than the recipients laid down in the texts.

﴿إنما الصدقات للفقراء والمساكين﴾ الآية. (جواہر الفتاوی: ۱/۸۱) ۔

It is on this basis that all the jurists write, with one voice:

ما يوضع في بيت المال أربعة أنواع الأول زكاة السوائم والعشور وما أخذه العاشر من تجار المسلمين الذين يمرون عليه ومحله ما ذكرنا من المصارف (أي الفقراء والمساكين)، الهندية: ۱/۱۹۰، كذا في رد المحتار: ۲/٦۳، بدائع الصنائع: ۲/٦۸،

This makes it clear that, just as it is not permissible to spend the zakat funds of the public treasury on anything other than the proper recipients, so it is not permissible to spend the zakat fund of religious madrasahs on anything other than the recipients of zakat. After the device of transferring ownership, however, it becomes permissible to spend it on the madrasah's other needs. (جواہر الفتاوی: ۱/۸۱ ۔ ۸۳، اسلامی کتب خانہ کراچی) ۔

Jadīd Fiqhī Mabāḥith states:

The view of Ḥakīm al-Ummah Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ashraf ʿAlī Thānawī (may his secret be sanctified) is that even if the muhtamim is taken and accepted as the agent of the students, this zakat money may still not be spent on teachers' salaries and the madrasah's other needs; it may be spent only on the students' food and drink, their clothing and their particular needs. (جدید فقہی مباحث: ۵/۱٦۰، مصرفِ زکوٰۃ، ادارۃ القرآن) ۔
