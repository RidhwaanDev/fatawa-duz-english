### TITLE
An investigation into whether the chain of residuary heirs is unending

### QUESTION
Does the chain of residuary heirs end with the children of the grandfather's paternal uncle, or does it run on further? That is, does it run to the grandfather's paternal uncle and his children, then to the paternal uncle of the grandfather's father and the paternal uncle of the grandfather's grandfather, and then to their children? And if the chain does run on further, why is it carried in al-Sirājī and similar works only as far as the grandfather's paternal uncle? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question, the chain of residuary heirs does not end with the children of the grandfather's paternal uncle; it runs on further — to the paternal uncle of the grandfather's father and his children, then to the paternal uncle of the grandfather's grandfather, and so on to the end of the chain. It is recorded in Fatāwā Bazzāziyyah:

أقربهم الابن ثم ابن الابن وإن سفل

وهكذا عمومة الأجداد وإن علوا وأولادهم الذكران وإن سفلوا. (الفتاوى البزازية بهامش الفتاوى الهندية: ٦/٤۵٦).
(وكذا في لسان الحكام في معرفة الأحكام لابن الشحنة الحلبي، ص ٤۲٦، ط: دار الفكر).

A detailed fatwa on this is given in Aḥsan al-Fatāwā. Consider it:

The chain of residuary heirs does not end with the grandfather's paternal uncle. Bazzāziyyah states:

وهكذا عمومة الأجداد وإن علوا وأولادهم الذكران وإن سفلوا،

and in Lisān al-Ḥukkām, on the margin of Muʿīn al-Ḥukkām, the express words

"وإن علوا"

also occur. The reason it is not stated expressly in the general run of books is that in the earlier ranks generality had already been stated expressly again and again, so that it was no longer needed at every rank; it was left instead, by way of analogy, to the reader's understanding, and some authors did mention it by way of precaution. Since inheritance turns upon the establishment of lineage, if a man establishes his line of descent through the names of his forefathers up to one of the deceased's ancestors, he will be an heir; otherwise not. Mere shared lineage in a vague and unspecified way is not enough, for in a state of vagueness it is impossible to tell which residuaries are nearer and which more remote, and it is equally impossible to distribute an inheritance over the whole world without regard to nearness and remoteness — and a statement that entails an impossibility is void. Thus no objection remains against giving inheritance to the ذوی الارحام (relatives through females) and to the later classes. In عمومۃ الاجداد the generality expressed by وإن علوا is stated expressly in the books mentioned above. The reason the honourable jurists (may Allah have mercy on them) mention عم الاب and عم الجد separately after جزء الجد is that in the giving of inheritance to residuaries three kinds of ordering are observed: first ordering by class (ترتیب فی الجہۃ), then ordering by degree (ترتیب فی الدرجہ), then ordering by strength and weakness of the tie (ترتیب فی القوۃ و الضعف). At the rank of جزء الجد there was a danger of supposing that the paternal uncle, the father's paternal uncle, the grandfather's paternal uncle and so forth are all one single category and equal in class; and to remove that misapprehension the words ثم عم الاب ثم عم الجد were added, so that the difference of class between them, and the fact that each is a separate category, should be plain. Hence in the presence of ابن ابن العم (the son of the paternal uncle's son), عم الاب will be excluded, because he comes later in class. Had they all been treated as one and the same class, the matter would have been the other way about, for once the category is one the nearer in degree takes precedence, and in that respect عم الاب is the nearer.

In short, the purpose of the honourable jurists (may Allah have mercy on them) in this addition is not to bring the chain of residuaries to an end, but to show that the male offspring of the deceased's fathers at every level form a separate category and differ in class. To mention two or three ranks out of an unlimited number of ranks, intending by them generality and inclusiveness, and to leave the rest by way of analogy to the reader's understanding, is a widespread and common practice. واللہ اعلم. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 9/445–447, third appendix, ed. Saʿīd).

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ashraf ʿAlī Thānwī's (may Allah have mercy on him) first opinion was that the residuaries come to an end with the descendants of the grandfather's paternal uncle to the end of that line; he later retracted this view. The weaker opinion is recorded in Imdād al-Fatāwā: 4/343, and in its footnote these words are written: "Ḥaḍrat (may Allah have mercy on him) retracted this, but the detail of it was not entered in the book." (Footnote of Imdād al-Fatāwā: 4/344).

Note: This question could not be found in Ashraf al-Aḥkām either, which is the supplement to Imdād al-Fatāwā.

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

Ḥaḍrat Thānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) retracted his opinion. The correct position is the one set down by Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Aṣghar Ḥusayn Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him). It is stated expressly in al-Jāmiʿ al-Wajīz that whenever no residuary can be ascertained, the estate will go to the ذوی الارحام. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 20/420, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

Consider the words of Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Aṣghar Ḥusayn Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him). He writes in Mufīd al-Wārithīn: The account of the residuaries of the second degree has been brought to a close here. In the same way the chain can run on to the fifth and sixth generation, to the great-great-grandfather and the great-great-great-grandfather, and beyond that too; and so long as any one of them is alive, however distant his generation, the residuaries of the third degree will on no account inherit. (Mufīd al-Wārithīn, p. 141, chapter five, the account of the residuaries, ed. Idārah Islāmiyyāt, Lahore).

Al-Sirājī states:

العصبات النسبية ثلاثة: عصبة بنفسه، وعصبة بغيره، وعصبة مع غيره، أما العصبة بنفسه: فكل ذكر لا تدخل في نسبته أنثي، وهم أربعة أصناف: جزء الميت، وأصله، وجزء أبيه، وجزء جده، الأقرب فالأقرب، يرجحون بقرب الدرجة

ثم جزء جده أي الأعمام ثم بنوهم وإن سفلوا ثم يرجحون بقوة القرابة، أعني به، أن ذا القرابتين أولي من ذي قرابة واحدة، ذكراً كان أو أنثى

وكذلك الحكم في أعمام الميت، ثم في أعمام أبيه، ثم في أعمام جده. (السراجي في الميراث، ص ۳۵. ۳۸، ط: مكتبة البشرى).
وقال العلامة نظام الدين الكيرانوي في حاشية السراجي: جزء جده: أي الأعمام، ثم عم الأب ثم عم الجد، ثم أبيه كذلك وإن سفل

فظهر أن جهات العصبة بنفسه أربعة

والرابعة ـ العمومة له أو لا بيه أو لجده وإن علا، وكذا بنوهم. (حاشية السراجي المسمى بدليل الوراث، ص ۳۷).

(And likewise in Imdād al-Aḥkām: 4/611).
