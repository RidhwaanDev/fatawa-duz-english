### TITLE
Dividing the meat when the food is shared

### QUESTION
If five brothers are partners in a sacrifice and all of them share their food, will it be necessary for the meat to be divided equally or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, when the partners share their food and drink, dividing the meat is not necessary.

Consider what Fatāwā Shāmī states:

(قوله ويقسم اللحم) انظر هل هذه القسمة متعينة أولا، حتى لو اشترى لنفسه ولزوجته وأولاده الكبار بدنة ولم يقسموها تجزيهم أولا، والظاهر أنها لا تشترط لأن المقصود منها الإراقة وقد حصلت. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۳۱۷، سعيد).

(وكذا في حاشية الطحطاوي على الدر المختار: ٤/۱٦۲، كوئته).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

Question: Parents and their sons and daughters together sacrificed a cow. Some of the meat was distributed among the poor, and they wish to cook the rest and eat it together. In this case, is it necessary to divide the meat and give each one his own share, or not? بینوا توجروا

Answer: In this case dividing it is not necessary, because this is merely permitting others to partake, and it does not carry the sense of transferring or taking ownership. (احسن الفتاوی: ۷/۵۰۰، بحوالہ شامی)۔

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

The gist of the question is that all the partners live in one place and all of them eat and drink together, so is division necessary or not?

He answered: Division is not required; even if they cook it together and eat it, that is valid. Shāmī. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۷/ ٤۲٤، جامعہ فاروقیہ)۔

ʿAzīz al-Fatāwā states:

Seven people may share in the sacrifice of a cow, whether they are one's own relatives or strangers. If one's own brothers or other relatives live in a single household, there is no need to divide it; they may all keep the meat together and eat it. Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) has stated explicitly that division is not required. (فتاوی دار العلوم دیوبند، جلدِ اول، ص 683، دار الاشاعت)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
