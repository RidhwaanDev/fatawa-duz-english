### TITLE
The ruling on religious books and pamphlets

### QUESTION
Generally, when religious books and pamphlets become old, people throw them into unsuitable places where there is a fear of their being treated disrespectfully. For this reason some people have begun the work of collecting them. The question now is: what should be done with them? Also, would it be permissible to recondition these pamphlets and the like and use them again? Kindly inform us of the ruling of the Sharīʿah and place us in your debt.

### ANSWER
Regarding religious books and pamphlets, it is written in some books that the name of Allah Almighty should be erased; but there is difficulty in that. Rather, they should be buried in a clean, pure place or set afloat in water. If these two options are not easily possible, then they may be burnt, and there is no sin in this either — just as Ḥaḍrat ʿUthmān (may Allah be pleased with him) burnt the copies of the Qur'an, the details of which have already been given above. Yes, burning them with the intention of showing disrespect is not correct.

Cleaning up (recycling) these pamphlets and religious books and bringing them back into use is also permissible and correct. See what is stated in al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq:

ولو كان فيه اسم الله تعالى أو اسم النبي ﷺ فيجوز محوه ليلف فيه شيء. (البحر الرائق: ۱/۲۱۲، دار المعرفة). (وكذا في الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ۱/۱۷۸، سعيد، والفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳۲۲، الباب الخامس من كتاب الكراهية).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:
It is not permissible to wrap packets in papers on which Qur'anic verses, discussions of Sharīʿah matters, or legal questions are written. However, if there are no discussions of Sharīʿah matters on them, then it is permissible to cut out the names of Allah Almighty, of the noble prophets, and of the divine scriptures, and to wrap a packet in the remaining paper. (احسن الفتاوی: ۸/۱۳)۔

وفي الدر: ويجوز لف شيء في كاغذ فقه ونحوه، وفي كتب الطب يجوز. وفي الشامية: ولوكان فيه اسم الله تعالى واسم النبي ﷺ يجوز محوه ليلف فيه شيء. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۳۸۷، سعيد).

Kifāyat al-Muftī states:
It is also permissible to bury worn-out pages of the Glorious Qur'an in a protected place; but burning them is better nowadays, because it is difficult to find such a protected place where neither people nor animals can reach. And Ḥaḍrat ʿUthmān's (may Allah be pleased with him) burning of the copies of the Qur'an is the proof of its permissibility. (کفایت المفتی: ۱/۱۲۷، دار الاشاعت)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
