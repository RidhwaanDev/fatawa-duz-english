### TITLE
Shortening the prayer after taking the longer route that meets the sharia travel distance

### QUESTION
A man sets out from home, and there are two roads to his destination: one is shorter, and the other most probably covers the sharia travel distance. If he takes the road that covers the sharia distance, may he shorten his prayers after 30 kilometres or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, if he intends to travel to that destination and his strong assumption is that the road covers the sharia distance, then after 30 kilometres he will shorten his prayers and will not pray them in full. Consider what al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

وإذا قصد بلدة وإلى مقصده طريقان أحدهم مسيرة ثلاثة أيام ولياليها والآخر دونها فسلك الطريق الأبعد كان مسافراً عندنا هكذا في فتاوى قاضيخان وإن سلك الأقصر يتم (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱۳۸، صلاة المسافر. وكذا في خلاصة الفتاوى: ۱/۱۹۸. والبحر الرائق: ۲/۱۲۹).
