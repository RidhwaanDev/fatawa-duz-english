### TITLE
Giving the value in charity when one has vowed to slaughter an animal and distribute the meat

### QUESTION
A man vowed to slaughter a goat and distribute it. Then it occurred to him that this carried the risk of upsetting his relatives, since it may only be given to the poor and the other relatives would therefore be displeased; so he thought he should quietly give the price of the goat to the poor instead. Can this be done or not? When I turned to the fatwas of our senior scholars on this matter, they appear to conflict.

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ẓafar Aḥmad ʿUthmānī (may Allah have mercy on him) declared it impermissible in Imdād al-Aḥkām. Consider:

Question: In an unspecified vow, will the vow be fulfilled by giving the value of the animal in charity instead of the animal itself? And is it better to give the animal or its value?

### ANSWER
If the vow was to slaughter an animal, then slaughtering it is what is required and giving its value in charity is not sufficient. If there was no intention of slaughtering, then giving the value in charity is sufficient as well. (امداد الاحکام ۳/۴۲) ۔

Ḥaḍrat Muftī Rashīd Aḥmad Ludhyānwī (may Allah have mercy on him), on the other hand, declared it permissible. Consider his words:

Apart from the ʿīd sacrifice, what is intended by a vow to slaughter is a vow to give the meat in charity; otherwise a vow of the act of slaughtering in itself is not valid, because outside the ʿīd sacrifice the slaughtering of an animal is not an act of worship in its own right. Since it is not the slaughtering that is intended but the giving of the meat in charity, it follows that slaughtering the animal is not obligatory; rather one has a choice. He may slaughter the goat and give the meat in charity, or give the goat away alive in charity, or give its value in charity, or give something else equal to its value.

قال في شرح التنوير: نذر أن يتصدق بعشرة دراهم من الخبز فتصدق بغيره جاز إن ساوي العشرة كتصدقه بثمنه. (رد المحتار ۳/۷۲).
(احسن الفتاوی: ۵/۴۸۳) ۔

So the man is now perplexed and wondering whether he may give the price of the goat in place of the goat itself.

The answer: From the passage in Fatāwā Bazzāziyyah it appears that the goat should be slaughtered. The fatwa of Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ẓafar Aḥmad ʿUthmānī (may Allah have mercy on him) should therefore be acted upon; there is caution in it, and with it the discharge of the obligation is certain. Moreover, the shedding of blood is not an act of worship outside the days of sacrifice, but there is a necessary act of the same kind as it — namely the sacrifice — and when the giving of the meat in charity is intended along with it, it becomes an act of worship.

Consider the passage of Fatāwā Bazzāziyyah:

قال: إن برئت من مرضي هذا ذبحت شاة أو على شاة أذبحها فصح لا يلزمه شيء ولو قال: على شاة أذبحها وأتصدق بلحمها لزمه. لله على أن أذبح جزوراً وأتصدق بلحمه يذبح مكانه سبع شياه. لزمه إراقة شاتين وسطين فذبح شاة سمينة تعدل وسطين لا يجزئه، لأن المقصود الإراقة والتصدق باللحم، والسمينة وإن عادلتهما في اللحم لا تعادلهما في الإراقة. (الفتاوى البزازية على هامش الهندية: ٤/۲۷۱).
