### TITLE
Building a house on endowed land

### QUESTION
If the land belongs to a madrasah and someone builds a house on it with the intention of endowing it, will this house be an endowment or not? And if the endower later wishes to lodge a particular teacher in the house, does he have the better right, or do those in charge of the madrasah have the greater right?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, since the land belongs to the madrasah, the house is subordinate to the land, and so the word of the madrasah's trustee will carry in the matter, not the word of the man who endowed the building.

Consider the following. Al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq states:

وفي المجتبى لا يجوز وقف البناء بدون الأصل هو المختار وفي الفتاوى السراجية: سئل هل يجوز وقف البناء والغرس دون الأرض؟ أجاب، الفتوي على صحة ذلك، وظاهره أنه لا فرق بين أن يكون الأرض ملكاً أو وقفاً

قال في الظهيرية:

وإن كانت (الشجرة) في أرض موقوفة فوقفها على تلك الجهة جازكما في البناء. (البحر الرائق: ۵/۲۰٤).

Muʿīn al-Ḥukkām states:

وإذا كان أصل القرية وقفاً على جهة قرية فبني عليها رجل بناء ووقف بنائها على جهة قرية أخري اختلفوا فيه فأما إذا وقف البناء على جهة القرية التي كانت البقعة وقفاً عليها فيجوز بالإجماع ويصير وقفاً تبعاً للقرية هذا هو الذي استقر عليه فتاوى أئمة خوارزم. (معين الحكام مع لسان الحكام: ۲۹٤، الفصل العاشر في الوقف).

In Imdād al-Fatāwā the author answers a question whose heading is "وقف شدن بناتبعاً للارض وتابع شدنش درجمیع احکام":

In the case in question all these houses have become endowed. Had they been excluded, they would not have been endowed, but as it is there is no doubt about their being endowed; and since they are endowed تبعاً للارض, they follow the land in the conditions governing their use as well. For example, if the income of the endowed land is designated for a madrasah, or a mosque, or the poor and the like, then these houses too are to be let out and their income spent on those same purposes. (امداد الفتاوی: ۲/٦۰۷) ۔

It is thus clear that endowed land belonging to a madrasah is in the charge of those responsible for the madrasah, and that their wishes carry in it; therefore their wishes will carry in the endowed house too. For this reason the house should be handed over into the charge of those responsible for the madrasah, since the house is subordinate to the land. On this ground the endower's condition that a particular man will live in this house will not be given effect. Thus, if land is endowed for the students of a madrasah and someone plants a tree on it and endows it for travellers, that will not be valid.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
