### TITLE
Making good damage to a borrowed article

### QUESTION
Zayd borrowed ʿAmr's car to use. While it was in use some damage was caused. Is Zayd obliged to make good the damage or not? And will it make any difference whether the damage was done deliberately or without intent? Further, what is the ruling if the damage occurred because he drove faster than the set limit?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, if Zayd used the car in the customary manner and some damage occurred without any transgression on his part, then Zayd is not responsible for it and no compensation is due from him. If, however, he went beyond the customary manner and transgressed, then Zayd is responsible for it. Likewise, if he drove faster than the set speed and broke the law, and some damage occurred, compensation will again be due from Zayd.

al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

وأما حكمها (العارية) فهو ملك المنفعة للمستعير بغير عوض أو ما هو ملحق بالمنفعة عرفاً وعادة ً عندنا كذا في البدائع، والعارية أمانة إن هلكت من غير تعد لم يضمنها

ولو تعدي ضمن بالإجماع نحو أن يحمل عليها ما يعلم أنها لا تحمل مثله وكذلك إذا استعملها ليلاً أو نهاراً فيما لا يستعمل فيه الدواب في العرف والعادة، فعطبت ضمن قيمتها كذا في غاية البيان. (الفتاوى الهندية: ٤/۳٦۳، كتاب العارية).

Sharḥ al-ʿInāyah states:

إن هلكت العارية، فإن كان بتعد كحمل الدابة ما لا يحمله مثلها أو استعمالها استعمالاً لا يستعمل مثلها من الدواب أوجب الضمان بالإجماع. (شرح العناية على هامش فتح القدير: ۹/۷، كتاب العارية، دار الفكر).

Islāmī Fiqh states:

If the lender laid down no restriction as to time, place or manner of use, then the borrower may use the article for as long as he wishes and in whatever way he wishes; and if any damage occurs in that case, no responsibility falls on the borrower. But if he uses it contrary to the way in which such a thing is ordinarily used, then he will have to pay compensation despite the permission. For example, a person borrowed someone's bicycle or motorcycle and began riding it recklessly in a crowd, or faster than the speed generally laid down for it, and it collided with something: the responsibility for the damage will fall on the borrower. In the same way he will be responsible for the damage done to whatever else it collided with — if, say, someone was injured or someone suffered a financial loss, he will have to pay compensation for it. (Islāmī Fiqh: 2/498, "The ruling on loan for use.")
