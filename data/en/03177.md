### TITLE
Ruling on holding Qur'an memorisation circles early in the morning

### QUESTION
In our institution the circles for memorising the Noble Qur'an are held in the morning, while the circles for worldly education are held in the evening. The administrators, however, are of the view that it would be easier if worldly education were in the morning and the memorisation classes were held in the evening, whereas the memorisation teachers want the morning. I now request you respectfully to explain, in the light of the Qur'an and the Sunnah, which time is effective and suitable for memorisation.

### ANSWER
According to our observation and experience, the morning is the more suitable and appropriate time for memorising the Noble Qur'an, and it helps the students in committing it to memory, because in the morning there is freshness and vigour in one's disposition. It is for this reason that many of the learned have preferred the morning time for memorisation, and this is the practice acted upon in the Arab lands and beyond. See the evidence for this from the Qur'an and the Sunnah:

(1) Allah Most High says:

﴿وقرآن الفجر، إن قرآن الفجر كان مشهوداً﴾ [الاسراء: ۷۸]،

(2) In the noble hadith it is stated:

عن على رضي الله عنه قال: قال رسول الله ﷺ: "اللهم بارك لا متى في بكورها". (رواه الترمذي، رقم الحديث: ۱۲۱۲، وأحمد: ۱۳۲).

Shaykh ʿAbd al-Raʾūf al-Munāwī says under this hadith:

أخذ منه أنه يندب لمن له وظيفة من نحو قراءة أو ورد أو علم شرعي أو حرفة فعله أول النهار. (التيسير بشرح الجامع الصغير: ۱/٤۱٤).

That is, whoever has some regular practice — for example, recitation of the Qur'an, remembrance and litanies and the like — it is recommended for him to complete it early in the morning, because at that time there is goodness and blessing from Allah.

(۳) عن أبي ذر رضي الله عنه قال: قال لي رسول الله ﷺ: يا أباذر لأن تغدو فتعلم آية من كتاب الله خير لك من أن تصلي مائة ركعة ولأن تغدو فتعلم باباً من العلم عمل به أو لم يعمل خير من أن تصلي ألف ركعة. (رواه ابن ماجه، رقم: ۲۱۹).

Ḥaḍrat Abū Dharr (may Allah Most High be pleased with him) says: the Prophet ﷺ said to me: O Abū Dharr! If you go out in the morning and learn a single noble verse, that is better than a hundred voluntary units of prayer; and if you learn a chapter of religious knowledge, whether it is acted upon at that time or not, it is superior to a thousand voluntary units of prayer.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
