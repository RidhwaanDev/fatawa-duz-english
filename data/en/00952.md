### TITLE
Zakat on money lent out

### QUESTION
(1) I lent Zayd ten thousand rand. He promised to repay the whole sum after three years, and those three years came to an end in January 2004.

(2) ʿAmr borrowed twenty thousand rand and promised to repay two thousand rand each month in instalments.

(1) Is the zakat for those 3 years due from me? (2) How is zakat to be paid on money received in instalments?

### ANSWER
(1) When the loan is recovered, the zakat for the past three years must be paid at that time.

(2) Whenever any part of the loan is recovered, zakat must be paid on that amount.

Consider what Marāqī al-Falāḥ states:

وزكاة الدين على أقسام: فإنه قوي ووسط وضعيف؛ فالقوي هو بدل القرض ومال التجارة إذا قبضه وكان على مقر ولو مفلساً أو على جاحد عليه بينة زكاه لما مضى ويتراخى وجوب الأداء إلى أن يقبض أربعين درهماً، ففيها درهم، لأن ما دون الخمس من النصاب عفو لا زكاة فيه وكذا فيما زاد بحسابه. (مراقي الفلاح: ۲٦۲، كتاب الزكاة، بيروت، كذا في الشامي: ۲/۳۰۵، سعيد).

Al-Muḥīṭ al-Burhānī states:

إذا قبض منها أربعين درهماً يجب عليه الأداء بقدر ما قبض، هذا كله قول أبي حنيفة رحمه الله. (المحيط البرهاني: ۳/۲٤٦).

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states:

Zakat becomes necessary once the loan has been recovered, but if it is paid before recovery that is permissible too. A loan that is recoverable now but may not be recoverable later carries the same ruling as has just been given: paying the zakat becomes necessary only when the money is actually recovered, although if he pays it straight away that is also valid. If the loan is recovered in instalments, he should pay the zakat on each amount as it comes in; and if he pays the zakat on the whole at one time, whether before or afterwards, that is valid as well. (فتاوی دار العلوم دیوبند: ٦/۹٦ مدلل و مکمل، دار الاشاعت)۔

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

Where a loan has been given to someone, or a trader has sold goods that were held for trade and their price is still outstanding: if the whole sum is received at once, zakat must be paid on all of it; and if it comes in after several years, the zakat for all those years is paid at one time. If the money is recovered a little at a time, he should pay the zakat on each amount as he receives it. If, however, the sum is less than one fifth of the zakat nisab, no zakat is necessary. In the terminology of fiqh this kind of debt is called a strong debt. (جدید فقہی مسائل: ۱/۲۱۲، نعیمیہ)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
