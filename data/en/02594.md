### TITLE
Giving children vaccinations against various diseases

### QUESTION
Nowadays children are given injections to protect them from various diseases. Some scholars object to this and say that these injections are harmful. What is the ruling of the sharia on giving them?

### ANSWER
If experience establishes that the benefit of an injection outweighs its harm, there is nothing wrong in giving it; indeed it should be given. Yes, if the harm is established, then it should not be given.

Muftī Kifāyatullāh (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

If an injection has been shown by experience to be beneficial, it is permissible. (کفایت المفتی: ۹/۱٤۹، دار الاشاعت)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
