### TITLE
The ruling on reciting the verses of Ṭalaʿa al-Badru ʿAlaynā at a worldly game

### QUESTION
At a soccer match, supporters of the Saudi Arabian team recited the verses of

طلع البدر علينا

and, waving their scarves over their heads, called out, "O Messenger of Allah, my beloved!" Ordinary Muslims were carried away by the sight and said "ما شاء اللہ". Was this conduct of the supporters and of these Muslims correct or not? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question, reciting such verses at places of sport and other frivolity was not at all fitting, for these verses were recited on the coming of the Noble Prophet ﷺ, when he arrived safe and sound at Madīnah al-Munawwarah. What connection can there be between the coming of the Noble Prophet ﷺ — the rising of the sun of religion and faith over Madīnah al-Munawwarah — and worldly frivolities and evils?

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā records that there are two views about the verses of Ṭalaʿa al-Badru ʿAlaynā: (1) that they were recited on his arrival at Madīnah al-Munawwarah; (2) that they were recited on his return from the expedition of Tabūk.

For a full account of the evidence see: (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā, vol. 1, pp. 317–319).

Moreover, in our time most people have become so absorbed in sport that both the rights of Allah and the rights of His servants are being neglected. On this basis one should keep away from games of this sort. Yes, if a game is played for a while with the intention of recreation or exercise, then, subject to its conditions, it is permissible and in order.

See what Takmilah Fatḥ al-Mulhim states:

وأما ما لم يرد فيه النهي عن الشارع، وفيه فائدة ومصلحة للناس، فهو بالنظر الفقهي على نوعين: الأول: ما شهدت التجربة بأن ضرره أعظم من نفعه، ومفاسده أغلب على منافعه، وانه من اشتغل به الهاه عن ذكر الله وحده وعن الصلوات والمساجد، التحق ذلك بالمنهي عنه، لاشتراك العلة، فكان حراماً أو مكروهاً.

والثاني: ما ليس كذلك، فهو أيضاً إن اشتغل به بنية التلهي والتلاعب فهو مكروه، وإن اشتغل به لتحصيل تلك المنفعة، وبنية استجلاب المصلحة فهو مباح، بل قد يرتقي إلى درجة الاستحباب أو أعظم منه. (تكملة فتح الملهم: ٤/٤۳۵، حكم الألعاب في الشريعة).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
