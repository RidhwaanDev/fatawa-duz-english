### TITLE
Being in a state of ablution when entering the mosque

### QUESTION
Is it obligatory or necessary to be in a state of ablution in order to enter a mosque?

### ANSWER
Being in a state of ablution in order to enter a mosque is neither obligatory nor necessary; it is recommended. If, however, a Muslim is in a state of major ritual impurity, then a ritual bath is necessary for him, and it is forbidden and impermissible for him to enter the mosque without it. See what Fatāwā al-Shāmī states:

دخول المسجد للمحدث فإنه يحل بدون الطهارة من الحدث الأصغر. (فتاوى الشامي: ۱/۲٤۳، سعيد).

Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ records:

ولا يباح للجنب دخول المسجد. (بدائع الصنائع: ۱/۳۸، سعيد). (وكذا في النتف في الفتاوى، ص ۳۱، ط: بيروت).

Qāmūs al-Fiqh states: Among the cases in which performing ablution is recommended is doing so for the purpose of entering a mosque. (قاموس الفقہ: ۵/۲۸۷، ط: زمزم) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
