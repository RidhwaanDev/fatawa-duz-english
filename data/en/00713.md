### TITLE
Whether compensation must be paid for a missed prostration of recitation

### QUESTION
If someone misses a prostration of recitation, does he pay compensation for it or not?

### ANSWER
It is fine to pay compensation for a prostration of recitation as a precaution, but it is not necessary.

Consider what Shāmī states:

ولا رواية في سجدة التلاوة أنه يجب أولا يجب كما في الحجة والصحيح أنه لا يجب كما في الصوفية إسمعيل. (شامي: ۲/۷۳، باب قضاء الفوائت، سعيد. وكذا في البحر الرائق: ۲/۹۱، كوئتة)

Ḥaḍrat Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ wrote in Jawāhir al-Fiqh:

If prostrations of recitation have been missed, the precautionary course is to give one and three-quarter sēr of wheat, or its value, in charity for each prostration. (Jawāhir al-Fiqh: 1/393, rulings on compensation for prayer, fasting and the like, Maktabah Dār al-ʿUlūm Karachi).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
