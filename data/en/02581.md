### TITLE
Drinking human urine as medicine

### QUESTION
In recent times medical science has disclosed that it is very beneficial for a person to drink his own urine, and that there is a cure in it for many illnesses, even for heart disease and cancer. So what is the ruling of the pure sharia on the following questions?

(1) Will it be permissible for Muslims to drink their own urine from time to time in order to remove their illnesses?

(2) At a time of severe necessity, will there be permission to drink urine in order to remove a fatal illness or not?

(3) Just as blood is transferred into another person's body, will it be sound to do the same with urine or not?

(4) Is it sound to store urine as blood is stored, and to set up a bank for it, or not?

(5) Will it be permissible to give one's own urine to someone else to use as a medicine or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
(1) Urine is intrinsically filthy. In ordinary circumstances drinking it and using it is in no way permissible or sound.

(2) Even at a time of severe necessity, when the need can be met by the urine of an animal whose flesh is eaten, the use of human urine is not permissible.

As for the scientists' claim that urine is beneficial in every situation, that is a false statement. Some doctors even say that masturbation and sodomy are fine — so will their word be accepted? Certainly not.

(3) Transferring urine as blood is transferred is not permissible.

(4) Storing urine is without need, and there is no permission for it.

(5) When there is a need, one should manage with the urine of an animal whose flesh is eaten.

Note what Fatāwā Shāmī states:

قال في النهاية وفي التهذيب يجوز للعليل شرب البول والدم والميتة للتداوي إذا أخبره طبيب مسلم أن فيه شفائه ولم يجده من المباح ما يقوم مقامه وإن قال الطبيب يتعجل شفاؤك به فيه وجهان

وما قيل: إن الاستشفاء بالحرام حرام غير مجري على إطلاقه وأن الاستشفاء بالحرام إنما لا يجوز إذا لم يعلم أن فيه شفاء أما إذا علم وليس له دواء غيره يجوز. (فتاوى الشامي: ۵/۲۲۸، سعيد).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
