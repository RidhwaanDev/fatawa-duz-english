### TITLE
Spreading a silk sheet over a bed

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to spread a silk sheet over one's bed and to sit on it, or not?

### ANSWER
In the view of Imam Abū Ḥanīfah (may Allah have mercy on him) it is permissible, but in the view of the two companions (may Allah have mercy on them) it is disliked, so one should keep away from it. The evidence on this question has already been given above.
