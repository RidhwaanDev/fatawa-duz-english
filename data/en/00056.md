### TITLE
How should such people be dealt with

### QUESTION
We have tried very hard to get those at the mosque to give up these practices, but in spite of this they are still attached to them. In that case, how should we deal with them?

### ANSWER
It is necessary to try to reform them, so keep working at reforming them. Until they do reform, one should not pray behind them, nor should one send one's children to study with them.
