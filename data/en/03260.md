### TITLE
Inheritance of provident fund money

### QUESTION
If a sum is deducted from a man's salary, either compulsorily or by his own choice, and the government or the company then adds a further amount of its own, and the man puts the money in the name of his wife or of someone else — will it belong to the person so nominated, or will it be divided among all the heirs? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case described, the provident fund money is the property of the deceased. It will therefore be divided among all the heirs according to the shares fixed by the sharia. Nobody becomes its owner merely by being nominated.

See what Qāḍī Mujāhid al-Islām Qāsimī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

In matters such as a provident fund, life insurance and a cooperative society, if a person nominates someone else for his money in accordance with their regulations, then whether the person nominated is an heir or not, his position is neither that of a legatee nor that of a donee; it is only that of a trustee. The money deposited and deducted by the person who made the nomination will therefore be divided, after his death, among his heirs according to the shares fixed by the sharia. (مجموعہ قوانین اسلامی، ص ۲۵۵، دفعہ ۱۰)۔

The details of the provident fund, both compulsory and voluntary, have already been given in Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā: 5/464–466, and may be consulted there. The explanation of inheritance among the rights attached to the deceased has also already been given under the pension fund.
