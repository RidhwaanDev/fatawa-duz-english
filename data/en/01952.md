### TITLE
Ruling on prize coupons given with goods sold

### QUESTION
If a person buys something from a company or a shop and pays the price of the goods, sometimes a ticket is given along with the goods in order to attract customers, and whoever's number comes up receives a prize. Is it permissible to buy goods from that company or shop with the aim of obtaining the prize, and to take the prize, or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, if the shopkeeper charges the same price for the goods as generally prevails in the market, then it is correct to buy goods from him with the intention of obtaining a prize, and it is also permissible to take the prize that comes with the prize coupon. This prize is a gratuity on the part of the shopkeeper and is not the counter-value of anything. But if the items purchased are being sold at more than the market price because of the prize coupon, then in that case it is impermissible and forbidden to go chasing after the hoped-for prizes, and it is necessary to keep away from it, because in such a case it comes under gambling, which is forbidden in the sharia.

See what Imdād al-Aḥkām states:

This arrangement is permissible and is a gratuity on the part of the seller, and it is permissible to make a gratuity subject to a condition. (Imdād al-Aḥkām: 3/386).

Ḥalāl awr Ḥarām states:

The nature of a puzzle competition is more or less the same. In it too the person who fills in the puzzle and sends it pays a fee; if the correct solution comes out he receives a larger sum, and if not, even his original money is not returned. Yes, if no fee is taken from those who send in the puzzle, then this form will be correct and that sum will count as a pure prize. (Ḥalāl o Ḥarām, p. 381).

It is permissible and correct to make a gift subject to a condition.

See what Fatāwā Shāmī states:

قوله والهبة والصدقة كوهبتك هذه المأة أو تصدقت عليك بها على أن تخدمني سنة،

وفي جامع الفصولين: ويصح تعليق الهبة بشرط ملائم كوهبتك على أن تعوضني كذا. (فتاوى الشامي: ۵/۲٤۹، باب ما يبطل بالشرط الفاسد، سعيد).

Jadīd Muʿāmalāt ke Sharʿī Aḥkām states:

If a person makes a gift subject to a condition, will the gift be valid or not? The rule in this matter is that if he imposes a suitable condition, both the gift and the condition are valid; and if he imposes an unsuitable condition, the gift will be valid and the condition void. (Jadīd Muʿāmalāt ke Sharʿī Aḥkām: 2/87, "The ruling on attaching a condition to a gift").
