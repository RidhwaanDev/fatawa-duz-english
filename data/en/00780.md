### TITLE
Raising the hands when the preacher supplicates

### QUESTION
When the preachers make supplication during the Friday and Eid sermons, is it permissible for those listening to raise their hands and say Amen, or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described it is permissible and correct to say Amen inwardly, without raising the hands. Raising the hands and saying Amen aloud, however, is strictly disliked. Consider what Shāmī states:

وقال البقالي في مختصره: وإذا شرع في الدعاء لا يجوز للقوم رفع اليدين ولا تأمين باللسان جهراً فإن فعلوا ذلك أثموا وقيل: أساءوا ولا إثم على هم، والصحيح هو الأول، و عليه الفتوى. (شامي: ۲/۱۷۲، باب صلاة الجمعة، سعيد. وكذا في الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱٤۷. والبحر الرائق: ۲/ ۱٥٥. وفتح القدير: ۲/٦۷. ومجمع الأنهر: ۱/۱۷۱. وحاشية الطحطاوي على الدر المختار: ۱/۳٤۷)

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh states: When the preacher supplicates in the sermon for the Muslims, it is not permissible for those listening to raise their hands or to say Amen aloud, and if they do so they will be sinful. This is the correct view and the fatwa is given upon it. Without raising the hands, they may pray in their hearts or say Amen. (ʿUmdat al-Fiqh: Kitāb al-Ṣalāh, part two, p. 451, on what is forbidden and disliked in the sermon, Mujaddidiyyah).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
