### TITLE
The meaning of the hadith "المدینۃ تنفي الناس"

### QUESTION
A hadith says:

"المدينة تنفي الناس كما ينفي الكير خبث الحديد" (صحيح البخاري: ۱/ ۲۵۲)

Madinah Munawwarah is like a blacksmith's furnace: it drives out corruption and evil. Yet sin and open disobedience are found there. What, then, does the hadith mean?

### ANSWER
Two answers are generally given:

(1) It applies specifically to the Muslims of the time of the Prophet ﷺ. The Jews and the hypocrites are not what is meant. (فتح الباري ٤/ ۸۸، عمدة القاري ۷/ ۵۷۷).

(2) It applies for all time, but what is meant is that Madinah will keep expelling the wicked, and this expulsion will be brought to completion in the era of ʿĪsā (peace be upon him) and Imam Mahdī (may Allah be pleased with him). (هذا ملخص ما في لامع الدراري: ۲/ ۲٤۵).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
