### TITLE
Leading tarawih when the beard is not kept according to the sunnah

### QUESTION
A ḥāfiẓ has been leading tarawih in a mosque for 40 years. He is of excellent character, sincerely devoted to the people, and takes part in shrouding, burial and similar duties, and many of the worshippers are his own students. He does not, however, keep his beard according to the sunnah; he trims it short. What should be done? If he is removed from leading tarawih there is a danger of discord among the worshippers. Please suggest a solution.

### ANSWER
It is disliked for someone who does not keep his beard according to the sunnah to lead tarawih. In the case described, the ḥāfiẓ follows the sharia in other matters and is of fine character — but keeping the beard to the length of a fist is likewise a command of the sharia and a constant sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ. He should therefore be asked to keep it according to the sunnah and not to trim it shorter than a fist's length. This will increase his obedience to the sharia, bring him nearer to the Prophet ﷺ, and increase the affection and goodwill of the worshippers towards him.

If the ḥāfiẓ does not accept this, he should be removed, because the demand of the sharia takes precedence. His other good deeds fall under acts of obedience, whereas leading tarawih falls under acts of worship, which take precedence over acts of obedience — and the great worship of all the congregation is bound up with it. The evidence has already been given in "باب الامامت".
