### TITLE
Performing ʿumrah after stopping menstruation with medicine and then seeing blood again

### QUESTION
A woman is in Madinah. During her menstrual days she saw a few drops for three days; then she took medicine and became pure, and on the fifth day she performed ʿumrah. Before the ten days were complete she saw blood again. Was her ʿumrah valid or not? Is a dam obligatory or not?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, since the bleeding began again within the days of her habitual period, the ʿumrah will be counted as having been performed in the state of menstruation. She should therefore perform the ʿumrah again; and if she does not repeat it, a dam will be obligatory.

See what is stated in Shāmī:

اعلم أنه لا يشترط استمرار الدم فيها بحيث لا ينقطع ساعة، لأن ذلك لا يكون إلا نادراً بل انقطاعه ساعة أو ساعتين فصاعداً غير مبطل كذا في المستصفى بحر، أي لأن العبرة لا وله وآخره. (فتاوى الشامي: ۱/۲۸٤، باب الحيض، سعيد).

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh states:

If the bleeding of a menstruating woman stops, whether by some medicine or without any medicine, or does not stop completely, and then — whether or not she performed the ritual bath — she performed ṭawāf, and afterwards her bleeding began again within the days of her habitual period, then her ṭawāf will be counted as having taken place in the state of menstruation. That is, the medicine makes no difference: the blood takes the ruling of being continuous, and the ʿumrah will be counted as having been performed during menstruation. (مستفاد از عمدۃ الفقہ: ٤/۵۲٦، مجددیہ).
