### TITLE
Divorce when a man makes a false admission and produces witnesses to it

### QUESTION
If someone, for some expedient reason, made a false admission of divorce — that he had divorced his wife — and produced witnesses to that admission, has divorce taken place or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question divorce has not taken effect as a matter of conscience by a false admission; and if he has proved by testimony that his admission was false, divorce has not taken effect in the judgement of a court either.

See what ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

لو أقر بالطلاق هازلاً أو كاذباً، فقال في البحر: إن مراده لعدم الوقوع في المشبه به عدمه ديانة، ثم نقل عن البزازية والقنية لو أراد به الخبر عن الماضي كذباً لا يقع ديانة، وإن أشهد قبل ذلك لا يقع قضاءً أيضاً. (فتاوى الشامي: ۳/۲۳۷، كتاب الطلاق، سعيد).

Ṭaḥṭāwī states:

الإقرار بالطلاق كاذباً يقع به قضاءً لا ديانة. (حاشية الطحطاوي على الدر المختار: ۲/۱۱۳، باب الصريح، كوئته).

Fatāwā Tātārkhāniyyah states:

وفي الصغرى: في أمالي أبي يوسف رحمه الله: اذا قال لها: قد طلقتك، أوقال لها: أنت طالق، وأراد الخبر عما مضي كذباً، وسعه فيما بينه وبين الله تعالى أن يمسكها. (الفتاوى التاتارخانية: ۳/۲٦۱، الفصل الرابع فيما يرجع إلى صريح الطلاق، إدارة القرآن).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

When the husband made the admission of divorce merely to put matters off for the moment, and had not in reality pronounced divorce in the court, divorce will not take effect as a matter of conscience. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۲/۲۳۷، مبوب و مرتب) ۔

It is also recorded in another place:

If the point was to give false information before the person addressed and to make a false admission, then divorce will not take effect as a matter of conscience, between him and Allah Most High. If he had beforehand set someone up as a witness that he was going to make a false admission, then it will not take effect in the judgement of a court either. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۲/۲۳٤، مبوب و مرتب) ۔

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states:

By a false report divorce does not take effect as a matter of conscience; and if he set up witnesses before the false report, divorce does not take effect at all, neither in the judgement of a court nor as a matter of conscience. But if the husband's aim is not to give a report but rather to bring a divorce into effect, then divorce takes effect there and then. (فتاوی دار العلوم دیوبند: جلد دوم ص۵۰۰) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
