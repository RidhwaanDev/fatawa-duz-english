### TITLE
Making an exception for lessons and the like in the sunnah iʿtikāf

### QUESTION
Zayd is a student. He entered iʿtikāf with his shaykh in one mosque, but he studies his lesson with a teacher in a different mosque. May he make an exception for his lesson or not? I have heard that it is correct to except certain things in iʿtikāf; please explain with references.

### ANSWER
In the sunnah iʿtikāf there is no room to leave the mosque for anything other than a need recognised by the Sacred Law or a natural bodily need; otherwise the iʿtikāf becomes invalid. So in the case in question too it is not permissible to leave the mosque for a lesson, nor is it correct to make an exception for it. Making an exception turns a sunnah iʿtikāf into a merely optional one. In a vowed iʿtikāf, however, making an exception is permissible and correct.

Consider what Tirmidhī Sharīf states:

عن عائشة رضي الله تعالى عنها أنها قالت: كان رسول الله ﷺ إذا اعتكف أدني إلى رأسه فأرجله، وكان لا يدخل البيت إلا لحاجة الإنسان". (رواه الترمذي: ۱/۱٦۵، باب المعتكف يخرج لحاجة أم لا).

Maʿārif al-Sunan states:

لا يخرج المعتكف من معتكفه إلا لحاجة شرعية أو طبعية. (معارف السنن: ۵/۵۳۹، سعيد).

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

قال: طبيعية كبول وغائط وغسل لو احتلم

(أو) شرعية كعيد وأذان. (الدر المختار: ۲/٤٤۵، باب الاعتكاف، سعيد).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

If in the sunnah iʿtikāf a person forms the intention of going out for a funeral prayer, for visiting the sick, or for attending a gathering of knowledge, the iʿtikāf becomes optional and the sunnah will not be fulfilled. The sunnah iʿtikāf is only that in which no exception has been made, and in it going out invalidates the iʿtikāf. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 4/499).

Dars-e Tirmidhī states:

Since no exception is established from the Prophet ﷺ in the sunnah iʿtikāf, an independent proof would be required for the validity of making an exception in it, and no such proof exists. Hence there appears to be no room for making an exception if the sunnah iʿtikāf is to be performed in the sunnah manner. What is evident is that if a person forms such an intention when beginning the sunnah iʿtikāf, his iʿtikāf will no longer be the sunnah one but will become optional, and whatever time he spends outside the mosque will not be counted as iʿtikāf. (Dars-e Tirmidhī: 2/650).

In a vowed iʿtikāf, however, making an exception is correct.

Consider what al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

ولو شرط وقت النذر والالتزام أن يخرج إلى عيادة المريض وصلاة الجنازة وحضور مجلس العلم يجوز له ذلك. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۲۱۲. ومثله في الدر المختار: ۲/٤٤۸، سعيد. وفي الفتاوى التاتارخانية: ۲/٤۱۲، الفصل الثاني عشر في الاعتكاف، إدارة القرآن).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
