### TITLE
Joining the congregation for tahajjud in the two Holy Sanctuaries

### QUESTION
In the two Holy Sanctuaries, during the last ten days of Ramadan, the tahajjud prayer is held in congregation after the tarāwīḥ prayer, at roughly midnight. The reliable texts record the ruling that a congregation for voluntary prayer is disliked. Can Hanafis take part in this congregation? Some people forbid it strongly, while others cite Ḥaḍrat Madanī (may Allah have mercy on him) and allow people to join in. What is the decisive position in the Hanafi school? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
According to the sounder position in the Hanafi school, performing voluntary prayer in congregation is disliked. This is what the reliable texts state, and the fatwa of most of our elders (may Allah have mercy on them) agrees with it. ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him), however, inclines to the view that the dislike here is a mild one — that is, it falls into the category of what is contrary to the preferable course. On that basis there is no harm in taking part in the tahajjud congregation in the two Holy Sanctuaries; for those in iʿtikāf (spiritual retreat) in particular, taking part is a relief.

Consider the words of ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him):

والنفل بالجماعة غير مستحب لأنه لم تفعله الصحابة في غير رمضان، وهو كالصريح في أنها كراهة تنزيه، تأمل. (فتاوى الشامي: ۲/٤۹، مطلب في كراهة الاقتداء، سعيد).

It also emerges from some other books of fiqh that outside Ramadan a congregation for voluntary prayer is not recommended. Consider what Al-Jawharah states:

قوله: ولا يصلي الوتر في جماعة في غير شهر رمضان، لأنه لم يفعله الصحابة رضي الله تعالى عنهم بجماعة في غير شهر رمضان

وفي الينابيع: إذا صلى الوتر مع الإمام في غير رمضان يجزئه ولا يستحب ذلك، والله أعلم. (الجوهرة النيرة: ۱/۳۹۱، باب قيام شهر رمضان).

Consider the detailed fatwa of Shaykh al-Islām Mawlānā Ḥusayn Aḥmad Madanī (may Allah have mercy on him):

Answer: Fatḥ al-Qadīr, volume one, Kitāb al-Istisqāʾ, p. 338, states:

وقد صرح الحاكم أيضاً في باب صلاة الكسوف من الكافي بقوله: ويكره صلاة التطوع جماعة خلا قيام رمضان وصلاة الكسوف هذا خلاف ما ذكر شيخ الإسلام رحمه الله، جلد أول رد المحتار ص ۲۲۳

It states:

قلت ويؤيده أيضاً ما في البدائع من قوله إن الجماعة في التطوع ليست بسنة إلا في قيام رمضان (وفيه) والنفل بالجماعة غير مستحب لأنه لم يفعله الصحابة ث في غير رمضان.

In the texts quoted above, the expression used is "the night worship of Ramadan", and it has not been restricted to the tarāwīḥ prayer. Since it is reported that the Prophet ﷺ led voluntary prayers in congregation on the third night, and that the Companions did so until the end of the night — as is reported in many places in the Muwaṭṭaʾ of Imam Mālik and elsewhere — the expression covers all the voluntary prayers performed on the nights of Ramadan, whether tarāwīḥ or tahajjud, whether at the beginning of the night or at its end. Congregation is permitted in all of them.

The Muwaṭṭaʾ of Imam Muḥammad (may Allah have mercy on him), p. 111, states:

قال محمد رحمه الله: بهذا نأخذ لا بأس بالصلاة في شهر رمضان أن يصلي الناس تطوعاً بإمام لأن المسلمين قد أجمعوا على ذلك. الخ. فتح الباري جلد رابع ص ۲۱۷ باب فضل من قام في رمضان

It states:

أي قام لياليه مصلياً والمراد من قيام الليل ما يحصل به مطلق القيام كما قدمناه في التهجد سواء وذكر النووي أن المراد بقيام رمضان صلاة التراويح أنه يحصل بهذا المطلوب من القيام لا أن قيام رمضان لا يكون إلا بها وأغرب الكرماني، فقال: اتفقوا على أن المراد بقيام رمضان صلاة التراويح الخ.

ʿAynī's Sharḥ al-Bukhārī, vol. 5, p. 354, states:

قال الكرماني: اتفقوا على أن المراد بقيام الليل صلاة التراويح قلت: قال النووي: إن المراد بقيام رمضان صلاة التراويح ولكن الاتفاق من أين أخذه بل المراد من قيام الليل ما يحصل به مطلق القيام سواء كان قليلاً أو كثيراً، وقال العيني، في الجلد الأول ص۲۳۳ من كتاب الإيمان من عمدة القاري ما نصه: ومعني من قام رمضان من قام بالطاعة في ليالي رمضان ويقال: يريد صلاة التراويح وقال بعضهم: لا يختص ذلك بصلاة التراويح بل في أي وقت صلي تطوعاً حصل له ذلك الفضل. الخ.

The following points emerge from the texts quoted above. (1) The dislike of congregation for voluntary prayers is not absolute; there are exceptions to it. (2) Among the exceptions, the night worship of Ramadan and the eclipse prayer are mentioned. (3) Imam Muḥammad, al-Ḥākim, the author of al-Badāʾiʿ and other early authorities used the expression "the night worship of Ramadan", which is not confined to tarāwīḥ. (4) To confine the night worship of Ramadan to tarāwīḥ is the weaker view; it is the position of ʿAllāmah al-Kirmānī and ʿAllāmah al-Nawawī (may Allah have mercy on them both). Against it, Ḥāfiẓ Ibn Ḥajar al-ʿAsqalānī (may Allah have mercy on him) and Imam al-ʿAynī (may Allah have mercy on him) take it to mean all voluntary prayers, whether tarāwīḥ or tahajjud or any other, and they interpret Imam al-Nawawī's words in the light of their own position, while calling al-Kirmānī's statement odd and unsound. This is also what the wording directly indicates. So a congregation for any of the voluntary prayers of Ramadan, whether people are called together for it or not, is permitted and indeed recommended, and it falls under the encouragement given in "من قام رمضان" الحدیث. To object to it would be incorrect; rather, all acts of obedience — ṭawāf, voluntary prayer, ʿumrah and the like — will be counted within it and encouraged (کما ذکرہ العینی رحمہ اللہ تعالی).

We ourselves found that the practice of Quṭb al-ʿĀlam Ḥaḍrat Ḥājī Imdādullāh (may his secret be sanctified) in Makkah was in accordance with this, and it was the regular practice of Ḥaḍrat Shaykh al-Hind Mawlānā Maḥmūd Ḥasan (may his secret be sanctified) as well. Support for it also comes from the long-standing practice in the two Holy Sanctuaries of the ten-unit sunnah and the like, which was the practice of the Shāfiʿīs in particular; from the practice of forty units, which the Mālikīs followed; and from the ancient practice of the people of Makkah of performing seven circuits of ṭawāf after every set of four units of tarāwīḥ. واللہ اعلم ۔ (Makhṭūṭāt-i Mubārakah, p. 103); (Fatāwā Shaykh al-Islām, pp. 43–45, under the heading: Congregational tahajjud in Ramadan).

In Kitāb al-Masāʾil, Muftī Salmān Manṣūrpūrī writes:

Apart from tarāwīḥ, holding a congregation for voluntary prayer — tahajjud, for example — is mildly disliked. If, however, there are only two or three followers, there is no dislike at all.

والنفل بالجماعة غير مستحب لأنه لم تفعله الصحابة في غير رمضان وهو كالصريح في إنها كراهة تنزيهة. (شامي زكريا: ۲/۵۰۰، بيروت، و۲/٤۳۷، هندية: ۱/۸٤) وإن كان متطوعاً فالجماعة فيه مكروهة كراهة تنزيهية إلا في شهر رمضان. (حاشية العلامة أبي الوفاء الأفغاني على كتاب الآثار: ۱/۲٤۸).

(Kitāb al-Masāʾil: 1/416, published by Kutub Khānah Naʿīmiyyah, Deoband).

Mawlānā Qāḍī Mujāhid al-Islām writes:

As for Ramadan, the explicit statements of the jurists are that in Ramadan the witr and the voluntary and supererogatory prayers may be performed in congregation as a regular arrangement. Now, in the light of the general principles, some scholars hold that "voluntary and supererogatory prayers" here means tarāwīḥ alone, while other scholars hold that tahajjud is included as well; this is why some senior scholars have made a practice of performing tahajjud in congregation in Ramadan. Difference of opinion among the scholars is a mercy, so both courses are open to us: we may perform tahajjud in Ramadan in congregation, or each of us may pray it alone.

2. Congregational tahajjud may be arranged in Ramadan; but if some people do not like it and pray on their own at home or in the mosque, it is a very bad thing to speak ill of them or to find fault with them. 3. It is not proper to stop people from holding a congregation for tahajjud in Ramadan. 4. As far as Ramadan is concerned, in my view there is no harm in the imam leading the tahajjud prayer. (Fatāwā Qāḍī, pp. 69–70).

In Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband (vol. 4, p. 223, large edition), Muftī ʿAzīz al-Raḥmān (may Allah have mercy on him) wrote that a congregation for voluntary prayer arranged by calling people together is strictly disliked. On this, Muftī Ẓafīr al-Dīn (may Allah have mercy on him) quoted the position of Ḥaḍrat Madanī (may Allah have mercy on him) in a footnote and then said: the learned Muftī wrote "strictly disliked" because of the word "innovation", as he argued in the previous question; but all that follows from the passage ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) quoted from al-Badāʾiʿ and other works is that it is contrary to the sunnah and to what is recommended, which is why al-Shāmī himself writes at the end: "وهو كالصريح في أنها كراهة تنزيهية". Yet if tahajjud is counted among the voluntary prayers of Ramadan — and voluntary prayers are exactly what they are — and it is performed in congregation in Ramadan, then there is no dislike even. واللہ اعلم، ظفیر ۔ (Footnote to Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband: 4/223, footnote no. 2, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

For the evidence for the first position (strictly disliked), see: (Fiqhī Maqālāt, volume two, pp. 37–56, under the heading "Congregational voluntary prayer in Ramadan", by Shaykh al-Islām Muftī Muḥammad Taqī, may his blessings endure).

It should not be taken as repetition but read as a fresh benefit.)
