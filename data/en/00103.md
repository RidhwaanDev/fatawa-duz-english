### TITLE
The meaning of the marginal note "آیتِ حمصیہ" in printed copies of the Qur'an

### QUESTION
In the copies of the Qur'an that are in common use among us there is a verse in Sūrah al-Burūj:

﴿إن الذين آمنوا وعملوا الصلحت لهم جنت تجري من تحتها الانهار ذلك الفوز الكبير﴾

In the middle of this verse, at "الأنهار", there is a note in the margin that reads "آیتِ حمصیہ". What does this note mean?

### ANSWER
This note has nothing to do with commentary on the Qur'an or with the science of the recitations. It belongs to a separate discipline within the Qur'anic sciences, the science of counting the verses of the Qur'an.

In Funūn al-Afnān fī ʿUyūn ʿUlūm al-Qurʾān, ʿAllāmah Ibn al-Jawzī (may Allah have mercy on him) sets out five schools on the number of the verses of the Qur'an, under the heading "فصل في مذاهب البلدان في عد آي القرآن". See:

والعدد منسوب إلى خمسة بلدان: مكة المدينة والكوفة والبصرة والشام. (فنون الأفنان، ص: ۲۳۷).

Further on, Ibn al-Jawzī (may Allah have mercy on him) gives the details of these five schools and the scholars who transmitted them. In the course of setting out the position of the people of Syria he writes:

وقد روي عن أهل حمص خلاف لما روي عن أهل الشام مطلقاً. (فنون الأفنان، ص ۲٤۱).

This passage shows that from the very beginning the people of Ḥimṣ had an independent position on the counting of the verses of the Qur'an. Accordingly, wherever all the views are listed, the report of the people of Ḥimṣ is also mentioned separately at the end. See:

"ونقل عن أهل حمص أنهم قالوا، واثنتان وثلاثون آية" (فنون الأفنان ص: ۲٤٤).

Under Sūrah al-Burūj the following is written:

سورة البروج اثنان وعشرون آية في قول الجميع، بلا خلاف بينهم في شيء منها، إلا قول أهل حمص فإنها في عدهم ثلاث وعشرون، قال أبو الحسن المنادي فإن كانوا عدوا ﴿تجري من تحتها الأنهر﴾ آية وإلا فلا يدري من أين جاءت زيادتهم. (فنون الأفنان ص ۳۲۱).

This passage makes it clear that in the view of the majority Sūrah al-Burūj has 22 verses, and that is also the number in the copies of the Qur'an in common use among us. The people of Ḥimṣ, however, have one verse more, so that for them Sūrah al-Burūj has 23 verses. This is because what the majority count as a single verse, verse 11, the people of Ḥimṣ count as two: in their reckoning a verse ends at ﴿تحتها الأنهر﴾, whereas for the majority the verse does not end there, since this falls in the middle of the verse. This difference over the verse is what the marginal note "آیتِ حمصیہ" indicates.
