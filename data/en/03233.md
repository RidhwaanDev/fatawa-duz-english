### TITLE
The method of writing a will

### QUESTION
A man has three sons and one daughter and wishes to write a will in accordance with Islamic law. How should he write it? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question it is enough to write in the will: "Let every heir be given his lawful share of my property and wealth in accordance with Islamic law." Beyond that, a bequest may validly be made up to a maximum of one third. Consider the following hadith:

عن عامر بن سعد بن أبي وقاص عن أبيه رضي الله تعالى عنه قال: كان رسول الله ﷺ يعودني عام حجة الوداع من وجع اشتد بي فقلت: إني قد بلغ بي من الوجع وأنا ذو مال ولا يرثني إلا ابنة أ فأتصدق بثلثي مالي قال: لا، فقلت: بالشطر فقال: لا، ثم قال: الثلث والثلث كبير أو كثير إنك أن تذر ورثتك أغنياء خير من أن تذرهم عالة يتكففون الناس وإنك لن تنفق نفقة تبتغي بها وجه الله إلا أجرت بها حتى ما تجعل في في امرأتك

الخ. (صحيح البخاري، رقم: ۱۲۹۵، باب رثي النبي ﷺ سعد بن خولة).

Before he dies, a person is obliged to make a bequest covering the fidyah, the expiations and the ḥajj badal for those obligatory and necessary duties that he was unable to discharge.

For example, the fidyah for missed prayers and fasts — which is half a ṣāʿ of wheat for each prayer and each fast (one kilogram six hundred and ninety-one grams, that is 1,691 grams) or its value — must be bequeathed. So too the expiation for a broken oath, namely feeding ten poor people or clothing them, must be bequeathed. Again, if Hajj had become obligatory upon a person and he was unable to perform it, he must bequeath that Hajj be performed on his behalf, which is called ḥajj badal. Likewise, if zakat is outstanding from him, he must bequeath that it be paid.

If the heirs are minors, no bequest should be made in favour of anyone. If the heirs are adults but poor, again no bequest should be made in favour of anyone. As for a son's son, a daughter's son, and other needy relatives who for some reason receive nothing from the estate, it is far better to make a bequest in their favour than in favour of others. (Majmūʿah Qawānīn Islāmī, p. 257).

(And likewise in al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq: 8/460, ed. Dār al-Maʿrifah, Beirut).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
