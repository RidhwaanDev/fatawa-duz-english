### TITLE
Using a gift received in a love affair

### QUESTION
A girl gave one of our friends a mobile phone in a love affair, and later the relationship came to an end. The young man now wants to sell the phone; may he use the money himself, or is it necessary for him to give it away in charity?

### ANSWER
In the case described, since a gift given in a love affair comes under the ruling of a bribe, it did not enter the young man's ownership; it is therefore necessary to return it to the girl who gave it. Selling it is not correct; but if he has sold it, he should convey the price to that girl, unless she forgives him and gives up her claim.

See what Fatāwā Shāmī says:

لو مات الرجل وكسبه من بيع الباذق أو الظلم أو أخذ الرشوة يتورع الورثة، ولا يأخذون منه شيئاً وهو أولى بهم ويردونها على أربابها إن عرفوهم، والا تصدقوا بها لأن سبيل الكسب الخبيث التصدق إذا تعذر الرد على صاحبه. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۳۸۵، فصل في البيع، سعيد).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
