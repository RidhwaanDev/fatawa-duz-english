### TITLE
Divorce pronounced over the telephone

### QUESTION
A man pronounced divorce over the telephone while addressing his wife. The wife states that she had put the phone down before he spoke the words of divorce and did not hear the divorce. Has divorce taken effect or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question divorce has taken effect, because for divorce to take effect it is not necessary that the wife be present or that she hear the words of divorce. Understand the matter in this way: if a man writes a letter of divorce to his wife and the letter reaches her house while she is not at home, divorce will still take effect.

See what Fatāwā Shāmī says:

ثم المرسومة لا تخلو إما أن أرسل الطلاق بأن كتب أما بعد فانت طالق فكما كتب هذا يقع الطلاق. (فتاوى الشامي: ۳/۲٤٦، مطلب في الطلاق بالكتابة، سعيد).

ʿAzīz al-Fatāwā states:

It is not a condition for divorce to take effect that the wife be present. In short, it is not a condition that the woman be present at the time of divorce. (عزیز الفتاوی: جلد اول: ٤۸۸) ۔

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

For divorce to take effect it is not a condition that the woman be present, or that the words of divorce be spoken within her hearing, or that the divorce be given naming the woman. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۸/۲٦٦، مبوب و مرتب) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
