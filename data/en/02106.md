### TITLE
Taking a loan in order to live in luxury

### QUESTION
May a loan be taken with no real need, merely to raise one's standard of living and to live in luxury, or not?

### ANSWER
Permission to take a loan is given at the level of genuine compulsion; one should keep away from taking a loan merely for the sake of luxury. Consider what Islāmī Fiqh states:

The sharia has permitted the taking of a loan in cases of severe financial need, or to protect one's honour and good name. Now, if a man takes a loan for extravagance and luxury, or to keep up the standing that people credit him with and an artificial standard of living; or if he does take a loan out of genuine need but then gives no thought to repaying it, or keeps putting it off even though he is able to pay — such a man is a wrongdoer both morally and in law. That is to say, he will be called to account in the Hereafter, and in this world too he deserves to be punished. (اسلامی فقہ: ۲/٤۲۹، بلا ضرورت قرض لینے کی مذمت) ۔
