### TITLE
Changing a child's name after a separation

### QUESTION
A man gave his son a name, and then the husband and wife were separated. The mother now wants to change the child's name so that the name given by the father does not remain. Will she be allowed to do this or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, changing the name given by the father for the reason that she hates the father is not correct; she should not do such a thing.
