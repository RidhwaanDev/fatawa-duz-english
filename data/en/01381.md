### TITLE
Making up the fasts after recovering, when compensation has already been paid

### QUESTION
My sister, who lives in America, was ill last Ramadan. Her illness was such that she had to take medicine every six hours, so for each fast she used to give the compensation payment (fidyah) to a poor person. She has now recovered. Is she obliged to make up those fasts or not, given that she has already paid the compensation? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, your sister has recovered and is now able to fast, so she must make up the fasts. Once a person is able to fast, the compensation payment is not enough. She will, of course, be rewarded for what she has already given, but she should make up the fasts. Fatḥ al-Qadīr states:

ولو قدر على الصوم يبطل حكم الفداء لأن شرط الخلفية استمرار العجز. (فتح القدير: ۲/۲۷۷، دار الفكر).

Fatāwā Shāmī states:

ومتي قدر أي الفاني الذي أفطر وفدي قضي لأن استمرار العجز شرط الخلفية. (فتاوى الشامي: ۲/٤۲۷، سعيد). وكذا في الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۲۰۸).
قال في تحفة الملوك: فإن قدر على الصوم بعد الفدية قضي. (تحفة الملوك، ص ۱٤۷، رقم المسألة: ۲۵۲).
