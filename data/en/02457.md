### TITLE
A whole goat is not a condition for conveying reward

### QUESTION
A person wishes to sacrifice one goat and to convey its reward to several deceased persons, or to several living persons. Is this correct, or will a separate goat be necessary for each one of them?

### ANSWER
For discharging the necessary sacrifice it is required that each person have a whole goat, or a seventh share of a cow or the like. But for conveying reward, a single sacrifice may be offered and its reward conveyed to a number of people. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۵/ ۳۹۲، فتاوی الشامی: ٦/ ۳۲٦) ۔
