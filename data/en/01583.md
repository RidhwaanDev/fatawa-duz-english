### TITLE
Annulment of the marriage when a Shīʿī husband walks out

### QUESTION
A Sunni woman married a Shīʿī man. After spending one night with her, the Shīʿī man said, "That is it — I am off, and I shall not be coming back." What should be done now?

### ANSWER
The fatwa of the learned scholars is that, in the light of their beliefs, the Shīʿah are not Muslims, since they hold such beliefs of disbelief as the corruption of the Qur'an, the reviling of the Companions, the doctrine of the imamate, and so forth. In the case asked about, therefore, no marriage was contracted at all. Nevertheless, since the outward form of a marriage did take place, the marriage should be annulled through the Jamʿiyyat al-ʿUlamāʾ; thereafter, once her ʿiddah (the waiting period) has passed, the girl may marry elsewhere.

The evidence for the above ruling may be consulted in Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā, volume three, Kitāb al-Nikāḥ, p. 598.
