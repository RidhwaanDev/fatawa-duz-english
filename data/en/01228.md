### TITLE
The ruling on coming out of iḥrām without a sacrifice when one is prevented

### QUESTION
If a person fears that he may be prevented from completing the rites while in iḥrām, and he states that wherever he is prevented he will come out of iḥrām, may he come out of it without a sacrifice or not?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, according to the well-known position of the school it is not permissible to come out of iḥrām without a sacrifice. But if a war or some other distressing circumstances break out and there is no other way, then in a situation of compulsion it is correct to come out of iḥrām without a sacrifice, as is permissible in the view of Imām Muḥammad (may Allah have mercy on him).

See what Zubdat al-Manāsik states:

A second kind of necessity may arise, namely that the sacrificial animal cannot be obtained because of poverty and inability — for example, the authorities stop the ships at Jeddah or Kamarān and even turn them back. In that case the well-off, if an animal can be obtained there on the ship itself, may buy one and slaughter it, or they may return and do it in their home town or elsewhere; but what are the poor to do? For this the following device is mentioned: that when one sets out to perform Hajj, he should make a stipulation at the time of entering iḥrām that if he is prevented, he will come out of iḥrām (this is called اشتراط الاحلال عند الاحرام). Then, at the time of being prevented, they write that it is permissible for him to come out of iḥrām even without slaughtering the sacrificial animal. The well-known position, however, is precisely that he will not come out of iḥrām without slaughtering, and this stipulation has not been regarded as valid. Nevertheless, Kirmānī and Sarūjī have transmitted from Imām Muḥammad (may Allah have mercy on him) that if this stipulation of coming out of iḥrām was made at the time of entering iḥrām and he was then prevented, it is permissible to come out of iḥrām without the sacrificial animal. And because so much upheaval is taking place in our times, if obstacles of this kind arise somewhere because of the outbreak of war or the like, then in a situation of helplessness some room can be found; coming out of iḥrām is not permissible without qualification. (Zubdat al-Manāsik: 431–432).

Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

ما لم يذبح لا يحل وهو قول عامة العلماء سواء شرط عند الإحرام الإهلال بغير ذبح عند الإحصار أو لم يشترط، ويجب أن يواعد يوماً معلوماً يذبح عنه فيحل بعد الذبح ولا يحل قبله. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۲۵۵).
