### TITLE
The ruling on giving a copy of Al-Ḥizb al-Aʿẓam to a non-Muslim

### QUESTION
A man was sitting in his shop reciting Al-Ḥizb al-Aʿẓam. A non-Muslim woman came and asked about the book. When the man told her about the supplications and so forth in it, the woman asked for a copy, because she is not satisfied with Christianity and is investigating other religions. The question is: may that man give her a copy of Al-Ḥizb al-Aʿẓam? Please inform me of the ruling of the sharia and earn a great reward.

### ANSWER
Al-Ḥizb al-Aʿẓam is a book of Mullā ʿAlī Qārī (may Allah have mercy on him). In it he has arranged various supplications and forms of durūd from the Qur'an and the hadiths, distributing them over the whole week with a particular portion for each day. Reciting it regularly is very beneficial.

The scholars have permitted giving the Noble Qur'an to a non-Muslim, as has been mentioned earlier; so permission to give this book will apply with greater reason. On this basis the man mentioned may give this woman Al-Ḥizb al-Aʿẓam — though he should at the same time impress upon her the respect and reverence due to the book.
