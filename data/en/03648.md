### TITLE

The ruling on taking employment as an accountant at a gambling house (casino)

### QUESTION

I am an accountant, and various companies engage me for a fee. At present a gambling company has called me for accounting work. May I take up accounting employment for them? Will this salary be permissible for me or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER

In the Noble Qur'an Allah Most High has declared gambling and games of chance unlawful. Therefore keeping its accounts amounts to cooperating in it, and cooperation in disobedience is impermissible. On this basis one should abstain from such employment, and taking its salary is likewise impermissible and sinful. Consider: the Noble Qur'an states:
﴿ولا تعاونوا بالإثم والعدوان﴾ [المائدة: ۲]،

Moreover, in the noble ḥadīth the one who keeps the accounts of interest has been made a partner in the sin of interest; in the same way the one who keeps the accounts of gambling will be a partner in the sin of gambling. Consider what Muslim Sharīf states:

عن جابر رضي الله عنه قال: لعن رسول الله ﷺ آكل الربا وموكله وكاتبه وشاهديه وقال: هم سواء. (رواه مسلم، رقم: ۱۵۹۸، باب لعن آكل الربا وموكله).

Jābir (may Allah be pleased with him) says that the Prophet ﷺ cursed the one who consumes interest, the one who pays it, the one who keeps its accounts and their witnesses, and said that all of these are equal partners in the burden of interest.
ʿAllāmah ʿAbd al-Raʾūf al-Munāwī (may Allah have mercy on him) has said in his commentary on this ḥadīth:

استحقاق الثلاثة اللعن من حيث أن كلا منهم راض به ومعين عليه. (التيسير بشرح الجامع الصغير: ۲/۵۷۱، ط: الرياض).

Imām al-Nawawī (may Allah have mercy on him) says that assisting falsehood is sinful and impermissible:
قال: هذا تصريح بتحريم كتابة المبايعة بين المترابيين والشهادة عليهما وفيه تحريم الإعانة على الباطل والله أعلم. (شرح صحيح مسلم: ۱۱/۲٦، ط: بيروت).

Kifāyat al-Muftī states: Answer: Yes! This ḥadīth is authentic, and its meaning too is precisely this: that the one who takes interest, the one who gives it, the witness and the scribe are all partners in the sin. (کفایت المفتی: ۱۱/۲۰۸، جامعہ فاروقیہ) ۔
Qāmūs al-Fiqh states:
Just as it is not correct to do an impermissible and unlawful work oneself, so too employment in such works and cooperation in them is not correct. Therefore such employment in banks, insurance companies, taverns and brothels will not be permissible in which a person becomes a means for the business of interest, gambling, liquor or prostitution, invites people towards it, or writes the accounts of interest. Yes, employment as a doorman and the like may be taken, since that has no direct connection with the business. (قاموس الفقہ: ۱/۵۰۰) ۔
واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
