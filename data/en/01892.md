### TITLE
Compelling a seller to buy something in return

### QUESTION
We buy ink from a certain company, and we use that ink for printing on the buckets manufactured at our premises. We told them: we buy ink from you, so you should buy buckets from us. They agreed and even promised to buy them, but even so they did not buy. We then wrote them a letter saying that if you do not buy buckets from us, we shall stop buying ink from you. The question to be determined now is this: is it correct to compel them to buy in this way, or not? And is it permissible for us to stop buying ink from them simply because they have not bought our buckets?

### ANSWER
The pure sharia has attached great importance to the consent of the two contracting parties in trade, and has therefore not declared trade and transactions sound and valid without such consent. For this reason you cannot compel anyone to buy.

Allah Most High says:

﴿يا أيها الذين آمنوا لا تأكلوآ أموالكم بينكم بالباطل إلا أن تكون تجارة عن تراض منكم﴾. (سورة النساء، الآية: ۲۹).

O you who believe, do not consume one another's wealth among yourselves wrongfully; but if it is in a lawful way — for instance, some trade that takes place by mutual consent — then there is no harm in it. (معارف القرآن: ۲/۳۷۷).

If there is trade, that is, an exchange of properties, but the consent of the two parties is not present in it, that too is a corrupt sale and is impermissible. (معارف القرآن: ۲/۳۸۰).

You therefore cannot compel anyone to buy your buckets; and to lay down the condition that "if you do not buy buckets, we shall not buy ink from you either" is not correct.

A hadith states:

عن عمر وبن شعيب عن أبيه عن جده أن النبي ﷺ نهى عن بيع وشرط. (المعجم الاوسط للطبراني: ٤/۳۳۵، القاهرة).

Sharḥ al-Majallah states:

وأما أن لا يقتضيه العقد ولا يلائمه ولا جري العرف باشتراطه وفيه نفع لأحد العاقدين أو لغيرهما من أهل الاستحقاق، فالبيع في هذه الصورة فاسد، قال في النهر: وإنما فسد البيع بهذا الشرط لا نهما إذا قصدا المقابلة بين المبيع والثمن فقد خلا الشرط عن العوض وقد وجب البيع بالشرط فكان الشرط زيادة مستحقة بعقد المعاوضة خالية عن العوض فيكون ربا، وكل عقد بشرط الربا يكون فاسداً. (شرح المجلة لمحمد خالد الأتاسي، فصل في حق البيع بشرط: ۲/٦۰. وكذا في الهداية: ۳/۵۹).

If, however, they made a promise and then were unable to fulfil it for some reason, no sin will attach to them. As for the matter of buying ink, you may buy it from whomever you wish; there is no restriction upon you.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
