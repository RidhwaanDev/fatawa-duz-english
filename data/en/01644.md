### TITLE
Telephoning the child and taking him out during holidays

### QUESTION
The child is in his mother's custody, and the mother's right lasts until he is seven. During that time, may the child's father speak to him on the telephone or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, while the child is with his mother, if the father wishes to speak to him by telephone or to see him, he may telephone him and he may see him. He may also take him out within the city, but he is not permitted to take him outside the city.

Consider the following from Fatāwā Shāmī:

لا يجبر أن يرسله، وكذا يقال في جانبها وقت حضانتها.
وفيه: كما يمنع الأب من إخراجه من بلد أمه بلا رضاها ما بقيت حضانتها. (فتاوى الشامي: ۳/۵۷۱، سعيد).

Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

الولد متي كان عند أحد الأبوين لا يمنع الآخر عن النظر إليه. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۵٤۳).

See further: Jāmiʿ Aḥkām al-Ṣighār: 1/101; al-Fatāwā al-Tātārkhāniyyah: 4/90, Ḥukm al-Walad ʿinda Iftirāq al-Zawjayn; al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq with the marginal notes: 4/173; Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah (arranged edition): 8/458, Bāb al-Ḥaḍānah; and Khizānat al-Fiqh, Bāb al-Maqādīr, Mā Zāda ʿalā Yawm Wāḥid, p. 324, al-Maktabah al-Ghafūriyyah al-ʿĀṣimiyyah.
