### TITLE
Making up a ṣalāh when menstruation begins in its final time

### QUESTION
A woman did not pray after the time of the ṣalāh had entered, until at the end of the time her menstruation began. After she becomes pure, will making up that ṣalāh be obligatory upon her or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
Making up the ṣalāh is not obligatory upon this woman after she becomes pure, because in the matter of making up a ṣalāh it is the final part of the time that is taken into account. If she is fit for ṣalāh at the end of the time, making it up is obligatory; and if at the end of the time she is not fit for ṣalāh — that is, menstruation has begun — then making it up will not be obligatory. See what Mullā ʿAlī al-Qārī (may Allah have mercy on him) writes in Sharḥ al-Niqāyah:

ولا من حاضت، أي لا تقضي فرضاً من حاضت وكذا من نفست فيه، أي في آخر الوقت مع تمكنها من الأداء في أول الوقت بقدر ما يسع الفرض ولم تصل حتى طرأ الحيض لما قدمنا. (فتح باب العناية: ۱/۱۸٦).
وقال في مجمع الأنهر: ثم المعتبر آخر الوقت عندنا فإذا حاضت في آخر الوقت سقطت، وإن طهرت فيه وجبت. (مجمع الأنهر: ۱/۷۹، ط: لبنان).

For more, see: (Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ: 1/95, Saʿīd; Durar al-Ḥukkām: 1/54; and Al-Mabsūṭ of Imām al-Sarakhsī (may Allah have mercy on him): 2/24, Karachi).
