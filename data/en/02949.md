### TITLE
A litany for disgracing and humiliating an enemy

### QUESTION
Please tell me some effective litany or supplication for humiliating and disgracing an enemy.

### ANSWER
In the case in question, recite this supplication every morning and evening to be preserved from the harm of an enemy:

First recite the blessings upon the Prophet three times, then recite this supplication:
"اللهمَّ إنَّا نَجْعَلُك فِيْ نُحُوْرِهمْ وَنَعُوْذُ بِك مِنْ شُرُوْرِهمْ"
and at the end too recite the blessings upon the Prophet three times. (انوارِ شمسیہ، ص ۹۵)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
