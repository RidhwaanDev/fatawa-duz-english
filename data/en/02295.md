### TITLE
The ruling on taking possession in a gift of a pledged item

### QUESTION
Ḥāmid took a loan from Maḥmūd and pledged jewellery against it. Ḥāmid then repaid the loan and made a gift of that jewellery to Maḥmūd. Since Maḥmūd has studied books of fiqh, in which it is written that possession by way of liability does not stand in place of possession by way of trust, a renewal of possession is required — yet Ḥāmid lives far away in another country. What should be done now? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
This question has already been dealt with in detail in our collection of fatwas. It is enough to write here that ʿAllāmah Qāsim ibn Quṭlūbughā (may Allah have mercy on him) wrote in Al-Taṣḥīḥ wa-l-Tarjīḥ that for the renewal of possession it is sufficient that Maḥmūd remain in the presence of the item for as long as would allow him to take possession of it. He writes:

أما لو كانت في يده "أي الموهوب له" رهناً يحتاج إلى تجديد القبض قال الاسبيجابي رحمه الله: بأن يرجع إلى موضع فيه العين ويمضي وقت يتمكن عن قبضها. (التصحيح والترجيح على مختصر القدوري، ص ۲۸٦).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
