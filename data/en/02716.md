### TITLE
The ruling on a woman removing the hair of her face

### QUESTION
If unwanted hair appears on a woman's face — beard hair, moustache hair and the like — is it correct to remove it or not? If she removes it, will she fall under the prohibition of the نامصہ and متنمصہ mentioned in the noble hadith or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
It is correct and permissible for a woman to remove the unwanted hair of her face. The prohibition in the noble hadith has come with regard to thinning the eyebrows and shaping them into a line.

See what ʿAllāmah al-ʿAynī (may Allah have mercy on him) says in ʿUmdat al-Qārī:

إذا نبتت للمرأة لحية أو شوارب فلا يحرم بل يستحب عندنا والنهي إنما هو في الحواجب وما في أطراف الوجه. (عمدة القاري: ۱۳/۳۸۸، باب وما آتاكم الرسول فخذوه، كتاب التفسير، ط: دار الحديث ملتان).

Fatāwā al-Shāmī states:

وفي تبيين المحارم: إزالة الشعر من الوجه حرام إلا إذا نبت للمرأة لحية أو شوارب فلا تحرم إزالته بل تستحب، وفي التاتارخانية عن المضمرات: ولا بأس بأخذ الحاجبين وشعر وجهه ما لم يشبه المخنث ومثله في المجتبى، تأمل. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۳۷۳، سعيد).

(و كذا في مرقاة المفاتيح: ۸/۲۸۹، باب الترجل، ط: إمدادية ملتان، والبحر الرائق: ۸/۲۳۳، دار المعرفة، والفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳۵۸، وحاشية الطحطاوي على مراقي الفلاح، ص ۵۲۷، ط: قديمي).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

It is permissible for a woman to remove the hair of her face, and if beard or moustache hair appears, removing it is recommended. What the curse upon the نامصہ and متنمصہ applies to is plucking the hair from the edges of the eyebrows so as to make a thin line,

كما يدل عليه التعليل بتغيير خلق الله

If the eyebrows are excessively spread out, it is permissible to trim them and bring them to their ordinary state. In short, adornment is recommended, and the recommendation of removing a defect is comparatively more emphatic, while تلبیس and تغییر خلق are impermissible. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 8/75).

Fatāwā Bayyināt states:

It is permissible for women to remove the unusual hair of their faces — for example the hair of the beard, moustache or forehead — or the hair of the forearms and shins. It is not appropriate, however, to pull these extra hairs out, because in that there is needless pain to one's own body; it is better to remove them with some powder or the like. (Fatāwā Bayyināt, volume four, p. 407).

Further explanation of the noble hadith has already been given above and may be consulted there.
