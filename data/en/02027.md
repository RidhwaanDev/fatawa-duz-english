### TITLE
Keeping money deposited in a bank

### QUESTION
Some people carry out public welfare and charitable work, but because they are short of funds they keep the money in a bank and take the interest so that the sum will grow; and sometimes they move it from one bank to another in order to obtain more interest. Is this procedure permissible or not?

### ANSWER
Public welfare and charitable work is very good, and work that brings benefit to God's creation should be done as much as possible. But keeping money in a bank for the purpose of taking interest is impermissible and forbidden. How can it be right to commit something forbidden for the sake of a recommended deed? And to take money out of one bank and place it in another in order to get more interest is worse still. Whatever interest has been taken should be given away in charity to the poor without any intention of reward; this should not be done in future, and the people concerned should repent. There is, however, room for keeping money in a bank for safekeeping — though caution is preferable even then.

Niẓām al-Fatāwā states:

It is correct to deposit money in a bank for the sake of safekeeping. Whatever money is then received from the bank under the name of interest should be spent on those who are entitled to it and on Muslims who are weighed down by debt and in distress. (Muntakhabāt-e Niẓām al-Fatāwā: 1/188).

It also records:

The first point is that if one has to deposit money in a bank for safekeeping or out of some compulsion, one should try to deposit it in a department or account in which interest is not calculated at all. (Muntakhabāt-e Niẓām al-Fatāwā: 1/190).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

Question: A committee that serves the general body of Muslims has deposited some money in a bank in a current account, on which no interest is received. The members of the committee now wish to place this money in a "savings account" so that interest may be received on it and spent on Muslims who are in distress. Will this step by the committee be correct according to the noble sharia? And will it be correct to spend money received as interest on Muslims?

Answer: Money may be kept in a bank for safekeeping or because of legal difficulties, but it is not correct to keep money there with the intention of obtaining interest, even if the intention is that the interest money will be given to the needy. It is not permissible to keep money in a bank with the intention of obtaining interest and spending it on the poor. (Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 9/270).

See further: Kitāb al-Fatāwā: 5/330.
