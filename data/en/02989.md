### TITLE
A woman becoming a judge

### QUESTION
Is it permissible for a woman to become a judge or not?

### ANSWER
It is disliked for a woman to become a judge. If someone does appoint a woman as a judge, then in those matters in which the testimony of a woman is valid her decision too will be valid, though the one who appointed her will be sinful; and in those matters in which the testimony of a woman is not valid her decision too is not valid, such as the prescribed punishments, retaliation and the like.

قال في الهداية: ويجوز قضاء المرأة في كل شيء إلا الحدود والقصاص اعتباراً بشهادتها فيهما وقد مر الوجه. (الهداية: ۳/۱٤۱، ط: المصباح).
وفي فتح القدير: وقد مر الوجه، يعني وجه جواز قضائها وهو أن القضاء من باب الولاية كالشهادة والمرأة من أهل الشهادة فتكون من أهل الولاية. (فتح القدير: ٦/۳۹۱). (وكذا في العناية: ٦/۳۹۱، والدر المختار: ۵/٤٤۰، سعيد، وبدائع الصنائع: ۷/۳، سعيد، والموسوعة الفقهية: ۱۲/٦۵۳۲، وفتاوى قاضيخان: ۲/۳٦٤).

Muftī Muḥammad ʿAbd al-Salām Chāṭgāmī (may his shade endure) writes in Jawāhir al-Fatāwā:

Imām al-Baghawī (may Allah have mercy on him) has given two fundamental reasons for a woman's being unsuited to the office of leadership and of judgeship:

1. The first is that the affair of a woman is one of concealment: she should remain behind the veil and remain within the home, and should not leave the home without a legitimate religious or natural need. But if she accepts the office of leadership or of judgeship, it is an absolute certainty that she will have to go out repeatedly and that, through mixing with men, she will be left unveiled. Hence the temperament and nature of a woman are not fit for the office of leadership and of judgeship, and she is therefore not suited to it.

2. The second reason is that womankind, however educated, intelligent and quick-witted she may be, is by nature and creation not complete in intellect, as the Messenger of Allah ﷺ has pointed out. For the office of leadership and of judgeship, however, it is necessary that there be such persons as, along with being highly educated in religious and worldly matters, also possess a complete and perfect intellect; and by nature and creation the male sex is entirely suited and appropriate for this. (جواہر الفتاوی: ۱/۳۵۵، اسلامی کتب خانہ، کراچی)۔
