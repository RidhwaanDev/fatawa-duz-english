### TITLE
A girl who has made herself a boy standing in the men's row

### QUESTION
A girl has made herself into a boy. May she stand in the row of the men or not? She has even grown a beard.

### ANSWER
The jurists have explained with regard to the khunthā (a person of indeterminate sex) that once the marks of a male become apparent, such a person comes under the ruling of a man, and it is correct for him to stand in the row with the men. So a girl who has made herself a boy and in whom the marks of a boy have appeared may stand in the men's row, and everyone's prayer will be valid. Such an act is, however, a repugnant one: it comes under altering the creation of Allah and is forbidden. One must give it up and fear the anger of Allah. Consider al-Durr al-Mukhtār:

فإن بال من الذكر فغلام

هذا قبل البلوغ فإن بلغ وخرجت لحيته أو وصل إلى امرأة أو احتلم كما يحتلم الرجل فرجل. (الدر مختار: ٦/۷۲۷، كتاب الخنثى، سعيد)

ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) says of the khunthā mushkil (one whose sex cannot be determined) that if such a person prays in the row of the men, the prayer is valid. All the more certainly, then, will the prayer of someone in whom the marks of a man predominate be valid in the men's row.

Consider the following:

وإن قام في صف الرجال فصلاته تامة. (الشامي: ٦/۷۲۸، كتاب الخنثى، سعيد. وكذا في الطحطاوي على الدر المختار: ٤/۳٥۰. والفتاوى الهندية: ٦/٤۳۷. والهداية: ٤/۷۰۱. وكنز الدقائق: ٤۸۹. والبحر الرائق: ۸/٤۷۲)

Mawlānā Khālid Sayfullāh writes:

The view of Islam is that the body is a trust from Allah and its form is a manifestation of Allah's creation, in which no self-devised alteration is permissible without a genuine religious or natural need. This is why the Messenger of Allah ﷺ ruled that attaching artificial hair, or creating a gap between the teeth for the sake of beauty, is impermissible, deserving of a curse, and an alteration of Allah's creation. It is therefore obvious that any such operation or bodily alteration carried out merely for adornment and fashion — as is done these days to the nose, the breasts and so on — is by no means permissible. (جدید فقہی مسائل: ۲/۱۷۱) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالیٰ اعلم
