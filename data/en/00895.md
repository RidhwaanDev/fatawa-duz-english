### TITLE
The ruling on someone martyred at the hands of an unknown wrongdoer

### QUESTION
If a person is martyred at the hands of an unknown wrongdoer, will he be given the ritual bath or not?

### ANSWER
A person killed at the hands of an unknown wrongdoer comes under the ruling of a martyr, so the rulings of the martyrs apply to him; that is, according to the Ḥanafīs he will not be given the ritual bath.

Al-Mabsūṭ states:

ومن صار مقتولاً من جهة قطاع الطريق لم يغسل أيضاً لأنه قتل دافعاً عن ماله وقد قال ﷺ: "من قتل دون ماله فهو شهيد" فلهذا لا يغسل. (المبسوط: ۲/٥۲، باب الشهيد، إدارة القرآن)

Al-Hidāyah states:

ومن قتله أهل حرب أو أهل البغي أو قطاع الطريق فبأي شيء قتلوه لم يغسل لأن شهداء أحد ما كان كلهم قتيل السيف والسلاح. (الهداية: ۱/۱۸۳، باب الشهيد، شركة علمية. ودرر الحكام شرح غرر الأحكام: ۱/۱٦۹، باب الشهيد)

Bahishtī Zewar states: the rulings of the martyr that are meant to be explained here apply only to the martyr in whom the following conditions are found:

(1) He is a Muslim.

(2) He is legally responsible, that is, sane and adult.

(3) He is free of major ritual impurity.

(4) He was killed although innocent.

(5) He was killed with a wounding instrument.

(6) The sharia did not from the outset lay down any financial compensation as the penalty for that killing; rather, retaliation became due.

(7) After receiving the wound, nothing of comfort or of the enjoyment of life occurred to him — such as eating, drinking, sleeping, taking medicine and so on — nor did he remain alive and in full possession of his senses for as long as the time of one prayer.

The ruling for a martyr in whom all these conditions are found is that he is not to be given the ritual bath, his blood is not to be removed from his body, and he is to be buried just as he is. The second ruling is that the clothes he is wearing are not to be taken off his body. However, if his clothes are fewer than the sunnah number, further clothes should be added to complete the sunnah number; likewise, any extra clothes should be taken off. If any one of these conditions is not found in a martyr, he will be given the ritual bath and will also be given a new shroud like other deceased people. (Bahishtī Zewar, part eleven: p. 100, Mudallal wa Mukammal, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
