### TITLE
Drivers, agents and the like passing the mīqāt without iḥrām

### QUESTION
Is it permissible for agents, drivers and others like them to travel to Makkah without iḥrām, or not? They say that it causes them hardship.

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, if the agents and drivers travel back and forth every day, then entering iḥrām should not be necessary for them. But those who do not travel repeatedly — agents, for instance, do not usually travel every day — will have to enter iḥrām.

See what is stated in Naʾe Masāʾil awr ʿUlamāʾ ke Fayṣle:

Under present conditions, when traders, office workers, taxi drivers and other working people have to enter the Ḥaram sometimes every day and sometimes every second or third day — and some of them more than once in a single day — it is extremely burdensome and difficult for such people to be bound to enter iḥrām and perform Umrah on every occasion. There will therefore be scope for such people to enter the precincts of the Ḥaram without entering iḥrām. (نئے مسائل اور علماء ہند کے فیصلے: ۳۷، از حضرت قاضی مجاہد الاسلام صاحب قاسمی).

ʿUmdat al-Qārī states:

قال أبو عمر: لا أعلم خلافاً بين فقهاء الأمصار في الحطابين ومن يدمن الاختلاف إلى مكة ويكثره في اليوم والليلة أنهم لا يأمرون بذلك لما عليهم فيه من المشقة. (عمدة القاري: ۷/۵۳۵، باب دخول الحرم ومكة بغير إحرام، دار الحديث ملتان).

Al-Hidāyah states:

من كان داخل الميقات له أن يدخل مكة بغير إحرام لحاجته لأنه يكثر دخوله مكة وفي إيجاب الإحرام في كل مرة حرج بين. (الهداية: ۱/۲۳۵، فصل في المواقيت).

Jadīd Fiqhī Mabāḥith states:

The question now arises that in our day people in business need to travel back and forth very frequently. The people of Makkah, for example, have to travel to Madinah again and again, and the people of Madinah have to travel to Makkah again and again for their business. If they are required to enter iḥrām and perform Umrah on every occasion, severe hardship and difficulty follow. Can there be any concession or scope for them in the sharia?

The answer is that for those who travel back and forth once in a month or two there will be no concession. As for those who travel every day or every week, we see two ways for them to pass the mīqāt without iḥrām.

The second way: it is narrated from Ḥaḍrat ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿAbbās (may Allah be pleased with him) that those who bring firewood from outside the mīqāt, and workers, traders and wage earners who travel back and forth repeatedly, are permitted to keep crossing the mīqāt without iḥrām.

This is because if they were bound to enter iḥrām every time, there would be a danger of severe hardship. In Muṣannaf Ibn Abī Shaybah, Nukhab al-Afkār and elsewhere, the narration of Ibn ʿAbbās (may Allah be pleased with him) is reported in these words:

عن ابن عباس رضي الله تعالى عنهما قال: لا يدخل أحد مكة إلا بإحرام إلا الحطابين والعمالين وأصحاب منافعهما، الحديث. (نخب الأفكار قلمي: ۵/۲۱۹، مصنف ابن أبي شيبة: ٤/۲۱۱، طحاوي شريف من عطاء: ۱/٤۳۸، تلخيص الحبير: ۱/۲۱۱.

(جدید فقہی مباحث: ۱۳/۱٦۱، ۳۵، ۳۷، ادارۃ القرآن).
