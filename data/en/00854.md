### TITLE
The funeral prayer for someone who drinks alcohol

### QUESTION
If a person drinks alcohol, will his funeral prayer be performed or not? And can a person who drinks alcohol be called a disbeliever?

### ANSWER
What the funeral prayer requires is that the deceased be a Muslim. So the funeral prayer will be performed for one who neglected the prayer, for one who drank alcohol, and for all such people. There is no objection, though, if the scholars and leading figures do not take part in it, so that others may take a lesson from this. Moreover, it is not correct to call anyone a disbeliever merely for drinking alcohol, unless he regards alcohol as lawful.

Kanz al-ʿUmmāl states:

قال النبي ﷺ صلوا خلف كل بر وفاجر وصلوا على كل بر وفاجر. (كنز العمال: ٦/٥٤).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
