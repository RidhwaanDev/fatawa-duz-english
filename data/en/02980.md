### TITLE
Trimming a bird's beak for a beneficial purpose

### QUESTION
If a bird's beak is trimmed for some beneficial purpose, so that it can eat even the finest grains, is this permissible or not? Does this not come under causing pain and torment to a living creature?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, there is permission to trim the beak on account of certain benefits; but such methods should be adopted as cause the animal the least possible pain, and nowadays such methods have indeed been devised.

The necessary details concerning the trimming of the beak are as follows:

The benefit of trimming a bird's beak:

If the beak is not trimmed and grows beyond the normal measure, eating becomes difficult.

A bird's beak is naturally long, the measure of the length differing according to the species; according to what the experts say, the beak of some birds grows up to 3 centimetres in a year.

The benefit of trimming hens' beaks:

When hens become numerous, they attack one another and cause injury; to avoid this harm the hens' beaks are trimmed.

Eating becomes easier for them.

Methods of trimming the beak:

There are veins in the beak, so that if the beak is trimmed very short there is a risk of bleeding, and the pain and distress are also greater. The following are ways of reducing the pain:

A piece of wood should be placed in the bird's cage so that the bird rubs its beak against it of its own accord. The following benefits are obtained from this:

The bird does not need to be taken out of its cage.

If the owner were to perform this act, it is possible that the bird would take fright.

The likelihood of a mistake in it is less.

Instruments should be used that have been made specially for this task.

The birds should be taken to a veterinary doctor, who knows its finer points well.

The ruling on trimming the beak in the eye of the Sharīʿah, and its evidences:

قال في البحر الرائق: وفي المحيط: أن الأصل إيصال الألم إلى الحيوان لمصلحة تعود إلى الحيوان يجوز ولا بأس بكي البهائم للعلامة. (البحر الرائق: ۸/۲۳۲، دار المعرفة، بيروت). (وكذا في الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳۵٦، وفتاوى الشامي: ٦/۳۸۸، سعيد).
وفي الفتاوى الهندية: ولا بأس بقطع إلية الشاة إذا انفلتت ويمنعها من اللحوق بالقطع ويخاف عليها الذئب. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳٦۱).
