### TITLE
Taking the male organ into the mouth

### QUESTION
Is it permissible for a wife to take her husband's organ into her mouth, or for the husband to place it in her mouth? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question this act should be avoided. The mouth is meant for eating and drinking, for permissible things and for the remembrance of Allah; it is not a place for the male organ. Furthermore, when it is taken into the mouth there is a strong likelihood of pre-seminal fluid coming out, which is filthy and impure. This act is therefore against sound nature and is disliked.

Consider what al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

في النوازل: إذا أدخل الرجل ذكره في فم امرأته قد قيل يكره وقد قيل بخلافه كذا في الذخيرة. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳۷۲).

In Aḥsan al-Fatāwā, following the passage from al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah, it is written:

أقول: المبيح مجهول منكر، وقوله مردود شرعاً وعقلاً. (
احسن الفتاوی: ۸/٤۵) ۔

That is: the one who declares it permissible is unknown and objectionable, and his statement is rejected both by the sharia and by reason.

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

There is no doubt that the outer part of the private part is pure, but it does not follow that every pure thing may be put to the mouth, taken into the mouth, kissed and licked. The moisture of the nose is pure — so can putting the tongue to the inside of the nose and taking its moisture into the mouth be a desirable habit? Can it be permitted? The outer part of the anus (the place of excretion) is likewise not impure but pure — so will kissing it be permitted? No, never. In the same way it is not permitted to kiss a woman's private part or to put the tongue to it. It is severely disliked and sinful, and it resembles the habit of animals such as dogs and goats. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ٦/۲۷۰) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
