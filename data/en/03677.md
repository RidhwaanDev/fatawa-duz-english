### TITLE
The ruling on a partner becoming a hired worker and an employee and taking a salary

### QUESTION
The ruling on a partner becoming a hired worker and an employee and taking a salary.

### ANSWER
In the view of some scholars of the present age, in a partnership arrangement a partner may become a hired worker; from this point of view Zayd's taking a wage is correct. Consider: in Aḥsan al-Fatāwā, Muftī Rashīd Aḥmad Ṣāḥib Ludhyānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) writes as follows.

After transmitting the passages of the books of fiqh, he has drawn attention by way of summary to a few points:

1. The impermissibility of engaging a partner as a hired worker is not established by any Sharʿī text.

2. There is no narration from Ḥaḍrat the Imām (may Allah Most High have mercy on him) concerning it.

3. There is a statement of Imām Muḥammad (may Allah have mercy on him), but no cause for it has been transmitted from him.

4. The causes which some of the shaykhs (may Allah have mercy on them) have stated are defective in the view of other shaykhs (may Allah have mercy on them).

5. ʿAllāmah al-Saʿdī (may Allah have mercy on him) has classed it as being like the leasing of an undivided share.

6. The leasing of an undivided share is permissible by the consensus of the four imāms; however, in the view of Imām Muḥammad (may Allah have mercy on him) it is impermissible in favour of a non-partner.

7. The three imāms are agreed upon the permissibility of engaging a partner as a hired worker, like the leasing of an undivided share.

8. Despite the prohibition of qafīz al-ṭaḥḥān being established by a Sharʿī text, some jurists have given the verdict of its permissibility on account of customary practice; but other jurists did not accept this, for the reason that this customary practice is peculiar to their lands, and it is not permissible to abandon a Sharʿī text on the basis of a particular custom.

9. For abandoning a Sharʿī text a general custom is a condition; but for abandoning the text of the school even a particular custom is sufficient.

10. From the standpoint of the Ḥanafī school, rulings based upon customary practice take precedence over the statements of the two companions (may Allah have mercy on them).

11. When the text of the school is abandoned even on the basis of a particular custom, and the engaging of the partners of a company as hired workers is a general custom, then with all the greater reason the text of the school will be set aside — especially when this text is not that of the Imām either, but rather the statement of Muḥammad (may Allah have mercy on him), over which customary practice takes precedence. Besides this, support for it is also derived from muḍārabah, for the muḍārib obtains profit from joint work. (احسن الفتاوی: ۷/۳۲۸) ۔
