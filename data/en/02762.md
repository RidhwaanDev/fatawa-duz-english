### TITLE

The ruling on replying to a salām given over the radio

### QUESTION

If salām is given over the radio, is it obligatory for the listener to reply or not?

### ANSWER

It is not obligatory to reply to a salām given over the radio; it is, however, better to reply by way of precaution, since it is a word of supplication, and supplication is also made for one who is absent.
Consider what Sharḥ Riyāḍ al-Ṣāliḥīn states:

والسلام بمعني: الدعاء بالسلامة من كل آفة، فإذا قلت لشخص: السلام عليك، فهذا يعني إنك تدعو له بأن الله يسلمه من كل آفة: يسلمه من المرض، من الجنون، يسلمه من الناس، يسلمه من المعاصي، وأمراض القلوب، يسلمه من النار، فهو لفظ عام، معناه: الدعاء للمسلَّم عليه بالسلامة من كل آفة. (شرح رياض الصالحين: ۲/۱۱۳۷).

Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) writes in Ālāt-e Jadīdah ke Sharʿī Aḥkām:
As for the question whether, when someone has said al-salāmu ʿalaykum over the radio, it is obligatory for those who hear it to reply, what the principles appear to require is this: that the discharge of this obligation in such a way that the one who gave the salām comes to know of the reply is not within the power of those who hear it, and so the obligation of replying should fall away. It is better, however, to give the reply to the salām by way of precaution, since it is a word of supplication, and supplication can also be made for one who is absent. (آلاتِ جدیدہ کے شرعی احکام، ص۱٦۷) ۔

قال في الهندية: لا يسقط فرض جواب السلام إلا بالإسماع كما لا يجب إلا بالإسماع كذا في الغياثية. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳۲٦).

Mawlānā Thānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) says in Imdād al-Fatāwā:
Making the reply known is necessary: if the person is near, then by letting him hear it; and if he is far off or deaf, then by a sign together with uttering it with the tongue. Some of the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them) replied to the salām of the Prophet ﷺ very softly, but that act was on account of an incidental circumstance, فلا یقاس علیہ غیرہ ۔ (امداد الفتاوی: ٤/۲۷٦) ۔
Muftī Rashīd Aḥmad Ludhyānwī (may Allah have mercy on him), however, says that if one has the ability, then letting the reply be heard is obligatory, and if one does not have the ability, then letting it be heard is not obligatory. See: (احسن الفتاوی: ۸/۱۳۹، وج ۹/۱۹، مسائل شتی) ۔
واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
