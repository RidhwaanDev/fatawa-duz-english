### TITLE
Inheriting unlawfully acquired wealth

### QUESTION
A man's father had unlawfully acquired wealth. After his death, may his heirs take this wealth or not — that is, does inheritance run in unlawful wealth or not? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case described, if an heir knows for certain of a particular item that it was obtained by unlawful means, he is obliged to hand that very item over to its owner; it is not permissible for him to take it as part of the inheritance. But if he does not know for certain of a particular item that it was obtained wrongfully, and knows only that the deceased used to acquire money by every means — interest, bribery and the rest — while he also had lawful ways of earning, and the lawful ways predominated, then, because the wealth is mixed and the unlawful part cannot be identified, inheritance will run in the whole of it. It is nevertheless better if the heirs, by mutual agreement, give some portion of the wealth in charity. See what Fatāwā al-Shāmī states:

مطلب فيمن ورث مالاً حراماً، قوله إلا في حق الوارث، أي فإنه إذا علم إن كسب مورثه حرام يحل له لكن إذا علم المالك بعينه فلا شك في حرمته ووجوب رده عليه. وفي منية المفتي: مات رجل ويعلم الوارث أن أباه كان يكسب من حيث لا يحل ولكن لا يعلم الطالب بعينه ليرد عليه حل له الإرث والأفضل أن يتورع ويتصدق بنية خصماء أبيه. وكذا لا يحل إن علم عين الغصب مثلاً وإن لم يعلم مالكه لما في البزازية: أخذ مورثه رشوة أو ظلماً إذا علم ذلك بعينه لا يحل له أخذه وإلا فله أخذه حكماً أما في الديانة فيتصدق به بنية إرضاء الخصماء.
والحاصل: أنه إن علم أرباب الأموال وجب رده عليهم وإلا فإن علم عين الحرام لا يحل له ويتصدق به بنية صاحبه وإن كان مالاً مختلطاً مجتمعاً من الحرام ولا يعلم أربابه ولا شيئاً منه بعينه حل له حكماً والأحسن ديانة التنزه عنه. (فتاوى الشامي: ۵/۹۹، سعيد). وكذا في الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳٤۹، كتاب الكراهية).

Imdād al-Fatāwā records:

Although some jurists have said without qualification that unlawful wealth is lawful for the heir, this report is not sound. The position given as the fatwa, and the reliable one, is that it is unlawful for the heirs as well. So if the heirs know the people whose rights are involved, they should return the very item to them if it is still intact, and otherwise its value. If those people are not known, then, if the unlawful wealth is distinct and identifiable, they should give it in charity on behalf of its owner; and if it is mixed and cannot be distinguished, then, if its amount and value are known, they should give that in charity, and otherwise they should estimate it and give that in charity. If Allah wills, they will not be called to account for it in the hereafter. This is on the authority of al-Shāmī. (امداد الفتاوی: ٤/۳۵۰)۔

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

If the real owner of this wealth is known, it must be returned to him. If he is not known, but it is known which wealth is specifically the unlawful wealth, then it must be given in charity to some poor person with the intention of conveying the reward to the real owner. And if the wealth is mixed, so that it is not known which part is unlawful and which is lawful, then it is in order for the heirs to use it; but it is better for them to be cautious about it — that is, to give it to the owner if he is known, and otherwise to give it in charity.

If the whole of the wealth is unlawful, it is not in order to use it. If the owner is known it must be returned to him, and if he is not known it should be given in charity. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۲۰/۳٤۰، جامعہ فاروقیہ)۔
