### TITLE
Did the Noble Prophet ﷺ ever call the adhān

### QUESTION
Did the Prophet ﷺ call the adhān or not? If the answer is in the negative, what is the wisdom behind his not performing this important act of worship? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā ʿAbd al-Ḥayy al-Lakhnawī (may Allah have mercy on him) wrote a treatise on this question, entitled

"خير الخبر في أذان سيد البشر "

In this treatise the ʿAllāmah set out the evidences of both those who affirm it and those who deny it, and gave preference to the position that the Prophet ﷺ did not call the adhān for ṣalāh. A summary is recorded below:

On this matter the noble scholars fall into two groups. (1) The inclination of Imām al-Nawawī (may Allah have mercy on him), ʿAllāmah al-Suyūṭī (may Allah have mercy on him) and their followers is that the Noble Prophet ﷺ called the adhān himself, in his own noble person.

(2) The view of Ḥāfiẓ Ibn Ḥajar (may Allah have mercy on him), Mullā ʿAlī al-Qārī (may Allah have mercy on him) and other authorities is that the Noble Prophet ﷺ did not himself call the adhān. Consider first the evidences of the first group, that is, of those who affirm it. Sunan al-Tirmidhī states:

عن كثير بن زياد عن عمرو بن عثمان بن يعلى بن مرة عن أبيه عن جده: أنهم كانوا مع النبي ﷺ في مسير فانتهوا إلى مضيق وحضرت الصلاة فمطروا السماء من فوقهم والبلة من أسفل منهم فأذن رسول الله ﷺ وهو على راحلته وأقام فتقدم على راحلته فصلى بهم يومي إيماء يجعل السجود أخفض من الركوع، قال أبو عيسى: هذا حديث غريب تفرد به عمر بن الرماح البلخي لا يعرف إلا من حديثه. (سنن الترمذي: ۱/۹٤، رقم: ٤۱۱، ط: فيصل).
قال في التلخيص الحبير: قال عبد الحق إسناده صحيح والنووي إسناده حسن وضعفه البيهقي وابن العربي وابن القطان لحال عمرو بن عثمان، وقد رواه الدارقطني من هذا الوجه بلفظ: فأمر المؤذن فأذن وأقام أو أقام بغير أذان ثم تقدم فصلى بنا على راحلته ورجح السهيلي هذه الرواية لأنها بينت ما أجمل في رواية الترمذي وإن كان الراوي له عن عمر بن الرماح عنده شديد الضعف. (التلخيص الحبير: ۱/۵۲۲/۳۱۳، ط: دار الكتب العلمية). (وكذا في سنن الدارقطني: ۱/۳۸۰).

The answer of the second group to this narration has already come, namely that the narration of al-Tirmidhī is undetailed while the narration of al-Dāraquṭnī is explanatory, and Ḥāfiẓ al-Suhaylī gave preference to this explanatory report. Further, this is of the same order as

"بنى الأمير المدينة"

that is, his calling the adhān means

"أمر المؤذن بالأذان "

Quite apart from all this, the narration is itself weak: there is severe criticism of ʿUmar ibn al-Rammāḥ.

(2) The second evidence of those who affirm it: ʿAllāmah al-Suyūṭī (may Allah have mercy on him) mentioned in his book

"التوشيح بشرح صحيح البخاري "

that the Prophet ﷺ himself called the adhān, and Imām al-Nawawī (may Allah have mercy on him) argued from the hadith of al-Tirmidhī. Ḥāfiẓ Ibn Ḥajar (may Allah have mercy on him) answered this by saying that in the Musnad of Aḥmad the wording is

"أمر بلالاً "

But I say: I came across a mursal narration in the Sunan of Saʿīd ibn Manṣūr to the effect that the Prophet ﷺ called the adhān:

ثنا أبو معاوية، ثنا عبد الرحمن بن أبي بكر القرشي، عن ابن أبي مليكة، قال: أذن رسول الله ﷺ مرة، فقال: حي على الفلاح،

and no interpretation is possible in this narration. اھ ۔

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā ʿAbd al-Ḥayy al-Lakhnawī (may Allah have mercy on him) says: The upshot of the investigation is that the Prophet's ﷺ calling of the adhān in an unrestricted sense is established, and there is no disagreement over this. The narration of al-Tirmidhī and Abū Dāwūd states:

عن أبي رافع رضي الله عنه قال: رأيت رسول الله ﷺ أذن في أذن الحسن بن علي حين ولدته فاطمة رضي الله تعالى عنها بالصلاة. (سنن الترمذي: ۱/۲۷۸/۱۵۱٤، وأبو داود، ص ٦۹٦/۵۱۰۷).

The disagreement is over his calling the adhān for ṣalāh. The details of the narration of Sunan al-Tirmidhī have already passed; and from the narration of the Sunan of Saʿīd ibn Manṣūr too, what is apparently meant is the adhān in the ear of a newborn (اذان فی اذن المولود) — otherwise what need was there to say

فقال: حي على الفلاح

Therefore the statement of Imām al-Suyūṭī (may Allah have mercy on him) that no interpretation is possible here is not comprehensible to us.

In any event, up to now the Prophet's ﷺ calling of the adhān for ṣalāh is not explicitly established, and we content ourselves with this; it is possible that some new investigation may come to light later.

لعل الله يحدث بعد ذلك أمراً.

The wisdom behind the Noble Prophet ﷺ not calling the adhān:

1. Had the Noble Prophet ﷺ called the adhān, congregation would have become obligatory, and in that there is hardship. This answer, however, is not satisfying.

2. Shaykh Abū al-Ḥasan al-Shādhilī (may Allah have mercy on him) says that he did not call the adhān because he avoided praise of himself, since it contains

"أشهد أن محمداً رسول الله "

But this too is not such as to set the mind at rest.

3. The Noble Prophet ﷺ was occupied with the accomplishment of the momentous works of the ummah, and for this reason he did not call the adhān; otherwise a disruption would have occurred in them. Summarised from

"خير الخبر في أذان سيد البشر "

Majmūʿah Rasāʾil al-Lakhnawī (4/323–332, published by Idārat al-Qurʾān, Karachi).

4. The adhān comprises, along with the testimony to divine oneness, the testimony to messengership as well, and testimony to messengership is more fitting from the tongue of another.

5. Whenever he performed an act he would perform it constantly, and constancy upon the adhān would have entailed hardship for him.

6. The adhān is an undertaking to convey the message of the religion to the whole world, and this task the Prophet ﷺ entrusted to the ummah.
