### TITLE
What the wedding feasts of the Prophet's blessed wives were like

### QUESTION
What were the wedding feasts (walīmah) of the Prophet's blessed wives like? That is, what food was served, and roughly how many people took part in the meal? And please do not forget the wedding feast of Ḥaḍrat Fāṭimah (may Allah be pleased with her) either. بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
The wedding feasts of the Prophet ﷺ and his blessed wives varied from one occasion to another. A few of them are as follows:

(1) The wedding feast of the Mother of the Believers, Ḥaḍrat Khadījah al-Kubrā (may Allah be pleased with her):

Concerning Ḥaḍrat Khadījah's (may Allah be pleased with her) wedding feast only one report is found, in which it says that the Prophet ﷺ slaughtered one camel — and according to one statement, two camels.

The number who attended the feast:

No number is mentioned in it either; but on the face of it, if this report is sound, the number must have been large, since a camel is a large animal and so those who ate would also have been many.

قال في السيرة الحلبية: باب تزويجه ﷺ خديجة بنت خويلد رضي الله تعالى عنها أو لم عليها ﷺ نحر جزوراً وقيل جزورين. (السيرة الحلبية: ۱/۱۲۹، دار إحياء التراث العربي).

(2) The wedding feast of the Mother of the Believers, Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah (may Allah be pleased with her):

Concerning Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah's (may Allah be pleased with her) wedding feast two kinds of report are found. In the first kind it says that a jafnah was sent from the house of Ḥaḍrat Saʿd ibn ʿUbādah (may Allah be pleased with him); in the second kind a qadaḥ and milk are mentioned. There is no contradiction between the two, because a jafnah means a large bowl and a qadaḥ also means a bowl. It may be that one and the same thing is meant by both, or that both were sent.

The number who attended the feast:

Those who took part were the Prophet ﷺ, Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah (may Allah be pleased with her) and a few of her companions. See: (مسند احمد: ٤۲/۵۰٤، رقم: ۲۵۷٦۹) with the notes of Shaykh Shuʿayb, and (السیرۃ الحلبیۃ: ۲/۳٤۱), and (سبل الہدی و الرشاد: ۹/۵۵), and (شرح الزرقانی علی المواہب: ۳/۲۳۲) ۔

(3) The wedding feast of the Mother of the Believers, Ḥaḍrat Umm Salamah (may Allah be pleased with her):

A report in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī states that the Prophet ﷺ held the wedding feast of one of his honoured wives with two mudds of barley. Ḥāfiẓ Ibn Ḥajar (may Allah have mercy on him) says that in all likelihood this was the wedding feast of Ḥaḍrat Umm Salamah (may Allah be pleased with her), and in support of this he brings a report of Ibn Saʿd. There is a report in al-Ṭabarānī that at Umm Salamah's (may Allah be pleased with her) feast the Prophet ﷺ served dates and clarified butter, but Ibn Ḥajar (may Allah have mercy on him) judged this to be a slip on the part of the narrator.

Consider the evidence: (فتح الباری: ۹/۲۳۹), and (الطبقات لابن سعد: ۸/۹۲), and (سبل الہدی و الرشاد: ۱۱/۸۹، و۹/ ۵۵), and (مرقاۃ المفاتیح: ٦/۲۵۲، مکتبہ امدادیہ) ۔

(4) The wedding feast of the Mother of the Believers, Ḥaḍrat Zaynab bint Jaḥsh (may Allah be pleased with her):

Concerning Ḥaḍrat Zaynab bint Jaḥsh's (may Allah be pleased with her) wedding feast two kinds of report are found. One report says that the Prophet ﷺ slaughtered a goat and served the noble Companions meat and bread; another says that Ḥaḍrat Umm Sulaym (may Allah be pleased with her) sent a sweet dish made of various ingredients and that this was served to the noble Companions. There is no contradiction between the two: meat and bread were served first, and in the course of the meal Ḥaḍrat Anas (may Allah be pleased with him) brought the sweet dish, which was then served as well.

The number who attended the feast:

According to the statement of Ḥāfiẓ Ibn Ḥajar (may Allah have mercy on him) about a thousand people partook of the meal (Fatḥ al-Bārī). Ḥaḍrat Anas (may Allah be pleased with him) says that the Prophet sent him to invite people and he invited about three hundred (Muslim); and in the report of Ibn Saʿd they were 71 or 72, but Muḥammad ibn ʿĪsā al-ʿAbdarī in its chain is weak. It may be that, before Ḥaḍrat Anas (may Allah be pleased with him) invited the three hundred, other people had finished their meal and left. On that basis this does not conflict with the statement of Ibn Ḥajar (may Allah have mercy on him).

See the references: (مشکوٰۃ شریف، ص ۲۷۸), and (فتح الباری: ۹/ ۲۲۷، دار نشر الکتب الاسلامیہ), and (سبل الہدی و الرشاد: ۱۱/۲۰۲), and (طبقات ابن سعد: ۸/۱۰٤), and (مسلم شریف: ۱/٤٦۱), and (لسان المیزان: ۷/٤۲٦) ۔

(5) The Mother of the Believers, Ḥaḍrat Umm Ḥabībah (may Allah be pleased with her):

Ḥaḍrat Umm Ḥabībah's (may Allah be pleased with her) marriage to the Prophet ﷺ took place in Abyssinia. Some reports indicate that the Negus conducted the marriage, while other reports indicate that Ḥaḍrat ʿUthmān al-Ghanī (may Allah be pleased with him) conducted it. Then some reports indicate that the Negus provided the food for the wedding feast, while others indicate that Ḥaḍrat ʿUthmān al-Ghanī (may Allah be pleased with him) served tharīd.

The number who attended the feast:

It was served to the Companions who had migrated, so the number was somewhere near that figure. (ماخوذ از سبل الہدی و الرشاد: ۱۱/۱۹۵، و الاستیعاب: ٤/۱۸٤٤) ۔

Ḥaḍrat Muftī Kifāyatullāh says:

Nor need there be any difficulty over this — that if this was the food of the wedding feast, how was it given before the consummation? — because some scholars hold that the wedding feast may be held before the consummation too, even though in the view of the great body of scholars it comes after the consummation. In the case of Ḥaḍrat Umm Ḥabībah (may Allah be pleased with her) the reason for giving it beforehand is plain: the marriage took place in Abyssinia while the Prophet ﷺ was in Madīnah, so the consummation was far off. The Negus, or Ḥaḍrat ʿUthmān (may Allah be pleased with him), therefore carried out the sunnah of the wedding feast and did not think it fitting to delay. (کفایت المفتی: ۵/۱۵۷، دار الاشاعت) ۔

(6) The wedding feast of the Mother of the Believers, Ḥaḍrat Ṣafiyyah (may Allah be pleased with her):

Concerning Ḥaḍrat Ṣafiyyah's (may Allah be pleased with her) wedding feast it is mentioned in the two Ṣaḥīḥ collections that a sweet dish was served; one report of al-Bukhārī mentions tamr (dried dates), aqiṭ (cheese) and samn (clarified butter); and al-Tirmidhī and others mention sawīq (parched barley meal) and tamr (dried dates). But there is no contradiction in this, because dried dates, cheese, clarified butter and the like are mixed into the sweet dish, and sometimes parched barley meal is put in as well.

The number who attended the feast:

Ḥaḍrat Ṣafiyyah's (may Allah be pleased with her) wedding feast took place on the return from Khaybar, and at that time the number of Muslims was about 1,600. It therefore stands to reason that those who took part were also somewhere near that number.

See: (مشکوٰۃ شریف: ۱/۲۷۸), and (تحفۃ الاحوذی: ٤/۱٦۳، دار الفکر), and (مرقاۃ المفاتیح: ٦/۲۵٤، ط: امدادیہ), and (شرح الزرقانی علی المواہب: ۲/۲۳۱), and (سبل الہدی و الرشاد: ۱۱/۲۱۳), and (طبقات ابن سعد: ۸/۱۲۵، دار صادر بیروت) ۔

(7) The wedding feast of the Mother of the Believers, Ḥaḍrat Maymūnah (may Allah be pleased with her):

No explicit report or mention of Ḥaḍrat Maymūnah's (may Allah be pleased with her) wedding feast has been found. Ḥāfiẓ Ibn Ḥajar (may Allah have mercy on him), however, says that reason requires that he held a feast of more than one goat, since conditions at that time were easy.

The number who attended the feast:

Ḥaḍrat Maymūnah's (may Allah be pleased with her) wedding feast took place on the return from ʿUmrat al-Qaḍāʾ, and at that time the number of the noble Companions was about two thousand or a little more; on that basis the number of those taking part should also be near that.

See: (فتح الباری: ۹/۲۳۷)۔

Of the blessed wives, no mention has come to hand of the wedding feasts of Ḥaḍrat Sawdah (may Allah be pleased with her), Ḥaḍrat Zaynab bint Khuzaymah (may Allah be pleased with her), Ḥaḍrat Juwayriyah (may Allah be pleased with her) and Ḥaḍrat Ḥafṣah (may Allah be pleased with her).

What the wedding feast of the mistress of the women of Paradise, Ḥaḍrat Fāṭimah (may Allah be pleased with her), was like:

Ḥaḍrat ʿAlī (may Allah be pleased with him) held a wedding feast at which, with all simplicity, he served whatever was available. At the feast there were barley bread, dates, ḥarīrah, cheese and the meat of a ram.
