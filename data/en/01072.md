### TITLE
Eid when different groups form over the establishing of the crescent

### QUESTION
In Albania the Muslims split into different groups over the moon of Shawwal, as follows:

(1) One group followed Saudi Arabia and neither fasted nor prayed the tarawih, but celebrated Eid on the next day.

(2) One group followed the people of Britain and celebrated Eid one day after Saudi Arabia; yet nobody had seen the moon either in Britain or in Albania, and there is only one hour's difference between the two countries.

(3) The third group completed thirty fasts, since the moon had not been seen in Albania; these people therefore celebrated Eid two days after the first group and one day after the second.

What we wish to know is this: according to the rules of the sharia, which of these groups celebrated Eid correctly?

### ANSWER
The Eid of the first group was wrong on every count — in terms of the sharia, of customary practice and of calculation.

The second group did not see the moon, neither in their own city nor in Europe. So if a sound report of the moon reached them from Asia or from nearby cities and they celebrated Eid on that basis, it was correct; but if they followed Britain without a sound report of a sighting, their Eid was not correct.

The Eid of the third group is also correct, because either no sound report reached them, or, if one did reach them, they did not rely on it because it had not come to them in the proper manner; or they did not accept the testimony of distant lands; or they did not accept it for some other reason. In any case, what they did is correct.

Consider the noble hadith:

وقال رسول الله ﷺ: "صوموا لرؤيته وأفطروا لرؤيته، فإن غم عليكم فعدوا ثلاثين ثم أفطروا". (رواه الترمذي: ۱/۱٤۷، باب ما جاء لا تتقدموا الشهر بصوم).

Al-Fiqh al-Ḥanafī wa Adillatuhu states:

إذا كان بين القطرين قريب بحيث تتحد المطالع فلا يعتبر، وإن كانت بعيدة بحيث تختلف المطالع فيعتبر فلو صام أهل قطر ثلاثين يوماً برؤية، وأهل قطر آخر تسعة وعشرين يوماً برؤية، فعليهم قضاء يوم إن كان بين القطرين قرب بحيث تتحد المطالع، وإن كانت بعيدة بحيث تختلف، لا يلزم أحد القطرين حكم الآخر، فالقرب مثل سورية، والعراق، والبعد مثل الكويت والمغرب، وجاء عن ابن عباس وعائشة رضي الله عنه ما صوم كل جماعة يوم يصومون، وفطرهم يوم يفطرون. (الفقه الحنفي وأدلته: ۱/۳۸۹، قبول خبر الواحد في روية هلال رمضان، دمشق).

For further evidence, see the question "The ruling on the difference of horizons".
