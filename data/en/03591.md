### TITLE
The ruling on a marriage contract by means of a Skype call

### QUESTION
If a person contracts a marriage by means of a Skype call (in which the parties converse while seeing each other's faces), in which two witnesses hear the conversation of the contracting parties and also see their faces, will a marriage be contracted in such a case or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
For a marriage to be contracted it is necessary that the two contracting parties make the offer and acceptance in one and the same gathering before two witnesses, and that the two witnesses hear it. If the gathering is different, the marriage will not be contracted; and in a Skype call this condition is not met. Therefore a marriage will not be contracted by means of a Skype call. Yes — if the girl appoints someone at the boy's location as her agent, and the agent then contracts the marriage in the marriage gathering before two witnesses, the marriage will take place.

Consider what Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ states:

فأما إذا كان أحدهما غائباً لم ينعقد حتى لو قالت امرأة بحضرة شاهدين زوجت نفسي من فلان وهو غائب فبلغه الخبر فقال: قبلت، أو قال رجل بحضرة شاهدين تزوجت فلانة وهي غائبة فبلغها الخبر فقالت: زوجت نفسي منه لم يجز وإن كان القبول بحضرة شاهدين ذينك الشاهدين وهذا قول أبي حنيفة رحمه الله ومحمد رحمه الله. (بدائع الصنائع: ۲/۲۳۲، سعيد).

Kitāb al-Fatāwā records: Question: Is it correct to contract a marriage over the internet, a website, fax, e-mail, a telephone conference or a telegram, or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

Answer: In a marriage it is necessary that the offer and acceptance take place in one and the same gathering. In the forms mentioned in the question it is evident that the gathering of the one who speaks or who presents his statement in writing is separate, and the gathering of the addressee is separate. For this reason the offer and acceptance of a marriage by means of these media of communication is not correct. However, a person may be appointed as an agent for the offer and acceptance, and he may contract the marriage of the one who appointed him. (کتاب الفتاوی: ٤/۳۰٦، ط: زمزم) ۔

Aham Masāʾil jin men Ibtilā ʿĀm Hai mentions:

Ruling: If, by means of a live video call (Live Video Call) on Imo (Imo) or Skype (Skype), a boy and a girl make an offer and acceptance, and two witnesses also hear the voices of the boy and the girl, even then the marriage will not be valid; because in the Sharīʿah, for a marriage to be valid it is necessary, along with the gathering of offer and acceptance being one, that two witnesses be present in that gathering who hear the words of offer and acceptance with their own ears. Yes! If the boy or girl living abroad appoints someone as an agent, and then in the marriage gathering the agent of the other party accepts the offer of the one making the offer, and two Muslim witnesses are also present in that gathering who hear the offer and acceptance, then the marriage will be valid. (اہم مسائل جن میں ابتلا عام ہے: ۹/۲۰۳، ط: اکل کوا) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
