### TITLE
Qualities required in the trustee of a mosque or madrasah

### QUESTION
What sort of person should the trustee of a mosque or a madrasah be?

### ANSWER
Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

"الصالح للنظر من لم يسال الولاية للوقف وليس فيه فسق يعرف هكذا في فتح القدير وفي الإسعاف: لا يولي إلا أمين قادر بنفسه أو بنائبه ويستوي فيه الذكر والأنثى، ويشترط للصحة بلوغه وعقله كذا في البحر الرائق." (الفتاوى الهندية: ۲/٤۰۸).

The Fatāwā of Ibn Taymiyyah states:

"ولا يجوز تولية الفاسق مع إمكان تولية البر." (فتاوى ابن تيمية: ۲۳/۳٤۱).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

A man should be made trustee who is trustworthy (not dishonest) and religious (not irreligious), and who has both the ability to manage the endowment and an interest in doing so. To appoint an openly sinful, irreligious man as trustee in the first place is a sin. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۲/۲۸۳) ۔

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

The trustee of a mosque should be a scholar who acts upon his knowledge. If he is not a scholar, he must certainly at least be religious and honest. It is impermissible to make an openly sinful man who is not a scholar the trustee. Allah Most High says:

﴿إِنَّمَا يَعْمُرُ مَسَاجِدَ اللَّهِ مَنْ آمَنَ بِاللَّهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الْآخِرِ﴾

Commenting on this verse, Mawlānā Abū al-Kalām Āzād writes: He has also made this truth plain, that the right to the trusteeship of Allah's house of worship belongs to the God-fearing Muslim, and that such people alone can keep it alive. From this it becomes clear that a sinful and corrupt man cannot be the trustee of mosques, because nothing fitting remains between the two; rather, two contradictory things would be brought together — mosques are places for the worship of God, while the trustee shrinks away from the worship of God. ترجمان القرآن (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۳/۱٦٤، ۱٦٦) ۔

In sum, the trustee should be a scholar who acts upon his knowledge. If such a man is not to be had, then the trustee should be the one in whom these qualities are most fully found: regular in fasting and prayer, trustworthy, familiar with the rules of endowments, of good character, compassionate, fair-minded, a lover of learning, and respectful towards the people of learning.
