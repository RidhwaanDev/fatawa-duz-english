### TITLE
Liability for breaking something placed in front of a worshipper

### QUESTION
A man put his glasses down in front of him and began his prayer. Someone walked past in front of him and broke the expensive glasses with his foot. Is he liable to pay for them or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, if the worshipper was engaged in prayer and the man passed within the area of his prostration and broke them as he went by, compensation is due, because the man who passed committed a transgression: the sharia forbids passing within the area of prostration. But if the worshipper was not engaged in prayer, there should be no compensation, because, as the man was not praying, the other had the right to pass.

Sharḥ al-Qawāʿid al-Fiqhiyyah states:

ما لو تلف بمروره بالطريق العام شيء أو أتلف دابته، بالطريق العام شيئا، بيدها أو فمها وهو راكبها أو سائقها أو قائدها فيضمن لأن مروره وإن كان مباحا لكنه مقيد بشرط السلامة. (شرح قواعد الفقهية، ص ٤۵۰).
