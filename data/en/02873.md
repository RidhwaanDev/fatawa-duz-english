### TITLE
Taking pictures for dentistry

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to use a digital image for dentistry or not? A computer digital image (Computer digital image) means that a picture is taken of the patient's teeth and the doctor tries out various methods of treatment to see how much benefit the patient will gain; the patient too can see what benefit there is in the treatment and what the shape will be before and after treatment. Will this be allowed or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
The disagreement of the scholars about the picture of a digital camera has already been given above, along with the evidence. Even those who hold it impermissible, however, allow it where there is necessity or need. See what Takmilat Fatḥ al-Mulhim states:

هذا هو حكم الصورة في الأصل، أما اتخاذ الصورة الشمسية للضرورة أو الحاجة كحاجتها في جواز السفر، وفي التأشيرة، وفي البطاقات الشخصية، أو في مواضع يحتاج فيها إلى معرفة هوية المرء، فينبغي أن يكون مرخصاً فيه، فإن الفقهاء استثنوا مواضع الضرورة من الحرمة، قال الإمام محمد رحمه الله في السير الكبير: وإن تحققت الحاجة له إلى استعمال السلاح الذي فيه تمثال فلا بأس باستعماله" وأعقبه السرخسي رحمه الله في شرحه (۲/۲۷۸) بقوله: لان مواضع الضرورة مستثناة من الحرمة

الخ. (تكملة فتح الملهم: ٤/۱٦٤).

Ḥalāl o Ḥarām states:

(6) Where there is necessity — for a passport, an identity card, a bus or railway pass, the keeping of photographs for the identification of criminals, or under some major national interest — taking a photograph will be permissible, since difficulties bring about ease in the rulings of the sacred law:

المشقة تجلب التيسير ۔ (حلال و حرام، ص۲۲۹) ۔ (و کذا فی جدید فقہی مسائل: ۱/۳۵۸) ۔

Ḥaḍrat Mufti Taqī Ṣāḥib writes in Taqrīr-e Tirmidhī in the following words:

The ruling on pictures in situations of need:

One thing that emerges from this disagreement is that the permissibility or otherwise of the matter has become a question open to reasoned judgement, on two counts. The first is that Imam Mālik (may Allah have mercy on him) differed on the subject. The second is that on the camera picture there exists the ruling of ʿAllāmah Bakhīt (may Allah have mercy on him). Although in our view that ruling is not correct, it remains the case that on a new matter there exists the statement of a scrupulous scholar, and for that reason the question has become one open to reasoned judgement; and in a question open to reasoned judgement, scope arises at a time of general need. Wherever, then, there is a general need — as with a passport or an identity card, or in any situation in which a person has to establish his identity, where matters cannot proceed without identification and identification is not possible without a picture — its use will become permissible on those occasions. Its use where there is no such need is not permissible, and it is necessary to abstain from it. (تقریر ترمذی، جلدِ دوم، ص ۳۵۰) ۔

In sum, taking a picture for dentistry is permissible. A picture of the face for advertisement is impermissible; only a picture of the teeth is permissible. Some physicians have stated that if no picture is taken, a patient sometimes brings a case against the doctor claiming that he did not treat him properly. Moreover, in order to explain the usefulness of the method of treatment a doctor has adopted, the picture is presented before students of medicine. If that is so, then on account of this need there is scope for taking the photograph.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
