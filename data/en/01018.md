### TITLE
Deducting the dower when paying zakat

### QUESTION
If a man owes his wife her dower and the amount is one hundred thousand rand, is that hundred thousand deducted when he pays his zakat, or not? Or must he pay zakat on the whole sum?

### ANSWER
If the husband has no intention of paying the dower, or if the wife by custom does not ask for it, zakat must be paid on the whole sum. But if he does intend to pay it, he pays zakat on what is left after deducting the amount of the dower.

Consider what al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

وذكر البزدوي في شرح الجامع الكبير: قال مشايخنا رحمه الله: في رجل عليه مهر مؤجل لا مرأته وهو لا يريد أدائه لا يجعل مانعاً من الزكاة لعدم المطالبة في العادة وأنه حسن أيضاً هكذا في جواهر الفتاوى. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱۷۳).

Even though the dower is deferred, if the man is concerned to pay it now, zakat is not due on that amount; otherwise it is due. (Imdād al-Muftīn, vol. 2, p. 451; Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah 9: 319, chapter-arranged edition; Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 1/221; Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm: 6/46; Imdād al-Aḥkām: 2/25).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
