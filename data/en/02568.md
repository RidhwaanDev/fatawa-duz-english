### TITLE
Aborting a malformed foetus

### QUESTION
If a doctor says that the child in a woman's womb is malformed, or is not capable of surviving, may it be removed by an operation or not?

### ANSWER
There are several cases of aborting a malformed foetus. The details of when it is permissible and when it is not may be seen in the following decision of the Fiqh Academy:

The decision of the Islamic Fiqh Academy of Makkah Mukarramah:

At its session, after consideration and discussion of the views of expert doctors and of the members on this subject, the following decision was taken by a majority:

If the pregnancy is of one hundred and twenty (120) days, aborting it is not permissible, even if it is established by medical diagnosis that the child is malformed. If, however, it is established by the report of a committee of expert and trustworthy doctors that the continuation of the pregnancy is certainly dangerous to the mother's life, then in such a case aborting the child is permissible in order to remove the greater harm, whether the child is malformed or not.

If one hundred and twenty days have not passed over the pregnancy, and it is established — on the basis of the report of a committee of expert and trustworthy doctors and of technical investigation by means of experimental methods and instruments — that the child is malformed to so dangerous a degree that it cannot be treated, and that if it remains and is born at its due time its life will be a burden and a source of pain both to it and to the members of the household, then in such a case aborting it is permissible at the parents' request. Together with this decision, the session urges doctors and parents to keep the fear of Allah and due caution before them in this matter. (عصر حاضر کے پیچیدہ مسائل کا شرعی حل، ص ۲٤۵)۔
