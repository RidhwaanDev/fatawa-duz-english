### TITLE
A man who shaves his beard leading the tarawih prayer

### QUESTION
What is the ruling on a man who shaves his beard leading the tarawih prayer?

### ANSWER
In the eyes of the sharia, a man who shaves his beard is a sinner, and it is strictly disliked for a sinner to lead the prayer. Tarawih should be prayed behind a righteous, God-fearing imam. If, however, no such imam is available and there is no power to remove him, the congregation should not be abandoned; one should pray behind him.
