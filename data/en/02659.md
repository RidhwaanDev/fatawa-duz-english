### TITLE
A woman addressing a gathering

### QUESTION
May a woman deliver a speech in a gathering of men while observing the veil?

### ANSWER
In the detailed fatwa above it was explained clearly that, according to the sounder view, a woman's voice is not part of what must be kept covered; but where there is a fear of temptation she will be stopped from letting it be heard. Since in a gathering of men there is a fear of temptation, a woman should not deliver a speech in such a gathering. Yes, if the gathering is of women only, then preaching and giving advice are permitted.

قال في مجمع الأنهر: إن المرأة إن رفعت صوتها فقد باشرت منكراً؛ لأن صوتها عورة

أقول: وفيه كلام؛ لأن صوتها مطلقاً ليس بعورة، وإلا يستلزم أن يكره تكلمها مع الأجنبي وليس كذلك، بل يكره رفع صوتها. (مجمع الأنهر، باب الاذان).
قال في حاشية البحر الرائق: إنا إذا قلنا صوت المرأة عورة إنا نريد بذلك كلامها؛ لأن ذلك ليس بصحيح فإنا نجيز الكلام مع النساء الأجانب ومحاورتهن عند الحاجة إلى ذلك ولا نجيز لهن رفع أصواتهن ولا تمطيطها ولا تليينها وتقطيعها لما في ذلك من استمالة الرجال إليهن وتحريك الشهوات منهم ومن هذا لم يجز أن تؤذن المرأة. (منحة الخالق: ۱/۲۷۰، باب شروط الصلاة، كوئته). وينظر: (حاشية الطحطاوي على مراقي الفلاح، ص ۲٤۲، و۵٤۰، ط: قديمي، والبحر الرائق: ۱/۲۷۰، ط: كوئته).

Hazrat Mufti Kifāyatullāh Sahib (may Allah have mercy on him) permitted a woman to speak even in a gathering of men. See what Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

The sacred sharia of Islam does not forbid women any Islamic service that is suited to them. So long as the veil is safeguarded, a woman may deliver a speech in a gathering of men. (کفایت المفتی: ۲/٦۹، ط: دار الاشاعت، کراچی).

But in Hazrat Mufti Sahib's day modesty and shame will still have prevailed. In our time people do not even know what modesty and shame are; on the contrary, a flood of nakedness and indecency is in full spate.

Hazrat Maulānā Muhammad Yūsuf Ludhyānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

To deliver a speech unveiled before non-mahrams is not permissible; it is unlawful. At a time of need, speaking from behind the veil is permissible, but the tone of voice should be firm and rough, so that no attraction towards the woman arises in the other person. Nowadays at public functions men and women address the audience together; this is an evil innovation of the new age of ignorance. (آپ کے مسائل اور ان کا حل: ۸/۹۲، طبع جدید).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

Question: What is the position if a woman speaker delivers a speech in a gathering of men while wearing a cloak?

الجواب: The learned scholars have written that even a woman's going to the mosque to pray along with men is forbidden, whether she goes wearing a cloak or without one, because there is much corruption and temptation in it. So it is likewise forbidden for a woman to go into a gathering of men and deliver a speech (unless there is a pressing need). (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۹/۱۹٤، جامعہ فاروقیہ).

In another place it is written:

If the gathering is of women only and the voice does not reach non-mahrams, then it is correct for a woman to preach and to say what she has to say. (فتاویٰ محمودیہ: ۱۹/۱۹٦، جامعہ فاروقیہ).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
