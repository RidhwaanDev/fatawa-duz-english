### TITLE
The ruling on using a machine to fill a grave with earth

### QUESTION
In our area, when someone passes away, filling in the grave and packing it with earth becomes difficult because the ground is rocky. May we fill the grave with a machine for the sake of ease?

### ANSWER
At a time of hardship, for the sake of ease and while maintaining the honour and respect due to the deceased, there is no objection to filling the grave with a machine. When people fill the grave by hand they also use implements such as a spade and the like, and implements are likewise used when digging it. Moreover, filling it by hand involves physical hardship while filling it by machine involves financial expense. In earlier times people drew water from a well by hand, whereas nowadays machines are used to draw water for wuḍūʾ, bathing and so on; just as a machine may be used there, so may it be used for filling a grave. Allah Most High created these implements for our ease, and there is no objection to using them. By way of corroboration and supporting evidence, consider the following Qurʾānic verses, hadiths and general legal maxims:

قال الله تعالى: ﴿يريد الله بكم اليسر ولا يريد بكم العسر﴾، وقال تعالى: ﴿ما جعل عليكم في الدين من حرج﴾. وقال رسول الله ﷺ: إن الله إنما أراد بهذه الأمة اليسر ولا يريد بهم العسر. وقال: إن خير دينكم أيسره إن خير دينكم أيسره. وقال أيها الناس إن دين الله يسر ثلاثاً. وقال: يسروا ولا تعسروا. (الدر المنثور).

وفي القواعد الفقهية: الحرج مدفوع، والمشقة تجلب التيسير.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
