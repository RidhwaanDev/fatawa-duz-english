### TITLE
Delivering an address of admonition at a marriage gathering

### QUESTION
Is it correct for a scholar to deliver an address of exhortation and advice at a marriage gathering, or not? Or is it an innovation?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, if a person does not regard it as a sunnah or as something binding, but rather as something beneficial for the occasion, then there is no harm in it. Even those Salafīs who regard newly invented practices as deadly poison declare this permissible.

Consider what Fatāwā ʿUlamāʾ al-Balad al-Ḥarām states:

لا مانع من القاء محاضرة نافعة على الحاضرين في أمرهم بالمعروف ونهيهم عن المنكر في حفل الزواج. (فتاوى علماء البلد الحرام: ۱۳۸۵، الباب الرابع عشر، النكاح).

Kitāb al-Fatāwā states:

The marriage sermon should not be delivered in Urdu. It is better that the meaning of the marriage sermon first be explained in Urdu and then the sermon itself be delivered in Arabic. In this way, on the one hand, people will understand the rulings of marriage in their own language and the real purpose of the sermon will be achieved; and the benefit of delivering the sermon in Arabic is that the sermon will be delivered in exactly the blessed words of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, and it is obvious that this is superior and preferable. (کتاب الفتاوی: ٤/۳۰۰)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
