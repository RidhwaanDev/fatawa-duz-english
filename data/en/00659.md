### TITLE
Making up missed tarāwīḥ in congregation

### QUESTION
Two rakʿahs of tarāwīḥ became invalid, so on the following night the whole congregation prayed 22 rakʿahs. Is it correct to make them up in this way? Also, will the recitation made in the two extra rakʿahs prayed with the intention of making up the missed ones be counted or not?

### ANSWER
When tarāwīḥ becomes invalid, making it up in congregation the next day is disliked. The recitation made in it will also not count — that is, it will not be included in the completion of the Qur'an. Consider what Qāḍī Khān states:

وإن تذكر في الليل أنه فسد عليهم شفع من الليلة الماضية فأراد القضاء بنية التراويح يكره لأنه زيادة على التراويح بنية التراويح. (فتاوى قاضيخان على هامش الهندية: ۱/۲۳٦، فصل في وقت التراويح)

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

If one recalls that a pair of rakʿahs of tarāwīḥ became invalid the night before, making it up in congregation with the intention of tarāwīḥ is likewise disliked. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۷/۲۸٦، باب التراویح، جامعہ فاروقیہ)

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
