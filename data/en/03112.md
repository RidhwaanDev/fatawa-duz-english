### TITLE
Some questions concerning the Chishtī and Naqshbandī orders

### QUESTION
(1) Which books are useful for gaining knowledge of the Naqshbandī order and of the remaining orders? (2) What are the essential litanies of the orders? (3) Who are the successors of Murshid-i ʿĀlam? (4) What is the way of recognising a genuine spiritual guide from a false one? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
For gaining knowledge of the orders, two books by Ḥaḍrat Ḥājī Imdād Allāh Muhājir Makkī (may Allah have mercy on him) are very useful: (1) Ḍiyāʾ al-Qulūb; (2) the book Irshād-i Murshid. Both of these books have been gathered together in Kulliyāt-i Imdādiyyah.

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ashraf ʿAlī Thānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) writes:

All the orders of sainthood that there are reach the Prophet ﷺ by way of the People of the House. Thus in one chain of the Naqshbandīs there is Ḥaḍrat Jaʿfar al-Ṣādiq, and in another Ḥaḍrat ʿAlī (may Allah be pleased with him), Ḥaḍrat Ḥusayn (may Allah be pleased with him), Ḥaḍrat Zayn al-ʿĀbidīn, Ḥaḍrat Muḥammad al-Bāqir, Ḥaḍrat Mūsā al-Kāẓim and Ḥaḍrat ʿAlī ibn Mūsā. In the Qādirī order there are Ḥaḍrat Ḥasan (may Allah be pleased with him), Ḥaḍrat Ḥasan al-Muthannā and Ḥaḍrat Sayyid ʿAbd Allāh al-Maḥḍ; in the Chishtī order, Ḥaḍrat ʿAlī (may Allah be pleased with him); and in the Suhrawardī order, Ḥaḍrat ʿAlī Mūsā al-Riḍā. So all these chains belong to the People of the House.

فهذه السلاسل كشجرة طيبة أصلها ثابت وفرعها في السماء تؤتي أكلها كل حين بإذن ربها. (امداد الفتاوی: ٦/۱۳۹)۔

The answer to (2):

The litanies of the Naqshbandī order:

The formula of seeking forgiveness, one hundred times daily:

أستغفر الله تعالى ربي من كل ذنب وأتوب إليه.

The blessings upon the Prophet, one hundred times daily:

اللهم صلي على سيدنا محمد وعلي آل سيدنا محمد وبارك وسلم عليه.

The formula of glorification, one hundred times daily:

سبحان الله والحمد لله ولا إله إلا الله والله أكبر.

And at the end, just once:

ولا حول ولا قوة إلا بالله العلي العظيم.

These should be recited regularly. (انوارِ شمسیہ، ص ۱۰٦)۔

The litanies of the Qādirī order:

Seeking forgiveness, one hundred times daily:

أستغفر الله تعالى ربي من كل ذنب وأتوب إليه.

The blessings upon the Prophet, one hundred times daily:

اللهم صل على سيدنا محمد وآله وعترته بعدد كل معلوم لك.

Negation and affirmation, one hundred times daily (the dhikr of the human realm):

لا إله إلا الله.

Affirmation alone, one hundred times daily (the dhikr of the realm of dominion):

إلا الله.

Meditation of the heart, one hundred times daily (the dhikr of the heart):

الله.

The Name of the Essence upon the tongue, one hundred times daily (the dhikr of the realm of might):

الله، الله.

The Name of the Essence upon the tongue, one hundred times daily (the dhikr of the realm of divinity):

هو، هو.

The Name of the Essence upon the tongue, one hundred times daily (the compound dhikr, the dhikr of ascent):

الله هو، الله هو.

The Name of the Essence upon the tongue, one hundred times daily (the uncompounded dhikr, the dhikr of descent):

هو الله، هو الله.

The dhikr of the tongue, one hundred times daily (the dhikr of humble entreaty):

أنت الهادي أنت الحق ليس الهادي إلا هو. (انوارِ شمسیہ، ص 107)۔

The litanies of the Chishtī order:

Concerning the audible dhikr of negation and affirmation and of the Name of the Essence, together with those twelve rosaries which are the practice of the Chishtī masters.

The method of performing the dhikr of these twelve rosaries is that twelve rakʿahs of tahajjud should be prayed with six salutations, reciting Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ three times in each rakʿah; and with the utmost humility and submission one should raise the hands three times, or five, or seven, and recite:

اللهم طهر قلبي. الخ.

and after repentance and seeking forgiveness one should recite:

أستغفر الله تعالى ربي من كل ذنب وأتوب إليه

twenty-one times, and then recite the blessings upon the Prophet

three times in the manner of ascending and descending. Then one should sit cross-legged, grip the vein firmly with the big toe of the right foot and the toe beside it, place both hands upon the knees, and face the qiblah. While saying lā ilāha one should raise the index finger, and while saying illā Allāh one should lower it, keeping oneself still and composed; and with sincerity of intention and in a pleasing voice, after saying aʿūdhu and bismillāh, one should recite:

لا إله إلا الله محمد رسول الله

three times, and the testification of faith once.

Apart from this there are a great many details; to learn them, Ḍiyāʾ al-Qulūb (۱۵) should be studied.

The answer to (3): the successor of Mawlānā Ghulām Ḥabīb Naqshbandī is neither Mawlānā Dhū al-Fiqār nor Mawlānā Ismāʿīl Wāḍī; rather his successor is his own son, who resides in Chakwāl.

The answer to (4): it is the guide who follows the Sunnah and who is devoting himself to the teachings of his own shaykh.

The litanies mentioned above cannot be learnt from something written down; they can be acquired only through the instruction of a perfect spiritual guide. Guides of every one of these orders are to be found in every part of the world. Most of our elders of Deoband have their connection with the Chishtī order.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
