### TITLE
Holding an annual gathering at madrasahs

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to hold an annual gathering at madrasahs or maktabs or not? And what is the ruling on giving prizes and the like to the students at it?

### ANSWER
It is permissible to hold an annual gathering at madrasahs or maktabs, but on condition that nothing takes place at it that is contrary to the sharia, that men and women do not mix, and that girls who have reached puberty or are close to it do not recite poems and the like in front of men. If the limits of the sharia are observed, this practice is beneficial: it creates enthusiasm in the children, it makes their parents keen to bring their children towards the faith, and it encourages the children. This is also the purpose of the annual gatherings of the madrasahs which have been held in our madrasahs down to the present day. The completion of Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī and similar occasions are likewise held at madrasahs in order to bring people closer to the faith. In short, at such a gathering, besides the fine performance of the children, matters of the faith will also reach people's ears.

(2) As for prizes: if the children are not given anything that is an image — that is, it does not carry a picture of a living being — then there is no harm in giving prizes.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
