### TITLE
Visiting the grave of an unbeliever

### QUESTION
In the light of the two hadiths given below, is it permissible to visit the grave of an unbeliever or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

عن سليمان بن بريدة عن أبيه، قال: زار النبي ﷺ قبر أمه، في ألف مقنع، فلم ير باكياً أكثر من يومئذٍ. هذا حديث صحيح على شرط الشيخين، ولم يخرجاه. (المستدرك على الصحيحين: ۱/۳۷٤/۱۳۸۹).

عن أبي هريرة رضي الله عنه قال: زار رسول الله ﷺ قبر أمه، فبكى وأبكى من حوله، ثم قال: استأذنت ربي أن أزور قبرها فأذن لي، واستأذنته أن أستغفر لها فلم يؤذن لي فزوروا القبور فإنها تذكر الموت. وهذا حديث صحيح على شرط مسلم ولم يخرجاه. (المستدرك للحاكم: ۱/۳۷٤/۱۳۹۰، وأخرجه مسلم، رقم: ۹۷٦).

### ANSWER
If the grave is visited in order to honour and respect the unbeliever, this is impermissible. Visiting it with the intention of taking a lesson is permissible, but if some religious harm arises from it one should not go. By harm is meant that if the visit gives rise to any suspicion of honouring and respecting the unbeliever, it is impermissible.

The two reports quoted above do not constitute a complete proof, because there is much difference of opinion regarding the faith of the parents of the Prophet ﷺ, and the scholars have written various treatises on the subject. Even if it were established that they did not have faith, the report still contains the words

فإنها تذكر الموت

— that is, visiting graves refreshes the remembrance of death — and visiting for that purpose is permissible. In Bayān al-Qurʾān, Ḥaḍrat Thānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) has written that it is permissible, unless some religious harm is involved. Visiting out of honour and esteem for an unbeliever, however, is not permissible. See what Tafsīr Maẓharī states:

ولا تصل: المراد بالصلاة الدعاء والاستغفار للميت فيشمل صلاة الجنازة أيضاً لأنها مشتملة على الدعاء والاستغفار

مات أبداً، ولا تقم علي قبره للدفن أو للزيارة. (التفسير المظهري: ٤/۲۷٦).

Maʿārif al-Qurʾān records:

This verse also establishes that it is unlawful to stand at the grave of an unbeliever, or to go to visit it, in order to honour and esteem him. If it is in order to take a lesson, or on account of some necessity, that is not contrary to this. (معارف القرآن: ٤/٤۳۷، ادارۃ المعارف) ۔

In Bayān al-Qurʾān, Ḥaḍrat Thānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) has written under this same noble verse:

The funeral prayer over an unbeliever and seeking forgiveness for him are not permissible. What is meant by standing at the grave is standing that is by way of honouring him, whether for the purpose of visiting or for the purpose of burial. If it is in order to take a lesson and to remember death, or on account of a need — as is stated in al-Hidāyah, that if a person's relative dies and the arrangements for him fall upon him, he should bathe and shroud him without observing the sunnah method and then bury him — then standing at the grave in this way is correct. But if there is some religious harm in visiting for the purpose of taking a lesson, it is not permissible. فقط ۔ (بیان القرآن: ٤/۱۳۱، تاج پبلشرز، دہلی) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
