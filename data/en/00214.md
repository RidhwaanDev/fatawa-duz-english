### TITLE
Answers to some questions about daʿwah and tablīgh

### QUESTION
(1) Where is it established from the Qur'an and hadith that going out with a jamāʿat is necessary?

(2) Where is it established that it is necessary for women to go out with a jamāʿat?

(3) Where is going out for a chillah (forty days) and for four months established from?

(4) Why do we not give daʿwah to non-Muslims?

(5) Why are large gatherings and assemblies held, and where is that established from?

(6) Where is it established that one should invite someone to prayer?

(7) If someone calls the Tablīghī Jamāʿat people of innovation, what is the ruling concerning him?

### ANSWER
(1) The hadiths contain many reports of the Prophet ﷺ going out for tablīgh. It was for tablīgh that he was pelted with stones in Ṭāʾif and that he went out into the markets of Makkah al-Muʿaẓẓamah. Besides this, the noble Companions also went out frequently to convey the religion.

(2) Women may go out for tablīgh with proper veiling and in the company of a maḥram. It is obvious that men cannot convey the message among women as effectively as women can. ʿĀʾishah (may Allah be pleased with her) and the other pure wives of the Prophet ﷺ were themselves teachers and conveyers of the message.

(3) Going out for a chillah (forty days) or for four months is not necessary; you may go out for a year as well. However, Allah has placed this blessing in the chillah, that in it what is raw ripens and reaches completion. The forty days of worship of Mūsā (peace be upon him), and his receiving the Torah afterwards, is itself mentioned in the Qur'an.

(4) There is greater hope of acceptance among Muslims, and that is why tablīgh is being done among Muslims. Moreover, Muslims should first make themselves a model of the Prophet ﷺ; after that, tablīgh among the disbelievers will be effective. And those who wish to do tablīgh among the disbelievers should gladly take companions along and do so — but they should not stop anyone from so important a work as tablīgh among the Muslims.

(5) The hadiths record the noble Companions going out together in a group for teaching and for conveying the religion.

(6) It is established from the noble Companions.

(7) To call the Tablīghī Jamāʿat an innovation is a sinful thing to say. Such a person should repent.
