### TITLE
The ruling on making a cake in the image of a living creature

### QUESTION
A woman has received an order from a church to make a cake in the shape of a duck, on which a passage from the Bible is also to be written. Is it correct to accept such an order and to make such a cake, or not? Likewise, is it permissible to make cakes bearing pictures for children? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the pure Sharīʿah, making the picture of a living creature and taking it up as a profession is forbidden and impermissible. On this basis it is not correct to make a cake in the shape of a duck, nor is it correct to accept such an order. Yes — if the head is not made, then it does not come under the ruling of a picture. Consider what is in Fatāwā Shāmī:

فظاهر كلام النووي في شرح مسلم الإجماع على تحريم تصوير الحيوان وقال: وسواء صنعه لما يمتهن أو لغيره فصنعته حرام بكل حال لأن فيه مضاهاة لخلق الله تعالى وسواء كان في ثوب أو بساط أو درهم وإناء وحائط وغيرها. (فتاوى الشامي: ۱/٦٤۷، سعيد).

It is recorded in Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ:

فإن كانت مقطوعة الرؤوس فلا بأس بالصلاة فيه لأنها بالقطع خرجت من أن تكون تماثيل وألحقت بالنقوش والدليل عليه ما روي أن رسول الله ﷺ أهدي إليه ترس فيه تماثيل طير فأصبحوا وقد محي وجهه. (بدائع الصنائع: ۱/۱۱۵، ۱۱٦، سعيد).

(وكذا في البحر الرائق: ۲/۳۰، كوئته، وشرح معاني الآثار: ۲/٦٦۳).

It is in Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah:

Pictures of living creatures are forbidden in every form — in stone, in clay, in sweets, in toys; it is necessary for Muslims to keep away from them. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 19/474, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

It is recorded in another place in Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: If the head of the peacock is not made, then it is permitted in the Sharīʿah. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 19/472).

It is mentioned in Jawāhir al-Fiqh:

That defective image which has no head may lawfully be made, whether it is a picture of the hands and feet, or of the eye or nose or other limbs alone, or of the whole body apart from the head. (Jawāhir al-Fiqh: 7/253).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
