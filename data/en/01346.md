### TITLE
Birth control by means of an operation

### QUESTION
A woman has reached the age of forty and has six children. Now both husband and wife wish to have no more children on account of her advanced age. Can this serve as a valid excuse, and will permission be given to stop having children by means of an operation or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, stopping the birth of children by means of an operation is by no means permissible. Both of them should exercise patience; and if that is not possible, they may use medicines — for although this too is disliked, it is not unlawful.

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

The fourth form of birth control is sterilisation, that is, an operation by which the reproductive capacity is permanently destroyed and the ability to procreate no longer remains… In the light of the Qur'ān and the hadith it is a non-Islamic procedure, and from the practice of the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them), as well as from the explicit statements of the jurists, it is known that the experts in Islamic law are agreed that it is not permissible. (جدید فقہی مسائل: ۱۳۷ ۔ ۱٤۳، نس بندی)۔

For further detail, see: Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 8/347–353, "Birth control and abortion"; and Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 137–143, "Sterilisation".
