### TITLE
Saying "أنت طالق" three times out of fear and without knowing its effect

### QUESTION
A new Muslim's father-in-law got three divorces out of him in English. The new Muslim did not know what pronouncing three would mean; out of sheer fear he uttered the words without any intention. So how many divorces took effect by these words:

"أنت طالق، أنت طالق، أنت طالق"

### ANSWER
In the case described only one revocable divorce took effect, because he did not mean the later two utterances as fresh divorces; he merely repeated the words because he did not know the difference between one and three. He will therefore be believed as a matter of conscience. If, however, the wife brings the case before a judge, the judge will rule that three divorces took place.

Tabyīn al-Ḥaqāʾiq states:

إذا قال: أنت طالق، طالق، طالق، وقال: إنما أردت به التكرار صدق ديانة لا قضاءً فإن القاضي مأمور باتباع الظاهر والله يتولى السرائر. (تبيين الحقائق: ۲/۲۱۸).

Al-Ashbāh wa al-Naẓāʾir states:

ولو كرر لفظ الطلاق فإن قصد الاستئناف وقع الكل أو التأكيد فواحدة ديانة والكل قضاءً وكذا إذا أطلق. (الأشباه والنظائر: ۱/۱۷۸).
