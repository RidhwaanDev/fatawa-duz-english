### TITLE
The ruling on a woman's ṣalāh if her throat remains uncovered

### QUESTION
A woman prayed and during the ṣalāh her throat was uncovered — there was no scarf or cloth over it — while the rest of her body was covered. Did this woman's ṣalāh count or not? That is, is covering the throat obligatory or not?

### ANSWER
A woman's entire body falls within the ʿawrah (the area that must be covered), and therefore covering it in ṣalāh is obligatory. Only the face, the two palms and the two feet — the covering of these limbs is not necessary. Accordingly, if she prayed leaving her throat uncovered, the ṣalāh did not count, and repeating it will be necessary. See Imdād al-Fattāḥ, in which it is recorded:

ولا بد لصحة الصلاة من سبعة وعشرين شيئاً

وستر العورة

وجميع بدن الحرة عورة إلا وجهها وكفيها وقدميها. (إمداد الفتاح، ص ۲۲٦).

ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) has mentioned the neck among the limbs that must be covered. See:

أعضاء عورة الرجل ثمانية

وفي الأمة ثمانية أيضاً

وفي الحرة هذه الثمانية ويزاد فيها ستة عشر

العنق. (فتاوى الشامي: ۱/٤۰۹، سعيد).

ʿUnq includes both the throat and the neck. ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him), while discussing slaughter by choice, has written that the word ḥalq is sometimes used for ʿunq, and that between the two there is the relationship of part to whole — that is, the ʿunq is the whole and the ḥalq is a part of it. See the text:

وذكاة الاختيارية ذبح بين الحلق واللبة، الحلق في الأصل الحلقوم كما في القاموس: أي من العقدة إلى مبدء الصدر، وكلام التحفة والكافي وغيرهما يدل على أن الحلق يستعمل في العنق بعلاقة الجزئية، فالمعنى بين مبدء الحلق؛ أي أصل العنق. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۲۹٤، سعيد).

ʿAllāmah al-Zaylaʿī (may Allah have mercy on him), in defining naḥr and dhabḥ, has written that naḥr takes place in the lower part of the ʿunq and dhabḥ in the upper part of the ʿunq — and it is a self-evident matter that both naḥr and dhabḥ take place in the ḥalq. From this it is known that the ʿunq includes the ḥalq.

See the text:

والنحر قطع العروق في أسفل العنق عند الصدر والذبح قطع العروق في أعلى العنق تحت اللحيين. (تبيين الحقائق: ۵/۲۹۳، إمداديه).

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh states: For free women the whole body apart from five limbs (the face, the two palms and the two feet) is ʿawrah, and these are thirty limbs: (5) the neck — the throat is also included in it. (ʿUmdat al-Fiqh: 2/54).

Generally, when women wear a burqaʿ or a scarf they cover the throat as well, so there is no difficulty in this either.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
