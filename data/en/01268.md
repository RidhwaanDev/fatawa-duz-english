### TITLE
Performing a marriage without witnesses

### QUESTION
In a certain city there are only two Muslims, one man and one woman, and the nearest other Muslims live about 2,500 kilometres away. How are these two to marry, when no witness is available?

### ANSWER
In the case in question a marriage without witnesses is not permissible or valid. They may, however, do this: appoint an agent in another country by telephone, and the agent may then perform the marriage on behalf of the two of them in a marriage gathering, in the presence of witnesses, and inform them of it. In this case one person may act as agent for both sides; and if each has a separate agent, that is valid too.

Another way would be for the two of them to travel to a country of Muslims and marry there in a gathering of Muslims.

Al-Fatāwā al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah states:

أجمع أصحابنا أن الواحد يصلح وكيلاً في النكاح من الجانبين. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۲۹۹)

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

ويتولى طرفي النكاح واحد بإيجاب يقوم مقام القبول في خمس صور كأن كان ولياً أو وكيلاً من الجانبين، وفي الشامي: قوله ولياً أو وكيلاً من الجانبين كزوجت ابني بنت أخي أو زوجت موكلي فلا ناً أو موكلتي فلانة، قال ط: ويكفي شاهدان على وكالته، ووكالتها وعلي العقد لأن الشاهد يتحمل الشهادات العديدة، وقدمنا أن الشهادة على الوكالة لا تلزم إلا عند الجحود. (الدر المختار مع الشامي: ۳/۹٦، سعيد).

Fatāwā Farīdiyyah states:

In a marriage one man may stand in the place of both parties. (فتاوی فریدیہ: ٤/۳۹۲).

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states:

Question: A man informed his spiritual guide by telegram that his marriage to a particular woman should be performed. What is the ruling in this case?

Answer: In this situation the guide may perform the marriage, and he may make the offer and the acceptance on behalf of the party who gave permission by letter or telegram. (فتاوی دار العلوم دیوبند: ۷/۱٤۰).

Majmūʿah Qawānīn-e-Islāmī states:

One and the same person may act as agent for both the man and the woman. (مجموعہ قوانین اسلامی: ۸٦، دفعہ ۹۹).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
