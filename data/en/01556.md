### TITLE
A case of a conditional ṭalāq

### QUESTION
A man's wife left him in anger. The husband said, "If you have not returned by 1 January 2010, the marriage is over and annulled." The wife did not return by that date. Has a ṭalāq now taken effect upon the wife or not? And if a ṭalāq has taken effect, which kind of ṭalāq is it?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, if the man spoke these words intending ṭalāq, then one irrevocable ṭalāq has taken effect upon his wife. Since in common usage the ending of a marriage is spoken of to mean the ending of the relationship, it appears that he did have that intention.

Fatāwā Qāḍīkhān states:

ولو قال لها لا نكاح بيني وبينك أو قال لم يبق بيني وبينك نكاح أوقال فسخت نكاحك يقع الطلاق إذا نوى. (فتاوى قاضيخان على هامش الهندية: ۱/٤٦۸).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
