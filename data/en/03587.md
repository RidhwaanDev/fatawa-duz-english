### TITLE
An engagement is a promise of marriage, not a marriage

### QUESTION
A man became engaged to a girl. Eight or ten people were present at the engagement gathering. The girl's father said: Very well, we have given the girl to the boy. The boy said: We have accepted her. There was no sermon, no register and nothing else. Now the girl's family wish to break off the engagement, while the boy's family say that an offer and acceptance took place at the engagement, so this is a marriage and it cannot now be broken. What is the ruling of the Sharīʿah? Did a marriage take place or not? Can the girl marry elsewhere or not?

### ANSWER
In an engagement what is intended is a promise of marriage. Therefore, in the case in question no marriage was contracted; only a promise of marriage was made. Yes, one should not break a promise without a valid Sharʿī excuse; fulfilling a promise is recommended. If there is the intention of causing hurt, it will be sinful; otherwise not. ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) says in his Fatāwā:

قال في شرح الطحاوي: لو قال: هل أعطيتنيها فقال: أعطيت، إن كان المجلس للوعد فوعد، وإن كان للعقد فنكاح. (فتاوى الشامي: ۳/۱۱، سعيد).

(وكذا في البحر الرائق: ۳/۸۹، دار المعرفة، بيروت، وفتح القدير: ۳/۱۹۱، دار الفكر، والموسوعة الفقهية الكويتية: ٤۱/۲۳۹).

قال الملا علي القاري في المرقاة: قال النووي: أجمعوا على أن من وعد إنساناً شيئاً ليس بمنهي عنه فينبغي أن يفي بوعده وهل ذلك واجب أو مستحب؛ فيه خلاف: ذهب الشافعي رحمه الله وأبو حنيفة رحمه الله والجمهور إلى أنه مستحب

ولا يأثم يعني من حيث أنه خلف وإن كان يأثم إن قصد به الأذى. (المرقاة: ۹/۱۷۷، باب المزاح، ط: إمداديه، ملتان).

Kifāyat al-Muftī records:

At the time of an engagement, the girl's father says: I have given my daughter so-and-so to your son so-and-so; and the boy's father says: Yes, I have accepted her for my son so-and-so. After that they do not allow the girl to meet the boy, and this is considered a half-marriage. Can this girl be married elsewhere without the permission of the boy or of his guardian?

Answer: The words that are uttered at the time of an engagement are words of a promise of marriage; contracting a marriage is not intended. That is precisely why the marriage is performed afterwards by convening a separate gathering. Hence the rulings of marriage cannot be applied to an engagement. (کفایت المفتی: ۵/۵۱، ط: دار الاشاعت) ۔

Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥal mentions:

An engagement is the name of a promise to give and take in marriage, but a marriage is not contracted by an engagement. For this reason, breaking off an engagement is a breach of promise, and breaking a promise without some reasonable and valid excuse is a sin. But since the marriage contract has not taken place, there is no need to obtain a divorce from the boy. (آپ کے مسائل اور ان کا حل: ٦/۸٤) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
