### TITLE
The sharʿī ruling on eating the flesh of the kangaroo

### QUESTION
According to scientists the kangaroo is like the deer. The question now is: will it also be like the deer in the sharīʿah, and will eating its flesh be permissible like the flesh of the deer? بینوا توجروا.

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, eating the flesh of the kangaroo is permissible and correct, just like the flesh of the deer. Enquiry was made of certain experts, and it emerged that the kangaroo does not eat flesh at all; rather, like the deer and similar animals, it eats only grass and fodder. Nor does it possess fangs with which it might hunt with its teeth and molars. Hence there is no objection to eating it.

Consider the following. Al-Fatāwā al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah states:

وأما المتوحش نحو الظباء وبقر الوحش وحمر الوحش وإبل الوحش فحلال بإجماع المسلمين. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۲۸۹).

الموسوعة الفقهية
states:

النوع الرابع: كل وحش ليس له ناب يفترس به وليس من الحشرات: وذلك كالظباء، وبقر الحش، وحمر الوحش، وإبل الوحش، وهذا النوع حلال بإجماع المسلمين، لأنه من الطيبات. (الموسوعة الفقهية الكويتية: ۵/۱۳٤، ط: وزارة الاوقاف).

٭ The kangaroo: an animal of Australia whose forelegs are short and whose hind legs are large. It has a pouch on its belly in which it settles its young. (فیروز اللغات، ص ۱۰۳٦، و قاموس انگریزی، عربی، ص ٦۲۸).

The encyclopaedia also records the following:

Its food generally consists of the leaves of trees, grass, plants and the like; and among its teeth there are no canines. (Encarta Encyclopedia.)
