### TITLE
The ruling on capping teeth with gold or silver

### QUESTION
(a) If a man's or a woman's teeth are defective, or have been extracted, will it be permissible to have gold or silver teeth fitted in their place, or to have a gold or silver cap put over them, given that teeth made of materials other than gold and silver are also available? (b) If someone has had them fitted, will zakat be due on them or not? (c) And will they be removed after death or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
(a) The jurists differ over having gold teeth fitted, so as long as there is no genuine need one should avoid it. To have them fitted merely for adornment is disliked. (b) If someone has had them fitted out of need, then if this gold amounts to the nisab, or if the nisab is completed by combining it with other gold or silver or with rands, zakat will be due. (For the details of when zakat becomes obligatory, see Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā, volume three, p. 92.) (c) After death, neither the tooth nor the cap on the tooth will be removed.

See what Imām Muḥammad (may Allah have mercy on him) says in al-Jāmiʿ al-Ṣaghīr:

ولا تشد الأسنان بالذهب، وتشد بالفضة، وقال محمد رحمه الله: لا بأس بالذهب أيضاً. وقال في النافع الكبير: قوله: وقال محمد الخ؛ وقيل قول أبي يوسف رحمه الله مثل قول محمد، ذكره في الأصل وذكره الكرخي رحمه الله في مختصره قول أبي يوسف رحمه الله مثل قول أبي حنيفة رحمه الله، فصار عن أبي يوسف رحمه الله روايتان. لهما أن عرفجة بن سعد رضي الله عنه أصيب أنفه يوم الكلاب، فاتخذ أنفاً من فضة فأنتن، فأمره رسول الله ﷺ، أن يتخذ أنفاً من ذهب. ولأبي حنيفة رحمه الله أن الحاجة تندفع بالفضة، فأما الأنف فما اندفعت الحاجة لأنه أنتن. (الجامع الصغير مع شرحه، ص: ٤۷۷، ط: عالم الكتب، بيروت). (وكذا في الهداية: ٤/ ٤۵۷، كتاب الكراهية).

Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

Having a gold cap put on because of some defect in the teeth is not impermissible; putting it on merely for adornment is disliked. (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 2/313, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

For further detail see: (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā, volume one, p. 649, and p. 662).

On binding teeth with gold and putting a gold cap on them, Ḥaḍrat Muftī Shafīʿ (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

Imdād al-Muftīn states:

If a person's tooth becomes loose, it is permissible to bind it with gold or silver wire. This is also the meaning of the hadith in Sunan Abī Dāwūd, as stated in Fatāwā Qāḍīkhān:

إذا تحركت ثنية الرجل إلى أن قال: فشدها بذهب أو فضة لا بأس به وليس هذا كالحلي. الخ. (Imdād al-Muftīn: 2/815, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

Imdād al-Fatāwā states:

There is a difference of opinion about fitting gold teeth, so there is room for it; but caution is the better course. کذا فی الدر المختار ۔ (Imdād al-Fatāwā: 4/137). For further detail see: (Imdād al-Aḥkām: 4/339).
