### TITLE
The amount of the nisab for gold and silver

### QUESTION
What is the nisab for gold and silver?

### ANSWER
The nisab for gold is 87 grams and 480 milligrams.

The nisab for silver is 612 grams and 35 milligrams.

Kitāb al-Fatāwā states:

The nisab for gold is 20 mithqāls of gold, which is seven and a half tolas, and 87.479 grams in modern weights. The nisab for silver is two hundred dirhams, which is fifty-two and a half tolas, and 612.35 grams in modern weights. (کتاب الفتاوی: تیسرا حصہ: ۲٦۱، زکوۃ کا نصاب، نعیمیہ ۔ و احسن الفتاوی: ٤/۲۵٤) ۔

Jawāhir al-Fiqh states:

The sharia nisab for gold is twenty mithqāls, and a mithqāl weighs four and a half māshas, so by the tola reckoning the nisab for gold comes to seven and a half tolas.

The nisab for silver is two hundred dirhams, and one dirham weighs three māshas, one rattī and a fifth of a rattī; so when the sum is worked out it is clear that the nisab for silver is fifty-two tolas and six māshas. (جواہر الفقہ "اوزانِ شرعیہ" ۱/٤۲۳، دار العلوم کراچی) ۔

Al-Hidāyah states:

ليس فيما دون مائتي درهم صدقة لقوله عليه السلام: ليس فيما دون خمس أواق صدقة" والأوقية أربعون درهماً،

ليس فيما دون عشرين مثقالاً من ذهب صدقة. (الهداية: ۱/۱۹٤، فصل في الفضة والذهب، شركة عالمية وبدائع الصنائع ۲/۱٦).
