### TITLE
A question about the imam of a mosque

### QUESTION
A man used to lead the prayers in a mosque, and a long time has now passed since these events. On one or two occasions it happened that during the prayer there was a possibility, or a strong likelihood, that a drop of urine had come out. On another occasion, on the first night of Ramadan, the sighting of the moon had not yet been announced and the imam had already prayed his witr; then, after the moon was announced, he led the people in witr a second time after the tarawih, although someone else had led the tarawih. At the time, because of the drop of urine — about which he was either in doubt or strongly suspicious — the imam did not announce that the prayer should be repeated. He now has no connection with that place, and most of the worshippers of that time will either have passed away or moved elsewhere. All this happened 30 years ago and there is no longer any benefit in informing anyone. What can the imam now do to clear himself of this?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, since the breaking of the imam's ablution by that drop was doubtful or strongly suspected, the imam should have made an announcement at the time so that the prayer could be repeated. The established Hanafi position is that the imam's prayer carries the prayer of those who follow him; this has already been explained in volume two, page 234. In view of the difficulties in the situation described, however, there is a particular ruling recorded by the jurists which shows that the announcement is not necessary.

Consider what Tuḥfat al-Mulūk states:

ولو ظهر حدث الإمام أعاد المأموم لقوله ﷺ: أيما رجل صلي بقوم ثم تذكر جنابة أعاد، وأعادوا. (فصل في الجماعة: ۱/۸٤٦)

In its commentary Muḥammad ibn ʿAbd al-Laṭīf ibn ʿAbd al-ʿAzīz ibn Malak writes:

هذا إذا علم المأموم حدث إمامه وإن لم يعلموا لا يجب عليهم الإعادة ولا على الإمام الإعلام بأنه صلي على غير طهارة ولا يأثم بتركه الإعلام. (شرح تحفة الملوك: ۱/۸٤۷. بتعليق عبد المجيد الدرويش).

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār and Radd al-Muḥtār have likewise transmitted from some of the jurists the view that no notification need be given.

وصحح في مجمع الفتاوى عدمه أي الإخبار مطلقاً لكونه عن خطأ معفو عنه لأنه لم يتعمد ذلك. (ص: ۵۹۱/۱).

As for the hadith cited in the base text — أيما رجل صلي بقوم ثم تذكر جنابة أعاد، وأعادوا — the annotator, Dr. ʿAbd al-Majīd al-Darwīsh, has quoted from Naṣb al-Rāyah that this narration is uncommon, and Ibn Ḥajar said of it: لم اجدہ مرفوعاً.

It is, however, narrated in mursal form from Saʿīd ibn al-Musayyab:

"أن رسول الله ﷺ صلي بالناس وهو جنب وأعاد وأعادوا"

This narration is mursal, and in its chain of transmission Abū Jābir al-Bayāḍ is a rejected narrator; Yaḥyā ibn Maʿīn called him a liar. Set against it is a narration recorded by al-Dāraquṭnī from al-Barāʾ ibn ʿĀzib (may Allah be pleased with him):

قال رسول الله ﷺ: أيما إمام سها فصلى بالقوم وهو جنب فقد مضت صلاتهم ثم ليغتسل هو ثم ليعد صلاته الخ

This narration too, however, is weak: it contains Juwaybir, who is a rejected narrator.
(تعليق عبد المجيد الدرويش على شرح تحفة الملوك: ۱/۸٤٦).

The author of Sharḥ Tuḥfat al-Mulūk, Muḥammad ibn ʿAbd al-Laṭīf (d. 854), well known as Ibn Malak, is described by Sāʾid Bakdāsh (may Allah preserve him) in his introduction to Munyat al-Ṣayyādīn, where he writes of his life:

"هو الإمام المحدث الفقيه الحنفي المعتبر محمد بن عز الدين عبد اللطيف بن عبد العزيز بن أمين الدين بن فرشته الرومي".

He was a scholar who left behind a number of written works. Some of them are:

(۱) شرح مصابيح السنة للبغوي، (۲) شرح الوقاية، (۳) شرح تحفة الملوك للرازي، (٤) منية الصيادين في تعلم الاصطياد وأحكامه، (۵) روضة المتقين في مصنوعات رب العلمين في المواعظ والعبادات.

In the annotations to Munyat al-Ṣayyādīn the references for his biography are given from the following books:

(۱) كتائب اعلام الأخيار من فقهاء مذهب النعمان المختار للكفوي رحمه الله، (۲) الشقائق النعمانية في علماء الدولة العثمانية، (۳) كشف الظنون لحاجي خليفة، (٤) الفوائد البهية في تراجم الحنفية لعبد الحي اللكنوي رحمه الله، السعاية، وعمدة الرعاية، (۵) هدية العارفين لا سماعيل باشا البغدادي، (٦) الأعلام للزركلي، (۷) معجم المؤلفين لعمر رضا كحالة وغيره. (مقدمة منية الصيادين لسائد بكداش، ص ۱۱).

His father, ʿIzz al-Dīn ʿAbd al-Laṭīf, was also a researching scholar and an author, and the editor has likewise recorded his life in the introduction to Sharḥ Tuḥfat al-Mulūk.

This account has been written because on one occasion, when this humble servant cited Sharḥ Tuḥfat al-Mulūk in a fatwa, some muftis raised the objection that this was an unknown book and asked where it had come from to be used as a reference. In fact several of the author's works — Munyat al-Ṣayyādīn, for example — are regarded as masterpieces. It should also be remembered that the base text, Tuḥfat al-Mulūk, is the work of Muḥammad ibn Abī Bakr al-Rāzī (d. 666).

The upshot is that he should inform the worshippers as far as it is possible to do so; and if that is altogether impossible, this view may be acted upon.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
