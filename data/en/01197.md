### TITLE
A mutamattiʿ performing the saʿy of ḥajj without iḥrām

### QUESTION
A man performing tamattuʿ, after coming out of iḥrām, performed a voluntary ṭawāf and then performed the saʿy of ḥajj after that voluntary ṭawāf without being in iḥrām. What is the ruling?

### ANSWER
If the saʿy of ḥajj is performed before the standing at ʿArafah, iḥrām is a condition for it; but if it is to be performed after the standing at ʿArafah, then performing it without iḥrām is sunnah. In the case asked about it appears that he performed it before the standing at ʿArafah, so it was not valid without iḥrām and it will be necessary for him to perform it again.

See what is stated in Ghunyat al-Nāsik:

الرابع تقديم الإحرام عليه وأما بقاء الإحرام حالة السعي، فإن كان سعيه للحج قبل الوقوف فيشترط، أو بعد الوقوف فلا يشترط، بل ويسن عدمه. (غنية الناسك: ۷۱، فصل في ركن السعي وشرائطه، إدارة القرآن. وص ۱۱۵. والشامي: ۲/۵۱۸، سعيد. ولباب المناسك مع شرحه: ۱۹۳، فصل في شرائط صحة السعي، بيروت).

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh states:

The iḥrām of ḥajj or ʿumrah must precede the saʿy; so if someone performs the saʿy before entering iḥrām, it will not be permissible or valid. But once the iḥrām has been entered into at the beginning of the ḥajj, it is not necessary that it continue up to the saʿy. So if he performs the saʿy of ḥajj before the standing at ʿArafah (that is, after the ṭawāf of arrival), then the presence of iḥrām is a condition, whether the pilgrim is a qārin, a mutamattiʿ or a mufrid. If he performs the saʿy of ḥajj after the standing at ʿArafāt (that is, after the ṭawāf of ziyārah), then it is no longer a condition that the iḥrām continue, because it is now permissible for him to perform the saʿy after coming out of iḥrām; indeed the sunnah now is that he perform the saʿy after finishing with the iḥrām. (عمدۃ الفقہ: حصۂ چہارم ۱۹۸، شرائط صحتِ سعی، مجددیہ).

Muʿallim al-Ḥujjāj states:

The third condition: that the iḥrām of ḥajj or ʿumrah precede the saʿy. If someone performs the saʿy before entering iḥrām it will not be valid, even if it is after a ṭawāf. It is not necessary that the iḥrām continue up to the saʿy; rather, there is a detail here: if he performs the saʿy of ḥajj (whether he be a qārin, a mutamattiʿ or a mufrid) and performs it before the standing at ʿArafah, then being in iḥrām at the time of the saʿy is a condition; and if he performs the saʿy after the standing at ʿArafah, then it is not a condition that the iḥrām continue — rather, it is sunnah not to be in iḥrām. (معلم الحجاج: ۱٦۹، شرائط سعی، ارادۃ القرآن).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
