### TITLE
Toys for little girls that bear images

### QUESTION
If the toys that little girls play with are in the shape of figures, is this permissible or not? And what is the true position regarding the toys of Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah (may Allah be pleased with her)? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
To play with a toy bearing an image, and to keep it in the house, where the form is plainly visible, is not permissible or correct; a prohibition against keeping a picture in the house has come down in the hadiths. Toys made of cloth, however, in which the face is not clearly visible, may be played with.

Ḥaḍrat Mufti Shafīʿ Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

If children's dolls and small toys bear images, buying and selling them and children's playing with them is permissible, as has already been established from the incident of Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah (may Allah be pleased with her) in hadith number 24. The details of the jurists' disagreement on this have been given above. From the reports of the Ḥanafīs it appears that the permission has been given for young children and is not general; and in the view of most authorities the preferred position for these too is that they are not permissible.

في متفرقات البيوع من الدر المختار في آخر حظر المجتبى عن أبي يوسف يجوز بيع اللعبة وأن يلعب بها الصبيان انتهى، قال الشامي: ونسبته إلى أبي يوسف لا تدل على أن الإمام يخالفه لاحتمال أن لا يكون في المسألة قول. (فتاوى الشامي: ۵/۲۲٦، ط: سعيد، ومثله في مكروهات الصلاة ج: ۱/٦۵۰، سعيد).

Imam al-Nawawī (may Allah have mercy on him) wrote in his commentary on Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim, explaining the hadith of Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah (may Allah be pleased with her):

المراد هذه اللعب المسماة بالبنات التي تلعب بها الجواري الصغار ومعناه التنبيه على صغر سنها قال القاضي: وفيه جواز اتخاذ اللعب وإباحة لعب الجواري بهن وقد جاء أنه عليه الصلاة والسلام رأي ذلك ولم ينكره، قالوا: وسببه تدريبهن لتربية الأولاد وإصلاح شأنهن وبيوتهن. (شرح صحيح مسلم: ۹/۲۰۸، باب جواز تزويج الأب البكر الصغيرة، ط: بيروت).

Mirqāt, the commentary on Mishkāt, states:

ويحتمل أن يكون مخصوصاً من أحاديث النهي من اتخاذ الصور لما ذكر من المصلحة ويحتمل أن يكون قضية عائشة رضي الله عنه في أول الهجرة قبل تحريم الصور. (المرقاة: ٦/۲۰٦، ملتان).

After these quotations Ḥaḍrat Mufti Shafīʿ Ṣāḥib writes:

In sum, the jurists hold four positions on the matter of girls' dolls. The first is that these too are unlawful, like pictures generally, and that the report of Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah al-Ṣiddīqah (may Allah be pleased with her) relates to the period before pictures were made unlawful, and was later abrogated. Most of the hadith scholars have adopted this view.

A second position, opposed to it, is that of Qāḍī ʿIyāḍ (may Allah have mercy on him): that by the very wording of this hadith children's dolls have been excepted from the prohibition of pictures, and are permissible.

The third position is that permission is given for girls below the age of puberty and is not a general permission. This is what the position of the Ḥanafīs appears to be from the passage of al-Durr al-Mukhtār quoted above. They hold this incident of Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah al-Ṣiddīqah (may Allah be pleased with her) to belong to the time before puberty, since Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah al-Ṣiddīqah (may Allah be pleased with her) came into the marriage of the Prophet ﷺ at the age of six or seven, which was not the time of puberty, and the incident may have occurred in that very period.

The fourth position is that the dolls mentioned in the report of Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah al-Ṣiddīqah (may Allah be pleased with her) were not complete images at all, so that there is no conflict at all between it and the reports on the prohibition of pictures. Against the first and third positions, however, stands the report of Abū Dāwūd transmitted through Abū Muslim, which gives the date of this incident as the return from the expedition of Khaybar or the expedition of Tabūk, that is, in the year 7 AH or 9 AH. That the rulings prohibiting pictures had still not been given by then, or that Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah al-Ṣiddīqah (may Allah be pleased with her) was still below puberty — both are extremely far-fetched.

Further on, however, he establishes that the mention of the expedition of Khaybar or of Tabūk is not found in the two Ṣaḥīḥs or in the Musnad of Aḥmad and the like, but only in one chain of Abū Dāwūd; so to all appearances some narrator fell into confusion. He then says:

From all these strong indications it has become clear that the incident of the dolls of Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah al-Ṣiddīqah (may Allah be pleased with her) took place right at the beginning of the Hijrah, when the rulings prohibiting pictures had not yet been given, and that Ḥaḍrat al-Ṣiddīqah (may Allah be pleased with her) was a girl of tender years. Those, therefore, who declared this hadith abrogated by the hadiths of prohibition, and those who declared it to be confined to girls below puberty, undoubtedly have scope for what they say. واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم ۔ (تصویر کے شرعی احکام، از ص: ۵۱ ۔ تا۵۵و۸۵) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
