### TITLE
The ruling on inheritance after the husband's death where an irrevocable divorce has been given

### QUESTION
1. If a man gave his wife an irrevocable divorce and the husband then died or was killed, will the wife be entitled to inherit, either during the waiting period or after it, or not? 2. If the husband had taken out life insurance, what should be done with that money? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
1. In the case in question, if the husband gave his wife an irrevocable divorce or a triple divorce while in good health, then whether he dies during the waiting period or after it, in neither case will the wife be entitled to inherit. If, however, he gave the irrevocable or triple divorce during his final illness and died while she was still in the waiting period, then the wife will be entitled to inherit; and if the husband died after the waiting period had ended, she is not entitled to inherit. Furthermore, if the wife herself had demanded the divorce, she will not inherit even then.

2. As for the insurance money, the original sum that was deposited will be divided among the heirs according to their prescribed shares, and the excess will be given away to the poor in charity without the intention of reward.

قال في الهداية: وإذا طلقها ثلاثاً وهو مريض ثم صح ثم مات لم ترث ولكنا نقول: المرض إذا تعقبه برء فهو بمنزلة الصحة لأنه ينعدم به مرض الموت فيتبين أنه لا حق لها يتعلق بماله فلا يصير الزوج فاراً.

Commenting on this passage of al-Hidāyah, the meticulous scholar Ibn al-Humām writes:

قوله فلا يصير الزوج فاراً يعني الفرار المستلزم للحكم الشرعي الخاص إنما يتحقق شرعاً بالإبانة في حال تعلق حقها ولا يتعلق إلا في مرض موته. (الهداية مع فتح القدير: ٤/۱۵٤، ط: دار الفكر).

It is recorded in Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ:

وإن كانت "أي العدة" من طلاق بائن أو ثلاث فإن كان ذلك في حال الصحة فمات أحدهما لم يرثه صاحبه سواء كان الطلاق برضاها أو بغير رضاها، وإن كان في حال المرض فإن كان برضاها لا ترث بالإجماع وإن كان بغير رضاها فإنها ترث من زوجها عندنا وعند الشافعي لا ترث. (بدائع الصنائع: ۳/۲۱۸، سعيد).
وينظر: (البحر الرائق: ٤/٤۳).

It is recorded in Majmūʿah Qawānīn Islāmī:

In dealings such as life insurance and cooperative societies, if under their regulations a person has nominated someone else to receive his money, then after the death of the one who made the nomination the sum he deposited and had deducted will be divided among his heirs according to their prescribed shares, and the interest money will have to be given to the poor without the intention of reward. (p. 256).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
