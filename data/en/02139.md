### TITLE
Gifting an undivided share to a co-owner

### QUESTION
On paper the shop is jointly owned by the wife and the husband. The husband said in front of a good many people, "I have given this shop to my wife," and the wife is in possession of it. The husband also goes to the shop, but he does more of the outside work. Has the wife become the owner of this shop or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, the husband's saying "I have given this shop to my wife" is a gift, and possession was already the wife's; the gift is therefore complete and the wife has become the owner of the shop. Whoever's name may be on the papers, the gift as recognised by the sharia is not affected by that. Further, since a shop is for the most part like a thing that does not admit of division, it is valid to gift it without dividing it.

See what Fatāwā Qāḍīkhān says:

وفيما لا يقسم كالعبد والدابة والثوب والحمام يجوز هبة المشاع من الشريك وغيره في قولهم جميعاً. (فتاوى قاضيخان على هامش الهندية: ۳/۲٦۷، كتاب الهبة).

وفي الفتاوى الهندية: وهبة المشاع فيما لا يحتمل القسمة تجوز من الشريك ومن الأجنبي كذا في الفصول العمادية. (الفتاوى الهندية: ٤/۳۷۸، الباب الثاني فيما يجوز من الهبة ومالا يجوز).

(وكذا في فتاوى الشامي: ۵/٦۹۲، سعيد. وبدائع الصنائع: ٦/۱۱۹، سعيد).

Fatāwā ʿUthmānī states:

The mere fact that a property stands on paper in the donor's name makes no difference to the gift. (Fatāwā ʿUthmānī: 3/459).
