### TITLE
Writing "یا بدوح" in amulets

### QUESTION
Some practitioners write "یا بدوح" in amulets. Is "بدوح" among the names of Allah or not? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
Some scholars have written that this is a name of Allah in the Hebrew language, but this is not certain; and if it were an Arabic name, then the names of Allah are tawqīfī — that is, they are established only by transmission. One should therefore refrain from writing this name.

Consider what Fatāwā ʿUthmānī states: یا بدوح — this name does not occur in the Qur'an, but some scholars have written that it is a name of Allah in the Hebrew language. (فتاوی عثمانی: ۱/۳۱۲)۔

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband has declared its recitation as a litany permissible. Consider it:

بدوح — this word is commonly known with a ḍammah on the bāʾ and a shaddah on the dāl, but this is not correct. The correct form is بدوح with a fatḥah on the bāʾ and a light dāl. The verified position is that it is not a word of the Arabic language

(هكذا أفاده شيخنا العلامة مولانا محمد أنور شاه الكشميري قدس سره)

but is rather a name of Allah in Hebrew; and if it is taken as Arabic, it means "the One who renders powerless". In any case, since it is established that it is a name of God, reciting "یا بدوح" as a litany is permissible without any hesitation. (فتاوی دار العلوم دیوبند، جلدِ دوم، ص ۲۱۵، دار الاشاعت)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
