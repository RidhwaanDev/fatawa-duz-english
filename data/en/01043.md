### TITLE
Paying teachers' salaries out of zakat money

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to pay the salaries of the teachers of Islamic madrasahs out of zakat money or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question it is not permissible to pay salaries out of zakat money. Yes, if the zakat money is given to students who are entitled to it and tuition fees are then collected from them, that money may be spent on salaries; for with zakat money it is necessary to make a poor person the owner of it without any return.

Consider what Kanz al-Daqāʾiq states:

هي تمليك المال بغير عوض من فقير مسلم غير هاشمي ولا مولاه بشرط قطع المنفعة عن المملك من كل وجه لله تعالى. (كنز الدقائق: ۵۵، كتاب الزكاة، مكتبه إمدادية).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

For obligatory charities to be discharged, they must be spent on the proper recipients (the poor and so on) by way of transfer of ownership without any return. Paying them as salary is therefore not permissible; if they are given to madrasah staff as salary without a valid device, the zakat and the like will not be discharged. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۹/٦۰۵، مبوب و مرتب) ۔

Īḍāḥ al-Masāʾil states:

It is not permissible to spend zakat money on the salaries of a teacher, an employee, a cook and so on. (ایضاح المسائل: ۱۱۸، نعیمیہ) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
