### TITLE
Returning the money when an agent does not get the work done

### QUESTION
Here in Malawi a man — Zayd, say — gave ʿAmr five hundred thousand rupees to have some documents prepared. ʿAmr gave the money to an official to prepare the documents. The official took the money but did no work at all. The question now is: is ʿAmr obliged to return Zayd's money? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question, if Zayd gave ʿAmr the money on the basis that ʿAmr would get the work done for him, and ʿAmr accepted responsibility for the work — as is generally the case — then, if the work is not done, the responsibility for returning the money falls on ʿAmr.

For example, if a man is hired to bake bread and the loaves burn before they are taken out of the oven, he is liable for the loss. See Al-Durr al-Mukhtār, which states:

ولو احترق (أي الخبز) قبله (قبل إخراجه) لا أجر له ويغرم اتفاقاً لتقصيره، درر وبحر. وفي الشامية: قوله لتقصيره أي بعدم القلع من التنور. (الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ٦/۱٦، سعيد).

In the same way, if a person engaged a cook to cook food for him and the cook burnt the food or did not cook it properly, he is liable for the loss.

قال في الدر المختار: فإن أفسد أي الطعام الطباخ أو أحرقه أو لم ينضجه فهو ضامن للطعام. (الدر المختار: ٦/۱۷، سعيد).

Likewise, if a person fixed a wage for a wet nurse to suckle a child and the wet nurse fed him goat's milk, she is not entitled to the wage.

وقال في الهداية: وإن أرضعته في المدة بلبن شاة فلا أجر لها، لا نها لم تأت بعمل مستحق عليها، وهو الإرضاع فإن هذا إيجار وليس بإرضاع. (الهداية: ۳/۳۰۵).

If, however, ʿAmr had said that he was not taking responsibility for the work and would merely pass the money on to the official as an agent, then no liability falls on ʿAmr.

قال في البدائع: ومنها أي من حقوق التوكيل وأحكامها، أن المقبوض في يد الوكيل بجهة التوكيل بالبيع والشراء وقبض الدين والعين وقضاء الدين أمانة بمنزلة الوديعة. (بدائع الصنائع: ٦/۳٤، كتاب الوكالة، سعيد).

And if nothing of the sort was made explicit, then custom will be looked at; and by custom so large a sum is handed over only on the basis of responsibility for the work, so ʿAmr will be responsible. If ʿAmr is genuinely in straitened circumstances, Zayd should deal leniently with him.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
