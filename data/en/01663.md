### TITLE
Foster kinship with a maternal aunt's daughter through the grandmother's milk

### QUESTION
A boy was suckled by his maternal grandmother. He now wishes to marry his maternal aunt's daughter. Is this permissible or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, marriage with the maternal aunt's daughter cannot take place, because the maternal grandmother has become his foster mother, the maternal aunt has become his foster sister, and the maternal aunt's daughter has become his foster niece.

The hadith states:

قال النبي ﷺ: "يحرم من الرضاع ما يحرم من النسب". (رواه البخاري).

A well-known couplet sums up these principles:

از جانب شیر دہ ہمہ خویش ـ شوند واز جانب شیرخوار زوجان و فروع

That is, on the side of the woman who gives the milk all her relatives become relatives and unmarriageable kin: the suckling woman becomes the mother, her husband becomes the father, her sister becomes the maternal aunt, and her sons and daughters become brothers and sisters. On the side of the child who takes the milk, however, the relationship is limited to the spouse and the offspring: so if the child is male, his wife is unlawful to the suckling woman's husband, and if the child who took the milk is female, her husband is unlawful to the suckling woman.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
