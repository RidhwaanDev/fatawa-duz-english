### TITLE
The ruling on the profit being obligatory to give in charity when the sale is defective

### QUESTION
A person took a loan from someone and started a business. From this business he made a profit of one lakh rand. Now, having repaid the loan, he started a new business with the profit money. Later he came to know that the first business was defective, on the basis of sale before taking possession and likewise other reasons. What is the ruling now? Which profit is obligatory to give in charity: that which was obtained from the first business, or both together? If he gives both together in charity, then nothing is left with him to eat and drink, because this alone is his source of income. بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question the first business had become defective on account of sale before taking possession and other things that vitiate a contract; therefore its profit of one lakh rand is obligatory to give in charity. In the new business the things that vitiate a contract were not present, and on this basis the profits of the second business are not obligatory to give in charity; permission is given to use them.

Consider: it is recorded in Fatāwā ʿĀlamgīrī:

ولو اشتري جارية شراء فاسداً وقبضها وباعها وربح فيها تصدق بالربح ولو اشتري بثمنها شيئاً آخر فربح فيه طاب الربح كذا في السراج الوهاج. (الفتاوى الهندية: ٣/١٤٩).

وقال في الهداية: ومن اشتري جارية بيعاً فاسداً وتقابضا فباعها وربح فيها تصدق بالربح ويطيب للبائع ما ربح في الثمن، والفرق أن الجارية مما يتعين فيتعلق العقد بها فيتمكن الخبث في الربح، والدراهم والدنانير لا يتعينان على العقود فلم يتعلق العقد الثاني بعينها فلم يتمكن الخبث فلا يجب التصدق، وهذا في الخبث الذي سببه فساد الملك. (الهداية: ٣/٦٦).

Ahamm Fiqhī Faysle mentions:

As a matter of principle it is not permissible to sell something before taking possession of it; however, if a sale is carried out before taking possession, then this sale will be defective, not void, and after taking possession it will convey ownership. (اہم فقہی فیصلے: ١٠٠) ۔
