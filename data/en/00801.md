### TITLE
Making a collective supplication after the Eid prayer

### QUESTION
What is the ruling on a collective supplication after the Eid prayer? Some people make it after the sermon; what is the ruling on that?

### ANSWER
Evidence for supplication after prayer is found in a great many hadiths, as has already been discussed. So, in the case in question, making a collective supplication after the Eid prayer is correct and sound. There is, however, no evidence for making the supplication after the sermon, so one should suffice with the supplication made after the prayer. Consider what Imdād al-Aḥkām states:

Narrations have come to hand concerning the supplication made after the Eid prayer:

وهي هذه: عن أم عطية رضي الله تعالى عنها قالت: كنا نؤمر أن نخرج يوم العيد حتى تخرج البكر من خدرها حتى تخرج الحيض فيكن خلف الناس فيكبرن بتكبيرهم ويدعون بدعائهم يرجون بركة ذلك اليوم وطهرته، أخرج البخاري في صحيحه كذا في فتح الباري: ص ۳۸٦ ج ۲. وأخرج الترمذي عن أم عطية رضي الله تعالى عنها أن رسول الله ﷺ كان يخرج الأبكار والعواتق وذوات الخدر والحيض في العيدين فأما الحيض فيعتزلن المصلي ويشهدن دعوة المسلمين، الحديث ص: ۷۰، وقال الترمذي رحمه الله: حديث ام عطية حديث حسن صحيح.

The supplication in this hadith cannot mean the supplication of the sermon, because during the sermon only the imam supplicates and the listeners cannot. The hadith shows that menstruating women used to stand behind the men at the Eid prayers, saying the takbīr along with the men's takbīr and supplicating along with their supplication; and this establishes that men and women alike made the supplication. There was certainly no time for takbīr and supplication before the prayer, so the supplication was certainly made after it. Moreover, in Tirmidhī this same hadith contains the words ویشہدن دعوۃ المسلمین, meaning that the women took part in the Muslims' supplication. So making a supplication after the Eid prayer is certainly permissible and recommended, and its being recommended and permissible cannot be denied. (امداد الاحکام: ۱/۷٤۲، فصل فی الجمعہ و العیدین، دار العلوم کراچی)

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:
Make the supplication after the Eid prayer; supplicating after the sermon has no basis. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۸/٤٦٤، مبوب و مرتب، جامعہ فاروقیہ)
