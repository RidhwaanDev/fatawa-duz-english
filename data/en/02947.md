### TITLE
A litany for removing difficulties and for success in one's aim

### QUESTION
Please tell me some effective litany transmitted from our elders for success in one's aim, and also some supplication that is effective in removing difficulties and calamity.

### ANSWER
In the case in question: 1. For success in a lawful aim of yours, recite the following litany one hundred times daily:

"اللهمَّ يا مُقَلِّبَ الْقُلُوْبِ وَالْأبْصَارِ وَيا خَالِقَ اللَّيلِ وَالنَّهارِ قَلِّبْ قُلُوْبَهمْ بِالْمَوَدَّة وَالإِعْزَازِ وَاْلإِحْسَانِ".
(انوارِ شمسیہ، ص ۹۷)۔

2. To remove difficulties, recite Sūrat al-Ḍuḥā and Sūrat al-Inshirāḥ seven times daily at the time of sunrise and at the time of sunset. (انوارِ شمسیہ، ص ۹٤)۔

To gain relief from anxieties and sorrows, first recite the blessings upon the Prophet three times, then recite
"لا حول ولا قوة إلا بالله"
313 times, and on the last occasion recite in full
"لَاحَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّة إِلَا بِالله الْعَلِيِّ الْعَظِيمْ"
and then recite the blessings upon the Prophet three times. (انوارِ شمسیہ، ص ۹٤)۔

Or, for every difficulty, recite this litany 313 times daily:
"رَبِّ إِنِّيْ مَغْلُوْبٌ فَانْتَصِرْ"
and recite the blessings upon the Prophet at the beginning and at the end. (انوارِ شمسیہ، ص ۸۷)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
