### TITLE
Dedicating reward to the men of the unseen

### QUESTION
Some pious people and specialists in spiritual practices make a habit, when setting out on a journey, of reciting something and dedicating its reward to the men of the unseen (rijāl al-ghayb) — even though these beings may be good or bad. What is the answer to this?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, if they are Muslims, then dedicating reward to them is permissible and correct, in line with the general rule: just as one may dedicate reward to the Muslims among human beings, so too it is permissible to dedicate it to the Muslims among the jinn.

But under the name of dedicating reward there must never be any seeking of help from them with the intention of pleasing their spirits. Furthermore, we have never heard of the senior scholars of Deoband or of our own spiritual guides dedicating reward to the men of the unseen, so it is better to avoid it.
