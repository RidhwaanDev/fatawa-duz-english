### TITLE
Abortion because of water on the foetus's head

### QUESTION
A woman is pregnant and six months have passed over the pregnancy. The doctor says that there is water on the child's head and that it should be aborted. The mother is in no distress. Will an abortion be permitted in the sharia or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, aborting the foetus after the soul has been breathed into it is forbidden by consensus; moreover, the mother is at present in no distress. Therefore an abortion will not be permissible merely on the doctor's say-so.

Note what Shāmī states:

وفي الذخيرة: لو أرادت إلقاء الماء بعد وصوله إلى الرحم قالوا: إن مضت مدة ينفخ فيه الروح لا يباح لها وقبله اختلف المشايخ فيه والنفخ مقدر بمائة وعشرين يوماً بالحديث. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۳۷٤، سعيد).

Jadīd Fiqhī Mabāḥith states:

In looking into the question of abortion we should consider its two stages. One case is that life has already come into the pregnancy. According to the hadith, the soul comes into being four months — that is, 120 days — after the pregnancy is established, and it appears that modern specialists in embryology confirm this as well. Once the soul has come into being, abortion is forbidden by consensus. Aḥmad ʿIlīsh Mālikī says:

والتسبب في إسقاطه بعد نفخ الروح فيه محرم إجماعاً وهو من قتل النفس. (فتح العلي المالك: ۱/۳۹۹).

And Fatāwā Shaykh al-Islām Ḥāfiẓ Ibn Taymiyyah states:

إسقاط الحمل حرام بإجماع المسلمين وهو من الوأد الذي قال تعالى فيه: ﴿وإذا الموؤدة سئلت بأي ذنب قتلت﴾. (٤/۳۱۷).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
