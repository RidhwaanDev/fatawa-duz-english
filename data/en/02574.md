### TITLE
Abortion because of rape

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to abort a pregnancy resulting from rape or not?

### ANSWER
In the case of rape, an abortion is permitted before the child's limbs have formed and before the soul has been breathed into it; but after the limbs have formed and the soul has been breathed in, it is not permitted.

Note what Jadīd Fiqhī Mabāḥith states:

In this connection this much should be clear, that a pregnancy, even though it arises from fornication, is inviolable and is itself guilty of no offence. It is for this reason that the author of the Hidāyah (may Allah have mercy on him) wrote that aborting a pregnancy resulting from fornication is likewise not permissible.

وهذا الحمل محترم لأنه لا جناية منه ولهذا لم يجز إسقاطه. (الهداية: ۲/۳۱۲، كتاب النكاح، فصل في بيان المحرمات، والبحر الرائق: ۳/۱۱٤، دار المعرفة، بيروت، وتبيين الحقائق: ۲/۱۱۳).

But since fornication is so widespread in this age, if we do not permit an abortion then many unmarried girls will be deprived forever of the blessing of married life. It is for this reason that the annotator of the Hidāyah, Mawlānā ʿAbd al-Ḥayy Farangī Maḥallī, wrote in his gloss on the Hidāyah:

وأما في زماننا يجوز وإن استبان الخلق وعليه الفتوى. (حاشية الهداية: ۲/۳۱۲).

In the light of the wording of both the Hidāyah and its gloss, my own view is that if the pregnancy came about as a result of rape, and four months have not passed over the pregnancy, and the woman is unmarried, then in that case there ought to be permission for an abortion, so that the poor woman is not deprived forever of the blessing of married life on account of an act she never intended. And if the pregnancy is the result of fornication by consent, or four months have passed over the pregnancy, then in these situations an abortion is not permitted. (جدید فقہی مباحث: ۱/۳۷۳، ط: ادارۃ القرآن) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
