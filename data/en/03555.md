### TITLE
The measure of worship on Shab-i Barāʾat

### QUESTION
Number 8: If on Shab-i Barāʾat a person does not stay awake the whole night but performs somewhat more worship than on other nights, will this worship too be counted within qiyām al-layl or not?

### ANSWER
Where merit has been mentioned for worship on the blessed nights, it does not follow from this that worship must be performed the whole night through. Rather, if worship, recitation and the like are performed during some portion of the night, reward and merit will be obtained by it and it will be included within the category of qiyām al-layl. It is stated in the noble hadith that whoever performs the ʿishāʾ prayer in congregation has stood in prayer for half the night, and if he then performs the fajr prayer in congregation as well it is as though he worshipped the whole night — that is, he obtains the merit of praying the whole night through. Consider the noble hadith:

عن عبد الرحمن بن أبي عمرة، قال: دخل عثمان بن عفان المسجد بعد صلاة المغرب فقعد وحده فقعدت إليه فقال يا ابن أخي سمعت رسول الله ﷺ يقول: من صلى العشاء في جماعة فكأنما قام نصف الليل ومن صلى الصبح في جماعة فكأنما صلى الليل كله. (رواه مسلم: ۱/٤۵٤).

وفي رواية لأبي داود عنه أنه قال: قال رسول الله ﷺ: من صلى العشاء في جماعة كان كقيام نصف ليلة ومن صلى العشاء والفجر في جماعة كان كقيام الليلة. (سنن أبي داود: ۱/۲۱۷، بيروت).

It is mentioned in Shaʿbān awr Shab-i Barāʾat ke Faḍāʾil wa Aḥkām:

Ḥaḍrat Ḥakīm al-Ummat (may Allah have mercy on him) states: Now the point worth considering is which portion of the night it is better to remain awake in. This is settled by the Qurʾān as well as by the hadith. From the Qurʾān it is learnt that remaining awake in the last part of the night has the stronger effect, for Allah Most High has said:

إن ناشئة الليل هي أشد وطأ وأقوم قيلاً.

And from the hadith its being superior is learnt, for hadiths in abundance have come concerning the merit of the last part of the night, and rational principles also bear witness to this, since that is the time for sleep and giving up sleep is difficult. From this too it is learnt that the last portion of the night is superior. But if remaining awake during that portion is difficult for someone, let him obtain the merit by worshipping in the early part of the night itself, the lowest degree of which is to remain occupied in worship until ʿishāʾ. There is no need to stay awake the whole night; indeed it is not even good, for it is stated in the hadith:

أحب الأعمال إلى الله أدومها،

So on the night of Shaʿbān one should stay awake only to the extent that one can keep up. (Shaʿbān ke Faḍāʾil wa Aḥkām, p. 136, published by Idārah-i Ghufrān, Pakistan).

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ḥusayn Aḥmad Madanī (may Allah have mercy on him) states: The attention of the Master, mighty is His name, is turned towards this world and its inhabitants on the fifteenth night of Shaʿbān more than on other nights. It was therefore considered fitting that man too should take a greater share in the worship of Allah Most High than on other nights, and should strive to absorb within himself as much of His mercy as is possible. So let him fill this night with supererogatory prayers, recitation of the Qurʾān, remembrance and supplication — whether the whole night, or most of it, or a portion of it. Neither is any particular act of worship specified, nor is any particular time fixed. On this night one should pray for oneself, for one's elders and for all the Muslims. (Maktūbāt-i Shaykh al-Islām: 4/78).

Muftī Taqī ʿUthmānī states:

Tell me, are your hours counted with Allah Most High — how many hours you spent in the mosque? Hours are not counted there; there sincerity is looked at. If even a few moments of connection with Allah Most High are attained with sincerity, then those few moments alone will, if Allah wills, carry the vessel across. (Shab-i Barāʾat kī Ḥaqīqat, p. 17).

It is written in Shaʿbān awr Shab-i Barāʾat ke Faḍāʾil wa Aḥkām:

It is not necessary to stay awake the whole of this night. If someone, while keeping away from sins, performs on this night a little more worship than on other days, he too will obtain a share of the merit of this night, because hours are not counted with Allah Most High; rather sincerity is looked at. If even a few moments in the special court of Allah Most High are attained with sincerity, those are a very great blessing and treasure. (Shaʿbān wa Shab-i Barāʾat ke Faḍāʾil, p. 134).
