### TITLE
Whether zakat is due on prize bonds

### QUESTION
Prize bonds are very common here in Bangladesh. You pay 100 taka to the bank and receive a bond worth a hundred taka, and once every two months the bank holds a draw and awards prizes to a few people. I bought bonds worth fifty thousand taka from them. Those bonds have been with me for the past few years, and in the hope of winning a prize I did not return them. Now I have needed the money, so I have returned the bonds and taken my money back. What I want to know is this: do I owe zakat for the past years or not? And what is the ruling on taking the prize on such a bond? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case described, your money was with the bank as a loan, and the ruling on a loan is that once it has been recovered, zakat must be paid for the past years as well. Marāqī al-Falāḥ states:

وزكاة الدين على أقسام فإنه قوي ووسط وضعيف؛ فالقوي وهو بدل القرض ومال التجارة، إذا قبضه وكان على مقر ولو مفلساً

زكاة لما مضى ويتراخى وجوب الأداء إلى أن يقبض أربعين درهماً ففيها درهم لأن ما دون الخمس من النصاب عفو لا زكاة فيه وكذا فيما زاد بحسابه

(مراقي الفلاح مع حاشية الطحطاوي، ص۷۱۵، قديمي). وكذا في بدائع الصنائع: ۲/۱۰، سعيد).

For a detailed discussion of taking the prize on a prize bond, see: (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā, volume five, pp. 474–476, Zamzam Publishers).

For present purposes it is enough to understand that it is not permissible to receive thousands of taka in return for a hundred taka.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
