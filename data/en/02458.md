### TITLE
Taking others as partners in an animal one owns

### QUESTION
A person has a cow or a buffalo of his own that he has reared at home. He wishes to keep a seventh share in it for himself and to sell the remaining shares. Is that permissible for him or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question he is permitted to keep one share in his own animal for himself and to sell the other six shares, and such a partnership is valid. (فتاوی قاضی خان، و فتاوی الشامی: ٦/) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
