### TITLE
A salam sale of mangoes

### QUESTION
A man's son is getting married in two months' time, and honoured guests from all over the country will be attending. Since the mangoes of that region are famous, he makes a salam contract (a sale with payment in advance) with a mango trader: "I want 1,000 kilos of such-and-such a variety of mango on such-and-such a date for such-and-such a price." At the moment, however, mangoes are not available in the open market. Is this salam sale permissible or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, instead of a salam sale he should take a promise from the trader to supply the mangoes at an agreed price. But if a salam contract is really needed, then in the view of Imam Abū Ḥanīfah (may Allah have mercy on him) it will not be a valid salam sale, because in salam the goods must be available somewhere in the market from the time of the contract until they are handed over, whereas mangoes are simply not available at present. In case of need, however, there is room to act on the view of Imam Shāfiʿī (may Allah have mercy on him). Ḥaḍrat Thānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) and Ḥaḍrat Muftī Walī Ḥasan Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) gave a fatwa in this matter on the view of Imam Shāfiʿī (may Allah have mercy on him).

Imdād al-Fatāwā states:

In a salam contract the Ḥanafīs make it a condition that the goods sold be available right up to the appointed time. If this condition is not met, the salam contract will not be permissible. In the view of Imam Shāfiʿī (may Allah have mercy on him), however, it is enough that they be available at the appointed time — كذا في الهداية، (۳/۹۳) — so if this view is acted upon in case of need, there is no blame; it is a concession. (امداد الفتاوی: ۳/۱۰٦).

In another place it is written: since this is a matter of general hardship, there is room to act on the view of Imam Shāfiʿī (may Allah have mercy on him). (امداد الفتاوی: ۳/۲۱).

Dars al-Hidāyah states:

In view of the difficulties of the present age, the jurists have said that there is room to give a fatwa in these matters on the view of Imam Shāfiʿī (may Allah have mercy on him), so that people's wealth may be saved from becoming unlawful.

A useful point: the world has a system of worship and a system of dealings. For acts of worship there must be texts, whereas in dealings every imam makes things easier. (درس الہدایہ، باب شروط السلم، ص ۳۲۹).
