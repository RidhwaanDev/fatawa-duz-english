### TITLE
Ruling on sending a second letter to confirm a divorce document

### QUESTION
A man wrote out a divorce document and sent it to his wife, and then wrote and sent a second letter to confirm it. How many ṭalāqs take effect?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, if in the second letter the husband intended only to confirm the first letter and did not intend a fresh ṭalāq, then only one ṭalāq has taken effect upon his wife.

If, however, he wrote the second letter intending a second ṭalāq, then two ṭalāqs have taken effect.

Radd al-Muḥtār states:

كرر لفظ الطلاق وقع الكل وإن نوى التأكيد دين، قوله وإن نوى التأكيد أي وقع الكل قضاءً وكذا إذا أطلق أشباه: أي بأن لم ينو استينافاً ولا تأكيداً لأن الأصل عدم التأكيد. (فتاوى الشامي: ۳/۲۹۳، سعيد).

If the ʿiddah (the waiting period a woman observes after a divorce) has passed, then in either case there is room for the marriage to be contracted afresh. In the first case, however, the husband will have the right of two further ṭalāqs after the marriage is renewed, while in the second case he will have the right of only one.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
