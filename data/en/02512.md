### TITLE
Walking on the eating spread

### QUESTION
What is the ruling on walking across the eating spread, or jumping over it, whether the food is laid out on it or not?

### ANSWER
No explicit ruling on this has been found in the books of fiqh. However, some hadiths indicate that it is disliked in the lesser sense, since the hadith says that if a morsel falls one should pick it up from the spread and eat it, and walking across the spread will soil it so that the morsel cannot be eaten. Other hadiths show that it is contrary to good manners, for the hadith says that blessing descends in the middle of the food, and to walk on the place where blessing descends is a discourtesy.

Note what the hadith states:

عن جابر رضي الله عنه قال: قال رسول الله ﷺ: إذا وقعت لقمة أحدكم فليأخذها فليمط ما كان بها من أذى وليأكلها ولا يدعها للشيطان

الخ. (رواه مسلم، رقم: ۲۰۳۳).

Note a second hadith:

عن ابن عباس رضي الله عنه أن النبي ﷺ قال: "البركة تنزل وسط الطعام فكلوا من حافتيه ولا تأكلوا من وسطه. رواه الترمذي وقال: هذا حديث حسن صحيح. (رقم: ۱۸۰۵).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
