### TITLE
Cutting the nails while in a state of major ritual impurity

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to trim the nails and remove hair while in a state of major ritual impurity, or not?

### ANSWER
Trimming the nails and removing hair while in a state of major ritual impurity is disliked; one should do so after taking the ritual bath and becoming pure. See what al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

حلق الشعر حالة الجنابة مكروه وكذا قص الأظافير. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳۵۸).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

The texts speak of dislike without qualification, and such wording generally means strict dislike; the contextual indications, however, show that what is meant here is mild dislike. (احسن الفتاوی: ۲/۳۸)۔

For further detail see: (فتاوی دار العلوم زکریا، جلد اول، ص ۷٦۲، جدید ایڈیشن)۔

واللہ اعلم۔
