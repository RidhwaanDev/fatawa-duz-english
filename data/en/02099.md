### TITLE
Taking an interest-bearing loan to pay tuition fees

### QUESTION
If a wealthy man takes a loan out of interest money in order to pay his son's university fees, is this permissible or not?

بينوا توجروا ولكم الأجر الجزيل.

### ANSWER
If taking a loan out of interest-bearing wealth means that he takes a loan from a bank and will pay interest on it, then this is not correct, because a university education is not obligatory, nor is it so pressing a necessity that something impermissible should be committed for its sake. If, on the other hand, what is meant is that someone has interest money lying with him and the father takes that very interest money as a loan for his son, while both father and son are wealthy, then the interest money must be given away in charity immediately; it is not correct to circulate it in loans. Yes, if the son is poor and does not own the nisāb (the threshold of zakatable wealth), and the interest money is made over to him as his own property, then it is correct, and he may afterwards use it wherever he wishes.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
