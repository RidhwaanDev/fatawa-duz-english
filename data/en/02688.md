### TITLE
The ruling on wearing coloured contact lenses

### QUESTION
A fashion is current these days in which people put lenses of various colours in their eyes for adornment. Will their use be permissible in the eyes of the sharia or not? Does this fall under تغییر لخلق اللہ or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
There is no harm in adopting adornment while staying within the limits of the sharia, so using such coloured lenses will also be permissible, provided there is no extravagance in it, no fear of temptation, and no other corrupting consequences — for this does not fall under تغییر لخلق اللہ but belongs rather to the category of adornment. If, however, it is done as a fashion and in imitation of other nations, then one should avoid it.

See what ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) says about the nose-ring:

قوله: لم أره قلت: إن كان مما يتزين النساء به كما هو في بعض البلاد فهو فيها كثقب القرط، وقد نص الشافعية على جوازه مدني. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/ ٤۲۰، سعيد).

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Muḥammad Yūsuf Ludhyānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

Women are indeed permitted nose jewellery as well, but respectable women must keep away from resembling the women of the marketplace. (Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥal: 8/375, new edition).

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

In the matter of adornment and beauty Islam has taken the path of balance and moderation. On the one hand it has forbidden extraordinary and undue affectation for the sake of adornment, and on the other hand it has allowed it within a certain limit. Thus it is correct to pierce girls' noses and ears so that a nose-ring, earrings and the like may be worn:

لا بأس بثقب أذن البنات. (خلاصة الفتاوى: ٤/ ۳۷۷)

And it is obvious that when this is correct, their use will be permissible too.

In the same way, it is permitted to put pins, flowers and the like of silver, gold or any other metal in the hair for beauty. (Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 1/313).

In sum, using coloured lenses as an adornment is permissible and correct, so long as it involves no resemblance to other nations and no other corrupting consequences. Even so, it does not befit a pious Muslim woman to take up every manner and habit of the women of the marketplace. Even if a thing lies within the bounds of permissibility, piety and purity remain a praiseworthy ornament in Islam, and to be adorned with it is the moral duty of every Muslim woman.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
