### TITLE
How to perform ʿIshāʾ and Fajr in Western countries

### QUESTION
In some Western countries the twilight does not disappear from May to July. How should the ʿIshāʾ and Fajr prayers be performed there? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
The scholars have set out four methods for this:

(1) Follow the nearest days: that is, take the time at which the twilight disappeared on the last day it did so, and treat that as the beginning of the time of ʿIshāʾ.

(2) Follow the nearest lands: that is, take the time at which the twilight disappears in a nearby place.

(3) Divide the period from sunset to sunrise into two halves. The first half will be for Maghrib and ʿIshāʾ, with a suitable interval between them — for example, ʿIshāʾ an hour and a quarter after Maghrib — and the second half will be for Fajr. On this reckoning the fast will begin from the start of the second half, and this is the more cautious course.

(4) Divide the period from sunset to sunrise into seven parts: six parts will be the night, and the seventh part will be Fajr. Mawlwī Muḥammad ʿUthmān, in Tafsīr al-Īmān Sindhī, quoting al-Durr al-Mukhtār, and Ḥaḍrat Thānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) in Imdād al-Fatāwā (2/98), have written that in every night the period from true dawn to sunrise is one seventh of the whole night. For details, study the book "صبح صادق و شفق" by Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Yaʿqūb Qāsmī.
