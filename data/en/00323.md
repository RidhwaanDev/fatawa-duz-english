### TITLE
Spending zakat money on a mosque by means of a legal device

### QUESTION
Can zakat money be spent on a mosque by using a legal device, or not?

### ANSWER
In a case of pressing need, zakat money may be put into the construction of a mosque and the like by employing a legal device. Ordinarily, though, there is no such need, so a mosque should be built from money donated for the sake of Allah, and devices and contrivances should not be resorted to when there is no need for them.

Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

وكذلك من عليه الزكاة لو أراد صرفها إلى بناء المسجد أو القنطرة لا يجوز، فإن أراد الحيلة فالحيلة: أن يتصدق به المتولي على الفقراء ثم الفقراء يدفعونه إلى المتولي ثم المتولي يصرف إلى ذلك كذا في الذخيرة. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۲/٤۷۳).

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

وحيلة التكفين بها التصدق على الفقير ثم هو يكفن فيكون الثواب لهما، وكذا في تعمير المسجد الخ.

Radd al-Muḥtār states:

(قوله وكذا) الإشارة إلى الحيلة. (الدر المختار ورد المحتار: ۲/۲۷۱، سعيد).

Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

In a case of pressing need it is permissible to spend zakat money on a mosque by means of this device: the money is handed over to someone entitled to zakat as a transfer of ownership, and once he has taken possession of it he puts it into the mosque on his own behalf, or spends it on some other cause on which zakat could not be spent directly. (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 4/305).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

If a person is compelled to use a device and has made someone entitled to zakat the owner of the money with full authority over it, without saying anything, and then encourages him to spend it on a good cause, and he willingly agrees, the zakat is discharged. (Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 2/8).
