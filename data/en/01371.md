### TITLE
Zakat on artificial fingers made of silver

### QUESTION
A man lost three fingers, so he had fingers of silver fitted. When a year has passed, will zakat be due on them together with his other wealth or not? And is it permissible to do this? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
A report in Jāmiʿ al-Tirmidhī states that ʿArfajah ibn Asʿad (may Allah be pleased with him) lost his nose at the battle of al-Kulāb in the days of ignorance. He had one of silver fitted, but it began to smell, so the Prophet ﷺ told him to have a nose made of gold. Imam al-Tirmidhī (may Allah have mercy on him) says that this hadith is ḥasan gharīb. Consider the following:

عن عرفجة بن أسعد رضي الله عنه قال: أصيب أنفي يوم الكلاب في الجاهلية فاتخذت أنفاً من ورق فانتن علي فأمرني رسول الله ﷺ أن أتخذ أنفاً من ذهب. قال أبو عيسى: هذا حديث حسن غريب. (رواه الترمذي، رقم: ۱۷۷۰).

From the hadith quoted above we learn that it is permissible to have fingers of silver fitted.

As for zakat, the detail is this. If the fingers are fixed to the hand in such a way that they cannot easily be taken off — that is, they have become a part of the body — then zakat is neither due nor binding on them. But if they can easily be detached, then once a year has passed zakat will be due on them together with his other wealth.

Imdād al-Fatāwā states:

A nose of gold is made and fitted to the face, and this nose can also be removed without difficulty; zakat is therefore due on such a nose. But gold that has been fitted into a tooth or used to fill it cannot be removed in that way, so zakat is not due on it. (Imdād al-Fatāwā: 2/78, "Zakat on a nose or teeth made of gold").

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

In certain circumstances, for particular reasons, artificial parts made of gold and silver are used — a nose, teeth, the filling of hollow teeth with gold or silver, the binding of a tooth with gold wire, and so on. The ruling on these is that if they can easily be taken out, zakat is due on them, just as the Messenger of Allah ﷺ made zakat due on jewellery. But if they cannot easily be taken out and have been fitted permanently, so that they become a part of the person's body that cannot be separated from it, then they now fall among a person's basic necessities, and zakat is not due on such things. (Summarised from Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 1/208, "Zakat on artificial parts made of gold and silver", Nuʿaymiyyah). See also: Īḍāḥ al-Masāʾil: 108, Nuʿaymiyyah.
