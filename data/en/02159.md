### TITLE
Sending a gift with a view to marriage

### QUESTION
If a young man loves a girl and there is no illicit relationship, but he sends a gift to her family so that the girl or her family may agree to the marriage — will this too come under the ruling of a bribe, or will it be permissible to do?

### ANSWER
In the case described, since there is no illicit relationship, this gift is a way of winning affection, and it is therefore permissible to accept it — just as giving a gift or charity to those whose hearts are to be reconciled (al-muʾallafah qulūbuhum) was for the attainment of a religious purpose. Here too, marriage is a religious purpose, and this is a means of attaining it.
