### TITLE
Killing mosquitoes with an electric device

### QUESTION
If a person installs a mosquito light and mosquitoes are killed by it, does this act fall under burning with fire or not? Also, is it permissible to burn insects and crawling creatures in fire or not? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the pure Sharīʿah, burning with fire in ordinary circumstances and without need is forbidden and impermissible; the jurists have, however, given permission for it in certain particular necessities. On this basis, killing mosquitoes with an electric device is permissible at a time of need. In the case in question, moreover, if the electric device is fixed in one place and the mosquitoes come to it of their own accord and die, then this is correct, for no killing has been carried out by the person — just as the jurists have written that feeding carrion or giving impure water to an animal is forbidden, but if a person places it at a distance and the animals eat it themselves, that is not forbidden.

Another parallel to this question is that the jurists have written that if bed-bugs become numerous and boiling water is poured over them in order to destroy them, this is permissible. In the same way, if there is no other easy method of getting rid of mosquitoes, it is correct to destroy or reduce them by means of an electric device — provided that they come to the device of their own accord and the device is not moved around after them. Note that the prohibition of burning with fire in ordinary circumstances is reported in the ḥadīth:

عن عبد الرحمن بن عبد الله عن أبيه قال: كنا مع رسول الله ﷺ في سفر فانطلق لحاجته فرأينا حمرة معها فرخان فأخذنا فرخيها فجاء ت الحمرة فجعلت تفرش فجاء النبي ﷺ، فقال: من فجع هذه بولدها ردوا ولدها إليها، ورأي قرية نمل قد حرقناها فقال: من حرق هذه، قلنا نحن، قال: إنه لا ينبغي أن يعذب بالنار إلا رب النار. (رواه أبو داود: ۲/۳٦۳، رقم: ۲٦۷۷).
وفي رواية له قال رسول الله ﷺ: إن وجدتم فلاناً فاقتلوه ولا تحرقوه فإنه لا يعذب بالنار إلا رب النار. (أبو داود، رقم: ۲٦۷۵).

Mosquitoes coming and falling upon the electric device of their own accord does not fall under the ruling of burning, just as throwing a dead animal far away and animals coming and eating it themselves does not fall under the ruling of feeding carrion. Consider the following:

Fatāwā Bazzāziyyah records:

ولا يحمل الجيفة إلى الهرة ويحمل الهرة إلى الجيفة. (الفتاوى البزازية بهامش الفتاوى الهندية: ٤/۸۲).

In Nafʿ al-Muftī wa al-Sāʾil, ʿAllāmah ʿAbd al-Ḥayy al-Laknawī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

ثم إن كان لا بد من سقي الخمر فرساً لا يشربه بل يضع الخمر بين يديه ليشربه، كما أن لا ينبغي أن يؤكل الميتة الكلب إلا بأن يضع الميتة بين يدي الكلب فيأكله بنفسه

(نفع المفتي والسائل، ص ٤۷۲).
(وكذا في نفع المفتي والسائل، ص ٤٦۹). (وكذا في المحيط البرهاني: ٦/۱۰۳).
وفي الشامية: قال بعض المشايخ لو قاد الدابة إلى الخمر لا بأس به ولو نقل إلى الدابة يكره. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/٤٤۹).

That is: it is not correct to carry mosquitoes to the electric device, and likewise it is not correct to move the device about over the mosquitoes; but it is correct to fix the electric device in one place so that the mosquitoes go towards it of their own accord.

ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) has permitted burning with fire at a time of need.

See Fatāwā Shāmī, which states:

لكن جواز التحريق والتغريق مقيد كما في شرح السير بما إذا لم يتمكنوا من الظفر بهم بدون ذلك بلا مشقة عظيمة فإن تمكنوا بدونها فلا يجوز.

(Fatāwā al-Shāmī: 4/129, Saʿīd).

Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥal states:

Question: An electric device is used for killing mosquitoes. Inside it there is a light from a fluorescent tube, and above it is a mesh through which an extremely powerful electric current runs. As soon as a mosquito tries to go near the light, it has to pass through this electrified mesh; and because the current in it is extremely powerful, the mosquitoes are burnt. What is the ruling of the Sharīʿah on using it?

Answer: It is permissible. (Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥal: 4/246, old edition).

If there is no other easy method of killing bed-bugs, it is correct to pour boiling water over them.

See Imdād al-Fatāwā, which states: Question: What is the ruling if a person pours hot water over a charpoy in order to get rid of the bed-bugs in it?

Answer:

في رد المحتار: كيفية القتال من كتاب الجهاد تحت قول الدر المختار وحرقهم ما نصه: لكن جواز التحريق والتغريق مقيد كما في الشرح السير بما إذا لم يتمكنوا من الظفر بهم بدون ذلك بلا مشقة عظيمة فان تمكنوا لا يجوز.

From this we learn that if there is no other easy method of getting rid of bed-bugs, then pouring boiling hot water over them is correct; otherwise it is forbidden. (Imdād al-Fatāwā: 4/263).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah records: Question: When bed-bugs cause distress, is it permissible to kill them by pouring boiling water over the bed or cot, or not?

Answer: When they cause distress and do not yield to any other means, then it is correct to pour boiling hot water over the charpoy. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 18/280, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).
