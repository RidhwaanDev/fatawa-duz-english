### TITLE
The requirement that socks be thick in order to be wiped over

### QUESTION
These days people wipe over thin socks and say to us: if wiping over socks is permissible, where do you get the condition that they must be thick and heavy? Is what they say correct?

### ANSWER
(1) In this connection it must be understood that what is really wiped over is leather socks, or those cloth socks that take the ruling of leather socks — and those are the thick ones.

(2) The hadiths point to this condition. Consider:

بعث رسول الله ﷺ بعثاً فأصابهم البرد فأمرهم أن يمسح على العصائب والتساخين. (صحيح: رواه احمد، رقم: ۵۷۷، وأبو داود، رقم: ۱٤۱، والحاكم، رقم: ۱٦۹، وصححه).

(ʿAṣāʾib means turbans, and tasākhīn means warm socks or foot coverings. Wiping over the turban is either abrogated, or what is meant is a supplementary wiping.)

(3) عن سعيد بن المسيب قال وروى شعبة عن قتادة عن سعيد بن المسيب والحسن انهما قالا يمسح على الجوربين إذا كان صفيقين. (مصنف ابن أبي شيبه ۱/ ۱۸۸)

(4) In earlier times people wore warm, thick socks to keep out the cold, and they used to wipe over them.

The research and explanation of ʿAllāmah Shams al-Ḥaqq Afghānī (may Allah have mercy on him):

On the question of thick socks, consider the following passage of the books of fiqh, from Radd al-Muḥtār, p. 188, under the discussion of thick socks:

بحيث يمشي فرسخاً ويثبت على الساق بنفسه ولا يري ما تحته ولا ينشف (الدر) وفي الدر وفي بعض الكتب ينشف وفسر في الخانية الأول بأن لا يشف الجورب الماء إلى نفسه كالأديم والصرم وفسر الثاني بأن لا يجاوز الماء إلى القدم وقال تحت "بنفسه" أي من غير شد

This passage shows that for wiping over thick socks to be permissible, real thickness must be present, and this is established by three things: (1) a man can walk three shariah miles or more in them without shoes; (2) they cling to the shin without being tied; (3) if water is poured on them, it cannot get inside.

All three of these together — and the third in particular — are not found in nylon socks, so wiping over them is not valid. Caution here is all the more necessary because the Noble Qur'an mentions "غسل الرجلين", which is decisive, while the hadiths on wiping over leather socks are mass-transmitted or well known and are therefore sufficient to restrict it. As for wiping over cloth socks alone, in my view there are no hadiths on that subject at the level of fame that are صحيح السند، صريح الدلالة، and for an analogy with leather socks a strong likeness and resemblance to them is required. (Fatāwā Farīdiyyah: 2/104).
