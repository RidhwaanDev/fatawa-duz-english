### TITLE
Hiring a strong man for a known task

### QUESTION
Zayd has a house that ʿAmr has been renting for a long time, and ʿAmr pays the rent for it. Now that the term of the agreement has ended, Zayd wants to evict ʿAmr from the house, because Zayd needs the house himself. ʿAmr is not willing to leave. Can Zayd pay a fixed fee to a strong man to remove ʿAmr, or not?

### ANSWER
Once the term has ended, ʿAmr's staying on by force is a wrong, so it is valid to pay a fee to a strong man to evict ʿAmr. Engaging a man for a specific, lawful and clearly defined task is permissible in the sharia.
