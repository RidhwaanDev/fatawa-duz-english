### TITLE

The ruling on a Muslim woman taking employment in the police department

### QUESTION

A Muslim woman lives in France. She wishes to ask whether employment in the police department is permissible or not; that is, is it permissible for a woman to become a police officer or not? بینوا بالبرہان توجروا عند الرحمن ۔

### ANSWER

Employment in the police is impermissible for a Muslim woman on account of the following evils:
1. Coming before people without ḥijāb.
2. The presence of mixing and mingling with strange men.
3. Acquiring this training from men, because it can only be acquired from men.
4. Displaying her adornment and beauty before non-maḥrams.
5. Wearing the sort of tight and close-fitting police uniform which reveals the shape of a woman's private parts; in it there is also resemblance to men, which is forbidden.
6. In this employment there is seclusion with men, which is unlawful.
7. The occurrence of negligence or difficulty in discharging the obligations of the religion.
8. The occurrence of difficulty and negligence in carrying out domestic responsibilities, especially in night duty.
9. In police employment the dangers for a woman are greater.
10. The awe and authority, and the sternness at the time of need, which police employment requires — by nature these qualities are not found in women. In a narration of Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim it is stated:

عن أبي هريرة رضي الله عنه قال: قال رسول الله ﷺ: الإيمان بضع وسبعون شعبة الحياء شعبة من الإيمان. (صحيح مسلم: ۱/٤۷، باب عدد شعب الإيمان).
قال الشيخ الملا علي القاري في المرقاة: والمراد به الحياء الإيماني وهو خلق يمنع الشخص من الفعل القبيح بسبب الإيمان كالحياء عن كشف العورة والجماع بين الناس. (مرقاة المفاتيح: ۱/۷۰، كتاب الإيمان).

In Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī there is the narration:

عن ابن عباس رضي الله عنه قال: لعن رسول الله ﷺ المتشبهين من الرجال بالنساء والمتشبهات من النساء بالرجال. (صحيح البخاري، رقم: ۵۸۸۵).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah records:
If a woman takes employment in a company, several forbidden things will be committed: there will be a lack of veiling; there will be mixing with non-maḥram men, and on some occasions the opportunity for seclusion and privacy may also arise; there will be informal conversation with them and coming and going with non-maḥram men, and so forth. For this reason permission for such employment cannot be given in the Sharīʿah. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۵/٤۵۸) ۔
Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥal states:
The answer: Allah Most High has made the sphere of activity of the man and the woman separate. The field of a woman's work is her home, and the man's field of action is outside the home. The work a man can do, a woman cannot do; and what a woman can do, a man cannot do. Both should work while remaining within their own spheres. Those who place the burden of the man upon the frail shoulders of the woman are doing injustice to the woman. (آپ کے مسائل اور ان کا حل: ۸/۵۵) ۔
واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
