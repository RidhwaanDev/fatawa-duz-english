### TITLE
The ruling on watching educational videos on the internet

### QUESTION
I am a university student. Sometimes a subject is not understood in the lecture hall — a subject in medicine, for example — so I look for that lesson on the internet on YouTube (youtube), a place where various videos are readily available; I search out what relates to my lesson and listen to it and watch it. My question is: will it be permissible for me to use YouTube for these lessons or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
Concerning the pictures that appear on video and on television there is disagreement among the scholars. Some scholars regard them as a reflection, and so, while keeping away from indecent pictures, there is room to view what one needs.

The references for this issue have already been given above and may be consulted there.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
