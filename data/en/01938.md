### TITLE
Buying and selling the fish in a pond

### QUESTION
I am a fishmonger and buy fish from various places. Which of these ways of buying are permissible?

(1) I buy the fish after they have been taken out of the pond.

(2) I buy the fish while they are still in the pond, since the pond was made for fish in the first place, and because the pond is small they are easy to catch; the land of the pond belongs to its owner.

(3) If the pond is large, so that catching the fish takes real effort, what is the ruling on buying them then?

(4) The pond is not owned by anyone, but certain people have taken control of it: they give permission for the fish in the pond to be caught and collect a levy of their own for it.

(5) The pond is not owned, but someone has made arrangements to bring water to it. (Please state the rulings on all of these briefly.)

### ANSWER
(1) It is permissible to buy the fish after they have been taken out of the pond, since one is then able to take hold of them easily.

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

وفسد بيع سمك لم يصد أو صيد ثم ألقي في مكان لا يؤخذ منه إلا بحيلة وإن أخذ بدونها صح. (الدر المختار: ۵/٦۰، سعيد).
وفي حاشية ابن عابدين: وأنه (السمك) يملك بالقبض. (فتاوى الشامي: ۵/٦۰، سعيد).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states: Question: If the fish are taken out and sold to a fisherman, is there any objection or impediment in the sharia? Answer: To take the fish out oneself and sell them to a fisherman is valid. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 24/33, Maktabah Maḥmūdiyyah).

(2) This is permissible.

Al-Hidāyah states:

ولوكان يؤخذ بغير حيلة اصطياد جاز. (الهداية: ۳/۵۱. وكذا في الفتاوى الهندية: ۳/۱۱۳. وكذا في فتاوى الشامي: ۵/٦۰، سعيد).

(3) When the fish cannot be caught easily, buying them is not permissible, because their delivery cannot be guaranteed.

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

وفسد بيع سمك لم يصد أو صيد ثم ألقي في مكان لا يؤخذ منه إلا بحيلة. وفي الشامية: قوله فلو سده ملكه أي فيصح بيعه إن أمكن أخذه بلا حيلة وإلا فلا، لعدم القدرة على التسليم. (الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ۵/٦۰، مطلب في البيع الفاسد، سعيد).
وفي رد المحتار: أو بحيلة لم يجز لأنه وان كان مملوكاً فليس مقدور التسليم. (رد المحتار: ۵/٦۱، مطلب في البيع الفاسد، سعيد).

Al-Hidāyah states:

ولا في حظيرة إذا كان لا يؤخذ إلا بصيده لأنه غير مقدور التسليم. (الهداية: ۳/۵۱).

(4) Two things are necessary for anything to be sold: first, that the thing being sold belongs to the seller; and second, that handing it over and delivering it is possible.

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states: There are three ways in which a person comes to own fish. (1) He has a pond dug for fish and fish come into it. (2) The ground was not prepared for that purpose, but once the fish came in with the water the way out was blocked. (3) He himself put fish into the water so that they would breed. In all three cases the fish are treated as capable of delivery only when they can be obtained without having to be hunted. (Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 4/306).

So, in the case in question, if the people who have taken control of the pond have adopted any one of the three arrangements mentioned above, they own the fish, and buying from them is permissible and valid.

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states: It is not valid to buy or sell the fish in a pond or the like without taking them out, unless the fish are so numerous that obtaining them requires no hunting. (Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 4/316).

(5) If the arrangement to bring water to the pond was made with the object of obtaining fish, and the way out is blocked once the fish have come in, then the fish belong to that person; and if the fish can then be obtained easily, buying and selling them is permissible as well.

Fatḥ al-Qadīr states:

وإن لم يكن أعدها لذلك لا يملك ما يدخل فيها فلا يجوز بيعه لعدم الملك إلا أن يسد الحظيرة إذا دخل فحينئذٍ يملكه ثم ينظر إن كان يؤخذ بلا حيلة جاز بيعه وإلا لا يجوز. (فتح القدير: ٦/٤۱۰، دار الفكر، وكذا في فتاوى الشامي: ۵/٦۱، سعيد).

For further detail see: Imdād al-Fatāwā: 3/46–50; Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 6/480; Imdād al-Aḥkām: 3/365; Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 4/301–316.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
