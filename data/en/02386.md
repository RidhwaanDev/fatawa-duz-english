The kinds of fish

### QUESTION
The kinds of fish

### ANSWER
(Consider a few kinds of fish in the table)

This fish prevents large boats from moving in the sea, and for this reason those on the boat throw towards it cloths stained with menstrual blood, whereupon the fish flees and does not come near the boat — as though menstruation were the means of warding it off. It is for this reason that it is called ḥūt al-ḥayḍ.
