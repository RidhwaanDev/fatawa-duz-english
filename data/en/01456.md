### TITLE
The ruling on video calls

### QUESTION
The ruling on video calls.

### ANSWER
If a video call is free of what the sacred law forbids — for instance looking at someone who is not a close relative, or the person at the other end being immodestly dressed — then in itself it is permissible to use, because the image that appears on the screen is a reflection of the real form and not a picture. Nowadays, however, this facility mostly involves frivolous and forbidden things, so one should try to keep away from it.
