### TITLE
Medical insurance

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to take out medical insurance or not?

### ANSWER
Medical insurance is much the same as medical aid, so its ruling too will be like that of medical aid: if the company provides medical treatment in exchange for the sum paid and does not hand the money back, it is sound and valid; but if it pays the money over, it is impermissible. Likewise the company's business and its sources of income must for the most part be lawful; if most of its sources of earning are unlawful, it is impermissible in that case too. For the evidence, see the detailed ruling on medical aid given above.
