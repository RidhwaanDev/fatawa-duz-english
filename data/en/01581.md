### TITLE
Annulment and separation on account of discord between the spouses

### QUESTION
The substance of the question and a summary of the difficulties are as follows.

If the account given by the wife and her family is true, then the complaints on the strength of which the wife and her family wish to have the marriage annulled are these:

(1) The husband does not speak to his wife for days on end and keeps her in mental distress.

(2) Even after she left with his permission, the husband gives her no maintenance.

(3) Children are desirable both in the sharia and by natural inclination. The hadith "تزوجوا الودود الولود" (رواه أبو داود), the verse "نسائكم حرث لكم" (سورة البقرة: الآية: ۲۲۳), the supplications of the prophets (peace be upon them) and countless similar texts make this plain; yet the husband regards children as a burden and either tries to prevent them or shows his dislike of them.

(4) The husband is utterly weary of his wife, so much so that he will not even tolerate any contact with her relatives; and as a result of this she too has grown weary of him, and so wishes to live apart from him.

(5) Out of this weariness the husband wants a divorce, but from natural greed, or covetousness, or with the intention of harassing his wife, he demands the large sum of two hundred thousand, which is an unreasonable demand.

(6) The husband is so obstinate by nature that he kept away even from visiting his own father when he was ill, and would not even attend his funeral prayer.

### ANSWER
The office-bearers of the Jamʿiyyah are requested to look into these matters. If these allegations are indeed correct then, in my view, if on account of them the husband is unwilling to divorce her, and is also unwilling to accept a khulʿ by taking back what he gave as mahr (dower), the Jamʿiyyah may annul the marriage. The great muftis have held that abusing and insulting a wife is a ground for separation, and not speaking to her for days at a time causes even greater distress than that.

Majmūʿah Qawānīn-e Islāmī states:

If the husband abuses and insults his wife in a way that brings her extreme humiliation and distress, or beats her severely, she has the right to seek a separation. The Noble Qur'an says:

﴿ولا تمسكوهن ضراراً لتعتدوا، ومن يفعل ذـلك فقد ظلم نفسه﴾. (سورة البقرة: ۲۳۱).

Clearly, what could be more harmful than a severe beating and abuse? (مجموعہ قوانین اسلامی، ص: ۱۹۹، دفعہ ۸۱)

Likewise, if there is discord (shiqāq) between the spouses which cannot be put right, a separation may also be granted.

An examination of the word shiqāq:

Shiqāq means enmity, hostility and opposition. As it is a verbal noun of the third form, its sense is that two parties fall into such mutual opposition that one stands on one side, at one extreme, and the other on the other side — that is, the enmity, hostility and opposition between two people has reached its furthest point. Imām Rāghib expresses this in his Mufradāt in these words:

الشقاق: المخالفۃ وکونک في شق غیر شق صاحبک ۔ (المفردات في غريب القرآن، ص ۲٦٤).

When discord arises between husband and wife, the ruling of the sharia upon it is found in the Noble Qur'an:

﴿وإن خفتم شقاق بينهما فابعثوا حكماً من أهله وحكماً من أهلها، إن يريدا إصلاحاً يوفق اللّه بينهما، إن اللّه كان عليما خبيراً﴾ ۔ (سورة النساء: ۳۵).

This discord is general in scope. Whether it has arisen between husband and wife because the husband beats her without cause, or because he uses her property unlawfully, or because he forces her to give up modest dress and adopt the nakedness of present-day fashion, or because he compels and coerces her into other forbidden things, or because he prevents her from discharging her obligatory and necessary duties, or because of similar matters — for all of these the command of the Qur'an is that the discord be removed through two arbitrators. (کتاب الفسخ و التفریق: ص: ۱۵۲)

Dr Tanzīl al-Raḥmān says in Majmūʿah Qawānīn-e Islām:

The literal meaning of shiqāq is "difference". The word is derived from shiqq, which means "a side". Since mutual difference divides husband and wife into two sides, the Noble Qur'an describes this state of affairs as shiqāq — much as Urdu speaks of nāchāqī, a falling-out. (مجموعہ قوانین اسلام: ۲/٦۴۴، بحوالہ اسلامی قانون نکاح و طلاق، از مولانا یعقوب قاسمی صاحب ص ۱۳۳)

ʿAllāmah al-Nasafī says:

الشقاق: العداوة، لأن كلا منهما يفعل ما يشق على صاحبه أو يميل إلى شق أي ناحية غير شق صاحبه. (تفسير النسفي: ۱/۲۲٤. وكذا في التفسير المنير: ۵/۵۸. وأحكام القرآن للجصاص: ۲/۱۹۰).

ʿAllāmah al-Ālūsī says:

والشقاق: الخلاف والعداوة، واشتقاقه من الشق، وهو الجانب، لأن كلاً من المتخالفين في شق غير شق الآخر. (روح المعاني: ۵/۲٦).

وفيه أيضاً: واختلف في أنهما (أي الحكمين) هل يليان الجمع والتفريق إن رأيا ذلك؟ فقيل: لهما، وهو المروي عن على كرم الله وجهه وابن عباس رضي الله عنه ما، وإحدى الروايتين عن ابن جبير، وبه قال الشعبي، فقد أخرج الشافعي في الأم والبيهقي في السنن وغيرهما عن عبيدة السلماني قال: جاء رجل وامرأة إلى على كرم الله وجهه ومع كل واحد منها فئام من الناس، فأمرهم على كرم الله وجهه أن يبعثوا حكماً من أهله وحكماً من أهلها، ثم قال للحكمين: تدريان ما عليكما؟ عليكما إن رأيتما أن تجمعا أن تجمعا، وإن رأيتما أن تفرقا أن تفرقا، قالت المرأة: رضيت بكتاب الله تعالى بما على فيه ولي، وقال الرجل: أما الفرقة فلا، فقال على كرم الله وجهه: كذبت والله حتى تقر بمثل الذي أقرت به. (روح المعاني: ۵/۲٦).

وفي التفسير المظهري: فقال مالك: يجوز لحكم الزوج أن يطلق المرأة بدون رضا الزوج، ولحكم المرأة أن يختلع بدون رضا المرأة، ويجب عليها المال إذا رأي الصلاح في ذلك حيث ملك على الحكمين الجمع والتفريق، وكذب الزوج على نفي الفرقة

الخ. (التفسير المظهري: ۲/۱۰۱، بلوجستان).

Majmūʿah Qawānīn-e Islāmī states:

If such intense aversion arises between the spouses that it does not appear possible for them to live married life while keeping within the limits set by Allah, then in such a case:

(a) The qāḍī will appoint two arbitrators, so that a way to reconciliation may be found.

(b) If, in spite of the arbitration, no way is found either of putting matters right or of separating by mutual consent, then the qāḍī will, on the ground of discord, grant a separation upon the wife's application.

Explanation: If difference and discord arise between the spouses — that is, if for some reason such intense aversion arises between them that no room is left for living together well and remaining within the limits set by Allah becomes difficult — then in such a case an attempt at reconciliation will first be made, and two arbitrators will be appointed for that purpose. The arbitrators will try either to remove this ill-feeling or to bring about a separation with the consent of both. If this attempt too fails, the separation will be effected through the qāḍī. It should be clear that Allah's command is that the spouses live together honourably, and for this love is required on both sides. The possibility of living together honourably comes to an end when the spouses begin to hate one another; and if even one of them begins to hate the other, that too will be counted as discord, since the willingness of both is necessary for living together honourably, and the absence of love and willingness on one side alone is enough to destroy it. So whether both hate each other, or the woman for any reason is overcome by intense aversion, the qāḍī should appoint arbitrators to find a way of setting matters right. And if, despite their efforts, matters cannot be set right and the marriage is emptied of its purposes — that is, if keeping her honourably becomes extremely difficult — then it is the husband's duty to release her with kindness; but if he avoids doing so, the qāḍī will grant the separation as the husband's deputy. (مجموعہ قوانینِ اسلامی: ۲۰۰، ۲۰۲، دفعہ ۸۲، مسلم پرسنل لا بورڈ)

Majmūʿah Qawānīn-e Islām states:

A separation granted between husband and wife by a court (or by a body standing in its place, such as the Jamʿiyyat al-ʿUlamāʾ) on the ground of oppression or of a falling-out is, in its legal effect, "one irrevocable divorce": "و الفراق في ذلک طلاق بائن" ۔

(مجموعہ قوانین اسلام، از ڈاکٹر تنزیل الرحمن صاحب: ۲/٦۷۵، بحوالہ اسلامی قانون نکاح و طلاق، ۱۳۳)

The conditions for the two arbitrators:

In appointing the arbitrators, full regard should be paid to the restrictions and conditions which must be observed in the Mālikī school. Al-Aḥwāl al-Shakhṣiyyah states:

ويشترط في الحكمين عندهم أربعة شروط: وهي الذكورة، والعدالة، والرشد، والعلم بما هو بسبيله، فلا يجوز تحكيم النساء ولا الصبيان ولا العبيد ولا المجانين ولا الكفار ولا الفسقة ولا السفهاء، ولا من لا علم عندهم بأحكام النشوز والصلح. ثم إن وجد حكمان من أهل الزوجين وأمكن تحكيمهما وجب تحكيمهما، ولم يجز للقاضي أن يبعث أجنبيين. ويندب كون الحكمين من جيران الزوجين، ولا يشترط رضاء الزوجين بما يحكمان به. (الأحوال الشخصية: ۴۰۸).

According to the Mālikīs four conditions must be met in the arbitrators:

(1) That he be male.

(2) That he be upright.

(3) That he be of sound judgement.

(4) That he be acquainted with the questions involved in the matter for which he is being appointed arbitrator.

It is therefore not permissible to appoint a woman, a child, an insane person, a disbeliever, an openly sinful person or a person of unsound judgement as an arbitrator; nor is it permissible to appoint anyone who does not know the rulings on marital discord and reconciliation.

Further, if two arbitrators are available from the families of the spouses and it is possible to appoint them, then appointing them is necessary, and it is not permissible for the qāḍī to send two outsiders. It is recommended that both arbitrators be neighbours of the spouses; and it is not a condition that the spouses agree to what the arbitrators decide. (ملخص از "کتاب الفسخ و التفریق" ص ۱۵۷)

Al-Mawsūʿah al-Fiqhiyyah states:

ذهب الفقهاء إلى أنه يشترط في الحكمين العدالة، والفقه بأحكام النشوز، واختلفوا في اشتراط الذكورة والحرية، وذلك في الجملة، ولهم تفصيل:

قال المالكية: شرط الحكمين الذكورة والرشد والعدالة والفقه بما حكما فيه، وبطل حكم غير العدل، وهو الفاسق والصبي والمجنون بإبقاء أو بطلاق بغير مال أو بمال في خلع، وبطل حكم سفيه "وهو المبذر في الشهوات ولو مباحة على المذهب" وحكم امرأة، وحكم غير فقيه بأحكام النشوز ما لم يتشاور العلماء في ما يحكم به، فإن حكم بما أشاروا به عليه كان حكمه نافذاً. (الموسوعة الفقهية الكويتية: ۴۷/۱۱، وزارة الأوقاف والشؤون الإسلامية).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
