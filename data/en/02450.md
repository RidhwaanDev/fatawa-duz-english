### TITLE
Sacrifice for partners in a joint business

### QUESTION
If several people are partners in a business and some money is earned from that business, and no single one of them individually has wealth to the amount of the niṣāb although the total sum does reach the niṣāb, upon whom does the sacrifice become necessary?

### ANSWER
If each individual's share amounts to the niṣāb, or he becomes an owner of the niṣāb when it is added to his other wealth, then the sacrifice is necessary upon that individual. But if it does not reach the niṣāb, the sacrifice will not be necessary. In short, the total sum is not what counts; what counts is each individual owning wealth to the amount of the niṣāb.
