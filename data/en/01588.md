### TITLE
Annulment on account of an illicit relationship with a strange woman

### QUESTION
A woman states that her husband keeps up an illicit relationship with a married woman who is a stranger to him, and that because of this he pays no attention whatever to the home and the children; indeed, he spends most of his free time with that woman. He also beats her, subjects her to mental pressure, and taunts and reproaches her. He does not give the full amount for household expenses; rather, it is usually my father who makes up what is needed. He has also not paid the children's medical costs. On all these grounds, may the woman demand the annulment of the marriage or not?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, if the woman's statement is established as correct, she will have the right to demand a divorce; and if no divorce is forthcoming, she should bring her case before the Jamʿiyyat al-ʿUlamāʾ. After investigating the matter, if the husband neither fulfils her rights nor desists from the illicit relationship, they will be authorised to annul the marriage.

Majmūʿah Qawānīn-e Islāmī states:

If the husband speaks ill of his wife or abuses her in a way that brings her extreme humiliation and distress, or beats her severely, she will have the right to a separation.

The Noble Qur'an says:

﴿ولا تمسكوهن ضراراً لتعتدوا، ومن يفعل ذـلك فقد ظلم نفسه﴾. (سورة البقرة: ۲۳۱).

Clearly, what could be more harmful than a severe beating and abuse? (مجموعہ قوانین اسلامی، ص: ۱۹۹، دفعہ ۸۱) ۔

It also states:

If such intense hatred arises between the spouses that it does not appear possible for them to live a married life while maintaining the limits set by Allah, then in such a case:

(a) The qāḍī will appoint arbitrators, so that a way to reconciliation may be found.

(b) If, despite the arbitration, no way is found either to put matters right or to separate by mutual consent, then the qāḍī will, on the ground of discord, effect a separation if the wife demands it. (مجموعہ قوانین اسلامی، ص: ۲۰۰، دفعہ ۸۲) ۔

For further evidence see the question on annulment and separation on account of discord (shiqāq).
