### TITLE
Zakat on an artificial nose or ear made of gold or silver

### QUESTION
Someone has had an artificial nose or ear made of gold or silver fitted because he needed it. Is zakat due on it or not?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, if the artificial parts made of gold or silver are attached in such a way that they can easily be detached and taken out, then zakat is necessary on them. But if they cannot be taken out, then, because they have become like a limb of the person himself, zakat is not necessary.

Consider what Sunan al-Tirmidhī states:

عن عمرو بن شعيب عن أبيه عن جده، أن امرأتين أتتا رسول الله ﷺ وفي أيديهما سواران من ذهب، فقال لهما: أتؤديان زكاته، فقالتا: لا، فقال لهما رسول الله ﷺ: أتحبان أن يسوركما الله بسوارين من نار، قالتا: لا، قال: فأديا زكاته. (ترمذي شريف: ۱/۱۳۸، باب ما جاء في زكاة الحلي)

Imdād al-Fatāwā states:

A nose is made of gold and fixed on the face, and this nose can be detached without difficulty; zakat is therefore necessary on it. But gold that is fitted into a tooth or used to fill it cannot be detached in that way, so zakat is not necessary on it. (امداد الفتاوی: ۲/٤۹، سونے کی بنائی ہوئی ناک یادانتوں پر زکوٰۃ) ۔

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

In certain circumstances, for some particular benefit, artificial parts of gold or silver are used — for example a nose, teeth, filling hollow teeth with gold or silver, binding a tooth with gold wire, and the like. The ruling on them is that if they can easily be taken out, zakat is necessary on them, just as Rasūlullāh ﷺ declared zakat necessary on jewellery. But if they cannot easily be taken out, having been fitted permanently so that they become a part of the person's body that cannot be separated from it, then they have entered into a person's basic necessities, and zakat is not necessary on such things. (ملخص از جدید فقہی مسائل: ۱/۲۰۸، سونے چاندی کے مصنوعی اعضاء پر زکوٰۃ، نعیمیہ) ۔
