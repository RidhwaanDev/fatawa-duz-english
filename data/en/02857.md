### TITLE
The ruling on listening to music along with devotional songs

### QUESTION
What do the scholars of the religion say concerning those poems and devotional songs (anāshīd) which also include music, wind instruments and other musical instruments? Will it be permissible to listen to such songs or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, listening to poems and devotional songs that are accompanied by music and wind instruments is forbidden and impermissible. The unlawfulness of music and of musical instruments has already been made clear in the detailed fatwa above, so there is no need to repeat the proofs here; they may be consulted there. Consider one further reference:

Shaykh ʿAbd al-Malik ibn ʿAbd al-Raḥmān al-Saʿdī states in Masāʾil Fiqhiyyah Muʿāṣirah:

الجواب: الأصوات الحديثية تنقسم إلى قسمين:
قسم يشبه صوت الدف فهو حلال؛ لأن صوت الدف مأذون فيه.
وقسم يشبه صوت المعازف والمزامير فهو محرم؛ لأن النبي ﷺ نهى عن الاستماع لصوتهما

(مسائل فقهية معاصرة، ص ۲۷٤، ط: دار النور، عمان)

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
