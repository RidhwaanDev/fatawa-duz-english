### TITLE
Pointing out a few useful treatises concerning Shab-i Barāʾat

### QUESTION
Number 12: Of the treatises that have been written in Urdu concerning Shaʿbān, please point out a few, for the purpose of study or of telling friends and companions about them.

### ANSWER
Studying the following few well-known treatises relating to the merits and rulings of Shab-i Barāʾat is beneficial:

1. Sāl Bhar ke Masnūn Aʿmāl, by Ḥakīm al-Ummat Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ashraf ʿAlī Thānawī (may Allah have mercy on him). (p. 17, published by Idārah-i Islāmiyyāt, Lahore).

2. Shab-i Barāʾat, by Muftī Ḥabīb al-Raḥmān Khayrābādī. (Published by Marghūb Book Depot, Deoband).

3. Shab-i Barāʾat kī Ḥaqīqat, by Muftī Muḥammad Taqī ʿUthmānī. (Published by Maiman Islamic Publishers).

4. Shaʿbān al-Muʿaẓẓam, by Ḥāfiẓ Tanwīr Aḥmad Sharīfī. (Published by Maktabah Rashīdiyyah).

5. Bārah Mahīnōn ke Faḍāʾil wa Aḥkām, by Muftī Sayyid ʿAbd al-Karīm Gumthalwī, authorised in the spiritual pledge by Ḥakīm al-Ummat. (p. 30, published by Idārah-i Islāmiyyāt, Lahore).

6. Shaʿbān wa Shab-i Barāʾat ke Faḍāʾil wa Aḥkām, by Muftī Muḥammad Riḍwān. (Idārah-i Ghufrān, Rawalpindi, Pakistan).
