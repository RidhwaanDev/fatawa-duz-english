### TITLE
Omitting the glorifications of bowing and prostration

### QUESTION
In the hot season I get a fever (that is, an illness in which one sneezes continually). Once, during the tarāwīḥ prayer, I began sneezing continuously, so that I had no opportunity to say the glorifications of the bowing and the prostration. Was my prayer valid or not? Explain and be rewarded.

### ANSWER
The glorifications of the bowing and prostration in prayer are among the sunnahs of the prayer. If they are omitted on account of some excuse, the prayer is valid and no defect arises in it. If, however, they are omitted merely out of laziness, this gives rise to dislikedness — though the prostration of forgetfulness does not become due. See what is quoted in Fatāwā Shāmī:

وأما من حيث الرواية فالأرجح السنية لأنها المصرح بها في مشاهير الكتب، وصرحوا بأنه يكره أن ينقص عن الثلاث وأن الزيادة مستحبة بعد أن يختم على وتر خمس أو سبع أو تسع ما لم يكن إماماً

وأن حكم السنة أن يندب إلى تحصيلها ويلام على تركها مع حصول إثم يسير وهذا يفيد أن كراهة تركها فوق التنزيه وتحت المكروه تحريماً. (فتاوى الشامي: ۱/٤۹٤، سعيد).

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh states: If some sunnah of the prayer is missed out of forgetfulness, the prayer is not invalidated, the prostration of forgetfulness does not become necessary, and there is no sin. If it is left out deliberately, the prayer is still not invalidated and the prostration of forgetfulness does not become necessary, but the one who leaves it deserves blame and reproach. (عمدۃ الفقہ: ۲/۱۰۲، ط: زوار اکیڈمی) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
