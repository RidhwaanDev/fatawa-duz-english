### TITLE
Answers to several questions concerning mosques

### QUESTION
(1) There are several other mosques a short distance away which can easily be reached from our mosque. Is it necessary for us to arrange another place for prayer and for other religious activities such as tablīgh?

(2) The mosque owns a house which was vacated only recently and which is close to the mosque. We have thought of putting it to use. Can all the prayers be performed in it? And what is the position regarding the Friday prayer?

### ANSWER
(1) Since there are other mosques near your mosque which can be reached easily, it is not necessary for you to arrange another place.

(2) By your own account the mosque already owns this house, and a fair number of worshippers gather. If the house is put to use for the prayers and the five daily prayers are performed there, that is a commendable thing. The house is, however, far too small for the Friday prayer. The Friday prayer is one of the emblems of Islam, and its purpose is that the emblems of Islam be displayed; that calls for a large and impressive congregation. On this point, see the answer given in Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah:

The mosque in question is very small. For it to be kept alive it is enough that the call to prayer is given five times a day and that the prayers are performed in congregation; keeping a mosque alive does not depend on the Friday prayer being established in it. Leading the Friday prayer is one of the emblems of Islam. Its purpose is that the emblems of the religion be displayed on a grand scale, and that can only happen when the Friday prayer is performed with a large congregation. When the Friday prayer is performed with small congregations in one neighbourhood after another, the purpose of establishing it is lost, and the other mosques are affected too. Permission will therefore not be given to establish the Friday prayer in this mosque. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۵/۸۰) ۔

That said, if the Friday prayer is performed in this house, the Friday prayer will be valid. واللہ اعلم

Question (3): If the present mosque is closed and no other place is used, what responsibilities does the committee have towards the imam and the muezzin? Will they still be paid their full salary?

Answer (3): In that case the matter will be settled according to the terms of the contract — that is, according to whatever contract the mosque made with them. If they are kept on, the salary for that period will have to be paid. See Sharḥ al-ʿInāyah, which states:

"ولا فرق بين طويل المدة وقصيرها عندنا إذا كانت بحيث يعيش إليها العاقدان، لأن الحاجة التي جوّزت الإجارة قد تحسن إلى ذلك، وهي مدّة معلومة يعلم لها مقدار المنفعة فكانت صحيحة كالأجل في البيع." (شرح العناية: ۹/٦۳)

If arrangements are made for congregational prayer in the house and an imam and a muezzin are needed, then they will have to be paid a salary for that work.
