### TITLE
Dogs howling and raising a clamour at the time of the adhān

### QUESTION
In a certain mosque, when the muezzin begins to call the adhān, the dogs of the neighbourhood start barking and raising a clamour. What connection is there between the muezzin's adhān and the barking of the dogs? Kindly explain and be rewarded.

### ANSWER
It comes in the sound hadith that when the adhān begins, Satan flees so that he may not hear the sound of the adhān; and sometimes animals see Satan fleeing, whereupon they begin to shriek and howl.

Consider the following. It is narrated in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī:

عن أبي هريرة رضي الله عنه قال: قال رسول الله ﷺ: إذا أذن بالصلاة أدبر الشيطان له ضراط حتى لا يسمع التأذين فإذا سكت المؤذن أقبل فإذا ثوب أدبر فإذا سكت أقبل فلا يزال بالمرء يقول له: اذكر ما لم يكن يذكر حتى لا يدري كم صلي. (رواه البخاري، رقم: ۱۲۲۲، باب ما يفكر الرجل في الصلاة).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

On hearing the adhān a dog always howls and cries out in front of our madrasah, and this happens in other places too. This is nothing to be alarmed at. On hearing the adhān Satan flees, and sometimes certain animals see him; alarmed at this they howl and make a noise. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۵/٤٤۳، جامعہ فاروقیہ).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
