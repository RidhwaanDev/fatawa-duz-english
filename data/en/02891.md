### TITLE
Shooting stars pursuing the jinn

### QUESTION
Is it established from the Qur'an and the hadith that the jinn whom the angels strike with shooting stars are those jinn who try to listen in on the talk of the highest assembly? And did the striking of the devils begin after the birth of the Prophet ﷺ, or after his being raised and sent as a messenger? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
It is established from the Qur'an and the hadith that when the jinn try to listen in on the talk of the highest assembly, shooting stars pursue them; and according to the sounder view this began after the Prophet ﷺ was raised as a messenger. Shooting stars did exist before that, but this work was taken from them only after his being raised ﷺ. Some books of history do record that it began with his birth ﷺ, but that view is the weaker one. Or else the pelting was slight at the time of his birth and became very much greater after his being raised; or it was suspended at the time of his birth and has continued from the time of his being raised until now.

Consider what ʿAllāmah al-Ālūsī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

وثانياً وهو الحق: بأنها كانت موجودة قبل البعثة لا سباب أخر ولا تنكر ذلك إلا أنه لا ينافي أنها بعد البعثة قد توجد بسبب دفع الشياطين وزجرهم،

(روح المعاني: ۱٤/۲۵، دار التراث).

قال الإمام الرازي: المقام الثاني: وهو أقرب إلى الصواب أن هذه الشهب كانت موجودة قبل المبعث إلا أنها زيدت بعد المبعث وجعلت أكمل وأقوي، وهذا هو الذي يدل عليه لفظ القرآن، لأنه قال: ﴿فوجدناها ملئت﴾ [الجن: ۸]. وهذا يدل على أن الحادث هو الملء والكثرة الخ. (التفسير الكبير: ۱۵/۱۵۸، ط: دار الفكر).

For further detail see: Rūḥ al-Maʿānī, Sūrat al-Ḥijr and Sūrat al-Jinn; al-Tafsīr al-Kabīr, Sūrat al-Jinn; Tafsīr Ibn Kathīr, Sūrat al-Jinn; Tafsīr-i Maẓharī; Maʿārif al-Qurʾān; Sīrat-i Ḥalabiyyah: 1/168; Mawāhib-i Ladunniyyah; and Kashf al-Bārī, Kitāb al-Tafsīr, by Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Salīmullāh Khān.

From the verses of some of the poets of the age of ignorance it appears that shooting stars were cast down even before the Prophet was raised as a messenger. Consider the following:

قال العلامة الآلوسي: وجاء أيضاً في شعر الجاهلية: قال بشر بن أبي حازم:
والعير يلحقها الغبار وجحشها
٭
ينقض خلفهما انقضاض الكوكب.
وقال أوس بن حجر:
وانقض كالدري يتبعه
٭
نقع يثور تخاله طنبا. (روح المعاني: ۱٤/۲٤، دار التراث).

What is a shooting star?

On this the ancient philosophers say that when vapour reaches the sphere of fire, turns into fire and blazes up, that is a shooting star. It should be borne in mind that the ancient philosophers held that there is a sphere of fire beneath the sky. ʿAllāmah al-Ālūsī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

إن انقضاض الكواكب مذكور في كتب قدماء الفلاسفة وذكروا فيه أن الأرض إذا سخنت بالشمس ارتفع منها بخار يابس فإذا بلغ كرة النار احترق بها فتلك الشعلة هي الشهاب. (روح المعاني: ۱٤/۲٤).

See further: Hidāyat al-Ḥikmah with the commentary of Maybudhī, p. 37; and Falakiyyāt-i Jadīdah by Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Mūsā Rūḥānī Bāzī, p. 282.

Modern scientists say that meteors are countless small bodies whose size is generally no bigger than a chickpea or a walnut. These bodies move through the vastness of space at very great speed — at twenty or twenty-five miles a second, and some of them at forty miles a second or even more. A meteor begins to break up at a height of about a hundred miles above the earth's surface, and by the time it comes within five or ten miles of the earth it is burnt up and reduced to ash. Now and then, however, if a meteoric stone is large, some of its fragments reach the earth intact and become the cause of great destruction.

Countless meteoric stones of this kind are preserved in the various museums of the world. The largest meteoric stone in the world was found by a traveller in Greenland, who brought it to America; its weight is about thirty tons. In 1917 a meteor fell in Scotland; one fragment of it weighs twenty-two pounds. On 10 November 1492 a meteoric stone fell in France and sank five feet into the ground; its weight was three and a quarter maunds, and people still go to see it. In Mexico a meteoric stone is preserved which weighs eight hundred maunds; this piece is of iron. (فلکیاتِ جدیدہ، ص: ۲۷۱ ۔ ۲۸۰، از مولانا موسی روحانی بازی) ۔

The reason why a shooting star is called a star:

ʿAllāmah al-Ālūsī (may Allah have mercy on him) has said that a shooting star is called a star because of its resemblance to a star in shining and giving light; and it is also possible that these flames are broken off from certain stars and cast down. He accordingly writes:

والشهاب على ما قال الراغب؛ الشعلة الساطعة من النار الموقدة من العارض في الجو ويطلق على الكوكب ببريقه كشعلة النار

إلى قوله: وإطلاق الرجوم على النجوم وقولهم: رمي بالنجم يحتمل أن يكون مبنياً على الظاهر للرائي الخ. (روح المعاني: ۱٤/۲۳. ۲٦).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
