### TITLE
Selling food that was donated for hospitality at gatherings

### QUESTION
At our tablīghī gatherings we sell our food at a small profit. Sometimes, however, some food items are given to the gatherings free of charge by other people, by way of hospitality, so that people may be hosted. Can we sell such food and spend the money on other expenses, or not? Sometimes it happens that a person who has paid for the food does not receive any at one of the meals; what should be done in that case? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
Selling food at tablīghī gatherings is permissible and correct, even though gifts also come in from outside, because in most cases it is raw grain or edibles that are received from outside as gifts, and the organisers of the gathering bear the expenses of preparing them. Recovering the cost of these expenses is therefore permissible and correct.

Then, on many occasions one or two food items are received as a gift and other items are added to them and the meal is cooked; recovering their cost is also permissible and correct.

As for the money that is received, the organisers of the gathering have discretion over it and may spend it on other expenses and the like.

If, however, the food runs short and does not reach those who have paid, they should be asked to excuse it and told to come early at the next meal; and if they do not excuse it, the amount for one meal should be returned to them.

It is mentioned in the books of fiqh that if a tenant sublets to another tenant at a higher rent, the excess will be lawful in the case where the genus of the rent is different, or where he has spent something on the property; in such a case taking a higher rent is correct. Consider what ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

قوله لو أصلح فيها شيئاً، بأن جصصها أو فعل فيها مسناة وكذا كل عمل قائم لأن الزيادة بمقابلة ما زاد من عنده حملاً لا مره على الصلاح كما في المبسوط. (رد المحتار: ٦ / ۲۹، سعيد).

وفي الهندية: ولو زاد في الدار زيادة كما لو وتد فيها وتداً أو حفر فيها بئراً أو طيناً أو أصلح أبوابها أو شيئاً من حوائطها طابت له الزيادة. (الفتاوى الهندية: ٤/٤۲۵).

It emerges from the passages above that after carrying out some improvement or repair on another's property one may take extra rent in exchange for it. In the same way, the expense of preparing the food or edibles given as hospitality, and the other items that have been added to them, may be recovered in the form of a price.

Moreover, this money is also taken as a kind of contribution and by way of assistance in the affairs of the gathering, in which a degree of leniency is customary. For this reason too there is scope for it.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
