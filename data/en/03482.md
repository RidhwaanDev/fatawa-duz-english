### TITLE
The ruling on giving a fresh sunnah shroud to those exposed by a flood

### QUESTION
If, on account of a flood, some corpses come out of the ground, should they be given a fresh shroud, or is it sufficient to wrap them in a cloth and bury them? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
If the bodies of these dead are sound and intact, they will be given the sunnah shroud and buried; but if the body has already decomposed, it will be wrapped in some cloth and buried, and the sunnah shroud will not be given. Consider what is in al-Durr al-Mukhtār maʿa Radd al-Muḥtār:

وآدمي منبوش طري لم يتفسخ يكفن كالذي لم يدفن مرة بعد أخرى وإن تفسخ كفن في ثوب واحد. وفي الشامية: قوله منبوش طري أي بأن وجد منبوشاً بلا كفن، قوله لم يتفسخ قيد به لأنه لو تفسخ يكفن في ثوب واحد كما صرح به بعده. قوله كالذي لم يدفن أي يكفن في ثلاثة أثواب، قوله مرة بعد أخرى، أي لو نبش ثانياً وثالثاً وأكثر كفن كذلك ما دام طرياً من أصل ماله عندنا. (الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ۲/۲۰۵، سعيد).

For more, see:

فتح القدير: ۲/۱۱۳، دار الفكر، وبدائع الصنائع: ۱/۳۰۹، سعيد، والبحر الرائق: ۲/۱۷۷، والفتاوى السراجية، ص ۱۳۰.

It is mentioned in ʿUmdat al-Fiqh:

If someone was shrouded and buried and his shroud was stolen, then if he has been buried recently — that is, his corpse is still fresh and has not burst open — he should be given the sunnah shroud again from his own wealth; and if it is stolen again, he should be shrouded again; and as long as that corpse has not burst open, he should continue to be shrouded however many times the shroud is stolen. If his wealth has been distributed among the heirs, the value of the shroud should be recovered from them in proportion to their shares, because the deceased is still its owner. But if the corpse has already burst open, there is no need for the sunnah shroud; wrapping it in a single cloth is sufficient. (ʿUmdat al-Fiqh: 2/507).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
