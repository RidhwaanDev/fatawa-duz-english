### TITLE
Zakat on a poultry farm and on eggs

### QUESTION
A man has a broiler chicken farm, and the chickens are raised for trade. Is there a separate threshold for them or not, or will zakat be due on their value? (2) If a man does not trade in chickens but keeps chickens on a poultry farm in order to obtain eggs, will zakat be due on the chickens or on the eggs? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
No separate threshold has been fixed for chickens. When the year ends, zakat will be due on their value; that is, two and a half per cent of the value should be paid as zakat. (2) When he does not trade in the chickens, zakat is not due on the value of the chickens; rather zakat will be due on the income from the eggs. Consider the proofs:

قال في الهداية: الذكاة واجبة في عروض التجارة كائنة ما كانت إذا بلغت قيمتها نصاباً من الورق أو الذهب. (الهداية: ۱/۱۹۵، فصل في العروض، المصباح). (وكذا في الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱۷۹، ومختصر القدوري: ۱/۵۷، ط: بيروت، والفتاوى التاتارخانية: ۲/۲۳۷).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

There is no zakat on the land, building and related equipment of a poultry house or a fish pond. If, at the time of buying the hens and chicks, the intention is to sell those very birds, then zakat is obligatory on their value; but if the intention is to sell their eggs and their young rather than the birds themselves, then there is no zakat (on the hens). (احسن الفتاوی: ٤/۳۰۰) ۔

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

If you do not trade in the hens but trade only in their eggs, then there is no zakat on the capital itself; rather it is due on the income from the eggs, provided you own the threshold amount. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۳/٦۰۸) ۔
