### TITLE
Attending a walīmah feast in which evils are present

### QUESTION
If a walīmah feast involves evils such as singing, music, video cameras and the intermingling of men and women, may one attend such a feast or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, there is no room to attend such feasts.

Consider what al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq states:

وإن كان هناك لعب وغناء قبل أن يحضر فلا يحضر لأنه لا يلزمه الإجابة إذا كان هناك منكر لما روي عن علي رضي الله عنه قال: صنعت للنبي ﷺ طعاماً فدعوته له فحضر فرأي في البيت تصاوير فرجع. (البحر الرائق: ۸/۱۸۸، كتاب الكراهية، كوئته، كذا في الهداية: ٤/۴۵۵، كتاب الكراهية).

Kitāb al-Fatāwā states:

Singing, music, videography and photography are sins and acts of disobedience, and it is not permissible to attend a feast in which acts of disobedience are committed. The famous jurist ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him), seeing the abundance of open sinfulness in his own age, wrote that in our time one should not attend a feast until it is known that there will be no innovation or disobedience in it:

"والامتناع أصل في زماننا إلا إذا علم يقيناً أن لا بدعة ولا معصية". (رد المحتار: ۹/۵۰۱)

In our own era, all the more so, one should not attend until one is satisfied that such feasts are free of evils. If the sensible and conscientious people of society keep themselves away from such feasts, perhaps some reform of the community may come about. (کتاب الفتاوی: ٤/۴۱٤، نکاح میں دعوت اور ولیمہ کے احکام ۔ و فتاوی حقانیہ: ٤/۴۲۹)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
