### TITLE
Taking away the blanket provided on an aeroplane

### QUESTION
Is it correct to take away the blanket that is provided for use on an aeroplane if the air hostess gives permission, or not?

### ANSWER
It is not correct to take an aeroplane's blanket with the stewardess's permission, because she is not the owner of that blanket; the real owner is someone else. Moreover, it is written on it that it is to be returned after use.

Consider what al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

ومنها (شرائط الهبة) أن يكون مملوكاً للواهب فلا تجوز هبة مال الغير بغير إذنه لاستحالة تمليك ما ليس بمملوك للواهب كذا في البدائع. (الفتاوى الهندية: ٤/۳۷٤).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
