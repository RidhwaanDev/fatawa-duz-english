### TITLE
Trading in foreign currency

### QUESTION
What is the position of buying and selling foreign currency? If a Muslim carries on this trade and invites other Muslims to invest money in it, will that be called investing money in a lawful venture or not?

### ANSWER
Buying and selling foreign currency is permissible, and a difference in amount in it is permissible too, because the currencies of different countries are termed different in genus, and in our time a currency note is not a real medium of price but a conventional one. This business is therefore permissible and correct, and on that basis investing money in this business will also be correct, and such investment will be in a lawful venture.

The evidences for the ruling mentioned above have already been set out in detail earlier.
