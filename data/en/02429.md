### TITLE
Giving sacrificial meat to a non-Muslim

### QUESTION
May the meat of a sacrifice be given to a non-Muslim or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, the meat of a sacrifice may be given to a non-Muslim, though giving it to a Muslim is more virtuous and preferable. Giving from a sacrifice that was vowed, however, is not correct.

Consider what Al-Fatāwā al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah states:

ويهب منها ما شاء للغني والفقير والمسلم والذمي كذا في الغياثية. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳۰۰).

Iʿlāʾ al-Sunan states:

وللمضحي أن يهب كل ذلك أو يتصدق به أو يهديه لغني أو فقير مسلم أو كافر. (اعلاء السنن: ۱۷/۲۵۸، إدارة القرآن). (وكذا في حاشية الطحطاوي على الدر المختار: ٤/۱٦٦، كوئته).

وانظر للمزيد: (اعلاء السنن: ۱۷/۲۸۳، إدارة القرآن).

Ḥaḍrat Thānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

Although the sacrifice itself is obligatory, distributing the meat is not obligatory; it is therefore either a gift or a voluntary charity. A ḥarbī, where there is a benefit in it, comes under the ruling of a mustaʾmin (one granted safe conduct), and so it is permissible to give it to him. However, it is not permissible to give from a sacrifice whose meat it is obligatory to distribute. (امداد الفتاوی: ۳/۵۵۰)۔

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Muḥammad Yūsuf Ludhiyānwī, the martyr (may Allah have mercy on him), says:

Question: Can the meat of a sacrifice be given to a non-Muslim?

Answer: It can be given, provided it is not a vowed sacrifice. (آپ کے مسائل اور ان کا حل: ٤/۲۰۹)۔

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

If there is a genuine benefit in it, it may be given, but it is not preferable, since there is no shortage of poor people among the Muslims. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۵/٤۱۵)۔
