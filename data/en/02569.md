### TITLE
Abortion because of harm to the mother

### QUESTION
A woman has an abortion before four months because of a severe illness. Is an abortion before four months permitted on grounds of compulsion or not? If it is not done, there is a grave danger to the mother's life.

### ANSWER
Even after the limbs have been formed, and before the soul is breathed in, abortion is not permissible according to the sound position; but where there is a danger of the mother losing her life, abortion will be permitted in that case. This is just as the jurists have stated explicitly: when a child dies inside the mother's womb and there is no way of bringing it out, and if it is not brought out there is a danger of the mother losing her life, then in that case it is permissible to cut the child up and remove it in order to save the mother's life.

وفي النوادر: امرأة حامل اعترض الولد في بطنها ولا يمكن إلا بقطعه أرباعاً ولو لم يفعل ذلك يخاف على أمه من الموت فإن كان الولد ميتاً في البطن فلا بأس به وإن كان حياً لا يجوز لأن إحياء نفس بقتل نفس أخري لم يرد في الشرع. (البحر الرائق: ۸/۲۳۳).
