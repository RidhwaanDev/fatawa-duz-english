### TITLE
Becoming an agent for an insurance company

### QUESTION
Zayd is an insurance agent on behalf of several companies. Those companies do long-term and short-term insurance business, and it is obvious that the companies pay him commission for supplying them with customers. Most insurance companies are involved in interest-based business. Is such an agency permissible or not? And what is the ruling on taking the commission?

### ANSWER
Nowadays the business of most insurance companies comprises interest and gambling. An agency for an interest-based company is therefore not permissible, and the commission received from an interest-based company is also impermissible; one should keep away from it. Yes, as for companies whose business is for the most part lawful, there is room for taking part in them.

Consider what the Noble Qurʾān states:

﴿أحل الله البيع وحرم الربا﴾ (سورة البقرة).
وقال تعالى: ﴿يا أيها الذين آمنوا اتقوا الله وذروا ما بقي من الربا إن كنتم مؤمنين﴾ (سورة البقرة:).
وقال تعالى: ﴿ولا تعاونوا على الإثم والعدوان﴾ (سورة المائدة: ۲).
وعن جابر رضي الله عنه قال: لعن رسول الله ﷺ آكل الربا وموكله وكاتبه وشاهده وقال هم سواء. (رواه مسلم: ۲/۲۷، باب الربا).

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

Insurance is in essence not free of interest and gambling, so it is not permissible for a Muslim to take up an agency for an insurance company and to make it a means of livelihood. (Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 1/438; and Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥall: 6/258).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
