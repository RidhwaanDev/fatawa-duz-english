### TITLE
Offering a sacrifice every year

### QUESTION
Some people say: we offer a sacrifice on our own behalf every year, and this year we intend to offer the sacrifice on behalf of our deceased relatives. Is this claim of theirs acceptable, given that they are people who own the niṣāb, upon whom zakat, the Hajj and everything else are binding, and who do carry them out?

### ANSWER
Any Muslim man or woman who owns wealth to the amount upon which the sacrifice becomes necessary must offer a sacrifice every year for as long as that amount of wealth remains in his ownership. It is not enough to offer a sacrifice in one year only, or in a few years. It is just as if a man were to say: we fast every Ramadan, so this year we are not fasting. Would such a statement be acceptable? (فتاوی الشامی: ٦/ ۳۱۲) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
