### TITLE
The prosperous life of the Jews and the Noble Qur'an

### QUESTION
Allah Most High has said in the Noble Qur'an concerning the Jews that humiliation and wretchedness have been stamped upon them; yet for a considerable time we have been seeing that they live a life of great honour. America and Europe dance at their signal, and although they rule over only a small portion of land among the Arab countries, they are possessed of nuclear power. What, then, is the meaning of the verse of the Noble Qur'an? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
The commentators have written a great deal in the books of commentary, but the upshot and the sum of it all is that wretchedness was stamped upon the Jews either permanently or for a certain period. This wretchedness is either an actual wretchedness — for most of them are poor, though a certain percentage of them have reached the very summit of wealth — or, in the view of most scholars, what is meant is a wretchedness of hearts, that is, that they have become proverbial for greed and miserliness, and people detest them. Ḥaḍrat Qāriʾ Muḥammad Ṭayyib (may Allah have mercy on him), the rector of Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband, has a treatise on this question, which may be consulted.

Humiliation and weakness have been cast upon them, and until some time ago everyone regarded them as lowly. If, however, they take hold of the rope of the religion of Allah Most High, or take refuge in the protecting skirt of other nations, their humiliation and weakness will come to an end, or their humiliation will be hidden. Today the Jews are sitting in the lap of other nations as a child sits in its mother's lap; indeed, the child has made a plaything of the mother, and the mother, in her love for the child, is overstepping the bounds of justice and truth, فإلي الله المشتكى.

From the commentary of Imām al-Rāzī (may Allah have mercy on him) and from Qurrat al-ʿAynayn, the notes on the Jalālayn, it appears that the meaning of "ضربت عليهم الذلة والمسكنة" and of "أينما ثقفوا إلا بحبل من الله وحبل من الناس" is that when the Jews do not desist from their mischief they are to be fought and thereby made lowly and wretched, as their lands and their wealth were seized at Khaybar — unless they escape fighting and warfare by entering into a covenant of protection. A parallel to this is "أينما ثقفوا أخذوا وقتلوا تقتيلاً"، that is to say, it is of the nature of a condition and its consequence. Consider:
