### TITLE
Spending money earned from qawwālī on a mosque

### QUESTION
What is the position on spending money earned from qawwālī on a mosque?

### ANSWER
Money earned from qawwālī (devotional singing) is disliked, indeed impermissible. It should not be spent on a mosque; only pure money should be spent on a mosque.

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

The mosque is the sacred and pure house of God. Lawful and pure wealth should be used in building it and keeping it in good order. Using unlawful earnings on a mosque is forbidden and disliked. A hadith states that Allah Most High accepts only pure wealth. The sharia therefore gives no permission for a mosque to be built with unlawful or doubtful wealth. (فتاوی رحیمیہ ٦/۹۹)

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

Money that is purely unlawful earnings may not be spent either on one's own personal needs or on religious works. (فتاوی محمودیہ ۵/۱۳٦) ۔

In short, money earned from qawwālī should not be put into a mosque, and spending such wealth on a mosque should be avoided.
