### TITLE
Having a company pay for the funeral and burial of the deceased

### QUESTION
A man is employed by a company that has a funeral-and-burial policy: when he or one of his dependants dies, the company pays the cost of the funeral and burial out of its own funds. Is it permissible to make use of this or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, the money received under the funeral-and-burial policy is a gift from the government or the company and a form of assistance. Receiving it and using it is therefore permissible, just as it is permissible in the sharia to take provident fund money, a bonus, a pension and the like. Consider what Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

The amount that is compulsorily deducted from the salary, the amount that is added to it under the name of a bonus, and the amount that is added to both of these under the name of interest — it is permissible for Muslim employees or their heirs to take all three amounts. The bonus is simply a gift, and the amount added under the name of interest does not fall within the limits of interest in the sharia; it too has the ruling of a gift. (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 8/96, Kitāb al-Ribā, Dār al-Ishāʿat)

It states in another place:

The pension an employee receives on retirement from service is permissible. (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 8/97, Kitāb al-Ribā, Dār al-Ishāʿat).
