### TITLE
Removing contact lenses from the eyes of the deceased

### QUESTION
If someone passes away and there are contact lenses in his eyes, should they be removed or not?

### ANSWER
Contact lenses cannot be used by anyone else, removing them is difficult, and they do not appear to be a foreign addition to the body either. They should therefore not be removed.

Consider what Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

If it is hard to take teeth out of the mouth, and making a greater effort would violate the dignity of the deceased, they should be left where they are; there is no objection to this in the ritual bath or the shrouding. The sanctity of the deceased is greater than the sanctity of property. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 4/240)

Shāmī states:

وإن كان حرمة الآدمي أعلى من صيانة المال لكنه زال احترامه بتعديه كما في الفتح ومفاده أنه لو سقط في جوفه بلا تعد لا يشق اتفاقاً. (شامي: ۲/۲۳۸، سعيد).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
