### TITLE

The ruling on transit insurance

### QUESTION

Is transit insurance permissible or not? For example, from South Africa to Botswana — because last month a friend's vehicle was looted, in which there were trade goods worth about two hundred thousand rand. Now he fears that such a thing may happen in the future as well, so will it be permissible for him to take out insurance or not?

### ANSWER

Every insurance in which a larger sum is received in exchange for the sum deposited is impermissible. Yes, if in exchange for the deposited sum the service of medical treatment is received, or the repair of a car and the like, then scope may be found for it. Outwardly, in the case described, money is received at the time when the trade goods are lost, and more is received; on this basis such a transaction is impermissible and one should keep away from it. In our time insurance is in reality a combination of interest and gambling, and both of these are impermissible in the sight of the Sharīʿah.
Muftī Kifāyat Allāh Ṣāḥib says:
Insuring shops, factories and lives with insurance companies is impermissible, because insurance comprises ribā and gambling, and both of these are impermissible. (کفایت المفتی: ۸/۸۳، دار الاشاعت) ۔
Kitāb al-Fatāwā records:
Insurance of life and property is in principle not permissible, because if the policy is completed, the company returns a larger sum than the amount the policy-holder pays, and this is interest; and if an accident occurs before the policy is completed, the full sum is received even though he has deposited only a few instalments. It is as though the policy-holder does not know the outcome of his policy: one person will receive the full amount upon paying two or three instalments, while another will have to pay all of them. Clearly this form falls under gambling. Thus insurance is a form compounded of interest and gambling, and in the Sharīʿah both of these are forbidden; therefore this form is in principle not permissible. (کتاب الفتاوی: ۵/۳۵۸) ۔
See further: (فتاوی دار العلوم زکریا: ۵/٤٤٦ ۔ ٤۵۲، ط: زمزم پبلشرز) ۔
However, transit insurance is sometimes compulsory and without it entry is not permitted; in that case there is scope for it.
