### TITLE
The meanings of the names of the four well-known angels

### QUESTION
What are the meanings of the names Jibrāʾīl, Mīkāʾīl, Isrāfīl and ʿAzrāʾīl? Kindly inform us and earn a great reward.

### ANSWER
Imām al-Suyūṭī (may Allah have mercy on him) has given the meaning of the names of the angels mentioned in the question as "the servant of Allah" in "الدر المنثور" (1/225, Dār al-Fikr). If, however, one attempts to derive them from Arabic, then jabr means to set right what is broken and īl carries the sense of Allah — that is, one who joins those who are broken to Allah by bringing revelation. Mīkāʾīl may in origin be mahk and īl: mahk meaning soft, that is, one appointed by Allah Most High over the works of gentleness and mercy, such as the clouds and the rain. Isrāfīl may be isrāf and īl, because when he blows the trumpet the sound of it will come forth with great excess and force, throwing the order of the world into confusion. And in ʿAzrāʾīl, ʿazr means to prevent: he brings the message of death and so prevents a person from worldly movement and going about.
