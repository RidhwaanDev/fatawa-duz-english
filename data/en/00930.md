### TITLE
Combining gold with rand to complete the zakat threshold

### QUESTION
If someone has two rand and half an ounce of gold, and adding the value of the gold to the rand completes the nisab, is zakat due or not?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, if adding the value of the gold to the rand completes the nisab (the threshold at which zakat becomes due), then zakat is necessary. If it does not complete the nisab, zakat is not necessary.

Consider what al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

وتضم قيمة العروض إلى الثمانين والذهب إلى الفضة قيمة كذا في الكنز حتى لو ملك مائة درهم وخمسة دنانير قيمتها مائة درهم تجب الزكاة عنده

يجب أن يكون التقويم بما هو أنفع للفقراء قدراً ورواجاً. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱۷۹، في زكاة الذهب والفضة).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

If a person has five rupees in cash over and above his needs, together with three tolas of gold, zakat becomes obligatory. This is because cash money takes the ruling of silver and gold, and three tolas of gold plus five rupees in cash together come to the value of fifty-two and a half tolas of silver; zakat therefore becomes necessary. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۵/۱٦٦، کتاب الزکوٰۃ).

Note: this ruling applies when silver is taken as the measure of the nisab. If gold is taken as the measure, then zakat is not necessary; the details of this will follow shortly.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
