### TITLE
Sacrificing a mad or mangy animal

### QUESTION
Is the sacrifice of a mad animal or of an animal suffering from mange permissible or not?

### ANSWER
If a mad animal can move about and graze, its sacrifice is permissible; and an animal that has mange but is fat and healthy — its sacrifice too is permissible. (در مختار) ۔ But if the mangy or mad animals are so weak that no marrow is left in their bones, then their sacrifice is impermissible. (فتاوی الشامی: ٦/۳۲۳، سعید، و فتاوی قاضيخان: ۳/۳۵۳) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
