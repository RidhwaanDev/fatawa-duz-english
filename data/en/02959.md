### TITLE
A wazīfah for being granted children

### QUESTION
I have been married for five years but have no children. I have had a great deal of medical treatment, but the doctors have told me that there is some weakness in my sperm. I now wish to know whether there is any duʿāʾ, wazīfah or food that may benefit me, so that Allah Taʿālā may fulfil my hope. بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
For being granted children, together with supplicating Allah Taʿālā and undergoing outward physical treatment, the following practice is tried and tested:

(1) He should recite this verse in abundance:

﴿رب هب لي من لدنك ذرية طيبة، إنك سميع الدعاء﴾ [ آل عمران: ۳۸].

Aʿmāl-i Qurʾānī states: Whoever has lost hope of having children should recite this verse; through the blessing of this verse the Most Generous Lord will grant him a righteous child, in shāʾ Allāh Taʿālā. (Aʿmāl-i Qurʾānī, p. 54).

(2)

﴿رب لا تذرني فرداً وأنت خير الوارثين﴾ [الأنبياء: ۸۹]،

one hundred times daily. Whoever has lost hope of having children should recite this verse; through its blessing the Most Generous Lord will grant him a righteous child, in shāʾ Allāh Taʿālā. (Anwār-i Shamsiyyah, p. 94, and Aʿmāl-i Qurʾānī, p. 54).

(3)

﴿والسماء بنيناها بأيد وإنا لموسعون، والأرض فرشناها فنعم الماهدون﴾ [الذاريات: ٤۷، ٤۸]،

Whoever has lost hope of having children should boil two eggs each day and remove the shells, and write on one of them ﴿والسماء بنيناها بأيد وإنا لموسعون﴾, and on the other ﴿والأرض فرشناها فنعم الماهدون﴾. The husband should eat the first egg and the wife the second. He should follow this procedure for forty days, and during this period continue to have marital relations; in shāʾ Allāh conception will take place. (Aʿmāl-i Qurʾānī, 54).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
