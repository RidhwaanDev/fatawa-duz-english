### TITLE
Dry ablution for someone who becomes ritually impure inside the mosque

### QUESTION
If a person becomes ritually impure while in the mosque, should he go out without performing dry ablution (tayammum) or should he perform it? And is there any point in performing tayammum when water is available?

### ANSWER
Such a person should perform tayammum in order to leave the mosque, and this tayammum is necessary; in fact he should perform it at once and leave the mosque. Yes, if there is some danger, he may perform tayammum and sleep there. Consider the following:

ولوكان نائماً فيه (أي المسجد) فاحتلم والماء خارجه وخشي من الخروج يتيمم وينام فيه إلى أن يمكنه الخروج، قال في المنية: وإن احتلم في المسجد يتيمم للخروج إذا لم يخف وإن خاف يجلس مع التيمم ولا يصلي ولا يقرأ. (فتاوى الشامي: ۱/ ۲٤۳).
