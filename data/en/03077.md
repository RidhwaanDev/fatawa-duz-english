### TITLE
The ruling on examining adult girls from behind a curtain

### QUESTION
The system in our maktabs and madrasahs is that scholars are invited to conduct the examinations, and the scholars examine the adult girls in nāẓirah and the like from behind a curtain: they can only hear their voices and cannot see the girls at all. Is this method permissible or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
The basis of the above question rests, in reality, upon another question, namely: is a woman's voice included in her ʿawrah or not? According to the sounder view a woman's voice is not included in her ʿawrah, and for that reason there will be room for conducting the examination from behind a curtain. Where there is a fear of temptation and the like, however, one should avoid it.

A detailed discussion of this question has already been given in Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā, vol. 7, pp. 228–230, and may be consulted there; here we confine ourselves to only one or two proofs.

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ẓafar Aḥmad Thānawī (may Allah have mercy on him) says in Aḥkām al-Qurʾān:

حكم صوت المرأة: وقع الخلاف في صوت المرأة، أنه من العورة فلا يجوز أن تتكلم بحيث يسمعها الأجانب، أو ليس بعورة فيرخص لها في التكلم، والحق الحقيق عند أرباب التحقيق، وهو أن صوت المرأة ليس بعورة في نفسه إلا أنه قد يكون سبباً للفتنة، فكان من القسم الثاني من سد الذرائع، فدار حكمه على الفتنة وعدمها فحيث خيفت الفتنة حرم إبداؤه وحيث لا، فلا، كيف وقد حرم الله سبحانه وتعالى إظهار صوت الخلخال وأمثاله فقال: ولا يضربن بأرجلهن، لمظنة الفتنة فكيف يجوز إظهار صوت نفسها مطلقاً؟ (أحكام القرآن: ۳/٤۸۲، ط: إدارة القرآن).

Ḥaḍrat Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ (may Allah have mercy on him) says in Maʿārif al-Qurʾān:

Is a woman's voice in itself included in her ʿawrah, and is it permissible for a non-maḥram to hear her voice? On this matter the Imāms differ. In the books of Imām al-Shāfiʿī (may Allah have mercy on him) a woman's voice is not included in her ʿawrah. Among the Ḥanafīs too there are differing statements: Ibn al-Humām (may Allah have mercy on him), on the basis of the narration of al-Nawāzil, held it to be included in the ʿawrah, and it is for this reason that among the Ḥanafīs the adhān of a woman is disliked. Yet it is established from the noble hadith that the pure wives of the Prophet used to speak with non-maḥrams from behind a curtain even after the revelation of the verse of ḥijāb. From all of this together, the preferred and correct position appears to be that wherever, on some occasion or in some situation, there is a danger of temptation arising from a woman's voice, it is forbidden; and where there is no such danger, it is permissible (al-Jaṣṣāṣ). Caution lies in this: that without need women should not converse with non-maḥrams even from behind a curtain. And Allah knows best. (Maʿārif al-Qurʾān: 6/406, and 7/123, Idārat al-Maʿārif).
