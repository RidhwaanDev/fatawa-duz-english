### TITLE
The authenticity of the hadith on "مسح العينين"

### QUESTION
Is it an authentic hadith that when the mu'adhdhin says "أشهد أن محمدا رسول الله" in the call to prayer, the person who hears it should kiss his thumbs and place them on his eyes?

### ANSWER
This report is disputed. According to al-Sakhāwī it is not authentic, and the version narrated from Khiḍr has unknown narrators as well as a broken chain, so the report cannot be used as evidence. For the assessments of the masters of hadith criticism on it, see:

(الموضوعات الكبير، ص ۱۰۸، والمقاصد الحسنة ص ۳۸۳، والأسرار المرفوعة في الأخبار الموضوعة، ص ۲۱۰، رقم ۸۲۹).

The practice is also mentioned in Fatāwā Kanz al-ʿIbād, Fatāwā Ṣūfiyyah and other such works, but these collections are not reliable. Apart from that, this act has been described as a remedy for ailments of the eyes, so it should not be done with the intention of gaining reward.
