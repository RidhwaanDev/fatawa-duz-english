### TITLE
Taking medicine for cancer

### QUESTION
If a woman suffering from the illness of cancer takes a medicine which causes her hair to fall out, will it be sound to take such a medicine or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
The jurists have permitted the shaving of a woman's hair because of the need for medical treatment. On this basis, even if her hair falls out from taking the medicine for cancer, there is no harm in taking the medicine.

Note what al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq states:

وإذا حلقت المرأة شعر رأسها فإن كان لوجع أصابها فلا بأس به. (البحر الرائق: ۸/۲۰۵، كوئته). (وكذا في الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳۵۸، وخلاصة الفتاوى: ٤/۳۷۷).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
