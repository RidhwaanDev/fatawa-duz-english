### TITLE
A litany for protection from the oppression of a tyrant

### QUESTION
When the oppression of a tyrant becomes unbearable, what supplication or litany may I recite in such a situation?

### ANSWER
For protection from an enemy, recite this supplication 101 times every morning:

"اللهمَّ إنَّا نَجْعَلُك فِيْ نُحُوْرِهمْ وَنَعُوْذُ بِك مِنْ شُرُوْرِهمْ".

Recite the blessings upon the Prophet three times at the beginning and three times at the end. (انوار شمسیہ ۹۵)۔

In severe circumstances, for deliverance from the oppression of tyrants, along with this recite Sūrat al-Fīl 313 times in a single sitting, either individually or collectively, and then make supplication. (انوار شمسیہ ص ۹٦)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
