### TITLE
The ruling on the zakat of past years when part of an inheritance has been concealed

### QUESTION
A man passed away thirty years ago and the deceased's estate had been distributed, but one heir concealed a piece of jewellery belonging to the estate. Thirty years later the fear of Allah stirred in his heart and he brought the jewellery to light. The matter to be ascertained is this: are those heirs who knew nothing whatever of the jewellery obliged to pay the zakat of the past thirty years, or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, the heirs who had no knowledge of it are not obliged to pay zakat; the one who concealed it, however, is obliged and bound to pay the zakat of the past thirty years to the extent of his own share. In relation to the heirs who knew nothing of it, this jewellery came under the ruling of absent wealth, that is to say māl ḍimār: their ownership of it was not complete, nor did they have possession of it. The ruling on māl ḍimār is that zakat becomes due once a year has passed after it is obtained, and that the zakat of the past years is not due.

Consider what ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

قوله ولا في مال مفقود، شروع في مسألة مال الضمار

قوله بعدها أي بعد سنين، قوله حديث على رضي الله عنه لا زكاة في مال الضمار

قال في البحر: وهو في اللغة: الغائب الذي لا يرجي فإذا رجي فليس بضمار وأصله الإضمار وهو التغييب والإخفاء، ومنه أضمر في قلبه. (فتاوى الشامي: ۲/۲٦٦، سعيد).

See further: (العناية شرح الهداية: ۲/۱٦٤، وفتح باب العناية: ۲/۸٤).

Imdād al-Fatāwā records: if it was itself lost, the zakat of the past years has lapsed; and if it was found after being lost, one must consider the matter: if it was found after the completion of that zakat year, the zakat of the days during which it was missing will not be due,

ولا في مال مفقود وجده بعد سنين

As for zakat becoming due in future, the ruling is that if this person already owns a nisāb of the same kind apart from it, its zakat will be paid together with that; but if it is less than a nisāb, then a complete year must pass from the time of finding it.

والمستفاد وسط الحول يضم إلى نصاب من جنسه فيزكيه بحول الأصل، قوله إلى نصاب قيد به لأنه لوكان النصاب ناقصاً وكمل بالمستفاد فإن الحول ينعقد عليه عند الكمال، شامي،

(Imdād al-Fatāwā 2/53)

See further: (ʿUmdat al-Fiqh 3/22). Further details concerning māl ḍimār have already been given above.
