### TITLE
Feeding the expiation meal to madrasa students

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to feed the expiation meal to the students of madrasas or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question it is permissible to feed the expiation meal to the poor and needy students of madrasas; it is not permissible to feed it to students who are well off.

قال الله تعالى: ﴿فإطعام عشرة مساكين من أوسط ما تطعمون أهليكم﴾. (سورة المائدة: ۸۹).

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:
وكفارته

إطعام عشرة مساكين. (الدر المختار: ۳/۷۲۵، سعيد).

Imdād al-Fatāwā records:

It is necessary to feed the poor; feeding the well-off is not correct. (امداد الفتاوی ۲/۵٦۰) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
