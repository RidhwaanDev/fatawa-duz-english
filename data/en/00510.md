### TITLE
The imam's duty to inform the congregation that the prayer was invalid

### QUESTION
If the imam's prayer became invalid because of bleeding, and the imam only realised this after the prayer, is he obliged to inform the congregation or not?

### ANSWER
If for some reason the prayer was not valid, the imam is obliged to inform those who prayed behind him. If the imam is an upright man, they must then repeat the prayer; otherwise repeating it is recommended. But if the followers cannot be identified, or informing them is difficult, then he is not obliged to inform them. Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

وإذا ظهر حدث إمامه وكذا كل مفسد في رأي مقتد بطلت فيلزم إعادتها لتضمنها صلاة المؤتم صحة وفساداًكما يلزم الإمام إخبار القوم إذا أمهم وهو محدث أو جنب أو فاقد شرط أوركن، وهل عليهم إعادتها، إن عدلا نعم، وإلا ندبت

بالقدر الممكن بلسانه أو بكتاب أو رسول على الأصح لو معينين أي معلومين وقال ح: وإن تعين بعضهم لزمه إخباره وإلا أي وإن لم يكونوا معينين كلهم بعضهم لا يلزمه. (الدر المختار مع الشامي: ۱/٥۹۱، ٥۹۲، سعيد)

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

Question: An imam led the prayer while in a state of major ritual impurity. What is the ruling?

The answer: In the case described neither the imam's prayer nor the followers' prayers were valid, and they must be prayed again. The imam should inform the followers one by one, or announce at prayer time that everyone who was present at the Fajr prayer on such-and-such a day should repeat that prayer. Those followers who cannot be reached with the news are excused. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ٤/۳٦٤)

Imdād al-Aḥkām states:

Question: An imam led the prayer without ablution by mistake. What should he do?

The answer: The imam must inform, in whatever way he can, everyone whom he knows to have taken part in the prayer. If the imam is an upright man, they must repeat the prayer once they are informed; if he is not upright, repeating it is recommended. (امداد الاحکام : ۱/۵۲۵) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
