### TITLE
The ruling on suspending a divorce upon an impossible condition

### QUESTION
A father and his son disagreed about a girl's marriage. The father said: I will not give my daughter to Zayd. The son said: if I do not marry my sister to Zayd this year, then three divorces upon my wife. The year has now passed and the son was unable to marry his sister to Zayd. Have three divorces fallen upon the son's wife or not? In this question let this aspect also be kept in view: whether, in the presence of the girl's father, the brother has any authority or power to give his sister in marriage; or whether the girl herself has that authority.

### ANSWER
In the presence of the father the brother has no authority to give his sister in marriage; hence this belongs to the category of suspension upon an impossibility, that is, divorce was made contingent upon something that is not within his power. Furthermore, if the girl is sane and adult, she is her own mistress in the matter of her marriage: if she marries within her equals, permission is a sunnah and not a requirement; yes, if she marries outside her equals, then the guardian's permission is necessary — and the guardian is the father, not the brother. Consider what is stated in al-Durr al-Mukhtār:

والولي في النكاح العصبة بنفسه بلا توسطة أنثى على ترتيب الإرث. قال الشامي: يقدم الأب ثم أبوه ثم الأخ الشقيق. (الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ۳/۷٦، سعيد).
وفيه أيضاً: فلو زوج الأبعد حال قيام الأقرب توقف على إجازته. (الدر المختار: ۳/۸۱، سعيد). وللمزيد راجع: (تبيين الحقائق: ۵/۲۹۷، وفتاوى الشامي: ۳/٦۱، سعيد).

Concerning suspension upon an impossibility, consider what is stated in al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq:

وخرج ما كان مستحيلاً كقوله: إن دخل الجمل في سم الخياط فأنت طالق فلا يقع أصلاً لأن غرضه منه تحقيق النفي حيث علقه بأمر محال وهذا يرجع إلى قولهما. (البحر الرائق: ٤/۲، باب التعليق، ط: كوئته). (وكذا في الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/٤۲۱).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

Zayd suspended the divorce upon a condition whose fulfilment is not possible; hence the suspension itself is not valid, and so in this case no divorce has occurred and the marriage remains intact as before. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۳/٤۱، جامعہ فاروقیہ) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
