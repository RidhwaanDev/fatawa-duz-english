### TITLE
A reply to those who do not permit a wealthy person to use zakat items

### QUESTION
Some muftis say that it is not lawful for a wealthy person. Muftī Rashīd Aḥmad Ludhyānwī (may Allah have mercy on him), for example, has written in Aḥsan al-Fatāwā that it is not permitted, and he has also said that those who allowed it did not investigate the matter. Here are his words: "Those who declared it permissible did not consult the books when writing their fatwa." (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 4/259) What is the answer to this?

### ANSWER
After examining the passages that indicate impermissibility, and finding real hardship and difficulty in that position, we saw that facing those very passages Mawlānā Saʿdī Chalabī has stated plainly that it is permissible. In view of that passage we expressed our inclination towards permissibility. The passage of al-Fatāwā al-Tātārkhāniyyah is also presented here.

Consider what al-Fatāwā al-Tātārkhāniyyah states:

الفقير إذا أباح للغني عين ما أخذ من الزكاة من الطعام هل يحل له التناول؟ قال بعض المشايخ: يحل، وإليه مال شيخ الإسلام. (الفتاوى التاتارخانية: ۲/۲٦۸، إدارة القرآن).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
