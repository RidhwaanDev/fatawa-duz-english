### TITLE
Intending three divorces by saying "You may go"

### QUESTION
A man said to his wife, "You may go." The wife said, "You have given three divorces." The husband said, "Yes." How many divorces have taken effect?

### ANSWER
In the case described, the husband's words "You may go" are among the allusive expressions. The ruling for allusive expressions is that the husband is asked about his intention: if he intended one divorce, one irrevocable divorce has taken effect; and if he intended three divorces, three divorces take effect. From the way the question is put, it appears that the husband acknowledged three divorces, so three divorces have fallen upon his wife and she is irrevocably divorced. She is not now lawful for her first husband without ḥalālah (an intervening valid marriage to another man).

Consider what al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

فالكنايات لا تطلق بها إلا بالنية أو دلالة الحال، فنحو اخرجي واذهبي وقومي

وثلاث إن نواه. (الدر المختار: ۳/۲۹۷، سعيد).

Al-Hidāyah states:

وبقية الكنايات إذا نوى بها الطلاق كانت واحدة بائنة، وإن نوى الثلاث كانت ثلاثاً. (الهداية: ۲/۳۷٤).

Sharḥ al-ʿInāyah states:

وأما جواز نية الثلاث فلأن الواقع بها إذا كان بائناً فالبينونة تتصل بالمرأة للحال. (شرح العناية على هامش فتح القدير: ٤/٦٤، دار الفكر).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
