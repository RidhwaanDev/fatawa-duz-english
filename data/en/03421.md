### TITLE
The ruling on a second Friday congregation for schoolchildren

### QUESTION
In some areas Muslim children study in non-Muslim schools, and the school authorities do not give permission for the Friday prayer. So the parents of these children requested the mosque committee that a second congregation be held for them later in the courtyard of the mosque. Now the question is this: is it permissible to hold a second congregation in the courtyard of the mosque or not? And is it correct to compel the appointed imām to lead it or not? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
It is permissible and correct to hold a second Friday congregation in the courtyard of the mosque. However, the appointed imām will not be compelled; rather, if there are several imāms in the mosque, they may lead the Friday prayer by turns; or if some pious man other than the imām of the mosque delivers a brief sermon and leads the prayer, that too is valid and correct. Consider what is recorded in al-Durr al-Mukhtār:

ولو أمكنه الذهاب إلى إمام آخر فعل لأنها تؤدى بمصر واحد بمواضع كثيرة اتفاقاً فإن عجز صلى أربعاً. (الدر المختار: ۲/۱۷٦، سعيد).

A second congregation is permissible in the courtyard outside the mosque; yes, inside the mosque it is disliked. Consider what is stated in al-Durr al-Mukhtār:

ويكره تكرار الجماعة بأذان وإقامة في مسجد محلة لا في مسجد طريق أو مسجد لا إمام له ولا مؤذن. وفي الشامي: قوله يكره، أي تحريماً لقول الكافي لا يجوز والمجمع لا يباح وشرح الجامع الصغير أنه بدعة كما في رسالة السندي. (فتاوى الشامي: ۱/۵۵۲، سعيد).

وللمزيد راجع: (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۸۳، وشرح منية المصلي، ص ٦۱٤، سهيل).

In Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah it is recorded:

Question: I am a teacher in an Urdu government school in the state of Maharashtra. The time of the Friday prayer falls between half past ten and two o'clock, that is, during school hours. Up to now we used to close the school and simply pray the Friday prayer; but now the officers cause trouble over this. What should be done in such a case? The evening school is from half past two until half past five, and apart from this employment there is no means of livelihood.

Answer: Make an effort and settle upon some place where the Friday prayer takes place at half past two. And Allah Most High alone knows best. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 8/34, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

Moreover, in Majmūʿat al-Fatāwā (1/372, published by Mīr Muḥammad Kutub Khānah), Imdād al-Aḥkām (1/783), and Khayr al-Fatāwā (3/95) too, a fatwā on the permissibility of a second Friday congregation is to be found.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
