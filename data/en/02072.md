### TITLE
The education policy

### QUESTION
The deceased took out an insurance policy for his daughter's education, paying a fixed sum every month to the insurance company. When the daughter reaches the age of 25 she will receive a large sum of money from the company. Will it be permissible to take that large sum and to pay it out for educational fees and the like, or not?

### ANSWER
In educational insurance too, money is received in exchange for money together with an increase; this is therefore an interest-based transaction and is impermissible. It is permissible to take and use only the sum that was paid in; the remaining additional, interest-bearing money should be given away in charity without the intention of reward.
