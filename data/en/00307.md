### TITLE
How to purify a hand pump that has become impure

### QUESTION
If a hand pump becomes impure, what is the way to purify it?

### ANSWER
The way to purify a hand pump is to work it and let out enough water to wash the pump three times over; the quantity of water needed can be judged by strong probability. Some books mention another method of making the water flow: that enough water be poured in from the top until the pipe fills and the water overflows from above, and that the pump becomes pure because the water has thereby flowed. This is open to question, and it is hard to call that flowing, because flowing means pouring in from one side and coming out from the other.

Radd al-Muḥtār states:

وبقي شيء آخر سئلت عنه وهو أن دلواً تنجس فافرغ فيه رجل ماء حتى امتلاء وسال من جوانبه هل يطهر بمجرد ذلك أم لا؟ والذي يظهر لي الطهارة أخذا مما ذكرنا ه هنا ومما مرانه لا يشترط إن يكون الجريان بمدد. (رد المحتار ۱/۱۹٦).
