### TITLE
Transplanting a kidney

### QUESTION
Both of a man's kidneys have failed. May this man arrange to preserve his life by having an organ transplant — that is, by having another person's kidney fitted — or not?

### ANSWER
There is a difference of opinion among the scholars regarding organ transplantation. Nevertheless, acting upon the view of those who permit it, and observing the conditions and restrictions they have laid down, it will be permitted to have a kidney replaced. For the evidence, our detailed fatwa may be consulted.

For further detail see also: (Majallah Fiqh Islāmī, compiled by Qāḍī Mujāhid al-Islām Qāsimī (may Allah have mercy on him): 1/169–248, "Transplantation of organs").
