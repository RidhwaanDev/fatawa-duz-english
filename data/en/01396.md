### TITLE
The levels of merit in fasting on ʿĀshūrāʾ

### QUESTION
What is the ruling on joining other days to ʿĀshūrāʾ in the month of Muḥarram, and how many levels does this have? بینوا توجروا

### ANSWER
It is recommended to join the fast of the ninth or of the eleventh to ʿĀshūrāʾ, and there are four levels to this:

(1) The first level: fasting on the ninth, the tenth and the eleventh. This is the most virtuous.

أخرج الإمام البيهقي في شعب الإيمان (۳۵۱۱) عن ابن عباس رضي الله عنه قال: قال رسول الله ﷺ: صوموا يوم عاشوراء وخالفوا اليهود فيه صوموا قبله يوماً وبعده يوماً.
قال الهيثمي: وفيه محمد بن أبي ليلي وفيه كلام. (۳/۱۸۸).
وأيضاً أخرجه أحمد (۱/۲۴۱، رقم: ۲۱۵٤)، وتمام في فوائده (رقم: ۸۷)، والبزار (رقم: ۵۲۳۸).

Shaykh ʿAbd al-Ḥaqq al-Dihlawī says in al-Lamaʿāt:

The fast of Muḥarram has three levels. The best is that a person fast the ninth day, the tenth day and the eleventh day — this is what has come in the narration of Aḥmad and al-Bazzār from Ibn ʿAbbās (may Allah be pleased with him). The second is the ninth and the tenth, and the third is the tenth alone. (Ashiʿʿat al-Lamaʿāt: 2/105, Maktabah Rashīdiyyah edition).

Some commentators have said that in this narration the word wāw ("and") carries the meaning of maʿa ("together with"), and that in other manuscripts أو ("or") appears in place of the wāw — as in al-Mirqāt (4/288, Multan edition). That is because opposing the Jews is achieved by fasting either one of the two days.

(2) The second level: fasting on the ninth and the tenth. Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim records:

عن ابن عباس رضي الله عنه عن النبي ﷺ قال: لئن بقيت إلى قابل لا صومن التاسع. وفي رواية عنه: فإذا كان العام المقبل إن شاء الله، صمنا اليوم التاسع. (رواه مسلم: ۱/۳۵۹).

(3) The third level: fasting on the tenth and the eleventh. This narration is reported in the books of hadith in many places:

عن ابن عباس رضي الله عنه عن النبي ﷺ قال: صوموا يوم عاشوراء، وخالفوا اليهود، صوموا قبله يوماً أو بعده يوماً. (صحيح ابن خزيمة: ۲/۱۰۰٦، رقم: ۲۰۹۵).

Since it was the fast of the ninth that the Prophet ﷺ expressed a wish to keep, fasting the ninth of Muḥarram takes the second level, while the eleventh takes the third.

(4) The fourth level: fasting on the tenth of Muḥarram alone. Its merit too is mentioned in the blessed hadiths. Thus a narration in Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim states:

وصيام يوم عاشوراء احتسب على الله أن يكفر السنة التي قبله. (مسلم شريف: ۱/۳٦۷).

ʿAllāmah Sayyid Banūrī (may Allah have mercy on him) says in Maʿārif al-Sunan:

وحاصل الشريعة: أن الأفضل (۱) صوم يوم عاشوراء وصوم يوم قبله وصوم يوم بعده، ثم الأدوَن منه: (۲) صوم عاشوراء وصوم يوم قبله أو (۳) صوم يوم بعده، ثم الأدون منه: صوم عاشوراء منفرداً، والصور الثلاث كلها عبادات بعضها فوق بعض. (معارف السنن: ۵/٤۳٤).

The jurists then differ as to whether fasting on the tenth of Muḥarram alone is disliked or not. Some have written that it is disliked because it involves resembling the Jews; but since that resemblance no longer exists, jurists generally have declared it permissible without any dislike — as in al-Badāʾiʿ. Moreover, joining the ninth to the tenth is recommended, and going against something merely recommended is not disliked; nor does acting contrary to a wish expressed by the Prophet ﷺ necessarily entail dislike. For instance, the Prophet ﷺ wished that the Ḥaṭīm be included within the Kaʿbah, yet that did not come about.

In sum, fasting on the tenth of Muḥarram alone is now either permissible or contrary to what is preferable; it is not disliked. And those jurists who did call it disliked meant a mild dislike, which amounts to the same thing as being contrary to what is preferable. This is what Mufti Niẓām al-Dīn (may Allah have mercy on him) stated in Niẓām al-Fatāwā, and Mufti Maḥmūd Ḥasan Gangōhī's (may Allah have mercy on him) signature appears on it as well.

For further detail see: (Niẓām al-Fatāwā, pp. 368–371, and Maʿārif al-Sunan: 5/434–435).
