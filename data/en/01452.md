### TITLE
Conducting a marriage without the agent's authorisation

### QUESTION
At a marriage gathering we saw a new method used by the man conducting the marriage. He neither asked the agent for permission to conduct the marriage, nor asked the agent, "Have you given so-and-so in marriage to so-and-so?" Instead, without asking the agent at all, he said to the bridegroom, "I have given so-and-so to you in marriage." Is this method correct? بینوا ولکم الاجر الجزیل ۔

### ANSWER
The good way of handling the agency is for the man conducting the marriage to say to the girl's agent, "You have given so-and-so in marriage to so-and-so," and for the agent to reply, "Yes, I have." In this way the agent discharges the agency entrusted to him.

A second way is for the man conducting the marriage to take permission from the agent and then to say, "I have given so-and-so in marriage to so-and-so." In this way the man conducting the marriage becomes the agent of the agent, and in the light of the wording of Qāḍī Khān this method is also correct.

In the third way, the one described in the question, the Mawlawī Ṣāḥib is neither the agent nor the agent of the agent; he is an uncommissioned party, and a marriage carried out by an uncommissioned party is suspended upon ratification. In the case in question, when the agent gave and received congratulations after the offer and acceptance, and the wife afterwards consummated the marriage with her husband, ratification was found and the marriage was contracted. Still, this method is not sound and must be corrected.

See what Fatāwā Qāḍī Khān records:

قال: والوكيل بالتزويج ليس له أن يوكل غيره فإن فعل فزوج الثاني بحضرة الأول جاز. (فتاوى قاضيخان على هامش الفتاوى الهندية: ۳/٤٦، فصل في التوكيل بالنكاح).

وللمزيد ينظر: (البحر الرائق: ۳/۳٦۰، دار المعرفة، والدر المختار: ۵/۵۲۸، سعيد، والاختيار لتعليل المختار: ۱/۲۵).

قال في المحيط البرهاني: حلف بطلاق كل امرأة يتزوجها، فزوجه رجل امرأة بغير أمره فأجاز هو قولاً أو فعلاً،

لأن الإجازة في الانتهاء بمنزلة الإذن في الابتداء من حيث إن العاقد بالإجازة يصير نائباً عن المجيز من ذلك الوقت، وفعل النائب كفعل المنوب عنه فيصير متزوجاً من ذلك الوقت. (المحيط البرهاني: ۳/۳۱۹، نكاح الفضولي، رشيديه).

وفي الشامية: إذا أجاز نكاح الفضولي بالفعل يجوز. (فتاوى الشامي: ۲/۱٤۲، سعيد، وكذا في ۳/۱۲، سعيد، ولسان الحكام، ص ۳۱٦، الفصل الثالث عشر في النكاح).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

The basic ruling is that the agent appointed for the marriage should conduct the marriage himself. He has no authority, without the permission of the woman who appointed him, to authorise anyone else to conduct it. If the agent for the marriage does authorise someone else to conduct it without his principal's permission, and that person conducts the marriage, then in the view of some it is the marriage of an uncommissioned party, and it remains suspended until the bride ratifies it in word or in deed, at which point it is contracted. Other jurists (may Allah have mercy on them) hold that if the first agent is present at the marriage gathering the marriage takes effect and is binding; and this is the preferred position and the one acted upon.

الوكيل بالتزويج ليس له أن يوكل غيره فإن فعل فزوجه الثاني بحضرة الأول جاز. (فتاوى قاضي خان: ۳/۵۸۰)

(فتاوی رحیمیہ: ٤/۲۹۰، ۲۹۱) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
