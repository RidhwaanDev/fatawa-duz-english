### TITLE
Ruling on Shia mosques and imambargahs

### QUESTION
Can the mosque mentioned in the previous question be called an imambargah (a Shia gathering hall) or not, given that programmes based on Shia beliefs and the like are held in it? Or should its present name, "Masjid Sayyidunā Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq", be kept?

### ANSWER
If the mosque was built by the Shia and they gave it the name "Masjid Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq" in order to mislead the public, then it is necessary to make the public aware of this. And if the mosque was built by our own people and then the wrong sort of people gained control of it, every effort should be made to end their control. In any case, that mosque of the Shia is just like an imambargah.
