### TITLE
Going to another mosque after missing the congregation in one's local mosque

### QUESTION
If a person can catch two units of the prayer in his own local mosque, while at another place that is not his local mosque he could catch the whole prayer in congregation, should he travel the extra distance, or should he simply join the last two units in his own mosque?

### ANSWER
Praying in congregation is either necessary or an emphasised sunnah.

كما في الدر: والجماعة سنة مؤكدة للرجال، قال الزاهدي: أرادوا بالتأكيد الوجوب وقيل واجبة و عليه العامة أي عامة مشايخنا. (الدر المختار ۱/٥٥۲، سعيد)

Furthermore, praying in congregation in the mosque of one's own neighbourhood is better, and that mosque has a right over him.

كما في الشامي: قوله ومسجد حيه أفضل من الجامع

لو لم يكن لمسجد منزله مؤذن فانه يذهب إليه ويؤذن فيه ويصلي ولو كان وحده لأن له حقاً عليه فيؤديه. (الشامي: ۱/٦٥۹، سعيد)

And if the congregation in one's local mosque has been missed, it is not necessary to go searching for a congregation in another mosque.

كما في بدائع الصنائع: إذا فاتته الجماعة لا يجب عليه الطلب في مسجد آخر. (بدائع الصنائع: ۱/۱٥٦، سعيد)

These passages show that if there is still hope of joining the congregation in one's own local mosque, then even if a few units are missed it remains better to pray there. But if the congregation there has finished altogether and there is hope of catching a congregation in another mosque, he has a choice: he may either pray alone in his own mosque or pray in congregation in the other mosque.

كما في بدائع الصنائع: إذا فاتته الجماعة في مسجد حيه فإن أتي مسجد اً آخر يرجو إدراك الجماعة فيه فحسن وإن صلى في مسجد حيه فحسن. (بدائع الصنائع: ۱/۱٥٦، سعيد)

In short, if there is another mosque close by besides his local mosque and he is certain of catching the congregation there, then he should go to that mosque, so that he gains the reward of both the congregation and the mosque, and so that the spot where he prayed in that mosque will bear witness for him on the Day of Judgement. The merit of congregational prayer reported in the hadiths is not restricted to one's own mosque.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
