### TITLE
Prohibition established by feeding milk in an unnatural way

### QUESTION
The natural way is for a child to take milk from its mother's breast. But if for some reason the milk is expressed and put into the child's mouth, or introduced through the nose, or given in some other unnatural way, is prohibition established or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, if the mother's milk is expressed and given or introduced to the child in an unnatural way within the period of suckling, prohibition is still established; indeed, according to Imām Muḥammad (may Allah have mercy on him) prohibition is established even by way of an enema.

Consider al-Fatāwā al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah, which states:

كما يحصل الرضاع بالمص من الثدي يحصل بالصب والسعوط والوجور

وعند محمد رحمه الله يثبت بالحقنة كما في التهذيب. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۳٤٤، كتاب الرضاع. وكذا في فتاوى قاضيخان على هامش الهندية: ۱/٤۱۷، باب الرضاع).
