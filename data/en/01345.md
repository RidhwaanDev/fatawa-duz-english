### TITLE
Preventing pregnancy for the sake of nursing a child

### QUESTION
During the period of suckling, may measures be adopted to prevent pregnancy for the sake of nursing the child, or not?

### ANSWER
It sometimes happens that while a child is still at the breast, a second pregnancy occurs — that is, the mother has not yet recovered from the weakness of the first birth when the concern of a second is upon her. Moreover, the mother's milk gradually dries up and the first child is not properly nourished, so the children become weak and the mother too is harmed. Therefore, in order to avoid this harm as well, it is permitted to adopt measures to prevent pregnancy. (بحث و نظر: ۲/۳۵۵)۔
