### TITLE
The ruling on obtaining a prize from the Emirates Lotto

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to take part in the Emirates Lotto and to obtain a prize from it or not? The procedure of the Emirates Lotto is that the Emirates people prepare certain special cards and sell them for 35 dirhams; whoever buys one can become a participant in the lotto. Then six numbers have to be selected out of 49 numbers; then 49 balls, on which the 49 numbers are inscribed, are spun in a lotto machine and six numbers are drawn out. So whoever's numbers correspond exactly to the six receives the grand prize (jackpot); if someone's five numbers correspond, he obtains the second-place prize; if 4 numbers correspond, he receives three hundred dirhams; and if three correspond, he receives free entry into the next game. The benefit of the card is that one can buy it from any of 19,000 places throughout the country, and upon buying the card one can become a participant in the lotto, otherwise not. Now, from the point of view of the Sharīʿah, what will be the ruling on this lotto? Kindly inform us and become deserving of a great reward.

### ANSWER
In the case in question, the preparing and selling of cards is for the purpose of boosting the lottery, so that as many people as possible may take part in the greed for the prize; then the buyer of the card too buys the card for the purpose of the prize. And on the one side the payment of the money is certain, while on the other side it is uncertain: in the eyes of the Sharīʿah this is called gambling, which is unlawful and impermissible by a decisive text. Therefore it is binding and necessary to avoid it entirely.

Consider what is in the Noble Qurʾān:

قال الله تعالى: ﴿إنما الخمر والميسر والأنصاب والأزلام رجس من عمل الشيطان فاجتنبوه، لعلكم تفلحون﴾ [المائدة: ۹۰]،

قال في تفسير السعدي: وأما الميسر فهو كل المغالبات التي يكون فيها عوض من الطرفين. (ص ۹۸، ط: الرسالة).

وأخرج الإمام أحمد في مسنده عن عبد الله بن عمرو رضي الله عنه، قال: قال رسول الله ﷺ: إن الله حرم على أمتي الخمر والميسر. (مسند أحمد، رقم: ٦۵٤۷).

قال في قواعد الفقه: القمار: مصدر قامر، هو كل لعب يشترط فيه غالباً أن يأخذ الغالب شيئاً من المغلوب وأصله أن يأخذ الواحد من صاحبه شيئاً فشيئاً في اللعب ثم عرفوه بأنه تعليق الملك على الخطر والمال في الجانبين. (قواعد الفقه، ص ٤۳٤).

وقال في تبيين الحقائق: وسمي القمار قماراً لأن كل واحد من المقامرين ممن يجوز أن يذهب ماله إلى صاحبه، ويجوز أن يستفيد مال صاحبه فيجوز الازدياد والانتقاص في كل واحد منهما فصار قماراً. (تبيين الحقائق: ٦/۲۲۷، إمداديه).

Muftī Taqī Ṣāḥib says:

One form of uncertainty, which the noble jurists have expressed as "suspending ownership upon a risk" — that is, in contracts of exchange, making the transfer of ownership contingent upon some risk — has as one of its branches gambling. Gambling, that is qimār or maysir, is where payment is certain from one side while payment from the other side is speculative. So all the lotteries that are well known in our age are gambling; they are "suspending ownership upon a risk" and are a branch of uncertainty, which is unlawful. (Islām awr Jadīd Maʿāshī Masāʾil: 4/76, Idārah-i Islāmiyyāt). For more, see: (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā, volume five).
