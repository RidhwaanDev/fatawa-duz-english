### TITLE
The ruling on comparing a watch strap to an iron ring

### QUESTION
Is it correct to draw an analogy between a watch strap and an iron ring and to declare it impermissible, or not?

### ANSWER
It is correct to compare a watch strap to a coat of mail or to a sword belt; comparing it to a ring appears far-fetched for the reasons set out below. And fitting iron to a sword belt is permissible by way of adornment.

قال سليمان بن حبيب سمعت أبا أمامة رضي الله عنه يقول: لقد فتح الفتوح قوم ما كانت حلية سيوفهم الذهب ولا الفضة إنما كانت حليهم العلابي والآنك والحديد. (رواه البخاري: ۱/٤۰۷، باب حلية السيوف).

قوله: العلابي: جمع علباء، وهو عصب العنق، وهما علباوان يميناً وشمالاً، كانت العرب تشد العصب على غلف سيوفها وهو رطب، ثم يجف فيصير كالقد. والآنك: الرصاص الأبيض وقيل: الأسود، وقيل: الخالص منه.

(1) The watch hangs from the strap, just as a sword hangs from its belt and baldric.

(2) The watch strap is subordinate to the watch, just as a sword belt is subordinate to the sword, whereas a ring is itself the object of adornment.

(3) A sword is necessary for a warrior, and a watch is necessary for a Muslim to know the times of prayer, since a pocket watch involves difficulty.

(4) A sword belt has the status of a necessity for the sword, whereas in this age a ring is only for adornment, because using it to affix a seal has now fallen out of practice.

See what Fatāwā Muftī Maḥmūd states:

A watch is a thing of human need, and the chain or strap is for its protection — just as a sword has one or two rings at its two ends. The jurists have stated explicitly that if these rings are of iron, copper or brass, they are permissible and there is no dislikedness in it. From this the permissibility of an iron or similar strap is apparently understood.

قال في الشامية (٦/۳۵۹): ولا يكره في المنطقة حلقة حديد أو نحاس وعظم وأيضاً تحت قوله: ولا يتختم إلا بالفضة أي بخلاف المنطقة فلا يكره فيها حلقة حديد ونحاس

(Fatāwā Muftī Maḥmūd: 10/301, and p. 403, and p. 251).

See further: (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 19/364, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah; and Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 8/213).
