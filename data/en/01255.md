### TITLE
Writing letters to a young woman with a view to marriage

### QUESTION
(1) If a man wants to get to know a young woman with a view to marriage, is it right for him to write letters to her and for her to answer them, or not? (2) If he has already got to know her, or knew her beforehand, what is the position on writing letters even then?

(a) When the letters contain talk of love and passion.

(b) What is the ruling when the letters contain no talk of love?

### ANSWER
Since the sacred sharia permits looking at a woman and speaking briefly with her when marriage is intended, writing a letter is also permissible in the sharia — provided it is not for enjoyment or to pass the time. And just as it is stressed at the time of looking that the young man and the young woman must observe the limits set by the sharia, so too those limits must be observed when writing letters. When the blessed hadiths permit looking, writing a letter is a lesser matter than that. Once the necessary information has been obtained through the letters, the correspondence should be closed.

(2) Once he has got to know the young woman and has obtained the necessary information, it is no longer right to write letters, whether they contain talk of love and passion or not, because until the marriage contract has been performed she has the status of a woman unrelated to him.

Al-Ṭaḥṭāwī states:

قوله والنظر إليها قبله، أي فإنه مندوب، لأنه داعية للألفة فينظر إلى وجهها وكفيها وإن لم تأذن له هي أو وليها إذا علم أنه يجاب في نكاحها. (حاشية الطحطاوي على الدر المختار: ۲/۵، كوئته).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
