### TITLE
Visiting the graveyard on the day of Eid

### QUESTION
What is the position on going to the graveyard on the day of Eid?

### ANSWER
Going to the graveyard on the days of the two Eids is correct; indeed the scholars have counted these among the best days for it, so it is recommended. It should not, however, be regarded as binding or as a sunnah. Consider what al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

وأفضل أيام الزيارة أربعة

وكذلك في الأزمنة المتبركة كعشر ذي الحجة والعيدين

(الفتاوى الهندية: ٥/۳٥۰، في زيارة القبور)

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh states:
Visiting graves is recommended on Friday, Saturday, Monday or Thursday, the best of these being Friday and the morning hours. It is also excellent to visit graves on Shab-e Barāʾat (the night of mid-Shaʿbān), during the ten days of Dhū al-Ḥijjah, on the two Eids, and during the first ten days of Muḥarram. (عمدۃ الفقہ: کتاب الصلاۃ حصہ دوم: ۵۳۸، زیارتِ قبور کا بیان، المجددیہ)

Also see: فتاوی محمودیہ: ۹/۲۰۱، عید کے دن زیارتِ قبور، جامعہ فاروقیہ) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
