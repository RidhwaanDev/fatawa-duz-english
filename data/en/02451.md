### TITLE
The ruling on the sacrifice for a person in debt

### QUESTION
If a man has wealth to the amount of the niṣāb, but he owes a debt greater than the niṣāb, does the sacrifice become necessary and binding upon him or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question the sacrifice is not binding or necessary upon such a person. (الفتاوی الہندیۃ ۵/۲۹۲) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
