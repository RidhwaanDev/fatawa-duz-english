### TITLE
Liability when charitable funds are confiscated

### QUESTION
A man has for a long time been running charitable work in another country — India, for example — while he himself lives in England or Africa. Many people give him zakāh, lillāh and other funds, and some place certain sums with him as a trust. Before travelling to that other country, India, he sends the funds across from England or Africa. A friend who assists him receives these funds in India and puts them away in a safe place in his house; this is done purely for safekeeping. A few weeks ago the police, acting on some suspicion, searched his house in India, arrested his assistant, and confiscated all the funds. What is the ruling now? Is the sender of the money responsible for these funds or not? And are those who paid zakāh obliged to pay their zakāh over again or not?

بينوا توجروا ولكم الأجر الجزيل.

### ANSWER
When a trust is placed with someone, the ruling of the Sacred Law is that the trustee must safeguard it as far as he is able. Therefore, if it perishes or is lost without any excess, heedlessness, slackness or laziness on his part, no compensation falls upon him. But if the depositary was remiss in safeguarding it, was heedless, and overstepped the mark, then he is liable for it. Similarly, if a person appoints someone as his agent to deliver something, the ruling for that agent is the same as for a trustee: if the property perishes or is lost in the agent's hands without any shortcoming on his part, no liability falls on him either.

Consider what al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

الإيداع شرعاً تسليط الغير على حفظ ماله صريحاً أو دلالة

وهي أمانة، هذا حكمها مع وجوب الحفظ والأداء عند الطلب، واستحباب قبولها، فلا تضمن بالهلاك. (الدر المختار: ۵/٦٦٤، سعيد).

al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

وأما حكمها فوجوب الحفظ على المودع، وصيرورة المال أمانة في يده، ووجوب أدائه عند طلب مالكه. (الفتاوى الهندية: ٤/۳۳۸).

Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ states:

إن المبيع أمانة في يد الموكل، ألا تري أنه لو هلك في يده فالهلاك على الموكل. (بدائع الصنائع: ٦/۳۷، سعيد).

al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah, in the course of discussing a case, states:

فهو ــ أي الوكيل ــ مصدق، لأنه أمين، كذا في محيط السرخسي في باب الوكيل مع الموكل إذا اختلفا. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۳/٦٤٤).

Further, when a person appoints someone as his agent to pay zakāh, and the poor recipients are specified and known — the zakāh being given to those very people every year — then the zakāh of the payers is discharged as soon as the agent takes possession. If thereafter the money should happen to be lost from the agent's keeping, it is not obligatory or necessary to pay it again.

This is what emerges from the fatāwā of our elders (may Allah have mercy on them): that the administrators of a madrasah and their collectors stand in the position of collectors of ṣadaqah and are therefore the agents of the poor. The donors' appointment of them as agents extends only this far: that they have recognised these men as agents of the poor and handed over their contributions to them. Once these men take possession of the money in their capacity as agents, that counts in law as possession by the poor. (Derived from Imdād al-Muftīn, volume two, 1085.)

Tadhkirat al-Rashīd states:

The rector of a madrasah acts as the deputy of the caretaker and of all the students. So whatever a person gives to the rector, the rector's taking possession of it is itself the students' taking possession of it; by his taking possession it leaves the ownership of the donor and becomes the property of the students. (Tadhkirat al-Rashīd: 164; Imdād al-Fatāwā: 3/316; Niẓām al-Fatāwā: 446; Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 9/506; Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 1/227.)

قال في الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: (لا يخرج عن العهدة)

بخلاف ما إذا ضاعت في يد الساعي، لأن يده كيد الفقراء. (الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ۲/۲۷۰، سعيد).

If, on the other hand, the poor and deserving recipients are not specified, and the agent spends the zakāh money at his own discretion, then the zakāh of the payers has not been discharged and it is obligatory and necessary to pay it again.

قال في الدر المختار: ولا يخرج عن العهدة بالعزل، بل بالأداء للفقراء. وفي الشامية:

(لا يخرج عن العهدة) فلو ضاعت لا تسقط عنه الزكاة، ولو مات كان ميراثاً عنه، بخلاف ما إذا ضاعت في يد الساعي، لأن يده كيد الفقراء. (الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ۲/۲۷۰، سعيد).

Khulāṣat al-Fatāwā states:

رجل عزل زكاة ماله ووضعها في ناحية بيته، فسرقها سارق لا يقطع يده للشبهة وعليه أن يزكيها. (خلاصة الفتاوى: ۱/۲۳۸).
