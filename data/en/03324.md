### TITLE
The followers' completion of the Qurʾān when the imam recites the basmalah silently in tarāwīḥ

### QUESTION
At the completion of the Qurʾān in tarāwīḥ the imam recited bismillāh in a low voice before "Qul huwa Llāhu aḥad". Was the sunnah of the completion fulfilled for the followers or not? بینوا توجروا

### ANSWER
The noble scholars have stated explicitly that the tasmiyah is a part of the Noble Qurʾān. It is therefore a sunnah for the imam to recite the tasmiyah aloud once in the whole Qurʾān, before some sūrah or other, so that the merit of the completion of the Noble Qurʾān may be attained.

In the case in question, since the imam recited it silently and the followers did not hear it, the sunnah of the completion of the Noble Qurʾān was not fulfilled for the followers. Consider what ʿAllāmah al-Laknawī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

قد صرحوا إن ختم القرآن بجميع أجزاء في التراويح مرة سنة مؤكدة، حتى لو ترك آية منه لم يخرج عن العهدة، وقد ثبت أن البسملة أيضاً آية منه على الأصح، فيستخرج منه أنه لو قرأ تمام القرآن في التراويح، ولم يقرأ البسملة في ابتداء سورة من السور سوى ما في سورة النمل، لم يخرج عن عهدة السنية، ولو قرأها الإمام سراً خرج عن العهدة، لكن لم يخرج المقتدون عن العهدة، وبه أفتيت حين سئلت. (Aḥkām al-Qanṭarah fī Aḥkām al-Basmalah, Majmūʿ Rasāʾil al-Laknawī 1/71, Idārat al-Qurʾān Karachi)

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states:

Reciting bismillāh al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm aloud in one place is because it is a part of the whole Qurʾān, and if it is not recited aloud even in one place, the listeners' hearing of the Qurʾān will not be complete. This, then, appears to be the basis for reciting it aloud; but since the completion of the whole of the noble Qurʾān in tarāwīḥ is a sunnah, reciting the tasmiyah aloud has also been called a sunnah. (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband 4/262, large edition, Dār al-Ishāʿat)

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā records: in tarāwīḥ it is necessary to recite it aloud once, so that the Qurʾān of the followers may be complete. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā 3/519)

Imdād al-Aḥkām records:

It is better that this verse be recited aloud once in tarāwīḥ, just as the whole Qurʾān is recited aloud in tarāwīḥ. If the imam does not recite bismillāh aloud anywhere, but rather recites it silently in some one place, then the imam's own completion will be complete, but in the completion of the listeners one verse will be lacking. (Imdād al-Aḥkām 1/630)
