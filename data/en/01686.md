### TITLE
Having the wife bring about emission by hand

### QUESTION
If a woman is menstruating, may the man have her bring about emission by hand or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question there is room for emission by the wife's hand where there is a danger of intercourse taking place during menstruation. In ordinary circumstances it is not permitted, and there is also the danger of its becoming a habit. So a man who cannot keep himself under control should stay away from his wife during menstruation.

Consider what Fatāwā Shāmī states:

ويجوز أن يستمني بيد زوجته وخادمته

ويدل أيضاً على ما قلنا ما في الزيلعي حيث استدل على عدم حله بالكف بقوله تعالى: ﴿والذين هم لفروجهم حافظون﴾. الآية. وقال: فلم يبيح الاستمتاع إلا بهما أي بالزوجة والأمة، فأفاد عدم حل الاستمتاع أي قضاء الشهوة بغيرهما هذا ما ظهر لي، والله أعلم. (فتاوى الشامي: ۲/۳۹۹، باب ما يفسد الصوم ومالا يفسده).

Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

To bring about emission by one's wife's hand is permissible without any dislike where there is a need for it — for instance, where he cannot have intercourse because of menstruation, post-natal bleeding or some similar excuse, and restraint is difficult because his desire is overpowering, and so on. If it is done without need, it is disliked. (امداد المفتین: ۷/۲٤۸) ۔
