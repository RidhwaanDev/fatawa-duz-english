### TITLE
Abortion when one of twins has died

### QUESTION
If a doctor tells a woman who is five months pregnant that there are two children in her womb, one of whom is dead, and that if she does not have an abortion the other will die too, or that the mother's life will be in danger at the time of delivery — what is the ruling of the sharia on this?

### ANSWER
The jurists have stated explicitly that once the soul has been breathed into the child in the womb, abortion is by agreement not permissible.

The stage at which the soul is breathed into the child comes after 120 days, that is, after four months. On this basis, abortion is not permissible after four months by the agreement of the scholars. If, however, the woman's health deteriorates altogether and, according to an expert and experienced doctor, her life cannot be saved without an abortion, then in that case, in the light of such accepted principles as

"يختار أهون البليتين"

and

"لو كان أحدهما أعظم ضرراً من الآخر فإن الأشد يزال بأخف"

there ought to be permission for an abortion in order to save the woman's life. This is because the loss of the woman's life is a grave harm, whereas aborting the foetus is the lighter of the two; for the woman's existence can be seen while the foetus's existence cannot, and the woman's existence is certain while the foetus's existence is not certain. So in order to save an existence that can be seen and is certain, an existence that cannot be seen and is not certain may be given up. (جدید فقہی مباحث: ۱/۳۷۰)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
