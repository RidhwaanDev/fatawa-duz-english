### TITLE
Paying traffic fines out of interest money

### QUESTION
May traffic fines be paid out of interest money or not?

### ANSWER
Laws laid down by the government which are not contrary to the sharia must be obeyed; but if they are oppressive or contrary to the sharia, then they should not be obeyed. Traffic laws too are made for people's safety and convenience, so it is necessary to abide by them. If, because of a violation, the police stop someone and impose a fine or the like on him, that fine is in order, and it is impermissible to pay it out of interest money. However, if the authorities impose an excessive amount unjustly, then there is room to pay it out of interest received from a government bank; but it is not permissible to give the government interest received from a private bank.

Fatāwā Shāmī states:

وتجب طاعة الإمام عادلاً كان أو جائراً إذا لم يخالف الشرع. (فتاوى الشامي: ٤/۲٦۳، سعيد).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

When a man lives under a government, he is legally bound to abide by its law, and to act against it amounts to theft in law. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱٦/۱٤۸، جامعہ فاروقیہ) ۔

Shāmī states:

(قوله عن طاعته)

ومثله ما ذكره عن الدرر وجهه أنه إذا لم يكن كذلك يكون عاجزاً أو جائراً ظالماً يجوز الخروج عليه. (فتاوى الشامي: ٤/۲٦٤، سعيد).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
