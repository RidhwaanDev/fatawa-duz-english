### TITLE
The ruling on executing a bequest of one third of the estate among three people

### QUESTION
A man made this bequest: that out of one third of my wealth, after the fidyah and expiations have been paid, the remainder should be given to my three daughters' sons. The deceased did not, however, make clear how much each of them was to be given. The question, then, is this: does the division of these shares rest on the discretion of the heirs, or is it to be divided equally among the three?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, after the fidyah and expiations have been paid out of one third of the deceased's wealth, the remaining money will be divided equally among the three daughters' sons; it does not rest on the discretion of the heirs.

قال في الفتاوى الهندية: ولو قال العبد: الذي أوصيت به لفلان وقد أوصيت به لفلان آخر يكون بينهما نصفين. (الفتاوى الهندية: ٦/۹۳).
وقال في الفتاوى السراجية: لو أوصي لزيد وعمرو بثلث ماله فإذا عمرو ميت فالثلث لزيد

إذا أوصي لرجل بثلث ماله ولآخر بجميع ماله ولم يجز الورثة فالثلث بينهما نصفين. (الفتاوى السراجية، ص ۵۷٤).
