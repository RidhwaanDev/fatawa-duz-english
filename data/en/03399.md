### TITLE
The ruling when in the second Friday congregation one person delivers the sermon and another leads the ṣalāh

### QUESTION
In Spain, in a certain prayer hall, the Friday ṣalāh is held twice on account of the narrowness of the place and the great number of people, since there is no room for all to pray together. Now the question is this: one imam delivered the sermon and led the ṣalāh for the first congregation; now that same imam delivers only the sermon for the second congregation, and the ṣalāh is led by a second imam who had not taken part in the first congregation. Will the ṣalāh be valid or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
For the validity of the Friday ṣalāh it is not a condition that the Friday ṣalāh be obligatory upon the one delivering the sermon. Even if a boy who has not reached puberty delivers the sermon and one who has reached puberty leads the ṣalāh, the Friday ṣalāh will be valid. Accordingly, in the case asked about too the ṣalāh was valid and correct. But this should not be done as a regular practice; whoever is to be the imam for the second congregation should also deliver the sermon himself. See Fatāwā Shāmī, which states:

خطب صبي بإذن السلطان وصلى بالغ جاز كذا في الخلاصة. (۲/۱٤۱، سعيد).
وقال في الدر المختار: وهو المختار، وقال الشامي: والأكثر على الجواز. (الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ۲/۱٦۲، سعيد).
وقال في مجمع الأنهر: ولا ينبغي أن يصلي غير الخطيب لأن الجمعة مع الخطبة كشيء واحد فإن فعل بأن خطب صبي بإذن السلطان وصلي بالغ جاز. (مجمع الأنهر: ۱/۱۷۲، ط: كوئته). وكذا في حاشية الطحطاوي (۱/۵۰۸) وزاد فيه: لكن الأولى الاتحاد كما في شرح الآثار. (وكذا في خلاصة الفتاوى: ۱/۲۰۵).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states: No explicit ruling on this matter was found; however, since it is not a condition for the validity of the sermon that the Friday ṣalāh be obligatory upon the one delivering it, it appears from this that such a person may deliver the sermon. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 4/128).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah records:

I have not seen this ruling stated explicitly anywhere. This much they do write, that it is not necessary for the one delivering the sermon and the imam to be one and the same person; it is, however, preferable that the one who delivers the sermon should also lead the Friday ṣalāh. Along with this there is also the point that if a boy who has not reached puberty delivered the sermon and one who has reached puberty led the Friday ṣalāh, even then the Friday ṣalāh will be validly performed — because a person who has already performed the Friday ṣalāh no longer has the Friday ṣalāh as an obligation upon him, and if he joins the Friday ṣalāh in some other masjid this ṣalāh will be supererogatory in his case, just as it is in the case of one who has not reached puberty. And since the sermon delivered by one who has not reached puberty is also sufficient for the validity of the Friday ṣalāh, in this way, to all appearances, his delivering the sermon will also be sufficient. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 8/268, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).
