### TITLE
Ascribing a manifestation in human form to Allah Most High

### QUESTION
In Fayḍ al-Bārī ʿAllāmah Kashmīrī has written (4/406) that it is possible for Allah Most High to manifest Himself in human form. He then states that it is possible for this to remain with all the concomitants of a human being after the manifestation, with its particular limb (حقو، فأخذت بحقو الرحمن). This is most astonishing. How is such a thing fitting for the majesty of Allah Most High? ʿAllāmah al-Kawtharī (may Allah have mercy on him) has written about it in the footnote of Kitāb al-Asmāʾ wa al-Ṣifāt: تعالى الله عن هذه الوثنيات بعد الإسلام. (ص: ۳٦۹).

### ANSWER
The explanation of what Shāh Ṣāḥib Kashmīrī (may Allah have mercy on him) has written has been given by Mawlānā Badr-e ʿĀlam (may Allah have mercy on him) in the footnote, and its substance is this. Just as the inner speech is an attribute of Allah Most High and is eternal, while the uttered speech is created and points to the inner speech but is not an attribute of Allah Most High, in the same way there is, on the one hand, the Being of Allah Most High, which is free of every kind of shape and form and of their concomitants; and, on the other hand, there is His manifestation, which appears in various forms. It has a form and a shape, but it points to the Being; it is not the Divine Being itself.

Another example of this may be light, which considered in itself is neither white nor red, neither long nor short; but when it appears in the form of a bulb or a rod it appears in various shapes. Or it is like the soul, which is neither red nor white, neither long nor broad, but after appearing in bodies is seen with various qualities.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
