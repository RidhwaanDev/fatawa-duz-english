### TITLE
Establishing the crescent moon by a radio announcement

### QUESTION
The decision of the Jamʿiyyat al-ʿUlamāʾ is generally broadcast on the radio. Is the sighting of the crescent established by this or not?

### ANSWER
A radio report has the status of an announcement. If this announcement is made by a properly constituted moon-sighting committee that reaches its decision in accordance with the testimony and rules of the sharia, and the one making the announcement is a trustworthy Muslim representative of that committee, then it is sound and correct to rely upon it.

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

If the moon-sighting department, after carrying out its inquiry in the manner laid down by the sharia, announces the sighting by telegram or radio on the order of the ruler of the country, then in that city in particular, and in the places attached to that city such as the towns in its vicinity, it is sound in the sharia to take that announcement into account and act upon it. (فتاوی محمودیہ ۱۰/۵۵، باب رؤیۃ الہلال) ۔

Īḍāḥ al-Masāʾil states:

A radio report by itself does not establish a sighting in the sharia; rather, for a radio report to be reliable the following conditions must be met:

(1) A Muslim ruler, or a religiously observant official of some moon committee who follows the sharia, should himself make the announcement on the radio, or have it made, after the sighting has been established in accordance with the sharia.

(2) The announcement should be made in these terms: that testimony has been taken, and the sighting has been established by testimony that satisfies the sharia.

(3) The official should also introduce himself, saying that he is so-and-so, son of so-and-so, and that he is the official of such-and-such a ruler or such-and-such a moon committee.

(4) The particulars of the radio announcement should be placed before responsible scholars, who should satisfy themselves by inquiry and investigation, and their directions should be followed.

(5) A radio announcement can be relied upon only so far afield that accepting it would never make the month twenty-eight or thirty-one days long in your own area.

(6) The sky in your area should not have been clear at the time of that sighting; otherwise the announcement will not be taken into account. With the conditions mentioned above a radio announcement is reliable; without them it is not. (ایضاح المسائل: ص ۸۰، نعیمیہ) ۔

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

Radio: If a judge appointed under the sharia, or a council of recognised scholars, or a moon committee whose decision the Muslims accept, takes testimony in the proper manner and decides the question of the moon, and that decision is broadcast over the radio by the judge, or by the head of the council of scholars or of the moon committee, or by their trusted representative, and the scholars of other places accept it, then it is correct to act upon it. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۵/۱۸۵) ۔

Fatāwā Farīdiyyah states:

The announcement of a moon committee and its report by radio and the like will be worthy of reliance when the report is a detailed one, mentioning who gave the ruling and the ground on which it was given, so that a strong probability is obtained. (فتاوی فریدیہ: ٤/۵۷) ۔
