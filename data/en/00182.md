### TITLE
The authenticity of the hadith "إذا تحيرتم في الأمور فاستعينوا بأهل القبور"

### QUESTION
What is the truth about "إذا تحيرتم في الأمور فاستعينوا باهل القبور"? Is it a hadith or not?

### ANSWER
This hadith is fabricated. Consider the following.

Iqtiḍāʾ al-Ṣirāṭ al-Mustaqīm states:

ما يرويه بعض الناس من أنه قال: (إذا تحيرتم في الأمور فاستعينوا بأهل القبور) أو نحو هذا فهو كلام موضوع، مكذوب باتفاق العلماء. (اقتضاء الصراط المستقيم لا بن تيمية: ۲ /۱۹٦).

Majmūʿ Fatāwā Ibn Taymiyyah states:

وإن كان بعض الناس من المشايخ المتبوعين يحتج بما يرويه عن النبي ﷺ أنه قال: (إذا أعيتكم الأمور فعليكم بأهل القبور أو فاستعينوا بأهل القبور) فهذا الحديث كذب مفتري على النبي ﷺ بإجماع العارفين بحديثه لم يروه أحد من العلماء بذلك ولا يوجد في شيء من كتب الحديث المعتمدة. (مجموع فتاوى ابن تيمية: ۱/۳۵٦).

It also states:

ويروون حديثاً هو كذب باتفاق أهل المعرفة وهو (إذا أعيتكم الأمور فعليكم بأصحاب القبور) وإنما هذا وضع من فتح باب الشرك. (مجموع فتاوى ابن تيمية: ۱۱/۲۹۳).

Majmūʿat al-Fatāwā states:

"إذا تحيرتم في الأمور فاستعينوا بأصحاب القبور"

"When you are perplexed in some matter, ask the people of the graves." This is not a hadith; it is somebody's saying. Its meaning, set out in full, is this: when you are in doubt as to whether something is lawful or unlawful, do not act on your own reasoning, but follow the early scholars who now lie asleep in their graves. It may also mean: when worldly affairs distress you, look at the people of the graves, who left this world and set out on the journey to the hereafter; you too will have to make that journey and leave this world. And it may mean: when you find yourself unable to achieve your purpose, then ask Allah, using the people of the graves as a means, so that through their blessing your supplication may be answered — not that you should regard them as being able to solve difficulties in their own right, or as partners with Allah in the running of the world, for that is open polytheism. واللہ اعلم ۔ (Muʿallim al-Fiqh, the Urdu translation of Majmūʿat al-Fatāwā: 1/159, 160).

Shāh ʿAbd al-ʿAzīz al-Muḥaddith al-Dihlawī (may Allah have mercy on him) writes:

"إذا تحيرتم في الأمور فاستعينوا بأصحاب القبور"

That is, "When you are at a loss in your affairs, seek help from the people of the graves." This is not a hadith; it is the saying of some pious elder, and it bears several meanings. One is that when you are perplexed by conflicting evidence about whether certain things are lawful or unlawful, you should set your own reasoning aside and follow those authorities who have passed away and reached their graves. This meaning is the closer to what has been reported from ʿAbdullāh ibn Masʿūd (may Allah be pleased with him) and Sufyān al-Thawrī (may Allah have mercy on him). Another meaning is that when worldly affairs distress you and your heart is straitened by them, you should look at the people of the graves and see how they left the world behind and turned towards the hereafter; and you should realise that the same resting place — the grave — awaits you where they have already arrived. Knowing this will make the hardships and trials of this world easier for you to bear. In short, this saying is not an explicit text about seeking help from the people of the graves. (Fatāwā ʿAzīzī: 1/179).

Mawlānā Muḥammad Sarfarāz Khān Ṣafdar says:

From this passage of Shāh Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) it is clear that this is not a hadith, and that it does not carry the meaning which grave-worshippers take from it. The saying of Ibn Masʿūd (may Allah be pleased with him) to which Shāh Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) alluded is recorded in Mishkāt (1/32), on the authority of Razīn, in the words "من كان مستناً فليستن بمن قد مات الخ". (Guldastah-i Tawḥīd, p. 151).

In sum, this hadith is fabricated.
