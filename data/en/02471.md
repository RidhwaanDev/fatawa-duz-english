### TITLE
The meaning of the hadith "الغلام مرتہن الخ"

### QUESTION
"الغلام مرتهن بعقيقته يذبح عنه يوم السابع ويحلق رأسه ويسمي". (سنن ابن ماجه، ص ۲۲۸)

What is the meaning of this hadith?

### ANSWER
The hadith mentioned above may bear four meanings:

(1) The child will be withheld and barred from intercession on account of the ʿaqīqah not having been performed; without an ʿaqīqah he will not intercede.

(2) The child is withheld and held back from acceptance so long as the ʿaqīqah is not performed.

In both these meanings, however, there is this difficulty: in the light of them the ʿaqīqah ought to be obligatory or an emphasized sunnah, whereas the ʿaqīqah is neither obligatory nor an emphasized sunnah — unless one holds that the obligation of the ʿaqīqah has been abrogated.

(3) The third meaning is that the child is like a pledged object. A pledge is given against a debt, and when the pledge returns to the pledger he should discharge the debt or loan. So when Allah Most High has bestowed a blessing or a mercy upon you, offer the ʿaqīqah for the sake of Allah Most High in return for it. In this meaning too a suspicion arises that the ʿaqīqah is binding.

(4) Rahn is used in the sense of confinement, so the meaning is that the child is held captive in soiled, blood-stained hair; on the seventh day perform the ʿaqīqah, shave his hair and release him from that captivity. This meaning is supported by the words: "فأميطوا عنه الأذى" ۔ ʿAllāmah al-Suyūṭī (may Allah have mercy on him) has written:

وقيل: معناه أنه مرهون بأذى شعره واستند بقوله: "فأميطوا عنه الأذى وهو ما علق به من دم الرحم. (حاشية سنن ابن ماجه، ص ۲۲۸، رقم الحاشية، ۶).

The meaning of ʿaqīqah, too, is that hair which is upon the child's head at the time of birth and which is cut off and cleaned away:

وهو الشعر الذي يكون على المولود حين يولد من العق والشق والقطع. (سنن ابن ماجه، ص: ۲۲۸، رقم الحاشية، ۵).
