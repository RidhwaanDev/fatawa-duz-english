### TITLE
When the Friday sermon begins during ṭawāf

### QUESTION
If the Friday sermon begins while a person is performing ṭawāf, is it correct for him to carry on with the ṭawāf, or should he stop and listen to the sermon? And if he does not stop, is he sinful or not?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, if the sermon begins in the middle of the ṭawāf, it is necessary to stop the ṭawāf and to listen to the sermon attentively and with full concentration. Carrying on with the ṭawāf disturbs one's listening to the sermon, so the ṭawāf should be stopped; otherwise the person will be sinful. Consider what Sharḥ al-Lubāb states:

فصل في مكروهاته (أي الطواف)

والطواف عند الخطبة أي مطلقاً لا شعاره بالإعراض ولو كان ساكتاً وإقامة المكتوبة فإن ابتداء الطواف حينئذٍ مكروه بلا شبهة. (شرح اللباب، ص۱۸۳). (وكذا في غنية الناسك، ص ٦۸).

Tuḥfah-yi Ḥujjāj, quoting Majmūʿah-yi Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Karachi, states:

Listening to the sermon silently and attentively is necessary; and since performing ṭawāf during the Friday sermon disturbs one's listening to the sermon, performing ṭawāf at that time is prohibited and must be avoided. (Tuḥfah-yi Ḥujjāj, p. 37).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
