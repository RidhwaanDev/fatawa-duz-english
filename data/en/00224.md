### TITLE
Performing ablution while standing

### QUESTION
In this country, and in some other countries, people perform ablution standing, since the way the washing area is built makes this easier. Is it permissible to perform ablution standing, and which is better in ablution — sitting or standing?

### ANSWER
Performing ablution standing is permissible and there is nothing objectionable in it. The basic principle in ablution is that a person should adopt whatever position makes it easy to wash the limbs. So if, because of the way the washing area is built, it is easier to wash some limbs seated and others standing, it is better to do that; for if a person sits down to perform ablution in a place built for standing, it will be awkward and the used water will also fall on his clothes. Thus, when Ḥaḍrat Muftī Maḥmūd al-Ḥasan Gangohī (may Allah have mercy on him) was asked about washing the feet while standing, he wrote that there is no harm in washing the feet standing; rather, washing them standing is better, since it protects one from the used water. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah 7/16)

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār also states:

والجلوس في مكان مرتفع تحرزاً عن الماء المستعمل. (الدر المختار ۱/ ۱۲۷)

This shows that adopting a position in ablution which makes things easy and keeps one clear of the used water is not merely acceptable but better.

Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī states:

عن كريب مولى ابن عباس رضي الله عنه أن عبد الله بن عباس رضي الله عنه أخبره أنه بات ليلة عند ميمونة زوج النبي وهي خالته فاضطجعت في عرض الوسادة واضطجع رسول الله ﷺ وأهله في طولها فنام رسول الله ﷺ حتى إذ انتصف الليل أو قبله بقليل أو بعده بقليل استيقظ رسول الله ﷺ فجلس يمسح النوم عن وجهه بيده ثم قرأ العشر الآيات الخواتم من سورة آل عمران ثم قام إلى شن معلقة فتوضأ منها. (صحيح البخاري باب قراءة القرآن بعد الحدث ۱/۳۰)

This report shows that the Prophet ﷺ performed ablution standing from a hanging waterskin. It should be kept in mind, however, that performing ablution standing is permissible on grounds of convenience, whereas the practice of the Prophet ﷺ in the manner of ablution, as reported from Ḥaḍrat ʿAlī (may Allah honour his face), was to perform ablution seated. Thus Sunan al-Tirmidhī states:

"عن أبي حية قال رأيت علياً توضأ فغسل حتى أنقاهما ثم مضمض ثلاثاً واستنشق ثلاثاً وغسل قدميه إلى الكعبين ثم قام فأخذ فضل طهوره فشربه وهو قائم ثم قال: أحببت أن أريكم كيف كان طهر رسول الله ﷺ. (الجامع الترمذي باب في وضوء النبي كيف كان: ۱/۱۷).

This report shows that his practice was to perform ablution seated. Performing ablution seated is therefore recommended, and performing it standing is permissible.
