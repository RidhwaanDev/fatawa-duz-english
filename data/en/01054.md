### TITLE
The correct measure of ṣadaqat al-fiṭr adopted by the senior scholars

### QUESTION
It is agreed upon that ṣadaqat al-fiṭr is half a ṣāʿ of wheat and one ṣāʿ of barley; but how much is half a ṣāʿ in kilograms?

### ANSWER
Most of the senior scholars (may Allah have mercy on them) have written that half a ṣāʿ is one and three-quarter sers by English weight.

In the treatise "al-Ṭarāʾif wa al-Ẓarāʾif" of Ḥakīm al-Ummat Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ashraf ʿAlī Thānawī (may Allah have mercy on him, may his secret be sanctified) it is recorded:

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Muḥammad Yaʿqūb Ṣāḥib possessed a mudd whose chain of transmission runs unbroken back to the mudd of Ḥaḍrat Zayd ibn Thābit (may Allah be pleased with him) (which he had made by measuring it against the mudd of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ). Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Thānawī (may his secret be sanctified) filled it twice and weighed it (since it consists of two mudds), and it came to a ser and a chhaṭānk by the ser of 88 tolas. (الطرائف و الظرائف: حصۂ دوم: ص۱۲) ۔

By this reckoning the weight of half a ṣāʿ comes to one hundred and forty tolas and three māshas, which by the ser of 80 tolas is one and three-quarter sers.

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

The safer course is that one and three-quarter sers of wheat, by the ser of eighty tolas, be given for one ṣadaqat al-fiṭr. (اوزانِ شرعیہ: ص: ۳۸) ۔

Mawlānā Khālid Sayfullāh says:

The opinion of most of the muftis of India is close to the opinion of Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him). (کتاب الفتاوی: تیسرا حصہ: ۳٦۲، زمزم) ۔

That is, by the modern reckoning, since one tola equals 11.664 grams, 140 tolas will come to 1.63296 kilograms.

Mawlānā Mujībullāh Nadwī (may Allah have mercy on him) writes in Islāmī Fiqh:

If someone gives wheat or wheat flour for ṣadaqat al-fiṭr, he should give one and three-quarter sers of wheat or flour by the ser of 80 tolas. In this age the best course is to give, instead of grain, whatever is the price of one and three-quarter sers of wheat or three and a half sers of barley. (اسلامی فقہ: ۱/٤۲۲) ۔

Mawlānā Sayyid Zawwār Ḥusayn Shāh Ṣāḥib, who was a scholar of careful research, says in ʿUmdat al-Fiqh:

By the weight of the English ser — that is, the ser which is of eighty tolas and is in use in India and Pakistan — one ṣāʿ is about three and a half sers and half a ṣāʿ is one and three-quarter sers. This is the view on which the verdict is given. (عمدۃ الفقہ: ۳/۱۷۰، مجددیہ) ۔

In the rulings on ṣadaqat al-fiṭr printed at the end of Ḥaḍrat Thānawī's (may Allah have mercy on him) Khuṭbāt al-Jumuʿah, one and three-quarter sers of wheat, or its value, is likewise recorded. Consider:

If he gives wheat, half a ṣāʿ is obligatory, which by English weight is one and three-quarter sers. (خطبات الاحکام لجمعات العام: ۱۵۸، احکامِ صدقۂ فطر) ۔

In Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm, Muftī ʿAzīz al-Raḥmān Ṣāḥib writes:

Ṣadaqat al-fiṭr, on the sevenfold system of weights, taking the mithqāl as so many māshas, as is well known, comes by English weight to about one and three-quarter sers of wheat, and the calculation of this has been worked out; this is also the more cautious course. (فتاوی دار العلوم دیوبند مدلل و مکمل: ٦/۳۰٤، مسائل صدقۃ الفطر، دار الاشاعت) ۔

In Fatāwā Muftī Maḥmūd, Ḥaḍrat Muftī Maḥmūd Ṣāḥib of Pakistan (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

The safer course is that one and three-quarter sers of wheat, by the ser of eighty tolas, be given for one ṣadaqat al-fiṭr. (فتاوی مفتی محمود وغیرہ من المفتین: ۳/۳۱۵) ۔

Ḥaḍrat Muftī Kifāyatullāh Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) writes in Kifāyat al-Muftī:

In short, the measure of a dirham is correctly 3 māshas by the Delhi tola, and on this reckoning the more cautious weight of half a ṣāʿ comes to about one and three-quarter sers by the ser of eighty rupees. So in ṣadaqat al-fiṭr one should give one and three-quarter sers of wheat by the ser of eighty rupees' weight. (کفایت المفتی: ٤/۳۱۱، دار الاشاعت) ۔

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

In ṣadaqat al-fiṭr one should give one and three-quarter sers of wheat by the ser of eighty tolas; half a ṣāʿ comes to one kilogram five hundred and seventy-five grams. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۵/۱۷۲، صاع کا وزن اور صدقۂ فطر کی صحیح مقدار) ۔

In another place it is recorded:

If it is pure wheat, then ṣadaqat al-fiṭr will be discharged if one and three-quarter kilograms are given. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۵/۱۷۷) ۔

Īḍāḥ al-Masāʾil states:

The weight of half a ṣāʿ is 135 tolas. (فتاوی دار العلوم: ٦/۳۰۵، ۳۲۷ ۔ و جواہر الفقہ: ۱/٤۲٤ ۔ و فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۵/۱۷٤) ۔

And one tola is eleven grams and 664 milligrams.

Consider the complete table:

• One rattī = 121½ milligrams,

• Ten rattīs = 1215 milligrams,

• 96 rattīs = 11664 milligrams = 11 grams 664 milligrams, the old tola. A tola of 96 rattīs: measured against the present-day tola of ten grams, one tola will be one gram 664 milligrams more.

• One māsha = 972 milligrams,

• 12 māshas = 11664 milligrams = eleven grams 664 milligrams = one tola.

• 135 tolas = 1620 māshas = 1574 grams 640 milligrams.

• One and a half kilograms 74 grams 640 milligrams = half a ṣāʿ, the measure of ṣadaqat al-fiṭr.

(ایضاح المسائل: ۱۰۱، صدقۂ فطر اور نصف صاع کے حساب کے لیے بہترین نقشہ، کتب خانہ نعیمیہ) ۔

The upshot is that, according to the research of the senior scholars (may Allah have mercy on them), the measure of ṣadaqat al-fiṭr works out to about one and three-quarter sers, that is, 140 tolas by the ser of eighty tolas, which by the modern reckoning is 1.63296 kilograms. In the last-mentioned book, Īḍāḥ al-Masāʾil, Muftī Shabbīr Aḥmad Ṣāḥib has quoted from Jawāhir al-Fiqh the research that puts it at 135 tolas, which works out to about one and a half kilograms, 74 grams and 640 milligrams.

In short, by way of caution one and three-quarter kilograms — that is, 1.750 kilograms — should be given as ṣadaqat al-fiṭr, and this will take in the research of most of the senior scholars (may Allah have mercy on them).

One and three-quarter kilograms is also easier to remember than one and three-quarter sers; and since the ser is not in use in most countries today, people do not understand what one and three-quarter sers is, and so they should be told one and three-quarter kilograms. The research of some senior scholars (may Allah have mercy on them), such as Muftī Rashīd Aḥmad Ludhyānwī (may Allah have mercy on him), differs from the research given above, but we have given preference to the statement and research of most of the senior scholars and eminent muftis (may Allah have mercy on them), and to walk on the path of the majority is more fitting and better.

Shaykh Asʿad Muḥammad Saʿīd al-Ṣāgharjī says in "الفقه الحنفي وأدلته" (۱/۳۷۸، مقدار الواجب):

وزنه نصف الصاع كيلوغرام ونصف، وثمان أجزاء من الألف من الغرام. والله أعلم.

That is, ṣadaqat al-fiṭr in the modern measure is 1 kilogram 625 grams, roughly one and three-quarter kilograms, which agrees with the measure fixed by the senior scholars (may Allah have mercy on them).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
