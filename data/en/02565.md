### TITLE
Using animal bone in dentistry

### QUESTION
When a person reaches old age the bones of his gums become weak, and because of this weakness, or because of some illness or other causes, those bones can support neither natural teeth nor artificial ones. To strengthen these bones the doctors have adopted a method: they prepare a compound from animal bone or its marrow and with it strengthen the gums, after which fixing the teeth becomes possible. So may animal bones be used in this way or not?

### ANSWER
Benefiting from the bones of animals is permissible and correct, whether those animals were slaughtered in the Sharia manner or not, because there is no life in bones and therefore death does not pass into them either.

Note what Al-Hidāyah states:

ولا بأس ببيع عظام الميتة وعصبها

والانتفاع بذلك كله لا نها طاهرة لا يحلها الموت لعدم الحياة. (الهداية: ۳/۵۵).

Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ states:

وأما عظم الميتة وعصبها وشعرها

فيجوز بيعها والانتفاع بها عندنا. (بدائع الصنائع: ۵/۱٤۲، سعيد).

Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

That this purpose be served by the organs of animals — this too has been in use since ancient times. Both of these forms are, from every point of view — religious, worldly, personal and social — free of risk and free of harm. Man uses all the creatures of the earth, minerals, plants and animals, for his own benefit, and for that benefit he may cut and trim them, pound and grind them, indeed burn them and use them by methods of chemical decomposition. (Jawāhir al-Fiqh, vol. 7, pp. 51–53).

See further: (Jadīd Fiqhī Mabāḥith, vol. 1; Naʾe Masāʾil awr ʿUlamāʾ-e Hind ke Fayṣle, p. 143; and ʿAṣr-e Ḥāḍir ke Pechīdah Masāʾil kā Sharʿī Ḥal, p. 158).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
