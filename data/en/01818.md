### TITLE
Turning part of a mosque into a path or a platform

### QUESTION
May part of a mosque be taken out of the mosque and turned into a path or a platform, or not?

### ANSWER
According to the position on which the fatwa is given, once a place has become a mosque in the legal sense, its character as a mosque can never come to an end until the Day of Judgement, nor can anyone bring it to an end. So in the case in question too, no part of the mosque may be taken out of it.

Consider what al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

لو بني فوقه بيتاً للإمام لا يضر لأنه من المصالح أما لو تمت المسجدية ثم أراد البناء منع ولو قال عنيت ذلك لم يصدق تاتارخانية، فإذا كان هذا في الواقف فكيف بغيره

(الدر المختار مع فتاوى الشامي: ٤/۸..).
وأيضاً فيه: ولو خرب ما حوله واستغني عنه يبقي مسجد اً عند الإمام والثاني أبداً إلى قيام الساعة وبه يفتي. (الدر المختار مع فتاوى الشامي: ٤/۳۵۸، سعيد).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

Land that has once been taken into a mosque will remain a mosque until the Day of Judgement; it cannot be taken out of the mosque for any need whatsoever. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 6/).

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

The fourth important and fundamental difference between mosques and other endowed land is that a mosque becomes a mosque for ever. Whether the mosque falls into ruin and becomes unusable, or is wrongfully seized, in every case it will remain a mosque until the Day of Judgement. (Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 2/).

Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ (may Allah have mercy on him) writes:

A place that has become a mosque will remain a mosque until the Day of Judgement. It is not lawful in any way to put that place to any other use. Even if someone wished to build ten mosques in place of the one, this mosque could still not lose its character as a mosque. (Imdād al-Muftīn: 2/636).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
