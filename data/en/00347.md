### TITLE
A mosque built with unlawful wealth

### QUESTION
If a mosque has been built with unlawful wealth, should it be pulled down, or may prayer be performed in it?

### ANSWER
Some of the senior scholars (may Allah have mercy on them) take a strict line on this question out of caution, holding that if a mosque has been built with wholly unlawful wealth, or with wealth that is mostly unlawful, then prayer in it is strictly disliked; it should be pulled down or demolished and another mosque built in its place, or else the mosque should be kept closed. Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ẓafar Aḥmad ʿUthmānī (may Allah have mercy on him), however, has written in Imdād al-Aḥkām that if an amount equal to the unlawful wealth that was used is given away in charity, then prayer in it will be permissible — provided that the materials were not bought by pointing to that unlawful money at the time of purchase. See the following:

Imdād al-Aḥkām states:

A mosque built with unlawful wealth is one in which the mortar, the bricks, the timber and so on are usurped property, or in which the land is usurped. If it is the money that is unlawful, then the money itself does not go into the mosque; what goes into the mosque is the material bought with it. Now, if the material was first taken on credit and the price was afterwards paid out of the unlawful money, then no unlawful wealth has gone into the mosque. If the price was paid in cash, there are two possibilities. The first is that the transaction was made by pointing to the unlawful money: "Give me such-and-such an item for this money." The second is that the transaction was not made by pointing to the unlawful money; rather he said, "Give me an item for ten rupees" or "for fifteen rupees," so that the price was an unspecified sum of money, and he then paid that price in cash out of the unlawful money. In the first case the goods bought will be unlawful, and using them in the mosque is itself unlawful. In the second case the goods bought have not become unlawful, and using them in the mosque is in order — although he has sinned by paying the price out of unlawful money. (امداد الاحکام: ۳/۳٤۳) ۔

Radd al-Muḥtār states:

(قوله اكتسب حراما الخ) توضيح المسألة ما في التاتارخانية حيث قال رجل اكتسب مالا من حرام ثم اشتري فهذا على خمسة أوجه: (۱) أما أن دفع تلك الدراهم إلى البائع أولا ثم اشتري منه بها (۲) أو اشتري قبل الدفع بها ودفعها (۳) أو اشتري قبل الدفع بها ودفع غيرها (٤) أو اشتري مطلقا ودفع تلك الدراهم (۵) أو اشترى بدراهم أخر ودفع تلك الدراهم، قال أبو نصر: يطيب له ولا يجب عليه أن يتصدق إلا في الوجه الأول وإليه ذهب الفقيه أبو الليث، لكن هذا خلاف ظاهر الرواية فإنه نص في الجامع الصغير: إذا غصب ألفا فاشتري بها جارية وباعها بألفين تصدق بالربح وقال الكرخي: في الوجه الأول والثاني لا يطيب وفي الثلاث الأخيرة يطيب، وقال أبو بكر: لا يطيب في الكل، لكن الفتوي الآن على قلو الكرخي دفعا للحرج عن الناس.

وفي الولوالجية: وقال بعضهم: لا يطيب في الوجوه كلها وهو المختار، ولكن الفتوي اليوم على قول الكرخي دفعا للحرج لكثرة الحرام وعلى هذا مشى المصنف في كتاب الغصب تبعاً للدرر وغيرها. (رد المحتار: ۵/۲۳۵).
