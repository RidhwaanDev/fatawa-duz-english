### TITLE
Who is entitled to a bonus given by a company

### QUESTION
Some companies give shopkeepers a bonus in order to promote their products. For example, on certain days of the year the company informs a shopkeeper: keep the invoices for whatever goods of our company you sell and let us know about them, and we will give a sum of money as a bonus on every item.

Now, who is to receive this bonus money? The owners of the shop say the money should come to them, because it is their goods that are sold in the shop and the staff receive their wages. The staff, however, demand that the bonus money should come to them, because the company itself has said that it gives it for the staff.

In support of this they also produced a letter from the company in which it is written that this bonus is to be given to those who work at your premises. Kindly inform us of the ruling of the sharia: who is entitled to take this bonus? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
According to general custom and practice, a bonus given by a company or by a seller, or a discount given on the purchase of goods, goes to the buyer — that is, to the owner of the shop — because it is given on account of the purchase of the goods.

In Imdād al-Aḥkām, Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ẓafar Aḥmad ʿUthmānī (may Allah have mercy on him) says that the commission given to a buyer at the end of the year on every hundred is a voluntary gift from the seller. (امداد الاحکام: ۳/۳۸٦) ۔

Kitāb al-Fatāwā states:

If some further item is given as a bonus to every buyer along with the goods purchased, there is no doubt about its permissibility; it is a kind of addition from the seller's side, and the jurists have held an addition to the goods sold to be permissible. (کتاب الفتاوی: ۵/۲٤۹، بعنوان "خریدار کو انعام" ) ۔

See further: (جدید معاملات کے شرعی احکام: ۱/۱۲٦) ۔

In the case asked about, however, the company itself has made clear several times that this bonus is for your staff. So in the sharia those who work in the shop will be the ones entitled to this bonus.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
