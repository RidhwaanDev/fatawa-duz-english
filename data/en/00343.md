### TITLE
Appointing a trustee for life

### QUESTION
Should trustees be chosen for life or for a fixed term? And once that term is over, may they put themselves forward again as candidates or not?

### ANSWER
Trustees may be chosen for life, and they may remain in that office until death, so long as no dishonesty and nothing contrary to the sharia comes from them. When the Rightly Guided Caliphs (may Allah be pleased with them) were given the caliphate, the appointment was not for a fixed term but for their whole lives. In our own time too, the members chosen for madrasahs are generally chosen for life rather than for a fixed term. That said, if the members have laid down in the (—) that their appointment runs for a set period, that is also correct.
