### TITLE
A woman working outside the home

### QUESTION
If a woman goes out of the house to work while observing the veil, does this go against ﴿وقرن في بيوتكن﴾ or not? And what is the ruling on her working? بینوا توجروا

### ANSWER
Islam has made the woman the queen of the home, and the make-up of her body is also different from that of a man. To lay the burden of outside work upon her is an injustice and an oppression. There are several reasons for this:

(1) Menstruation: in those days a woman's constitution carries a kind of burden.

(2) Pregnancy (الحمل): to work outside along with the difficulties of pregnancy is extremely hard.

(3) Childbirth and post-natal bleeding: in these days a woman is in the greatest need of complete rest and comfort.

(4) Suckling and nursing: feeding the child, bringing him up and caring for him properly, and so forth.

(5) Physical build: because of all these things a woman has neither the time nor the strength for such work.

The grounds and reasons why work outside the home is not permitted:

(1) When a woman leaves the house it is binding and necessary for her to be covered completely, from head to foot.

(2) A journey that stirs up temptation is forbidden to a woman, and today this is part and parcel of working outside the home.

(3) Mixing with non-mahrams is impermissible in the light of the texts of the sharia, and in work outside the home it is extremely difficult to avoid.

(4) Most women involved in such work fall into going unveiled and into displaying the places of adornment and the beauty of the body.

(5) Where is the time left over for running the household well?

Hazrat Maulānā Muhammad Yūsuf Ludhyānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) says: Those who place a man's burden on a woman's frail shoulders are doing her a wrong. (آپ کے مسائل اور ان کا حل: ۸/۵۵).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

If a woman takes up employment in a company, many forbidden things will be committed: she will appear unveiled; there will be mixing with non-mahram men, and on some occasions there may even arise the chance of being alone with them in seclusion; there will be free and easy conversation with them, and coming and going with non-mahram men, and much else besides. For this reason the sharia cannot permit such employment. If a woman is in distress, then some other lawful means should be adopted for her livelihood. The best course is for her to remarry in a suitable place. As long as this is not possible, or the woman is for some reason unwilling to remarry, she should begin teaching young boys and girls at home, or take up some household craft and meet her expenses from it. If no such arrangement can be made and the woman and her daughters have no wealth, so that they face starvation, then it becomes binding upon their relatives and kinsfolk to arrange food and maintenance for them. If they do not do so, this duty falls upon the people of the locality and the Muslim community. (فتاویٰ رحیمیہ: ۵/٤۵۸، مکتبۃ الاحسان، دیوبند).

Yes, if no arrangement can be made and the woman is compelled to go out, she may go out while observing the following conditions:

(1) The permission of the husband, or of the guardian and the one in charge, is necessary.
كما هو مرقوم في عامة كتب الفقه.

(2) She should go out wearing a face-veil, and in a plain cloak; she should avoid a cloak with decoration and embroidery on it.

(3) She should not apply perfume, powder and the like — that is, she should not dress herself up.

(4) On a journey of the distance fixed by the sharia, her husband or a mahram must be with her.

(5) There should be no mixing with non-mahrams and no fear of temptation.

(6) Every employment in which being alone in seclusion with a man cannot be avoided is impermissible and forbidden.

عن أسماء بنت أبي بكر رضي الله عنه قالت: تزوجني الزبير رضي الله عنه وما له في الأرض من مال ولا مملوك ولا شيء غير فرسه قالت: فكنت أعلف فرسه وأكفيه مؤنته وأسوسه وأدق النوي لناضحه وأعلفه وأستقي الماء وأخرز غربه وأعجن ولم أكن أحسن أخبز وكان يخبز لي جارات من الأنصار وكن نسوة صدق وكنت أنقل النوي من أرض الزبير التي أقطعه رسول الله ﷺ على رأسي وهي على ثلثي فرسخ، قالت: فجئت يوماً والنوي على رأسي فلقيت رسول الله ﷺ ومعه نفر من أصحابه فدعاني ثم قال: إخ إخ ليحملني خلفه
