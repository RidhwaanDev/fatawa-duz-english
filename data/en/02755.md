### TITLE

The ruling on applying henna to the feet

### QUESTION

Is applying henna to the feet a Hindu custom? Is there any ruling in a book of fiqh that forbids it? Kindly favour me with an answer.

### ANSWER

It is permissible and correct for women to apply henna to the feet. It is not a Hindu custom, nor is it peculiar to the Hindus; rather it falls under the head of adornment and beautification, and women are permitted it.
Mullā ʿAlī al-Qārī (may Allah have mercy on him) writes in Mirqāt, the commentary on Mishkāt:

وأما خضب اليدين والرجلين فيستحب في حق النساء ويحرم في حق الرجال إلا للتداوي. (مرقاة المفاتيح: ۸/۳۰٤، مكتبة إمداديه).
وفي الجوهرة النيرة: ويكره للإنسان أن يخضب يديه ورجليه بالحناء وكذلك الصبي ولا بأس به للنساء. (الجوهرة النيرة، ص ۳۸۳، مكتبه إمداديه).
(وكذا في فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۳٦۲، فصل في اللبس، سعيد).

Kitāb al-Fatāwā states:
The Sharīʿah has permitted women to adopt adornment in the places of beautification and ornament, such as the hands, the feet, the neck, the breast and the like. Applying henna to the feet is therefore correct, and it will be called neither a sunnah nor an innovation, but rather permissible; that is, these matters are among those which it is allowed to adopt. (کتاب الفتاوی: ٦/۸۷) ۔
واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
