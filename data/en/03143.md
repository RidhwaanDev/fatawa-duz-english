### TITLE
The ruling on the expenses of the maid of a disabled girl

### QUESTION
A man has a disabled, non-adult daughter. Her mental balance is not sound, to the extent that she does not even know that she has hands, feet and other limbs; her power of hearing, power of sight and so forth are also very weak. A maid has been engaged to serve her, who is with the girl at all times. Now the question is: if the parents go on a journey for her treatment, or for ʿumrah and the like, upon whom will the expenses of the maid be binding? Can they be recovered from the girl's own wealth or not? Kindly explain and be rewarded.

### ANSWER
In the case in question, it is correct to arrange the necessary things — for example maintenance and so forth — out of the girl's wealth. But it is not correct to spend the girl's wealth on things that are not necessary; and ʿumrah is a sunnah, it is not necessary. Accordingly it is not correct to recover the expenses of ʿumrah out of the girl's wealth. Yes, if a journey has to be made to another city for the girl's treatment, then it is correct to recover the expenses of the necessary things — for example the ticket, maintenance, lodging and so forth. In the wealth of a non-adult girl a great deal of caution is required.

قال في الهندية: ونفقة الصبي بعد الفطام إذا كان له مال في ماله هكذا في المحيط. (۱/۵٦۲)، وفيه أيضاً: ونفقة الإناث واجبة مطلقاً على الآباء ما لم يتزوجن إذا لم يكن لهن مال كذا في الخلاصة. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۵٦۳).

قال في الدر المختار: النفقة: هي لغة ما ينفقه الإنسان على عياله، وشرعاً: هي الطعام والكسوة والسكني، وعرفاً: هي الطعام. وفي الشامية: وشرعاً هي الطعام، كذا فسرها محمد بالثلاثة لما سأله هشام عنها كما في البحر عن الخلاصة، قوله وعرفاً، أي في العرف الطاري في لسان أهل الشرع هي الطعام فقط، وكذا يعطفون عليه الكسوة والسكني والعطف يقتضي المغايرة. وعبارة المتون كالكنز والملتقي وغيرهما على هذا. (الدر المختار: ۳/۵۷۲، سعيد). (وكذا في اللباب في شرح الكتاب: ۱/۲۹۲، دار الكتاب العربي).

From the passages above it has become clear that if the girl has wealth, then it is correct to arrange for necessary items such as food and drink, clothing and dress and so forth out of the girl's wealth, and it is not binding upon the father. But it is not within the father's discretion to spend the girl's wealth on matters that are not necessary.

The Sage of the Ummah, Ḥaḍrat Thānwī (raḥimahullāh), has written: Whoever is a non-adult who owns some wealth, his maintenance will first of all be from that wealth; while the wealth is there it will not be obligatory upon the father. So gifts should be spent on the necessary maintenance of that non-adult. (امداد الفتاوی: ۳/٤۸۰)۔

The necessary expenses of the girl's medical care and treatment are also the responsibility of the father, and if the girl has wealth they will be obligatory in the girl's wealth. In Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā it occurs:

Maintenance includes food, drink, clothing, accommodation and living, medicine, medical care and treatment, school fees and so forth — all the necessary expenses.

وفي "الفقه الحنفي في ثوبه الجديد": النفقة: وهي ما ينفقه الإنسان على عياله وتشمل الطعام والكسوة والسكني وغيرها من ضروريات الحياة. (۲/۲۳٦، باب النفقة، دمشق).
(فتاوی دار العلوم زکریا: ٤/۳٦۵۔۳٦٦)۔
