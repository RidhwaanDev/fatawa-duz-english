### TITLE
Paying zakat on jewellery by weight or by value

### QUESTION
If a person wishes to pay the zakat due on his jewellery, should he pay it by weight or by value?

### ANSWER
If a person wishes to pay the zakat on gold with gold, value is not taken into account; he should pay the zakat by weight. For example, he should pay one tola for every forty tolas. The workmanship is not taken into account here. If he wishes to pay with something of a different kind, he should have the whole piece of jewellery valued and pay a fortieth of that value.

Consider what al-Shāmī states:

أنه لو أدي من خلاف جنسه اعتبرت القيمة. (الشامي: ۲/۲۹۷، سعيد).

Al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq states:

فلو أدي من خلاف جنسه تعتبر القيمة بالإجماع. (البحر الرائق: ۲/۲۲۷، باب زكاة المال، كوئته. وكذا في تبيين الحقائق: ۱/۲۷۸. والفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱۷۹).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

With gold and silver jewellery it is not the value that counts but the weight. So if a person has 200 tolas of silver jewellery, 5 tolas are due as zakat. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۹/۳۷۸، مبوب و مرتب).

It further states:

If a person does not give silver as his zakat but gives its value instead, then the price at which it would sell in the market is what counts. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۹/۳۷۹، مبوب و مرتب).
