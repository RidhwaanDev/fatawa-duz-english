### TITLE
Buying repossessed goods

### QUESTION
A man took money from a non-Muslim bank to buy a car, and the bank laid down the condition that if he did not pay within a certain period the car would be seized. On the basis of that condition the bank then seized the car. Is it now permissible to buy that car from the bank or not? In common usage such a vehicle is called "repossessed". بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
If the non-Muslim bank made an agreement with the buyer that, should he fail to pay a certain number of instalments, they would seize the car, then this is like a financial penalty imposed as a discretionary punishment, for which some scholars hold there is room. In that case the bank or the institution has become the owner of the car, and buying it from them will be correct. Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ẓafar Aḥmad ʿUthmānī (may Allah have mercy on him) held that a government's seizure of property is a cause of ownership, the details of which have already been given elsewhere; and the question of a financial penalty as a discretionary punishment has also been dealt with in volume four of Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā.

If, however, the bank imposes a fine on a defaulting borrower because he has not paid his instalments for some months, that is impermissible and is interest. In the age of ignorance they used to impose a fine on a defaulting borrower for the delay in repaying, and it is precisely this that is called the interest spoken of in the Qur'an, that is, "زيادة القرض لزيادة الأجل ۔

One form of the case asked about above is that the bank or some other institution takes the car back and returns the original price. The statements of some of the scholars shed light on the permissibility of this too. Ḥaḍrat Mufti Rashīd Aḥmad Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) writes:

If the buyer neither pays the price nor agrees to cancel the sale, the seller has the right to annul it. The buyer's failure to pay the price will be taken as his withholding of consent and as annulment on his side; so with the seller's annulment, annulment from both sides is realised. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 6/506).

Al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq and al-Hidāyah state:

لما تعذر استيفاء الثمن من المشتري فات رضا البائع فيستقل بفسخه. (البحر الرائق: ۷/۳٦، كوئته. وكذا في الهداية: ۳/۱٤۷).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
