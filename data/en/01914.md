### TITLE
Trading in clothing that does not conform to the sharia

### QUESTION
What is the ruling on trading in women's clothing that does not conform to the sharia — garments in which the arms and the like are left uncovered?

### ANSWER
In the case described, trading in such clothing is permissible and valid; it is, however, better and preferable to avoid it.

Radd al-Muḥtār states:

وفي المحيط: لا يكره بيع الزنانير من النصراني والقلنسوة من المجوسي لأن ذلك إذلال لهما وبيع المكعب المفضض للرجل إن ليلبسه يكره، لأنه إعانة على لبس الحرام وإن كان إسكافاً أمره إنسان أن يتخذ له خفاً على زي المجوس أو الفسقة أو خياطاً أمره أن يتخذ له ثوباً على زي الفساق يكره له أن يفعل لأنه سبب التشبه بالمجوس والفسقة. (رد المحتار: ٦/۳۹۲، فصل في البيع، سعيد).

Al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah states:

بيع الزنار من النصارى والقلنسوة من المجوس لا يكره. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۳/۲۱۰).

Fiqhī Maqālāt states:

If an article or a garment can be put to a lawful use as well as an unlawful one, then buying and selling that garment or article is permissible in the sharia. If someone then buys it and puts it to an unlawful use, that sin will fall on the buyer. (فقہی مقالات: ۳/۱۰۰) ۔

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

It is valid to sell such cloth; even so, it is better to be cautious and avoid this kind of trade. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱٦/۱۳۸، مبوب و مرتب) ۔

Maḥmūd al-Fatāwā states:

Question: If a Muslim tailor makes clothes for Hindus that do not conform to the sharia, is it valid for him to take a fee for sewing such clothes?

Answer: It is valid, though caution is preferable. (محمود الفتاوی: ۳/۸۲) ۔

Kitāb al-Fatāwā states:

It is not permissible to wear clothes of this kind in front of a non-maḥram (a man whom the woman could lawfully marry); but there is some latitude for wearing certain garments whose sleeves are not full in front of a maḥram, and in private with her husband a woman may wear any kind of clothing. Since the seller does not sell such garments with the aim, or with the instruction, that they be worn immodestly in front of non-maḥrams, and since in some situations there is in principle some latitude for women to wear them, it is permissible for him to sell clothing of this kind. Anyone who buys them with a wrong intention, however, and who then wears them, will be sinful. (کتاب الفتاوی: ۵/۲۷۱) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
