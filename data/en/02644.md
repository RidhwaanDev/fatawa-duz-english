### TITLE
Covering the head and hair for women

### QUESTION
(1) What is the ruling on a woman covering her hair?

(2) Is it necessary for a woman to keep her hair covered among other women?

(3) If a woman is alone at home, may she remain bare-headed?

(4) Is it mentioned in any book that Rasūlullāh ﷺ once went to the house of a Companion who was not at home, so he returned? When that Companion reached home his wife told him that Rasūlullāh ﷺ had come, and that since he was not there he had gone back. The Companion at once came to Rasūlullāh ﷺ and invited him to a meal at his house, but he ﷺ said: "I will not come, because when I came to your house your wife was bare-headed and was kneading dough. Satan urinated on her head and drops of the urine fell into the dough. Go and throw that dough away."

(5) What is the ruling on a woman uncovering or covering her head in front of her permanent mahrams (relatives she may never marry)? Kindly favour us with an answer.

### ANSWER
(1) A woman is the adornment of the home, and her hair is an adornment for her. A woman is permitted to display her adornment inside her own house, provided that no non-mahram is present in it.

(2) For a woman to keep her hair covered among other women is preferable and better, but it is not necessary. See what al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq states:

نظر المرأة إلى المرأة كنظر الرجل إلى الرجل حتى يجوز للمرأة أن تنظر منها إلى ما يجوز للرجل أن ينظر إليه من الرجل إذا أمنت الشهوة والفتن لان ما ليس بعورة لا يختلف فيه الرجال والنساء فكان لها أن تنظر منه ما ليس بعورة وإن كان في قلبها شهوة أو أكبر رأيها أنها تشتهي أو شكت في ذلك يستحب لها أن تغض بصرها. (البحر الرائق: ۸/۱۹۳، ط: كوئته).

Besides this, there is also the fear of mischief from women of bad character. Thus ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

ولا تنبغي للمرأة الصالحة أن تنظر إليها المرأة الفاجرة لأنها تصفها عند الرجال، فلا تضع جلبابها ولا خمارها كما في السراج. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۳۷۱، سعيد).

(3) If a woman is alone in her house she may uncover her hair. See what ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

يرخص للمرأة كشف الراس في منزلها وحدها فأولي لها لبس خمار رقيق يصف ما تحته عند محارمها. (فتاوى الشامي: ۱/٤۰٤، مطلب في ستر العورة، سعيد).
(وكذا في الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳۳۳).

Hazrat Maulānā Muhammad Yūsuf Ludhyānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) says in Āp ke Masāʾil:

The better course is that a woman should not uncover her head; still, if there is no non-mahram at home, uncovering the head is permissible. In front of a non-mahram it is not permissible. (آپ کے مسائل اور ان کا حل: ۸/٦۴، ط: طبع جدید).

(4) Despite a long search and much investigation, we have not found this narration in any book of hadith.

(5) In the eye of the sharia it is permissible and correct for a woman to uncover her head in front of her permanent mahrams. Even so, out of modesty, wearing a thin scarf on the head is better than leaving the head uncovered, as ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) has said.

See what al-Hidāyah states:

وينظر الرجل من ذوات محارمه إلى الوجه والرأس والصدر والساقين والعضدين ولا ينظر إلى ظهرها وبطنها وفخذها. والأصل فيه قوله تعالى: ﴿ولا يبدين زينتهن إلا لبعولتهن

الآية﴾ والمراد والله أعلم مواضع الزينة

ولأن البعض يدخل على البعض من غير استيذان واحتشام والمرأة في بيتها في ثياب مهنتها عادة فلو حرم النظر إلى هذه المواضع أدي إلى الحرج وكذا الرغبة تقل للحرمة المؤبدة فقل ما تشتهي. (الهداية: ٤/۴۶۱، ط: مكتبة شركة علمية).

Āp ke Masāʾil states:

The part below the knee and the part above the chest, the head, the face and the arms may be uncovered in front of a mahram. (آپ کے مسائل اور ان کا حل: ۸/۸۲، طبع جدید).

Khawātīn ke Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

A woman may uncover before her mahram relatives — her father, her brother, her sons and so on — only as much of her body as she needs to uncover while doing the housework and moving about. (خواتین کے فقہی مسائل، ص ۲۰۳).

Kitāb al-Fatāwā states:

If only the husband and mahram relatives are at home, there is room for her to leave her head uncovered; but caution in every case lies in keeping the head covered even inside the house, because once the habit of remaining bare-headed sets in, a state of heedlessness develops and the difference between a mahram and a non-mahram also disappears. (کتاب الفتاوی: ٦/۹۹).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
