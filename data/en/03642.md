### TITLE
The ruling on giving a reward out of the donation money to someone who helps in collecting it

### QUESTION
What is the ruling on giving a gift from the madrasah's funds to a person who helps in collecting donations and the like for the madrasah? That is, if the madrasah's collector goes to some village for donations and some acquaintance there takes him along and gets donations made, is it permissible to give him something as a gift out of the donations or not, so that there may be encouragement for the future and help may be obtained in collecting?

### ANSWER
The madrasah's money is not one's own personal property, and a gift or present is given out of one's own personal property. Yes, as for those gentlemen who are helpers of the madrasah and assist in obtaining donations, there is room to give them something by way of a gift.

قال في الشامية: والحاصل أن الوجه يقتضي أن ما كان قريباً من العمارة يلحق بها في التقديم علي بقية المستحقين

فيقدم أولاً العمارة الضرورية ثم الأهم فالأهم من المصالح والشعائر بقدر ما يقوم به الحال فإن فضل شيء يعطي لبقية المستحقين إذ لا شك أن مراد الواقف انتظام حال مسجد ه أو مدرسته لا مجرد انتفاع أهل الوقف

إنما هو فيما إذا لم يكن الوقف معيناً على جماعة معلومين كالمسجد والمدرسة إما لو كان معيناً كالدار الموقوفة على الذرية أو الفقراء فإنه بعد العمارة يصرف الريع إلى ما عينه الواقف بلا تقديم لا حد على أحد، فاغتنم هذا التحرير. (فتاوى الشامي: ٤/۳٦۸، سعيد).
وقال أيضا: لكن أفتي في الخيرية: بأنه إذا كان في ريع الوقف عوائد قديمة معهودة يتناولها الناظر بسعيه له طلبها لقول الأشباه عن إجارات الظهيرية والمعروف عرفاً كالمشروط شرطاً فهو صريح في استحقاقه ما جرت به العادة. (فتاوى الشامي: ٤/٤۵۰، سعيد).

Note what Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

If the donors have specified the purpose, then the donation will be spent on that very purpose and not contrary to it. If they have not specified the purpose but have given the principal full authority to spend it on the interests of the madrasah, then it is correct to spend it on any beneficial purpose. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 15/466, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

Imdād al-Fatāwā records: The upshot of the question is that a board has been hung up with the name of the madrasah written on it. Some are of the opinion that its cost should not be paid from the madrasah's funds, since this is not the madrasah's business.

The jurists have laid down a principle that decoration and ornamentation of a mosque is not permissible from endowment funds, but strengthening it is permissible. So on this analogy the ruling in the case in question is that if the hanging of this board brings the madrasah some clear benefit, then it is permissible to spend the madrasah's funds on it; and if there is no considerable benefit but only the degree of a possibility, then one should pay its price from one's own pocket. (Imdād al-Fatāwā: 2/594, Karachi).
