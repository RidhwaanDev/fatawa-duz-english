### TITLE
An examination of the second marriage of Umm Ḥakīm (may Allah be pleased with her)

### QUESTION
Faḍāʾil-e Aʿmāl, quoting Usd al-Ghābah of Ibn al-Athīr, relates the story of how Umm Ḥakīm bint al-Ḥārith (may Allah be pleased with her) accepted Islam and took part in the Battle of Ajnādayn. It also says that when the fighting with the Romans took place in the caliphate of Abū Bakr (may Allah be pleased with him), ʿIkrimah (may Allah be pleased with him) took part in it and his wife Umm Ḥakīm was with him; that ʿIkrimah (may Allah be pleased with him) was martyred in that battle; that Khālid ibn Saʿīd (may Allah be pleased with him) then married her; and that on that same journey, at a place called Marj al-Ṣuffar, he intended to bring her home, and so on. The difficulty with this account is that Umm Ḥakīm bint al-Ḥārith (may Allah be pleased with her) contracted a second marriage on that very journey, after the martyrdom of her husband ʿIkrimah (may Allah be pleased with him), without completing her ʿiddah (the waiting period) — for the account makes no mention of any ʿiddah.

### ANSWER
Usd al-Ghābah makes no mention of her completing an ʿiddah, but other history books do state that she observed an ʿiddah of four months and ten days.

Consider what Ṭabaqāt Ibn Saʿd states:

أخبرنا محمد بن عمر قال حدثني عبد الحميد بن جعفر عن أبيه قال: شهد خالد بن سعيد فتح أجنادين وفِحلٍ ومرج الصفر وكانت أم حكيم بنت الحارث بن هشام تحت عكرمة بن أبي جهل فقتل عنها بأجنادين فاعتدت أربعة أشهر وعشراً وكان يزيد بن أبي سفيان يخطبها الخ. (الطبقات الكبرى لابن سعد: ٤/۹۸، ط: دار صادر).

وللاستزادة انظر: (الوافي بالوفيات: ۳/۸۲، لصلاح الدين خليل بن أيبك الصفدي، بيروت، وتاريخ مدينة دمشق: ۷۰/۲۲۷، أم حكيم بنت الحارث بن هشام).

Al-Istīʿāb states:

قال: كان أم الحكيم بنت الحارث بن هشام تحت عكرمة بن أبي جهل فقتل عنها بأجنادين فاعتدت أربعة أشهر وعشراً وكان يزيد بن أبي سفيان يخطبها الخ. (الاستيعاب لابن عبد البر: ٤/۱۹۳۲).

These passages make it clear that Umm al-Ḥakīm bint al-Ḥārith did observe an ʿiddah of four months and ten days, after which her marriage to Khālid ibn Saʿīd (may Allah be pleased with him) took place at Marj al-Ṣuffar, with a mahr (dower) of 400 dirhams agreed upon. A second point is that ʿIkrimah (may Allah be pleased with him) was martyred at the Battle of Ajnādayn, and according to the explicit statements of history the Battle of Ajnādayn took place in Jumādā al-Ūlā 13 AH, in the last days of Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq (may Allah be pleased with him), while the affair of Marj al-Ṣuffar took place in Muḥarram 14 AH, in the caliphate of ʿUmar (may Allah be pleased with him). On this reckoning there is a gap of roughly seven months between the death of the first husband and the second marriage.

Tārīkh Dimashq states:

وقال الواقدي: واليقين عندنا أن أجنادين كانت في جمادي الأولى سنة ثلاث عشرة وبشربها أبو بكر رضي الله عنه وهو بآخر رمق. (تاريخ مدينة دمشق لابن عساكر: ۲/۱۰۳).

Concerning the second marriage, al-Ṭabaqāt al-Kubrā states:

وكانت وقعة مرج الصفر في المحرم سنة أربع عشرة في خلافة عمر بن الخطاب رضي الله عنه. (الطبقات الكبرى لابن سعد: ٤/ ۹۹، دار صادر).

قال الواقدي: وفي سنة أربع عشرة كان فتح مرج الصفر. (مختصر تاريخ دمشق: ۱/٦۹).

This makes it clear that there is a gap of about seven months between the two events, and that there is an explicit statement about the ʿiddah of Umm Ḥakīm bint al-Ḥārith as well. In any case, it is unthinkable that the noble Companions would set aside a command of the sharia.
