### TITLE
The ruling on making a bequest in favour of an adopted child

### QUESTION
A woman passed away. She had made a bequest of some jewellery in favour of an adopted daughter. Will this bequest be carried out or not? And will an adopted child receive a share of the inheritance or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, an adopted child does not have the ruling of a real daughter, and for that reason she is not entitled to inherit. On that basis a bequest in her favour is valid. Then, once the deceased's debts have been paid, if the jewellery amounts to one third of the entire estate it is in order; and if it exceeds one third, the bequest will be executed up to one third only, unless the remaining heirs give their permission, in which case it is in order. Consider what Mufti Muhammad Shafiʿ (may Allah have mercy on him) writes in Aḥkām al-Qurʾān:

المتبني لا يلحق بالأبناء في الأحكام

الثاني: أن الدعي والمتبني لا يلحق في الأحكام بالابن، فلا يستحق الميراث ولا يرث عنه المدعي، ولا يحرم حليلته بعد الطلاق والعدة على ذلك المدعي ولا عكسه. (أحكام القرآن: ۳/۲۹۱، إدارة القرآن، كراتشي).

Muṣannaf Ibn Abī Shaybah states:

إن معاذ بن جبل رضي الله عنه قال: إن الله تصدق عليكم بثلث أموالكم زيادة في حياتكم، يعني: الوصية. (مصنف ابن أبي شيبة: ۱٦/۱۸۱).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

If a person adopts a child, that is, takes him as a so-called son, then in reality that child does not become his son, nor does he become the child's father. The child's real father is the one from whose seed he was born; the child's lineage is established from that real father alone, and it is from him alone that he inherits. He will not inherit from the man who adopted him. If that man is attached to the child, he may give him something out of a third of his wealth by way of gift, or he may make a bequest in his favour. (Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 5/523 and 501).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
