### TITLE
What a woman should do if her menses begin while she is in the Sacred Mosque

### QUESTION
A woman is sitting in the Sacred Mosque when her menses begin, and because of the great crowd it is difficult for her to get out immediately. What should she do? بینوا توجروا

### ANSWER
As soon as her menses begin, a woman should leave the mosque. If, however, leaving is difficult because of the crowd, then out of necessity she should perform tayammum (dry ablution) and remain sitting where she is, while continuing to try to get out as far as she is able. This is like what the jurists have written concerning a man in a state of major ritual impurity: if he has a wet dream in the mosque he should leave it at once, but if there is danger in going out, he may perform tayammum and remain in the mosque. Consider what is recorded in Fatāwā al-Shāmī:

ولو كان نائماً فيه فاحتلم والماء خارجه وخشي من الخروج يتيمم وينام فيه إلى أن يمكنه الخروج، قال في المنية وإن احتلم في المسجد تيمم للخروج إذا لم يخف وإن خاف يجلس مع التيمم ولا يصلي ولا يقرأ. (فتاوى الشامي: ۱/۲٤۳، سعيد).
(وكذا في حاشية الطحطاوي على مراقي الفلاح، ص ۱٤٤، والبحر الرائق: ۱/۲۰٦، دار المعرفة).

In ʿUmdat al-Fiqh, Mawlānā Sayyid Zawwār Ḥusayn says: If someone has a wet dream in the mosque, he should perform tayammum and leave quickly; this tayammum is permissible but not necessary. But if he cannot leave quickly because of the fear of an enemy or of an animal, and has to stay where he is, then he should stay after performing tayammum, and this tayammum is necessary. (ʿUmdat al-Fiqh: 1/259, Zawwār Academy)
