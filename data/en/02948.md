### TITLE
A litany for a marriage of one's choice

### QUESTION
Respected Muftī, kindly tell me some litany for a marriage of my choice, by which the means may be made available for the marriage I intend. بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question, keep reciting this litany 41 times daily for your need:
"لَا إِله إِلَّا أنْتَ سُبْحَانَك إِنِّيْ كنْتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ"۔
(انوارِ شمسیہ، ص ۸۷)۔

Or, for a lawful aim of yours, recite this supplication one hundred times daily as a regular litany:
"اللهمَّ يا مُقَلِّبَ الْقُلُوْبِ وَالْأبْصَارِ وَيا خَالِقَ اللَّيلِ وَالنَّهارِ قَلِّبْ قُلُوْبَهمْ بِالْمَوَدَّة وَالإِعْزَازِ وَاْلإِحْسَانِ".
(انوارِ شمسیہ، ص ۹۷)۔

Write out Sūrat Ṭā Hā, wrap it in a piece of green silk and keep it with you; if a proposal of marriage is then sent, God willing it will meet with success. (اعمالِ قرآنی، ص ۵۲)۔

For an important aim, one should sit facing the qiblah in a state of purity and recite Sūrat Alam Nashraḥ 103 times, reciting the blessings upon the Prophet eleven times at the beginning and at the end; God willing, the aim will be attained. (کمالین: ۷/٦۳۱)۔

There is, however, no permission for any practice by which the person concerned is stripped of his free will, nor for words whose meaning is unknown. Consider what Shaykh Ibn Ḥajar (may Allah have mercy on him) states in Fatḥ al-Bārī:

وقد أجمع العلماء على جواز الرقي عند اجتماع ثلاثة شروط أن يكون بكلام الله تعالى أو بأسمائه وصفاته وباللسان العربي أو بما يعرف معناه من غيره وأن يعتقد أن الرقية لا تؤثر بذاتها بل بذات الله تعالى. (فتح الباري: ۱۰/۱۹۵، كتاب الرقي، دار الفكر. وكذا في المرقاة).

The gist of the passage is that the scholars are agreed upon the use of any ruqyah or amulet in which three conditions are found: 1. That it be from the Qur'an and hadith, or from the names and attributes of Allah Most High, and in the Arabic language.

2. If it is in a language other than Arabic, that its sense and meaning be known.

3. That the ruqyah or amulet be treated as a means only and not regarded as effective in itself; rather one's trust should be placed in the being of Allah Most High.

Imdād al-Fatāwā records: With respect to their effect, such practices are of two kinds. 1. The first kind is that the person upon whom the practice is performed becomes subdued and his reason overpowered. Such a practice is not permissible for an aim that is not obligatory in the Sharīʿah — just as marrying a particular man is not obligatory in the Sharīʿah, so such a practice is not permissible for that purpose.

The second kind is that the person concerned is merely turned towards the aim, without being overpowered. Such a practice is permissible for such an aim, and in this ruling the Qur'an and other than the Qur'an are alike. (امداد الفتاوی: ٤/۸۹، و فتاوی محمودیہ: ۲۰/۷۵)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
