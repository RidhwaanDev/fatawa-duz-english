### TITLE
The religion of Abū Ṭālib

### QUESTION
What was the religion of Abū Ṭālib? Is it correct to use "Janāb" or "Khwājah" with his name?

### ANSWER
Abū Ṭālib was the Prophet's ﷺ own paternal uncle, and although he remained in disbelief he served and brought up the Prophet ﷺ. According to the majority of scholars, however, he left this world in a state of disbelief. For this reason the word "Ḥaḍrat" should not be used for him, because "Ḥaḍrat" is used to convey a high degree of respect; our senior scholars have used only the name "Abū Ṭālib".

Mawlānā Abū al-Ḥasan ʿAlī Nadwī (may Allah have mercy on him) writes in Nabī-e Raḥmat:

After his grandfather passed away, he came to live with his uncle Abū Ṭālib. (نبی رحمت: ۱/۱۰٦).

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Idrīs Kāndhlawī (may Allah have mercy on him) says in Sīrat al-Muṣṭafā:

After the death of ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib, he came into the nurturing care of his uncle Abū Ṭālib. (سیرۃ المصطفی ۱ /۸۷).

The Grand Mufti of Pakistan, Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ (may Allah have mercy on him), says in Sīrat Khātam al-Anbiyāʾ:

After that, his own paternal uncle Abū Ṭālib became his guardian. (سیرۃ خاتم الانبیاء ص ۱٦).

That said, in view of the services he rendered, it is acceptable to use some mild term of respect for him.

Mawlānā ʿAbd al-Raʾūf Dānāpūrī, for instance, used the words "Janāb Khwājah Abū Ṭālib" in Aṣaḥḥ al-Siyar. (اصح السیر ص ۵۱).

Mawlānā Ḥifẓ al-Raḥmān Sīwhārwī (may Allah have mercy on him) likewise used only the name "Abū Ṭālib". Since the Shīʿah consider Abū Ṭālib to be the greatest Muslim of all, one should avoid resembling them and should not use "Ḥaḍrat" for Abū Ṭālib — although doing so is not impermissible or forbidden either.

The substance of all this is as follows:

(1) Abū Ṭālib passed away in a state of disbelief.

(2) The senior scholars did not use the word "Ḥaḍrat" with his name.

(3) Using the word "Ḥaḍrat" involves resembling the Shīʿah.

(4) In view of the services he rendered, there appears to be room for using a mild term such as "Janāb" — just as the Prophet ﷺ used the expression عظیم الروم for Heraclius.
