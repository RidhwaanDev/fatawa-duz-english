### TITLE
Keeping the seventh share for the Noble Prophet ﷺ

### QUESTION
Six men together each kept his own necessary share in the sacrifice of a large animal, and in the seventh share they all together made the intention of a voluntary sacrifice for the Prophet ﷺ or for the whole ummah. Has the sacrifice of all of them been fulfilled in this way or not? And is it correct to do this or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
For the conveying of reward several people may share in a single portion, just as one portion is enough, by way of conveying reward, for a number of deceased persons. The Prophet ﷺ slaughtered a ram on behalf of those members of his ummah who had not offered a sacrifice.

Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ states:

فإن قيل: أليس أنه روي أن رسول الله ﷺ ضحي بكبشين أملحين أحدهما عن نفسه والآخر ممن لا يذبح من أمته فكيف ضحي شاة واحدة عن أمته؟ فالجواب: أنه عليه الصلاة والسلام إنما فعل ذلك لاجل الثواب وهو أنه جعل ثواب تضحيته بشاة واحدة لا مته. (بدائع الصنائع: ۵/۷۰، سعيد).

The jurists have also written that if a person had a share in a sacrifice but died before the Eid, his heirs may offer the sacrifice on his behalf by way of conveying reward — and this is a case in which several heirs became owners of his share, and even so they may offer the sacrifice for the deceased.

In Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ, ʿAllāmah Abū Bakr Kāsānī (may Allah have mercy on him) writes:

وجه الاستحسان أن الموت لا يمنع التقرب عن الميت بدليل أنه يجوز أن يتصدق عنه ويحج عنه

الخ. (بدائع الصنائع: ۵/۷۲، سعيد).

The marginal notes on Sharḥ al-Niqāyah state:

وجه الاستحسان أن الورثة يقومون مقام المورث بعد موته والتبرع من الوارث عن مورثه بالقرب المالية صحيح كالتصدق. (حاشية شرح النقاية لمولوي إلياس: ۲/۲۷۱، ط: سعيد).

Since the jurists have likened this to giving in charity, it is obvious that different people may join together and give charity for one deceased person or for several; so in the same way there is no harm in offering the sacrifice in this manner.

Muftī ʿAbd al-Raḥīm Lājpūrī Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) has written in Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah:

Question: Six men together kept their own necessary shares in a large sacrificial animal, and in the seventh share all of them joined together and made the intention of a voluntary sacrifice for the Prophet ﷺ. Is this correct or not? Does it not have a bad effect on the necessary sacrifice? It should be borne in mind that the seventh share is not on behalf of one person; rather six are sharing in one share. Please, therefore, give the answer with a reference to a book.

Answer:

إن مات أحد السبعة المشتركين في البدنة وقال الورثة: اذبحوا عنه وعنكم صح عن الكل استحساناً لقصد القربة من الكل ولو ذبحوها بلا إذن الورثة لم يجزهم. (الدر المختار مع الشامي: ٦/۳۲٦، سعيد).

From the report of fiqh mentioned above it appears to be permissible by juristic preference, because when the seventh shareholder died his share passed to his heirs and the heirs became the owners of that share; and when they, in their capacity as owners of that seventh share, gave permission for the sacrifice, the sacrifice of all of them became valid. In the same way, in the case asked about, when the six companions bought the seventh share and assigned it to the Prophet ﷺ, it ought to be correct. Other scholars should also be consulted. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۵/۳۹۲، دیوبند) ۔
