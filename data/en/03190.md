### TITLE
Conveying greetings of peace to the two shaykhs

### QUESTION
It is generally said to someone travelling to the two sanctuaries: "Convey our greetings of peace to the Prophet ﷺ." So would it be permissible to say, "Convey our greetings of peace to the two shaykhs as well"? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, it is correct to say to one visiting the two noble sanctuaries: "Convey our greetings of peace to the two shaykhs, that is, to Ḥaḍrat Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq (may Allah Most High be pleased with him) and Ḥaḍrat ʿUmar al-Fārūq (may Allah Most High be pleased with him)"; and it is likewise permissible and correct for the visitor to convey that greeting. See what is mentioned in Ghunyat al-Nāsik:

ثم يتأخر كذلك قدر ذراع فيسلم على عمر رضي الله عنه لأن رأسه من الصديق من النبي ﷺ، فيقول: السلام عليك يا أمير المؤمنين

ويزيد عليه وينقص إن ضاق الوقت ويبلغ سلام من أوصاه به. (غنية الناسك في بغية المناسك، ص ۲۰۵).

In Jawāhir al-Fiqh, where the greeting of the two shaykhs is discussed, it is written: he should convey the greeting of anyone who has asked him to do so. (Jawāhir al-Fiqh 4/175.)

In ʿUmdat al-Fiqh it is stated: if time is short, or if he does not remember that much, he may shorten the wording of the greeting to these two personages; and if there is time and he remembers further wording, he may add to it as well. And if someone has asked him to convey a greeting, he should convey that person's greeting also to these two personages. (ʿUmdat al-Fiqh 4/295; likewise in Faḍāʾil wa Masāʾil-e Ḥajj by Muftī Rashīd Aḥmad Qāsimī, p. 86.)

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
