### TITLE
Terminating a muḍārabah

### QUESTION
Zayd and ʿAmr began a muḍārabah in 2002. Zayd, the owner of the capital, was to have a 25 per cent share and ʿAmr, the working partner, a 75 per cent share. In 2007 the working partner gave the owner of the capital only 3,000 as profit and told him to take his capital back. When the rest of the profit was asked for, the working partner said that he would pay it off little by little.

The question is this: has the muḍārabah come to an end or not, and is it correct to take the remaining profit later or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, the muḍārabah contract may be brought to an end by giving notice to the owner of the capital; and if the working partner does not have the money at present, it is also permissible for the rest of the profit to be collected later. But if the working partner does have it, it is not permissible for him to put the matter off.

See what Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ states:

وأما صفة هذا العقد فهو أنه عقد غير لازم ولكل واحد منهما أعني رب المال والمضارب الفسخ لكن عند وجود شرطه وهو علم صاحبه لما ذكرنا في كتاب الشركة ويشترط أيضاً أن يكون رأس المال عيناً وقت الفسخ دراهم أو دنانير حتى لو نهي رب المال المضارب عن التصرف ورأس المال عروض وقت النهي لم يصح نهيه. (بدائع الصنائع: ٦/۱۰۹، سعيد).

Bidāyat al-Mujtahid states:

أجمع العلماء على أن اللزوم ليس من موجبات عقد القراض، وان لكل واحد منهما فسخه ما لم يشرع العامل في القراض، واختلفوا إذا شرع العامل، فقال مالك: هو لازم

وقال الشافعي وأبو حنيفة: لكل واحد منهم الفسخ إذا شاء. (بداية المجتهد: ۲/۱۸۱، القول في أحكام القراض، دار نشر الكتب).

Fatāwā Ḥaqqāniyyah states:

Either of the two parties may end a muḍārabah contract whenever and however he wishes, but for the contract to be cancelled it is necessary that the other party be informed of it. This cancellation will only be effective, however, when the capital is present in the form of cash. If the capital is in the form of goods, the working partner will be given the opportunity to sell those goods so that the actual profit for which the contract was made may be determined. (Fatāwā Ḥaqqāniyyah: 6/354).
