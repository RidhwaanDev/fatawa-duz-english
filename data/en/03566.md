### TITLE
The ruling on the iḥrām being free of actual impurity while performing ṭawāf

### QUESTION
While in the state of iḥrām a person had a dream in which he experienced a nocturnal emission. He performed a ritual bath, but the sheets of his iḥrām were clean and no stain of semen or anything else was visible. He had no other iḥrām with him, so he put the same one on and performed the ṭawāf of ʿumrah. Has the ʿumrah been fulfilled or not? Has any penalty become binding or not?

### ANSWER
According to the most correct position, the clothing being free of actual impurity for ṭawāf is not among the obligatory elements of ṭawāf. The ṭawāf is therefore valid, albeit with dislikedness, and no penalty becomes binding. Moreover, in this case there is not even certainty of impurity but only a doubt. On this basis no defect entered the ṭawāf and the ʿumrah was validly performed.

Consider what ʿAllāmah al-Sarakhsī (may Allah have mercy on him) states:

ولو طاف طواف الزيارة وفي ثوبه نجاسة أكثر من قدر الدرهم أجزأه ولكن مع الكراهة ولا يلزمه شيء كذا في المحيط. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۲٤٦، وكذا في اللباب مع شرحه: ۱٦۵).

It is recorded in Muʿallim al-Ḥujjāj under the sunnahs of ṭawāf: The body and clothing being free of actual impurity. (Muʿallim al-Ḥujjāj, p. 147).

If a person had a dream and nothing was discharged, then neither is the ritual bath obligatory nor is washing the clothes obligatory. Consider the following:

قال في منية المصلي: وإن احتلم ولم يخرج منه شيء أي تذكر الاحتلام ولم ير بللاً لا غسل عليه إجماعاً. (غنية المتملي في شرح منية المصلي، ص ٤۳، ط: سهيل).

قال في مجمع الأنهر: والطاهر لا يزول طهارته بالشك. (مجمع الأنهر: ۱/٦۳).

It is recorded in Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥal:

Whatever thing is not certainly or predominantly impure, regard it as pure, no matter how many misgivings arise; pay them no heed. And whatever thing you strongly suppose to be impure, purify it, and after that entertain no misgiving. (Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥal: 3/177). See further: (ʿUmdat al-Fiqh: 4/183).

To remove the doubt, however, it is correct to sprinkle a little water, as is reported in the narrations of the predecessors:

عن أبي هريرة رضي الله عنه أنه، كان يقول في الجنابة في الثوب: إن رأيت أثره فاغسله، وإن علمت أن قد أصابه ثم خفي عليك فاغسل الثوب، وإن شككت فلم تدر أصاب الثوب أم لا فانضحه. (مصنف ابن أبي شيبة: ۱/۵۰۸/۹۰٤).
