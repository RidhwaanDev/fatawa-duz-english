### TITLE
The correct pronunciation of the name Riḍā

### QUESTION
My name is Riḍā. Is it with a fatḥah on the rāʾ or with a kasrah? People generally say Raḍā with a fatḥah; is that correct or not?

### ANSWER
Riḍā is an Arabic word, and in Arabic it comes with a kasrah on the rāʾ; a fatḥah on the rāʾ is not correct. It is a verbal noun, and its meaning is contentment, pleasure and being well pleased.

In common usage, however, people do not pay much attention to vowelling when speaking Urdu. For that reason, in speech, Raḍā with a fatḥah is in order and will not be called wrong. There are countless Arabic words in use in the Urdu language whose vowelling is not correct in pronunciation, although in Urdu they are correct and in order. For instance: "this is so-and-so's mawqaf"; "so-and-so attained this manṣab"; "so-and-so succeeded in his maqṣad"; "so-and-so came upon a great khazānah". These words are correct in Arabic with a kasrah, but in Urdu they are used with a fatḥah. And mustaqbil is used in Urdu with a kasrah, while in Arabic it also comes with a fatḥah.
