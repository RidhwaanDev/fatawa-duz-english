### TITLE
The husband denying three divorces he has pronounced

### QUESTION
My husband gave me three divorces in private in a state of anger, and I heard them with my own ears. When I was about to leave for my family's home he stopped me and denied the three divorces. What course is open to me now?

### ANSWER
The jurists have written that a woman is like a judge in her own case. Since you heard the divorce with your own ears, you should keep apart from your husband. But if that proves impossible — if the husband will neither release you nor agree to khulʿ (release from the marriage at the wife's request in return for a payment) — then place your case before a Sharīʿah judge or an arbitrator. Since you have no witnesses, the oath falls upon the husband; and if the husband swears the oath, you may then live with him, and the sin will be upon him. This same point is stated in several places in Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband.

An objection may be raised here: if a man testifies to the sighting of the moon of the blessed month of Ramaḍān and his testimony is not accepted, he still keeps the fast himself. This shows that as against the judge it is the word of the one who witnessed the event that is relied upon — yet in the question of divorce the judge's word has been given effect. The answer, as it appears, is this: in the matter of Ramaḍān the fast concerns only the one fasting and no one else, so he keeps the fast; in the case under discussion the marriage concerns the husband too, and he denies it on oath. So, acknowledging his right, and while she keeps apart from him as far as she is able, the sin of marital relations will fall upon the husband. Moreover, in the case of the fast there is no conflict between the judicial ruling and the ruling of conscience — this man fasts while others do not — whereas between a husband and wife there is such a conflict between the judicial ruling and the ruling of conscience.

When a case in which the husband denied three divorces and the woman claimed them came before Muftī Walī Ḥasan (may Allah have mercy on him), and he was appointed arbitrator, he would ask the woman for witnesses; and when the woman had no witnesses he would put the husband on oath. When the husband swore that he had not given three divorces, he would tell the wife: you may live with your husband, and if the husband is lying the sin will be upon him. There is ease in this, and this is where this servant's own inclination lies.

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

سمعت من زوجها أنه طلقها ولا تقدر على منعه من نفسها إلا بقتله

وقال الأوزجندي: ترفع الأمر للقاضي فإن حلف ولا بينة لها، فالإثم عليه. وفي الطحطاوي: قوله فالإثم عليه ولا إثم عليها بتمكينه من نفسها. (حاشية الطحطاوي على الدر المختار: ۱/۱۷۸، باب الرجعة، كوئته).

Radd al-Muḥtār states:

والمرأة كالقاضي إذا سمعته أو أخبرها عدل لا يحل لها تمكينه والفتوي على أنه ليس لها قتله ولا تقتل نفسها بل تفدي نفسها بمال أو تهرب كما أنه ليس له قتلها إذا حرمت عليه وكلما هرب ردته بالسحر. وفي البزازية عن الأوزجندي: أنها ترفع الأمر للقاضي فإن حلف ولا بينة لها فالإثم عليه. قلت: أي إذا لم تقدر على الفداء أو الهرب ولا على منعه عنها فلا ينافي ما قبله. (فتاوى الشامي: ۳/۲۵۱، باب الرجعة، سعيد).

Khulāṣat al-Fatāwā states:

وفي فتاوى النسفي عن السيد الإمام أبي شجاع هكذا وفي فوائد شمس الإسلام إن لم يكن بينة ترفع إلى القاضي وتحلفه فإن حلف فالإثم عليه. (خلاصة الفتاوى: ۲/۱۲۰، المكتبة الرشيدية).

Majmaʿ al-Anhur, the commentary on Multaqā al-Abḥur, states:

وفي التتارخانية وغيرها: سمعت المرأة من زوجها أنه طلقها ولا تقدر على منعه من نفسها إلا بقتله لها قتله بالدواء ولا تقتل نفسها وقيل لا تقتله وبه يفتي وترفع الأمر إلى القاضي فإن لم تكن لها بينة تحلفه فإن حلف فالإثم عليه. (مجمع الأنهر شرح ملتقي الأبحر: ۳/۳۲٦، باب الرجعة).

Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

If the husband denies having pronounced divorce, testimony is required to establish the divorce before the judge; and if there are not two witnesses, the husband will be put on oath. If he swears that he did not pronounce divorce, the judge will rule that no divorce has occurred. But this ruling will not make the woman lawful for the husband if he has in fact pronounced divorce, and he will be caught in unlawful relations for the rest of his life. And that is all. (کفایت المفتی: ٦/۸۲، دار الاشاعت) ۔

Īḍāḥ al-Nawādir states:

But if in such a situation the woman finds no way out, and the husband keeps her with him by swearing a false oath, the woman will not be sinful; the whole sin will be upon the husband. The jurists state this aspect of the question in these words:

"والمرأة كالقاضي إذا سمعته أو أخبرهاعدل لايحل لها تمكينه"
