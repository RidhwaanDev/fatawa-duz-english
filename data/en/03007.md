### TITLE
The ruling on women taking part in politics and government

### QUESTION
In view of present-day political conditions around the world, is it permissible for women to take part and participate in politics and the like, or not? My question concerns the Western democratic governments (America, England, Australia and so on). I do not know much about Africa; it is possible that matters are the same there. Kindly explain and be rewarded.

### ANSWER
In the case in question, as far as a woman becoming head of state is concerned, that is not correct in the light of the Qur'an and the hadith, because one of the conditions for becoming head of state is that the person be male, and this condition is absent.

As for whether it is correct for a woman to be active in politics and in parliament, or to take part in voting and the like, there is room for it provided the limits of the sharīʿah — such as the veil and so forth — are observed. In our own times, however, the intermingling of women with men is very great and there is fear of temptation; for this reason it should be avoided.

Consider the detailed treatment given in Fatāwā Ḥaqqāniyyah:

In this age of trials, and in view of prevailing conditions, a woman ought to avoid taking up membership of an assembly or parliament, because under present conditions it appears difficult for a woman to maintain the veil while campaigning for elections. Nevertheless, if a woman takes part in elections in such a way that the sharʿī veil is carefully maintained and no unlawful matters are committed, then in that case there is room in the sharīʿah for a woman to contest an election and for her to be given membership of parliament.

That is because the members of an assembly or parliament are the representatives of the people and the nation on their behalf, and it is permissible for a woman to act as someone's agent. Nowhere in the books of Ḥanafī fiqh is maleness mentioned among the conditions of an agent, and it appears from the wording of Bidāyat al-Mujtahid that in the view of the four imāms a woman's agency is valid; only in the case of the contract of marriage can a woman not act as an agent according to Imām al-Shāfiʿī (raḥimahullāh) and Imām Mālik (raḥimahullāh).

لما قال الإمام ابن رشد الحفيد: وشروط الوكيل أن لا يكون ممنوعاً بالشرع من تصرفه في الشيء الذي وكل فيه فلا يصح توكيل الصبي ولا المجنون ولا المرأة عند مالك رحمه الله والشافعي رحمه الله على عقد النكاح. (بداية المجتهد: ۲/۲۹٦، الباب الاول كتاب الوكالة).

Likewise, the members of an assembly or parliament hold the position of the people who "loose and bind" (ahl al-ḥall wa'l-ʿaqd); it is by their votes that the prime minister and the president of the state are then elected. The renowned authority on statecraft, ʿAllāmah al-Māwardī (raḥimahullāh), did not mention maleness among the conditions of the people who loose and bind. He says:

فأما الاختيار فالشروط المعتبرة فيهم ثلاثة: أحدها: العدالة الجامعة لشروطها، والثاني: العلم الذي يتوصل به إلى معرفة من يستحق الإمامة على الشروط المعتبرة فيها، والثالث: الرأي والحكمة المؤديان إلى اختيار من هو للإمامة أصلح وبتدبير المصالح أقوم وأعرف. (الأحكام السلطانية، ص ٦، الباب الأول في عقد الإمامة).

If one casts an eye over the books of Islamic history and of the science of narrators, it is plainly evident that Ḥaḍrat ʿUmar al-Fārūq (raḍiyallāhu taʿālā ʿanhu), during his caliphate, appointed a woman Companion to superintend the administration of the market, and used to give her precedence in offering opinions and counsel. The name of this woman Companion is Ḥaḍrat Shifāʾ (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhā).

ʿAllāmah Ibn Ḥajar al-ʿAsqalānī (raḥimahullāh) writes in his notice of this Companion:

الشفاء بنت عبد الله بن عبد شمس بن خلف

وكان عمر رضي الله عنه يقدمها في الرأي ويرعاها ويفضلها وربما ولاها شيئاً من أمر السوق. (الإصابة في تمييز الصحابة: ۸/۲۰۲، ط: دار الكتب العلمية، بيروت).

Besides this, among the noble Companions (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhum) the Mother of the Believers, Sayyidah Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah al-Ṣiddīqah (raḍiyallāhu taʿālā ʿanhā), held a lofty rank. Whenever the noble Companions (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhum) encountered a difficulty in some matter, she would always be asked about it, and her opinion carried great weight.

ʿAllāmah Ibn Ḥajar (raḥimahullāh) writes as follows concerning her eminence:

وقال أبو الضحى عن مسروق رأيت مشيخة أصحاب رسول الله ﷺ الأكابر يسألونها عن الفرائض، وقال عطاء بن أبي رباح رحمه الله كانت عائشة رضي الله عنه أفقه الناس وأعلم الناس وأحسن الناس رأياً في العامة

وقال أبو بردة بن أبي موسى عن أبيه ما أشكل علينا أمر فسألنا عنه عائشة رضي الله عنه إلا وجدنا عندها فيه علماً. (الإصابة في تمييز الصحابة: ۸/۲۳۳، ترجمة عائشة رضي الله عنه، بيروت).

From these passages it is established that women are permitted to play some part in the administration of the state as well, over and above domestic affairs. In the view of the Ḥanafīs a woman may become a judge in matters other than the prescribed penalties and retaliation.

If a woman becomes a member of an assembly or parliament while observing the requirements of the sharīʿah, there is no objection to it in the sharīʿah. Yet, keeping in view the role of women in parliament in the present age, granting permission for this is of no benefit to any religious purpose; rather, women in parliament generally do more work of an irreligious nature under the banner of reviving women's rights, and for this reason it is more sound not to send them into the assemblies.

Nevertheless, nothing in the evidence set out above establishes anywhere that a woman may become head of state, because clear proofs exist in the hadith and in the Glorious Qur'an regarding a woman's headship; and besides this, wherever the conditions of the supreme leadership have been stated, the condition of maleness has necessarily been mentioned. (فتاوی حقانیہ: ۲/۳۱۷۔۳۲۰).

Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

It is not forbidden for women to become voters; yes, at the time of voting it will be necessary to observe the sharʿī veil. Standing as a candidate, however, is not commendable for women, because taking part in a council or assembly while observing the requirements of the sharīʿah is impossible for women. (کفایت المفتی: ۳/۲٦۱).
