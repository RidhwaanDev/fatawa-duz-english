### TITLE
Divorce given while the wife is intoxicated

### QUESTION
A woman is always in a state of intoxication. Her husband, worn out by this, gave three divorces; at that time too the woman was intoxicated, and she abused him and said, "Get out of here." The husband now wishes to know whether divorce took place in this state or not.

### ANSWER
In the case in question three divorces have taken effect, and it is now not permissible to marry this woman without a valid ḥalālah according to the Sharia. A valid ḥalālah is that after the waiting period this woman marries another man, and then he divorces her or dies; after she has completed the waiting period she may marry her first husband.

Note that for divorce to take effect it is not even necessary for the wife to be present. Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states:

Question: A man divorced his wife in her absence. Has divorce taken effect or not?

Answer: In this case divorce has taken effect upon his wife, because it is not necessary for the woman to be present and nearby at the time of giving divorce. (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband: 9/42, "Mudallal wa Mukammal", Dār al-Ishāʿat).

Elsewhere it states: for divorce to take effect it is not a condition that the wife be present. (9/80).

And in another place it is recorded: it is not a condition that the wife be addressed or be face to face; if the wife is absent and is not addressed and divorce is given in her absence, even then divorce takes effect. (9/85).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

For divorce to take effect it is not a condition that the woman be present, or that she hear the words of divorce, or that divorce be given naming the woman. (Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 4/503, Deoband).
