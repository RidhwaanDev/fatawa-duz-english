### TITLE
Kissing the thumbs during the adhan

### QUESTION
What is the position on kissing the thumbs during the adhan?

### ANSWER
To kiss the nails of the thumbs and place them on the eyes on hearing the blessed name of the Prophet ﷺ during the adhan, to regard this as a sunnah, to suppose it established by a prophetic hadith, and to treat anyone who does not do it as deserving of abuse and blame — all of this is wrong, and it is a distortion of the religion. This much is correct: some scholars have declared the act permissible. But even they allowed it as a practice and remedy for ailments of the eye, not as an act of worship, not as a sunnah in its own right, and not as a way of honouring and exalting the Prophet ﷺ.

ʿAllāmah Ibn ʿĀbidīn al-Shāmī says:

وفي كتاب الفردوس "من قبل ظفري إبهامه عند سماع "أشهد أن محمدا رسول الله"

وذكر ذلك الجراحي وأطال، ثم قال: ولم يصح من المرفوع من كل هذا شيء. (شامي: ۱/۳۹۸، سعيد)

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

The hadiths and reports that have come down about kissing the thumbs during the adhan are quoted, on the authority of al-Daylamī's Musnad al-Firdaws, in Mawḍūʿāt al-Kabīr, Tadhkirat al-Mawḍūʿāt, al-Fawāʾid al-Majmūʿah fī al-Aḥādīth al-Mawḍūʿah and similar works.

Citing ʿAllāmah al-Sakhāwī (may Allah have mercy on him), Mullā ʿAlī al-Qārī (may Allah have mercy on him) says of these reports: "لا يصح" (موضوعاتِ کبیر: ص ۷۵) — that is, the reports are not sound.

ʿAllāmah Muḥammad Ṭāhir (may Allah have mercy on him) likewise writes: "ولا يصح" (تذکرۃ الموضوعات ص ۳۴) — this report is not sound.

And the great master of hadith, ʿAllāmah Jalāl al-Dīn al-Suyūṭī (may Allah have mercy on him), writes:

الأحاديث التي رويت في تقبيل الأنامل وجعلها على العينين عند سماع اسمه ﷺ عن المؤذن في كلمة الشهادة كلها موضوعات.

That is: all the hadiths cited about kissing the fingers and placing them on the eyes on hearing the Prophet's ﷺ blessed name from the muezzin in the testimony of faith are fabricated — false and invented. (تیسیر المقال وغیرہ) And acting on a fabricated hadith is not permissible. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۱/۶۰)

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states:

Kissing the thumb at the two testimonies in the adhan is not correct if it is taken to be a sunnah. And since in our times most people do it believing it to be a sunnah, and blame and revile anyone who leaves it, the leading scholars have now abandoned the practice. (فتاوی دار العلوم دیوبند: ۲/۹۰، از مفتی عزیز الرحمٰن صاحب رحمه الله)

Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

There is no evidence for kissing the thumbs and putting them to the eyes at the two testimonies in the adhan. (کفایت المفتی: ۳/۵۱) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
