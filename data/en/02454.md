### TITLE
The ruling on the sacrifice for one whose wealth is unlawful

### QUESTION
If a person possesses wealth that is unlawfully acquired, does the sacrifice become necessary upon him on account of it or not?

### ANSWER
The whole of unlawfully acquired wealth must be given away in charity, and so it is not wealth upon which a sacrifice falls due: there is no sacrifice in respect of unlawful wealth. (فتاوی الشامی: ۲/ ۲۹۱، باب زکوۃ الغنم، سعید) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
