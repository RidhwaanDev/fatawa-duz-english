### TITLE
A Muslim resident of South Africa taking interest

### QUESTION
Is it permissible for a Muslim of South Africa to take interest from a non-Muslim bank here in South Africa, or not?

### ANSWER
South Africa is a land of security, not a land of war. And even supposing it were a land of war, it would still be impermissible for a resident here to take interest from a non-Muslim bank or from a non-Muslim person. Yes, if a Muslim of South Africa goes to China, America or Russia, takes up temporary residence there and takes a profit from the non-Muslims there with their consent, or from a non-Muslim bank, then — if we call those countries a land of war — this transaction will be permissible in the view of the Ṭarafayn, Imām Abū Ḥanīfah and Imām Muḥammad (may Allah have mercy on them).
