### TITLE
Sacrificing an animal that is no longer able to breed

### QUESTION
If an animal is no longer able to bear young on account of its advanced age, will its sacrifice be valid or not?

### ANSWER
The sacrifice of such an animal is permissible. (Fatāwā ʿĀlamgīriyyah: 5/297).
