### TITLE
Medicine left over from a medical-aid company

### QUESTION
A man has taken out medical aid. If he wishes to draw medicine through this medical aid for his sick brother, is that permissible or not? Likewise, if he obtained medicine from the company for himself but some was left over, may he give it to another person or not?

### ANSWER
If someone obtained medicine from a medical-aid company for himself and it was left over for some reason, and there is no rule or arrangement for returning it, then it is permissible to give that medicine to someone else. But if he uses his own name and draws medicine for another person, this is deception and fraud and is impermissible. Yes, if permission has been given by the medical-aid company that one may also draw medicine for one's children or parents, then drawing medicine for them is permissible.
