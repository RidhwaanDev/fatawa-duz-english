### TITLE
The ruling on a man bathing a young girl

### QUESTION
If a girl of one or two years of age passes away, may a man bathe her, or will only a woman bathe her? And would a man's bathing her be disliked or permissible?

### ANSWER
It is permissible and correct for a man to bathe a young girl. Consider what is recorded in al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq:

والصبي الذي لا يشتهى والصبية كذلك غسلهما الرجال والنساء. (البحر الرائق: ۲/۱۸۸، دار المعرفة بيروت).

It is recorded in Fatāwā Hindiyyah:

فإن كانت الميت صغيراً لا يشتهى جاز أن يغسله النساء وكذا إذا كانت صغيرة لا تشتهى جاز للرجل غسلها. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱٦۰).

It is recorded in ʿUmdat al-Fiqh:

If a boy is so young that he is not an object of desire, it is permissible for women to bathe him; and likewise if a girl is young and has not yet reached the age at which she becomes an object of desire, it is permissible for men to bathe her. A boy or girl who has reached the age of being an object of desire has the ruling of an adult; so a strange woman cannot bathe such a boy, and a man cannot bathe such a girl. (ʿUmdat al-Fiqh: 2/501).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
