### TITLE
Breaking one's ablution during ṭawāf

### QUESTION
If a person's ablution breaks during ṭawāf and he goes to perform ablution, should he begin the ṭawāf afresh when he returns, or resume from the point where he stopped? Similarly, if an obligatory prayer begins and he goes to pray, will he start afresh after the prayer or carry on from where he left off?

### ANSWER
In both of the above cases, if he had already completed the greater part of the ṭawāf, then after finishing the prayer he should resume from the point at which he stopped; and if he starts again from the beginning, that too is correct. If the greater part of the ṭawāf had not been completed, but only one or two circuits had been done, then in that case starting afresh is recommended, though building on what has already been performed is also permissible. Consider what Ghunyat al-Nāsik states:

ولو خرج من الطواف أو من السعي إلى جنازة أو مكتوبة أو تجديد وضوء ثم عاد بني لو كان ذلك بعد إتيان أكثره ولو استأنف لا شيء عليه فلا يلزمه إتمام الأول لأن هذا الاستيناف للإكمال بالموالاة بين الأشواط ويستحب الاستيناف في الطواف إذا كان ذلك قبل إتيان أكثره. (غنية الناسك، ص ٦۸، فصل في مكروهات الطواف).

Kitāb al-Fatāwā states:

He should go to perform ablution from the very point in the ṭawāf at which his ablution broke, and after performing ablution he should resume the ṭawāf from that same point and complete the seven circuits. The circuits performed earlier are not lost because his ablution broke; rather he will count them and complete the seven circuits. (Kitāb al-Fatāwā: 4/47).
