### TITLE
The ruling on ṣalāh over a loudspeaker with an echoing (echo) sound

### QUESTION
Nowadays there is a loudspeaker in masjids, and sometimes the sound echoes in it. In such a state will the ṣalāh be valid or not? And is this contrary to the humility of the ṣalāh or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
If, with the echoing sound, the recitation is heard clearly and no strain is felt upon the minds of the worshippers, then the ṣalāh is valid and it is not contrary to humility. But if the worshippers experience mental distraction, or there is a repetition of the sound at a very high volume, then such a loudspeaker should not be used in the ṣalāh.

For the evidences that ṣalāh over a loudspeaker is valid, please see: (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā, volume two, from p. 398 to p. 406, Majlis al-Buḥūth, Mumbai).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
