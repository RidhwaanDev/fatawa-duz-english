### TITLE
Marrying the sister of one's stepmother

### QUESTION
Is the sister of a man's stepmother — that is, of his father's wife with whom the father has consummated the marriage — a maḥram to him or not? In other words, may he marry her?

### ANSWER
In the case in question the sister of the father's wife with whom he has consummated the marriage — that is, the sister of the stepmother — is not a maḥram, and there is scope to marry her.

قال الله تعالى: ﴿وأحل لكم ما وراء ذلكم﴾.
وفي روح المعاني للعلامة الآلوسي البغدادي الحنفي: ﴿ما وراء ذلكم﴾ إشارة إلى ما تقدم من المحرمات أي أحل لكم نكاح ما سواهن انفراداً وجمعاً. (روح المعاني: ۵/٤، القاهرة).

Al-Shāmī states:

ولا تحرم بنت زوج الأم ولا أمه ولا أم زوجة الأب ولا بنتها. (شامي: ۳/۳۱، فصل في المحرمات، سعيد).

Fatḥ al-Qadīr states:

فلذا أجاز التزويج بأم زوجة الابن وبنتها، وجاز للابن التزوج بأم زوجة الأب وبنتها. (فتح القدير: ۳/۲۱۱، فصل في المحرمات، دار الفكر).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states: Marriage with a maternal aunt is forbidden, but a maternal aunt is the sister of one's real mother. The sister of a stepmother — that is, of the father's second wife — is not a maternal aunt, and marriage with her is not forbidden. So it is valid for the son to marry the full sister of his father's second wife, provided there is no other relationship that creates prohibition, such as fosterage. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 11/271, Mubawwab wa Murattab).

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states: If there are two full sisters and one of them is married to the father while the other is married to the son, this is valid; there is no objection to it in the sharia. It comes under

﴿وأحل لكم ما وراء ذلكم﴾

The principle is that it is forbidden for two sisters to be joined in marriage to one man; there is no prohibition on their being married to a father and his son. (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm: 7/176, Mudallal wa Mukammal, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states: Question: Zayd's wife Hind died. Zayd then married another woman, and a son was born to her. Can this son now marry the sister of Zayd's deceased first wife or not? Answer: He can. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 5/85).
