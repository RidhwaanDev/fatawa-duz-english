### TITLE
Obedience to the husband takes precedence over the shaykh in lawful and permissible matters

### QUESTION
If a difference arises between the shaykh and the husband, is it necessary for the woman to follow her husband or her shaykh? Which of the two will she give preference to? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In matters that the pure sharia has made obligatory and binding, following the sharia is necessary, and in matters contrary to the sharia obedience to anyone at all is not permissible. As for permissible and lawful matters, however, if a difference arises between the husband and the shaykh, then, since the right of the husband takes precedence over that of the shaykh, it is necessary to accept the word of the husband. Many narrations show that the right of the husband takes precedence. Note the narrations below:

أخرج الإمام أبو داود في سننه (۲۱٤۲) بسنده عن قيس بن سعد، وفيه: قال: لوكنت آمراً أحداً أن يسجد لا حد لا مرت النساء أن يسجدن لا زواجهن لما جعل الله لهم عليهن من الحق.

The upshot is that the Prophet ﷺ said: the husband has such a right over the woman that, if it were permissible to prostrate to anyone other than Allah Most High, I would have commanded women to prostrate to their husbands.

One hadith states: the Prophet ﷺ said: when the husband calls his wife, she should carry out his command at once, even if she is at the oven.

أخرج الإمام الترمذي في سننه (۱۱٦۰) بسنده عن طلق بن على رضي الله عنه قال: قال رسول الله ﷺ: إذا الرجل دعا زوجته لحاجته فلتأته وإن كانت على التنور.

One hadith states: if a woman guards the five daily prayers, guards her chastity, keeps the fasts of the blessed month of Ramadan and obeys her husband, then let her enter Paradise by whichever of its gates she wishes.

إذا صلت المرأة خمسها، وأحصنت فرجها، وصامت شهرها، وأطاعت بعلها، فلتدخل من أي أبواب الجنة شاء ت. رواه الربيع بن صبيح: عن يزيد الرقاشي، عن أنس رضي الله عنه والربيع ضعيف. (ذخيرة الحفاظ: ۱/۳۳۲/۳۳۷، ومسند أحمد، رقم: 1٦٦۱، وابن حبان، رقم: ٤۱٦۳). (وكذا أخرجه ابن عدي في الكامل (۳/۱۳۳). قال الشيخ شعيب الارنؤوط: حسن لغيره وهذا إسناد ضعيف لضعف ابن لهيعة. (التعليقات على مسند أحمد، رقم: 1٦٦۱).

In matters contrary to the sharia, to accept anyone's word is impermissible and a sin. Note what the hadith states:

عن ابن عمر رضي الله عنه عن النبي ﷺ، قال: على المرء المسلم السمع والطاعة فيما أحب وكره، إلا أن يؤمر بمعصية، فإذا أمر بمعصية فلا سمع ولا طاعة. (متفق عليه).

وعن على رضي الله عنه أن النبي ﷺ بعث جيشاً وأمر عليهم رجلاً فأوقد ناراً وقال: ادخلوها فأرادوا أن يدخلوها وقال آخرون: إنما فررنا منها فذكروا للنبي ﷺ، فقال للذين أرادوا أن يدخلوها: لو دخلوها لم يزالوا فيها إلى يوم القيامة وقال للآخرين: لا طاعة في معصية إنما الطاعة في المعروف. (صحيح البخاري، رقم: ۷۲۵۷).

Āp kē Masāʾil awr Un kā Ḥal states: if the command of one of them is correct in the sharia and that of the other is wrong, then the command of the one whose command is correct should be obeyed, whether it be the mother or the husband; and if both the matters are permissible, then for the woman the command of the husband takes precedence. (Āp kē Masāʾil awr Un kā Ḥal 6/320).

It is written in Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: if after the marriage the parents stop the woman from obeying her husband in lawful matters, they have no right to do so, and in that situation it is not binding upon the girl to obey them either. Respect for the parents and for the husband is binding upon all; but it is not permissible to accept anyone's word when it is contrary to what is right. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah 18/600).

Kifāyat al-Muftī states: obedience to the husband, the guarding of her chastity and the safeguarding of his house are binding upon the wife. If both the father and the husband are in need of service, then service of the husband takes precedence. (Kifāyat al-Muftī 5/235, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

The Prophet's ﷺ attribute of being a mercy to the worlds does not mean that he distributes bounties:

Question: a certain person says that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ is a mercy to the worlds, that is, that after his demise he distributes mercies. Is this meaning correct or not? بینوا توجروا۔

Answer: in the case in question the meaning and sense of "mercy" is this:

الرفقة والتعطف

(Tāj al-ʿArūs), that is, gentleness, compassion and sympathy. It is for this reason that when the Prophet ﷺ was asked to invoke a curse, he ﷺ would say:

"إني لم أبعث لعاناً وإنما بعثت رحمة"

The application of "mercy" to the Prophet ﷺ is from several points of view:

1. He brings people out of the darknesses of misguidance and leads them towards the light of guidance.

2. He unites with Allah those servants who have turned away from Allah.

3. He teaches man praiseworthy character and beautiful qualities.

4. On the Day of Judgement he will intercede for the community in every place: the plain of the gathering, the pool of Kawthar and elsewhere.

5. He will not be pleased until all the Muslims have entered Paradise.

6. He is a mercy in the sense that a general punishment will not come upon this community as it came upon earlier communities.

7. "Mercy" here means a bounty: whoever valued him ﷺ and believed will go to Paradise, and whoever denied him will enter Hell. (For these meanings the books of tafsīr and the commentaries on hadith may be consulted.)

To take the meaning of "mercy" as

إعطاء الرحمة والنعمة

that is, in the sense of bestowing, is not correct, because the word "mercy" has been used for the earlier prophets (peace be upon them) as well; for instance about Ḥaḍrat ʿĪsā (peace be upon him):

﴿ولنجعله آية للناس ورحمة منا﴾،

about Ḥaḍrat Ṣāliḥ (peace be upon him):

﴿وآتاني رحمة من عنده﴾،

about Ḥaḍrat Khiḍr (peace be upon him):

﴿وآتيناه رحمة من عندنا﴾،

and it has even been used for the noble Companions. Note:

﴿أشداء على الكفار رحماء بينهم﴾

and in other places besides. Yet no one holds the belief that the prophets and the Companions have the power to send mercy, because the bestowing of mercy is particular to "al-Raḥmān" alone, for the reason that in the language the meaning of al-Raḥmān is:

ذو الرحمة التي لا غاية بعدها في الرحمة" فالرحمن الذي وسعت رحمته كل شيء.

And the word

"الرحمن"

is particular to Allah Most High.

قال الله تعالى: ﴿قل ادعوا الله أو ادعوا الرحمن﴾ [الإسراء: ۱۱۰]

The upshot is that mercy is to be sought from Allah Most High alone, and Allah Most High has Himself taught us this:

﴿ربنا لا تزغ قلوبنا بعد إذ هديتنا وهب لنا من لدنك رحمة، إنك أنت الوهاب﴾

O our Lord, do not let our hearts swerve after You have guided us, and grant us mercy from Your presence; without doubt You are the great Bestower. (Bayān al-Qurʾān 2/3).
