### TITLE
A particular practice with Sūrah Yā Sīn for the fulfilment of a need

### QUESTION
A practice with Sūrah Yā Sīn is carried out for every kind of need. The method is that the word mubīn occurs nine times in the whole of Sūrah Yā Sīn, so that each time one reaches mubīn one begins again from the start; in this way Sūrah Yā Sīn has to be recited nine times. Is such a practice permissible or not? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
The basic principle is that it is not necessary for such practices to be established from the Qurʾān and the ḥadīth; it is sufficient that they are not contrary to the Qurʾān and the ḥadīth. The reason for this is that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said to certain people: Present your practices to me — that is, if they are contrary to the Sharīʿah they will be rejected, and if not, then there is no harm in them. Since reciting Sūrah Yā Sīn in the manner described above is not contrary to the Qurʾān and the ḥadīth, this practice may be carried out on the basis of the experience of some of our elders.

Kamālāt-i ʿAzīzī states: The khatm of Sūrah Yā Sīn is also established from the mashāʾikh for every important need. When some need arises, one should recite the said sūrah in this order: at every occurrence of the word mubīn he should begin again from the start. When the sūrah has been completed in this way, he should uncover his head, go into sajdah, and ask for his need with the utmost humility. He should keep this up daily until his object is attained. (Kamālāt-i ʿAzīziyyah, p. 85).

Further, it is stated in the ḥadīth that whoever recites Sūrah Yā Sīn in the morning, that day's tasks are made easy for him; and if he recites it in the evening, the tasks from the evening until the morning are made easy.

See Sunan al-Dārimī, which states:

حدثنا عمرو بن زرارة، حدثنا عبد الوهاب، حدثنا راشد أبو محمد الحماني، عن شهر بن حوشب، قال قال ابن عباس رضي الله عنه: من قرأ يسٓ حين يصبح، أعطي يسر يومه حتى يمسي، ومن قرأها في صدر ليله، أعطي يسر ليلته حتى يصبح. إسناده حسن وهو موقوف على ابن عباس. (سنن الدارقطني مع التعليق: ٤/۲۱۵۱/۳٤٦۲).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
