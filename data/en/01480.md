### TITLE
Saying "divorce" but intending the divorce from the first husband

### QUESTION
A man had earlier given his wife two divorces. Some time later, during a quarrel, he said "divorce", but he says that what he meant by it was: "You were divorced by your first husband." Is the wife now allowed by the sharia to remain with her husband or not? Note that the wife had indeed been divorced by her first husband.

### ANSWER
In the case described, if the husband's intention was exactly what is stated in the question, then no divorce has taken place and the wife may remain with her husband. In future, however, he should avoid words of this kind.

Al-Ṭaḥṭāwī states:

وكذا لو نوى طلاقها من زوجها الأول على الصحيح (خانية) أي يصدق قضاءً وديانة إذا كان لها زوج طلقها قبل. (حاشية الطحطاوي على الدر المختار: ۲/۱۱۳).

In short, the better course is for the couple to take their case to an arbitrator. The arbitrator will put the husband on oath; if he swears to it, and the wife had in fact been divorced by her first husband, then she may remain with her husband.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
