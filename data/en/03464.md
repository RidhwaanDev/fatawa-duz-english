### TITLE

Wiping the head and passing the fingers through the beard when washing the deceased

### QUESTION

When washing the deceased, is it sunnah to pass the fingers of one hand through the fingers of the other and through the beard, and to wipe the head, as it is sunnah in the ablution of a living person?

### ANSWER

The jurists have stated that when washing the deceased a complete ablution should be performed for him, as is done for prayer; except that water is not to be put into the mouth or the nose, because of the difficulty of getting it out again. It is better, though, to pass moist cotton wool around inside the mouth and the nose, so that the sunnah of rinsing the mouth and drawing water into the nose is fulfilled and there is no need to get water out of the mouth. It is also better to wipe the head, and the fingers should be passed through the beard and between the fingers as well.

Consider what is recorded in al-Ḥujjah ʿalā Ahl al-Madīnah:

قال أبو حنيفة رحمه الله في غسل الميت يجرد ثيابه ويطرح على عورته خرقة ويوضع على تخت ويوضأ وضوءه للصلاة ولا يمضمض ولا يستنشق ويغسل رأسه ولحيته بالخطمي. (الحجة على أهل المدينة: ۱/۳٤۸، ط: عالم الكتب).

قال في الهندية: ثم يوضأ وضوءه للصلاة

واختلفوا في مسح رأسه والصحيح أنه يمسح رأسه. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱۵۸). وكذا في بدائع الصنائع: ۱/۳۰۰، سعيد، وفتح القدير: ۲/۷۲، دار الفكر).

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh states: then perform for the deceased an ablution like that for prayer, but in this ablution do not rinse the mouth or put water into the nose, because it would then be impossible or difficult to get the water out of the mouth and nose. Then wipe the head — this is the correct view and it is the apparent narration of the school. (ʿUmdat al-Fiqh: 2/488.)
