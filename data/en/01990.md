### TITLE
Paying a butcher in advance and taking meat from him

### QUESTION
I am very fond of cow and buffalo trotters. I gave a butcher one thousand rupees in advance on the understanding that he would give me cow or buffalo trotters at the rate of ten rupees a day. Some people call this the purchase of something non-existent and say it is impermissible. Is this transaction permissible or impermissible?

### ANSWER
This transaction is permissible. For the detailed references, see the previous fatwa; repeating them here would only make this longer.

See further: (Jadīd Muʿāmalāt ke Sharʿī Aḥkām: 1/122).
