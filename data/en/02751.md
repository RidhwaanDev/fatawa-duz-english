### TITLE

The ruling on men using black hair dye

### QUESTION

Is it permissible for men to dye the hair and the beard with black henna, or not? A certain man dyes his hair black and pleads as his excuse that his wife is young and that his hair turned white before its time; and as proof he says that Ḥusayn (may Allah be pleased with him) too used to use black dye. Kindly inform me of the ruling of the Sharīʿah and earn a great reward.

### ANSWER

In our view it is makrūh taḥrīmī (prohibitively disliked) for men to use black dye, and the prohibition of it has come in numerous narrations. In a narration of Muslim Sharīf it is stated that the Prophet ﷺ ordered the father of Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq (may Allah be pleased with him) to dye his hair and commanded him to keep away from black dye. Some of the reports set out below, however, point towards permissibility. Consider:

واستدل المجوزون بآثار كثير من الصحابة والتابعين، قال ابن القيم رحمه الله في زاد المعاد (۳/۱۸٤): فقد صح عن الحسن والحسين رضي الله تعالى عنهما أنهما كانا يخضبان بالسواد، ذكر ذلك ابن جرير رحمه الله عنهما في كتاب تهذيب الآثار، وذكره عن عثمان بن عفان وعبد الله بن جعفر، وسعد بن أبي وقاص، وعقبة بن عامر، والمغيرة بن شعبة، وجرير بن عبد الله، وعمرو بن العاص رضي الله تعالى عنهم أجمعين، وحكاه عن جماعة من التابعين

الخ. (تكملة فتح الملهم: ٤/۱۵۰).

The answer to this is that what is meant in these reports is not pure black dye but a mixed one, which is permitted; or else that, set against the sound marfūʿ narrations in which the prohibition of black dye has come, these mawqūf reports will not be given weight. ʿAllāmah al-Ḥamawī (may Allah have mercy on him) said that the noble Companions were fighters in jihād, and for a fighter it is correct.

قال الحموي رحمه الله: وهذا في حق غير الغزاة ولا يحرم في حقهم للإرهاب ولعله محمل من فعل ذلك من الصحابة. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۷۵٦، سعيد).

This is also supported by the report of ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb (may Allah be pleased with him). Consider:

إنه كان يأمر بالخضاب بالسواد ويقول: هو تسكين للزوجة وأهيب للعدو. (عمدة القاري: ۱۵/۹۷، باب الخضاب، ط: ملتان)
Use black dye, for it is effective in striking fear into the enemy.
Moreover, once the hair has turned white, black dye does not even look becoming; a man looks odd with it.
Thus Dr Samrīn Farīd writes:
To colour hair that is turning white, use hair colours of a good and suitable quality. With advancing age the skin becomes thin, and a black dye does not sit well on it. (خواتین کی صحت، ص۳٦٦، دار الشعور، لاہور) ۔
The medical harm of black dye:
According to the most recent research of the scientists of the National Institute of America, the dye (hair dye) used to blacken the hair contains an ingredient on account of which cancer can be contracted. The apprehension which a scientist of the University of California expressed about such dye some years ago has today been confirmed by the research of the American institute.
Chemical hair colours and dyes also increase the risk of cancer of the breast and of the ovaries, so women should avoid using such chemical dyes and should use henna and the like in their place. (اسلام صحت اور جدید سائنسی تحقیقات: ۱۲۸، ادارۂ اشاعت اسلام) ۔
A useful point:
أول من صبغ لحيته بالسواد ففرعون موسي عليه السلام. (مصنف ابن أبي شيبة: ۱۲/۵۵٦، وعمدة القاري: ۱۵/۹۷، باب الخضاب، دار الحديث ملتان، المرقاة: ۸/۲۳۵، باب الترجل، رشيديه).

That is, the first to use black dye on the beard was Pharaoh.
Further evidence has already been mentioned above and may be consulted there.
There is, however, a narration from Imām Abū Yūsuf (may Allah have mercy on him) that a man whose wife is young is permitted to use black dye; but the fatwa has not been given on it. Radd al-Muḥtār states:

وروي عن أبي يوسف رحمه الله أنه قال: كما يعجبني أن تتزين لي يعجبها أن أتزين لها كذا في الذخيرة. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/٤۲۲، سعيد).
(وكذا في الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳۵۹، والمبسوط للامام السرخسي رحمه الله: ۱۰/۱۹۹، ط: دار الفكر، والمحيط البرهاني: ٦/، الفصل الحادي والعشرون).
