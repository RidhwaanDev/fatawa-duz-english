### TITLE
Girls reciting the Qur'an and naʿt

### QUESTION
May adult girls recite the Qur'an, naʿt (poetry in praise of the Prophet ﷺ) and the like in a mixed gathering of men and women, or not?

### ANSWER
Adult girls and women should not recite the Qur'an or naʿt in a fine voice for men to hear; there is a fear of temptation in this. Yes, if the gathering is not mixed but consists of women alone, and they recite in a plain manner without displaying a fine voice, then the sharia leaves room for it, provided there is no temptation.

The proofs have been set out in detail above. The upshot is that, according to the sounder view, a woman's voice is not part of what must be kept covered; but she will be stopped because of the fear of temptation, and reciting in a gathering of men is not free of temptation, so it should be avoided.

Hazrat Mufti Kifāyatullāh Sahib says:

Beating the duff (hand-drum) on the occasion of a wedding is permissible, and it is permissible for women to sing songs among women on two conditions: the first is that their voices should not reach the ears of non-mahram men; the second is that the song should contain nothing obscene and no impermissible words. (کفایت المفتی: ۹/۲۰۰، دار الاشاعت).

Kitāb al-Fatāwā states:

The real aim is to close the doors to temptation. Wherever there is a fear of temptation in the voice reaching a non-mahram, or in its being made to reach him, it is necessary to guard against it — for instance, letting a non-mahram hear a naʿt or a ḥamd. الخ ۔ (کتاب الفتاوی: ٦/۹۹).
