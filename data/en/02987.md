### TITLE
The wisdom in not changing the Companion's name ʿUkkāshah, which means spider

### QUESTION
The Prophet ﷺ used to change names that were disliked, yet one of the Companions was named ʿUkkāshah ibn Miḥṣan, which means "spider", a small and lowly creature. Why then did the Prophet ﷺ not change it, and what was the wisdom in this? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question, the principle in choosing names is that one does not look at the outward meaning of the word alone; the good qualities and good characteristics associated with it are also taken into account. There are many examples of this:

1. Ḥanẓalah means "bitter", but what is meant is bitterness towards falsehood and towards the disbelievers.

2. ʿIkrimah means "pigeon", which is likewise a small and lowly creature; but in former times pigeons were used to carry messages, so the sense is one who conveys the religion of Allah.

3. A girl is given the name Ḥirāʾ, and by Ḥirāʾ what is intended is an allusion to the blessed place of the first revelation.

Derived from Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā (7/580).

Some of the good qualities of the spider, then, are as follows:

1. The spider is a creature of simple living, for the spider's house is the frailest of houses:

وإن أوهن البيوت لبيت العنكبوت

Under this noble verse, Shaykh Muḥammad Sharīfullāh Ṣāḥib has set out four kinds of house in al-Tafsīr al-Badīʿ. (a) A house of stone: it gives shade, and safety from heat, cold and wind, and also from water, fire, dust and the like. (b) A house of wood: it gives shade and the benefit of protection from cold and heat, but there is no safety from fire. (c) A tent of wool or hair: it gives shade, but there is no safety from cold, heat and so on. (d) The spider's house: there is no benefit in it at all — neither shade nor safety from cold and heat. It is a dwelling of the very lowest order, and so it embodies contentment and simplicity. (Al-Tafsīr al-Badīʿ: 2/108).

Ḥayāt al-Ḥayawān states:

أحرص الأشياء الذباب وأقنع الأشياء العنكبوت

that is, the greediest of creatures is the fly and the most patient of creatures is the spider. (Ḥayāt al-Ḥayawān: 2/27).

2. Another quality of the spider is that it kills the fly, that is, it drives off the insects of the earth. This is like the dream Imam al-Bukhārī (may Allah have mercy on him) saw, in which he was driving flies away from the Prophet ﷺ. The interpreters explained it to mean that he would drive falsehood and interpolation away from the hadiths of the Prophet ﷺ; and he then composed Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī. (Hady al-Sārī, chapter one, p. 6). In the same way the spider kept the idolaters, who were worse than animals, from reaching the Prophet ﷺ in the cave.

3. Killing a fly is a very difficult task, yet the spider accomplishes it with consummate skill; and this points to the consummate skill of the one who bears the name.

4. At the time of the migration, in the cave of Thawr, the spider screened the Prophet ﷺ. In appreciation of this act Allah revealed an entire chapter of the Noble Qur'an. This honour alone is sufficient reason for the name not to be changed. So it was for the reasons mentioned above that the Prophet ﷺ did not change the name.

See the references: (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1111; al-Munjid fī al-Lughah, p. 522; al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 619; al-Ṣiḥāḥ of al-Jawharī: 3/850; Lisān al-ʿArab: 6/319; Lisān al-Lisān: 2/209; and Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā: 7/573).
