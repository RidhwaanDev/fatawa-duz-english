### TITLE
Saying "Go, you are free; I set you free"

### QUESTION
Respected Muftī Ṣāḥib, I come to you respectfully with a problem and seek your considered opinion. In a domestic quarrel my husband said to me, "What are you worth? Giving a divorce takes one minute. You want to be free? Go, you are free; I set you free." In reply I said, "Fine — if it takes a minute, then take it. I don't want to live with you either; I want a divorce too." Bearing these circumstances in mind, has my divorce taken place or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, if the husband really did say the words recorded in the question, one irrevocable divorce has taken effect upon his wife. There are two reasons why an irrevocable divorce takes effect with these words. The first is that in common usage these words are used for divorce. ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) wrote of "أنت على حرام" — that is, "You are unlawful to me" — that because of people's usage an irrevocable divorce takes effect with it.

Muftī Rashīd Aḥmad Ludhiyānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) writes: the third sentence, "I have set you free", is an explicit and irrevocable divorce; so whether he intended divorce by it or not, one irrevocable divorce has taken effect. (احسن الفتاوی۵/۲۰۲) ۔

The second reason is that talk of divorce was already going on, namely, "What are you worth? Giving a divorce takes one minute. You want to be free? Go, you are free; I set you free." So one irrevocable divorce has taken effect upon the wife. In all likelihood the ʿiddah after the divorce has already passed, since for a woman who menstruates the ʿiddah is the passing of three menstrual cycles. If the ʿiddah has passed, the woman may marry elsewhere; she may also marry her former husband, and no ḥalālah of any kind is needed. If the husband denies these words and the wife has no valid witnesses, the oath falls upon the husband; if he swears that he did not say them, his word will be accepted. But since the wife is certain that these words were said, she should not keep her husband with her without a fresh marriage. If she does marry her former husband again, he will thereafter have the right of only two divorces; and if at some point he gives those two divorces, she will be irrevocably divorced.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
