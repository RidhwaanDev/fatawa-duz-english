### TITLE
Praying behind people who hold corrupt beliefs

### QUESTION
In some mosques the imams are Shia, or hold false beliefs. Is it permissible to pray behind them, and to send children to their madrasa?

### ANSWER
Prayer performed behind an imam who holds Shia beliefs is not valid, so avoid it. Likewise, if the people running their madrasa and school also hold those same beliefs, then children must never be enrolled in such a school or maktab either.

For a detailed fatwa see: volume two, the chapter on leading the prayer.
