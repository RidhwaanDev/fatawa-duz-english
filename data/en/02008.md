### TITLE
The procedure of forex and its ruling

### QUESTION
What is the procedure of forex, and what is the ruling of the sharīʿah on it?

بينوا بالتفصيل توجروا بأجر جزيل.

### ANSWER
The gist of forex is that Zayd opens a bank account with one thousand dollars, whereupon the company deposits a further ninety-nine thousand dollars with him as a loan. This person can then do business with one hundred thousand dollars. Thereafter, through the company, he can buy a lot worth one hundred thousand from some bank — as though one hundred thousand dollars had come under his guarantee. Then, after some days, if the price of the dollar rises, this person telephones to sell one hundred thousand dollars for pounds through the company, and the company sells the dollars for pounds. In between, the company provides the following facilities: (1) contact by telephone, (2) a market where one can sit and do business, (3) an internet system, (4) ninety thousand as surety, besides several other facilities. Then, each time currency is bought and sold, the company collects a fixed sum as commission; and if, after the purchase of the currency, there is a delay in selling it, the company collects a certain sum on a daily basis.

In our view this business is, on the face of it, correct. But apparently several objections arise against it.

Objection (1): The first objection is that in it no possession is taken of the currency, whereas in buying and selling currency possession of the currency is necessary; that is, at the time of the purchase and sale that money is not with this person or with his agent, but is merely entered in his account.

The answer: The answer to this is that when Zayd can buy and sell with the money that has come into his account, this is tantamount to constructive possession, even though sensory possession has not materialized.

The book named al-Maʿāyīr al-Sharʿiyyah, which was compiled by twenty-seven expert muftis and researchers, records:

يتحقق القبض بحصوله حقيقة أو حكماً وتختلف كيفية قبض الأشياء بحسب حالها واختلاف الأعراف وبما يكون قبضاً لها، إذا أودع في حساب العميل مبلغ من المال مباشرة أو بحوالة مصرفية إلى آخر ما قال

(معايير شرعيه، ص ۱۲۲).

From this passage it is learnt that transferring money into someone's account in such a way that he can trade with it is constructive possession.

Sharḥ al-ʿInāyah states:

إن الفائدة المطلوبة بالعقد إنما هي التمكن من التصرف وذلك يترتب على التعيين فلا يحتاج إلى القبض. (شرح العناية: على هامش فتح القدير: ۷/۱۸، دار الفكر).

ʿAṣr-i Ḥāḍir ke Pechīdah Masāʾil records:

An entry in the bank's register will be regarded as carrying the ruling of possession in favour of the person who wishes to convert one currency into another, whether this conversion is sought in the currency which that person is handing over to the bank or in the currency deposited in the bank. (ʿAṣr-i Ḥāḍir ke Pechīdah Masāʾil kā Sharʿī Ḥal, compiled by Qāḍī Mujāhid al-Islām Qāsimī (may Allah have mercy on him), p. 232).

Objection (2): The second objection is that the money taken from the company is a debt owed by Zayd, since this money is merely written into Zayd's account, and the pounds he bought are also merely entered in his account — that is, they are a debt; so this is impermissible on account of being a sale of debt for debt.

The answer: Once it has been established that entering money in an account is tantamount to constructive possession, and that one can buy and sell with it — that is, complete power of disposal has been obtained — then how is this a sale of debt for debt? Rather, the dollars were already recorded in his account and constructive possession of them had been obtained; now, by the entry of the pounds, they too came into his possession. Hence the form of a sale of debt for debt is not present, and there is no ground for calling it impermissible.

Objection (3): The third objection is that the commission the company collects is apparently on account of the debt the company has advanced, so it is an interest-bearing transaction and therefore impermissible.

The answer: The answer to this is that it is a broker's fee, which is taken from both parties, and taking commission from both parties is permissible; since the company provides purchase and sale and other facilities, a service fee ought to be allowed. This ruling is mentioned in the books of fiqh, namely that taking commission from both parties on a percentage basis is permissible and correct.

Consider what Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

Question: Is it permissible in the sharīʿah to take a broker's fee from both parties or not?

الجواب و منہ الصدق و الصواب: It is permissible, provided that the fee is clearly specified.

قال في الشامية من البزازية: إجارة السمسار والمنادي والحمامي والصكاك ومالا يقدر فيه الوقت ولا العمل تجوز. (رد المحتار: كتاب الإجارة، باب الإجارة الفاسدة، ٦/٤۷، سعيد). (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 7/272).

See further: (رد المحتار: ٤/۵٦۰، سعید ۔ فتاوی محمودیہ: ۲۵/۲۸٦ ۔ نظام الفتاوی: ۱/۲۹۷ ۔ و محمود الفتاوی: ۳/۸۵) ۔

Therefore taking this commission does not fall under interest or gambling; rather it is permissible under the heading of a broker's fee.

(4) The company further lays down the condition that Zayd must himself use the loaned money in some market. This is correct, just as taking a pledge or a guarantor to safeguard a loan is correct; transfer of debt and suretyship in the case of a loan serve to secure the loan.

(5) There is also the objection that the company is the agent of both the seller and the buyer, and the apparent difficulty is that one man cannot be the agent of both the seller and the buyer at one and the same time.

The answer: The answer to this is that with the permission of the principal one person can become the agent of both the seller and the buyer. Consider what ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

الوكيل بالبيع لا يملك الشراء لنفسه لأن الواحد لا يكون بائعاً ومشترياً فيبيعه من غيره ثم يشتريه منه، وإن أمره الموكل أن يبيعه من نفسه وأولاده الصغار أو ممن لا تقبل شهادته فباع منهم جاز. (فتاوى الشامي، باب الوكالة بالبيع والشراء، ۵/۵۲۳، سعيد. وكذا في البحر الرائق: ۷/۱٦۷، فصل في الوكيل بالبيع، كوئته).

(6) If Zayd delays in selling the currency, he has to pay something on a daily basis; this is interest, and abstaining from it is obligatory. If this clause is removed, the transaction will be correct.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
