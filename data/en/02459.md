### TITLE
An animal whose hide has been burnt so that no hair grows on it

### QUESTION
If an animal's hide has been burnt so that hair has not grown back on it, and there is no wound or anything of the sort, and all its limbs are sound and whole, is the sacrifice of such an animal valid or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question the sacrifice of such an animal is valid. (امداد الفتاوی: ۳/۵۹۷) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
