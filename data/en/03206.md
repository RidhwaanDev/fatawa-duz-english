### TITLE
The unbelieving doctrines of Rashad Khalifa and his group

### QUESTION
There is a group in America called "United submitters international". I want to know what its beliefs are. Are the followers of this group Muslims? Is marriage with them permissible or not? بینوا توجروا

### ANSWER
In the case in question, an Egyptian man by the name of Rashad Khalifa (1935–1990) claimed prophethood in America and formed a group whose members did not call themselves Muslims but rather "submitters". These people reject the hadith. They have a mosque in the American city of Arizona, and it was from there that Rashad Khalifa began his work. The following are some of the beliefs of this group:

Rashad Khalifa is called "God's Messenger of the Covenant", sent to correct the Qurʾān and the other revealed scriptures.

After the Prophet ﷺ passed away the Qurʾān was corrupted.

Rashad Khalifa has produced a translation of the Noble Qurʾān which is the purest Qurʾān in 1400 years, one in which no alteration of any kind has taken place.

The Prophet ﷺ wrote the Noble Qurʾān with his own hand.

The hadiths and the sunnahs are all a satanic innovation.

Anyone who dies before the age of forty is destined for Paradise.

It is permissible to eat the fat of swine.

The Ascension was spiritual and not physical.

The number 19 is the pivot of the Qurʾān, and it is this that proves the Qurʾān authentic.

The verses of Sūrat al-Tawbah:

لقد جاء كم رسول من أنفسكم الخ.

and

فإن تولو فقل حسبي الله الخ.

are forgeries. Rashad therefore removed both of them from his own authorised Qurʾān.

Whatever a Muslim does is wrong.

To utter the blessed name of the Prophet ﷺ in the testimony of faith, in the tashahhud, in the call to prayer, at the time of ablution and of the Hajj, or in other acts of worship, is like idol-worship.

The world will come to an end in the year 2280.

Adam (peace be upon him) was in Paradise, and at that time human beings and jinn together rebelled against Allah Most High. They were therefore sent down to this world so that they might be punished for their deeds, because in Paradise they had been on the side of Satan.

In Paradise Satan challenged the sovereignty of Allah Most High, and it was for this reason that disorder broke out in Paradise.

The mission of the Prophet Muḥammad ﷺ in this world was only to deliver the Qurʾān, not to explain it; Rashad Khalifa was sent afterwards to explain it.

The people of Gog and Magog will emerge in the year 2271.

Hadith is a source for history, but it is forbidden to use it for religious instruction.

Allah Most High has made Satan a temporary god over this wide earth.

The Prophet Jesus has died and will not return.

Plural marriage is permissible only when the three conditions laid down by Rashad Khalifa are met.

Allah Most High did not command Ibrāhīm (peace be upon him) to sacrifice his son.

The intercession of the Prophet ﷺ on the Day of Judgement is a fabrication.

As a punishment for theft the thief's hand will not be cut off; it is enough for him to pay the value of the stolen article.

A murderer will not be put to death in retaliation; paying blood money is enough.

An adulterer will not be stoned; he will merely be given 100 lashes.

Ḥaḍrat ʿAlī (may Allah be pleased with him) was fighting a war against the one who corrupted the Qurʾān, and this is one of the causes that led to the martyrdom of Ḥaḍrat Ḥusayn (may Allah be pleased with him).

There are two lower Paradises and two higher Paradises as well — one for the believers and one for the jinn.

Hell does not exist at present; it will be brought into being on the Day of Judgement.

Where Allah, Glorified and Exalted, has used the plural form in the Qurʾān, He means Allah Most High, the angels, the Prophet Muḥammad, and Rashad Khalifa.

Besides Paradise and Hell there is also a place which is neither Paradise nor Hell.

According to the Qurʾān there are three prophets in the religion of Islam: Ibrāhīm (peace be upon him), Muḥammad ﷺ, and Rashad Khalifa.

Rashad Khalifa went on the Ascension during the days of the Hajj in 1971 and met all the prophets.

Whoever does not accept the prophethood of Rashad Khalifa is an unbeliever.

It is permissible to take interest, provided one does not take a great deal.

It is not necessary for a woman to observe the veil.

(Taken from the internet website: islamicreed.wordpress.com.)

The ruling on Rashad Khalifa's group:

If these are indeed the beliefs of this group, then Rashad Khalifa and those who follow him have no connection whatever with Islam; those who hold such beliefs are outside Islam. The hadiths have pointed to such people. Thus it is narrated in Musnad Aḥmad:

عن ثوبان رضي الله عنه قال: قال رسول الله ﷺ:

وأنه سيكون في أمتي كذابون ثلاثون كلهم يزعم أنه نبي وأناْ خاتم النبيين لا نبي بعدي. (رواه أحمد في مسنده: ۵/ ۲۷۸).
قال ابن كثير رحمه الله: وقد أخبر الله تبارك وتعالى في كتابه ورسوله ﷺ في السنة المتواترة عنه أنه لا نبي بعدي، ليعلموا أن كل من ادعي هذا المقام فهو كذاب وأفاك، دجال، ضال، مضل. (تفسير ابن كثير: ۳/۵٤۳).
ومن ادعي النبوة، أو صدق من ادعاه، فقد ارتد؛ لأن مسيلمة لما ادعي النبوة فصدقه قومه صاروا بذلك مرتدين. وكذلك طليحة الأسدي ومصدقوه، وقال النبي ﷺ: لا تقوم الساعة حتى يخرج ثلاثون كذابون كلهم يزعم أنه رسول الله. (المغني لابن قدامة: ۱۰/۱۰۳، كتاب المرتد، فصل: حكم من ادعي النبوة، ط: دار الفكر بيروت).
ودعوي النبوة بعد نبينا صلي الله عليه وسلم كفر بالإجماع. (شرح الفقه الأكبر، ص ۱٦٤، ط: قديمي كتب خانه).
والأصل أن من اعتقد الحرام حلالاً فإن كان حراماً لغيره كمال الغير لا يكفر وإن كان لعينه فإن كان دليله قطعياً كفر، وإلا فلا. (رد المحتار: ٤/۲۲۳، سعيد).
ولا يجوز للمرتد أن يتزوج مرتدة ولا مسلمة ولا كافرة أصلية وكذلك لا يجوز نكاح المرتدة مع أحد. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۲۸۲).

The decision of المجمع الفقه الإسلامي — the fourth decision, on the unbelief of Rashad Khalifa:

At the session, consideration was given to the file concerning Rashad Khalifa, the imam of the Tucson mosque in America. It contains his address to the Secretary-General of the Muslim World League, together with an account of his other claims, his activities and his publications. After investigation it was established before the session that the person named Rashad has made entirely false claims, among which are the following:

First: denial of certain verses of the Noble Qurʾān.

Second: denial of the noble sunnah of the Prophet.

Third: the claim that the prayer of the Muslims is the prayer of idolaters.

Fourth: the claim to messengership.

Each one of the above claims, taken by itself, puts a person outside Islam and renders him an unbeliever, for all these matters are fundamentals of Islam. Al-Majmaʿ therefore rules that, on the basis of what Rashad Khalifa has claimed, he has become an apostate; he is outside Islam and is an unbeliever. Muslims should be on their guard against him and beware of his foulness, and should give him no cooperation of any kind. Prayer behind this man is invalid. The false claims of this apostate are a continuation of the very claims of other apostates such as the Qadianis and the Bahais, on whose refutation and rejection all Muslims are agreed; they have no connection with Islam, and the detailed replies that Muslim scholars have written in refutation of them are themselves sufficient to refute the false claims of this criminal apostate. Allah Most High says: "

يريدون أن يطفئوا نور الله بأفواههم ويأبي الله إلا أن يتم نوره ولو كره الكافرون" [التوبة: ۳۲]

This decision bears the signatures of the president and the vice-president of the council of al-Majmaʿ al-Fiqhī and of 15 members.
