### TITLE
The correct measure of the mudd according to the Ḥanafīs, and an examination of some mudds in current use

### QUESTION
How many grams is a mudd according to the Ḥanafīs? And is there any difference in the mudd on account of the difference between the Ḥijāzī ṣāʿ and the Iraqi ṣāʿ, or not? Nowadays a certain mudd is well known and some scholars in England are publicising it a good deal; a chain of transmission for it is also cited. Does it correspond to the Ḥanafī measure or not? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the Ḥanafī school the mudd is 796.68 grams, and there will be a difference in the mudd on account of the difference between the Ḥijāzī ṣāʿ and the Iraqi ṣāʿ. The Ḥijāzī ṣāʿ is five and one-third raṭls and the Iraqi ṣāʿ is eight raṭls, so in this respect there is a difference between the two. According to the research of ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him), however, there is no real difference, because among the people of Ḥijāz the raṭl is thirty istārs while among the people of Iraq it is twenty; so in terms of the result they are equal, though in practice there is a difference between the two, and to outward appearance this is a genuine difference over the ṣāʿ. Note what ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

في الزيلعي والفتح: اختلف في الصاع فقال الطرفان ثمانية أرطال بالعراقي وقال الثاني: خمسة أرطال وثلث، قيل: لا خلاف لأن الثاني قدره برطل المدينة لأنه ثلاثون استاراً والعراقي عشرون وإذا قابلت ثمانية بالعراقي بخمسة وثلث بالمديني وجدتهما سواء وهذا هو الأشبه

(فتاوى الشامي: ۲/۳٦۵، سعيد).

Qāmūs al-Fiqh records:

Regarding the measure of the ṣāʿ it is agreed that one ṣāʿ is four mudds; but over the measure of the mudd itself there is a difference between the jurists of Iraq and the jurists of Ḥijāz. According to the jurists of Ḥijāz it is five and a third raṭls, and according to the jurists of Iraq it is eight raṭls. But according to the research of ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) this is no real difference, because among the people of Ḥijāz the raṭl is thirty istārs and the Iraqi raṭl is twenty istārs, so the two are equal and no real difference is found in their measure. In present-day weights the ṣāʿ equals 3.18 kilograms. (Qāmūs al-Fiqh: 4/216).

Mawlānā Ẓafar Aḥmad ʿUthmānī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

Mawlānā Muḥammad Yaʿqūb (may Allah have mercy on him) had a mudd whose chain of transmission runs unbroken back to Zayd ibn Thābit (may Allah be pleased with him), who made that mudd by measuring it against the mudd of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him). Mawlānā Thānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) filled this mudd twice and weighed it, and by his own seer it came to one and three-quarter seers. (Imdād al-Aḥkām: 2/43, "Research on the weight of the ṣāʿ").

Muftī Rashīd Aḥmad says in Aḥsan al-Fatāwā:

This humble one learnt that Pīr Wahb Allāh Shāh of Pīr Jhando, district Hyderabad, possesses a mudd which his grandfather Mawlānā Rushd Allāh Shāh brought from Madīnah Ṭayyibah. Mawlānā Rushd Allāh Shāh was a good scholar, and he used to discharge the ṣadaqat al-fiṭr with this very mudd, along with which he would also give some cash. I sent for the mudd and examined it; the following text was inscribed on it:

صنع هذا المد المشرف إبراهيم الغزنوي المدني الحنفي على مد الشيخ أحمد بن الشيخ عبد القادر الطاهر المدني الشافعي وهو صنع على مد مولانا أحمد بن مولانا على الإدريسي المالكي، وهو صنعه على مد أمير المؤمنين راجياً به البركة في بيته والمتابعة للنبي ﷺ لأنه كال طعامه به وكان يتوضأ بمقداره ماء، في بعض أزمانه وتطهر بصاع وهو أربعة إمداد وبه تخرج صدقة الفطر عن كل رأس مدان من بر ودقيقة أو سويقة أو زبيب أو أربعة إمداد من تمر أو شعير وكان ذلك ثلاث عشرة وثلاث مأة وألف.

I filled it with wheat, black mash lentils and red lentils and weighed it, and the following results emerged:

Without heaping / With heaping

Wheat: 50 tolas / 55 tolas

Black mash: 52½ tolas / 56½ tolas

Red lentils: 53½ tolas / 57½ tolas

The depth of this vessel is considerably greater in proportion to its width; it is also broad at the bottom and narrow at the mouth, resembling a cone, so there is no great difference between its weight when heaped and when not heaped. In any case, the weight mentioned is far less than the weight determined by the researching scholars generally. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 4/389, "Basṭ al-Bāʿ li-Taḥqīq al-Ṣāʿ").

At the end of Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ's treatise "Awzān-e Sharʿiyyah" it is recorded:

Mudd or man: 13.65 chhaṭānk; 796.68 grams. (Awzān-e Sharʿiyyah, p. 62, Idārat al-Maʿārif, Karachi).

In the introduction to Majmaʿ al-Baḥrayn, Ilyās Qablān has written:

المد عند الحنفية: ۵ ء ۸۱۳ جم، وعند الجمهور: ۵۱۰ جم. (مقدمة مجمع البحرين، ص ٤۲، بيروت).

See further: (Fatāwā Rashīdiyyah, p. 432; ʿAzīz al-Fatāwā: 361, "Research on the weight of the ṣāʿ"; Imdād al-Muftiyīn: 382, "Research on the weight of the ṣāʿ"; and Kifāyat al-Muftī: 6/285, "Research on the ṣāʿ", Karachi).

Nowadays three kinds of mudd are well known and current among people:

(1) A mudd made of plastic:

On this mudd a chain of transmission is inscribed:

سند مد النبي ﷺ: سيدنا محمد رسول الله ﷺ، زيد بن ثابت رضي الله عنه، أبو إسحق، وأبو جعفر، أبو بكر أحمد خالد بن إسماعيل، أبو جعفر أحمد، أبو جعفر أحمد علي، أبو منصور إبراهيم النجاشي، حسين اليشكري، مولانا يعقوب، أبو الحسن عبد الحق، أبو الحسن الصادق، محمد الحياة، شاه رفيع الدين، شاه محمد إسحق، عبد القيوم، محمد أحمد، عبد الحي كفليتوي، يوسف متالا.

Filled with wheat: 615 grams.

Filled with sugar: 705 grams heaped, 686 grams unheaped.

Filled with water: 750 millilitres.

(Note): The weight of this mudd is far less than the weight of the ordinary Ḥanafī mudd.

Regarding this mudd, Al-Jawāhir al-Zawāhir states: it is a measure equal to one quarter of a ṣāʿ, which was equal to the mudd of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him), and it was sent along with its chain of transmission. (Al-Jawāhir al-Zawāhir, p. 10).

(2) A mudd made of brass:

On this mudd there is a mention of Zayd ibn Thābit (may Allah be pleased with him), but no complete chain of transmission is inscribed.

Filled with ʿajwah dates: 585 grams.

Filled with wheat: 720 grams.

Filled with sugar: 797 grams heaped, and 775 grams unheaped.

Filled with water: 850 millilitres.

This brass mudd is closer to the Ḥanafī mudd.

(3) A mudd made of iron: nothing at all is inscribed on this mudd; it is completely plain.

Filled with sugar: 715 grams.

Filled with wheat: 660 grams.

Filled with water: 800 millilitres.

In sum, the mudd made of brass is closer to the Ḥanafī mudd.
