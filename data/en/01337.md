### TITLE
Scattering dates at the marriage ceremony

### QUESTION
What is the ruling on scattering dates on the occasion of the marriage ceremony? Is it established from any narration or not?

### ANSWER
The jurists have permitted distributing dates on the occasion of the marriage ceremony, and scattering them as well. However, if the marriage takes place in a masjid, it is better to distribute them, since that shows greater regard for the respect due to the masjid. Narrations have also been reported in this connection, but Imām al-Bayhaqī (may Allah have mercy on him) said that all these narrations are weak.

Consider what al-Sunan al-Kubrā states:

قال الإمام البيهقي رحمه الله وقد روي في الرخصة فيه أحاديث كلها ضعيفة. (السنن الكبرى: ۷/۲۸۷).

For details and verification of the narrations, see: Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā, vol. 1, p. 406, Abwāb al-Ḥadīth.

Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

لا بأس بنثر السكر والدراهم في الضيافة وعقد النكاح كذا في السراجية. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳٤۵، الباب الثالث عشر في النهبة ونثر الدراهم والسكر).

Kitāb al-Fatāwā states:

If a marriage takes place inside a masjid, it is better to distribute the dates than to scatter them, since that shows greater regard for the respect due to the masjid. (کتاب الفتاوی: ٤/٤٤۵)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
