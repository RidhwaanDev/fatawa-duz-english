### TITLE
The sharia ruling on eating the hoopoe

### QUESTION
Is it permissible in the sharia to eat the hoopoe or not, given that the hoopoe performed a very great service in the time of Sulaymān (peace be upon him), which Allah Most High has mentioned in the Noble Qur'an? بینوا توجروا

### ANSWER
Some of the jurists — ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him), for instance — have declared it disliked, but ʿAllāmah al-Ṭaḥṭāwī (may Allah have mercy on him) has written that it is lawful; and in Lisān al-Ḥukkām, al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah, al-Fatāwā al-Walwālijiyyah and elsewhere too it is recorded that there is no harm in it. Lawfulness is therefore the basic position, and the dislikedness may be taken to be of the mild kind, on account of the bird's nobility and honour.

See what ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

قال في غرر الأفكار: عندنا يؤكل الخطاف والبوم ويكره الصرد والهدهد. (رد المحتار: ٦/۳۰٦، سعيد).

Ḥāshiyat al-Ṭaḥṭāwī ʿalā al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

ويؤكل القمري والسوادين والزرزور والصلصل والهدهد والبوم والطاووس. (حاشية الطحطاوي على الدر المختار: ٤/۱۵۷، كوئته).

Lisān al-Ḥukkām states:

وفي فتاوى الولوالجي: أكل الهدهد لا بأس به لأنه ليس بذي مخلب من الطيور. (لسان الحكام، ص ۳۸۱، دار الفكر).

Al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah states:

أكل الخطاف والصلصل والهدهد لا بأس به لانها ليست من الطيور التي هي ذوات مخلب كذا في الظهيرية. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۲۹۰).

Mullā ʿAlī al-Qārī (may Allah have mercy on him) declared it unlawful, arguing from the following hadith:

نهي عن قتل أربع من الدواب النملة والنحلة والهدهد والصرد. (المرقاة: ۸/).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

The passages of Ḥāshiyat al-Ṭaḥṭāwī and al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah are explicit as to the lawfulness of the hoopoe, and so that view will be given preference. Mullā ʿAlī al-Qārī's (may Allah have mercy on him) argument from the hadith is open to question, because the reason for the prohibition of killing the hoopoe may also be the honour due to it; and in the presence of the texts of the jurists this is also what analogy favours. The reason for the honour due to it is that Allah Most High chose it for the service of Sulaymān (peace be upon him), and it became the means of a whole people coming to faith — so it enjoys a distinguished standing among all the birds.

Mullā ʿAlī al-Qārī (may Allah have mercy on him) has also mentioned the opinion of lawfulness;

ونصه: وقيل يحل أكله لأنه يحكي عن الشافعي وجوب الفدية فيه وعنده لا يفتدي إلا المأكول. (المرقاة: ۸/۱۵۱).

The dislikedness that ʿAllāmah Ibn ʿĀbidīn (may Allah have mercy on him) has transmitted from Ghurar al-Afkār may also be taken, on account of this same honour, to be dislikedness of the mild kind. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 7/400).

In sum, eating the hoopoe is disliked; but one should not reproach a person who eats it, and one's own abstaining from it should be out of respect — for the use of the word "killing" in the hadith, and the mention of the bird among things that are not eaten, is evidence of its being disliked.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
