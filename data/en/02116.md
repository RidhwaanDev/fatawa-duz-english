### TITLE
Taking back loaned land before the agreed term

### QUESTION
Zayd gave ʿAmr a piece of land on loan so that he could build a house on it, telling him that he could use it for twenty years. ʿAmr built a house of unbaked brick. After three years Zayd fell out with ʿAmr and now wants to turn him out, while ʿAmr regards this as a betrayal. If ʿAmr now pulls his house down, can he recover the loss from Zayd or not? بینوا توجروا

### ANSWER
ʿAmr can recover the loss from Zayd. Suppose the building as it stands is worth twenty thousand, and after demolition the rubble is worth ten thousand: the difference between the two is ten thousand, and ʿAmr may recover that ten thousand from Zayd. If, however, the land had been given for sowing a crop and it was asked for back before the appointed time, then the crop would be left standing until it ripened, and Zayd could recover from ʿAmr the rent for the days between the demand and the ripening. So it is stated in the books of fiqh.
