### TITLE
Zakat on game animals

### QUESTION
A man has bought game animals — for example the eland, that is, a large antelope, and the gemsbok, that is, a large African antelope. The question is whether zakat is due on these animals or not. Moreover, the hunting farm is very large and the number of animals is not even known; the animals are spread out over the whole bush and they run off before we can even reach them, so counting them and finding out their number is very difficult. Likewise only the large animals are hunted, not the young. So on which animals will zakat be due? And how will the value of the young be worked out?

بينوا بالدليل توجروا بالأجر الجزيل.

### ANSWER
Zakat is due on merchandise, and merchandise means something bought with the intention of selling it on. The animals of such a farm are not for sale: they are either for touring and viewing or for hunting, and animals kept for hunting are not called merchandise. Rather, like a butcher's animals, they have been bought in order to be slaughtered, so there is no zakat on them. Yes, whatever price is obtained after the hunt must be added to one's other funds, and when the year passes zakat must be paid on it. Consider what the author of al-Hidāyah says:

وإن اشترى شيئاً ونواه للتجارة كان للتجارة لاتصال النية بالعمل بخلاف ما إذا ورث ونوى التجارة، لأنه لا عمل منه ولو ملكه بالهبة أو بالوصية أو النكاح أو الخلع أو الصلح عن القود ونواه للتجارة كان للتجارة عند أبي يوسف رحمه الله لاقترانها بالعمل وعند محمد لا يصير للتجارة لأنها لم تقارن عمل التجارة وقيل الاختلاف على عكسه. (الهداية: ۱/۱۸۷، كتاب الزكاة، ط: المصباح).

Bihishtī Zēwar states:

There is no zakat due on wild animals such as antelope and the like. Yes, if they are bought and kept with the intention of trade, then the zakat of trade goods will be due on them. (بہشتی زیور، گیارہواں حصہ، ص ۱۱۱، ط: المکتبۃ المدنیۃ) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
