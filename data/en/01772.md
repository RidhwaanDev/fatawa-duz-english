### TITLE
Compensation in the case of a car collision or overturning

### QUESTION
If a driver was driving a car or a bus and some part of it broke, or the car or bus overturned, or it collided with another car or bus, and some of the people sitting inside were injured or killed while the driver survived — will compensation or blood money be due or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, if the driver deliberately did something excessive — for example he took the wrong road and the vehicle overturned, or he was driving far too fast, say at a speed of more than 200, or he was driving with complete carelessness — then in all these cases the driver is responsible and compensation falls upon him. But if that was not so, and the driver was driving with full responsibility when an accident occurred unexpectedly, then the driver is not responsible for it and no compensation of any kind falls upon him. If, however, he struck and killed a person outside the vehicle, then blood money becomes obligatory.

Mawlānā Mujīb Allāh Nadwī Ṣāḥib has set out these questions in detail:

The ruling on inanimate conveyances such as a motorcycle, a train, an aeroplane and the like is that whether the loss of property is caused deliberately or through negligence without intent, the compensation for it is taken not from the owner of the conveyance but from the driver, the conductor or the guard. For example, if a man brought a train into the station without a signal and it collided; or the driver was taking a car or a bus down the wrong side of the road; or he was driving faster than the fixed speed — in these cases the responsibility for the loss of property lies upon him. If, however, some other person places a brick or a stone in front of the vehicle and the driver cannot see it and an accident occurs; or a pedestrian was walking on the wrong side of the road and was struck by a motorcycle; or someone suddenly lies down in front of the vehicle — in such a case no responsibility rests on the driver, the owner or the government. But if a motor vehicle or a train collides by accident, or an aeroplane meets with an accident, and one person or many lose their lives, then the responsibility for that loss of life does not lie upon the driver or the pilot but upon the company or the government. That is why the jurists place responsibility for the blood money in such accidental cases upon the ʿāqilah. In the earliest period of Islam the ahl al-dīwān — the people registered together in one class — were all one another's ʿāqilah; after that the responsibility was placed upon the family, the tribe and the community; but now this situation too has changed, and the responsibility should now rest upon the company, the factory or the government. The jurists have treated such cases as unintentional homicide. The company, the factory owner or the government may punish the driver or the pilot, but cannot place the responsibility for the blood money upon them. In any case these are new questions, and the scholars need to give them further consideration. (اسلامی فقہ، ۲/٦٤۲ تا ٦٤۳) ۔

Majmaʿ al-Ḍamānāt states:

لو اصطدم فارسان حران فماتا فعلى العاقلة كل واحد منهما الدية للأخرى كما في الهداية، قال في الإصلاح والإيضاح وههنا شرط مذكور في الفتاوى الظهيرية وهو أن يقع كل واحد منهما على قفاه

وشرط آخر مذكور في المحيط وهو أن لا يكونا عامدين في ذلك الاصطدام فإنهما لوكانا عامدين فيه ضمن كل نصف الدية للآخر

ولو اصطدم دابتان فعطبت إحداهما ولكل منهما سائق فضمان التي عطبت على الآخر، من قاضيخان. (مجمع الضمانات: ۱/٤۲۳، الفصل الخامس في جناية البهيمة، للعلامة أبي محمد البغدادي، والهداية: ٤/۳٤۸)
