### TITLE
The ruling on a husband giving his wife flowers as a gift

### QUESTION
Can a husband give flowers as a gift to his wife, or children to their parents, or not? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
Husband and wife, or parents, or relatives giving and taking gifts among themselves — provided it is not by way of a customary exchange, and not in accordance with the usage of Europe and on their special days — is not merely permissible; rather it is a means and instrument of love and affection among them. Encouragement to it is found in a noble hadith:

عن أبي هريرة رضي الله عنه عن النبي ﷺ قال: "تهادوا تحابوا" رواه البخاري في "الأدب المفرد" (۵۹٤) وأبو يعلي في مسنده (٦۱٤۸) بإسناد حسن. (بلوغ المرام رقم: ۹۳۵).

Therefore, in the case asked about, giving a gift of flowers is also permissible and correct. But giving a gift of flowers and the like in imitation of Western civilisation — for example on a wedding anniversary or a birthday and so forth, or on particular dates such as 14 February — is not permissible or correct. A hadith states that the Prophet ﷺ said: whoever imitates a people, on the Day of Resurrection he will be raised together with that people.

من تشبه بقوم فهو منهم

(Recorded by Abū Dāwūd: 4/44, published in Beirut).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
