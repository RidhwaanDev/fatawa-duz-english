### TITLE
Taking out insurance with a company

### QUESTION
A removals company transports goods for a fixed fee — say, 50 rand — and in return for an additional fee (say 10 per cent) it offers insurance on the goods being transported. It is possible that this company obtains the insurance from another company. The item being transported is an expensive one, for example a laptop computer or the like. Is it permissible to take out such insurance?

### ANSWER
Since two kinds of corruption are found in insurance — (1) interest and (2) gambling — and both are forbidden, insurance too is impermissible. Therefore, in the case mentioned, it is impermissible for the person having his goods transported by the company to take out insurance, whether the removals company itself provides the insurance or obtains it from another company. That is because in this case the company is, as it were, an agent on behalf of the person having the goods transported, and the actual contract has been concluded between him and the insurance company. Accordingly it is necessary to keep away from such a transaction.

Consider what Aḥkām al-Qurʾān states:

لا خلاف بين أهل العلم في تحريم القمار وأن المخاطرة من القمار قال بن عباس رضي الله عنه: إن المخاطرة قمار وإن أهل الجاهلية كانوا يخاطرون على المال والزوجة وقد كان ذلك مباحاً إلى أن ورد تحريمه. (أحكام القرآن للجصاص: ۱/۳۲۹).

Fatāwā al-Shāmī states:

وسمي القمار قماراً لأن كل واحد من القمارين ممن يجوز أن يذهب ماله إلى صاحبه ويجوز أن يستفيد مال صاحبه وهو حرام بالنص. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/٤۰۳، سعيد).

It also states:

الربا هو فضل خال عن عوض بمعيار شرعي شرط لأحد المتعاقدين في المعاوضة. (فتاوى الشامي: ۵/۱٦۸، سعيد).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

Insurance contains interest, and gambling as well. Both of these things are forbidden, so insurance too is forbidden. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 16/387, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

Medical aid, however, is permitted, because in it one pays in rand and in return one does not receive money but treatment, whereas in the case mentioned above money is received in exchange for money.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
