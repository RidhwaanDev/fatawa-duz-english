### TITLE
The ruling on some of the heirs dealing with an inherited house

### QUESTION
A man passed away leaving only one house in his estate. Before repairs the value of this house was three hundred thousand rand. Two of the sons among the heirs had the house repaired with their own money, and the value of the house has now risen to about nine hundred thousand. When this house is sold and divided among the lawful heirs, may the two sons deduct the money they spent, or not? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question, if the two sons carried out the repairs with the permission of the other heirs, they may deduct the whole of their expenditure, and the remaining sum will then be divided among the heirs. But if they spent the money of their own accord without permission, they cannot deduct it, and the entire value of the house will be divided among the heirs. Consider what Fatāwā Hindiyyah mentions:

لو تصرف أحد الورثة في التركة المشتركة وربح فالربح للمتصرف وحده كذا في الفتاوى الغياثية. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۲/۳٤٦).

Durar al-Ḥukkām, the commentary on Majallat al-Aḥkām, records:

إذا أخذ الورثة مقداراً من النقود من التركة قبل القسمة بدون إذن الآخرين وعمل فيه فخساره يعود عليه، كما أنه لو ربح لا يأخذ الورثة حصة فيه

أما إذا أعمل المال بإذن فإذا أعمل بشرط أن يكون الربح له خاصة فتكون حصة الورثة الآخرين قرضاً وإذا أعمل على أن يكون الربح مشتركاً فتكون المعاملة شركة مضاربة في حصة الورثة

الخ. (درر الحكام شرح مجلة الأحكام: ۳/۵۰، ط: دار الكتب العلمية، بيروت).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

Whoever bought that land, if he bought it for himself, then it is his alone and no one else is a partner in it. If he paid the price from jointly-owned money, then he is obliged to give the partners their money in proportion to their shares.

لو تصرف أحد الورثة في التركة المشتركة وربح، فالربح للمتصرف وحده، كذا في الفتاوى الهندية.

If he bought it for the partnership, then it is jointly owned, and the partners will share in the price too. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 20/310, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

It is recorded in Kifāyat al-Muftī:

The profit ʿAmr made by trading with the jointly-owned property, and the increase he brought about in it, will all be divided among Zayd's heirs; it will not be reckoned the estate of ʿAmr alone. (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 13/450, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).
