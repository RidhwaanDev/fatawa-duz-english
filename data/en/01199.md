### TITLE
Performing ʿumrah from Tanʿīm after the ḥajj

### QUESTION
In Saudi Arabia some people distribute pamphlets in which it is written that one should not perform ʿumrah from Tanʿīm after the ḥajj, and they also say by word of mouth that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and the noble Companions did not perform ʿumrah after the ḥajj — whereas our own people perform ʿumrah after the ḥajj in great numbers and regard it as a source of reward. What is the ruling of the sharia on this?

### ANSWER
In the view of the Ḥanafīs, performing ʿumrah is an act of reward throughout the whole year apart from the days of ḥajj; that is, one should not perform ʿumrah from 9 Dhū al-Ḥijjah until 13 Dhū al-Ḥijjah, while in the rest of the year ʿumrah may be performed at any time.

When Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah (may Allah be pleased with her) had finished the ḥajj, she went to Tanʿīm in order to make up the ʿumrah that was still outstanding from her. Her brother ʿAbd al-Raḥmān was with her, and the two of them performed ʿumrah from Tanʿīm on the night of 14 Dhū al-Ḥijjah (بخاری شریف جلد اول، ص ۲۱۲). The Messenger of Allah ﷺ told ʿAbd al-Raḥmān to take his sister outside the Ḥaram and have her perform ʿumrah. Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah then says:

"حتى فرغت وفرغ أي عبد الرحمن أيضاً "كما في الشرح" من الطواف ثم جئته بسحر فقال: هل فرغتم" (صحيح البخاري: ۱/۲۱۲)،

and in some narrations the wording فرغتما has also come.

It is recorded in Muwaṭṭaʾ Imām Mālik:

كانت عائشة رضي الله تعالى عنها تعتمر بعد الحج من مكة في ذي الحجة ثم تركت بعد ذلك فكانت تخرج قبل هلال المحرم حتى تأتي الجحفة فتقيم بها حتى تري الهلال فإذا رأت الهلال أهلت بعمرة. (الموطأ: ص ۳۸۲).
وفي الأوجز: ولعل ذلك لتحصيل الفصل بين الحج والعمرة امتثالاً لأمر أمير المؤمنين كما سيأتي قريباً في باب العمرة، أفصلوا بين حجكم وعمرتكم فإن ذلك أتم لحج أحدكم ولعمرته أن يعتمر في غير أشهر الحج. (أوجز المسالك: ٦/۵۵۵، دار القلم دمشق).

That is, Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah (may Allah be pleased with her) used to perform ʿumrah after the ḥajj in Dhū al-Ḥijjah; then she gave this up, and would go out to al-Juḥfah before the new moon of Muḥarram, stay there, and enter iḥrām for ʿumrah once the new moon of Muḥarram had appeared.

Awjaz states: this was so that the command of the Commander of the Faithful might be obeyed, for he had said that a separation should be kept between the ḥajj and the ʿumrah; it is better for the completion of both the ḥajj and the ʿumrah that a person perform ʿumrah after the months of ḥajj.

Further, if someone says that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them) did not perform ʿumrah after the ḥajj, and that we too should therefore not perform it, the answer is that the Emigrants were required to return quickly, and that is why they returned quickly and did not perform ʿumrah. How does it follow from this that ʿumrah should not be performed? If someone were to say that all pilgrims must depart on the 14th, because the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them) departed then, would that be acceptable? In the same way it is not acceptable to say that, because the Messenger of Allah ﷺ did not perform ʿumrah in Dhū al-Ḥijjah after the ḥajj, we too should not perform it. They simply did not have the opportunity. Had the Messenger of Allah ﷺ performed ʿumrah, and had most people performed ʿumrah in following him and still departed on the 14th, how much difficulty that would have caused.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
