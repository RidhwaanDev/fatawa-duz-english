### TITLE

The ruling on one heir trading with the estate

### QUESTION

A man passed away and his estate had not been divided. Two of the deceased's sons carried on their father's business and earned a handsome profit. Now, after a period of ten years has passed, they wish to divide the estate. Will all the heirs share in the profit, or will only what was present at the time of the deceased's death be divided? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER

Whatever profit was made from the deceased's property before it was divided, all the heirs will share in the entire wealth. Even though the remaining heirs took no part in trading with the jointly owned property, they will still share in the profit, and the whole of the property together with the profit will be divided among all the heirs in the manner laid down by the Sharīʿah. Yes, it is appropriate if those who did the work are given something over and above their share, but this is not obligatory in the Sharīʿah.
Al-Ashbāh wa'l-Naẓāʾir states:

إذا عمل أحد الشريكين دون الآخر بعذر أو بغيره فالربح بينهما قوله إذا عمل أحد الشريكين

الخ. إنما كان الربح بينهما، لأن استحقاق الربح بحكم الشرط في العقد لا العمل، كما في البزازية في آخر فصل ما يكون للشريك، وقوله بعذر لا يصح تعلقه بالفعل المذكور كما هو ظاهر وليس ثم غيره يصح تعلقه به، وحينئذٍ فالصواب أن يقول كما في البزازية، ويستوي أن يمتنع الآخر بعذر أو بغير عذر، لأن العقد لا يرتفع بمجرد امتناعه. (الأشباه والنظائر مع غمز عيون البصائر: ۲/ ۹۷).

The gist of the passage above is that even if one partner does no work, he will still receive his full share of the jointly owned property, whether that partner had an excuse for not working or not.

وللاستزادة انظر: (المجلة وشرحها لمحمد خالد الأتاسي: المادة: ۱۳۹۲، ۸۳).

It should be borne in mind that the jurists describe this kind of partnership as sharikat al-amlāk (joint ownership).
Tabyīn al-Ḥaqāʾiq states:

شركة الملك أن يملك اثنان عيناً إرثاً أو شراء

وكل أجنبي في قسط صاحبه أي كل واحد منهما أجنبي في نصيب صاحبه حتى لا يجوز له أن يتصرف فيه إلا بإذنه كما لغيره من الأجانب. (تبيين الحقائق شرح الكنز الدقائق: ۳/ ۳۱۳).

Shams al-Aʾimmah al-Sarakhsī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

فشركة الملك أن يشترك رجلان في ملك مال وذلك نوعان ثابت بغير فعلهما كالميراث، وثابت بفعلهما وذلك بقبول الشراء أو الصدقة أو الوصية والحكم واحد وهو أن ما يتولد من الزيادة يكون مشتركاً بينهما بقدر الملك وكل واحد منهما بمنزلة الأجنبي في التصرف في نصيب صاحبه. (المبسوط للسرخسي: ۱۱/ ۱۵۱).

An objection and its answer:
The objection: Some people say that the profit belongs exclusively to those who handled the property and did the work, and that the other heirs will receive nothing out of that profit; and in support they cite the following passage of Al-Fatāwā al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah:

لو تصرف أحد الورثة في التركة المشتركة وربح فالربح للمتصرف وحده، كذا في الفتاوى الغياثية. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۲/ ۳۴٦).
What is the answer to this?
The answer: Since the passage quoted above runs contrary to the passages of the other books of fiqh, its meaning must be that he took some money out of the joint funds and then dealt with that, in which case the profit will be his. Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah also points to this; see: (۲۰/۳۰۹، مع التعلیقات) ۔
Or the answer is that our senior scholars (may Allah have mercy on them) did not give their fatwa on that basis; rather the fatwa of the senior scholars (may Allah have mercy on them) is to the contrary.
Kifāyat al-Muftī states:
Whatever profit ʿAmr has gained by trading and the like with the jointly owned property, and whatever increase he has brought about in it, will also be divided among all the heirs; it will not be regarded as ʿAmr's estate alone.
وعمله وتصرفه يكون تبرعاً ووجهه أنه شريك في بعضه وعامل بنت أخيه في بعضه وهي في عياله، وليس ههنا عقد ولا غصب
۔ (کفایت المفتی: ۸/۲٦۹، کتاب الفرائض) ۔
An answer of the same kind is also given elsewhere. See: (کفایت المفتی: ۸/۲۷٤، کتاب الفرائض) ۔
Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm states:
Answer: Since Zayd and ʿAmr are both purchasers of the property in question, the two are partners in it, each with a half; and after them their heirs stand in their place. The heirs' claim is therefore valid and rightful in the Sharīʿah,
في رد المحتار: قوله ولزمه نصف الثمن بناء على أن مطلق الشركة يقتضي التسوية
۔ (فتاوی دار العلوم دیوبند: ۱/۵۹۷، کتاب الشرکۃ) ۔
For further detail see: (امداد الاحکام: ۳/۳۱۹ ۔ ۳۲۲) ۔
If we were to say that an heir's dealing with the property is like dealing with the property of an outsider and that this is impermissible, the answer to that too would be the same: whatever profit is earned by dealing with an outsider's property must, in the view of the jurists, be given away in charity, and when the owner is known it must be handed over to the owner. Here the heirs are known, so the profit too will be divided along with the original property according to the shares laid down by the Sharīʿah.
Radd al-Muḥtār states:

وفي القهستاني: وله أن يؤديه إلى المالك ويحل له التناول لزوال الخبث. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/ ۱۸۹، كتاب الغصب، سعيد).

Imdād al-Aḥkām states:
Question: Before the inheritance money had been divided, one of the co-heirs gave it out to someone on a muḍārabah (profit-sharing) basis without informing the other co-heir, and the muḍārib dealt with it — he bought goods and sold them, and a profit was made. Is this muḍārabah valid or not, and is the muḍārib entitled to take the agreed half of the profit from it or not?
The answer: The agreed profit has indeed become the property of the co-heir who gave the money out on a muḍārabah basis, but only so much of it as corresponds to his own share of the inheritance is lawful for him; the rest of the profit is tainted. He should therefore give the other heirs their shares in due proportion. (امداد الاحکام: ۳/۳۱۹، کتاب الشرکۃ و المضاربۃ) ۔
واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
