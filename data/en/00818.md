### TITLE
When a doctor may break an obligatory prayer out of necessity

### QUESTION
If a doctor is on call and is performing an obligatory or necessary prayer at that moment, may he break that prayer or not?

Being "on call" means that the doctor completes his usual eight hours a day at the hospital, leaves a nurse with his patients and goes home. The nurse looks after the patients and monitors them according to the doctor's instructions, but if she feels a patient's condition is serious she telephones the doctor and calls him in at once. Sometimes when he is on call there is no deputy available. If such a call comes during an obligatory prayer or during the Friday sermon, should he continue the prayer or break it? And if he breaks the prayer, does he have to make it up?

### ANSWER
When a patient's condition becomes very serious, with a risk that the illness will worsen or that his life will be lost, and no deputy is present who can do the work properly, it is necessary to break the prayer and go, whether the prayer is obligatory or optional. If, however, the prayer is close to finishing, or will finish within a few minutes, he should complete it and then go, since a few minutes usually make no difference.

He should then perform the prayer he broke afterwards if its time is still there; otherwise he should make it up later.

If a competent deputy is present, he should complete the prayer and then go.

If such a need arises while he is listening to the sermon, there is room for him to leave the sermon, and nothing has to be made up for it.

If the need to break the prayer and go arises during the tarāwīḥ prayer, he should afterwards make up the two units.

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

ويجب القطع لنحو إنجاء غريق أو حريق. وفي الشامية: والحاصل أن المصلي متي سمع أحداً يستغيث وإن لم يقصده بالنداء، أو كان أجنبياً، وإن لم يعلم ما حل به أو علم، كان قدرة على إغاثته وتخليصه، وجب عليه إغاثته وقطع الصلاة فرضاً كانت أو غيره قطع الصلاة

والواجب لا حياء نفس. (الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ۲/٥۱، ٥۲، سعيد).

Al-Fatāwā al-Sirājiyyah states:

إذا خاف المصلي أن يسقط أعمي أو صبي من سطح، أو تحرقه نار، أو يغرقه ماء، عليه قطع الصلاة، ولو سرق منه ما يساوي درهماً له قطع الصلاة. (الفتاوى السراجية، ص: ۱۰٥).
وللاستزادة انظر: (الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ۱/٦٥٤، سعيد، والفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱۰۹، والبحر الرائق: ۲/۷۱، كوئته).
