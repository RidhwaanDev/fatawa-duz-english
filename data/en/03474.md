### TITLE
The arrangement of Qurʾān recitation at Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband and Saharanpur upon the passing of a senior figure

### QUESTION
Was it the practice from earlier times at Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband and Dār al-ʿUlūm Saharanpur to gather the students and arrange for the conveying of reward upon the passing of a senior figure, or not?

### ANSWER
At Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband and Dār al-ʿUlūm Saharanpur the custom of condolence gatherings and tributes for eminent scholars is generally established; moreover, such incidents are also found in the record in which Qurʾān recitation and the conveying of reward were arranged. Consider the following few incidents:

1. Mawlānā Muḥammad Aḥmad Thānawī (may Allah have mercy on him), who belonged to the town of Thānah Bhawan in the district of Muzaffarnagar, was only a year old when his father passed away. Once he came of understanding, he was enrolled in the Madrasah Ashrafiyyah of the Khānqāh Imdādiyyah in the service of Ḥakīm al-Ummat Mawlānā Ashraf ʿAlī Thānawī (may Allah have mercy on him). It is recorded in his biography that when he passed away, the religious madrasahs and religious associations arranged Qurʾān recitation and the conveying of reward. (Akābir-e Deoband, p. 317).

2. Ḥāfiẓ Muḥammad Aḥmad (may Allah have mercy on him), who was the worthy son of Mawlānā Nānautwī (may Allah have mercy on him), rendered service at the Dār al-ʿUlūm for forty-five years, of which he discharged the duties of rector for thirty-five years. It is mentioned in his biography that his passing was regarded as a tremendous loss for the Dār al-ʿUlūm and for the Muslims, and that countless gatherings for the conveying of reward were held throughout the length and breadth of India, among both the Deobandī and the non-Deobandī schools of thought. (Mukammal Tārīkh-e Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband: 2/232).

3. Concerning the passing of Ḥaḍrat Shaykh al-Ḥadīth ʿAllāmah Muḥammad Anwar Shāh Kashmīrī (may Allah have mercy on him) it is written that the Jāmiʿah of Dabhel remained closed for three days, and that morning and evening all the students and teachers gathered in the Dār al-Ḥadīth and the other lecture halls and arranged Qurʾān recitation, the completion of the kalimah ṭayyibah and the conveying of reward. (Mukammal Tārīkh-e Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband, p. 37).

4. Upon the passing of Ḥājī Muḥammad Ismāʿīl Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him), a completion of the noble Qurʾān was recited at the Dār al-ʿUlūm for the conveying of reward, and the honourable teachers offered their condolences. (Monthly Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband, p. 47, March 1967).

5. Upon the passing of the honoured wife of Ḥaḍrat ʿAllāmah Anwar Shāh Kashmīrī (may Allah have mercy on him), the kalimah ṭayyibah and the noble Qurʾān were recited at the Dār al-ʿUlūm for the conveying of reward to the deceased, and the madrasah was closed for the day. (Monthly Dār al-ʿUlūm, p. 48, August 1967).

6. Al-Ḥājj Mawlānā Muḥammad ibn Mūsā Miyāṅ Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him), the deceased, had a special connection not only with the Dār al-ʿUlūm but with all the religious and national institutions of India and Pakistan, and many religious works received assistance from his open-handedness and generosity. Early this morning a completion of the noble Qurʾān was recited within the precincts of the Dār al-ʿUlūm for the conveying of reward to the departed Mawlānā, and all the teachers and students made supplication for his forgiveness. (Monthly Dār al-ʿUlūm, p. 48, June 1963).

7. The daughter of Ḥaḍrat Shaykh al-Hind Mawlānā Maḥmūd al-Ḥasan Ṣāḥib (may his secret be sanctified), who was about eighty years of age, answered the call of death at the end of July. Her funeral prayer was performed within the precincts of the Dār al-ʿUlūm and she was committed to the earth amid grief and sorrow. The next day a completion of the Qurʾān and the conveying of reward were arranged. (Monthly Dār al-ʿUlūm, p. 47, September 1966).

8. The late Mawlānā Muḥammad Naʿīm Ṣāḥib, full brother of Mawlānā Muḥammad Salīm Ṣāḥib, the chief rector of Dār al-ʿUlūm al-Ḥaram, Madrasah Ṣawlatiyyah, Makkah al-Mukarramah, answered the call of death on the 2nd of Shawwāl al-Mukarram from a heart complaint. My request is that arrangements be made in scholarly circles and madrasahs for a completion of the Noble Qurʾān and the conveying of reward for the departed Mawlānā, and that Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Muḥammad Salīm Ṣāḥib be informed of this by letters of condolence at the address given below. (Monthly Dār al-ʿUlūm, pp. 47, 48, May 1963).

9. The honourable Ḥaḍrat Ḥāfiẓ Allāh Diyye Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him), the departed and forgiven, a disciple of the divinely-guided scholar Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Rashīd Aḥmad Ṣāḥib Gangohī (may Allah fill his resting place with light), passed away at about ninety years of age after an illness of thirteen or fourteen days from typhoid fever. For the departed, a completion of the Qurʾān and the conveying of reward were arranged at the Dār al-ʿUlūm. (Monthly Dār al-ʿUlūm, 48, November 1976).

10. As soon as news arrived of the tragic passing of the leader of the Jamāʿat-e Tablīgh, Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Inʿām al-Ḥasan Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him), an atmosphere of grief and sorrow spread over the Dār al-ʿUlūm, and immediately arrangements were made for the conveying of reward, in which all the students, teachers and staff took part and supplication was made. The Dār al-ʿUlūm was closed for one day, and the students, teachers and staff were instructed to maintain the continuity of the conveying of reward. (Monthly Dār al-ʿUlūm, p. 56, July 1995).

11. Upon the passing of Quṭb al-ʿĀlam Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā ʿAbd al-Qādir Ṣāḥib Rāʾipūrī (may Allah have mercy on him), reward was conveyed at the Dār al-ʿUlūm by means of a completion of the Noble Qurʾān and a completion of the kalimah ṭayyibah, in which scholars, students and members of the Dār al-ʿUlūm took part in large numbers — in particular the head of the teaching staff Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ibrāhīm Ṣāḥib, Mawlānā Mubārak ʿAlī Ṣāḥib the deputy rector, Mawlānā Sayyid Fakhr al-Ḥasan Ṣāḥib and others. After the completion, a gathering was held in the lower hall of the Dār al-Ḥadīth. (Monthly Dār al-ʿUlūm, 48, October 1962).

12. Upon the tragic passing of Mujāhid-e Millat Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ḥifẓ al-Raḥmān Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him), a recitation of the Noble Qurʾān and a completion of the kalimah ṭayyibah were begun at the Dār al-ʿUlūm; the lecture halls and offices of the Dār al-ʿUlūm were closed and everyone at the Dār al-ʿUlūm came and took part in the completion. Immediately after finishing the completion, all the teachers, students and office staff gathered in the lower hall of the Dār al-Ḥadīth; and as soon as the honourable rector (may his shade endure), Ḥaḍrat ʿAllāmah Mawlānā Muḥammad Ibrāhīm Ṣāḥib (may his shade endure), the head of the teaching staff, and Ḥaḍrat Shaykh al-Ḥadīth Mawlānā Fakhr al-Dīn Ṣāḥib (may his shade endure) came onto the stage, the assembly became all ears. (Monthly Dār al-ʿUlūm, 48, September 1962).

13. Upon the passing of Ḥaḍrat Muftī Kifāyat Allāh Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) the atmosphere of the Dār al-ʿUlūm became sorrowful and grief-stricken, and a general holiday was announced at the Dār al-ʿUlūm. All the teachers, students and employees of the Dār al-ʿUlūm recited a completion of the noble Qurʾān until the evening and conveyed the reward to the Muftī Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him). Then, in connection with condolences for the late Muftī Ṣāḥib, a gathering was held at the Dār al-ʿUlūm over which the honourable rector (may his shade endure) presided; besides those associated with the Dār al-ʿUlūm, the Muslims of the town also took part in this gathering. (Monthly Dār al-ʿUlūm, p. 4, Jumādā al-Ūlā 1372 AH).

14. Addressing a condolence sitting upon the martyrdom of Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Samīʿ al-Ḥaqq Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him), the head of the Jāmiʿah, Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Muftī Muḥammad Rafīʿ Ṣāḥib (may his shade endure), said: "Today we suspended the lessons in our Jāmiʿah Dār al-ʿUlūm Karachi and made supplications for him and had completions performed: a completion of the Qurʾān was done, a khatm-e khwājagān was done, a khatm of the āyat-e karīmah was done; and above all, five completions of Bukhārī Sharīf were done, because supplications are greatly accepted after the completion of Bukhārī as well, and this has been the practice of the elders. All praise be to Allah, abundant supplications were made for him." (Monthly al-Balāgh, p. 16, Rabīʿ al-Awwal 1440 AH / December 2018 CE).

Besides these, condolence gatherings were held for the following few great personalities, mention of which is made in Mukammal Tārīkh-e Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband, Naqsh-e Ḥayāt, Asīrān-e Malta, Sīrat-e Ashraf and elsewhere:

1. The founder of Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband, Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Qāsim Ṣāḥib Nānautwī (may Allah have mercy on him).

2. The rector of Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband, Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Qārī Muḥammad Ṭayyib Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him).

3. The muftī of Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband, Ḥaḍrat Muftī Maḥmūd Ḥasan Ṣāḥib Gangohī (may Allah have mercy on him).

4. Shaykh al-Islām Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ḥusayn Aḥmad Madanī (may Allah have mercy on him), and other personalities.
