### TITLE
Zakat on shares held as trading capital

### QUESTION
Are shares that are held as trading capital subject to zakat or not?

### ANSWER
Shares that are held as trading capital are subject to zakat.

Consider what Jadīd Fiqhī Mabāḥith states:

In the light of the rulings and principles of fiqh, zakat is due on the value of the capital and the profit as they stand at the time the zakat is paid, since in trade goods zakat is due on both the capital and the profit. The market price is not taken into account here. If, however, a person's business is precisely the buying and selling of shares, then zakat is due according to the market price of those shares at the time the zakat is paid. (Jadīd Fiqhī Mabāḥith: 6/251, Idārat al-Qurʾān wa al-ʿUlūm al-Islāmiyyah).

Īḍāḥ al-Masāʾil states:

If a person has taken a stake in a company by buying holdings and shares, zakat is due on both his capital and his profit. Imdād al-Fatāwā 2/21. (Īḍāḥ al-Masāʾil: 106, Nuʿaymiyyah).

Kitāb al-Fatāwā states:

Since shares represent trade goods, and zakat is due on trade goods, zakat is due on holdings according to their market value. (Kitāb al-Fatāwā: part three, p. 268, Nuʿaymiyyah).

See also: Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 9/420, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah; Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 2/14; and Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband: 6/140.
