### TITLE
Iḥrām when one is heading for Madinah at the time of passing the mīqāt

### QUESTION
A man wished to go for Hajj. The agent told him that they were to go to Madinah first, so he did not put on iḥrām and set off for Madinah. After reaching Jeddah, the agent told him that they were going to Mecca first. What should the man do now? Where should he put on iḥrām? And will a dam be obligatory or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, at the time of passing the mīqāt his destination was not the Sacred Precinct but Madinah, so putting on iḥrām was not required of him and no dam is due. But now that his plan has changed in Jeddah and he is to go to Mecca, he should put on iḥrām in Jeddah itself, and then no penalty will be due.

Sharḥ Lubāb al-Manāsik states:

ومن جاوز وقته أي الذي وصل إليه حال كونه يقصد مكاناً في الحل كبستان بني عامر أو جدة مثلاً بحيث لم يمر علي الحرم وليس له عند المجاوزة قصد أن يدخل الحرم بعد دخول ذلك المكان ثم بدا له أي ظهور أي حارث أن يدخل مكة أي أو الحرم ولم يرد نسكاً حينئذٍ فله أن يدخلها أي مكة، وكذا الحرم بغير إحرام. (شرح اللباب: ص۹٦، بيروت. وغنية الناسك: ۳۲).

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

Those pilgrims who wish to go to Madinah first should not put on iḥrām, because their journey from the mīqāt is not towards the Sacred Precinct, and iḥrām is required only of those who are travelling from the mīqāt towards the Sacred Precinct. (جدید فقہی مسائل: ۱/۲۳۹، نعیمیہ) ۔

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh states:

If his intention is not Hajj or Umrah but to go to Bustān Banī ʿĀmir or some such place for some need of his, then nothing is due from him. If afterwards some matter arises because of which he has to go to Mecca or to some other place within the Sacred Precinct, and he then forms the intention of Hajj or Umrah, his mīqāt is the whole land of the Ḥill. (عمدۃ الفقہ: حصۂ چہارم: ۱۰٤، مجددیہ) ۔

Imdād al-Fatāwā states:

Question: A Yemeni set out with the intention of going to Madinah and did not put on iḥrām when passing the mīqāt of Yalamlam. When he reached Jeddah he then decided to go to Mecca and put on iḥrām at Jeddah. Will a dam now be obligatory or not?

Answer: His iḥrām from Jeddah was valid, and no penalty falls due upon him. (امداد الفتاوی: ۲/۱٦۲) ۔
