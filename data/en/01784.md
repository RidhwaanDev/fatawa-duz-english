### TITLE
Liability when a dyer goes against the owner's instructions

### QUESTION
Cloth was given to a dyer to be dyed, but he did not dye it the colour the owner had asked for and used a different colour instead. Is he liable to pay compensation or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described the owner has two options. If he wishes, he may leave the cloth with the dyer and recover the value of the cloth undyed; or, if he wishes, he may take the dyed cloth and pay the going rate for the work, but no more than the agreed price.

Al-Fatāwā al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah states (al-baqm being a red dyewood):

ولو أمر رجلا ليصبغ ثوبه بالزعفران أو بالبقم فصبغه بصبغ من جنس آخر كان لرب الثوب أن يضمنه قيمة ثوبه أبيض وترك ثوبه عليه وإن شاء أخذ الثوب وأعطاه أجر مثله لا يزاد على المسمى. (الفتاوى الهندية: ٤/٤۹۵، مسائل الضمان).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
