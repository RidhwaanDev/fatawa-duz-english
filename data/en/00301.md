### TITLE
Bleeding after a four-month miscarriage when a second child is still in the womb

### QUESTION
A woman was four months pregnant when she miscarried, but it then emerged that a second child was still in her womb. Bleeding has continued without a break since the miscarriage. Before the second child is born, does this blood count as menstruation, istiḥāḍah, or postnatal bleeding?

### ANSWER
There are two possibilities in this case. (1) If the second child is born within forty days of the first, then the bleeding counts as postnatal bleeding for forty days from the delivery of the first child, and whatever comes after that is istiḥāḍah (irregular bleeding). (2) If the second child is born more than forty days after the first, then this blood is istiḥāḍah and not postnatal bleeding.

Radd al-Muḥtār states:

والمرئي عقيب الثاني إن كان في الأربعين فمن نفاس الاول والا فاستحاضة. (رد المحتار: ۱/ ۳۰۱).

قال في الطحطاوي: ما تراه عقب الثاني إن كان قبل الأربعين فهو نفاس للأول لتمامها واستحاضة بعد تمامها فتغسل وتصلي كما وضعت الثاني وهو الصحيح كذا في البحر. (الطحطاوي على الدر المختار ۱/۱۵٤).
