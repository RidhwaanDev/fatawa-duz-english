### TITLE
Did Ḥaḍrat Abū Sufyān (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhu) oppose the caliphate of Ḥaḍrat Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhu)?

### QUESTION
It is mentioned in a hadith that Ḥaḍrat Abū Sufyān came to Ḥaḍrat ʿAlī (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhu) and said: What has happened to the caliphate, that it has gone to such people as are the fewest and the lowest in rank among the Quraysh — that is, Abū Bakr (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhu)? By God, if I wish I can raise an army against him — and so on. In which books is this hadith found? And what is the technical status of this hadith? Kindly explain and be rewarded.

### ANSWER
In the case in question, the narration you have referred to — that Abū Sufyān (raḍiyallāhu taʿālā ʿanhu) said, "If I wish I can raise an army against Abū Bakr" — this narration is in Al-Mustadrak of al-Ḥākim (۳/۸۳/٤٤٦۲) and in Faḍāʾil al-Khulafāʾ of Abū Nuʿaym (no. ۱۹۲), but in its chain Abū al-Shaʿthāʾ Yazīd ibn Muhāṣir (in Fatḥ al-Bāb fī al-Kunā wa'l-Alqāb it is "al-Muhājir") al-Kindī al-Kūfī is an unknown narrator. Imām al-Bukhārī (raḥimahullāh) mentioned him in Al-Tārīkh al-Kabīr (۸/۳۸۳) and Ibn Abī Ḥātim in Al-Jarḥ wa'l-Taʿdīl (۹/۲۸۷), but no criticism or endorsement of him is mentioned.

Furthermore, there are several persons named Abū al-Shaʿthāʾ, among whom are: (1) Sulaym ibn al-Aswad al-Muḥāribī al-Kūfī, who is trustworthy and a narrator of al-Bukhārī. (2) Jābir ibn Zayd, the Successor, al-Azdī al-Baṣrī Abū al-Shaʿthāʾ — he too is a narrator of al-Bukhārī and is trustworthy. (3) ʿAlī ibn Ḥasan ibn Sulaymān al-Ḥaḍramī al-Wāsiṭī al-Baṣrī Abū al-Shaʿthāʾ — he is a narrator of Muslim. (4) Yazīd ibn Muhāṣir al-Kindī al-Kūfī — he is unknown; Imām al-Bukhārī and Imām Ibn Abī Ḥātim mentioned him, but he is of unknown condition. There are several others also; for the details see: (فتح الباب فی الکنی و الالقاب: ۱/٤۲۰، و تہذیب الاسماء ۷/۲٦۲،
و سیر اعلام النبلاء: ٤/٤۸۱، و تہذیب الکمال: ۱۱/۳٤۰)۔

Consider the wording of the hadith:

أخبرني عبد الله بن الحسين القاضي بمرو ثنا الحارث بن أبي أسامة ثنا محمد بن سابق ثنا مالك بن مغول عن أبي الشعثاء الكندي عن مرة الطيب قال: جاء أبو سفيان بن حرب رضي الله عنه إلى على بن أبي طالب رضي الله عنه فقال: ما بال هذا الأمر في أقل قريش قلة وأذلها ذلة يعني أبابكر والله لئن شئت لا ملأنها عليه خيلاً ورجالاً فقال علي: لطالما عاديت الإسلام وأهله يا أبا سفيان، فلم يضره شيئاً إنا وجدنا أبابكر لها أهلاً. (المستدرك: ۳/۷۸/٤٤٦۲).

The second narration is in Muṣannaf ʿAbd al-Razzāq (۵/٤۵۱), Tārīkh al-Ṭabarī (۳/۲۰۹) and Al-Istīʿāb (٤/۱٦۷۹). In its chain is Ibn Abjar, who is a follower of the Successors; he did not meet the Companions, so this narration is broken.

The third narration is in Tārīkh Dimashq (۲۳/٤٦٤), and in its chain Ziyād ibn ʿAbd al-Raḥmān Abū al-Khaṣīb is unknown. (میزان الاعتدال: ۲/۹۲)۔

The fourth narration is in Ansāb al-Ashrāf of al-Balādhurī (۱/۵۸۸); in its chain al-Wāqidī is an abandoned narrator. (میزان الاعتدال: ۳/٦٦۵)۔

The fifth narration is mentioned in Ansāb al-Ashrāf of al-Balādhurī (۱/۵۸۸); in its chain the shaykh of Rabīʿ ibn Ṣabīḥ is unknown. The character of an illustrious Companion cannot be marred or impugned by weak narrations of this kind.

Ḥaḍrat Abū Sufyān ibn Ḥarb (raḍiyallāhu taʿālā ʿanhu) is an illustrious Companion and a general. The Messenger of Allah ﷺ made this announcement at the conquest of Makkah: "من دخل دار أبي سفيان فهو آمن" — whoever enters the house of Ḥaḍrat Abū Sufyān (raḍiyallāhu taʿālā ʿanhu) has safety from us. One of his eyes was lost in the path of Allah Most High at the Battle of Ṭāʾif and the other at the Battle of Yarmūk. At the Battle of Yarmūk all voices were silent and one voice was being raised: "O help of Allah, draw near!" The one raising this call was Ḥaḍrat Abū Sufyān (raḍiyallāhu taʿālā ʿanhu). The banner of that jihād was with his son Yazīd ibn Abī Sufyān. (الاصابہ: ۳/۳۳٤)۔

Note: some people have fallen into some confusion about the narrator and have called this narration sound. Thus some have declared Abū al-Shaʿthāʾ reliable and accepted him as a narrator of al-Bukhārī, whereas the reality is the opposite: the two are different. And some, in the chain of ʿAbd al-Razzāq and al-Ṭabarī, have called Ibn Abjar "Ibn al-Ḥurr", who is among the senior Successors, and have thus declared it sound — whereas this is an error. (تاریخ طبری: ۲/۲۳۷، ط: دار الکتب العلمیۃ)۔

For the details see: (المقتني في سرد الكني: ۱/۳۰۵، للامام الذهبي، وتبصير المنتبه بتحرير المشتبه: ٤/۱۳۲٦، والكني والأسماء لمسلم: ۸/٤/۱٦۰۰، وتهذيب الأسماء، جلد ۱، ٤ و۷، وفتح الباب في الكني والألقاب: ۱/٤۲۰).
