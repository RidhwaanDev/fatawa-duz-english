### TITLE
Recovering from the government the amount taken unjustly

### QUESTION
A man's electricity bill for one month came to one hundred thousand rand, whereas the bill for a household like his is normally two or three thousand. He contacted the municipal committee through a lawyer and asked for proof of this bill, but the authorities furnished no proof. In the end he paid the amount. May that man now recover from the government, by any possible means, an amount equal to the money that was taken from him unjustly, or not?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, it is permissible and correct to recover from the government, by any possible means, an amount equal to the money the government took unjustly. But to adopt a wrongful method for the sake of a few coins and thereby trample one's own honour is not the act of a wise man; permission will therefore not be given to trample one's self-respect.

Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ says that whatever money the government has unjustly taken from a Muslim as tax, that person may recover an equal amount from any government body under the heading of tax.

Imdād al-Muftīn states:

Question: The Government of India has issued its prize bond notes, on which interest is received at the rate of six per cent a year. I pay the Government of India about three thousand rupees a year in income tax. Would it be permissible for me to buy the bonds and take the interest on them with the intention that the government has already taken this amount from me as income tax — which is an unlawful demand in the sharia — and that I am taking it back?

Answer: You may recover from a government bank or from other government departments, by whatever means is possible, an amount equal to the money the government takes from you by way of tax. Whether the government calls it interest or something else, there is no harm in your taking it with the intention of recovering your rightful claim, and it will not be interest as far as you are concerned. In such situations the jurists have even permitted a person to recover the amount of his right from his debtor by taking it stealthily or by seizing it.

قال في الشامية في باب حد السرقة: إذا ظفر بمال مديونه له الأخذ ديانة، بل الأخذ من خلاف الجنس على ما نذكره. (فتاوى الشامي: ٤/ ۹۵، سعيد). انتهى
(Imdād al-Muftīn: 2/706, the book of interest and gambling, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

Note: it should also be borne in mind that Muftī Shafīʿ's fatwa relates to a government bank; recovering the money from a private bank is not permissible.

By way of corroboration, note a few general maxims:

وفي قواعد الفقه: الضرر يدفع بقدر الإمكان. (قواعد الفقه، ص ۸۸).
وفي الأشباه والنظائر: الضرر يزال. (الأشباه والنظائر: ۱/ ۵۲۰).
وفي قواعد الفقه عن السير: المظلوم له أن يدفع الظلم عن نفسه بما قدر عليه، لكن ليس له أن يظلم غيره. (قواعد الفقه: 124).
