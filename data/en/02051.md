### TITLE
Giving interest money as a bribe

### QUESTION
If a person is forced to pay a bribe because of some necessary work of his, may he use interest money for it or not?

### ANSWER
It is not permissible or correct to use interest money to pay a bribe. Doing so involves a double sin: one for making use of interest money, and another for giving a bribe.

Āp ke Masā'il states:

Question: Interest is unlawful, and bribery is also unlawful. What is the ruling on spending something unlawful on something unlawful? That is, may interest money be given as a bribe or not?

Answer: It will be a double sin — one for taking interest and one for giving a bribe. (آپ کے مسائل اور ان کا حل: ٦/۲٤۲) ۔

Maḥmūd al-Fatāwā states:

Question: When houses are to be built on land that is under cultivation, the N-A procedure has to be carried out through the government, and in it the government and the officials cause a great deal of trouble. The government charges a fee for the N-A procedure, and the officials also demand a bribe. May interest money be paid in both of these cases?

Answer: Whatever sum the government legally collects for the N-A procedure may be paid out of interest money received from a government bank; but it may not be given in the bribe that is paid to the officials, because in that case the money does not reach the government. (محمود الفتاوی: ۳/۷۲، باب الربا) ۔
