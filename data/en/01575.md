### TITLE
Seeking khulʿ because of a husband's ill-treatment

### QUESTION
A husband treats his wife cruelly and unjustly, and he is not willing to divorce her either. How is the woman to obtain her release? In the eyes of sharia, what way out is there for her?

### ANSWER
The family should first of all try to bring about a reconciliation between the two in some way, so that they may begin to live together in affection and love. If no way to reconciliation can be found, and the wife cannot bear to live with him because of his ill-treatment, then the husband should be brought round to giving a divorce. If he will not agree to anything, she should obtain a khulʿ (a divorce in return for compensation) by giving up her dower or some other property, and in this way free herself from her husband's cruelty. It is not permissible, however, to demand a heavy payment for a khulʿ; the khulʿ should be settled by returning the dower that was given.

قال الله تعالى: ﴿وإن خفتم شقاق بينهما فابعثوا حكماً من أهله وحكماً من أهلها إن يريدا إصلاحاً يوفق الله بينهما﴾. (سورة النساء: الآية: ۳۵).

Al-Fatāwā al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah states:

إذا تشاق الزوجان وخافا أن لا يقيما حدود الله فلا بأس بأن تفتدي نفسها منه بمال يخلعها به فإذا فعلا ذلك وقعت تطليقة بائنة ولزمها المال كذا في الهداية. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/٤۸۸. وكذا في بدائع الصنائع: ۳/۱٤۵، سعيد).

Majmūʿah-e Qawānīn-e Islāmī states:

Khulʿ is a kind of agreement in which the husband gives up the rights he holds through the marriage in return for an agreed payment; it therefore carries the ruling of an irrevocable divorce. Since the payment has to be made by the wife, her consent is essential. The offer of khulʿ may come from either spouse. The husband may say, "I grant you khulʿ in return for the dower," and the wife say, "I accept"; or the wife may say, "Grant me khulʿ in return for the dower," and the husband say, "I have granted the khulʿ."

Only those rights are given up by a khulʿ, and only that payment becomes due, which the two have agreed to give up and to pay.

If in the khulʿ the wife expressly gives up maintenance for the waiting period, it is given up. But housing during the waiting period, and maintenance for the children, are not given up even if she waives them.

Whatever amount of property is agreed upon in a khulʿ is valid, but fixing and taking more than the amount of the dower is disapproved in every case. (مجموعہ قوانین اسلامی، قانون خلع: ۱۸۱ ۔ ۱۸۳، مسلم پرسنل لا بورڈ) ۔

If a wife dislikes her husband because of his bad character, there is likewise room for a khulʿ; and if she dislikes him for no particular reason and both are agreed on a khulʿ, that too is allowed.

ʿAllāmah al-Shaʿrānī (may Allah have mercy on him) says in Al-Mīzān al-Kubrā:

واتفق الأئمة على أن المرأة إذا كرهت زوجها لقبح منظر أو سوء عشرة جاز لها أن تخالعه على عوض وإن لم يكن من ذلك شيء وتراضيا على الخلع من غير سبب جاز ولم يكره. (الميزان الكبرى: ۲/۱۱۹، كتاب الخلع، دار الفكر).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
