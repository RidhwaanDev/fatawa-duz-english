### TITLE
How to perform the funeral prayer over several deceased at once

### QUESTION
If several funeral prayers are performed together, and the deceased include men, women and children, how should the bodies be placed in front of the imam?

### ANSWER
It is best that a separate prayer be performed for each one, but it is also permissible to pray over all of them together. There are three ways of doing this. (1) One body is placed in front of the imam, with the head of the second at the feet of the first and the head of the third at the feet of the second. (2) The second body is placed on the qiblah side of the body that is in front of the imam, and the third on the qiblah side of the second, so that the chest of each one is in front of the imam. (3) The second body is placed toward the qiblah from the first in such a way that the head of the second is level with the shoulders of the first, and likewise the head of the third is level with the shoulders of the second. In all three arrangements the body of the man should be nearest to the imam, then the boy, then the woman. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 4/208; ʿUmdat al-Fiqh: Kitāb al-Ṣalāh, part two: p. 523, al-Mujaddidiyyah)

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

First arrangement: all the biers are lined up in a row running north to south. First place the man's bier, at his feet the bier of a boy who has not reached puberty, at his feet the bier of a woman, and at her feet the bier of a girl who has not reached puberty; and the imam should stand beside the most excellent of them.

Second arrangement: all the biers are placed one after another in front of the imam in such a way that the imam is level with the chests of them all: first the man's bier, then that of the boy who has not reached puberty, then that of the woman, and then that of the girl who has not reached puberty. This arrangement is preferable to the first.

Third arrangement: it is also permissible for the second bier to be set a little back from the first, so that the head of the second body is beside the shoulder of the first, the head of the third body beside the shoulder of the second, and the head of the fourth body beside the shoulder of the third (like a flight of steps). (Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 5/100, Raḥīmiyyah)

وإذا اجتمعت الجنائز فأفرد الصلاة على كل واحدة أولى من الجمع وتقديم الأفضل أفضل وإن اجتمع جاز، ثم إن شاء جعل الجنائز صفاً واحداً وقام عند أفضلهم، وإن شاء جعلها صفاً واحداً مما يلي القبلة واحداً خلف واحد بحيث يكون صدر كل جنازة مما يلي الإمام ليقوم بحذاء صدر الكل وإن جعلها درجاً فحسن لحصول المقصود وراعي الترتيب المعهود خلفه حالة الحياة، فيقرب منه الأفضل فالأفضل الرجل مما يليه، فالصبي، فالخنثى فالبالغة فالمراهقة، والصبي الحر يقدم على العبد، والعبد على المرأة. وفي الشامي: قوله وإن جمع جاز أي بأن صلي على الكل صلاة واحدة، قوله صفاً واحداً أي كما يصطفون في حال حياتهم عند الصلاة بدائع: أي بأن يكون رأس كل عند رجل الآخر فيكون الصف على عرض القبلة، (قوله وإن شاء جعلها صفاً واحداً) ذكر في البدائع التخيير بين هذا والذي قبله، ثم قال هذا جواب ظاهر الرواية. وروي عن أبي حنيفة رحمه الله في غير رواية الأصول أن الثاني أولى لأن السنة هي قيام الإمام بحذاء الميت، وهو يحصل في الثاني دون الأول.
