### TITLE
Hiring out a wedding hall to a non-Muslim

### QUESTION
May a Muslim hire out his wedding hall to a non-Muslim or not, when it is certain that the non-Muslims will engage in dancing, music and other unlawful things in that hall?

### ANSWER
In the view of Imām Abū Ḥanīfah (may Allah have mercy on him) it is permissible to hire a hall out to a non-Muslim, while in the view of the two disciples (may Allah have mercy on them) it is disliked. There is also a difference among the fatawa of our elders (may Allah have mercy on them). Hazrat Mufti Rashīd Aḥmad resolved this difference by saying that giving it to a non-Muslim involves the milder kind of dislike. In the case asked about, since the tenant is a non-Muslim, this transaction will be contrary to what is preferable; it will not be unlawful or strictly disliked.

The evidences for this have already been set out in detail, and repeating them would only make the answer long.
