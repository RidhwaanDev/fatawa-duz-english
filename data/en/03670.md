### TITLE
A bank charging a fee for acting as an agent

### QUESTION
If a buyer appoints a bank as his agent to purchase a car and fixes ten thousand as the bank's fee for the agency, will this fee be permissible for the bank or not? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
For an agent to work for a fee is permissible and correct, provided that the agent is known and recognised as one who takes a fee, or that this is stated beforehand, and that the fee too is specified, so that no dispute arises later. Accordingly, for the bank to take ten thousand and purchase a car for the one who appointed it is permissible and correct. See Fatḥ al-Qadīr, in which it is recorded:

والصواب أن مراد صاحب العناية هو أنه قد يكون في نفس الوكالة التعاوض كما إذا أخذ الوكيل الأجرة لا قامة الوكالة فإنه غير ممنوع شرعاً، إذ الوكالة عقد جائز لا يجب علي الوكيل إقامتها فيجوز أخذ الأجرة فيها. (فتح القدير: ۸/۳، دار الفكر، وفتاوى الشامي: ۷/۲٦۹).

Islāmī Fiqh states: Under a paid agent are included all those people who do some work for a fee or on commission. All the employees of a government are its agents, and for this reason they should work in accordance with the wishes of their government. If you have some work done by your personal employee, he will be regarded as your agent; likewise, if you appoint a commission agent, he will be regarded as your agent — that is, he should work in accordance with your instructions. (Islāmī Fiqh: 2/647).

For more, see: (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā: 5/720 and 717, published by Zamzam; and Jadīd Muʿāmalāt ke Sharʿī Aḥkām: 1/228).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
