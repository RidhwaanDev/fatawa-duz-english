### TITLE
A solution to the problem of women standing level with men in the Ḥaram

### QUESTION
The problem of women standing level with the men (muḥādhāt) in the Ḥaram seems insoluble. Is there any room for leniency in it, or any room to give a ruling according to the school of another imam? Have the jurists dealt with this matter explicitly or not?

### ANSWER
The rule that a woman standing level with a man invalidates the prayer belongs to the Hanafi school alone; in the view of the other three imams, standing level counts for nothing. Furthermore, the only evidence the Hanafis have on this question is the statement of ʿAbdullāh ibn Masʿūd (may Allah be pleased with him):

أخّروهن من حيث أخرهن الله

Mullā ʿAlī al-Qārī (may Allah have mercy on him) said in Sharḥ al-Niqāyah that the report is not sound as a saying traced back to the Prophet ﷺ, though it is sound as a statement of ʿAbdullāh ibn Masʿūd (may Allah be pleased with him) himself. Even then, all that can be established from it is a recommendation: that it is recommended for women to pray at the back, just as it is recommended for children to be at the back. And even if one grants that the command implies obligation, and that such a statement of a Companion has the force of a prophetic hadith, there is still no evidence in it that the prayer becomes invalid when a woman stands level with a man.

In short, invalidating a prayer requires firm evidence, and there is none here; so the prayer ought not to be invalid. Mullā ʿAlī al-Qārī's words, however, differ from the standard texts, the commentaries and the fatwa works, and it is the texts, commentaries and fatwa works that must be relied upon. The two positions may be reconciled in this way: in ordinary circumstances the standard texts and the rest are what count, so that standing level does invalidate the prayer; but in the Ḥaram, where avoiding it is extremely difficult, indeed all but impossible, it would be appropriate — given the necessity involved — to issue the ruling on the basis of this statement of Mullā ʿAlī al-Qārī (may Allah have mercy on him).

For ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) and others have stated plainly that if a mufti issues a ruling based on one of the less well-known positions in situations of necessity, with an eye to making things easier, that is sound and there is room for it. If, on the other hand, we were to say that the prayer is invalid even in the Ḥaram, that would place people in great difficulty and hardship.

Some scholars have offered the answer that, since the imams of the Ḥaram do not make the intention of leading the women, the women are not in fact following them and so no standing level is established. But, in the first place, according to the schools of the other imams no separate intention for the women is required; and in the second place, the imams themselves have stated that they do make the intention of leading the women. Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā ʿAlāʾ al-Dīn Ṣāḥib (may his shade endure) says that he himself asked Shaykh Subayl, who replied that they make the intention for the women as well. So the argument that, because they do not make the intention for the women, no standing level occurs, is hard to accept.

The upshot is this: the solution to the problem of standing level in the Ḥaram can only be to issue the ruling on the basis of Mullā ʿAlī al-Qārī's words, following ʿAllāmah Shāmī's statement that at a time of need one may keep the easier course in view.

Consider the words of Mullā ʿAlī al-Qārī (may Allah have mercy on him):

وأما قول صاحب الهداية لقوله ﷺ أخّروهن من حيث أخّرهن الله فغير معروف رفعه وأغرب منه أنه جعله من المشاهير وهذا خلاف ما عليه الجماهير، والحاصل أنه لا يصح رفعه لكنه ثبت عن ابن مسعود رضي الله عنه ووقفه رواه الطبراني

والحديث مع كونه موقوفاً لا دلالة له فيه إلا على الاستحباب فأخّروهن عن الرجال كتأخر الأطفال وفق ما ثبت في الأحاديث المرفوعة وعلى تسليم أن الأمر للوجوب بناء على أنه في حكم المرفوع فلا دلالة فيه على إبطال الصلاة حال المحاذاة. (شرح النقاية: ۱/ ۲۰٤، ترتيب الصفوف، سعيد)

Consider too ʿAllāmah Shāmī's statement about giving a ruling on a less well-known report in situations of necessity:

وفي المعراج عن فخر الأئمة: لو أفتي مفت بشيء من هذه الأقوال في مواضع الضرورة طلباً للتيسير كان حسناً. (شامي: ۱/۲۸۹ باب الحيض، مطلب لو أفتي مفت الخ، سعيد. ورسم المفتي: ٤٤)

ʿAllāmah Ibn Nujaym al-Miṣrī (may Allah have mercy on him) has also quoted this statement in al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq. Consider:

وفي معراج الدراية معزياً إلى فخر الأئمة لو أفتي مفت بشيء من هذه الأقوال في مواضع الضرورة طلباً للتيسير كان حسناً. (البحر الرائق: ۱/۱۹۳، باب الحيض، الماجدية)

It also accords with the principles and the temper of sharia — that hardship and constriction should not be imposed, but rather the easier course adopted. Consider:

قال الله تعالي: ﴿وما جعل عليكم في الدين من حرج﴾ و﴿يريد الله بكم اليسر ولا يريد بكم العسر﴾
عن أبي بردة قال بعث النبي ﷺ جده أبا موسى ومعاذ إلى اليمن فقال: "يسّرا ولا تعسّرا وبشّرا ولا تنفّرا وتطاوعا ولا تختلفا" متفق على ه. (مشكاة: ۲/۳۲۳ باب ما على الولاة من التيسير)

Sharḥ al-Majallah states:

المشقة تجلب التيسير. المادة ۱۷: (شرح المجلة ۲۷. ۲۸)
إن الصعوبة تصير سبباً للتسهيل ويلزم التوسيع في وقت المضايقة

اعلم أن أصل الشرع مبناه على اليسر والتسهيل، قال الله تعالي: ﴿يريد الله بكم اليسر ولا يريد بكم العسر﴾ و﴿وما جعل عليكم في الدين من حرج﴾ وقال ﷺ: "أحب الدين إلى الله الحنيفية السمحة". المادة ۱۷: (شرح المجلة: ۱/٤۸، رشيديه، محمد خالد الاتاسي)

The Grand Mufti of Pakistan, our respected teacher Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Muftī Walī Ḥasan Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him), also used to give the ruling that a prayer in the Ḥaram is valid despite this standing level.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
