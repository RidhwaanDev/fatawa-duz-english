### TITLE
The ruling on observing iʿtikāf in a prayer room when there is no mosque

### QUESTION
Is it necessary for the sunnah iʿtikāf that there be a mosque in the legal sense? If a person observes iʿtikāf in a nearby prayer room which is not a legal mosque, will he receive the reward of the sunnah iʿtikāf or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
For iʿtikāf to be valid it is necessary that there be a mosque in the legal sense; the sunnah iʿtikāf will not be discharged in a temporary place of worship or in a prayer room. See what al-Durr al-Mukhtār records:

هو لغة: اللبث وشرعاً لبث، المكث ذكر ولو مميزاً في مسجد جماعة هو ما له إمام ومؤذن أديت فيه الخمس أو لا، وعن الإمام اشتراط أداء الخمس فيه. (الدر المختار: ۲/٤٤۰، ط: سعيد، وكذا في الهداية مع العناية: ۲/۳۰۸).

Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

وأما شروطه ومنها مسجد الجماعة فيصح في كل مسجد له أذان، وإقامة هو الصحيح كذا في الخلاصة، وأفضل الاعتكاف ما كان في المسجد الحرام ثم في مسجد النبي عليه الصلاة والسلام، ثم في بيت المقدس ثم في الجامع ثم فيما كان أهله أكثر وأوفر كذا في التبيين. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۲۱۱).

Bihishtī Zēwar records:

Three things are necessary for iʿtikāf: (1) staying in a congregational mosque; (2) staying with the intention of iʿtikāf — thus merely staying without purpose or intention is not called iʿtikāf; and since it is a condition for the soundness of the intention that the one who makes it be a Muslim and of sound mind, the conditions of sanity and Islam are also covered under the intention; (3) being free and pure of menstruation and post-natal bleeding, and being pure of the state of major ritual impurity. (Bihishtī Zēwar, p. 959, published by Dār al-Ishāʿat).

Kitāb al-Fatāwā states:

Since you did not formally make the intention that this place should be a mosque in the legal sense, but gave it the status of a place of worship only temporarily, it is not correct to observe iʿtikāf in it. Iʿtikāf is in a legal mosque, and that too a mosque in which the five daily prayers are performed in congregation. (Kitāb al-Fatāwā: 8/396).

If, however, there is no legal mosque in a settlement but only a prayer room, and it is there that the five daily prayers are performed in congregation, then if iʿtikāf is observed in it, it is to be hoped that the reward of iʿtikāf will be obtained. Thus Muftī ʿAbd al-Raḥīm ṣāḥib states:

Since there is no mosque at all in the settlement, iʿtikāf should be observed in the building in which arrangements are made for the five daily prayers in congregation, and it is to be hoped that the reward of the emphasised sunnah will be received. If it is not done, the burden of neglect will remain. One should carry out as much as one is able; acceptance rests with the will of Allah Most High.

قالوا لما سقط عن المرأة في صلاتها المسجد الجامع كذلك سقط في اعتكافها المسجد الجامع أيضاً. (رسائل الأركان، ص: ۲۲۹)، فقط والله تعالى أعلم بالصواب.

Note: in the building in which the prayer is performed in congregation, the reward of congregation will be obtained, but the reward of the mosque will be lacking; efforts to build a mosque should therefore be kept up. (Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 4/137, published by Maktabat al-Iḥsān, Deoband).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
