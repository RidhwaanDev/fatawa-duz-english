### TITLE
The ruling on paying interest money in rates tax

### QUESTION
Is it correct to pay interest money in the rates on one's house or not? Rates means the amount fixed by the government as tax upon houses, land and other things.

### ANSWER
The basic ruling concerning interest money is that it should be returned to its owner; but if the owner is not known, it should be given away in charity to the poor and the needy without the intention of reward. As for paying it in the government's rates tax, that is not permissible, because the banks of South Africa are private ones and not government banks.

Consider Maʿārif al-Sunan, where the ruling on unlawful wealth is mentioned in these terms:

قال شيخنا: ويستفاد من كتب فقهائنا كالهداية وغيرها أن من ملك بملك خبيث ولم يمكنه الرد إلى المالك فسبيله التصدق على الفقراء، قال: ومثله يقول ابن القيم في بدائع الفوائد، قال: والظاهر أن المتصدق بمثله ينبغي أن ينوي به فراغ ذمته ولا يرجو به المثوبة، نعم يرجوها بالعمل بأمر الشارع، وكيف يرجو الثواب بمال حرام ويكفيه أن يخلص منه كفافاً رأساً برأس. (معارف السنن: ۱/۳۵).

Niẓām al-Fatāwā records:

The basic principle is that whatever wealth is unlawful, the fundamental ruling concerning it is that it should be returned by some means into the ownership of the one whose wealth it is

(إذا علم المالك بعينه فلا شك في حرمته ووجوب رده عليه، فتاوى شامي، باب البيع الفاسد، سعيد) اور

and when he cannot convey it into the ownership of the real owner, then, with the intention of saving himself from its evil consequences, he should give it as charity to someone deserving of charity and remove it from his own ownership as soon as possible; he should not use it himself for any purpose, nor should he form the intention of reward after giving it away in charity,

(وأما إذا كان عند رجل مال خبيث فأما إذا ملكه بعقد فاسد أو حصل له بغير عقد ولا يمكنه أن يرد إلى مالكه ويريد أن يدفع مظلمته فليس له حيلة إلا أن يدفع إلى الفقراء، بذل المجهود: ۱/۳۷)

Interest money received from the central government may be given in income tax and sales tax, since it belongs to the central government; but it may not be given in municipal corporation board tax, municipality tax, or any of the other taxes besides these. (نظام الفتاوی: ۱/۱۸۵) ۔
