### TITLE
Paying zakat for a person who dies before discharging it

### QUESTION
If zakat became due from a person and he died before paying it, will the zakat be taken out of his wealth after his death or not?

### ANSWER
There are several situations here. (1) If the zakat became due and the man died before paying it, the zakat will not be taken out of his wealth after his death, because an intention is a condition for zakat and here it is absent.

Consider what al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

إذا مات من عليه الزكاة سقطت الزكاة بموته، كذا في المحيط. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱۷٦).

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

وشرط صحة أدائها نية مقارنة له أي للأداء ولوحكماً. (الدر المختار: ۲/۲٦۸، سعيد).

(2) If the deceased left instructions that the zakat on his wealth be paid, and the amount of the zakat is less than or equal to a third of the estate, then the heirs are obliged to pay the zakat.

(3) If he set the zakat money aside before his death, or handed it to an agent, and then died before it was paid out: if the deceased had left instructions to that effect, it will be paid from a third of the whole estate. If he left no such instructions, the money set aside is counted as part of the estate and divided among the heirs, because the death of the zakat payer, who was the principal, dismisses the agent, so that any dealing by the agent with the money is no longer valid. (مستفاد از احسن الفتاوی: ٤/۲٦۵).

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

ولو مات فأداها وارثه جاز، وفي الشامي: "جاز" في الجوهرة: إذا مات من عليه زكاة، أو فطرة أو كفارة أو نذر لم تؤخذ من تركته عندنا، إلا أن يتبرع ورثته بذلك وهو من أهل التبرع ولم يجبروا عليه، وإن أوصي تنفذ من الثلث. (الشامي: ۲/۳۵۹، سعيد).

It further states:

ولا يخرج عن العهدة بالعزل بل بالأداء للفقراء أو تصدق بكله، وفي الشامي: فلو ضاعت لا تسقط عنه الزكاة ولو مات كانت ميراثاً عنه. (شامي: ۲/۲۷۰، سعيد).

(4) If the deceased left no instructions about the zakat, but an adult heir wishes to pay the zakat on behalf of the one he has inherited from out of his own share, it is to be hoped that Allah will accept it and clear the deceased of the obligation.

Al-Shāmī states:

إلا أن يتبرع ورثته بذلك وهم من أهل التبرع، ولم يجبروا عليه. (شامي: ۲/۳۵۹، سعيد).

Kitāb al-Fatāwā states:

If zakat was due from a person and he neither paid it himself nor left instructions for it to be paid, his heirs are under no obligation to pay it. But the course of good character and kindness is that the heirs should try, as far as they are able, to pay the zakat on his behalf, for it may be that Allah will forgive him because of it.

"وما ذلك على الله بعزيز". (کتاب الفتاوی: ۳/۳۳٦).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
