### TITLE
Bleeding after a miscarriage or after the womb has been cleaned out

### QUESTION
If a woman miscarries, or if she has her womb cleaned out, does the bleeding that follows count as menstruation, as irregular bleeding, or as post-natal bleeding?

### ANSWER
If one or two of the child's limbs had already formed at the time of the miscarriage, the bleeding that follows it is post-natal bleeding. If no limb at all had formed and it was nothing but a piece of flesh, then this blood is not post-natal bleeding; if it can be menstruation it counts as menstruation, and if not it is irregular bleeding. (Bihishtī Zēwar, summarised, p. 62, part two).

ʿAllāmah Ibn al-Humām writes:

والسقط الذي استبان بعض خلقه كأصبع أو ظفر ولد فلو لم يستبن منه شيء لم يكن ولداً فان أمكن جعله حيضاً بان امتد جعل إياه والا فاستحاضة. (فتح القدير ۱/۱۸۷).
