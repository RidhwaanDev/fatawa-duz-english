### TITLE
Ablution and the ritual bath with a gold tooth or a gold cap

### QUESTION
If someone has had a gold tooth fitted without needing it, or has had a gold cap put on a tooth without needing it, will his ablution and ritual bath be valid or not?

### ANSWER
Since the jurists differ about having a gold tooth fitted, one should avoid it as long as there is no real need. But once it has been fitted and cannot be removed without hardship, it is like a part of the body, and it will therefore not prevent the ritual bath.

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states: in the view of the Great Imam it is permissible to have a silver pin, a silver plate or a silver tooth fitted, but it is not permissible to have a gold pin or plate fitted. In the view of Imam Muḥammad a gold pin and the like are also allowed. Because of this disagreement, avoiding a gold pin is the safer course. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah 5/218)

Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

قال محمد في الجامع الصغير: ولا يشد الأسنان بالذهب ويشدها بالفضة وهو قول أبي حنيفة وقال محمد: يشدها بالذهب أيضاً وذكر الحاكم في المنتقي لو تحركت سن رجل وخاف سقوطها فشدها بالذهب أو بالفضة لم يكن به بأس عند أبي حنيفة وأبي يوسف وروى الحسن عن أبي حنيفة أنه فرق بين السن والأنف. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/ ۳۳٦).

Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥal states: it is permissible to fit caps made of gold, or of a metal resembling it, over the teeth, and in that condition a person's ablution and ritual bath are valid. Anything fixed in place so firmly that it can no longer be removed — for example, a gold or silver cap set on the teeth in such a way that it cannot come off — its outer surface is given the ruling of the tooth itself, and the ritual bath is valid without removing it. (Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥal 2/52)

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states: when, by way of treatment, silver or gold is put into the cavities of a tooth and they are thereby closed and filled, the material put in becomes a part of the body, and in the ritual bath and in ablution it is enough for the water to reach that material. (Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah 3/10)

Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

ولو کان سنه مجوفاً فبقي فيه أو بين أسنانه طعام أو درن رطب في أنفه تم غسله على الأصح کذا في الزاهدي.

(الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/ ۱۳).
