### TITLE
Using live worms for fishing

### QUESTION
Most people use live worms for catching fish, because by this means a fish can be caught very quickly and easily. Is it correct to use live worms merely for the purpose of catching fish, or not?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, it is not correct to use live worms; rather they should be killed first and then used, because this involves causing pain to a living creature, and that is forbidden.

Consider the following. Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

ويكره تعليم البازي بالطير الحي لتعذيبه. (الدر المختار: ٦/٤۷٤، باب الصيد، سعيد).

Al-Fatāwā al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah states:

ويكره تعليم البازي بالطير يأخذه ويعذبه ولا بأس بأن يعلم بالمذبوح. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳٦۲).

تكمله فتح الملهم
states:

وكل طريق أدي الحيوان إلى تعذيب أكثر من اللازم لا زهاق روحه فهو في النهي ومأمور بالاجتناب عنه. (تكملة فتح الملهم: ۳/۵٤۰، باب الامر بإحسان الذبح).
قال الحافظ في الفتح في باب ما يكره من المثلة والمصبورة

وفي هذه الأحاديث تحريم تعذيب الحيوان الآدمي وغيره. (فتح الباري: ۹/٤٤۵، ط: لاهور).

Fatāwā Rashīdiyyah states:

Question: There is an insect called ghīnsā; people break it into pieces, fix it upon a hook and thereby catch fish. What, then, is the ruling on catching fish in this way and on eating such fish?

Answer: To kill it first and then cut it into pieces and fix it upon the hook is correct, and to fix it on while it is still alive is forbidden, for causing pain to a living creature is strictly disliked. (فتاوی رشیدیہ، ص ٦۳۵، ط: لاہور).

Imdād al-Fatāwā states:

If the earthworm is first killed and fishing is then done with it, this is permissible. On that occasion a fisherman stated that fish come just as readily to a dead earthworm. (امداد الفتاوی: ۳/۵۵۷).

Fatāwā Muḥammadiyyah states:

If an earthworm is killed and then fixed upon the hook and used for fishing, this act is correct. To fix a living creature upon a hook and fish with it is forbidden. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۷/۲۱۸، ۲۱۹، جامعہ فاروقیہ).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
