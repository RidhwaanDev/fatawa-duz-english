### TITLE
Who pays the land tax on rented land

### QUESTION
Zayd rented land from ʿAmr at an annual rent of five hundred rupees per kanāl (a quarter of a bīghah). Who is liable for the land tax or land revenue on this land — the owner or the tenant?

### ANSWER
Whatever is settled in the agreement is valid: it may validly be placed on the owner or on the tenant, whichever of the two it is placed on. If there is no mention of who is to bear it, then it should be placed on whichever party customarily bears it, and that will be valid. In sharecropping (muzāraʿah), however, the land tax is placed on the owner. (See Fatāwā ʿUthmānī: 3/380).
