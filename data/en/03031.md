### TITLE
The ruling on celebrating Valentine's Day

### QUESTION
Is it permissible for Muslims to celebrate Valentine's Day (valentines day) or not? May a husband give his wife a gift or present on this day? And what is its basis? Kindly explain and be rewarded.

### ANSWER
Islam is unique and distinctive in its civilisation and culture. It does not permit resembling other nations or walking shoulder to shoulder with them. It teaches its followers a pure and wholesome way of social life and blocks every kind of indecency and disobedience. Accordingly, it is not correct, in imitation of other nations, to celebrate a day of this kind whose foundation is built upon romance, love affairs and fornication.

As for a husband and wife treating each other, and their other relatives, with good character, and giving one another gifts and presents — this is not confined to this day alone; rather it should be practised from time to time.

Consider the following brief background to Valentine's Day:

Valentine's Day is celebrated every year on 14 February. It began as a Western Christian festival in honour of one or more priests named Valentine. Many ancient Christian martyrs bore the name Valentine, but the Valentines in whose honour the day of 14 February is celebrated are the Valentine of Rome and the Valentine of Terni. The Valentine of Rome was a priest who was martyred in 269 CE, and the Valentine of Terni was a great priest (a bishop) who was martyred in 273 CE. In the beginning this festival had no connection with romantic sentiments; rather, the very basis of this festival was the sacrifices of religious leaders.

Then, after this, a great poet of the fourteenth century CE (Geoffrey chauser) composed verses filled with romantic sentiments and love, and because of this the festival became ensnared in the trap of love and romance.

Then, in the eighteenth century CE in England, it turned into a festival of love and romance in which lovers give flowers and other gifts and presents to their beloved. Later, in the twentieth and twenty-first centuries, it took the form of a custom and convention and spread to various countries of the world. (Summarised from Wikipedia.)

The Holy Prophet ﷺ forbade imitating other nations and adopting a resemblance to them:

وعن ابن عمر رضي الله عنه قال: قال رسول الله ﷺ: "من تشبه بقوم فهو منهم".
قال الشيخ الملا على القاري: أي من شبه نفسه بالكفار مثلاً في اللباس وغيره أو بالفساق أو الفجار أو بأهل التصوف والصلحاء الأبرار، فهو منهم، في الإثم والخير، قال الطيبي: هذا عام في الخَلق والخُلق والشعار ولما كان الشعار أظهر في التشبه. (مرقاة المفاتيح: ۷/۱۵۵، كتاب اللباس، ط: رشيدية).

The Holy Prophet ﷺ encouraged the giving of gifts and presents to one another:

وعن أبي هريرة رضي الله عنه عن النبي ﷺ، قال: "تهادوا تحابوا". رواه البخاري في الأدب المفرد، وأبو يعلي بإسناد حسن. (بلوغ المرام، ص ۹۳۵).

The Holy Prophet ﷺ also commanded compassion towards animals.

عن أبي هريرة رضي الله عنه عن رسول الله ﷺ قال: غفر لا مرأة مومسة مرت بكلب على رأس ركي يلهث قال: كاد يقتله العطش فنزعت خفها فأوثقته بخمارها فنزعت له من الماء فغفر لها بذلك. (صحيح البخاري، رقم: ۳۳۲۱).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
