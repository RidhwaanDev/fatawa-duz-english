### TITLE

The way to stir a spoon in a cup of tea

### QUESTION

People have the habit of putting sugar in the cup and then stirring the spoon round in it. If someone is facing west, will it be better to move the spoon towards the south or towards the north?

### ANSWER

No explicit ruling on this question has come to my notice. Nevertheless, drawing an analogy with the writing of Islamic script, one should stir towards the south: just as a person writing begins from the right-hand side, that is, from near his right hand, and carries the pen over to the left-hand side — and a man facing west is thus carrying it towards the south — and then brings the pen back to the right-hand side and begins again from the right, so too this will be the more fitting way with the spoon. For the rest, it is permissible in either direction. Scientists too hold that the movement of the earth is in this same way, and when people perform ṭawāf they walk in this same way, which is the sum of the Prophet's teachings.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
