### TITLE
A clear parallel showing that the shedding of blood is what is intended in a vow to slaughter

### QUESTION
A clear parallel showing that the shedding of blood is what is intended in a vow to slaughter.

### ANSWER
A man vowed to slaughter his son, and the jurists said that he should slaughter a goat in place of the son.

Consider what al-Tātārkhāniyyah states:

إذا نذر الرجل ذبح ولده لزمه شاة لكل واحد يذبحها بمكة. (الفتاوى التاتارخانية: ۵/٤۳. وكذا في الفقه الإسلامي وأدلته: ۲/۳۱۸ باب النذر).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
