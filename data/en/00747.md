### TITLE
A person remains a traveller until he enters his home town

### QUESTION
A man has been staying at the madrasah for several years. At first he used to go home after two or three weeks; now he goes every week. His home is in Roshnee, and it is obvious that a resident of Roshnee is not a traveller here. He went to Durban for a day on some errand and has come back. Is he now a traveller or a resident before he reaches home?

### ANSWER
A person who has covered the sharia travel distance is treated as a traveller until he enters his own town, or forms the intention of staying somewhere for 15 days or more, in which case he will then pray in full.

Consider what al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

ولا يزال على حكم السفر حتى ينوي الإقامة في بلدة أو قرية خمسة عشر يوماً أو أكثر كذا في الهداية. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱۳۹، باب صلاة المسافر)

Shāmī states:

قوله: حتى يدخل موضع مقامه إن سار مدة السفر أي إنما يدوم على القصر إلى الدخول إن سار ثلاثة أيام. (شامي: ۲/۱۲٤، باب صلاة المسافر)
