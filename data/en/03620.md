### TITLE
Divorce taking effect by saying "If there is no reconciliation by nine o'clock tonight, then the third divorce"

### QUESTION
A man had earlier given his wife two divorces. He has now said to his wife, "If we have not reconciled by nine o'clock tonight, then I am issuing the third divorce." Now, if there is no reconciliation, will the third divorce take effect or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, if there is no reconciliation by nine o'clock at night the third divorce will take effect, and the woman will become irrevocably forbidden to him; thereafter it will not be permissible to marry her without a valid shar‘ī ḥalālah. Consider what Tabyīn al-Ḥaqāʾiq states:

الطلاق المعلق بالشرط فإنه ليس بطلاق للحال ولأنه إعدام ويصير طلاقاً عند وجود الشرط. (۳/۱۹۳، ط: إمداديه). (وكذا في فتاوى الشامي: ۳/۳٤۱، سعيد، والفقه الحنفي في ثوبه الجديد: ۲/۱۸۳، دار القلم).

Divorce takes effect by a verb in the present tense. See: (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā 4/45, Mumbai edition)

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
