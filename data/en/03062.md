### TITLE
The ruling on istimnāʾ bi'l-yad (masturbation)

### QUESTION
Is istimnāʾ bi'l-yad unlawful or merely disliked? And what is the evidence for its unlawfulness or its being disliked? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
The act of istimnāʾ bi'l-yad (masturbation) is impermissible and sinful, and it is also harmful from a medical point of view. Those with experience report that performing this act leads to it becoming a habit, and that it destroys a man's virility. In short, it is medically ruinous, and it is therefore necessary to keep away from this vile act. Consider what is in Fatāwā al-Shāmī:

قوله الاستمناء حرام، أي بالكف إذا كان لاستجلاب الشهوة، أما إذا غلبته الشهوة وليس له زوجة ولا أمة ففعل ذلك لتسكينها فالرجاء أنه لا وبال عليه كما قال أبو الليث ويجب لو خاف الزنا. (فتاوى الشامي: ٤/۲۷، سعيد).

It is recorded in Rūḥ al-Maʿānī:

اختلف في استمناء الرجل بيده

فجمهور الأئمة على تحريمه وهو عندهم داخل فيما وراء ذلك

وقال ابن الهمام رحمه الله: يحرم فإن غلبته الشهوة ففعل إرادة تسكينها به فالرجاء أن لا يعاقب. (روح المعاني: ۱۸/ ۱۰).

It is written in Qāmūs al-Fiqh:

This act is forbidden and unlawful. The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said:

ناكح اليد ملعون.

[قال الرهاوي في حاشية المنار: لا أصل له: كشف الخفاء: ۲/۳۲۵، وكذا نقله عنه ملا على القاري في "المصنوع في معرفة الحديث الموضوع، رقم: ۳۷۸، وقال الشيخ الألباني: ضعيف، وللمزيد راجع: الضعيفة: رقم: ٤۸۵۱]

Its unlawfulness may also be argued from verses 5 to 7 of Sūrat al-Muʾminūn, in which only two channels are specified for the fulfilment of sexual desire — one, a wife, and the other, a bondwoman — and it is evident that this is a third form. The jurists have classed it as an offence liable to discretionary punishment.

To do such a thing with the intention of satisfying desire is by no means permissible. Yes, if desire is overwhelming and it is done in order to avoid fornication and to bring calm and stillness to one's desire, then the jurists have written that it is hoped there will be no evil consequence or punishment for it. Falling under this same heading of necessity is the extraction of semen for the purpose of treatment or medical testing. All of this, however, relates to an occasional occurrence; it is not permitted as a habit. (Qāmūs al-Fiqh: vol. 2, p. 125).

For more, see: (Fatāwā Bayyināt: 4/365, 370; 8/249; and Fatāwā Ḥaqqāniyyah: 2/452).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
