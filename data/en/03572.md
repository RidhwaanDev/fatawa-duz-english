### TITLE
The ruling on the cleanliness of the iḥrām for one who performs ʿumrah frequently

### QUESTION
A person keeps performing more than one ʿumrah. Many times the iḥrām becomes soiled, or it becomes dirty because some drink or the like has spilled on it. Is it necessary each time to wash the iḥrām and to wear a clean one, or is an ʿumrah valid in such an iḥrām? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
For entering the state of iḥrām it is recommended to use two white sheets, either new ones or old ones that have been washed clean. However, if a person performs ʿumrah again and again and gets no opportunity to wash them, then using them without washing is also permissible, though contrary to what is preferable, and the ʿumrah is valid without any dislikedness. Washing them before use, nevertheless, is superior and better.

Consider what is transmitted in Fatāwā Shāmī:

ولبس إزار ورداء

جديدين أو غسيلين طاهرين أبيضين ككفن الكفاية. قوله جديدين، أشار بتقديمه إلى أفضليته، وكونه أبيض أفضل من غيره وفي عدم غسل العتيق ترك المستحب، بحر. (فتاوى الشامي: ۲/٤۸۱، سعيد). (وكذا في اللباب في شرح الكتاب: ۱/۹۲، ط: دار الكتاب العربي).

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh records: To wear two white cloths, new or washed — that is, an upper sheet and a lower wrapper. Wearing the two cloths, that is the upper sheet and the lower wrapper, is a sunnah; and that the remaining attributes be found in them — that is, that they be white and new or washed — is recommended. That the two cloths be new is superior, and in using an old cloth without washing it there is the omission of something recommended. (عمدۃ الفقہ: ٤/۱۲۲) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
