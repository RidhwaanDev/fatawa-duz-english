### TITLE
Saying "Get out of the house" without intending divorce

### QUESTION
Five years ago a man said to his wife, "Get out of the house", and at the time he said that he had not intended divorce. In the meantime a child was born. Now he says that he did intend divorce. Does a divorce take effect through his saying this five years later or not? And is the child's parentage established?

### ANSWER
In the case described, the words "Get out of the house" are among the allusive expressions of divorce. The ruling for them is that if he intended divorce, the divorce takes effect; but since the husband denied it, saying that he had no intention of divorce, no divorce has taken effect. His saying now, after a lapse of five years, that he did intend divorce has no effect at all, because this amounts to going back on an admission, and no weight is given to that. The children's parentage, too, is established.

Consider what al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

الكنايات لا تطلق بها قضاءً إلا بنية أو دلالة الحال وهي حالة مذاكرة الطلاق أو الغضب. وفي الشامي: قوله قضاء، قيد به لأنه لا يقع ديانة بدون النية ولو وجدت دلالة الحال، فوقوعه بواحد من النية أو دلالة الحال إنما هو في القضاء فقط، كما هو صريح البحر وغيره. (الدر المختار مع الشامي: ۳/۲۹٦، باب الكنايات. وكذا في الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۳۷٤. والبحر الرائق ۳/۲۹۸، باب الكنايات في الطلاق).

Sharḥ al-Majallah states:

لا يصح الرجوع عن الإقرار في حقوق العباد. (شرح المجلة، ص: ۸۷٦).
وفي تكملة فتح القدير: لأن الرجوع عن الإقرار باطل مفصولاً كان أو موصولاً. (نتائج الأفكار تكملة فتح القدير: ۸/۳٦٤، دار الفكر).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

Whether a divorce takes effect by allusive words rests upon the intention. If he said these words intending divorce, an irrevocable divorce takes effect; and if he did not say them intending divorce, no divorce has taken effect and the marriage stands as before. In the matter of the intention it is the husband's word that is accepted. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۲/۵۰۰، مبوب و مرتب) ۔

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states:

With such words (the allusive expressions), if there was an intention of divorce, one irrevocable divorce has taken effect; and if there was no intention of divorce, no divorce has taken effect and the woman is his wife as before. (فتاوی دار العلوم دیوبند: ۹/۳۸۱، مدلل مکمل) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
