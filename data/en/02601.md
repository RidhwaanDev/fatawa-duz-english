### TITLE
The ruling on letting the wrapper hang as a fashion

### QUESTION
A man lets his wrapper hang below his ankles and says that he does not do so out of pride but because it is the fashion, and that this is why he lets it hang. Would you now state the ruling of the Sharīʿah as to whether letting the wrapper hang as a fashion is permissible or not?

### ANSWER
The prohibition that has come in the narrations on this subject is unqualified, so both cases involve dislike, though the degree of dislike is lighter in one of them. That is, if it is done with the intention of pride it is severely disliked and unlawful, while in letting it hang as a fashion the dislike is somewhat less. Consider what is written in the notes on Sunan Ibn Mājah:

قال الشيخ عبد الغني المجددي رحمه الله: وهذا التطويل والتوسيع الذي تعارف في بعض ديار العرب من الحجاز ومصر مخالف للسنة وإسراف موجب لا إضاعة المال فما كان منهما بطريق الخيلاء فهو حرام وما كان بطريق العرف والعادة وصار شعار القوم لا يحرم وإن كان الإسراف فيه لا يخلو عن كراهة. (حاشية سنن ابن ماجه، ص: ۲۵۵، ط: قديمي).

Imdād al-Fatāwā states:

In the view of the Hanafis, in such a case the unqualified text remains unqualified and the qualified text remains qualified, and it is necessary to act upon both, as is stated explicitly in the books of legal theory. (امداد الفتاوی: ٤/۱۲۳) ۔

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

This is the dress of the proud and of the openly sinful. If it is worn with the intention of resembling them, or with the intention of pride, it is unlawful; otherwise it is disliked:

ولا يجوز الإسبال تحت الكعبين إن كان للخيلاء؛ إذ قد نص عليه الشافعي رحمه الله، وبغير الخيلاء، منع للتنزيه لا للتحريم، مرقاة. [۸/۱۲۹]

Nowadays this dress generally belongs to those who are possessed by the spirit of the West, who think their own long-standing style and manner of living something bad and take pride in Western civilisation. To resemble such people is also blameworthy. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۹/۲۷۲، جامعہ فاروقیہ) ۔
