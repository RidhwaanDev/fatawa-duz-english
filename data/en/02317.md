### TITLE
Books and notebooks left behind by students at a madrasah

### QUESTION
At the end of the year many students, both local and foreign, go home leaving their own books and notebooks behind in the classrooms, in the residence rooms and in various other places around the madrasah. Usually they have no further interest in these things, and it is quite certain that they will never come back to collect their book or notebook. Would it be correct for another student to use such books and keep them for himself, or not? بینوا توجروا

### ANSWER
In the case described, if the owner of a book is known and his name is written on it, he should be contacted and asked what he wants done with the book — the name is generally written on the book. If no name is written on it and the owner is not known, then these books come under the ruling of lost property: enquiries should be made about the owner, and if it is most likely that the owner will not be found, the books should be given to needy students on the owners' behalf. If the person who picked the book up does not himself own the nisab (the threshold amount of wealth), he may take it for himself.

The evidence on lost property has been given in detail earlier; it may be consulted there.
