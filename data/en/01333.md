### TITLE
Taking a fee for conducting a marriage

### QUESTION
Is taking a fee for conducting a marriage permissible or not?

### ANSWER
Taking a fee for conducting a marriage is permissible, so long as there is no element of compulsion in it, and the fee is fixed voluntarily and without coercion. However, if the guardian of a minor girl is himself the one conducting the marriage, it is not permissible for him to take a fee out of the minor girl's wealth. Likewise, if in a certain place there is only one person who conducts marriages, and he does not allow anyone else to conduct them and charges whatever fee he sees fit, then it is not permissible for him either to take a fee.

Consider what al-Muḥīṭ al-Burhānī states:

وفي "فتاوى النسفي" وإذا كان القاضي يتولى القسمة بنفسه حل له أخذ الأجرة وكل نكاح باشره القاضي وقد وجبت مباشرته عليه كنكاح الصغار والصغائر، فلا يحل له أخذ الأجرة عليه، وما لم تجب مباشرته عليه حل له أخذ الأجرة عليه. (المحيط البرهاني: ۸/۵۰۲، الفصل السابع عشر، دار إحياء التراث العربي).
(ومثله في تكملة رد المحتار على الدر المختار: ۷/۵۹، سعيد. والفتاوى الهندية: ۳/۳٤۵، الباب الخامس عشر).

Fatāwā Bazzāziyyah states:

ولو تولى نكاح صغير لا يحل له أخذ شيء لأنه واجب عليه وكل ما وجب عليه لا يجوز أخذ الأجر وما لا يجب عليه أخذ الأجر. (الفتاوى البزازية على هامش الهندية: ۵/۱٤۰).

Imdād al-Fatāwā states:

Just as it is correct, in view of need, to give or to take a fixed fee or a customary fee for teaching the Qur'ān, teaching fiqh, leading the prayer and calling the adhān, in the same way it is correct in the sharīʿah for the one who conducts marriages to take a fixed fee or a customary fee for explaining the occasions of prohibition and permissibility in marriage and the legally prescribed manner of the offer and acceptance, the fixing of the dower and so on, and for those who contract and arrange the marriage to pay it. Just as, for example, there is a fear that the science of fiqh would be lost if the taking and giving of a fee for teaching fiqh were stopped, in the same way there is a grave danger that, if the above-mentioned fee of the one who conducts marriages were stopped, marriages would become defective and void in the sharīʿah and other harms would arise. (امداد الفتاوی: ۲/۲۷۱)۔

Fatāwā Farīdiyyah states:

The fee of the one who conducts a marriage is a gift, كما لا يخفي علي من راجع إلى العرف, and even if it is a fee it is still permissible, لكونها أجرة على تعليم الإيجاب والقبول وتلقينها. (فتاوی فریدیہ: ٤/۳۸۲)۔

Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

It is permissible to take a fee for conducting a marriage by the mutual consent of the two parties, that is, the one who conducts it and the one who calls him. (کفایت المفتی: ۵/۱٤۸، دار الاشاعت)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
