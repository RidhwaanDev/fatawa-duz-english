### TITLE
The wisdom in the order of the Qur'an differing from the order of its revelation

### QUESTION
What is the wisdom in arranging the Qur'an in an order different from the order in which it was revealed?

### ANSWER
In the order of revelation, the Meccan surahs were sent down first, during the Meccan period, because the need for them came first in Makkah.

In Makkah what was needed was the correction of beliefs, the truthfulness of Rasūlullāh ﷺ, the truth of the Noble Qur'an, taking warning from the destruction of past nations, the condemnation of disbelief and idolatry, the good news of Paradise, the terrifying scenes of Hell, an account of the kinds of disbelief and idolatry, fear of the Day of Judgement, the importance of worship, and urging the Muslims to be patient. This is why the Meccan surahs were revealed first. Later, an Islamic state took shape in Madinah, and there the needs were fasting and prayer, jihad and mutual dealings, the importance of spending in the path of Allah, mosques and places of worship, rulings and acts of worship, the settling of disputes among people, Islamic manners and character, eagerness for the hereafter and detachment from this world, social life and morals, the condemnation of the hypocrites and of hypocrisy and an exposure of their scheming, the disciplining of the self, the management of the household, the governance of the city, the rights of husband and wife, the rights of relatives, and so on. So in Madinah the Medinan surahs, in which all these matters are set out, were revealed.

In the arrangement of the Qur'an, however, the Medinan surahs were placed first, since they are comprehensive and contain rulings of every kind.

Some Meccan surahs that are long and comprehensive, and that contain a complete refutation of Judaism and Christianity, were also placed early — Sūrah al-Aʿrāf and Sūrah al-Anʿām, for example. And since Sūrah al-Fātiḥah serves as the core text of the entire Noble Qur'an, it was placed at the very beginning.
