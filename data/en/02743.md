### TITLE
Removing a misunderstanding

### QUESTION
Removing a misunderstanding.

### ANSWER
بسم اللہ الرحمن الرحیم

A certain Mawlānā of our area, District Swabi, Pakistan, needed material on the subject of the feast held for a circumcision. This humble servant told him that whatever material we had on this question for Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā I would, God willing, pass on to him.

The answer and the detailed article that we had prepared for volume seven of Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā — that typed article is what I placed before him. Some time later a certain Mufti Ṣāḥib compiled a treatise under the name دعوتِ ختنہ کی شرعی حیثیت (The legal status of the feast held for a circumcision) and took our entire article into that treatise. He then had various muftis write commendations for the treatise — among them the head mufti of Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband, Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ḥabīb al-Raḥmān Khayrābādī — and included them in it. Honesty required that our article be printed under our name, but he did not do so.

The purpose of troubling the reader with all this is that no one should suppose this article of ours, or this question and answer, to be scholarly theft. The question had appeared earlier in Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā in brief, but because of the objections raised by some people a detailed treatment was called for, and it was written. The mistake we made in the matter was that, before volume seven went to press, we handed over to others the detailed article that was ready for printing.

فإلى الله المشتكى ۔

Written by: (Ḥaḍrat Muftī) Riḍāʾ al-Ḥaqq, may Allah pardon him.
