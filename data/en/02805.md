### TITLE
The suitable and good meanings of the name Ḥudhayfah

### QUESTION
A man wants to name his son Ḥudhayfah and asks for a sound meaning of the name. What are its good and suitable meanings?

### ANSWER
Ḥudhayfah comes from ḥadhaf, which means a small black goat. Since a goat is meek and lowly, one meaning of Ḥudhayfah is a person of gentle temperament and humility; it also means trimmed, put right and excellent.

(2) Ḥudhayfah also carries the meaning of a piece of cloth, that is, a humble servant.

(3) One meaning of ḥadhaf is a duck, and ducks are white, so the name would mean one whose outward and inward are bright and white. The diminutive in names is used to express affection and gentleness, کما مر.

Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ states:

الحذف غنم سود جرد صغار ليس لها آذان ولا أذناب، وضرب من البط صغار. (المعجم الوسيط، ص ۱٦۳، ط: ديوبند).

Al-Ṣiḥāḥ states:

والحذف غنم سود صغار من غنم الحجاز. (الصحاح: ۳/۱۱۰۸، ط: إحياء التراث العربي).

قال في لسان العرب: الحذفة: القطعة من الثوب. (لسان العرب: ۹/٤۰، دار الفكر).

In sum, Ḥudhayfah has several good and suitable meanings: humble; put right and cleansed; white and bright. In my view the most suitable and best meaning is trimmed, corrected and well-groomed, since one of the meanings of ḥadhf is the trimming of hair.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
