### TITLE
A pilgrim performing tamattuʿ entering iḥrām for Hajj from Minā

### QUESTION
A man performed tamattuʿ Hajj, and after completing the Umrah he entered iḥrām for the Hajj from Minā during the days of Hajj and performed the Hajj. Was his iḥrām from Minā valid or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question, after finishing the Umrah the man mentioned was resident in Makkah and so comes under the ruling of the people of Makkah. For the people of Makkah the mīqāt for the iḥrām of Hajj is the whole of the Ḥaram, and Minā lies within the sacred Ḥaram. On this basis his iḥrām is valid and his Hajj was valid too. It is better, however, to enter iḥrām for the Hajj from al-Masjid al-Ḥarām.

See what Sharḥ Lubāb al-Manāsik states:

فصل في الصنف الثالث وهم من كان منزله في الحرم، كسكان مكة ومني فوقته الحرم للحج ومن المسجد أفضل أو من دويرة أهله

وكذلك أي مثل حكم أهل الحرم كل من دخل الحرم من غير أهله وإن لم ينو الإقامة به كالمفرد بالعمرة والمتمتع أي من أهل الآفاق والحلال أي وكغير المحرم من أهل الحل إذا دخله أي الحرم لحاجة أي غير إرادة للنسك. (لباب المناسك مع شرحه، ص۹۳).

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh states:

By "the people of the Ḥaram" are meant those who live inside the boundaries of the Ḥaram, whether they are its permanent residents or have come from elsewhere, and whether they are resident there or travelling. So for those who live in Makkah al-Muʿaẓẓamah, or anywhere else inside the boundaries of the Ḥaram such as Minā, the mīqāt for the Hajj is the whole of the land inside the boundaries of the Ḥaram, and they may enter iḥrām from wherever in it they wish. It is best to enter iḥrām from al-Masjid al-Ḥarām, the mosque of the Sacred House of Allah, and after that it is best for them to enter iḥrām from their own homes. A person from outside the sacred precincts who is performing tamattuʿ Hajj also comes under the ruling of the people of the Ḥaram. (عمدۃ الفقہ: ٤/۹٦ ۔ ۹۸، اہل حرم کا میقات) ۔

And Minā is three to three and a half miles from Makkah al-Mukarramah. (عمدۃ الفقہ: ٤/٤۳۸) ۔

Zubdat al-Manāsik states: Minā is one farsakh from Makkah al-Muʿaẓẓamah. (زبدہ، ص۱۵۵) ۔

A farsakh is three legal miles, which comes to about 5.4864 kilometres. And Minā lies inside the boundaries of the Ḥaram.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
