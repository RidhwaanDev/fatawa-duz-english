### TITLE
Giving zakat to the infant daughter of a wealthy man

### QUESTION
A wealthy man who owns the nisāb (the threshold of wealth at which zakat becomes due) has a small daughter, two months old, who is in hospital because of a hole in her heart. This man is not in a position to bear the cost. Can zakat be given to her for the hospital bills and so on, or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question it is not correct to give zakat money to the daughter of a wealthy man who owns the nisāb. It may, however, be given to her mother, who in all likelihood does not own the nisāb herself. If she does own some jewellery, she should gift it to her husband and then take the zakat and spend it on her daughter; or she may first spend the money she has on the daughter and then take zakat.

Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ states:

وأما ولد الغني فإن كان صغيراً لم يجز الدفع إليه وإن كان فقيراً لا مال له، لأن الولد الصغير يعد غنياً بغني أبيه وإن كان كبيراً فقيراً يجوز، لأنه لا يعد غنياً بمال أبيه فكان كالأجنبي ولو دفع إلى امرأة فقيرة وزوجها غني جاز ۔ (بدائع الصنائع: ۲/٤۷، سعيد ۔ وكذا في الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱۸۹، المصارف) ۔

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm states:

It is not correct to give zakat or other obligatory charities to the needy minor children of a wealthy person; zakat is not discharged by doing so. (فتاوی دار العلوم: ۶/۲۱۲، مصارفِ زکوٰۃ، مدلل و مکمل) ۔
