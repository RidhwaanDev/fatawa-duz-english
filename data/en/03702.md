### TITLE
The ruling on the seller taking rent from the buyer until the legal formalities of the sale are completed

### QUESTION
A person bought a house. At the seller's request the buyer transferred the entire price into the agent's account. The agent said to the buyer: you may live in the house, but until the legal formalities are completed you will have to pay rent for the house. Is it permissible in the Sharīʿah to take this rent or not? The agent has also advised that your money will be placed in the bank and that the rent will be paid out of the interest that accrues. Is this arrangement permissible? Explain with the evidence and be rewarded with an abundant reward.

### ANSWER
In the case in question, once the contract was concluded and the buyer paid the price, the buyer became the owner of the house, and after that it is not permissible to take rent from the buyer. In the Sharīʿah the completion of a sale does not rest upon legal documents; without them the sale is completed by offer and acceptance. Nor is it permissible for the seller's agent to place the money in an interest-bearing account and obtain interest. If it is not appropriate to leave the interest with the bank, then the seller or his agent should distribute the interest money among the poor without the intention of earning reward. But if, in spite of the buyer's forbidding it, the agent takes the interest on his own initiative and takes the rent, then the agent himself is answerable for it. Consider the evidences:

قال في الهداية: وإذا حصل الإيجاب والقبول لزم البيع ولا خيار لواحد منهما إلا من عيب أو عدم رؤية. (الهداية: ۳/۲۰).

It is recorded in Majmaʿ al-Anhur:

وإذا وجد الإيجاب والقبول من المتعاقدين لزم البيع وفيه إشارة إلى أن البيع يتم بهما ولا يحتاج إلى القبض ولا إلى إجازة البائع بعدهما وهو الصحيح بلا خيار مجلس إلا من عيب أو عدم رؤية. (مجمع الأنهر: ۳/۱۱، دار الكتب العلمية، بيروت).

وقال في الشامية: لو مات الرجل وكسبه من بيع الباذق أو الظلم أو أخذ الرشوة يتورع الورثة ولا يأخذون منه شيئاً وهو أولى بهم ويردونها على أربابها إن عرفوهم وإلا تصدقوا بها لأن سبيل الكسب الخبيث التصدق إذا تعذر الرد على صاحبه. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۳۸۵).

It is recorded in Niẓām al-Fatāwā:

The basic rule is that with regard to any unlawful wealth the primary ruling is that it be returned by some means into the ownership of the one whose wealth it is; and when it cannot be conveyed into the ownership of the original owner, then, with the intention of saving oneself from its evil consequence, one should give it as charity to someone entitled to charity and so put it out of one's own ownership as quickly as possible. One should not use it oneself for any purpose, nor, after giving it in charity, should one make the intention of earning reward for it. (Niẓām al-Fatāwā: 1/185).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
