### TITLE
Whether zakat is due on diamonds and precious stones

### QUESTION
If I buy a diamond or a precious stone intending that, when I am in need, I will sell it and meet my need from it — because money and currency are not dependable, while diamonds and precious stones hold a good value — is zakat due on it or not?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, since the diamonds and precious stones were not bought for the purpose of trade but in order to meet a need, zakat is not necessary on them.

Consider what al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

لا زكاة في اللآلي والجواهر كاللؤلؤ والياقوت والزمرد وأمثالها، درر عن الكافي، وإن ساوت ألفاً اتفاقاً، في نسخة ألوفاً، إلا أن تكون للتجارة، والأصل أن ما عدا الحجرين (أي الذهب والفضة) إنما يزكي بنية التجارة

وشرط مقارنتها لعقد التجارة وهو كسب المال بالمال بعقد شراء أو إجارة أو استقراض ولو نوى التجارة بعد العقد أو اشتري شيئاً للقنية ناوياً أنه إن وجد ربحاً باعه، لا زكاة عليه. (الدر المختار مع الشامي: ۲/۲۷۳، سعيد).

Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

وكذا لا (زكاة في) الجوهر واللؤلؤ والياقوت والبلخش والزمرد ونحوها إذا لم يكن للتجارة. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱۷۲، كتاب الزكاة).

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

As a matter of principle, the sharia has not made zakat necessary on any mineral other than gold and silver. On this principle, zakat is not necessary on diamonds and precious stones, unless they have been bought for the purpose of trade. (جدید فقہی مسائل: ۱/۲۰۷، نعیمیہ) ۔

Jadīd Fiqhī Mabāḥith states:

The jurists have stated plainly that, apart from the two currencies and grazing livestock, zakat becomes necessary on trade goods and the like only when they are held as merchandise. Indeed, the Hanafi jurists have stated explicitly that if diamonds and precious stones are not held for trade, no zakat is necessary on them, however many thousands of rupees they may be worth. So in the case described, too, no zakat is necessary in the view of the Hanafis, whether those diamonds and precious stones were kept as a store of wealth or for ornament and display. And if a person keeps his capital in the form of diamonds and precious stones for some purpose other than escaping zakat, he will not be called to account for it before Allah either. (جدید فقہی مباحث: ۷/۵۰٤، و ٦/۸۹) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
