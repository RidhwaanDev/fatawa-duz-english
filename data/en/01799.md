### TITLE
Demolishing an endowed building and rebuilding it

### QUESTION
A man endowed a piece of land on which there was a building. Is it now valid and correct to pull that building down and put up another building without the endower's permission, or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, once the land has been handed over to the administrator, and the administrator makes a change in it or puts up another building because it is in the interest of the endowment, this is valid and correct. After the building is pulled down, its bricks and materials will go into the other building, and the ongoing charity will remain in force.

Furthermore, pulling the building down does not bring the ongoing charity to an end, just as wiping off the water of ablution does not bring the reward of the ablution to an end. And when the mosque built by the Prophet ﷺ was rebuilt from scratch in the time of ʿUthmān (may Allah be pleased with him), no one raised the objection that the reward of those who had built it would come to an end; rather that reward stood recorded. If dust settles on someone in the path of Allah, the fire of Hell will not touch him.

"من اغبرت قدماه في سبيل اللّه لم تمسه النار". (رواه الترمذي: ۱/۲۹۲).

But this does not mean that he should not wash his feet. That reward is engraved in the record of deeds, even if the thing itself no longer remains.
