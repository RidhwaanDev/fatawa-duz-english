### TITLE
The ruling on using Facebook

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to use Facebook or not, seeing that it contains the following things:

٭ Men and women conversing with one another.

٭ Pictures of every kind.

٭ It also contains video and music.

٭ In some places people gather and hold debates and discussions about Islam — whether Islam is worthy of being accepted.

Along with all these evils, in 2010 a page was set up on it in which people were invited to draw a picture of the Prophet ﷺ (we seek refuge with Allah), on account of which Pakistan and other Islamic countries declared it banned, and because of this the Facebook people removed that page. بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
There are various ways of using Facebook:

(1) That one uses Facebook at the time of need and to the extent of the need — for instance, for the identification of criminals and on other occasions of necessity. In that case there is room for it, provided one keeps away from the forbidden things. For example, abstaining from music and from looking at pictures of non-maḥram women and the like is necessary.

(2) The second is that it be used for unlawful relationships, or that there be a danger of unlawful relationships, or that it be for taking pleasure in the picture of a non-maḥram. Use of this kind is forbidden and impermissible, and keeping away from it is necessary.

Even where there is only an apprehension of wrongful relationships it is still forbidden, for the sense of the noble hadith is that whoever grazes his cattle around a state pasture, there is a strong likelihood that the animals will enter the state pasture and a fine will be imposed or the cattle confiscated. Moreover, whatever act becomes a means to an unlawful end must be avoided.

Since Facebook consists of music, pictures of every kind, and debates, discussions and objections concerning Islam and such like, it is necessary to keep away from using it. Further, from their audacity in drawing a picture of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ the intention of the Facebook people has also become plain — that they wish to wipe out Islamic beliefs and Islamic principles.

(3) The third is that a person does not use it for any forbidden and impermissible purpose, nor does it become a means to what is forbidden — as when someone looks at the face of his daughter, his sister or his mother on Facebook, or friends look at one another's faces, and there are no beardless youths involved. In this case its use will not be impermissible. Even so, its use is not fitting, because once the habit is formed there is every likelihood of the limits being overstepped.

This is the ruling on that Facebook which contains music and pictures; if these things are not present, then its use is correct.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
