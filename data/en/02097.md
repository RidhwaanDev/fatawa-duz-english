### TITLE
Recovering one's debt from an insurance company

### QUESTION
A man borrowed money from someone and is now unable to repay it, and he has since died; but he had life insurance. Can the creditor recover his debt from the insurance company or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, the creditor should recover his debt from the deceased's original capital, and the further interest money should be given away in charity to the poor and the needy with no intention of reward; it is not permissible or correct to put it to his own use. If the original capital does not cover the debt in full and the deceased left no other wealth, then he may take the interest money towards his debt after carrying out the device of transferring ownership.

Consider what Kitāb al-Fatāwā states:

Taking out insurance is not permissible. Of the money the insurance company pays out, the portion that the policy holder himself paid in is lawful; the amount over and above that falls under interest, and it is necessary to spend it on the poor with no intention of reward. (کتاب الفتاوی: ۵/۳٦۰) ۔

Radd al-Muḥtār states:

والحاصل أنه إن علم أرباب الأموال، وجب رده عليهم، وإلا فإن علم عين الحرام لا يحل له، ويتصدق به بنية صاحبه. (رد المحتار: ۵/۹۹، مطلب فيمن ورث مالاً حراماً، سعيد).

Consider what Niẓām al-Fatāwā states:

After the device of making a deserving person its owner, it will be correct to use it even for cleaning the lavatories of a mosque and the like. The device of transferring ownership is that the amount which is interest is handed to poor and needy people who deserve charity, and they are made owners of it; that deserving recipient then gives it to the mosque as a donation of his own.

وقدمنا أن الحيلة أن يتصدق على الفقير، ثم يأمره بفعل هذه الأشياء. الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ۲/۳٤۵، كتاب الزكاة، باب المصرف، سعيد. (نظام الفتاوى: ۱/۱۹۹).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
