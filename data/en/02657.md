### TITLE
Travelling with a boy who has reached the age of discernment

### QUESTION
Can a boy who has reached the age of discernment act as a mahram for a woman or not? That is, will it be permissible to travel with him or not?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, a discerning boy who is not yet close to puberty and is not mature cannot act as a mahram for a woman, so travelling with him is not permissible. Yes, if he is close to puberty and mature — that is, about 12 years of age — he can act as a mahram. See what al-Jawharah states:

والصبي والمجنون ليسا بمحرم والمراهق كالبالغ. (الجوهرة النيرة: ۱/۱۸٤، ط: إمداديه).
(وكذا في حاشية الطحطاوي على الدر المختار: ۱/٤۸٤، وفتاوى الشامي: ۲/٤٦٤، سعيد).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

Travelling with a child under twelve years of age is impermissible by agreement; after twelve there is a difference of opinion as to whether it is permissible. So if a boy of twelve is mature and, in body and mind, appears to be like an adult, there is room to travel with him. (احسن الفتاوی: ۸/۳۰).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
