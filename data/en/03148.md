### TITLE
Who will lead the funeral prayer of Ḥaḍrat Mahdī (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhu)?

### QUESTION
Some people say that Ḥaḍrat ʿĪsā (ʿalayhis salām) will lead the funeral prayer of Ḥaḍrat Mahdī (raḍiyallāhu taʿālā ʿanhu). Is this correct or not? And is an explicit statement of this, or an indication towards it, present in any noble hadith or not? Kindly explain and be rewarded.

### ANSWER
Despite turning the pages of the books of hadith, it has not been found in any hadith that Ḥaḍrat ʿĪsā (upon our Prophet and upon him be blessings and peace) will lead the funeral prayer of Ḥaḍrat Mahdī (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhu). Yes, in a narration of Abū Dāwūd only this much is mentioned, that the Muslims will pray his funeral prayer. However, it is related from some scholars that Ḥaḍrat ʿĪsā (ʿalayhis salām) will lead his funeral prayer. Consider the narration of Abū Dāwūd:

عن أم سلمة رضي الله عنه زوج النبي ﷺ عن النبي ﷺ قال: يكون اختلاف عند موت خليفة فيخرج رجل من أهل المدينة هارباً إلى مكة فيأتيه ناس من أهل مكة

فيلبث سبع سنين ثم يتوفى ويصلي عليه المسلمون. قال أبو داود: قال بعضهم عن هشام "تسع سنين" وقال بعضهم: "سبع سنين". (رقم الحديث، ٤۲۸٦).

But Shaykh Shuʿayb al-Arnāʾūṭ has written in his notes that this narration is weak.

Shaykh Marʿī ibn Yūsuf al-Maqdisī al-Ḥanbalī writes in his book "فرائد فوائد الفكر":

قال: ذكر العلماء أن المهدي يستمر مع عيسى عليه السلام إلى بيت المقدس، فيموت بها، ويصلي عليه هو ومن معه من المسلمين. (فرائد فوائد الفكر في الامام المهدي المنتظر، ص ۳۳۳).

Ḥaḍrat Shāh Rafīʿ al-Dīn Dihlawī (raḥimahullāh) writes in Āthār-i Qiyāmat:

It should be clear that Ḥaḍrat Mahdī (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhu) will spend seven years in crushing the trial of the Christians and establishing justice and equity in the land; the eighth year will pass in war and combat with the Dajjāl, and the ninth year will pass with Ḥaḍrat ʿĪsā (ʿalayhis salām). By this reckoning his age will be forty-nine years. After this Ḥaḍrat Mahdī (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhu) will pass away; Ḥaḍrat ʿĪsā (ʿalayhis salām) will lead his funeral prayer and will lower him into the grave. (آثارِ قیامت، ص ۳٤)۔

On the authority of Ḥaḍrat Shāh Rafīʿ al-Dīn Ṣāḥib it is recorded in Fatāwā Ḥaqqāniyyah as well. Consider. (فتاوی حقانیہ: ۱/۳۰٤)۔
