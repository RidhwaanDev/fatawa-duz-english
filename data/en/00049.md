### TITLE
Keeping relations with an apostate

### QUESTION
If a person becomes an apostate (العیاذ باللہ), what is the ruling on keeping relations with him?

### ANSWER
If a person becomes an apostate (العیاذ باللہ) and has no wish to return to Islam, the ruling on him is that of disbelief, and keeping relations with him is like keeping relations with a disbeliever. The Noble Qur'an forbids heartfelt friendship with a disbeliever. About the apostate the Noble Qur'an says:

﴿ومن يرتدد منكم عن دينه فيمت وهو كافر﴾ (سورة البقرة: ۲۱۷).

﴿لا يتخذ المؤمنون الكافرين أولياء من دون المؤمنين﴾ (سورة آل عمران: ۲۸).

Tafsīr Maẓharī states:

That is, the believers must not take disbelievers as friends in place of the believers. Believers are forbidden close attachment to disbelievers, whether it takes the form of kinship, or friendship, or seeking their help in fighting and in religious matters — all of it is forbidden by the words ﴿من دون المؤمنين﴾. (تفسير مظهري: ۲/ ۲۱۳).

Maʿārif al-Qurʾān states:

Close attachment to disbelievers, that is, loving them, takes three forms. The first is to hold them dear on account of their religion; this is plainly disbelief. The second is to regard their creed and religion as evil in one's heart while dealing with them courteously in worldly affairs; this is permissible by consensus, and to a degree even commendable. The third form lies between the two: to regard their religion as evil in one's heart, yet to keep friendly relations with them out of kinship, friendship or some worldly interest, to assist and support them, or at times to spy on the Muslims for them. This form is not disbelief, but it is a serious sin. (معارف القرآن: ۱/ ۵۹۵، از مولانا ادریس کاندھلوی رحمہ اللہ).

Since the apostate is a disbeliever, to be attached to him in one's heart and to be pleased with his apostasy is outright disbelief, and there is no room for it whatsoever. Keeping contact with him so that he may return to the religion, however, is rewarding and commendable.

Jāmiʿ al-Rumūz states:

(قوله هذا عند أبي حنيفة رحمه الله)؛ اعلم أن تصرفات المرتد يتوقف في الكسبين جميعاً وهو الصحيح وقال بعض المشايخ إن تصرفه في كسب الردة نافذ في ظاهر الرواية وموقوف في رواية الحسن والأول أصح، وهذا كله عند الامام، وأما عندهما فتصرفاته نافذة في الكسبين. (قوله وعندهما) والخلاف بينهم في تصرفات وقعت قبل اللحاق، وأما بعده قبل الحكم فهي موقوفة بالإجماع كولايته على أولاده الصغار. (فتح المعين: ۲/ ٤٦٤، وجامع الرموز: ٤/ ۵۸۵).

قال في الدر المختار: ويتوقف منه عند الإمام وينفذ عندهما كل ما كان مبادلة مال أو عقد تبرع كالمبايعة والصرف والسلم والرهن والإجارة والصلح عن إقرار وقبض الدين لأنه مبادلة حكمية. (الدر المختار: ٤/ ۲۵۰، سعيد).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

All the various groups of the Shia, the Qādiyānīs, the Dhikrīs, the deniers of hadith and the Anjuman-i Dīndārān are heretics, and the rulings that apply to them are even harsher than those for other disbelievers, indeed than those for apostates. Buying and selling with them and every other kind of dealing is impermissible, and keeping friendly relations with them and treating them affectionately runs against the honour of faith. It is obligatory to avoid every kind of dealing with them as far as one possibly can. If someone does enter into a transaction with them, such as a sale or a hire contract, it is not validly concluded; according to the two companions of Abū Ḥanīfah, however, it takes effect despite being impermissible, and in a time of widespread hardship and pressing need there is room to act on that view. (احسن الفتاوی: ۸/ ۲۵۰).

It emerges from this that, despite the impermissibility, the contract takes effect in the view of the two companions, and in a time of pressing need there is room to act on their view. Since this country (South Africa) is not a land of Islam and there is no sharia law in force for the apostate, a transaction concluded with him will be valid according to the view of the two companions (may Allah have mercy on them). Even so, because it runs against the honour of faith, one should not deal with him. If the apostate's family members are disbelievers, though, one may deal with them.

واللہ اعلم۔
