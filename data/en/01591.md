### TITLE
The ruling on an annulment effected by a non-Muslim judge

### QUESTION
In America a woman went to court and filed a divorce suit, seeking a decree of divorce through the court, as a result of which the court decreed a divorce against the husband. Will this divorce decree take effect or not, given that the husband does not consent to it?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, the decision of a non-Muslim judge concerning divorce or the separation of spouses is not valid; this woman therefore remains, as before, in that man's marriage. Although the marriage has come to an end in the government's register, the Islamic marriage remains in force.

Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ states:

الصلاحية للقضاء لها شرائط منها العقل ومنها البلوغ ومنها الإسلام ومنها الحرية ومنها البصر ومنها السلامة عن حد القذف. (بدائع الصنائع: ۷/۳، كتاب أدب القضاء، ط: سعيد).

Al-Ḥīlah al-Nājizah states:

In places where there is no sharia judge, those officials — judges, magistrates and the like — who are empowered by the government to decide such matters may, if they are Muslims and decide in accordance with the rules of the sharia, have their ruling stand in the place of a qāḍī's judgement. But if in some place the deciding official is a non-Muslim, his decision is entirely invalid; an annulment or the like can never be effected by his ruling. "لان الکافر لیس باہل للقضاء علی المسلم کما ہو مصرح فی جمیع کتب الفقہ".
(الحيلة الناجزة: ۲۳، مقدمه، دار الاشاعت، ديوبند).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

The decision of a non-Muslim magistrate (judge) is not valid in the sharia. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۸/۳۷۷) ۔

Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

The decision of a non-Muslim official is not sufficient. (کفایت المفتی: ٦/۱۳۲، دار الاشاعت) ۔

Īḍāḥ al-Nawādir states:

If a non-Muslim judge gives a decision concerning divorce or the like, his decision will not be sound or valid in the sharia; on the strength of such a decision the wife will not obtain her freedom in the matter of divorce. ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) transmitted this ruling in these words:
"لم ینفذ حکم الکافر علی المسلم وینفذ للمسلم علی الذمي" ۔ (ایضاح النوادر: ۱۵۲) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
