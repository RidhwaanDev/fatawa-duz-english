### TITLE
Medical experiments on animals

### QUESTION
Are medical experiments on animals permissible or not?

### ANSWER
Allah brought all the creatures of the earth — minerals, plants and animals — into being for the benefit of man. On that basis it is permissible and correct for man to use them and to draw benefit from them by cutting and trimming them, by pounding and grinding them, and even by burning them and breaking them down by chemical means. In the same way medical experiments on animals are permissible, since this too is a kind of benefit.

قال الله تعالى: ﴿هو الذي خلق لكم ما في الأرض جميعاً﴾ [البقرة: ۲۹].

Allah has mentioned the benefits of animals in the Qur'an as a favour and a kindness he has shown:

قال الله تعالى: ﴿والله جعل لكم من بيوتكم سكناً وجعل لكم من جلود الأنعام بيوتاً تستخفونها يوم ظعنكم ويوم إقامتكم، ومن أصوافها وأوبارها وأشعارها أثاثاً ومتاعاً إلى حين﴾ [النحل: ۸۰].

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

Animals often have to be used to test the effects and benefits of various medicines. First, germs are introduced into their bodies to produce the disease; then the medicines that might prove useful against those diseases are tried on them. Such practices are permissible. There is no doubt that Islam has not allowed animals to be tormented needlessly, nor allowed the watching of such torment to be made a form of entertainment. But on the other hand it has also set out the principle that everything in the universe is a servant to man; and that is why we are allowed to ride animals, to eat their flesh as food, to wear their hides as clothing, and to graft a part of an animal into a human body for the sake of a person's health. In the cases mentioned there is likewise no entertainment and no purposeless torment; the real intention is to take service and benefit from them for a genuine and unavoidable human need. There is therefore nothing objectionable in it. (جدید فقہی مسائل: ۱/۳۳۷)۔

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

Allah created animals for the benefit of man, so there is no harm if some pain is caused to them in the course of benefiting from them. That is why it is permissible by agreement to castrate an animal in order to improve its meat; the Prophet ﷺ sacrificed castrated rams. The pain of castration is far greater than that of an injection. (احسن الفتاوی: ۸/۲۲۳)۔

Yes, if the animals suffer pain, they should be given such medicines as will lessen the pain and give them ease and comfort.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
