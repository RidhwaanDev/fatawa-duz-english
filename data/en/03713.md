### TITLE
The ruling on making software and a website for a company

### QUESTION
There is an IT company which makes software and websites for various companies. It has now received an order from a company that manufactures televisions; so will it be permissible for this company to make the software and website of the television company or not? Likewise, what is the ruling on making software and websites for a beauty parlour, a photo shop and a barber? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
To make software and a website for companies whose business is permissible and lawful is permissible and correct, and the income from it is also lawful. Yes, as for those companies that are active in unlawful business or in sinful activities, one should avoid making their websites and software, since in this there is found a kind of assistance in sinful work; nevertheless, if someone did make them, the income would not be unlawful but would be disliked.

Likewise, if the predominant business of a beauty parlour and a barber is carried on within the limits of the Sharīʿah, then it is fine; otherwise it will be disliked. But in beauty parlours the objectionable features are many, so one should avoid this. Moreover, in photo shops pictures of animate beings are generally made and beautified; making software for people with such a business is disliked, and one should avoid this too. Consider what is mentioned in Fatāwā Shāmī:

قوله معزياً للنهر قال فيه من باب البغاة وعلم من هذا أنه لا يكره بيع ما لم تقم المعصية به كبيع الجارية المغنية والكبش النطوح والحمامة الطيارة والعصير والخشب الذي يتخذ منه العازف، وما في بيوع الخانية من أنه يكره بيع الأمرد من فاسق يعلم أنه يعصي به مشكل. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۳۹۱، سعيد).

It is mentioned in Jawāhir al-Fiqh:

ثم السبب إن لم يكن محركاً وداعياً، بل موصلاً محضاً، وهو مع ذلك سبب قريب بحيث لا يحتاج في إقامة المعصية به إلى إحداث صنعة من الفاعل، كبيع السلاح من أهل الفتنة وبيع العصير ممن يتخذه خمراً

فكله مكروه تحريماً بشرط أن يعلم به البائع والآجر من دون تصريح به باللسان، فإنه إن لم يعلم كان معذوراً. (جواهر الفقه: ۲/٤۵۲).

In the annotations to Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah it is written that if it becomes known with certainty concerning some person that he will use the item only in sin, then selling it to such a person is disliked. (Taʿlīqāt-i Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 16/129, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

It is in Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥal:

Women are permitted to adorn and beautify themselves, provided it is within the prescribed limits; but in the profession of beauty parlours as it is practised in the present age there are numerous objectionable features on account of which this profession is unlawful. Those objectionable features, in brief, are these:

1. In some places men carry out this work, and this is sheer shamelessness.
2. Such women go about displaying their beauty in the markets, and this too is shamelessness.
3. After coming from the beauty parlours it becomes difficult to distinguish between man and woman and between boy and girl, whereas a man's resembling women and a woman's resembling men is a cause of the curse of Allah. (Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥal: 8/325).

For further detail, see: (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā: 7/373).

قال في البحر الرائق: وظاهر كلام النووي في شرح مسلم الإجماع على تحريم تصوير صورة الحيوان حرام شديد التحريم وهو من الكبائر لأنه متوعد عليه بهذا الوعيد الشديد المذكور في الأحاديث يعني مثل ما في الصحيحين عنه ﷺ، أشد الناس عذاباً يوم القيمة المصورون يقال لهم أحيوا ما خلقتم. (البحر الرائق: ۲/۲۹، دار الكتاب الإسلامي).

For further detail, see: (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā, volume seven).
