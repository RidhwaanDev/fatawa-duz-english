### TITLE
The ruling on women wearing brief clothing

### QUESTION
It is generally the custom among us that women wear very brief clothing when going to sleep, and use it during the day as well. Is it permissible for a Muslim woman to use this kind of clothing in the house or not?

### ANSWER
A few necessary principles concerning a woman's dress in the house are set out below.

(1) In the house it is not necessary for a wife to conceal any part of her body from her husband; before her husband she may wear whatever clothing she wishes, by night or by day. Even so, it is not fitting to wear immodest clothing.

(2) It is correct for a woman to leave the places of adornment — the face, the hands and so forth — uncovered before her unmarriageable male relatives. She may also uncover as much of the body as needs to be uncovered while doing the housework and moving about.

(3) Before those relatives who are not unmarriageable but who live in the same house, where the constant coming and going makes veiling the face at all times difficult, it is correct to leave the face uncovered. The rest of the body must be covered. Thus it is established that Umm Hāniʾ, the paternal cousin of the Prophet ﷺ, went to him on the day of the conquest of Makkah and spoke with him, and there is no mention that Umm Hāniʾ veiled her face. Yes, if there is fear of temptation then veiling the face is also necessary; and being alone with, or mixing with, relatives who are not unmarriageable is forbidden.

(4) Before strangers it is binding and necessary to cover the whole body, so much so that it is not permissible even to leave the face uncovered without need.

Imām Abū Bakr al-Jaṣṣāṣ says in Aḥkām al-Qurʾān:

قوله تعالى: ﴿ولا يبدين زينتهن إلا لبعولتهن﴾ الآية، قال أبو بكر: ظاهره يقتضي إباحة إبداء الزينة للزوج ولمن ذكر معه من الآباء وغيرهم ومعلوم أن المراد موضع الزينة وهو الوجه واليد والذراع لان فيها السوار والقلب والعضد وهو موضع الدملج والنحر والصدر موضع القلادة والساق موضع الخلخال فاقتضي ذلك إباحة النظر للمذكورين في الآية إلى هذه المواضع وهي مواضع الزينة الباطنة لأنه خص في أول الآية إباحة الزينة الظاهرة للأجنبيين وأباح للزوج وذوي المحارم النظر إلى الزينة الباطنة ۔ (أحكام القرآن: ۳/۳۱٦، ط: سهيل) ۔

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

(وينظر الرجل) (ومن عرسه وأمته) (إلى فرجها) بشهوة وغيرها والأولى تركه لأنه يورث النسيان (ومن محرمه) هي من لا يحل له نكاحها أبداً بنسب أو سبب ولو بزنا (إلى الرأس والوجه والصدر والساق والعضد إن أمن شهوته) وشهوتها أيضاً وإلا لا، لا إلى الظهر والبطن وينظر من الأجنبية ولو كافرة إلى وجهها وكفيها فقط للضرورة ۔

وفي رد المحتار: قوله ومن عرسه وأمته، فينظر الرجل منهما وبالعكس إلى جميع البدن من الفرق إلى القدم ولو عن شهوة لان النظر دون الوطء الحلال ۔ (الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ٦/۳٦٦، سعيد) ۔

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

If the arm becomes uncovered to the elbow or above it before an unmarriageable relative such as a father or a brother, there is no blame for that; but complete concealment from those who are not unmarriageable is binding. (فتاویٰ محمودیہ: ۱۹/۳۱۲، جامعہ فاروقیہ) ۔

Āp ke Masāʾil aur un kā Ḥal states:

If she lives in her parents' house and there is no marriageable man there, it is correct to wear a kurta with half sleeves. (آپ کے مسائل اور ان کا حل: ۸/۳۷۱) ۔

Women are further permitted to use different kinds of clothing according to the different seasons; the sacred Sharīʿah has not imposed any restriction of a particular dress or cut. It has, however, laid down certain limits for women's dress in ordinary circumstances, and it is not correct to go beyond them. They are as follows:

(1) The clothing must not be so tight or so fine that the shape and form of the body become apparent.

(2) There must be no resemblance to non-Muslims and to the openly sinful.

(3) There must be no resemblance to men's dress.

(4) Pride and vanity, or notoriety and display, must not be the object.

(5) There must be no extravagance or waste. See: (آپ کے مسائل اور ان کا حل: ۸/۳٦۹، طبع جدید) ۔

In sum, a woman may wear brief clothing in her house before her husband; but when she goes outside it will be necessary to cover the whole body. Even so, it is fitting — indeed necessary — for a righteous Muslim woman to pass her life in complete modesty, shame, chastity and purity, and to avoid resembling unbelieving and sinful women.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
