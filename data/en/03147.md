### TITLE
The identification of the Dajjāl in the hadith of the Jassāsah, and the six kinds of creatures

### QUESTION
In the hadith of Ḥaḍrat Tamīm al-Dārī (raḍiyallāhu taʿālā ʿanhu) there is mention of the Dajjāl being imprisoned on some island, and the Jassāsah is with him. Nowadays the world has become like a single neighbourhood, so if the Dajjāl is on some island, why is he not seen? And what sort of thing is the Jassāsah? Kindly explain and be rewarded.

### ANSWER
The answer to the above question is mentioned in the following useful preface, under number 5, "the Dajjāl":

This humble servant's view is that Allah Most High has created six kinds of creatures: three are simple and three are composite. The simple ones are these: 1. human beings, 2. angels, 3. jinn. In the light of the hadith, human beings were created from clay, the angels from light and the jinn from fire. The three kinds which I understand to be like an intermediate state are these:

4. Ḥaḍrat Khiḍr (ʿalayhis salām): Ḥaḍrat Khiḍr (ʿalayhis salām) is an angel, a celestial being, but is like a human being. Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ḥabībullāh Aḥmad Kīrānwī (raḥimahullāh) adopted this very view in Ḥall al-Qurʾān, and this exegesis was examined by Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ashraf ʿAlī Thānwī (raḥimahullāh), who wrote an endorsement of it. (حل القرآن: ۲/۲)۔

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ḥabīb Aḥmad Kīrānwī (raḥimahullāh) writes: "Now a few things have to be stated concerning this. First, that the name of this personage is given in the hadith as Khiḍr; but there is no explicit statement, neither in the Qur'an nor in the hadith, that he was a human being or an angel. Therefore it is not at all far-fetched that the Real One sent some angel in the form of a man, and matters of the cosmic order do pertain to the angels." Further on he writes: "But if he was a human being, as is well known, though it is without proof." (حل القرآن: ۲/۲)۔

The endorsement which Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ashraf ʿAlī Thānwī (raḥimahullāh) wrote on this exegesis is printed at the beginning of the exegesis. In it he wrote that he had examined this exegesis word by word from beginning to end, and then, after mentioning ten of its distinctive features, declared this exegesis to be beneficial for all, the common people and the elite alike.

This clarification had to be made because Mawlānā Mawdūdī Ṣāḥib too declared Khiḍr (ʿalayhis salām) to be an angel, and Muftī ʿAzīz al-Raḥmān Bijnorī Ṣāḥib wrote a book entitled "Taqṣīrāt-i Tafhīm", in which he criticised some passages of Mawdūdī Ṣāḥib's exegesis; among these passages he declared the statement that Ḥaḍrat Khiḍr (ʿalayhis salām) was an angel to be open to criticism. This criticism extends from page 175 to page 181. We have adopted this position not in imitation of Mawdūdī Ṣāḥib, but in the light of the exegesis "Ḥall al-Qurʾān" of Mawlānā Ḥabīb Aḥmad Ṣāḥib Kīrānwī (raḥimahullāh) and of other evidences.

As for the point that he asked for food — he did ask, but that Ḥaḍrat Khiḍr (ʿalayhis salām) partook of food is not established. This point is also supported by an incident of the ḥajj journey of Ḥaḍrat Imām Aḥmad (raḥimahullāh) with Ḥaḍrat Khiḍr (ʿalayhis salām): that during the journey Imām Aḥmad (raḥimahullāh) would partake of the finest food while Ḥaḍrat Khiḍr (ʿalayhis salām) would not eat.

Thus Qāḍī Abū Yaʿlā related in Ṭabaqāt al-Ḥanābilah from Imām Aḥmad (raḥimahullāh): I went as far as Qādisiyyah to see off the pilgrims. An intense longing for the ḥajj arose in my heart; along with this the thought also came to me: how can I go for ḥajj? I have nothing except these five dirhams, or these clothes whose price may be five dirhams. Meanwhile a man came to me and said: O Abū ʿAbdillāh! Your name is great, but your resolve is weak. Do you wish to go for ḥajj with me? Imām Aḥmad (raḥimahullāh) answered in the affirmative and set out on the journey with that man. They had neither any food nor any drink. Imām Aḥmad (raḥimahullāh) says: at the time of eating he said to me: bring food from such-and-such a place. I brought from a certain place piping hot food containing vegetables, and an excellent soup in which the bones of meat had been dissolved, and a basket of hot loaves and a waterskin of water. When I presented the food, that man was engaged in prayer. He shortened the prayer and said: You eat — but he himself did not eat. When I wanted to put away the leftover food he said: This food is not to be stored. Imām Aḥmad (raḥimahullāh) says that throughout the journey this man's dealing with me remained the same: he kept feeding me the finest food and himself did not eat. Moreover, we would cover a long distance in a short time, until we completed the ḥajj, and that man took his leave of me after bringing me to the very place from which he had taken me with him. See: (طبقات الحنابلۃ: ۱/۱۹۱۔۱۹۲)۔

The narrator of this account, Imām Abū al-Qāsim al-Baghawī, says: My supposition is that this was Ḥaḍrat Khiḍr (ʿalayhis salām).

أقول: If he was not Khiḍr, then to remain alive for so many days without food seems difficult.

And in ﴿آتيناه رحمة من عندنا﴾ what is meant by "mercy" is revelation, which is called divinely bestowed knowledge; ﴿وعلمنه من لدنا علما﴾ [الكهف: ٦۵].

By accepting him to be an angel, the question of his remaining alive is also solved, and meeting the friends of Allah will also not remain a matter of astonishment. And Allah knows best.

The account of Ḥaḍrat Khiḍr (ʿalayhis salām) with Ḥaḍrat Mūsā (ʿalayhis salām) also supports this: that Ḥaḍrat Khiḍr (ʿalayhis salām) carried out the act of tearing out a plank of the boat and making a hole in it, but the passengers of the boat did not see this angelic act; otherwise they would have raised an outcry and started beating him. He severed a boy's neck from his body, but the boys did not raise an outcry that our companion had been killed. He straightened the wall with his hand, which was very easy for an angel. There are also some other pieces of evidence for his being an angel, but this is not the place to mention them. If it is so, then the question of his remaining alive until the Day of Judgement is solved of itself, because the angels will remain alive until the Day of Judgement.

5. The Dajjāl: The Dajjāl is originally a jinn and a devil, but very closely resembles human beings — that is, he is like an intermediate state. His being a jinn is related from Jubayr ibn Nufayr, Kathīr ibn Murrah, Abū Nuʿaym the shaykh of Imām al-Bukhārī, and others. Muḥammad Rasūl al-Barzanjī wrote: "وعليه فإما أنه شيطان موثق في بعض الجزائر، أو هو من أولاد شق الكاهن المشهور، أو شق نفسة وكانت أمه جنية عشقت أباه فأولدها شقاً". (الإشاعة لأشراط الساعة، ۱۸۹، المقام الاول في اسمه ونسبه ومولده، دار النمير دمشق).

In a narration of Musnad Aḥmad it occurs that Ḥaḍrat ʿĪsā (ʿalayhis salām) will say to the Muslims: Confront the Dajjāl. Then they will say: This person is a jinn; confronting him is difficult. "ثم ينزل عيسى بن مريم فينادي من السحر فيقول: يا أيها الناس! ما يمنعكم أن تخرجوا إلى الكذاب الخبيث؟ فيقولون: هذا رجل جني

". الحديث. (مسند أحمد، رقم: ۱٤۹۵٤.. قال الشيخ شعيب: إسناده صحيح على شرط مسلم).

If it is so, then this objection of Mawdūdī Ṣāḥib will not stand: that "people have seen various islands, but there is no trace of the Dajjāl" — because when there is an admixture of the jinn in him, or he is a jinn, then his appearing to Tamīm al-Dārī (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhu) would be contrary to the norm, and remaining hidden is the norm. Yes, since near the Day of Judgement the manifestation of marvels will be very great, the Dajjāl will emerge, and many other strange events will appear.

If he is accepted to be a jinn resembling a human being, then the question of the difference of narrations concerning his shape and appearance will also be solved easily. Concerning him there are both narrations, of forty cubits and of being short. In some narrations he is one-eyed in the right eye, and in some the left eye is the defective one. With him will be an army of devils. All these indications show that he will originally be a jinn, appearing sometimes in one shape and sometimes in another. Yes, he will not have the power to remove the defect of the eyes, and near the Day of Judgement he will emerge appearing entirely in the guise of human beings. And from those narrations in which it is mentioned that the Dajjāl will kill Ḥaḍrat Khiḍr (ʿalayhis salām) and then will not have the power to kill him a second time, this too is understood: that the Dajjāl too is a creature of the intermediate state, and that he will kill Ḥaḍrat Khiḍr at first, but in the end the angelic side of the intermediate state will prevail. Concerning the Dajjāl it has come in the hadith that a single step of his donkey — that is, his mount — will be equal to a distance of three days; as if he will cover a distance of 82 kilometres in one second.

In the Pacific Ocean the Devil's Islands, and in the Atlantic Ocean the Bermuda, are two such places where numerous ships and aircraft have gone missing — more in Bermuda and fewer in the Devil's Islands — and numerous flying saucers have also been seen there. Scientists have preserved the rays of these two places, and according to them, if these rays were put into a car its speed would become hundreds of thousands of times greater. It is quite possible that one of these places, namely Bermuda, is the centre and seat of government of the devil, and that the devil has spread his satanic throne over it, and that the other place is the prison-house or abode of the Dajjāl. For details of the circumstances of the Dajjāl, the book "Dajjāl" of our brother Abū Lubābah Shāh Manṣūr is worth studying.

A useful point: the Dajjāl is also called al-Masīḥ, لأنه يمسح الأرض, or ممسوح العين اليمني; and the Dajjāl is called Dajjāl because the meanings of دجل are كذب، يا خلط الحق بالباطل. (فتح الباري: ٦/ ٤۷۲، و ۱۳/۹۱، والنهاية في غريب الحديث والأثر (م س ح). ومرقاة المفاتيح: ۱۰/۱۸۷، و ۱۰/۱۸٤).

The upshot of the meaning of "Dajjāl" is this: to gild and polish falsehood with truth, and to conceal the truth. The Dajjāl will misguide people by putting a polish of truth on his crooked deeds. Even before the coming of the Dajjāl, this is the very way of the people of the party of the Dajjāl: they put a polish of truth on falsehood and thereby promote falsehood. Upon friendship with the Rāfiḍīs, the polish of statements opposing them; upon the falsehood of ending the ʿUthmānī caliphate, the polish of friendship with and support for the Arabs; upon the falsehood of interest-bearing loans to the Muslims, the polish of aid; upon the falsehood of seizing the resources of the Arabs, they apply the polish of their possessing nuclear weapons and of the atrocities of Qadhdhāfī. Upon effacing the veiling of women, the plaster of the violation of women's rights; upon the falsehood of continuing their own atrocities, the plaster of pinning the label of "terrorist" (terrorism) on their opponents; upon the falsehood of reducing the Muslim population, the ornamented veil of family planning; upon the falsehood of decking out gatherings of misguidance, the beautiful veil of the fine arts. In the same way the Dajjāl will apply the polish and plaster of his own godhood and truthfulness upon his defective body and upon certain acts contrary to the norm.

6. دابة الأرض: The Beast of the Earth, which is like an intermediate state between the jinn and the animals, will appear in animal form and come out of the earth. It is mentioned in a hadith that it will come out when people abandon enjoining good and forbidding evil and the Day of Judgement is near. Allah Most High says in Sūrat al-Naml:

﴿وإذا وقع القول عليهم أخرجنا لهم دابة من الأرض تكلمهم أن الناس كانوا باىتنا لا يوقنون﴾ [النمل: ۸۲].

(And when the time for Our word to be fulfilled comes upon these people, We shall bring forth for them a beast from the earth which will speak to them, that people had no certainty in Our signs.)

It occurs in the hadith that this beast will have with it the ring of Sulaymān (ʿalayhis salām) and the staff of Mūsā (ʿalayhis salām).

عن أبي هريرة رضي الله عنه مرفوعاً: "تخرج الدابة ومعها خاتم سليمان بن داود، وعصا موسى بن عمران". (الحديث). (سنن الترمذي، رقم: ۳۱۸۷، وسنن ابن ماجه، رقم: ٤۰٦٦، وفي إسناده: على بن زيد، وهو ضعيف، وشيخه أوس بن خالد مجهول).

And in a narration of Sunan Ibn Mājah it has come that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ pointed out a hand-span of ground outside Makkah al-Mukarramah, saying that the Beast of the Earth would come out from here. (رقم: ٤۰٦۷) In its chain Khālid ibn ʿUbayd is an abandoned narrator, accused of fabrication.

The beast's coming out of the earth, its speaking, and its having the ring of Sulaymān (ʿalayhis salām) — this points to its being a jinn; then the coming of differing narrations concerning its shape also appears to be among the marvels. And Ḥaḍrat ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿAmr ibn al-ʿĀṣ رضي الله عنه indeed holds the Jassāsah of the Dajjāl to be the Beast of the Earth. In the sound narrations there are not many details of the Beast of the Earth. Shaykh Muḥammad Aḥmad Kanʿān writes: "والصحيح أنه لا دليل يعتمد عليه بخصوص هذا الدابة غير ما جاء مجملاً في القرآن الكريم". (قرة العينين على تفسير الجلالين، ص ۵۰٤)

Therefore it is possible that the Beast of the Earth is an animal form of the jinn, saying: O ill-fated human beings! You abandoned the work of enjoining good and forbidding evil, and you abandoned accepting it too, and most people have drowned in misguidances; now you have gone very far from certainty.

And this strangely formed speaking animal will be very hairy. People will be terrified by its appearance; they will not be able to harm it. It will proclaim the completion of the proof against people, and this will be a sign of the nearness of the Day of Judgement. For conveying and calling to the religion, countless means are available nowadays: radio, the internet and so forth. For the completion of the proof, a strangely formed animal will also be made to appear.

إن كان هذا صواباً فمن الله، وإن كان خطأ فمني ومن الشيطان.

Clarification of the Jassāsah:

In the hadith of Ḥaḍrat Tamīm al-Dārī (raḍiyallāhu taʿālā ʿanhu) the Jassāsah is called a "دابة أهلب" — that is, a very hairy beast — and it is like the "C.I.D." of the Dajjāl, لأنه يتجسس الأخبار للدجال — that is, to supply news for the Dajjāl. Some people have called it the "دابة الأرض" which is to appear near the Day of Judgement, and the author of Badhl al-Majhūd counted it as one of the kinds of devils. (بذل المجہود: ۱۲/۳۷۷، ط: دار البشائر الاسلامیۃ)۔
