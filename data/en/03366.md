### TITLE
A way of passing in front of someone praying when there is no sutrah

### QUESTION
If a person wants to pass in front of someone who is praying and it is necessary for him to pass, but there is no sutrah or anything of the sort, can he stand in front of him and then move forward and pass? Explain and be rewarded.

### ANSWER
Passing in front of someone praying when there is no sutrah is a sin and is forbidden. But if there is no sutrah and it is necessary to pass, then he should stand with his back towards the person praying and then move forward and go on his way.

In Fatḥ al-Mulhim, Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Shabbīr Aḥmad ʿUthmānī (may Allah have mercy on him) writes:

قوله: من أن يمر بين يديه، ظاهر الحديث أن الوعيد المذكور يختص بمن مر، لا بمن وقف عامداً ـ مثلاً ـ بين يدي المصلي، أو قعد، أو رقد، لكن إن كانت العلة فيه التشويش على المصلي فهو في معنى المار، قاله الحافظ رحمه الله في الفتح.
(فتح الملهم: ۳/٦۸۵، ط: دار العلوم كراچي). (وكذا في فتح الباري: ۱/۵۸٦).

Iʿlāʾ al-Sunan (إعلاء السنن) states:

وكلام فقهائنا في الفتاوى يفيد جواز الوقوف بين يديه. (إعلاء السنن: ۵/۸۲، ط: إدارة القرآن).

From the passages quoted above it is clear that one may stand in front of a person who is praying. Furthermore, if two people wish to pass, one of them should stand in front of the person praying and the other should pass under his cover; then the first man should do the same, and so both of them may pass. Fatāwā Shāmī states:

ولو مر اثنان يقوم أحدهما أمامه ويمر الآخر ويفعل الآخر هكذا يمران. (فتاوى الشامي: ۱/٦۳٦، سعيد). (وكذا في الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱۰٤).

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh records:
If two people wish to pass, one of them should stand with his back towards the person praying and the other should pass under his cover; then the first man should do likewise, and in this way both may pass. From this it is clear that there is no harm if a man sitting in front of a person praying gets up and goes away. The same is stated in Imdād al-Fatāwā. (عمدۃ الفقہ: ۲/۲۷٦) ۔

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states that a person sitting in front of someone praying may get up and go. (احسن الفتاوی: ۳/٤۰۸) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
