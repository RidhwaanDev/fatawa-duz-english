### TITLE
Removing the hair of the neck

### QUESTION
People generally have the hair of the neck removed as well when they have their hair cut. Is it correct to shave the hair of the neck or not?

### ANSWER
It is permissible and correct to shave the hair of the neck.

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states: it is permissible to trim or to shave the hair of the neck, which lies below the lobes of the ears.

كذا يشير إليه بعض ألفاظ الشامي من الحظر والإباحة وكذا صرح به حضرة الشيخ الگنگوهي رحمه الله في فتاواه: ۲/۸۳.

(Imdād al-Muftīn, vol. 2, p. 818, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

Imdād al-Aḥkām states: in sum, it is permissible to shave the hair of the neck and of the cheeks, while concerning the throat there is a difference of opinion. (Imdād al-Aḥkām: 4/334).

Fatāwā Rashīdiyyah states: the neck is one limb and the head is another, so it is correct to shave the hair of the neck. The joint of the head is felt separately behind the lobe of the ear; below that is the neck. (Fatāwā Rashīdiyyah, p. 630, Urdu Bazaar, Lahore).
