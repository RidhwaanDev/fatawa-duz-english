### TITLE
The ruling on keeping the name Yaqīnullāh

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to keep the name Yaqīnullāh or not?

### ANSWER
A meaning can be worked out for this name only with some strain, and there is no need for such strain; so do not keep this name. Keep a name whose sense and meaning are sound and better without any straining. If, however, the name is kept simply as "Yaqīn", or as "Muḥammad Yaqīn", without annexing it to the word Allah, that is fine: it becomes the name Muḥammad, and Yaqīn will mean one who has certainty, taken in an intensive sense — as in Zayd ʿAdl, meaning that Zayd is exceedingly just.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
