### TITLE
The sharia rulings on several kinds of sea creature

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to eat the following, or not?

(1) Crab: (crab).

(2) Al-dalfīn (dolphin).

(3) Al-ḥūt (whale).

(4) Al-akhṭabūṭ: (octopus).

(5) Al-qirsh: (shark).

(6) Prawn: (prawns).

(7) Calamari: (calamari).

### ANSWER
First of all it should be understood that zoologists have written down four marks by which a fish is recognised: (1) a backbone; (2) gills for breathing; (3) fins for swimming; (4) the body temperature rising and falling according to the surroundings.

Whatever has these marks will be called a fish; and in the Ḥanafī school, of all sea creatures only fish is permitted to be eaten.

The upshot is that among sea creatures only fish is lawful, and a fish is that in which the four marks mentioned above are found.

Now consider the ruling on each in turn:

(1) Crab (crab): eating it is impermissible in the view of the Ḥanafīs. It does not fall within the definition of a fish, and in shape and form too it is quite different from a fish.

(2) Al-dalfīn (dolphin): eating it is permissible and correct. All the marks of a fish are present in it.

(3) Al-ḥūt (whale): in a noble hadith the ʿanbar fish is called ḥūt, and it has already been mentioned that it is lawful and wholesome.

(4) Al-akhṭabūṭ (octopus): this is a sea creature which has not been counted among the kinds of fish, and the marks of a fish are not present in it either. Eating it is therefore impermissible.

(5) Al-qirsh (shark): this too is among the kinds of fish, and all the marks are present in it. It has already been mentioned that eating it is permissible and correct.

(6) Prawn (prawns): this resembles the crab. It is a disputed matter, and caution lies in not eating it.

(7) Calamari (calamari): this too, like the octopus, is a sea creature. The marks of a fish are not present in it, and so eating it is impermissible as well.

The Encarta Encyclopaedia records:

calamari is not permissible to eat because it is not a fish.

That is, it is not permissible to eat calamari, because it is not a fish.
