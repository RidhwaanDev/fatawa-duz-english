### TITLE
Sacrificing an emaciated animal

### QUESTION
Is the sacrifice of an emaciated, weak and lean animal permissible or not?

### ANSWER
The sacrifice of an emaciated, weak animal in whose bones no marrow is left is impermissible; but if it is not so weak as that, its sacrifice is permissible — though the sacrifice of a fat, well-fleshed animal is better. (Al-Durr al-Mukhtār: 6/323, Saʿīd; and Fatāwā Qāḍī Khān: 3/353).
