### TITLE
Discounting a hundi or bill of exchange

### QUESTION
Zayd bought goods worth one hundred thousand rand from ʿUmar, and the two agreed that the price would be paid after two months, and a cheque was signed which ʿUmar can cash only after two months. But ʿUmar, who is the seller, needs the money immediately, so he sells that bill or cheque to Bakr or to a bank for ninety-eight thousand, and after two months Bakr will receive one hundred thousand rand. Is this transaction permissible or not?

بينوا توجروا.

### ANSWER
In the case in question this is an interest-based transaction, because Bakr or the bank paid out ninety-eight thousand and will receive one hundred thousand later. Yes, if there is a pressing need, then ʿUmar may sell some goods to Bakr or to the bank for ninety-eight thousand and take the ninety-eight thousand; then, once the buyer has taken possession of those goods, he may sell them back to ʿUmar for one hundred thousand payable after two months. This is a legal device and stratagem, and there is room to resort to it at a time of need.

For further details, see: Jadīd Muʿāmalāt ke Sharʿī Aḥkām, vol. 1, p. 147, under the heading "Selling a bill at a discount"; Islām awr Jadīd Maʿīshat o Tijārat, p. 123; Islām awr Jadīd Maʿāshī Masāʾil: 7/251, "Bill of exchange"; and Gharar kī Ṣūratayn, pp. 334–337.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
