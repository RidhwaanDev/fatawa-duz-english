### TITLE
Passing in front of a worshipper by hanging down a scarf or standing up a stick

### QUESTION
In order to pass in front of someone who is praying, may a person hang down his scarf, or stand his stick upright, and then pass by, or not?

### ANSWER
On the face of it this appears permissible. Another form of it is that a person stands his stick upright, passes by, and takes hold of it again before it falls. Consider what Radd al-Muḥtār states:

فإن كان معه شيء يضعه بين يديه ثم يمر ويأخذ ه

أقول: وإذا كان معه عصا لا تقف على الأرض بنفسها فأمسكها بيده ومر من خلفها هل يكفي ذلك؟ لم أره. (شامي: ۱/٦۳٦، باب ما يفسد الصلاة وما يكره فيها، سعيد).

ʿAllāmah Anwar Shāh states:

وفي حاشية العناية للشيخ سعد الدين أنه لو أسبل غشاوة من السقف كفاه للسترة. قلت: وعلى هذا فمن كان لا بد أن يمر بين يدي المصلي فليسبل منديله أمامه ثم ليمر، ولعله يكون أيسر له من مروره كما هو. (فيض الباري: ۲/۸۳).

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh states:

If the person passing has with him a stick that cannot be made to stand up on its own, may he hold it upright in his hand in front of the worshipper and pass in front of him, or not?

No explicit statement on this has been found (Shāmī). On the face of it, it appears permissible. One form of it is that he lets go of the stick and passes by before it falls, and then takes hold of it again. (عمدۃ الفقہ حصہ دوم کتاب الصلوۃ: ۲۷٦، سترہ کے مسائل، مجددیہ) ۔

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

Question: In order to pass in front of someone praying, a man hangs down his scarf or stands his stick upright and passes behind it. Is this permissible?

Answer: ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) says that he found no explicit ruling on this matter. On the face of it there is nothing to prevent its being permissible, so there is room for it when there is a need. (احسن الفتاوی: ۳/٤۱۰ باب مفسدات الصلاۃ) ۔

Inʿām al-Bārī states: if a person has a scarf in his hand, he may let the scarf hang and pass by; this too is permissible. (انعام الباری: ۳/۲٦۱، بحوالہ فیض الباری: ۲/۸۳) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
