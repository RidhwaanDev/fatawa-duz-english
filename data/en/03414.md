### TITLE
The ruling on the prayer when ﷺ is recited along with the name of the Prophet in the prayer

### QUESTION
An imām recited in the Fajr prayer thus: "لقد كان لكم في رسول الله ﷺ أسوة حسنة". Was the prayer valid or not? And if the prayer is valid, will reciting it in this way be disliked, or merely unfitting? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
The prayer will not be invalidated by reciting ﷺ along with the blessed name of the Prophet ﷺ in the midst of the recitation in the prayer, because it is neither a sentence of reply nor is it similar to the speech of people. Yes, since in the standing posture of the prayer there should be nothing other than the Qurʾān, from this point of view reciting ﷺ along with the name of the Prophet in the prayer will be disliked — that is, contrary to what is preferable.

For the evidences of this question, see under the preceding question concerning صدق اللہ العظیم. Here it will suffice to cite a single passage of Ḥaḍrat Gangōhī (may Allah have mercy on him):

والأصل تجريد القراءة عن غير القرآن، في الصلاة، فلا يتحول عنه إلا بدليل، ولو عمل أحد في الصلاة لا تفسد، انتهى. (الكوكب الدري على جامع الترمذي: ٤/۳۲۲).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
