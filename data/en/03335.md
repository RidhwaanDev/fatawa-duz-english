### TITLE
Prayer in close-fitting and tight clothing

### QUESTION
If a person prays wearing trousers so tight that the shape of the parts that must be covered is visible, will the prayer be valid or not? And if it is not valid, will making it up be obligatory or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
Praying in such tight, close-fitting trousers is disliked; nevertheless the prayer will be valid, though with dislikedness, and repeating it is not obligatory. Wearing such close-fitting clothing is disliked outside the prayer as well. Fatāwā Shāmī mentions:

قوله ولا يصف ما تحته بأن لا يرى منه لون البشرة احترازاً عن الرقيق ونحو الزجاج، قوله ولا يضر التصاقه أي بالألية مثلاً وقوله وتشكله من عطف المسبب على السبب وعبارة شرح المنية أما لو كان غليظاً لا يري منه لون البشرة إلا أنه التصق بالعضو وتشكل بشكله فصار شكل العضو مرئياً فينبغي أن لا يمنع جواز الصلاة لحصول الستر. (فتاوى الشامي: ۱/ ٤۱۰، سعيد).

Kitāb al-Masāʾil records:

Wearing clothing so close-fitting that the shape of the parts that must be covered becomes apparent is indeed disliked and a sign of shamelessness; nevertheless, if the cloth is thick enough that the skin beneath is not visible, praying in it is valid. (Kitāb al-Masāʾil 1/273)

In another place it is recorded: although the prayer is valid, with dislikedness, when performed wearing tight jeans and a T-shirt, in our custom this dress is regarded as contrary to the dress of the righteous. (Kitāb al-Masāʾil 1/271)

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā records:

A few basic principles of dress: (6) If a person wears trousers below and a shirt above, this dress is not impermissible; but since it is not the dress of the righteous it is contrary to what is preferable, and if the shape of the parts that must be covered is apparent in it, a further dislikedness will arise.

وأما اللباس المكروه فعلي ثلاثة أوجه:

والثاني: لباس الرقاق الذي يبين منه البدن لأنه لباس أهل التكبر والخيلاء والأشر، ومن لا اهتمام له بأمر الآخرة

(النتف في الفتاوى، ص ۱٦۱، ۱٦۲). (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā 7/82, Zamzam Publishers)

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
