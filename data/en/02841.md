### TITLE
The ruling on playing carrom board

### QUESTION
Generally, after finishing the ʿIshāʾ prayer, some young men gather together in one place for a short while and play carrom board. On account of it neither are obligatory duties and rights lost, nor is any impermissible matter such as gambling involved; recreation alone is intended. So will it be permissible to play such a game or not?

### ANSWER
There is nothing objectionable in carrom board in itself. Yes, at times the absorption in it becomes so great that heedlessness of the obligatory duties begins to occur on its account; such absorption is forbidden. Otherwise, if it is played for a short while merely for recreation, as is mentioned in the question, then this is permissible and correct.

Ḥaḍrat Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ Ṣāḥib says in Aḥkām al-Qurʾān:

اعلم أن الشريعة المصطفوية السمحة البيضاء لا تمنع الارتفاقات المصالح التي فطرت عليها الطبيعة البشرية ولا ترتضي الرهبانية والتبتل، بل تقتضي المدنية والمعاشرة الصالحة، نعم: تمنع الغلو في المسليات والانهماك فيها بحيث يلهي عن الضروريات الدينية أو المعاشية، ومن المعلوم أن من الحاجات المفطور عليها الإنسان تمرين البدن وترويح القلب وتفريحه ساعة فساعة، ومن ههنا قال عليه الصلاة والسلام: "روحوا القلوب ساعة فساعة"، أخرجه أبو داود في مراسيله عن ابن شهاب مرسلاً، وأبو بكر المقري في فوائده، والقضاعي عنه عن أنس رضي الله عنه. (أحكام القرآن: ۳/۱۹٤).

In Kifāyat al-Muftī it is said:

If no wager (that is, any sum upon winning and losing) is laid in carrom, and it is played for a short while merely for the purpose of recreation, and no disruption occurs on its account in any necessary or religious matter, then it will be permitted. (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 9/204, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

In Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah it is said:

If there is no winning and losing, and no disruption occurs on its account in the injunctions of the Sacred Law, then there is scope for this game (carrom) now and then, to dispel gloom and divert the heart; even so, one should not make a habit of it, and should strive to give it up. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 19/536, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
