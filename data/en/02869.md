### TITLE
The ruling on using books that contain pictures

### QUESTION
What is the ruling on books containing pictures in schools and madrasas, seeing that understanding a thing becomes easier through them? Is their use permissible or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question, making a picture is impermissible. As for using one, there is some detail: in a place of respect and veneration it is impermissible, while in a place of disrespect it is permissible — and being in books is a place of respect. On this basis one should avoid using them without need.

Consider what Muftī Shafīʿ Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

There is a certain amount of detail regarding keeping a picture in the house and using it. Pictures so small that if they were placed on the ground and a man of average eyesight looked at them standing, the details of the limbs in the picture would not be visible — to keep such a picture in the house and to use it is permissible, although making it too is impermissible. Pictures made upon some object or in some place that is customarily trodden underfoot and regarded as lowly and contemptible — for example on a floor covering that is trampled, or on bedding, or on a sitting mattress, cushion, chair and the like, or on the sole of a shoe, or underneath on the base of utensils — to keep these in the house and to use them is permissible, although making them too is impermissible. But a floor covering that is not in a place of disrespect — a prayer mat, for instance — it is not permissible for a picture to be on it. Likewise, if the cushions bearing pictures are large ones on which the picture appears upright, then using them too is impermissible.

Ruling: Pictures on utensils that are anywhere other than the base do not come under the ruling of being trodden underfoot and treated with contempt; hence if they are large pictures, using those utensils is not permissible either,
لما في بلوغ القصد والمرام: الصور في الأواني ليست بممتهنة. (ص ۱۷ . ۱۸).
(تصویر کے شرعی احکام، از مفتی محمد شفیع صاحب رحمه الله، ص۸۳ ۔ ۸۵) ۔

In sum, the pictures in books are intended and are clearly visible; therefore one should avoid using such books as far as possible.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
