### TITLE
Whether the sacrifice is necessary upon every member of the household

### QUESTION
Is the sacrifice necessary upon every owner of the nisab in a household, or upon one senior person? If it is necessary upon all of them, will the obligation be discharged on behalf of them all by one person offering a sacrifice, or not?

### ANSWER
The sacrifice is necessary (wājib) upon every free, resident Muslim who owns the nisab. Even if there are several people in one house, if all of them own the nisab then, just as with ṣadaqat al-fiṭr, the sacrifice will be necessary upon all of them; and the obligation will not be discharged on behalf of them all by one person offering a sacrifice. However, if all the sons bring their earnings and give them to the father, and individually they do not own the nisab but only the father does, then the sacrifice will be binding on the father alone.

See the noble hadith, which states:

عن أبي هريرة رضي الله عنه أن رسول الله ﷺ قال: من كان له سعة ولم يضح فلا يقربن مصلانا. (رواه ابن ماجه، ص: ۲۲٦).

The author of Al-Hidāyah says:

الأضحية واجبة على حر مسلم مقيم موسر في يوم الأضحى عن نفسه. (الهداية: ٤/٤۴۳). (وكذا في البحر الرائق: ۸/۱۷۳، ط: كوئته، والدر المختار مع الشامي: ٦/۳۱۵، ط: سعيد).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

The sacrifice is necessary upon every owner of the nisab in the house; the sacrifice of one cannot suffice for all. If the sacrifice of a goat is too costly, then a large animal should be sacrificed. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۵/٤۱۵، مکتبۃ الاحسان دیوبند) ۔

"Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥall" states:

If the father, the sons and the daughters are all earning and own the nisab, then a separate sacrifice is necessary upon each one of them, because the sacrifice is necessary upon every sane, adult man and woman who owns the nisab, whether married or unmarried. (آپ کے مسائل اور ان کا حل: ٤/۱۷۷) ۔
