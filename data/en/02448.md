### TITLE
The ruling on the sacrifice for a woman

### QUESTION
Is the sacrifice necessary upon a woman or not?

### ANSWER
If a woman possesses wealth to the amount of the niṣāb (the minimum amount of wealth) upon which the sacrifice becomes necessary, then the sacrifice is binding and necessary upon that woman just as it is upon men. It is not necessary that a full year should pass over the wealth (ḥawalān al-ḥawl). (فتاوی الشامی ٦/۳۱۵ ۔ سعید، و تنویر الابصار: ٦/۳۱۲) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
