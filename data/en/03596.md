### TITLE
The ruling on marrying one's step-maternal uncle

### QUESTION
A girl's mother passed away, and her father, at an advanced age, married a young woman who has a brother. Is it permissible or not for this girl to marry her stepmother's brother, that is, her step-maternal uncle? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question, the stepmother's brother is not this girl's real maternal uncle; for this reason the marriage is permissible and valid. Outwardly there is no ground for the marriage being impermissible.

قال الله تعالى: ﴿وأحل لكم ما وراء ذلكم﴾ [النساء: ۲٤].

Consider what is stated in Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband:

Question: A man has two wives; from the first wife he has three daughters, while the second wife is childless. Now, is it valid or not for one of the first wife's daughters to be married to a real brother of the second wife?

Answer: It is valid for the second wife's brother to marry one of the first wife's daughters; there is no ground in it for the prohibition of marriage. (فتاوی دار العلوم دیوبند: ۷/۱۹٦، مکمل و مدلل، ط: دار الاشاعت) ۔

Kifāyat al-Muftī states: It is permissible to marry a daughter born of the first wife to the brother of the second wife:

قال الله تعالى: ﴿وأحل لكم ما وراء ذلكم﴾ أي ما عدا من ذكرن من المحارم هن لكم حلال. تفسير ابن كثير: ۲/۲۵۸.
(کفایت المفتی: ٦/۵٤۱، جامعہ فاروقیہ) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
