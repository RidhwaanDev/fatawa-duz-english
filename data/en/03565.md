### TITLE
The ruling on an ʿumrah in which the undergarment was forgotten when entering iḥrām

### QUESTION
A person entered iḥrām, took off his sewn clothes and put on the two sheets, but forgot to remove his undergarment (underwear). Has his ʿumrah now been fulfilled or not? Has any penalty become binding or not?

### ANSWER
An undergarment or shorts falls under sewn clothing. Therefore, if it was worn for a day and a night or longer while in the state of iḥrām, a dam becomes binding; and if it was worn for less than that, a penalty to the measure of ṣadaqat al-fiṭr becomes binding.

Consider what is stated in al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah:

إذا لبس المحرم المخيط على الوجه المعتاد يوماً إلى الليل فعليه دم وإن كان أقل من ذلك فصدقة كذا في المحيط سواء لبسه ناسياً أو عامداً عالماً أو جاهلاً مختاراً أو مكرهاً هكذا في البحر الرائق. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۲٤۲).

(وكذا في المحيط: ۲/۷۳۲، ط: دار إحياء التراث العربي، وتحفة الفقهاء، ص ٤۱۹، ط: بيروت).

It is recorded in Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: Wearing shorts is impermissible in every case, and the penalty for wearing sewn clothing is binding on account of it. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 4/521).

It is mentioned in Fatāwā Ḥaqqāniyyah: Wearing sewn clothes for a period shorter than a day or a night necessitates ṣadaqah, and wearing sewn clothes for a period longer than a day or a night necessitates a dam — on the authority of Shāmī. (Fatāwā Ḥaqqāniyyah: 4/24).

It is recorded in ʿUmdat al-Fiqh:

Whether a person commits the violations deliberately or in error (by mistake), whether it is the first time or repeated a second and a third time, whether he commits them while remembering the iḥrām or forgetfully, and whether he knows the ruling or does not know it — the ruling for all is equal with respect to the penalty becoming binding. (ʿUmdat al-Fiqh: 4/428).
