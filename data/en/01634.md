### TITLE
The lineage of a child conceived from an outsider's semen

### QUESTION
There is a method in use whereby, if for some reason a couple cannot have a child through the husband, a child is produced by placing another man's semen in the wife's womb. From whom will this child's lineage be established — from the husband, or from the outsider whose semen was used?

### ANSWER
In the case described, this procedure — obtaining a child by injection from someone other than the husband — is unlawful and entirely contrary to Islamic principles. Nevertheless, if the conception is carried through, the lineage will be established from that woman's husband; it will not be established from the outsider.

Consider the following hadith:

قال رسول الله ﷺ: "الولد للفراش وللعاهر الحجر". (رواه البخاري: ۲/۹۹۹، باب الولد للفراش وللعاهر الحجر).

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

Every form of combining the germinal material of a man and a woman who are strangers to each other is sinful, and in terms of the ruling it is fornication. If a woman who is married to a man becomes pregnant, or has a child, from an outsider's material, the child's lineage will be established from her actual husband. (جدید فقہی مسائل: ۵/۱۵۲، ٹسٹ ٹیوب سے تولید اور اس سے متعلق احکام) ۔

It also says elsewhere:

If the conception is in fact carried through in this way, the lineage will be established, and the rulings of inheritance, fosterage and the like will follow. For lineage to be established, intercourse in its natural form is not required; even without it, if the semen reaches the woman's womb in some way, lineage will be established. Some passages of the jurists point to this.

Khulāṣat al-Fatāwā states:

البكر إذا جومعت في ما دون الفرج فحبلت بأن دخل الماء في فرجها فلما قرب أوان ولادتها تزال عذرها ببيضة أو بحرف درهم. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳٦۰، كتاب الكراهية، باب ۲۱. وكذا في المحيط البرهاني: ٦/۱۲۷، كتاب الاستحسان والكراهية).

That is: if a virgin is approached without penetration and then becomes pregnant, in that the semen enters her private part, then when the time of birth draws near her hymen should be removed by means of an egg or the edge of a coin.

There is no doubt that in practice this amounts to fornication. The sharia punishment for fornication cannot, however, be carried out for it in Muslim countries, because that punishment is not for unlawful pregnancy alone but for the two taking pleasure in each other. (جدید فقہی مسائل: جلد اول، ص: ۱۵۱) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
