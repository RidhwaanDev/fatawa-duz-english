### TITLE
The ruling on conveying reward to a minor child

### QUESTION
May reward be conveyed to minor children or not?

### ANSWER
Minor children are sinless, so they have no need of the conveying of reward; nevertheless, if reward is conveyed to them too, then according to the sound position the reward will reach them — just as the Messenger of Allah ﷺ is sinless, yet reward may be conveyed to him and ought to be conveyed. People have continued in this practice: they perform a sacrifice, an ʿumrah, a ṭawāf, a good deed and so forth for him ﷺ and convey the reward. Consider some pieces of evidence:

قال في الدر المختار: دعاء له أيضاً بتقدمه في الخير، لا سيما وقد قالوا: حسنات الصبي له لأبويه. (الدر المختار: ۲/۲۱۵، سعيد). (وكذا في البحر الرائق: ۲/۲۱۲، دار المعرفة، وحاشية الطحطاوي على مراقي الفلاح، ص ۳۸۸، ط: مصر).

It is mentioned in Aḥsan al-Fatāwā:

A minor receives the reward of his own good deeds; from this it is established that another may also convey to him the reward of his good deeds. Moreover, the supplication in the funeral prayer over him is also of benefit to him, and from this too the benefit of conveying reward is established. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 4/205).

It is recorded in Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah:

Children are sinless; there is no need to convey reward on their behalf in order to have sins forgiven. Yes, if supplication is made for them to obtain bounties, that is fine, as is done in the funeral prayer. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 9/239, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

It is in Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim Sharīf that at the sacrifice the Messenger of Allah ﷺ made this supplication:

اللهم تقبل من محمد وآل محمد ومن أمة محمد

(Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim: 2/56/1967).

And the children are certainly included in the ummah; so it is established that reward may be conveyed to children.

The Noble Prophet ﷺ is sinless, yet Ḥaḍrat ʿAlī (may Allah Most High be pleased with him) performed a sacrifice on behalf of the Messenger ﷺ:

عن حنش قال: رأيت علياً يضحي بكبشين فقلت ما هذا؟ فقال: إن رسول الله ﷺ أوصاني أن أضحي عنه فأنا أضحي عنه. (رواه أبو داود، رقم: ۲۷۹۲).

Further evidence for conveying reward to the Messenger ﷺ may be seen in Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā (2/843).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
