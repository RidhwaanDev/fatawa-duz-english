### TITLE
Meeting all of a wife's needs

### QUESTION
Is a man obliged to meet all his wife's needs? And what is the standard for maintenance?

### ANSWER
If the man is wealthy, or his income is a good one, and the woman too is from a wealthy family, then he will have to provide maintenance — food, clothing and housing — with regard to her standing and her standard of living. If the man is well off, or has the income of a well-off man, but the woman is from a poor family, then he should provide food and maintenance according to his own standing, not according to hers.

If, however, the man is poor and in straitened circumstances, and the woman too is from a poor household, then he should provide food and clothing according to his own means. And if a man is poor while the woman is from a wealthy and comfortable family, then he should provide maintenance keeping in view both his own standing and hers; but it is the woman's own moral duty not to demand of him more maintenance than his means allow.

Those items of grooming and adornment that are necessary for a woman's health and cleanliness are also included in maintenance, and providing them too is necessary for the man — for example oil, a comb, soap, and water for bathing and ablution. As for things that are purely for adornment and beauty and serve no real need — for example betel leaf, tobacco, cream, powder and lipstick — providing these is not necessary for the man. For details see: (اسلامی فقہ: ۲/۱۱۵) ۔

What sort of house should it be? The jurists have set out the details in this way:

As far as possible the wife should live agreeably with the people of her husband's household, so that the man does not have to take on needless trouble on her account. Even so, the proper course is that the husband himself, or his family, should set aside for the woman a corner of the house or a room, so that she can keep her belongings safely in one place, that husband and wife can live there at ease, and that no occasion for disagreement arises with the other people of the house.

If the woman does not wish to live with everyone and demands a separate house for herself, then the man must set aside for her a separate room, or at the very least some corner of the house, which she can shut. In the place or room he has set aside for her, the woman may admit whomever she wishes and keep out whomever she wishes. Apart from this, it is not necessary to give her a separate bathroom, toilet and kitchen. But this is the ruling where the husband is of modest means; if the husband is wealthy, he should give her a house containing everything she needs, such as a bathroom, toilet and kitchen.

For details see: (اسلامی فقہ: ۲/۱۲۱) ۔

In reality, maintenance and housing rest upon custom. If in some country the custom and practice is to give the wife a separate house, then the husband should give his wife a separate house. In our view, it is the practice in South Africa to give a separate house; so in this country, when the wife demands it, the husband should give her a separate house containing the necessary facilities such as a bathroom, toilet and kitchen.

Consider Fatāwā Shāmī, which states:

وينبغي اعتماده في زماننا هذا، فقد مر أن الطعام والكسوة يختلفان باختلاف الزمان والمكان. وأهل بلادنا الشامية لا يسكنون في بيت من دار مشتملة على أجانب، وهذا في أوساطهم فضلاً عن أشرافهم إلا أن تكون داراً موروثة بين إخوة مثلاً، فيسكن كل منهم في جهة منها مع الاشتراك في مرافقها، فإذا تضررت زوجة أحدهم من أحمائها أو ضرتها، وأراد زوجها إسكانها في بيت منفرد من دار لجماعة أجانب وفي البيت مطبخ وخلاء يعدون ذلك من أعظم العار عليهم، فينبغي الإفتاء بلزوم دار من بابها. نعم ينبغي أن لا يلزمه إسكانها في دار واسعة كدار أبيها أو كداره التي هو ساكن فيها، لأن كثيراً من الأوساط والأشراف يسكنون الدار الصغيرة. وهذا موافق لما قدمناه عن الملتقط من قوله اعتباراً في السكني بالمعروف، إذ لا شك أن المعروف يختلف باختلاف الزمان والمكان، فعلى المفتي أن ينظر إلى حال أهل زمانه وبلده، إذ بدون ذلك لا يحصل المعاشرة بالمعروف. وقد قال الله تعالى: ﴿ولا تضاروهن لتضيقوا عليهن﴾. (فتاوى الشامي: ۳/٦۰۱. ٦۰۲، ط: سعيد).

وفي البحر الرائق: واتفقوا على وجوب نفقة الموسرين إذا كانا موسرين، وعلى نفقة المعسرين إذا كانا معسرين، وإنما الاختلاف فيما إذا كان أحدهما موسراً والآخر معسراً

أما على المفتي به فتجب نفقة الوسط في المسئلتين، وهي فوق نفقة المعسرة ودون نفقة الموسرة. (البحر الرائق: ٤/۱۷۵).

وفي المجتبى أن ذلك يختلف باختلاف الأماكن والعادات فيجب على القاضي اعتبار الكفاية بالمعروف في كل وقت ومكان. (البحر الرائق: ٤/۱۷۷).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

If the wife is wealthy, it is obligatory to give her a separate house. If she is of middle standing, then besides an independent room in the same house it is necessary that the kitchen, bathroom and toilet also be independent. If she is poor, a single room is enough, and there is no harm in the kitchen, bathroom and toilet being shared. (احسن الفتاویٰ: ۵/۵۷٦) ۔

In the event of a quarrel, however, or where the husband's relatives come and go at all hours, the wife may demand a separate house.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالیٰ اعلم
