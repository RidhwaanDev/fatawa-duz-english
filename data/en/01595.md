### TITLE
The correct meaning of the ʿiddah in the pure sharia

### QUESTION
The correct meaning of the ʿiddah in the pure sharia.

### ANSWER
The ʿiddah is the name of the period which the sharia has fixed for a woman in order that the effects of the marriage may come to an end.

Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ states:

فالعدة في عرف الشرع اسم لأجل ضرب لانقضاء ما بقي من آثار النكاح. (بدائع الصنائع في ترتيب الشرائع: ۳/۱۹۰، سعيد).
