### TITLE
Annulment of the marriage for the wife of a chronically ill husband

### QUESTION
A woman lived with her husband for some time after the marriage, after which the husband was afflicted with a severe illness on account of which blood and pus discharge from his body. The husband is not impotent, but because of his weakness and his illness he is unable to have marital relations, and if he does it causes him great pain. There is no shortfall in the expenses the husband provides, but the woman does not wish to remain with him and wishes of her own free will to have the marriage annulled. Can the marriage be annulled or not?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, if the husband is unable, because of his chronic illness, to fulfil the rights of his young wife, and the woman cannot bear it patiently, and there is a strong fear of her falling into sin, then the scholars have permitted an annulment in view of the danger of fornication and sin. Accordingly, the woman should bring her case before a qāḍī or the Jamʿiyyat al-ʿUlamāʾ; after investigating the matter they should grant the husband one lunar year's grace for treatment. If even then there is no recovery, and the wife files her case again and asks for a separation, then the qāḍī or the Jamʿiyyat al-ʿUlamāʾ will be authorised to annul the marriage.

Majmūʿah Qawānīn-e Islāmī states:

There may be several forms of a husband's being incapable of intercourse: for instance, that the husband's male organ has been severed; or that his organ is so small that on account of it he is incapable of intercourse; or that the organ is present but on account of some illness he is incapable of intercourse with a woman. In all these cases the woman will have the right to have the marriage annulled through the qāḍī. In the first and second cases the qāḍī will annul the marriage immediately; in the third case he will grant a period of grace for treatment of one lunar year, and if even after treatment the husband is unable to have intercourse, then upon the woman's demand the qāḍī will annul the marriage at once. (مجموعہ قوانین اسلامی: ص۱۹۳، دفعہ۷٤) ۔

It also states:

If the husband became afflicted with some harmful disease after the marriage, then upon the woman's application the qāḍī will, after investigating the matter and establishing proof in accordance with the sharia, grant the husband one lunar year's grace for treatment. If even after that there is no recovery and the wife again asks for a separation, the qāḍī will effect the separation. (مجموعہ قوانین اسلامی: ص۱۹٤، دفعہ۷۵) ۔
