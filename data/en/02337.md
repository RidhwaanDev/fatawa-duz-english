### TITLE
Meat slaughtered by Qādiyānīs and Agha Khānīs

### QUESTION
If a Qādiyānī or an Agha Khānī slaughters an animal after saying "Bismillāh", is it permissible to eat it or not? If the answer is no, does that make them worse and lower than the People of the Book, since the animals slaughtered by the People of the Book are permissible while theirs are unlawful — and this even though these people accept Muḥammad ﷺ as the Messenger of Allah, whereas the People of the Book do not? And if the answer is yes, why?

### ANSWER
The Qādiyānīs, the Agha Khānīs and the Khojas are all apostates and heretics, and the animals they slaughter are unlawful and impermissible.

ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) states:

إذ لا خلاف في كفر المخالف في ضروريات الإسلام من حدوث العالم وحشر الأجساد ونفي العلم بالجزئيات وإن كان أهل القبلة المواظب طول عمره على الطاعات كما في شرح التحرير. (رد المحتار: ۱/۵٦۱، سعيد).

ʿUmdat al-Qārī states:

واختلفوا في تفسيره (أي الزنديق) فقيل: هو المبطن للكفر المظهر للإسلام كالمنافق وقيل: من لا دين له وقيل: هم طائفة من الروافض تدعي السبائية

واختلف في الزنديق هل يستتاب؟

وقول أبي حنيفة رحمه الله وأبي يوسف رحمه الله مختلف فيه، فمرة قالا: بالاستتابة ومرة قالا: لا قلت: روي عن أبي حنيفة رحمه الله أنه قال: إن أتيت بزنديق استبته فإن تاب وإلا قتلته. (عمدة القاري: ۱٦/۲۰۰، باب حكم المرتد والمرتدة، ط: دار الحديث، ملتان).

Al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq states:

الزنديق

هو من لا يتدين بدين، وأما من يبطن الكفر والعياذ بالله ويظهر الإسلام، فهو المنافق، ويجب أن يكون حكمه في عدم قبولنا توبته كالزنديق؛ لأن ذلك في الزنديق لعدم الاطمينان إلى ما يظهر من التوبة إذا كان قد يخفي كفره الذي هو عدم اعتقاده ديناً. (البحر الرائق: ۵/۱۲٦، باب أحكام المرتدين، كوئته).

Ḥaḍrat Shāh Kashmīrī (may Allah have mercy on him) states:

ولا ينجو من الكفر إلا من أكفر ذلك الملحد (أي غلام أحمد القادياني) بلا تلعثم وتردد. (اكفار الملحدين، ص ۱۰، ط: المجلس العلمي).

The author of al-Hidāyah states:

ولا تؤكل ذبيحة المجوسي والمرتد. (الهداية: ٤/٤۳٤).

Khulāṣat al-Fatāwā states:

ولا يؤكل ذبيحة المجوسي والمرتد والمرتدة والوثني. (خلاصة الفتاوى: ٤/۳۰۵، كتاب الصيد والذبائح).

The jurist of the ummah, Ḥaḍrat Muftī Maḥmūd Ḥasan Gangohī (may Allah have mercy on him), states:

Mirzā Ghulām Aḥmad Qādiyānī adopted beliefs of disbelief, on account of which he left Islam and became an apostate. Anyone who affirms his beliefs of disbelief comes under the same ruling. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 2/116, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Yūsuf Ludhyānwī Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) states:

The Agha Khānī community, like the Qādiyānī community, is heretical and apostate. From the earliest generations down to the present day, all the people of knowledge are agreed on their disbelief, apostasy and heresy. (Adyān-e Bāṭilah awr Ṣirāṭ-e Mustaqīm, p. 89, by Muftī Muḥammad Naʿīm Ṣāḥib).

The actual creed of the Agha Khānīs:

أشهد أن لا إله إلا الله وأشهد أن محمداً رسول الله وأشهد أن أمير المؤمنين على ولي الله. "يا علي مدد"

— this, they say, is our Islam. (Mukālamah bayn al-Madhāhib, p. 229, Maktabah Fārūqiyyah; and Adyān-e Bāṭilah, p. 88).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

If the descendants of Qādiyānīs — Mirzāʾīs and Qādiyānīs by birth — regard Ghulām Aḥmad Qādiyānī as a prophet, or at the very least as a Muslim, then they too are disbelievers. The animals they slaughter must be unlawful and carrion, and there is no sense in giving them the ruling of the People of the Book. ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) holds the extremist Rāfiḍīs to be disbelievers and does not regard them as People of the Book; so how could the descendants of the Qādiyānīs be counted among the People of the Book?

والظاهر أن الغلاة من الروافض المحكوم بكفرهم لا ينفكون عن اعتقادهم الباطل في حال إتيانهم بالشهادتين وغيرهما من أحكام الشرع كالصوم والصلاة فهم كفار لا مرتدون ولا أهل كتاب. (رسائل ابن عابدين، ص ۳۷۰، ط: سهيل).

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Muḥammad Yūsuf Ludhyānwī (may Allah have mercy on him), who has considerable insight into this subject and has written several treatises refuting Qādiyānism, writes that the summary of all these discussions is as follows:

(1) A person who himself apostatised to Qādiyānism is both an apostate and a heretic.

(2) His own children, being subordinate to their parents, are apostates in law as well, and heretics too.

(3) His grandchildren are not apostates but purely heretics.

(4) Both the apostate and the heretic are liable to the death penalty; marriage with either is void, and the animals slaughtered by either are unlawful and carrion. An animal slaughtered by a Qādiyānī is therefore lawful under no circumstances whatever. (Risālah Qādiyānī Dhabīḥah, pp. 24, 25). (Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 1/154, Maktabat al-Iḥsān Deoband).

Ḥaḍrat Muftī Rashīd Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) states:

On the basis of what al-Shāmī wrote about the Muʿtazilah, I used to call the Shia People of the Book. Later I realised that these people are heretics, and so it is not correct to include them among the People of the Book. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 1/88).

In another place he states:

The Shia, the Qādiyānīs, the Agha Khānīs, the Dhikrīs, the Parwēzīs, the Anjuman-e Dīndārān and other sects of this kind, who call themselves Muslims although they are disbelievers, who distort Islam, present their beliefs of disbelief as Islam and propagate them — all of these are heretics, and the animals they slaughter are unlawful. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 7/402).

In short, the animals slaughtered by these misguided sects are not lawful. The difference between them and the People of the Book is that the People of the Book acknowledge a revealed religion, whereas these misguided sects come under the ruling of heretics: sheltering behind Islam, they keep their disbelief hidden by means of their foul beliefs and corrupt interpretations while making an outward display of Islam, and through them Islam suffers severe harm.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
