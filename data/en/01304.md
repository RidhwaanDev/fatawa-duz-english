### TITLE
An objection to the impermissibility of marrying a Shīʿah or Qādiyānī, and its answer

### QUESTION
If someone marries a Qādiyānī or Shīʿah girl, why is it impermissible? These women have not themselves apostatised — it was the father who apostatised. Why do they not come under the ruling of the People of the Book, when to all appearances they do accept the Qur'an and hadith, whereas the People of the Book do not accept them at all?

### ANSWER
The People of the Book are those who do not accept the religion of Islam and who are Christians or Jews. But people who consider themselves a sect of Islam while opposing Islam and cutting away its roots are called heretics; there is no room for entering into a marriage tie with them. Heretics and deniers of religion are something quite apart from the People of the Book. Furthermore, the scholars have examined the beliefs of the Shīʿah and the Qādiyānīs and have declared them apostates, and it is not permissible to form a marriage tie with an apostate either.

Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

وكل من يعتقد ديناً سماوياً وله كتاب منزل كصحف إبراهيم عليه السلام وشيث عليه السلام وزبور داود عليه السلام فهو من أهل الكتاب فتجوز مناكحتهم وأكل ذبائحهم كذا في التبيين. (الفتاوى الهندية: القسم الرابع المحرمات بالشرك: ۱/۲۸۱).

Marriage with misguided sects is not permissible. Consider what al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

ولا يجوز نكاح المجوسيات والوثنيات

ويدخل في عبدة الأوثان عبدة الشمس والنجوم والصور التي استحسنوها والمعطلة والزنادقة، والباطنية والإباحية وكل مذهب يكفر به معتقده كذا في فتح القدير. (الفتاوى الهندية: القسم الرابع المحرمات بالشرك: ۱/۲۸۱).

It also states:

ولو قذف عائشة رضي الله تعالى عنها بالزني كفر بالله

من أنكر إمامة أبي بكر الصديق رضي الله تعالى عنه فهو كافر، وكذلك من أنكر خلافة عمر رضي الله تعالى عنه

ويجب إكفار الروافض في قولهم برجعة الأموات إلى الدنيا وتناسخ الأرواح وبانتقال روح الإله إلى الأئمة، وبقولهم في خروج إمام باطن وبتعطيلهم الأمر والنهي إلى أن يخرج الإمام الباطن، وبقولهم إن جبرئيل عليه السلام غلط في الوحي إلى محمد ﷺ دون علي بن أبي طالب رضي الله تعالى عنه، وهؤلاء القوم خارجون عن الملة الإسلام وأحكامهم أحكام المرتدين كذا في الظهيرية. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۲/۲٦٤، الباب التاسع في أحكام المرتدين. ومثله في الفتاوى التاتارخانية: ۵/۵۳۸، كتاب أحكام المرتدين، إدارة القرآن).

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

Those who have left Islam for Qādiyānism are apostates, and the question of whether marriage with them is permissible does not even arise. But those who are Qādiyānī by descent are also heretics and corrupt in religion, and marriage with them too is not permissible. On this very basis the jurists (may Allah have mercy on them) did not allow marriage with the Muʿtazilah, even though they belong to the people of the qiblah.

المناكحة بين أهل السنة وأهل الاعتزال لا يجوز. (خلاصه الفتاوى: ۲/٦)

For this reason the Qādiyānīs do not come under the ruling of the People of the Book; rather they are heretics, and no kind of marriage relationship with them is permissible. (Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 1/286, Naʿīmiyyah Deoband).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

A Shīʿah woman is not lawful for a Muslim man, because the Shīʿah are unbelievers. Some are of the view that the Shīʿah are People of the Book; even so, marriage with a Shīʿah woman is not permissible, for the following reasons:

(1) Most scholars do not count the Shīʿah as People of the Book, so caution is obligatory.

(2) In their view, only that Shīʿah is a person of the Book whose father and grandfather were also Shīʿah. If a Muslim becomes Shīʿah, neither he nor his own children come under the ruling of the People of the Book; rather he is an apostate, and marriage with such a woman is forbidden. Once marriage to a Shīʿah woman were permitted, marriages would begin to take place without any investigation into whether the woman belongs to the People of the Book or is an apostate, and in this way the door to unlawful relations would be opened.

(3) The great majority of ordinary people already regard the Shīʿah as a sect of the Muslims, and permitting marriage to a Shīʿah woman would only confirm them in this mistaken belief. As a result, it is not unlikely that ignorant people would give a Muslim woman in marriage to a Shīʿah man, which is absolutely forbidden. Treating the Shīʿah as Muslims has other dangerous consequences as well: mixing freely with them places one's faith in serious danger.

For these reasons there is no justification whatever for marrying a Shīʿah woman. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 5/90).

See further: Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 10/330, Mubawwab wa Murattab; and Fatāwā Farīdiyyah: 4/476, 479.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
