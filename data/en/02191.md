### TITLE
The ruling on an agent appointed to sell selling on commission

### QUESTION
Zayd appointed ʿUmar as his agent to sell a certain building, and told him: sell it at a minimum of 12,000 per square metre with a 4 per cent commission; but if you sell it for more — say you sell it for 18,000 — then of the additional 6,000, half will be yours and half mine. Is this arrangement permissible in the sharia or not?

### ANSWER
An arrangement in which a commission agent is paid a percentage is permissible and correct on account of the established practice of people and custom.

See what Imdād al-Fatāwā states:

Since there is work and effort involved in brokerage, the wage is correct. Once the permissibility of the wage has been established, among its conditions is that the wage be specified, and this too is one form of specification, so it appears to be permissible. (Imdād al-Fatāwā: 3/363, "An examination of some rulings concerning the broker").

Imdād al-Aḥkām states:

Question: There are two men, Zayd and Bakr. Zayd said to Bakr: I have goods, and if you use your own connections to get them sold to someone, we will give you such-and-such an amount as commission. Is it correct to take commission in this way or not?

Answer: Al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah, on the authority of al-Dhakhīrah, has written that this case is unlawful, and Shāmī too has transmitted its prohibition from al-Tātārkhāniyyah; but he has transmitted from Muḥammad ibn Salamah that there is room for it, and Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Thānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) adopts permissibility in this case.

والجواب عن الفساد للجهالة، أن هذه الجهالة لا يفضي إلى النزاع، فكانت يسيرة، وهي لا يفسد الإجارة والبيع،

And in this age the need for it is also very great; so it is better to declare it permissible. واللہ اعلم (Imdād al-Aḥkām: 3/589, "The ruling on the broker's fee").

The proofs have been set out in detail earlier, and may be consulted there.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
