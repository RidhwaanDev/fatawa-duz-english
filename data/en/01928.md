### TITLE
The ruling on recovering the costs of a lawsuit from the buyer

### QUESTION
ʿAmr bought something from Zayd, and the price was settled for a fixed date. Even after the date had passed ʿAmr did not pay the price, so Zayd filed a lawsuit. The question now is whether Zayd may recover the costs of the lawsuit from ʿAmr, the buyer, or not.

### ANSWER
The scholars differ over recovering the costs of a lawsuit from the buyer. Some scholars, such as Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā ʿAbd al-Ḥayy Lakhnawī (may Allah have mercy on him) and others, hold that it is not permissible to recover the costs of a lawsuit from the buyer. Certain other scholars, such as Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Rashīd Aḥmad Gangohī (may Allah have mercy on him) and others, hold that recovering the costs of a lawsuit from the buyer is permissible and sound. Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Muftī ʿAzīz al-Raḥmān Ṣāḥib, after quoting both positions in Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband and discussing the matter at length, said that the view of Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Rashīd Aḥmad Gangohī (may his soul be sanctified) appears preferable, because in this age there has come to be a great falling short in the discharging of rights — indeed this malady had already become widespread before this age — and it is on this basis that the jurists have stated expressly that the holder of a right may, to the extent of his right, take from the property of the one who owes it in whatever way he can, even if it is not of the same kind as his right. (عزیز الفتاوی، جلد اول، ص ٦۲۷، دار الاشاعت، کراچی) ۔

Accordingly, in the case in question, if the buyer, despite having the means, is negligent and refuses to pay, and the seller is compelled to sue in order to recover the debt, then in that situation it is permissible and sound to take the court costs from the recalcitrant buyer.

Imdād al-Fatāwā states:

If someone is compelled, in order to protect his right, to bring a suit — that is, to file a lawsuit — and has to bear heavy expenses because of the wholly hostile proceedings of the opposing party, then in that case the money spent may be recovered, in the view of many scholars, among them Mawlānā Rashīd Aḥmad Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him). (امداد الفتاوی: ۳/۱۲۳) ۔

Objection and answer:

Objection: Some have raised the following difficulty here, namely that the buyer is only the indirect cause while the seller is the direct actor, and the rule is that liability falls upon the direct actor and not upon the indirect cause; on this ground it is impermissible to recover the costs of the lawsuit from the buyer, who is merely the indirect cause. What is the answer to this?

Answer: The answer is that where the jurists have stated this rule they have also stated its exceptions. For example, ʿAllāmah Ibn Nujaym al-Miṣrī (may Allah have mercy on him) said in Al-Ashbāh wa-l-Naẓāʾir that "تضمين الساعي" is an exception to this rule; for although informing against someone is merely an indirect cause, the later scholars have declared it a ground of liability.

Al-Ashbāh wa-l-Naẓāʾir states:

القاعدة التاسعة عشر: إذا اجتمع المباشر والمتسبب أضيف الحكم إلى المباشر

وخرجت عنها مسائل

الخامسة: الإفتاء بتضمين الساعي وهو قول المتأخرين لغلبة السعاية. (الأشباه والنظائر: ۱/٤۰۵، الفن الاول في القواعد الكلية).

وفي مجمع الضمانات: وضمن عند محمد رحمه الله وبه يفتى لغلبة السعاية في زماننا. (مجمع الضمانات: ۱/۳٦۲).
