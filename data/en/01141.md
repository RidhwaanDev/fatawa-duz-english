### TITLE
A woman travelling for Hajj with her father-in-law

### QUESTION
Hajj is obligatory on a woman, but she does not have the money to take a maḥram (a man she may never marry) along with her, and her father-in-law is about to go for Hajj. May this woman go with her father-in-law for the obligatory Hajj or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, the woman may travel for Hajj with her real father-in-law. However, if there is any fear of temptation, she should avoid being alone with him: the man should stay with the other men and the woman with the other women. Even where there is no such fear, they should still keep apart, so as to avoid suspicion and misgivings.

Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

والمحرم الزوج ومن لا يجوز مناكحتها على التأبيد بقرابة أو رضاع أو مصاهرة، كذا في الخلاصة. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۲۱۹).

Shāmī states:

قوله مع زوج أو محرم والمحرم من لا يجوز له مناكحتها على التأبيد بقرابة أو رضاع أو صهرية كما في التحفة. (شامي: ۲/٤٦٤، سعيد).

Elsewhere it states:

قوله مصاهرة

وموطوءات أبنائه وأبناء أولاده وإن سفلوا. (فتاوى الشامي: ۳/۲۸، سعيد).

Zubdat al-Manāsik states:

On the journey of Hajj a woman must have with her a maḥram — a man whom she is forbidden to marry for life, whether that prohibition arises from blood relationship, or from suckling, or from the prohibition created by marriage ties. (Zubdat al-Manāsik, the third condition for the obligation to be discharged, p. 32).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
