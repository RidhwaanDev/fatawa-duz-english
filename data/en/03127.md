### TITLE
The meaning of being a person of nisbah

### QUESTION
We hear from our elders and from some scholars that such and such an elder, or scholar, or devoted worshipper is a person of nisbah. What does it mean to be a person of nisbah? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
The first thing to be understood is that the notion held by some people, that nisbah means certain particular states such as spiritual struggle and exertion, is not correct. Likewise, some people take nisbah to mean pledging allegiance to a shaykh and having a connection with him; this definition too is not correct. The reality of nisbah is set out in Anfās-i ʿĪsā. See there:

The literal meaning of nisbah is attachment and connection, and its technical meaning is a servant's special connection with Allah Most High — namely, unbroken obedience together with sincerity and a predominance of remembrance — and Allah Most High's special kind of connection with the servant, namely His acceptance and good pleasure.

From this it is clear that nisbah is the name of one particular kind and species of connection. The stronger the connection, the stronger the nisbah will be. Every Muslim has a general connection with Allah, glorified be His majesty; but this nisbah is the fruit of a special kind of love and a particular connection. Nisbat o Iḥsān records:

Ḥaḍrat Shāh ʿAbd al-Qādir Rāʾipūrī (may Allah have mercy on him) says in one of his recorded sayings that a special connection with Allah Most High is what is called nisbah. (نسبت و احسان، ص ۲٦)۔

It also records: Shaykh Shihāb al-Dīn Suhrawardī (may his innermost being be sanctified) says that nisbah is the name of a state that comes to the knower of God, by which a strong link with Allah Most High is produced in him. From it wondrous effects arise: a perpetual sense of Allah's presence, relish and longing, godfearingness and purity, a settled disposition of outward and inward humility and lowliness, and a readiness to obey the commands of Allah Most High. The lower self becomes so effaced that a man comes to regard himself like a corpse in the hands of the one who washes it. It is precisely this that is called nisbah in the terminology of the Sufis. (نسبت و احسان، ص ۲۷)۔

Al-Takashshuf ʿan Muhimmāt al-Taṣawwuf states:

Through occupying oneself with remembrance, a strange and delightful state is produced in the heart, and by perseverance it becomes firmly established. In the terminology of the Sufis this is called nisbah.

Al-Qawl al-Jamīl, under the seventh section, records:

مرجع الطرق كلها إلى تحصيل هيئات نفسانية تسمي عندهم بالنسبة لأنها انتساب وارتباط بالله عز وجل وبالسكينة والنور وحقيقتها كيفية حالة في النفس الناطقة من باب التشبيه بالملائكة أو التطلع إلى الجبروت، وتفصيله أن العبد إذا داوم على الطاعات والطهارات والأذكار حصل له صفة قائمة بالنفس الناطقة وملكة راسخة لهذا التوجه.

Translation: the point to which all the paths of the shaykhs return — that is, their aim, their end and their outcome — is the acquisition of a certain state, which the Sufis call nisbah, by means of which the servant attains attachment and linkage to Allah Most High. This same nisbah is what is called sakīnah, and it is also called nūr. The reality of nisbah is that it is the name of a state that permeates the rational soul, on account of which an angelic quality is produced within the soul and a capacity is developed for receiving things from the higher world.

The detail of this is that when a person perseveres in acts of obedience, in purity, in the remembrances and so on, a state is thereby produced in his soul on account of which he acquires a firmly rooted disposition to do every deed for the pleasure of Allah. This is what is called nisbah. (ماخوذ از نسبت و احسان)۔

ʿIrfān-i Muḥabbat, the commentary on Fayḍān-i Muḥabbat, states:

(When this special kind of connection comes about between Allah, glorified be His majesty, and the servant, then) that servant's solitude and his public life alike are steeped in the remembrance of Allah; not a single moment of his passes in heedlessness of Allah; he sacrifices every desire and longing of life for the command of Allah Most High; and the remembrance of Allah Most High becomes ingrained in his heart and mind. (جلد اول، ص ۵٤)۔

In sum, whoever has this state produced within him will, in whatever condition he may be and whatever work he may be engaged in, keep in mind what the command of Allah, glorified and sanctified, is at that moment; and the one in whom this state comes about is called a person of nisbah.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
