### TITLE
A study of wiping over cloth socks

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to wipe over socks that are not thick — that is, thin ones — or not?

### ANSWER
Thin socks made of cloth, wool or cotton may not be wiped over according to any of the four imams.

In the view of Imam Abū Ḥanīfah (may Allah have mercy on him), the conditions for wiping over socks are these: they must be thick; water must not seep through them; it must be possible to walk two miles in them; and they must stay up on the shin without being tied.

Tadhkirat al-Rashīd states:

Question: Ḥaḍrat Gangohī (may Allah have mercy on him) was asked: if socks are made of bānāt or kashmīrah or something similar, or of some thick, heavy cotton cloth, is it permissible to wipe over them or not?

Answer: A sock made of cloth thick enough that it does not soak up water and does not slip down while walking is valid, and wiping over it is permissible. (Tadhkirat al-Rashīd: 1/182). (Bānāt is the name given to a thick woollen cloth.)

Mufti Niẓām al-Dīn (may Allah have mercy on him), the mufti of Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband, writes:

There is room to permit wiping over non-leather socks as well — whether nylon, cotton or anything else — provided they have the particular qualities and characteristics of leather socks. Those characteristics are: that leather socks cover the feet along with the ankles, so that no part of the covered limb is visible from above; that the moisture of the wiping does not reach inside; and that if one walks in them without shoes and without tying them, one can walk a mile or two in such a way that they neither tear nor slip down from the shin. So any non-leather sock — nylon, cotton or wool — that has these qualities, that is, one thick enough that the moisture of the wiping does not reach the body and strong enough that one can walk a mile or two in it without shoes and without tying it, and it neither tears nor comes off the foot, may be wiped over just like leather socks. (Niẓām al-Fatāwā: 1/42).

Thin socks, however, may not be wiped over.

In the view of the Mālikīs, the conditions for wiping over socks are these: leather must be stitched onto the upper part of the socks; that leather must be pure; the socks must cover the ankles; and it must be possible to walk in them easily.

In the view of the Shāfiʿīs, the socks must be thick, and according to one view they must also have a leather sole, while another view holds that a leather sole is not necessary. Imam al-Nawawī (may Allah have mercy on him) wrote in Sharḥ al-Muhadhdhab:

أما ما لا يمكن متابعة المشي فيه لرقته فلا يجوز المسح عليه بلا خلاف. (شرح المهذب: ۱/۵۰۱، باب المسح على الخفين، ط: دار الفكر).

The Ḥanbalīs likewise mention that the socks must be close-woven, that is, thick, that walking in them must be easy, and that they must stay up on the shin without any need to tie them.

Mawlānā ʿAbd al-Raḥmān Mubārakpūrī, who did not follow any school of law, also laid down the condition that they be thick.

Tuḥfat al-Aḥwadhī states:

والراجح عندي أن الجوربين إذا كانا صفيقتين ثخينين فهما في معنى الخفين يجوز المسح عليهما وأما إذا كانا رقيقين بحيث لا يستمسكان على القدمين بلا شد ولا يمكن المشي فيهما ليسا في معنى الخفين. (تحفة الأحوذي: ۱/۱۱۲/۹۲، باب ما جاء في المسح على الجوربين والنعلين).

The author of Tuḥfat al-Aḥwadhī (may Allah have mercy on him) has written that the words of ʿAllāmah Ibn Taymiyyah indicate that socks in which walking is possible take the same ruling as leather socks, and that wiping over them is permissible.

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

A sock that is not of leather but is thick enough that water does not seep through it, and in which walking a mile is not difficult, may also be wiped over: the sharia allows a resident to do so for one day and one night, and a traveller for three days and nights. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 5/195, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

Before this, Ḥaḍrat also expressed hesitation about wiping over leather-soled socks; that ruling is in the ninth volume. But the ruling permitting it is dated 94 AH, whereas the ruling counselling caution against wiping over leather-soled socks is dated 59 AH.

In short, wiping over thick socks is permissible, and wiping over thin socks is not valid.
