### TITLE
Sharia weights and measures expressed in modern units

### QUESTION
What are the amounts of the ṣāʿ, the mudd, the mile, the farsakh, the qafīz, the wasq, the nisab of gold and silver, the blood money, and so on, in modern weights and measures?

### ANSWER
The amounts of the sharia weights and measures in modern measurement may be seen in the table below.

Ṣāʿ: 3.184272 kilograms = 8 raṭl = 4 mudd = 273 tolas = 1040 dirhams
Mudd: 796.068 grams = 2 raṭl = 68.25 tolas = 260 dirhams
Raṭl: 398.034 grams = ½ mudd = 34.125 tolas = 130 dirhams
Wasq (60 ṣāʿ): 191.05632 kilograms = 480 raṭl = 240 mudd = 16380 tolas = 62400 dirhams
Tola: 11.664 grams = 3.8095 dirhams
Qafīz (12 ṣāʿ)*: 38.211264 kilograms = 96 raṭl = 48 mudd = 3276 tolas = 12480 dirhams

* حاشية الهداية: ۳/۲٤: وفي بعض كتب اللغة أن القفيز مكيال قدر اثنا عشر صاعاً.

The table above has been drawn up according to the reckoning of the sharia weights. Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 4/93 gives the same reckoning, except that it differs over the well-known reckoning of the dirham. According to Aḥsan al-Fatāwā, therefore, the table would be as follows:

(1) According to our fatwa the mahr fāṭimī is 480 dirhams; for the details see Kitāb al-Nikāḥ, Bāb al-Mahr. (2) and (3): the well-known view has been adopted here out of caution.

That is roughly 78 kilometres. Most of the senior scholars (may Allah have mercy on them) adopted this view.

Then there is 45 sharia miles, that is 82.296 kilometres. This is the position given as the fatwa by the imams of Khwārazm, and other scholars have adopted it; but they have fixed it at 78 kilometres, whereas converting 45 sharia miles directly into kilometres gives a figure somewhat above 82 kilometres.

And there is 48 sharia miles, that is 87.7824 kilometres. Some scholars have adopted this view.
