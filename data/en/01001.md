### TITLE
Paying zakat in instalments

### QUESTION
If a person wishes to pay his zakat month by month in instalments rather than as a single lump sum, what is the ruling on paying it in this way?

### ANSWER
In the case described, paying the zakat in instalments is perfectly correct and valid, and the zakat will be discharged by it.

Consider what al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

وشرط صحة أدائها نية مقارنة له أي للأداء ولو حكماً أو مقارنة بعزل ما وجب كله أو بعضه. (الدر المختار: ۲/۲۷۰، سعيد).

Kitāb al-Fatāwā states:

The sharia has allowed a great deal of latitude in the payment of zakat. Zakat may be paid before a year has passed over the nisab, and after the year has passed there is still time in hand, so that a person may pay later as his circumstances and opportunities allow. Even so, he should try to pay the zakat as early as he possibly can. In the same way zakat may be given in a single sum or in instalments, so it is also sufficient to pay it at the rate of a hundred rupees a month. (کتاب الفتاوی: ۳/۳۲۳، زمزم).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

It is not necessary to hand over the whole amount at once in Ramadan; the zakat is discharged even when it is paid a little at a time. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۹/٤٦۷، مبوب و مرتب).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
