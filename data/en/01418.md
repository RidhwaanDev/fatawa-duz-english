### TITLE
Purity from ritual impurity during ṭawāf

### QUESTION
Is it necessary during ṭawāf to be free of ritual impurity, that is, of minor impurity (ḥadath aṣghar) and major impurity (ḥadath akbar), or not? In other words, if a person performs ṭawāf without ablution or in a state of major impurity, will that ṭawāf count or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
According to the sound position, being free of ritual impurity during ṭawāf is necessary (wājib). If a person performs ṭawāf in a state of major impurity or without ablution he is sinful; repeating a ṭawāf performed in a state of major impurity, menstruation or post-natal bleeding is necessary, while repeating a ṭawāf performed without ablution is recommended. In both cases a penalty becomes due if the ṭawāf is not repeated. The details are as follows.

Muʿallim al-Ḥujjāj states:

Ruling: If he performed the whole of the ṭawāf al-ziyārah, or most of it, without ablution, he should offer a sacrifice. If he performed the ṭawāf al-qudūm, the farewell ṭawāf, a voluntary ṭawāf, or less than half of the ṭawāf al-ziyārah without ablution, he should give half a ṣāʿ in charity for each circuit; and if the charity for all the circuits together amounts to the value of a sacrifice, he should reduce it slightly. In all these cases, if he performs ablution and repeats the ṭawāf, both the expiation and the sacrifice lapse.

Ruling: If he performed the whole of the ṭawāf al-ziyārah, or most of it, in a state of major impurity or menstruation, a badanah — that is, a whole camel or a whole cow — will be necessary; and if he performed the ṭawāf al-qudūm, the farewell ṭawāf or a voluntary ṭawāf in these states, a goat will be necessary. In all these cases the expiation lapses if the ṭawāf is repeated in a state of purity.

Ruling: Repeating a ṭawāf performed in a state of major impurity, menstruation or post-natal bleeding is necessary, and repeating one performed without ablution is recommended. (Muʿallim al-Ḥujjāj, p. 185, on omitting one of the necessary acts of Hajj).
