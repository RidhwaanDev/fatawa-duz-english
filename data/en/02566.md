### TITLE
Using human bone in dentistry

### QUESTION
Nowadays artificial teeth are fixed into the jaw and are not taken out again and again, and to fix them a paste is prepared from human bones. Is it permissible to use a paste made from human bones to fix teeth in this way or not? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
The scholars differ over the use of human body parts as a means of treatment. Even those who allow it have permitted it only at the level of compulsion, for the purpose of saving a life, whereas in the case described there is no compulsion at all. Moreover, animal bone can serve the purpose — indeed it is already doing so — and therefore using human bone is impermissible.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
