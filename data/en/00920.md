### TITLE
An objection concerning the report of ʿAmr ibn al-ʿĀṣ

### QUESTION
An objection: regarding the report of ʿAmr ibn al-ʿĀṣ (may Allah be pleased with him), in which he said "stay a while at my grave so that I may take comfort", the Salafīs object that it conflicts with the hadith which states that the two questioning angels come only after the people have left.

Consider what al-Bukhārī records:

عن أنس رضي الله تعالى عنه عن النبي ﷺ قال: العبد إذا وضع في قبره وتولي وذهب أصحابه حتى أنه يسمع قرع نعالهم أتاه ملكان

الحديث.

What is the answer to this?

### ANSWER
In connection with the report of ʿAmr ibn al-ʿĀṣ (may Allah be pleased with him), the word "تولي" means "فرغوا عن دفنه" — "أي تولي أصحابه عن التدفين وذهبوا عن التدفين" — that is, his companions have finished the burial and have turned away from it. If it were taken to mean that the people have left the graveyard, then, if people were to remain sitting at someone's grave for a whole year, would there be no questioning at all?

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
