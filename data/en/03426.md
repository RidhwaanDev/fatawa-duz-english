### TITLE
The ruling on repetition in the prostration of recitation on account of the unity of the sitting

### QUESTION
If someone recites a verse of prostration in the first rakʿah and then recites that same verse in the second rakʿah as well, how many prostrations will be obligatory? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
By analogy only one prostration will be obligatory, because the whole prayer is a single sitting, and on account of the unity of the sitting only one prostration will be obligatory. But by juristic preference two prostrations will be obligatory. The fatwā is upon analogy.

Consider what is mentioned in Tuḥfat al-Fuqahāʾ:

أما إذا كرر آية السجدة في الصلاة فإن كانت في ركعة واحدة لا تجب إلا سجدة واحدة لاتحاد المجلس حقيقة وإن قرأها في كل ركعة فالقياس أن لا يجب إلا سجدة واحدة، هو قول أبي يوسف رحمه الله لاتحاد المجلس حقيقة وفي الاستحسان يجب بكل تلاوة سجدة وهو قول محمد لأنه لا حرج في الوجوب لأن تكرار آية سجدة في كل ركعة نادرة في الصلاة لأنها ليست بموضع التعليم. (تحفة الفقهاء، ص ۲۳۷، ط: بيروت).

In Kashf al-Asrār it is mentioned:

القسم الثاني من القياس وهو الذي ترجح على الاستحسان بقوة أثره الباطن، قسم عز وجوده

ومنها ما إذا قرأ السجدة في ركعة فسجدها ثم أعادها في الركعة الأخرى

الخ. (كشف الاسرار: ٤/۱۰، ط: كراچي).

وفي الخانية قال: وبالقياس نأخذ. (فتاوى قاضيخان بهامش الهندية: ۱/۱۵۸).

The whole sitting takes a single ruling. Consider a few parallels to this:

(1) If blood came out little by little and became flowing blood, then if the sitting is one, the ablution is broken; otherwise not.

(2) If vomiting occurred little by little and, when taken together, filled the mouth, then if the sitting is one, the ablution is broken; otherwise not.

(3) For the establishment of the prescribed punishment for fornication it is necessary that the confession be made in four separate sittings; if it was confessed repeatedly in a single sitting, it will be counted as one confession only.

(4) If the blessed name of the Prophet (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) is repeated again and again in one sitting, the invocation of blessings is obligatory once.

(5) If a gross mistake was made in the first rakʿah and it was corrected in the second rakʿah, the prayer is valid.

(6) If an obligatory element is omitted in the first rakʿah, it is made good at the end by the prostration of forgetfulness.

(7) In the sitting of a sale, the offer and acceptance are regarded as valid even though there be a long interval of time between the two.

(8) In the sitting of divorce, the woman's exercise of the option given to her, and the husband's granting of that option, are regarded as valid.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
