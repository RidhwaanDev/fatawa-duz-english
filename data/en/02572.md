### TITLE
Abortion where the child will be disabled

### QUESTION
A woman is six months pregnant. Various doctors have said that the child will be more or less disabled, that it will be quite incapable of standing, sitting, walking or moving about, and that other disorders are present as well; there is a danger to its mother's life too. Is an abortion permitted in this state or not?

### ANSWER
If the doctors are agreed that the child will be disabled and that there is a danger to the mother's life, then in that case there ought to be scope for an abortion. But after the soul has been breathed in, most of the authorities have not permitted an abortion.

Jadīd Fiqhī Mabāḥith states:

The jurists have stated explicitly that once the soul has been breathed into the child in the womb, abortion is by agreement not permissible.

The stage at which the soul is breathed into the child comes after 120 days, that is, after four months. On this basis, abortion is not permissible after four months by the agreement of the scholars. If, however, the woman's health deteriorates altogether and, according to an expert and experienced doctor, her life cannot be saved without an abortion, then in that case, in the light of such accepted principles as

"يختار أهون البليتين"

and

"لوكان أحدهما أعظم ضرراً من الآخر فإن الأشد يزال بأخف"

there ought to be permission for an abortion in order to save the woman's life. (جدید فقہی مباحث: ۱/۳۷۰) ۔

The decision of the Islamic Fiqh Academy of Makkah Mukarramah:

At its session, after consideration and discussion of the views of expert doctors and of the members on this subject, the following decision was taken by a majority:

If the pregnancy is of one hundred and twenty (120) days, aborting it is not permissible, even if it is established by medical diagnosis that the child is malformed. If, however, it is established by the report of a committee of expert and trustworthy doctors that the continuation of the pregnancy is certainly dangerous to the mother's life, then in such a case aborting the child is permissible in order to remove the greater harm, whether the child is malformed or not.

If one hundred and twenty days have not passed over the pregnancy, and it is established — on the basis of the report of a committee of expert and trustworthy doctors and of technical investigation by means of experimental methods and instruments — that the child is malformed to so dangerous a degree that it cannot be treated, and that if it remains and is born at its due time its life will be a burden and a source of pain both to it and to the members of the household, then in such a case aborting it is permissible at the parents' request. Together with this decision, the session urges doctors and parents to keep the fear of Allah and due caution before them in this matter. (عصر حاضر کے پیچیدہ مسائل کا شرعی حل، ص ۲۴۵) ۔
