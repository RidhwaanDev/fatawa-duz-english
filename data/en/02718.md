### TITLE
The ruling on fitting hair on a bald head

### QUESTION
A man's hair has fallen out because of an illness and he has gone bald. Is he permitted to have artificial hair fitted on his head or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question it is permissible and correct for a bald man, in order to remove a defect, to have his own hair fitted or to have plastic hair fitted; but it is impermissible to fit another person's hair. Then, in ablution and the ritual bath, if the water reaches the roots, that is fine. Otherwise, if it can be removed, it will be necessary to remove it and let the water reach; and if removing it is difficult, then letting water run over it, or wiping over it, will be enough.

Doctor Muftī ʿAbd al-Wāḥid (MBBS) writes:

Hair transplantation (Hair Transplantation): two methods of it are rationally possible:

(1) The hair is taken from some other place together with its root and set into the skin of the head, that is, it is implanted (Implant). If it is hair from one's own body, this is permissible. If the hair is obtained from another person, it is not permissible, because it is not permissible to fit one person's hair on another; and if the hair has been bought from another person for a price, that is a second defect.

(2) Skin along with its hair is taken from some other place, the skin of the head is scraped, and it is attached to it. If it is skin from one's own body, it is permissible; if it is skin from another person's body, it is not permissible.

٭ Hair by Hair process

In this method human hair is embedded in a natural-looking way into an artificial membrane or skin, so that any style of hair can be made. This artificial membrane or skin also has pores through which sweat and water pass out. If some natural hair of one's own is present on the head, it is trimmed down to a particular required length, and then this membrane is joined by means of a special liquid to the natural hair of the head, right down to its roots. This liquid is waterproof, that is, it does not absorb water and it stops water from reaching the natural hair. Once the membrane has been fitted, two months pass easily, until the natural hair underneath grows out. When the hair underneath has grown, the membrane is taken off, the natural hair on the head is trimmed to the required length, and the membrane is stuck on again. (Marīḍ awr Muʿālij ke Islāmī Aḥkām, p. 386).
