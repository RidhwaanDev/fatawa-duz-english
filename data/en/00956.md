### TITLE
Zakat on a dower before it is received

### QUESTION
If a woman's dower is owed by her husband and he has not yet paid it, is she required to pay zakat on it or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, zakat is not due before the dower is received.

Al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq states:

قسم أبو حنيفة رحمه الله الدين على ثلاثة أقسام قوي

وضعيف

وهو بدل ما ليس بمال كالمهر والوصية وبدل الخلع

وفي الضعيف لا تجب ما لم يقبض نصاباً ويحول الحول بعد القبض عليه. (البحر الرائق: ۲/۲۰۷، كوئٹه، والشامي: ۲/۳۰٦، باب زكاة المال، سعيد، والفقه على المذاهب الأربعة: ۱/۹٦۱).

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states:

Zakat is not due on it before it is received. (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband: 6/57; and Īḍāḥ al-Nawādir, part two: 11).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

When the husband hands the dower over to the woman, and it amounts to the nisab, and a year passes over it, then she is required to pay zakat on it. If it is less than the nisab, but she already holds enough wealth which, added to the dower, would make up a full nisab, then zakat is paid on the combined amount. If a full nisab is still not reached, no zakat is due on her. Likewise, no zakat is due before the dower is received. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 9/377, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).
