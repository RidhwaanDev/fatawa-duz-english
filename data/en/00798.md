### TITLE
Women going to the Eid ground

### QUESTION
Since, in the light of authentic hadiths, women used to go to the Eid ground, why do Muslim women in South Africa not go to the Eid ground?

### ANSWER
In the time of the Prophet ﷺ women used to go to the Eid ground, and there is evidence that they went out for the other prayers as well. In the present age, however, because of the corruption of the times, the later scholars are agreed that it is forbidden for women to go to the Eid ground or to the mosques. This question has already been discussed in detail under "The chapters on leading the prayer, section two: the rulings of congregational prayer"; that discussion may be consulted there.
