### TITLE
The meaning of the hadith "فإذا قدمت فالكيس الكيس"

### QUESTION
There is a hadith whose meaning I cannot understand, so I am taking the liberty of asking you about it. Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī records the hadith of Jābir (may Allah be pleased with him) and his own account: his camel was slow, and then, through the attention and supplication of the Prophet ﷺ, it became swift. The Messenger ﷺ then asked, "Have you married?" I said, "Yes." He said, "A virgin or a woman who had been married before?" I said, "One who had been married before." He said, "Why did you not marry a virgin?" I said that my sisters had been left behind, and that I had married a woman of experience who would be able to look after them. Then he said:

"أما انك قادم فإذا قدمت فالكيس الكيس"

That is, use your good sense once you reach home (بخاري ۱/۲۸۲).

What is meant by the words of the Prophet ﷺ, "فالكيس الكيس" — use your good sense?

### ANSWER
The scholars have given several explanations in answer to this, of which two are important.

The first is: once you reach your home in Madinah, seek a child, and be careful in that — that is, avoid intercourse during menstruation and the like.

In this servant's view, however, the straightforward explanation is this: when a wife and sisters come together in one house, quarrels and sharp exchanges sometimes break out. So the Prophet ﷺ advised Jābir (may Allah be pleased with him) to avoid partiality and taking sides, and to act with intelligence and care. In other words, to keep a balance between a wife and sisters one has to cross a bridge as narrow as the Ṣirāṭ, and that takes intelligence and care.
