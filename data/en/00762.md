### TITLE
An objection to the admonition given before the sermon, and its answer

### QUESTION
What do the scholars say about this matter: the trustees of a mosque object to the admonition and talk given before the Friday prayer. They say that in the time of the Prophet ﷺ there was only a single sermon, in Arabic, so there is no need for an admonition, and that this is a new practice in religion. The mosque in question is the largest in the city and has a large number of worshippers.

### ANSWER
The admonition and talk given in the local language before the Arabic sermon is established by the practice of the Companions, so it is not correct to call it a new practice. We do not regard it as a sunnah, but as a matter of benefit.

Consider what al-Mustadrak of al-Ḥākim states:

أخبرنا أحمد بن سليمان الفقيه حدثنا إسماعيل بن إسحاق القاضي ثنا أحمد بن يونس ثنا عاصم بن محمد بن زيد عن أبيه قال: كان أبو هريرة رضي الله عنه يقوم يوم الجمعة إلى جانب المنبر فيطرح اعقاب ن عليه في ذراعيه ثم يقبض على رمانة المنبر يقول: قال أبو القاسم ﷺ قال محمد ﷺ قال رسول الله ﷺ قال الصادق المصدوق ﷺ ثم يقول في بعض ذلك ويل للعرب من شر قد اقترب فاذا سمع حركة باب المقصورة بخروج الإمام جلس. هذا حديث صحيح ولم يخرجاه. (المستدرك للحاكم: ۱/۱۰۸، كتاب العلم)

The substance of this narration is that on Fridays Abū Hurayrah (may Allah be pleased with him) would stand beside the pulpit and give an admonition drawn from the hadiths, and when he heard the imam coming out he would sit down. Al-Ḥākim graded this narration as authentic.

In al-Mawḍūʿāt al-Kabīr, Mullā ʿAlī al-Qārī (may Allah have mercy on him) records this practice of Tamīm al-Dārī (may Allah be pleased with him) during the caliphates of ʿUmar and ʿUthmān (may Allah be pleased with them): he asked ʿUmar (may Allah be pleased with him) for permission to give an admonition before the Friday sermon. ʿUmar (may Allah be pleased with him) refused at first, then gave him permission for a single day and said: give the admonition before I come out for the Friday prayer. Consider what al-Mawḍūʿāt al-Kabīr states:

وأخرج ابن عساكر رحمه الله عن حميد بن عبد الرحمن أن تميماً الداري رضي الله عنه استأذن عمر رضي الله عنه في القصص سنين، فأبي أن يأذن له، فاستأذن في يوم واحد، فلما أكثر على ه، قال له ما تقول؟ قال: أقرأ عليهم القرآن، وآمرهم بالخير، وأنها هم عن الشر، قال عمر رضي الله عنه ذلك الذبح ثم قال: عظ قبل أن أخرج في الجمعة، فكان يفعل ذلك يوماً واحداً في الجمعة.
(موضوعاتِ كبير: ص ۲۰، مير محمد كتب خانه كراچي)

(He probably refused at first because he did not know what the admonition would consist of; then, when Tamīm al-Dārī (may Allah be pleased with him) mentioned the Qur'an, he gave permission.)

Mawlānā ʿAbd al-Ḥayy (may Allah have mercy on him) has recorded that in the time of ʿUthmān (may Allah be pleased with him) Tamīm al-Dārī (may Allah be pleased with him) gave this admonition on two days rather than one. This shows that at first the talk was given only before the Friday sermon, and that when its benefit came to be felt more strongly a further day was added. (مجموعة رسائل اللكنوي "إقامة الحجة على ان الإكثار في التعبد ليس ببدعة: ص ۱۳)

Mufti Maḥmūd Ḥasan Gangohī (may Allah have mercy on him) also quoted the narration about Tamīm al-Dārī (may Allah be pleased with him) from al-Mawḍūʿāt al-Kabīr and said that it shows that ʿUmar (may Allah be pleased with him) granted Tamīm al-Dārī (may Allah be pleased with him) permission to give an admonition before the Friday prayer after he had asked repeatedly. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 8/254, 255, chaptered and arranged edition, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah)

See also: Imdād al-Aḥkām: 1/277, on the ruling of giving an admonition before the sermon; Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 1/264; and Kifāyat al-Muftī: 3/214.
