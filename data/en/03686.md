### TITLE
The ruling on a contract of muḍārabah with a motor-vehicle company

### QUESTION
A company has been set up in Mauritius: people give money and the company trades in motor vehicles as their agent. After giving the money it is necessary to leave it for at least six months; before that the principal cannot take it. After six months the account of profit and loss is drawn up. The company's target is to earn six per cent profit annually on the capital. Then, after six months, if less than six per cent profit has been obtained, the capital together with the profits is given to the principal; and if it is more than six per cent, [the excess] will belong to the agent. In the case of a loss, there will be a reduction in proportion to the capital. In every case the agent takes a fee for running the company. Is this arrangement permissible in the Sharīʿah or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question, from one side there is work and from the other side there is capital; therefore this is a contract of muḍārabah. The agent is the muḍārib and the principal is the owner of the capital. In muḍārabah it is permissible and correct for the muḍārib to take a wage along with a percentage of the profit; in the view of the muftīs of the present age a partner may become a hired worker.

However, the method of dividing the profit is, to all appearances, not sound. Let it be corrected in this way: that whatever profit accrues to the company will be divided by percentages, and let percentages be fixed for this. And, God forbid, if a loss occurs, then the owners of the capital will be responsible for it; the company (the muḍārib) will not be responsible for it.

Furthermore, a contract of muḍārabah may also be made for a fixed term, namely that no one may take his money back before six months and that it will be returned after six months; but let an agreement be drawn up beforehand and signatures taken upon it.

Consider: it is recorded in Al-Durr al-Mukhtār:

المضاربة

وشرعاً عقد شركة في الربح بمال من جانب رب المال وعمل من جانب المضارب. وفي رد المحتار: قوله وشرعاً عقد شركة، قال في النهاية: ومن يحذو حذوه أنها دفع المال إلى غيره ليتصرف فيه ويكون الربح بينهما على ما شرطا. (الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ٥/٦٤٥، سعيد).

وفيه أيضاً: وشرطها:

وكون الربح بينهما شائعاً فلو عين قدراً فسدت، وكون نصيب كل منهما معلوماً عند العقد. (الدر المختار: ٥/٦٤٨، سعيد).

Islām awr Jadīd Maʿāshī Masāʾil mentions:

If, at the very time of entering into the contract of muḍārabah, the two parties agree that neither party will terminate the muḍārabah within a specified period except in a few particular circumstances, then this does not appear to be against any principle of the Sharīʿah — especially in the light of the hadith in which it has come:

المسلمون على شروطهم إلا شرطاً أحل حراماً أو حرم حلالاً. (رواه الترمذي، رقم: ١٣٥٢، وقال: هذا حديث حسن صحيح).

(اسلام اور جدید معاشی مسائل: ٥/٤٧، ط: ادارۂ اسلامیات) ۔

A partner may become a hired worker, and for him a monthly salary may be fixed in addition to the percentage of the profit. Consider: (احسن الفتاوی: ٧/٣٢٨، سعید) ۔
