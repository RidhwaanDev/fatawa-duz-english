### TITLE
An examination of the report that the accepted pebbles are taken up from the pillars

### QUESTION
It is reported that the accepted pebbles are taken up while the rejected ones stay lying there. Is this report authentic?

### ANSWER
The report mentioned above is weak. It may, however, be explained in this way: in earlier times there was no arrangement for clearing the pebbles away. In the very lifetime of the Prophet ﷺ the Muslims performed two Hajjs — one under the leadership of Ḥaḍrat Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq (may Allah be pleased with him), and at the other the Prophet ﷺ himself was present — yet the number of pebbles was very small. From this it appears that the accepted pebbles were taken up and the rejected ones were left behind. In view of the weakness of the report, however, this is not certain; it remains at the level of supposition.

Consider the following hadith:

عن أبي الطفيل قال سألت ابن عباس رضي الله تعالى عنه عن الحصى الذي يرمي في الجمار منذ قام الإسلام، فقال: ما تقبل منهم رفع وما لم يتقبل منهم ترك ولو لا ذلك لسد ما بين الجبلين. (السنن الكبرى للبيهقي: ۵/۱۲۸، باب أخذ الحصى لرمي جمرة العقبة، بيروت).

In the chain of this narration the transmitter Yazīd ibn Sinān is weak.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
