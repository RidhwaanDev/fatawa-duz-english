### TITLE
The ruling on reciting the ṣalawāt and the supplication in the first sitting of four rakʿahs of a supererogatory prayer

### QUESTION
Is it correct to recite the blessings upon the Prophet and the supplication in the first sitting of four rakʿahs of a supererogatory prayer or not? If a person does recite them, what will be the ruling on the prayer? And will there be any difference between doing this in an emphasised sunnah and in a non-emphasised one, or not?

### ANSWER
In the Ḥanafī school it is narrated from some of the later jurists that reciting the blessings upon the Prophet and the supplication in the first sitting of four rakʿahs of a non-emphasised sunnah or of a supererogatory prayer is correct, and that no defect will thereby enter the prayer, because every two rakʿahs of a supererogatory prayer are in the ruling of an independent prayer. This, however, is not narrated from the earlier jurists; on this basis one should not do so, but should pray in the ordinary manner — that is, in the first sitting of a four-rakʿah non-emphasised sunnah or supererogatory prayer it is better to confine oneself to the tashahhud alone.

In an emphasised sunnah, however, it is not correct to recite them. If a person recites the blessings upon the Prophet and the supplication after the tashahhud in the first sitting of the four rakʿahs of an emphasised sunnah, then according to the soundest view the prostration of forgetfulness will become obligatory.

Consider what is recorded in al-Durr al-Mukhtār:

ولا يصلي على النبي ﷺ في القعدة الأولى في الأربع قبل الظهر والجمعة وبعدها ولو صلى ناسياً فعليه السهو وقيل لا، شمني، ويستفتح إذا قام إلى الثالثة منها أشبهت الفريضة وفي البواقي من ذوات الأربع يصلي على النبي ﷺ ويستفتح ويتعوذ ولو نذراً لأن كل شفع صلاة وقيل لا يأتي في الكل وصححه في القنية. (الدر المختار: ۲/۱٦، سعيد). (وكذا في البحر الرائق: ۱/۳٤٦، ط: بيروت).

قال في رد المحتار: الأصح أنه لا يصلي ولا يستفتح في سنة الظهر والجمعة وكون كل شفع صلاة على حدة ليس مطرداً في كل الأحكام

لوقوعه في وسط الصلاة لأن الأصل كون الكل صلاة واحدة للاتصال واتحاد التحريمة ومسألة الاستفتاح ونحوه ليست مروية عن المتقدمين وإنما اختيار بعض المتأخرين. (رد المحتار: ۲/۱٦، سعيد).

(وكذا في مراقي الفلاح مع حاشية الطحطاوي، ص ۳۹۲، ط: قديمي، وإمداد الفتاح، ص ٤۳۱).

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh records:

When one prays the four-rakʿah emphasised sunnahs — that is, the four rakʿahs before Ẓuhr and before Jumuʿah and the four after Jumuʿah — he should not recite the blessings upon the Prophet in their first sitting; he should recite only al-Taḥiyyāt and stand up. And if he recited the blessings forgetfully, he should perform the prostration of forgetfulness. Apart from these, when he prays a four-rakʿah non-emphasised sunnah or a supererogatory prayer, he has a choice: he may recite the blessings and the supplication as well in the first sitting, and in the third rakʿah recite Subḥānaka Allāhumma and the taʿawwudh as well, since every pair of rakʿahs of the supererogatory prayers is a separate prayer; or he may, as in the obligatory prayers, recite only al-Taḥiyyāt and stand up, and not recite the thanāʾ and the taʿawwudh in the third rakʿah, but recite only bismillāh al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm and begin Sūrat al-Fātiḥah, and in the last sitting recite the blessings and the supplication and make the salām. According to the sounder view this second manner is the better one. (عمدۃ الفقہ: ۲/۳۱۰، سنن و نوافل کے مخصوص مسائل) ۔
