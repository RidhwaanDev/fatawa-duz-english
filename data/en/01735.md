### TITLE
A vow to spend on students

### QUESTION
A man said: "Every month I will spend 100 rand of my salary on the students of the Dār al-ʿUlūm." Is a vow validly made by these words or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question the sentence mentioned contains no wording of binding commitment, nor does it indicate one; so no vow has taken effect by these words.

Consider what Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ states:

ركن النذر هو الصيغة الدالة عليه وهو قوله لله عز شانه على كذا أو على كذا أو هدي أو صدقة أو مإلى صدقة. (بدائع الصنائع: ۵/۸۱، سعيد).

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states:

Question: While he was unemployed, a man uttered the following sentences, and at the time of uttering these words his intention was firm as well. The sentences are: "I will give a fortieth of my income to the poor and the needy, and a twentieth to the mosques and the Eid prayer ground." Now this man says that working out his income and then taking a fortieth and a twentieth out of it is extremely difficult for him at present. What should he do now? How is he to save himself from this sin?

The answer: If he used exactly the words mentioned in the question, and alongside them said nothing such as "if I get a job" or "if I have an income, then I will do this," and likewise used with those words no expression of binding obligation — such as "for the sake of Allah I will do this," or "it is upon me to do this" — then the words mentioned are neither the words of an oath nor of a vow, and nothing has become binding upon this man. He is free to give in charity as much as he finds easy whenever he wishes. ʿAllāmah Ibn Nujaym al-Miṣrī (may Allah have mercy on him), while setting out the wordings of a vow in his treatise "al-Nadhr bi'l-Taṣadduq", has written:

"أما الصيغة فـ للّه وعلى ونذرت للّه وأناْ أفعل إن كان معلقاً كأناْ أحج إن دخلت الدار بخلاف أنا أحج منجزاً ۔
(آخر الأشباه والنظائر: طبع مصر صفحة: ۱۰٤).

This passage also states explicitly that even if the speaker intends a vow, no vow takes effect by intention alone so long as he does not utter words of binding obligation and the like. (امداد المفتین: ۲/٦۱۲، دار الاشاعت) ۔

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

For a vow, wording of binding commitment is necessary. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱٤/۵۹، مبوب و مرتب) ۔
