### TITLE
The ruling on naming a Muslim girl Fariyāl

### QUESTION
Is it correct to name a Muslim girl Fariyāl or not? And what is its meaning?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, Fariyāl is a Persian word and in fact comes with a fatḥah on the fāʾ. It is compounded of "far" and "yāl". The meanings of "far" are light, beauty and comeliness and so forth, and the meanings of "yāl" in Persian are neck, face and so forth. So now the meaning of Fariyāl will be "one with a beautiful face". Therefore, according to the Sharīʿah, giving the name Fariyāl is permissible and correct.

قال في "المعجم الذهبي": فَر: نور، شأن، مكانة، حسن، جمال

ويال: عنق، عضد، ابن، وجه، شجاع

(المعجم الذهبي، ص ٤۲۵، ٦۱۸).

In Lughāt-i Kishwarī it is recorded: far: dignity, majesty, splendour, light; and yāl: neck, and figuratively the hair of a horse's neck. (لغاتِ کشوری، ص ۳٤۳، ۵۷۹)۔

In Qāmūs al-Fārsiyyah it is mentioned: far: greatness, beauty, competence, power. (٤۸۹، ۸۲٦)۔

موسوعة الأسماء ومعانيها: فريال: التي جاء ت بأمر عجيب وغريب محير

(۱۰۱).

Translation: Fariyāl — one who accomplishes astonishing feats.

The upshot is that the meanings of Fariyāl are: beautiful, and one who accomplishes astonishing feats, or a girl of dignity and majesty.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
