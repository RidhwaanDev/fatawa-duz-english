### TITLE
The ruling on making small pictures

### QUESTION
Is it permissible for me to make the following cloths and curtains or not?

(1) Cloths on which pictures of animals are printed.

(2) Cloths on which there are pictures of human beings.

(3) Cloths on which there are pictures of famous people of the world.

(4) Cloths on which there are pictures of people playing a musical instrument and dancing. (5) Cloths on which there are figures, some of the pictures being of naked people.

I sell these wholesale in large quantities to Muslims and to non-Muslims. Is this permissible? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question it is impermissible for you to make such pictures and to sell cloths bearing pictures, because in these cloths the pictures are intended in their own right. As for the pictures that appear on cloths by way of a label, trading in them incidentally is permissible — yet even so, making a small picture is altogether impermissible. There is, however, permission to make and to sell pictures of things without life. Consider what the noble hadith states:

عن سعيد بن أبي الحسن قال: كنت عند ابن عباس رضي الله عنه إذ أتاه رجل فقال: يا ابن عباس إني إنسان إنما معيشتي من صنعة يدي وإني أصنع هذه التصاوير فقال ابن عباس رضي الله عنه: لا أحدثك إلا ما سمعت رسول الله ﷺ يقول: سمعته يقول: من صور صورة فإن الله معذبه حتى ينفخ فيها الروح وليس بنافخ فيها أبداً فربا الرجل ربوة شديدة واصفر وجهه فقال: ويحك إن أبيت إلا أن تصنع فعليك بهذا الشجر كل شيء ليس فيه روح. (أخرجه البخاري، رقم: ۲۲۲۵، باب بيع التصاوير التي ليس فيها روح وما يكره من ذلك).

Further evidence concerning picture-making has already been given above and may be consulted there.

Ḥaḍrat Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ Ṣāḥib says:

In buying and selling, if the pictures are not what is intended but come in as subordinate to other things — as figures are often printed on clothes, or as is commonly the practice on utensils and other modern manufactured goods — then buying and selling them incidentally is permissible,

كما يستفاد من بلوغ القصد والمرام معزياً للهيثمي (بلوغ، ص: ۱۸) ولما هو من القواعد المسلمة من فقه الأحناف أن كثيراً من الأفعال لا يجوز قصداً ويجوز تبعاً كما صرحوا في جواز بيع الحقوق تبعاً للدار ولا أصالة وقصداً.

But where the buying and selling of the pictures themselves is what is intended, then both buying and selling are impermissible. (تصویر کے شرعی احکام، ص ۸۸) ۔
