### TITLE
Saying "I have no connection with you"

### QUESTION
A man said to his wife, "I have no connection with you; the matter between me and you is finished." Has divorce taken place or not? Is an intention required or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, if the husband intended divorce by the words "I have no connection with you", one irrevocable divorce has taken effect; without an intention no divorce takes effect. The state of his intention can be learned from the husband himself.

Al-Fatāwā al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah states:

وفي الفتاوى: لم يبق بيني وبينك عمل ونوى يقع، كذا في العتابية. (الفتاوى الهندية ۱/۳۷٦، الفصل الخامس في الكنايات. وكذا في البحر الرائق: ۳/۳۰٤، كوئته).

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states:

If the husband's intention in the words "I have no connection with you" was divorce, then one irrevocable divorce has taken effect upon his wife. The state of his intention can be learned from the husband himself. (فتاوی د ار العلوم دیوبند: ۹/۳۹٤، مدلل مکمل) ۔

In another place it is written:

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states plainly that words indicating a severing of the relationship, even if uttered in a state of anger, do not effect divorce without an intention. The following passage of Al-Durr al-Mukhtār bears precisely this sense:

"وفی الغضب توقف الأولان، إن نوی وقع وإلا لا ۔ (فتاوی دار العلوم: ۹/٤۳۷) ۔

The second sentence, "The matter between me and you is finished," is also among the allusive expressions; and when several different allusive sentences are uttered, only one divorce takes effect.

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states:

If the husband utters several different allusive sentences, only one irrevocable divorce takes effect upon his wife, as Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

"البائن لا یلحق البائن، المراد بالبائن الذي لا یلحق ہو ما کان بلفظ الکنایۃ،
لأنہ ہوالذي لیس ظاہراً في إنشاء الطلاق، کذا في الفتح ۔ (فتاوی دار العلوم: ۹/٤۷۱، مکمل و مدلل) ۔

Majmūʿah Qawānīn Islāmī states:

Once an irrevocable divorce has taken effect by an allusive word, no further irrevocable divorce can take effect by an allusive word, whether the same allusive word was used again and again or several different allusive words were used. (مجموعہ قوانین اسلامی ص۱٤۹، دفعہ ۳٤) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
