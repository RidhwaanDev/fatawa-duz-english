### TITLE

Waiving a loan as a way of paying zakat

### QUESTION

Whenever my friend needs money I give it to him. His situation is now such that he cannot return the money. When I gave him the money I made no particular intention. May I now make the intention of zakat for this money? Will my obligation of zakat be discharged by that?

### ANSWER

Making the intention at the time of paying zakat is a condition, and the intention is necessary either at the time of payment or at the time of setting the money aside from one's other wealth. Zakat is not discharged by giving money without the intention, so your zakat has not been discharged. You may, however, give him zakat to the amount of your money and then recover your loan from him, provided that he is entitled to receive zakat.

Consider the following statements of the jurists:

ولا يجوز أداؤها إلا بنية مقارنة لعزل الواجب أو للأداء. (الاختيار لتعليل المختار: ۱/۱۰۸، بيروت).

وقال في الهداية: ولا يجوز أداء الزكاة إلا بنية مقارنة للأداء أو مقارنة لعزل مقدار الواجب لأن الزكاة عبادة فكان من شرطها النية والأصل فيها الاقتران إلا أن الدفع يتفرق فاكتفى بوجودها حالة العزل تيسيراً. (الهداية: ۱/۱۸۸، ط: شركة علمية).
