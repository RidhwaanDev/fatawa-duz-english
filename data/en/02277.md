### TITLE

The ruling on placing the liability for a loss on one partner

### QUESTION

A number of people formed a partnership and some loss occurred in it. Will only the one who did the work be liable, or will the loss be divided among all of them?

### ANSWER

The jurists have said that a condition of disparity in the profit is permissible, but if a loss occurs, the liability for it is shared out equally among all the partners. For example, if each of five men put in 1000 rand and formed a partnership, and a loss of 1000 rand happened to occur, it is not correct to hold one man alone responsible; rather each partner will be responsible for 200 rand. Radd al-Muḥtār states:

مطلب اشتراط الربح متفاوتاً صحيح، بخلاف اشتراط الخسران، قال: فما كان من ربح فهو بينهما على قدر رؤوس أموالهما، وما كان من وضيعة أو تبعة فكذلك، والخلاف أن اشتراط الوضيعة بخلاف قدر رأس المال باطل واشتراط الربح متفاوتاً عندنا صحيح. (فتاوى الشامي: ٤/۳۰۵، سعيد).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
