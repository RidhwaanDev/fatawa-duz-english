### TITLE
The ruling on an estate agent charging a service fee

### QUESTION
Is it permissible for an estate agent to charge a service fee calculated as a percentage, or not?

### ANSWER
An estate agent's service fee comes under the ruling on brokerage, and a broker's fee is permissible calculated as a percentage as well.

See what Shāmī states:

تتمة: قال في التاتارخانية: وفي الدلال والسمسار يجب أجر المثل وما تواضعوا عليه أن في كل عشرة دنانير كذا فذلك حرام عليهم، وفي الحاوي: سئل محمد بن سلمة عن أجرة السمسار، فقال: أرجو أنه لا بأس به وإن كان في الأصل فاسداً لكثره التعامل، وكثير من هذا غير جائز فجوزوه لحاجة الناس إليه كدخول الحمام. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/٦۳، سعيد).

Mālī Muʿāmalāt par Gharar ke Athrāt states:

In the matter of estate agency, neither party knows how much running about the agent will have to do in order to sell a particular house, and the fee too is unknown. But since the uncertainty found in these cases does not become a cause of dispute, and since in the present age such dealings are found in very large numbers and people have need of them day after day, the noble jurists have declared this permissible. (Mālī Muʿāmalāt par Gharar ke Athrāt, p. 90).
