### TITLE
Fixing a set place for the muezzin in the mosque

### QUESTION
Is it permissible in the sharia to fix a place for the muezzin behind the imam in the mosque by spreading a prayer mat for him, or to spread a prayer mat for him at all?

### ANSWER
Muftī Rashīd Aḥmad Ludhyānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) did not consider this correct.

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

The same ruling applies to the muezzin. The custom of setting aside a particular place for him and spreading a separate prayer mat for him is not sound. Whoever reaches the mosque and sits in a place has the better right to it. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: ٦/٤۵۷).

Mawlānā Fatḥ Muḥammad, a student of Mawlānā ʿAbd al-Ḥayy Lakhnawī (may Allah have mercy on him), on the other hand, considered it correct.

Ḥalāl Ḥarām ke Aḥkām, known as ʿIṭr al-Hidāyah, states:

(۲) Property whose use is free to all: this comes under the ruling of endowment property — such as mosques, some wells, bridges, roads and the like. The general public has a right in these, subject to certain necessary considerations. In a mosque, for example, everyone has a right to pray in the first row, but it is necessary to have regard to the special position of the imam, the preacher, the one who relays the call to prayer within the mosque, and the muezzin. (Ḥalāl Ḥarām ke Aḥkām, p. ۳۱٦, the chapter on things that are permitted).

The hadith "ليلني منكم أولوا الأحلام" likewise shows that certain particular people have a greater right to sit near the imam.
