### TITLE
The ruling on turning the face during the adhān for a newborn

### QUESTION
I read in Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā, volume two, that when calling the adhān in the ear of a newborn it is also recommended to turn to the right and the left. Is this the only position, or is there another position as well? If there is, kindly give the reference.

بينوا توجروا.

### ANSWER
The second position on this question is that turning the face is not recommended, because this is done in order to convey the voice, and here conveying the voice is not intended.

Consider the reference:

قال ابن عابدين الشامي رحمه الله: قوله ولو وحده أشار به إلى رد قول الحلواني أنه لا يلتفت لعدم الحاجة إليه. (فتاوى الشامي: ۱/۳۸۷، سعيد).

It is recorded in the books of the Shāfiʿīs as well that there is no turning of the face.

Consider what Iʿānat al-Ṭālibīn states:

أما الأذان في أذن المولود فلا يطلب فيه رفع ولا التفات لعدم فائدته. (إعانة الطالبين: ۱/۵۱۵).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
