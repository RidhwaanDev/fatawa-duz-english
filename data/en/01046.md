### TITLE
Giving zakat to someone who owns a television

### QUESTION
A man is poor in the technical sense, but he owns a television (T.V) and lives at the ordinary standard of life; he also has a good many items lying about that are surplus to his needs. Is such a person entitled to zakat or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, if the television (T.V) he owns is an old one, which is worth very little, then owning it does not make a man wealthy or an owner of the nisāb (the threshold of wealth at which zakat becomes due). Yes, if along with the television (T.V) he has other things too that are surplus to his needs, and they come to the value of the nisāb, then he is an owner of the nisāb; but the other members of his household will still be entitled to zakat, so let him give it to them.

Consider what al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

ولا يجوز دفع الزكاة إلى من يملك نصاباً أي مال كان دنانير أو دراهم أو سوائم أو عروضاً للتجارة أو لغير التجارة فاضلاً عن حاجته الأصلية وهي مسكنه وأثاث مسكنه وثيابه وخادمه ومركبه وسلاحه

ويجوز دفعها إلى من يملك أقل من النصاب وإن كان صحيحاً مكسباً كذا في الزاهدي. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱۸۹).
