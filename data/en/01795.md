### TITLE
How endowed dirhams and dinars are to be spent

### QUESTION
If dirhams and dinars, or rand, have been endowed, what is the method of using them? That is, what are their permissible uses?

### ANSWER
The capital of the endowed dirhams and dinars, or rand, is to be kept intact and its profits spent on good works — for example, the money may be placed in a muḍārabah partnership so that the original capital remains and the profit is spent.

See what ʿAllāmah Shāmī says:

وعن الأنصاري وكان من أصحاب زفر رحمه الله فيمن وقف الدراهم أو ما يكال أوما يوزن أيجوز ذلك قال: نعم، قيل: وكيف! قال: يدفع الدراهم مضاربة ثم يتصرق بها في الوجه الذي وقف عليه وما يكال أو يوزن يباع ويدفع ثمنه مضاربة أو بضاعة. (فتاوى الشامي: ٤/۳٦٤، سعيد. وكذا في خلاصة الفتاوى: ٤/٤۱۸. وكذا في مجمع الأنهر: ۱/۷۳۹).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

The endowment of dirhams and dinars is valid; but since in an endowment the benefit taken is from the usufruct while the substance itself remains, it is a condition in the endowment of dirhams that the dirhams themselves not be spent. Rather their profits should be spent, or something should be bought with them and its profits spent on the poor. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 6/416).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
