### TITLE
Supporting a non-Muslim party at the time of elections

### QUESTION
At the time of elections, is it permissible or not to make agreements with non-Muslim political parties for the sake of the interests of the Muslim community, to take part in them and to support them?

### ANSWER
After the Prophet ﷺ came to Madīnah Munawwarah he made agreements with the Jews of Madīnah. On this basis, in the case in question, to make an agreement with non-Muslim political parties for the sake of religious and communal interests, to take part in them and to support them, will be permissible and correct. Yes, to hold the disbelievers as friends of the heart is not permissible; that is forbidden and prohibited by a decisive text. Consider what Durr al-Mukhtār states:

ومفاده جواز الاستعانة بالكافر عند الحاجة وقد استعان عليه الصلاة والسلام باليهود على اليهود. (الدر المختار: ٤/۱٤۸، سعيد).

Kifāyat al-Muftī records:

The Prophet ﷺ made this agreement with the Jews, that they would stand with the Muslims in war. (کفایت المفتی: ۹/۳۷۹)۔

Moreover, from the principles of the noble sharīʿah the permissibility of joint action with disbelievers is established.

In another place it is stated: To make friends with disbelievers with the aim of gaining worldly honour is not correct; but if the aim is the protection of the religion and that can be attained by joint action with the disbelievers, then such joint action is correct. It is a separate matter if worldly power should be obtained through this joint action; as long as that is not itself the object, it is not forbidden or prohibited. (کفایت المفتی: ۹/۳٦٤، دار الاشاعت)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
