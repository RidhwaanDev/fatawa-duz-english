### TITLE
Marriage with communists

### QUESTION
Does the sacred law permit a marriage tie to be established with communists or not?

### ANSWER
Communism has a complete system and outlook in every field, from thought and belief through to economics. Its intellectual foundation is atheism and the rejection of religion and the hereafter. A person who is a communist holding all of these ideas therefore neither remains a Muslim nor can be counted among the People of the Book; he falls in the category of unbelievers, and marriage with such people is absolutely impermissible. They also deny private ownership, whereas the Qur'an and hadith are filled with private ownership, so denying it amounts to denying the Qur'an and hadith.

Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

لا يجوز نكاح المجوسيات والوثنيات

والمعطلة والزنادقة والباطنية والإباحية وكل مذهب يكفر معتقده كذا في فتح القدير. (الفتاوى الهندية: القسم الرابع المحرمات بالشرك، ۱/۲۸۱).

Faqīh al-Ummah Muftī Maḥmūd Ḥasan Gangohī (may Allah have mercy on him) writes in Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah:

Communism begins with rebellion against God and religion, and as a matter of belief it is plainly opposed to Islam. Discussing communism, the author of Ḥukm al-Islām fī al-Ishtirākiyyah writes:

"إن العقيدة الأساسية للنظام الاشتراكي هي العقيدة المادية التي تقول: إن المادة هي أصل الأشياء، ولا شيء لغير المادة، وهذا يعني إنكار وجود الخالق العظيم سبحانه وتعالى، وبالتالي إنكار كل دين سماوي واعتبارها الإيمان بذلك أفيوناً يخدر الشعوب". ص ۱۱۹، (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 2/136, Mubawwab wa Murattab).

See further: Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 1/285; and Mukālamah bayn al-Madhāhib, "Communism", pp. 261–269, Maktabah Fārūqiyyah.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
