### TITLE
The ruling on wearing jewellery on particular parts of the body

### QUESTION
Nowadays a new fashion of beautification and adornment is current in which women wear jewellery on certain particular parts of the body — for example the navel, the tongue, the private parts and so on. These parts are pierced and the jewellery is worn in them, and it is worn for adornment and beauty. The jewellery may be of gold, silver, iron or the like. Can a married woman wear it for her husband, with his consent, or not? And what is the ruling for an unmarried girl? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the hadiths and the books of fiqh we find mention of wearing jewellery in the ears and on the hands, and the books of fiqh also mention wearing an ornament in the nose. But wearing jewellery in parts other than these — for example in the navel or the private parts — is mentioned neither in the books of hadith nor in the books of fiqh, nor is it the way of Muslim women. It is rather an invention of immoral and dissolute women, and Muslim women should keep away from it. When there is an ornament in the navel or a similar place, there will inevitably also be an effort to show it to others, whereas these parts are meant to be covered, not displayed. May Allah protect the Muslims from imitating other nations. Amen.

See what Bukhārī Sharīf states:

فجعلت المرأة تلقي القرط والخاتم. (رواه البخاري: ۱/۲۰).

Al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah states:

ولا بأس بثقب آذان النسوان كذا في الظهيرية ولا بأس بثقب آذان الأطفال من البنات لأنهم كانوا يفعلون ذلك في زمان رسول الله ﷺ من غير إنكار كذا في الكبري. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳۵۷). (وكذا في الموسوعة الفقهية الكويتية: ۷/۸۷).

قال في الدر المختار: قلت: وهل يجوز الخزام في الأنف لم أره. وقال في الشامية: قلت: إن كان مما يتزين النساء به كما هو في بعض البلاد فهو فيها كثقب القرط، وقد نص الشافعية على جوازه. (الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ٦/ ٤۲۰، سعيد).

(وكذا في الطحطاوي على الدر المختار: ٤/۲۰۹، كوئته، وإمداد الفتاوى: ٤/۱۳۵).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
