### TITLE
Zakat on a factory, mill, machinery, vehicle and the like

### QUESTION
If a person owns a factory, a mill, machinery, a vehicle and similar things, is zakat due on them or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, zakat is not due on a factory, mill, machinery, vehicle and the like. However, if he trades in such things, then zakat is due on their value.

Consider what al-Hidāyah states:

وليس في دور السكني وثياب البدن وأثاث المنازل ودواب الركوب وعبيد الخدمة وسلاح الاستعمال زكاة لأنها مشغولة بالحاجة الأصلية وليست بنامية وعلى هذا كتب العلم لأهلها وآلات المحترفين لما قلنا. (الهداية: ۱/۱۸٦، كتاب الزكاة).

Jawāhir al-Fiqh states:

Zakat is not obligatory on the machinery of workshops, mills and the like, but it is obligatory on the goods produced in them. (Jawāhir al-Fiqh: 1/385, Masāʾil-e Zakāt, Dār al-ʿUlūm Karachi).

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm states:

There is no zakat on the tools of tradesmen, as al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

وكذلك آلات المحترفين

(Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm: 6/93; Imdād al-Fatāwā: 2/42; and Īḍāḥ al-Masāʾil: 106).
