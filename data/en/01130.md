### TITLE
The sunnah iʿtikāf without fasting

### QUESTION
An elderly man who cannot fast because of extreme old age or because of severe illness — will his sunnah iʿtikāf be valid or not?

### ANSWER
Fasting is a condition for the sunnah iʿtikāf, so in the case in question the elderly man's iʿtikāf will be an optional one and the sunnah will not be fulfilled.

Consider what Fatāwā Shāmī states:

ومقتضي ذلك أن الصوم شرط أيضاً في الاعتكاف المسنون لأنه مقدر بالعشر الأخير حتى لو اعتكفه بلا صوم لمرض أو سفر ينبغي أن لا يصح عنه، بل يكون نفلاً فلا تحصل به إقامة سنة الكفاية. (الشامي: ۲/٤٤۲، باب الاعتكاف، سعيد).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

The iʿtikāf of the last ten days is an emphasised communal sunnah. If this iʿtikāf is performed without fasting it will not be the sunnah iʿtikāf but will become an optional one. If, however, a person did not fast on just one day, then making up only that one day's iʿtikāf will be obligatory. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 10/220, the classified and arranged edition).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

Question: A person intends to keep the iʿtikāf of the last ten days but does not have the strength to fast. Is iʿtikāf without fasting valid or not?

Answer: Fasting is a condition for the sunnah iʿtikāf, so iʿtikāf without fasting is an optional iʿtikāf, not the sunnah iʿtikāf. (Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 3/110, the book of iʿtikāf).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
