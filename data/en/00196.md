### TITLE
A study of the report "أنا أملح وأخي يوسف أصبح"

### QUESTION
A hadith is well known among people, that Rasūlullāh ﷺ said:

أنا أملح وأخي يوسف أصبح

Allah gave me great charm, and my brother Yūsuf (peace be upon him) great beauty. Is this a hadith or not? And if Rasūlullāh ﷺ was more beautiful than Yūsuf (peace be upon him), why did the incident of the women cutting their fingers happen with Yūsuf (peace be upon him) and not with Rasūlullāh ﷺ?

### ANSWER
Ḥaḍrat Shāh Walīullāh recorded this narration in his treatise "الدر الثمين في مبشرات النبي الأمين". Shāh Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) reports it from his father, who related it with the words "بلغني" ("it has reached me"), and he then goes on to record a confirmation of it received from Rasūlullāh ﷺ in a dream. Consider the wording:

أخبرني سيدي الوالد قال: بلغني أن النبي ﷺ قال: أنا أملح وأخي يوسف أصبح فتحيرت في معناه لأن الملاحة توجب قلق العشاق أكثر من الصباحة وقد روي في قصة سيدنا يوسف عليه الصلاة والسلام أن النساء قطعن أيديهن حين رأينه وأن الناس ماتوا عند رؤيته ولم يرو عن نبينا ﷺ من هذا الباب شيء، فرأيت النبي ﷺ في المنام فسألته عن ذلك فقال: جمالي مستور عن أعين الناس غيرة من الله عز وجل ولو ظهر لفعل الناس أكثر مما فعلوا حين رأوا يوسف. (الدر الثمين، ص: ۱٦۱، الحديث العشرون).

(1) The gist of the reply is that Rasūlullāh ﷺ said that Allah has kept his beauty hidden from people's eyes; had it appeared in full, people would have done even more than they did when they saw Yūsuf (peace be upon him). (2) A second answer is also possible: what happened with Yūsuf (peace be upon him) did not happen all the time. It occurred at one particular moment, with particular women. It is not the case that whenever people saw Yūsuf (peace be upon him) they fainted or died. Allah shielded the beauty of Rasūlullāh ﷺ from the eyes of such frail and weak people. In short, it was a matter of those women's weakness.

(3) A third answer is that so many of the noble Companions had their necks struck off out of love for the perfect beauty of Rasūlullāh ﷺ; they gave their necks for his religion and out of love for him. In short, every account of martyrdom can stand as an example of men laying down their lives for the beauty of the Prophet ﷺ.
