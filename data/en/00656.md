### TITLE
Praying tarāwīḥ behind an imam who is careless about tajwīd

### QUESTION
What is the ruling on praying behind a ḥāfiẓ who recites the Qur'an very quickly and does not observe the rules of tajwīd (correct Qur'anic recitation), even though he knows them?

### ANSWER
In the case described, if the ḥāfiẓ recites so quickly that those praying behind him cannot follow anything, and he commits laḥn jalī (an obvious error, such as mispronouncing one letter as another), the prayer will not be valid. If he commits laḥn khafī (a subtle error in the finer rules of recitation), the prayer will not be invalid, but it will certainly be disliked. Moreover, reciting the Noble Qur'an carelessly and without observing tajwīd is a serious sin. Consider what al-Fatāwā Qāḍī Khān states (khwushkhwān means one who has a beautiful voice, and durustkhwān one who recites correctly):

ولا ينبغي للقوم أن يقدموا في التراويح الخوشخواں ولكن يقدموا الدرستخواں
فإن الإمام إذا كان يقرأ بصوت حسن يشغل عن الخشوع والتدبر والتفكر وكذا لوكان الإمام لحاناً لا بأس بأن يترك مسجد ه. (فتاوى قاضيخان على هامش الهندية: ۱/۲۳۸، فصل في مقدار القراءة في التراويح. وكذا في شرح منية المصلي: ٤۰۷، سهيل. وكذا في الفتاوى الهندية.
وعلى هامشه قال: قوله: الخوشخواں معناه حسن الصوت والدرستخواں صحيح القراءة ۱/۱۱٦)

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

Jamāl al-Qur'ān says: reciting the Qur'an contrary to tajwīd, or reciting it wrongly, or reciting it without any rule, is called laḥn. This laḥn is of two kinds. The first is that one letter is read in place of another — for instance, reading الھمد in place of الحمد, reading س in place of ش, reading the small ھ in place of the large ح, reading ز in place of ذ, or reading س in place of ص. Such mistakes are called laḥn jalī, and this is forbidden. In some places the meaning is corrupted by it and the prayer is lost as well. (جمال القرآن)

So a person who recites in this way is not fit to lead the prayer. He must first learn to recite the Qur'an correctly, and only then lead. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ٤/۳٥۱، باب الامامۃ)

In another place it states:

When the imam's recitation is not clear and correct, and those praying behind him cannot follow it, it is not permissible for him to lead them. They should arrange for an imam who recites the Noble Qur'an clearly and correctly. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۱/۱۸۸)
