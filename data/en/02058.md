### TITLE
Becoming a member of a medical-aid company

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to become a member of a medical-aid company or not?

### ANSWER
If the medical-aid company provides medical treatment in exchange for the money paid, and the predominant part of its business is lawful, then becoming a member of that company is permissible and valid. Otherwise it is impermissible.
