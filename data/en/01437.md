### TITLE
Expiation for killing cockroaches while in iḥrām

### QUESTION
A man killed three cockroaches while in the state of iḥrām. What amount of charity is due from him? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question the cockroach comes under the class of crawling creatures of the earth, and according to the explicit rulings of the jurists no compensation becomes due for killing such creatures. See what Sharḥ Lubāb al-Manāsik records:

فصل فيما لا يجب شيء بقتله في الإحرام والحرم

ولا شيء مطلقاً أي قليلاً ولا كثيراً، سواء في الحل أو الحرم محرماً أو غيره

ولا بشيء بقتل هو ام الأرض أي حشراتها في الحل والحرم والإحرام، ولا جزاء بقتلها، ولا إثم على فعلها

والصرصر قال صاحب القاموس: الصرصور دويبة كالصرصر. (شرح اللباب، ص ٤۱۸).

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh records:

In the same way, killing other harmful animals and crawling creatures — whether outside the sacred precincts, within them, or while in iḥrām — makes no compensation due, and no sin is incurred by the act. Those creatures are: the centipede, the cockroach, the mosquito, the flea, the bed bug, the fly, the moth, the lizard, the crab, the ṣarṣar (a kind of small insect) and other harmful animals and crawling creatures of the earth. Killing them makes no compensation due, because they are not game. (عمدۃ الفقہ: ٤/۵۵۲) ۔
