### TITLE
The evidence for the durūd "ﷺ"

### QUESTION
A man recites only this durūd, "ﷺ", and says that the noble Companions used to recite the durūd sharīf in this way. Is this correct or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
It is established from a great many hadiths that the noble Companions (may Allah be pleased with them) used to invoke blessings with this wording, "ﷺ"; and they also used the other wordings that are established from the hadiths.

أخرج الإمام البخاري رحمه الله في صحيحه (۱/۲/۱) بسنده عن علقمة بن وقاص الليثي يقول: سمعت عمر بن الخطاب رضي الله تعالى عنه على المنبر قال: سمعت رسول الله ﷺ يقول:

الخ.

There are countless hadiths of this kind in the books of hadith.

From this it is known that the noble Companions (may Allah be pleased with them), when narrating a hadith, would say ﷺ along with the blessed name or the blessed mention of the Prophet ﷺ; and it is recorded in this same way in the books of hadith.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
