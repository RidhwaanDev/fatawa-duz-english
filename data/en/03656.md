### TITLE

The ruling on doing advertising for the media

### QUESTION

Is it permissible or not to become an advertising agent for media centres (radio and television)? The form of it would be that on bringing in a customer through advertisements there will be a 20 per cent profit. It should also be kept in mind that all the companies carry on lawful business in accordance with the Sharīʿah. Will the income of such an agent be lawful or not?

### ANSWER

In the case described, publicising a company's products and its advertisements, when they remain within the bounds of the Sharīʿah — that is, when the advertisements do not include a picture of an animate being, or music, or amusement and diversion and the like — are permissible; such an agency is allowed and its income too is lawful. Consider what is transmitted in Radd al-Muḥtār:

قال في البزازية: إجارة السمسار والمنادي والحمامي والصكاك وما لا يقدر فيه الوقت ولا العمل تجوز لما كان للناس به حاجة ويطيب الأجر المأخوذ لو قدر أجر المثل. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/٤۷، سعيد).
وفيه أيضاً: تتمة: قال في التاتارخانية: وفي الدلال والسمسار يجب أجر المثل وما تواضعوا عليه إن في كل عشرة دنانير كذا فذاك حرام عليهم، وفي الحاوي: سئل محمد بن مسلمة عن أجرة السمسار فقال: أرجوا أنه لا بأس به وإن كان في الأصل فاسداً لكثرة التعامل وكثير من هذا غير جائز فجوزوه لحاجة الناس إليه كدخول الحمام. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/٦۳، سعيد).

Qāmūs al-Fiqh records:
Nowadays the method of commission has become current in various trades, that is, instead of fixing a salary the company fixes a percentage for those who sell its goods — for example, that ten per cent of whatever is sold will be given to him as a wage. In many religious and modern institutions which run on people's cooperation, this method too is now becoming current: that instead of giving them a fixed salary, a certain percentage is given as a wage. In this the madrasah authorities regard it as safer for themselves, because if a salary is fixed it may happen that a person slackens in his effort. Among the Ḥanafīs, the mashāʾikh of Balkh have permitted this out of regard for custom. (قاموس الفقہ: ۱/٤۹۹) ۔
In another place it is stated: Just as it is not correct to perform an unlawful and un-Islamic task oneself, so too employment in and assistance with such tasks is not correct. For this reason, employment in a bank, in insurance, in taverns or in brothels will not be permissible where a man becomes the means of the business of interest, gambling, liquor or prostitution, invites people towards it, or writes the accounts of interest. Yes, the employment of a peon and the like may be taken up, because he has no direct connection with that business. (قاموس الفقہ: ۱/۵۰۰) ۔
Islāmī Fiqh states:
Those things which give encouragement to some crime or sin — to sell them and to buy them is all unlawful. For example, if from some article, or from a picture made on an article, or from an advertisement, there is encouragement towards fornication, towards taking interest, towards drinking liquor, towards theft or robbery or any other crime, then both the buying and selling and the dissemination of these are unlawful. The present-day business of the cinema, the uncivilised, film-making, naked pictures, indecent songs and indecent novels — all of these are unlawful. (اسلامی فقہ: ۲/۳۸۰) ۔
