### TITLE
A device for validly gifting a house that is full of the donor's belongings

### QUESTION
Zayd has a large house consisting of twelve rooms, and the house is full of belongings: a large library, a freezer, a fridge, several heavy machines, exercise equipment, a bed, carpets, kitchen equipment and so on. This man has three sons; two of them have houses of their own, while one has no house, lives with his father, serves his father and is a religious scholar. Zayd wishes to gift this house to that scholar son, but he does not wish to give away the contents, since he thinks it better that after his death the contents be divided among all the heirs, and he wants to use them during his lifetime. Zayd is himself a religious scholar and knows that to gift a house it is necessary to hand over possession, and that to hand over possession the house must be emptied; but emptying the house is a formidable business. What device is open to Zayd so that the house may be gifted and he may also be spared the trouble of emptying it? Zayd regularly teaches the Hidāyah, and from that it appears that the house will have to be emptied.

### ANSWER
Zayd is right that in order to hand over possession the house will have to be emptied; but the jurists have set down an easy device for this by which the matter becomes simple. The device is that he should place all the contents of the house with the person to whom the gift is made as a deposit and hand them over to him — for which clearing the way is enough — and after that gift him the house and hand over possession of it as well, that is, give him the key of the house and himself move out. The goods left on deposit can then be taken back whenever he wishes.

See what Shaykh ʿAlī Ḥaydar says in Durar al-Ḥukkām Sharḥ Majallat al-Aḥkām:

الحيلة في هبة المشغول هي أن يودع المال الشاغل للموهوب له ويسلم إليه أولاً ثم تسلم الدار إليه وفي هذا تكون الهبة والتسليم صحيحين. (درر الحكام شرح مجلة الأحكام: ۲/۳۹٦، بيروت).

Al-Jawharah al-Nayyirah states:

ولو وهب داراً فيها متاع للواهب وسلم الدار إليه وسلمها مع المتاع لم يصح لأن الدار مشغولة بالمتاع والفراغ شرط لصحة التسليم والحيلة فيه أن يودع المتاع أولاً عند الموهوب له ويخلي بينه وبينه ثم يسلم الدار إليه فيصح لا نها مشغولة بمتاع هو في يده. (الجوهرة النيرة: ۲/۱۱، كتاب الهبة، مكتبه إمدادية، ملتان).

وكذا في حاشية الطحطاوي على الدر المختار: (۳/۳۹۵، كوئته)، وغمز عيون البصائر شرح الأشباه والنظائر: (۲/۳٤۲، نقلاً عن الجوهرة).
