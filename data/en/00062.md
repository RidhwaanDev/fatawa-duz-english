### TITLE
Reliable books on the subject of taqlīd

### QUESTION
On the subject of taqlīd and of opposition to taqlīd, which books does Ḥaḍrat Muftī Ṣāḥib (may his shade endure) consider the most comprehensive and reliable? Kindly let us know.

### ANSWER
The following books are reliable and useful on this subject.

Niẓām al-Islām, by Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Quṭb al-Dīn Khān (most probably).

Īḍāḥ al-Adillah, by Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Shaykh al-Hind (may Allah have mercy on him) (1268–1339 AH).

Al-Adillah al-Kāmilah, by Ḥaḍrat Shaykh al-Hind (may Allah have mercy on him) (1268–1339 AH).

Sabīl al-Rashād, by Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Rashīd Aḥmad Gangohī (may Allah have mercy on him) (1244–1323 AH).

Khayr al-Taqlīd, by Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Khayr Muḥammad Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him).

Tanwīr al-Ḥaqq, by Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Khayr Muḥammad Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him).

Taḥqīq Masʾalah-e Taqlīd (An Examination of the Question of Taqlīd), by Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Muḥammad Amīn Ṣafdar Okarwī (may Allah have mercy on him) (1355–1421 AH).

Al-Kalām al-Mufīd fī Ithbāt al-Taqlīd, by Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Sarfrāz Khān Ṣāḥib (d. 1432 AH).

Taqlīd kī Sharʿī Ḥaythiyyat (The Status of Taqlīd in Sharia), by Justice Muftī Muḥammad Taqī ʿUthmānī (b. 1362 AH).

Taqlīd kī Sharʿī Ḍarūrat (The Necessity of Taqlīd in Sharia), by Ḥaḍrat Muftī ʿAbd al-Raḥīm Ṣāḥib Lājpūrī (may Allah have mercy on him) (1321–1422 AH).

Further useful books on this subject may also be obtained from Gujranwala and Bahawalnagar in Pakistan.
