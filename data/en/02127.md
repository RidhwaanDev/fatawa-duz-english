### TITLE
Making an invitation to a meal conditional upon a donation

### QUESTION
In some institutions it happens that, when money is needed and expenses have grown heavy, they hold a public dinner, collect an additional sum from those who attend, and put whatever is left over towards the expenses of their institution. Is it correct to take this additional sum and spend it on their own work or not?

### ANSWER
The dinner is in essence a gratuity on the part of the institution, and the condition of an additional payment has been attached to it without any compulsion. Whoever does not accept the condition has the option of not attending. This arrangement therefore falls under the ruling of a conditional gratuity and is permissible and valid.
