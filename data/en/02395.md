### TITLE
Sacrificing three cows without specifying the shareholders

### QUESTION
Twenty-one men together offered the sacrifice of three cows, but they did not specify which seven persons were sharing in which cow. Have the sacrifices been fulfilled or not?

### ANSWER
The intention of offering the sacrifice is enough; it is not necessary to fix the shares for each one. On this basis, in the case asked about the sacrifices have been fulfilled on behalf of all of them. It is, however, preferable and better that in future the seven persons sharing in each large animal be specified.

See Imdād al-Aḥkām, which states:

Question: If fourteen men share in two cows and offer the sacrifice in such a way that each one's share is not fixed in a particular cow, and it is not said that this cow is for seven persons and the other cow for the other seven, but rather it is said that both these cows are jointly on behalf of the fourteen persons — will the sacrifice be valid in this way or not?

أمر حضرة الشيخ دام مجده وعلاه بتحقيقه.

Answer: By analogy this form is not permissible; but by juristic preference it is permissible.

ولو اشترك سبعة في سبع شياه لا يجزيهم قياساً لأن كل شاة بينهم على سبعة أسهم وفي الاستحسان يجزيهم وكذا اثنان في شاتين. (رد المحتار: ۵/۳۰۸).
(امداد الاحکام: ٤/۲۷۳) ۔
