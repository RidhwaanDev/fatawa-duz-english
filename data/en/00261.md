### TITLE
Whether fluid from a woman's breast breaks the ablution

### QUESTION
Does the fluid that comes out of a woman's breast break the ablution or not?

### ANSWER
Fluid that comes out because of a sore — that is, pus — is impure, and the ablution is broken by it. Fluid that comes out where there is no sore, however, is pure, and the ablution is not broken by it.

Marāqī al-Falāḥ states:

قوله كدم وقيح إشارة إلى أن ماء الصديد ناقض كماء الثدي والسرة والأذن إذا كان لمرض على الصحيح. (مراقي الفلاح مع حاشية الطحطاوي، ص: ۸۷، قديمي).

Bihishtī Zēwar states:

If fluid comes out of the breast and there is pain as well, it is impure and the ablution is lost by it. If there is no pain, it is not impure and the ablution is not broken by it either. (Bihishtī Zēwar, part one, p. 51).
