### TITLE
What is permissible during the waiting period

### QUESTION
An account of what is permissible during the waiting period.

### ANSWER
(1) Bathing, washing the head, and keeping the body and the clothes clean and tidy are permissible and correct. (آپ کے مسائل ۵/۴۱۰) ۔

(2) Putting oil in the hair when there is a need for it, and combing the hair, are also permissible — for instance where there is a fear of lice. (احسن الفتاوی ۵/۴۴۲) ۔

(3) It is not necessary for her to sit in one particular room of the house; she may stay wherever she wishes in the house, and she may also move about inside it.

(4) Household work and the like are also allowed; there is no prohibition in that.

(5) Where there is a need, there is scope for her to go to a hospital to see a doctor. As far as possible she should call the doctor to the house for treatment, and if she needs to stay in hospital that too is allowed. (آپ ے مسائل: ۵/۴۱۰، و احسن الفتاوی: ۵/۴۴۳)

(6) If after her husband's death she has no means of livelihood and has nothing in hand with which to meet her expenses, she may go out to work or to a job, observing the veil. But she should return home for the night, and during the day too she should come straight home once her work is finished; staying out of the house without need is not permissible. (آپ کے مسائل: ۵/۴۱۰) ۔

(7) If a woman has to give evidence before a judge in court, or has to sign necessary documents, or if her own and her children's financial interests depend on her appearing in court, she may go to court for such a need. She must return home as soon as the business is finished. (آپ کے مسائل: ۵/۴۱۰) ۔

(8) She is also allowed to go for office formalities such as collecting a pension, when it is necessary for her to go herself. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۹/۳٦٤، غیر مرتب) ۔

(9) If a woman needs groceries or other necessary items and there is no one to fetch them, she is allowed in that case to leave the house to the extent of the need, and she should come back home as soon as the need is met. (عصر حاضر کے پیچیدہ مسائل: ۲/۱۳۵) ۔

(10) If there is a danger of the house collapsing; or she fears for her belongings or her life in it; or the house is rented and she is unable to pay the rent; or the house is part of an estate that is being divided and the share coming to her is not enough to live in; or she feels a severe dread in the house —

in all the cases mentioned above the woman is allowed to move to another house close by. In the waiting period of divorce she will move to a nearby house chosen by her husband, and in the waiting period of death she will move to a nearby house of her own choosing. (عصر حاضر کے پیچیدہ مسائل، جلد دوم، ۱۳٤، ۔ و احسن الفتاوی: ۵/۴۴۰) ۔

(11) During the waiting period of a revocable divorce it is permissible to eat pān. (احسن الفتاوی: ۵/۴۴٦ ۔ و آپ کے مسائل: ۵/۴۱۰) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
