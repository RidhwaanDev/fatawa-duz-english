### TITLE
Earnings and wages for a jointly owned taxi

### QUESTION
If two men bought a taxi and one of them went away, and the one who was present paid the whole price of the taxi, and the absent one came back after three months and paid half the price — does the absent partner share in the three months' earnings or not? And if the one who was present drove it, can he recover three months' wages from his partner's share or not?

### ANSWER
When the buyer who was present paid the price on the absent partner's behalf as his agent, both became partners in what was bought; and once they are partners in what was bought, they are partners in its profits as well. So the absent partner will share in the three months' earnings. But since the partner was not present and had no agent either, the driver cannot recover wages out of his share, because a contract of hire requires the consent of both parties and the conclusion of a contract, and that is missing here. The partner should, however, give the one who drove the taxi something in return for his labour.

See Al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq, which states:

ولو غاب أحد المشتريين فللحاضر دفع كل الثمن وقبضه وحبسه حتى ينقد شريكه

فصار كمعير الرهن وصاحب العلو والوكيل بالشراء إذا أدي الثمن من ماله. (البحر الرائق: ٦/۱۷۵، كوئته).

In another place it states:

وكل واحد منهما قائم مقام صاحبه في التصرف فكان شراء أحدهما كشرائهما

(البحر الرائق: ۵/۱۷۰، كوئته).
قال في الهداية: الإجارة عقد يرد على المنافع بعوض. (الهداية: ۳/۲۹۳، كتاب الإجارات).
وفي الفتاوى الهندية: أما تفسيرها شرعاً فهي عقد على المنافع بعوض. (الفتاوى الهندية: ٤/٤۰۹).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā, answering a detailed question, states: Zayd had not settled with his father that he would work for a wage, so his doing the work was a voluntary service. The father should, however, bearing Zayd's labour in mind, give him suitable assistance. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 6/319).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
