### TITLE
Offering the sacrifice on the night of the thirteenth of Dhū al-Ḥijjah

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to offer the sacrifice during the night of the thirteenth of Dhū al-Ḥijjah, or not?

### ANSWER
Once the sun has set on the twelfth of Dhū al-Ḥijjah the days of sacrifice have come to an end and the sacrifice is no longer valid. During the night of the thirteenth, therefore, it is with even greater reason not valid. (فتاوی الشامی: ٦/ ۳۱۸، و الفتاوی الہندیہ: ۵/۲۹۵) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
