### TITLE
The ruling on producing musical sounds without instruments

### QUESTION
In order to keep the young Muslim generation away from music and to provide them with an alternative, there is a group of Muslim singers in this country whose method is as follows: two or three people sing, while others stand behind the singers, place their hands over their mouths and produce various sounds that resemble music but are made without instruments. They do not use musical instruments at all. Will singing in this manner be permissible or not? بینوا توجروا

### ANSWER
In the case in question, singing in the manner described is forbidden and impermissible. The reason is that in the pure Sacred Law it is not the musical instruments themselves that are the object of the prohibition but the sound; so when the sound produced is like that of music, its use is not correct and it must be avoided. Furthermore, some scholars declare it impermissible on the ground of imitating the dissolute, and they bring it under the warning contained in the hadith: من تشبه بقوم فهو منهم

Thus Mullā ʿAlī al-Qārī (may Allah have mercy on him) says in Mirqāt, commenting on this hadith:

من تشبه بقوم، أي من شبه نفسه بالكفار مثلاً في اللباس وغيره أو بالفساق أو الفجار أو بأهل التصوف والصلحاء الأبرار، "فهو منهم" في الإثم والخير، قال الطيبي رحمه الله: هذا عام في الخَلق والخُلق والشعار. (مرقاة المفاتيح: ۸/۲۵۵، كتاب اللباس، ط: إمداديه، ملتان).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
