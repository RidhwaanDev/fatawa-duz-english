### TITLE
A merely verbal gift without transfer of possession

### QUESTION
A father said to his daughter, "I have given you this house," but the house was not registered in the daughter's name. The parents lived in this house and bore all its expenses. The daughter and the parents all took it that the house belonged to the daughter. Now, after the death of the parents, the other heirs are demanding their right in this house, while the daughter claims that her parents gifted the house to her. Please tell us who is the rightful owner of the house.

### ANSWER
In the case described, the father gifted the house to the daughter only verbally, and during his lifetime he did not give her possession of it as owner; the gift was therefore not valid, and the house will be divided among all the heirs according to the shares laid down by the sharia.

See what Fatāwā Hindiyyah says:

منها (شرائط الهبة) أن يكون الموهوب مقبوضاً حتى لا يثبت الملك للموهوب له قبل القبض. (الفتاوى الهندية: ٤/۳۷٤).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

For a gift to be complete and valid, complete possession is a condition. Al-Durr al-Mukhtār says:

شرائط صحتها في الموهوب أن يكون مقبوضاً غير مشاع مميزاً غير مشغول

وتتم الهبة بالقبض الكامل. (الدر المختار: ۵/٦۸۸).

So, since the deceased himself remained in possession of the property and dealt with it until his death, and no possession as owner or dealing on the part of the son or the daughter is established during his lifetime, this gift is not to be given effect; all the heirs are heirs and may take their shares. Fatāwā Mahdawiyyah states:

إذا مات الواهب قبل قبض الموهوب له الهبة بطلت وتكون ميراثاً من الواهب كما في متروكاته. (٤/۵۷۳).

(Taken from Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 3/177).
