### TITLE
Repeating al-Fātiḥah and the prostration of forgetfulness

### QUESTION
If someone repeats some of the verses of Sūrat al-Fātiḥah, is the prostration of forgetfulness necessary, or must the prayer be repeated?

### ANSWER
If al-Fātiḥah is repeated by mistake, the prostration of forgetfulness is necessary; and if it is repeated deliberately, the prayer must be repeated. As to how much repetition makes the prostration of forgetfulness necessary or makes repeating the prayer necessary, Shāmī mentions most of al-Fātiḥah while al-Ṭaḥṭāwī mentions part of it. The two can be reconciled by taking "part" to mean "most", that is, more than half; and there is ease in this. So if more than half is repeated by mistake, the prostration of forgetfulness is necessary, and if it is done deliberately, repeating the prayer is necessary. Consider what al-Ṭaḥṭāwī states:

ولو كرر الفاتحة أو بعضها في إحدى الأوليين قبل السورة سجد للسهو. (الطحطاوي على مراقي الفلاح: ٤٦۰، باب سجود السهو، قديمي)

Shāmī states:

قوله وكذا ترك تكريرها: فلو قرأها في ركعتين من الأوليين مرتين وجب سجود السهو لتأخير الواجب وهو السورة كما في الذخيرة وغيرها، وكذا لو قرا أكثرها ثم أعادها كما في الظهيرية. (شامي: ۱/٤٦۰، سعيد)

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh states:

If, in the first two units of an obligatory prayer, a person recites al-Ḥamd a second time before joining a sūrah to it, or recites more than half of it a second time, the prostration of forgetfulness becomes necessary. (عمدۃ الفقہ: ۲/۳٦٤، مجددیہ)

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
