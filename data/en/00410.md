### TITLE
Intending a particular number of rakʿahs

### QUESTION
Someone formed the intention of four rakʿahs in place of a two-rakʿah prayer and then performed only two rakʿahs. Is the prayer valid or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
If two rakʿahs were performed with the intention of four, the prayer is still valid, because intending the number of rakʿahs is not a condition. Consider what al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

ولا يشترط نية عدد الركعات هكذا في شرح الوقاية. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/٦٦. وكذا في الشامي: ۱/۳۹۰، سعيد).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
