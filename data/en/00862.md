### TITLE
Ruling on a congregational supplication after the funeral prayer

### QUESTION
Is any supplication reported after the funeral prayer or not?

### ANSWER
No supplication is reported after the funeral prayer. On the contrary, the jurists have ruled that a loud congregational supplication at that point is disliked. Consider what al-Fatāwā al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah states:

كره أن يقوم رجل بعد ما اجتمع القوم للصلاة ويدعو للميت ويرفع صوته. (الفتاوى الهندية: ٥/۳۱۹، الباب الرابع في الصلاة والتسبيح)

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

The jurists have forbidden people to stand as a fixed practice after the funeral prayer is over, once the salām has been given, and make a congregational supplication for the deceased. Khulāṣat al-Fatāwā, a reliable work of Hanafi jurisprudence, has prohibited it.

لا يقوم بالدعاء بعد صلاة الجنازة. خلاصة الفتاوى: ۱/۲۲٥، الفصل الخامس العشرون في الجنائز، رشيديه.

(فتاوی محمودیہ: ۸/۷۱۰، مبوب و مرتب) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
