### TITLE
The ruling on the multiplication of reward for the congregational prayer in the sacred precinct

### QUESTION
The reward of one prayer in the sacred precinct of Makkah is a hundred thousand. So with the congregation will it be twenty-five or twenty-seven times that, or will it remain a hundred thousand?

### ANSWER
One meaning of the multiplication is the one mentioned in the question, that is, twenty-five or twenty-seven hundred thousand. The other meaning of the multiplication has been explained by Ḥaḍrat Shaykh Zakariyyā (may Allah have mercy on him) in Faḍāʾil-e Namāz (chapter two, p. 337), namely that one keeps doubling by multiplication up to the number twenty-five, so that a single prayer in congregation becomes three crore thirty-five lakh fifty-four thousand four hundred and thirty-two. This figure is then multiplied by a hundred thousand, and the product is the reward in the sacred precinct of Makkah; and if it is multiplied by one thousand, the product is the reward of the congregation in the sacred precinct of Madīnah and in the mosque of Bayt al-Maqdis. The table of the doubling up to the number twenty-five is given below; study it well.

1. 1 × 2 = 2
2. 2 × 2 = 4
3. 4 × 2 = 8
4. 8 × 2 = 16
5. 16 × 2 = 32
6. 32 × 2 = 64
7. 64 × 2 = 128
8. 128 × 2 = 256
9. 256 × 2 = 512
10. 512 × 2 = 1024
11. 1024 × 2 = 2048
12. 2048 × 2 = 4096
13. 4096 × 2 = 8192
14. 8192 × 2 = 16384
15. 16384 × 2 = 32768
16. 32768 × 2 = 65536

25. 16777216 × 2 = 33554432

Total: 33554432
