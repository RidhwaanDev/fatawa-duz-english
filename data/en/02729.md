### TITLE
Trimming the hair of the ears

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to cut the hair of the ears or not? Some people have hair on their ears which is plainly visible. بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question it is permissible and correct to cut the hair of the ears, just as it is permissible either to shave the hair of the head or to let it grow.

قال رسول الله ﷺ: الأذنان من الرأس. (رواه الترمذي: ۱/۱٦).

This hadith concerns wiping in ablution: the head is wiped, and since the ears come under the ruling of the head, they too are wiped. Reasoning along the same lines, it would not be wide of the mark to say that, just as cutting the hair of the head is permissible, cutting the hair of the ears is permissible as well. See: (مردوں کے لباس اور بالوں کے شرعی احکام، ص ۷۲) ۔
