### TITLE
Hosting a feast on the Day of ʿĀshūrāʾ

### QUESTION
Is it correct to be generous with one's family and to host a general feast on the Day of ʿĀshūrāʾ, or not?

### ANSWER
Being generous with one's family on the Day of ʿĀshūrāʾ is established from the noble hadith. Hosting a general feast is likewise included in that generosity, and the merit mentioned in the noble hadith will be attained through it as well.

For a detailed discussion of this issue, see Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā, vol. 1, p. 414.
