### TITLE
Praying with the intention of exercise

### QUESTION
May I recite a short sūrah in prayer so that there are as many bowings and prostrations as possible and my body becomes light and supple? For in America a doctor told me that every kind of exercise is found in prayer.

### ANSWER
Prayer is a most tremendous act of worship and the best of all acts of worship. Its purpose is Allah's good pleasure and approval, and it should be performed with that purpose and intention. Exercise should be done outside of prayer. Although the prayer is valid with a short sūrah after Sūrat al-Fātiḥah, or even with three short verses, one should avoid forming the intention of exercise in prayer. See what is mentioned in al-Durr al-Mukhtār under the necessary elements of prayer:

وضم أقصر سورة كالكوثر، أو ما قام مقامها وهو ثلاثة آيات قصار نحو: ثم نظر، ثم عبس وبسر، ثم أدبر واستكبر، وكذا لو كانت الآية أو الآيتان تعدل ثلاثاً قصاراً ذكره الحلبي. (الدر المختار: ۱/٤۵۸، سعيد).

Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah records:

وتجب قراءة الفاتحة وضم السورة أو ما يقوم مقامها من ثلاث آيات قصار أو آية طويلة في الأوليين بعد الفاتحة كذا في النهر الفائق وفي جميع ركعات النفل والوتر، هكذا في البحر الرائق. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۷۱).

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh records:
The prayer has certain necessary elements whose omission does not invalidate the prayer, but which make it necessary to repeat the prayer when the necessary element has been left out deliberately, or has been left out through forgetfulness and the prostration of forgetfulness has not been performed.

(5) In the first two rakʿahs of the obligatory prayers, and in all the rakʿahs of the necessary prayer (witr), the sunnah prayers and the voluntary prayers, one must recite after Sūrat al-Fātiḥah some very short sūrah such as Sūrat al-Kawthar, or in its place three short verses, or one long verse, or two verses that are equal to three short ones. (عمدۃ الفقہ: ۲/۹۸، زوار اکیڈمی) ۔ (و كذا فی کتاب المسائل: ۱/۳۱۳) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
