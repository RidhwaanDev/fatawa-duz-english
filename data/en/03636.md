### TITLE
The ruling on three divorces during the waiting period of an apostate woman

### QUESTION
If a woman becomes an apostate and the marriage is annulled, will a waiting period be obligatory or not? And if the husband gives three divorces during the waiting period, will the woman become irrevocably divorced or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question the waiting period is binding upon the woman, and if he gave three divorces before the waiting period ended they will take effect and the woman will become irrevocably divorced. But if he gave the three divorces after the waiting period ended, they will not take effect, because the woman has become like a stranger. Note what Fatāwā Shāmī states:

وأفاد وجوب العدة سواء ارتد أو ارتدت بالحيض أو بالأشهر لو صغيرة أو آيسة أو بوضع الحمل كما في البحر. (فتاوى الشامي: ۳/۱۹٤، سعيد).

See further: (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā: 4/295, Mumbai).

Concerning the taking effect of divorce, ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) has transmitted from the learned Ibn al-Humām (may Allah have mercy on him) that divorce will take effect.

قال في الفتح ويقع طلاق زوج المرتدة عليها ما دامت في العدة لأن الحرمة بالردة غير متأبد فإنها ترتفع بالإسلام فيقع طلاقه عليها في العدة مستتبعاً فائدته من حرمتها عليه بعد الثلاث حرمة مغياة بوطء زوج آخر. (فتاوى الشامي: ۳/۱۹۳، سعيد). (وكذا في فتح القدير: ۳/٤۲۰، دار الفكر، وحاشية تبيين الحقائق: ۲/۱۷٤، ط: امدايه).

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband records:

If, in the case of abandoning Islam, his wife is unlawful for him and his marriage is annulled, is there a legal waiting period for such a woman? Answer: The waiting period is obligatory. (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband: 10/214).
