### TITLE
Verification of the narration concerning the revelation of the Noble Qur'an in a single instance

### QUESTION
It is well known that the revelation of the Noble Qur'an took place in one go from the Preserved Tablet to the lowest heaven, and in this connection the well-known hadith is mentioned. Is that hadith sound or not? Is the argument from the word "anzalnā" correct? Has it come that the meaning of "anzalnā" is to send down all together and the meaning of "tanzīl" is to send down gradually, or not? Al-Biqāʿī, in Maṣāʿid al-Naẓar, has called the narration of the revelation in a single instance weak, on the authority of al-Ṭabarānī. Is this narration weak? Kindly explain and be rewarded.

### ANSWER
The narration of the revelation of the Noble Qur'an in a single instance is related from Ḥaḍrat ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿAbbās (raḍiyallāhu taʿālā ʿanhumā). There is criticism of the chain of al-Ṭabarānī's Al-Kabīr, but the other chain is sound. Keeping this in view, the revelation of the Noble Qur'an in a single instance is related from a Companion as a mawqūf report, and this mawqūf report has the ruling of a marfūʿ report.

Yes, to take "inzāl" to mean revelation in a single instance and "tanzīl" to mean gradual revelation is open to question. However, it is possible that in "anzalnā" there is an indication of the intensity at the time of the revelation upon the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, and in "tanzīl" an indication of gradual revelation, because the coming of the form afʿāl for emphasis and of the form tafʿīl for abundance is mentioned among the properties of the two forms. Please consider the narration with a sound chain concerning the revelation in a single instance:

أخرج الحاكم في مستدركه (۲/٤٦۲، كتاب التفسير، ط: دار ابن حزم) بسنده عن ابن عباس رضي الله عنه قال: نزل القرآن جملة إلى السماء الدنيا ثم نزل بعد ذلك في عشرين سنة،

وقال: هذا حديث صحيح الإسناد ولم يخرجاه.

This narration is also present in another place in Al-Mustadrak (۲/۲۷۸، رقم ۲۸۷۸ و ۲۸۷۹), and there too he declared it sound. Indeed, in one place he said صحيح على شرطهما ولم يخرجاه.

Furthermore, this narration is present in Al-Sunan al-Kubrā of al-Nasāʾī (٦/٤۲۱/۱۱۳۰۸), in Shuʿab al-Īmān of al-Bayhaqī (۲/٤۱۵، دار الکتب العلمیۃ، بیروت), in Al-Asmāʾ wa'l-Ṣifāt of al-Bayhaqī (۲/۲۹/٤۸۱), in Faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān of Qāsim ibn Sallām (۲/۲۳۳/٦٦۹), and in Faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān of Muḥammad ibn Ḍuray (ص ۱۲۷/۱۱٦).

The following scholars have declared al-Ḥākim's narration sound: ʿAllāmah Ibn Kathīr in Faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān (۱/۳٦، مکتبۃ ابن تیمیہ); Shaykh Abū Jaʿfar Aḥmad ibn Muḥammad al-Naḥḥās in Iʿrāb al-Qurʾān (۵/۷۸، ط: عالم الکتب، بیروت); ʿAllāmah al-Suyūṭī (raḥimahullāh) in Al-Itqān fī ʿUlūm al-Qurʾān (۱/۳۹، ٤۰، ط: سہیل اکیڈمی لاہور); Shaykh al-Zurqānī in Manāhil al-ʿIrfān fī ʿUlūm al-Qurʾān (۱/٤٤); and Shaykh Mannāʿ al-Qaṭṭān in Mabāḥith fī ʿUlūm al-Qurʾān (۱/۱۰۱، ط: مکتبۃ المعارف). ʿAllāmah Badr al-Dīn al-Zarkashī (raḥimahullāh) related al-Ḥākim's authentication in Al-Burhān (۱/۱٦۵، المکتبۃ العصریۃ). Consider the verification of the narration of Sunan al-Nasāʾī:

٭ Aḥmad ibn Sulaymān: ṣadūq, thiqah. (تہذیب الکمال: ۱/۳۲۰، مؤسسۃ الرسالۃ)۔

٭ Yazīd ibn Hārūn: thiqah. (تہذیب الکمال: ۳۲/۲٦٦، ط: مؤسسۃ الرسالۃ)۔

٭ Dāwūd ibn Abī Hind, whose name is Dīnār: thiqah. (تہذیب الکمال: ۸/٤٦٤، ط: مؤسسۃ الرسالۃ)۔

It is learnt that all the narrators are trustworthy; therefore the narration of Sunan al-Nasāʾī is sound. Yes, there is criticism of al-Ṭabarānī's narration.

Consider: ʿAllāmah al-Haythamī said in Majmaʿ al-Zawāʾid:

رواه الطبراني والبزار باختصار ورجال البزار رجال الصحيح وفي إسناد الطبراني عمرو بن عبد الغفار وهو ضعيف. (مجمع الزوائد: ۷/۱٤۰، باب سورة انا انزلناه، دار الفكر).

Shaykh al-Biqāʿī (raḥimahullāh) too related this same statement in Maṣāʿid al-Naẓar (ص ۲۱۷، ط: الریاض).

Consider the clarification concerning inzāl and tanzīl:

In "وأنزل من السماء ماء" [البقرة: ۲۲] inzāl is for graduality. In "يسئلك أهل الكتاب أن تنزل عليهم كتاباً من السماء" [النساء: ۱۵۳]، tanzīl is for revelation all together. In "يؤمنون بما أنزل إليك وما أنزل من قبلك" [البقرة: ٤]، inzāl has come for all the scriptures. In "و لو نزلنا عليك كتاباً في قرطاس فلمسوه بأيديهم" [الأنعام: ۷]، tanzīl has come for revelation in a single instance. In "وأنزلنا إليك الذكر لتبين للناس ما نزل إليهم" [النحل: ٤٤]، both have one and the same sense. See further: (لسان العرب: ۱۱/٦۵٦، ط: دار الفكر، والمحكم والمحيط الاعظم، وتاج العروس، ومقدمة المنجد، ص ۲۷، وشرح الشافية للرضي: ۱/۹۲، ط: دار الكتب العلمية).

Consider the reference of Shaykh al-Biqāʿī: (مصاعد النظر: ۲۱۷، ۲۱۸، ط: مکتبۃ المعارف، الریاض)۔
