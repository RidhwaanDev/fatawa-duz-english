### TITLE
The meanings of the names of the places of Hajj

### QUESTION
What do the names Muzdalifah, ʿArafāt, Minā, Wādī Muḥassir, Masjid Namirah, Masjid Khayf, Ṣafā and Marwah mean?

### ANSWER
Muzdalifah is from "إزدلاف", which means nearness: at this place nearness to Allah is attained.

Or it is because the place lies close at hand as soon as one leaves ʿArafāt.

Or it is because Ādam (peace be upon him) drew near to Ḥawwāʾ there.

Or it is because people arrive there during زلف اللیل, that is, in a part of the night.

٭ ʿArafāt may be from عرف, which means fragrance, because this place is fragrant in contrast to Minā, which is a place of slaughter. Minā is not so, on account of the animals sacrificed there.

Or it is because Ādam (peace be upon him) and Ḥawwāʾ came to know one another there in this world.

Or it is because when Jibrīl (peace be upon him) taught Ibrāhīm (peace be upon him) the rites of Hajj, Ibrāhīm said "عرفت" — "I have learnt them."

Or it is because on the night of ʿArafah, when Ibrāhīm (peace be upon him) again saw the dream of sacrificing his son, he realised that the dream was to be taken at face value and that the command to sacrifice was what was intended.

٭ Minā means to make blood flow. Since the blood of the sacrificial animals is shed there, the valley came to be known by this name.

Or it is from tamannā (longing), that is, it is the place where longings are fulfilled through supplication. A poet says:

بوادي مني نلنا المنى إذ تبسمت
٭
ليال وأيام ملاح المباسم

Translation: In the valley of Minā we attained our longings, when those days and nights smiled and their smiles shone out.

سرور بعيد واجتماع أحبة
٭
وقرب وقربان وخير مواسم

Translation: There is the joy of Eid and the gathering of loved ones, nearness to Allah, sacrifice, and the finest of seasons.

٭ Muḥassir, with a kasrah on the sīn, has the meaning of wearing out. Since the elephants of Abrahah's army grew exhausted here and were unable to advance, the valley was named accordingly.

٭ Masjid Namirah: namirah is the name given to a sheet marked with black and white stripes. Perhaps part of the mountain there is black and part of it white.

٭ Masjid Khayf: khayf is the name for the lower part of a mountain that stands above the course of the water.

٭ Ṣafā means smooth stone, and Marwah means gleaming stone.

Or it is because Ādam, Ṣafiyyullāh (peace be upon him), sat on Ṣafā, while his marʾah — that is, his wife — sat on Marwah.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
