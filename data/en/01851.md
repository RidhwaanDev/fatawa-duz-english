### TITLE
Praying in congregation outside the mosque, in an open ground

### QUESTION
Some people object to the Tablīghī Jamāʿat, saying that they leave the mosque and hold their congregational prayer outside in the gathering ground. The objectors claim that congregational prayer in the mosque is an emphasised sunnah and that there is no merit in a congregation held outside. Is this claim of theirs correct or not? They also cite a report of ʿAbd Allāh ibn Masʿūd (may Allah be pleased with him) in which congregational prayer in the mosque is called one of the ways of guidance.

### ANSWER
If the congregational prayer is performed outside the mosque, the reward of the congregation is still obtained; the reward of praying in congregation in the mosque, however, is greater than that.

In Fatāwā Sirājiyyah it is recorded:

قوم تخلفوا عن المسجد وصلوا في البيت بجماعة فإنهم ينالون فضل الجماعة لكن دون ما ينالون في المسجد. (الفتاوى السراجية: قبيل باب الإمامة، ص ۷۸).

Ṭaḥṭāwī ʿalā Marāqī al-Falāḥ states:

حتى لو صلي في بيته بزوجته أو جاريته أو ولده فقد أتي بفضيلة الجماعة، كذا في الشرح، لكن فضيلة المسجد أتم. (حاشية الطحطاوي على مراقي الفلاح: ص ۲۸۷، باب الإمامة).

In Fatāwā Qāḍīkhān it is mentioned:

والصحيح أن للجماعة في البيت فضيلة وللجماعة في المسجد فضيلة أخرى فإذا صلى في البيت بجماعة فقد حاز فضيلة أدائها بالجماعة وترك الفضيلة الأخرى.

He wrote this in connection with the tarāwīḥ prayer, and then afterwards he says:

وكذلك في المكتوبات. (فتاوى قاضيخان على هامش الهندية: ۱/۲۳۳، باب التراويح).

In Sharḥ Munyat al-Muṣallī it says:

لو صلي جماعة في البيت على هيئة الجماعة في المسجد نالوا فضيلة الجماعة وهي المضاعفة بسبع وعشرين درجة لكن لم ينالوا فضيلة الجماعة الكائنة في المسجد فالحاصل أن كل ما شرع فيه الجماعة فالمسجد فيه أفضل لما اشتمل عليه من شرف المكان وإظهار الشعائر وتكثير سواد المسلمين. (

(He said this in connection with the tarāwīḥ prayer, but before it he had written:)

وهكذا في المكتوبات أي الفرائض. (شرح منية المصلي، ص ٤۰۲، أبواب التراويح، سهيل).

From the passages of fiqh quoted above it is perfectly clear that the brothers of the Tablīgh do receive the reward of congregation at the gathering ground and the sunnah is fulfilled. Yes, the mosque is better; but for certain practical reasons not everyone can go to the mosque, and the people attending such a gathering cannot even fit into the mosques.

As for the merits of the mosque, you can easily find them under the virtues of mosques. For example, seven people will be in the shade of the Throne, and among them are those whose hearts are attached to the mosque, so that when they leave it they are already thinking of coming back. The incident of the two Companions is well known, that after praying in the mosque, in pitch darkness, light shone from their staffs. And for the one who goes to the mosque morning and evening, Allah Most High will arrange hospitality for him in Paradise. And when a person sets out for the mosque with the sole intention of prayer, his rank is raised and his sins are wiped away; then, when he has finished his prayer, the angels pray for mercy for him. All these merits are mentioned in the books of hadith and are well known.

The report of ʿAbd Allāh ibn Masʿūd (may Allah be pleased with him) means exactly this: that the congregation is a sunnah and that abandoning it is a mark of hypocrisy. Thus he says:

"من سره أن يلقي الله غداً مسلماً فليحافظ على هؤلاء الصلوات الخمس حيث ينادي بهن فإن الله شرع لنبيكم ﷺ سنن الهدي وأنهن "أي الصلوات الخمس بالجماعة" (فتح الملهم) من سنن الهدي "أي من طريق الهدي" (فتح الملهم) ولو أنكم صليتم في بيوتكم كما يصلي هذا المتخلف في بيته لتركتم سنة نبيكم ولو تركتم سنة نبيكم لضللتم. (رواه مسلم: ۱/۲۳۲).

In this report the words "kamā yuṣallī hādhā al-mutakhallif" show that Ibn Masʿūd (may Allah be pleased with him) regarded the man who stays behind and prays alone as deserving the warning, whereas over against him it is praying with the congregation that is praiseworthy and commendable. Then there is the other report mentioned in Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim, in which these words occur:

لقد رأيتنا وما يتخلف عن الصلاة إلا منافق قد علم نفاقه أو مريض ان كان المريض ليمشي بين رجلين حتى يأتي الصلاة وقال: إن رسول الله ﷺ علمنا سنن الهدي وإن من سنن الهدي الصلاة في المسجد الذي يؤذن فيه. (رواه مسلم: ۱/۲۳۲).

At the beginning of this report too there is mention of the hypocrite who stays behind, that is, the one who leaves the congregation; so over against him it is precisely the congregational prayer that is praiseworthy and deserving of reward. And since in towns and settled places the congregation is generally held in the mosque, the mosque was mentioned because that is what usually happens; so apparently this restriction is not meant to exclude other cases. In another report the Messenger of Allah ﷺ described the congregational prayer as twenty-seven degrees superior to the prayer of one who prays alone, and in it there is no mention of the mosque. There is also the hadith:

"الصلاة مع الإمام أفضل من خمس وعشرين صلاة يصليها وحده. (مسلم ۱/۲۳۱).

Here too the merit of the congregation is stated over against praying alone.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
