### TITLE
One bound by the sequence who forgets the witr and prays the current prayers

### QUESTION
If a person bound by the sequence forgets the witr and remembers it after five prayers, will he make up the other prayers along with the witr or not? بینوا توجروا

### ANSWER
If he has already performed the current prayers forgetfully, then after remembering, only making up the witr is obligatory; the remaining prayers are sound and valid, and there is no need to repeat them. Consider what is recorded in Fatāwā Hindiyyah:

ثم الترتيب يسقط بالنسيان وبما هو في معنى النسيان كذا في المضمرات. ولو تذكر صلاة قد نسيها بعد ما أدى وقتية جازت الوقتية كذا في فتاوى قاضيخان. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱۲۲). (وكذا في الهداية: ۱/۷۳، المكتبة الإسلامية، والمحيط البرهاني: ۲/۳٤، ط: دار إحياء التراث).

Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥal records:

It is necessary for a person bound by the sequence first to make up the missed prayer and then to pray the current prayer. But if he prayed the current prayer forgetfully and afterwards remembered the missed prayer, his current prayer was valid, and he should make up the missed prayer. And if he remembered the missed prayer before beginning the current prayer, or remembered it during the prayer, then he should make up the missed prayer and pray the current prayer again. (Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥal 3/624)

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh records:

If a forgotten missed prayer is remembered after performing the current prayer, the current prayer was valid and there is no need to repeat it. By forgetting a missed prayer, and by those things which come under the ruling of forgetting, the sequence lapses, because forgetting is a heaven-sent excuse in which the servant has no choice. In the case of forgetting, the ruling of the sequence in performing the missed prayer is conditional upon remembering it — that is, its time comes with the remembering; as long as it is not remembered, its time has not come and the sequence is not binding. (ʿUmdat al-Fiqh 2/350)
