### TITLE
Ruling on taking up employment with people of corrupt beliefs

### QUESTION
I am being offered employment at a Barelwī madrasah. Their own teachers teach various books there; I am not required to teach. I am only responsible for the administration and management of the madrasah and the like, and I have to carry out these duties sitting in the office. So may I take up such employment or not? Kindly explain and be rewarded.

### ANSWER
In the case in question you may take up the employment with the intention of reform. Yes, do not lend support to their innovations — just as Ḥaḍrat Yūsuf (ʿalayhis salām) took up employment with a disbelieving king:

﴿اجعلني على خزائن الأرض﴾ [يوسف:
۵۵
]

"Appoint me over the treasuries of the land."

In particular, if effort is put into their children and, in the capacity of a supervisor, matters of dīn are discussed with them, there will be greater benefit.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
