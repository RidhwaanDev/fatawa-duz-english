### TITLE
Placing a monitor (screen) in the masjid for the ṣalāh times and the like

### QUESTION
Is it correct or not to place a screen inside the masjid, near the pulpit, to show the ṣalāh times and the date, as well as the phase of the moon and so on? Please note that there is no picture of any living being on it.

### ANSWER
There is permission to use, for the ṣalāh times or other needs, a screen on which there are no pictures of people. However, since it is placed close to the pulpit, there is a danger of disturbance in the ṣalāh of the worshippers; for this reason the need should be met by placing it outside the masjid, within the masjid's precincts. It should not be placed in the masjid proper, in which the congregational ṣalāh is performed.

Islām awr Jadīd Maʿāshī Masāʾil records:

Those that are installed at airports are indeed televisions, but they are used as a monitor or as closed circuit, so this is a permissible use of it. (Islām awr Jadīd Maʿāshī Masāʾil: 4/14)

Fiqh al-Muʿāmalāt states:

Nevertheless, in the present age a permissible use of it is also possible — for example, that it be used for viewing pictures of inanimate things, such as the movement of buildings, places, parks, oceans and the like, or scenes of sunrise and sunset and so forth; or that it be used for the checking of luggage and the like, and for announcing the timetables of aeroplanes and making announcements; or that it be used in security arrangements and other such matters. (Fiqh al-Muʿāmalāt: 1/76, Dār al-Ishāʿat, Karachi).

For more, see: (Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 1/237; and Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 16/129, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).
