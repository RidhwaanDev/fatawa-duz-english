### TITLE
Giving the meat of a sacrifice with the intention of zakāt

### QUESTION
Zayd performed several sacrifices, some of them obligatory and some voluntary. He then valued a large quantity of the meat and gave it to poor people with the intention of zakāt. Can the intention of zakāt be made with the meat of a sacrifice? Zayd says that the meat is his own property, so he can give it with the intention of charity and zakāt. بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
It is not permissible to give the meat of a sacrifice with the intention of zakāt, and if it has been given, the zakāt has not been discharged. Consider what Fatāwā Shāmī states:

وإذا دفع اللحم إلى فقير بنية الزكاة لا يحسب عنها في ظاهر الرواية. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۳۲۸، سعيد).

Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

تصدق بلحم الأضحية على الفقير بنية الزكاة لا يجزئه في ظاهر الرواية. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳۰۸).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
