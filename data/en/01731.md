### TITLE
A vow to give charity without specifying an amount

### QUESTION
A man's young daughter was ill, and he used these words in a vow: "If my daughter recovers from this illness, I will give something in charity." By Allah's grace the girl recovered. How much charity must this man now give?

### ANSWER
In the case in question the man must give in charity the value of ten ṣadaqat al-fiṭr payments, or something equal to it. That is, if he discharges it in wheat, he must give five ṣāʿ (a measure of volume), or its equivalent in cash or in some other item. Whether he gives it to a single poor person or to several, the vow will be fulfilled either way.

Note: five ṣāʿ comes to about 16 kilograms by modern measure.

قال في الدر المختار: ولو نوى صياماً بلا عدد لزمه ثلاثة أيام ولو صدقة فإطعام عشرة مساكين كالفطرة، قال ابن عابدين: قوله ولو صدقة، أي بلا عدد (قوله كالفطرة) أي لكل مسكين نصف صاع بر. (الدر المختار مع فتاوى الشامي: ۳/۷٤۲، ط: سعيد).
قال العلامة الرافعي رحمه الله: قول الشارح فإطعام عشرة مساكين: لأن أقل ما أوجبه الله تعالى في كتابه من الصدقة عشرة مساكين، سندي. (التحرير المختار: ۳/۱٦، سعيد).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

No particular amount of charity was specified in the words of the vow. In such a case feeding ten poor people is obligatory — that is, one must give in charity wheat amounting to ten times ṣadaqat al-fiṭr, or its value in cash, or some other item; and whether it is given to one poor person or to several, the vow will be fulfilled either way. (احسن الفتاوی: ۵/۴۸۴) ۔
