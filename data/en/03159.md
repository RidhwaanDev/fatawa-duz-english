### TITLE
How the jinn will enter Paradise

### QUESTION
What will the difference between human beings and jinn be in Paradise? That is, will the bodies of the jinn be like those of human beings, or will their state be something else? بینوا توجروا

### ANSWER
The scholars differ as to whether the jinn will enter Paradise or not. According to the preferred view, they will enter it. ʿAllāmah al-Ālūsī (may Allah have mercy on him) has written in Rūḥ al-Maʿānī that the jinn will enjoy the favours of Paradise just as human beings will. No explicit statement about their bodies has been found. For the rest, the conditions of Paradise and of the hereafter cannot be measured by the conditions of this world: in this world the vision of Allah with these present eyes is impossible, while in the hereafter that vision is certain. ʿAllāmah al-Ālūsī (may Allah have mercy on him) writes in Rūḥ al-Maʿānī:

واستدل بالآية على أن الجن يدخلون الجنة ويجامعون فيها كالإنس فهم باقون فيها منعمين كبقاء المعذبين منهم في النار وهو مقتضي ظاهر ما ذهب إليه أبو يوسف رحمه الله ومحمد رحمه الله وابن أبي ليلى رحمه الله والأوزاعي رحمه الله وعليه الأكثر كما ذكره العيني رحمه الله في شرح البخاري

فإن ظاهره أنهم كالإنس يوم القيامة

قيل: نراهم ولا يرونا عكس ما كانوا عليه في الدنيا. (روح المعاني: ۲۷/۱۱۹، ط: دار التراث).

For further detail see: (al-Ashbāh wa al-Naẓāʾir with Sharḥ al-Ḥamawī: 3/91–100, published by Idārat al-Qurʾān; and Ākām al-Marjān fī Aḥkām al-Jānn by ʿAllāmah Badr al-Dīn al-Shiblī).

Is repentance and seeking forgiveness enough for the sin of backbiting, or is it necessary to obtain pardon from the person concerned?

Question: Zayd backbit ʿAmr. What is the way to seek forgiveness for this sin? That is, along with repenting, is it also necessary to ask ʿAmr's pardon or not? If the backbiting has not reached that person, is it still counted among the major sins or not? In which cases have the scholars permitted backbiting? بینوا توجروا

Answer: It is a settled matter that backbiting is a grave offence and is unlawful. Allah has forbidden backbiting in the Noble Qur'an:

﴿ولا يغتب بعضكم بعضاً﴾

and He has declared the backbiter to be one who eats the flesh of his dead brother:

﴿أ يحب أحدكم أن يأكل لحم أخيه ميتاً فكرهتموه﴾ [الحجرات: ۱۲].

The scholars have written that in the rights owed to Allah's servants there is one additional condition for repentance: that pardon be sought from the person concerned. Without this, the repentance will not be complete. Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ashraf ʿAlī Thānwī (may Allah have mercy on him), however, has written that some scholars have said that as long as news of the backbiting does not reach that person, it does not become a right owed to a fellow servant; but even in this case it is necessary to retract one's words before the person in whose presence the backbiting was done. (Bayān al-Qurʾān: 11/47, published by Taj Publishers, Delhi).

Maʿārif al-Qurʾān records: The expiation for backbiting is that one should pray to Allah for the forgiveness of the person who was backbitten, saying: O Allah, forgive our sins and his. (7/133).

(Likewise in Tafsīr al-Maẓharī: 11/47, 50). The same is written in Maẓāhir-e Ḥaqq Jadīd. (5/480, Bāb Ḥifẓ al-Lisān, published by Idārah-e Islāmiyyāt, Deoband). ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) states:

إذا تاب المغتاب قبل وصولها تنفعه توبته بلا استحلال من صاحبه

وإن بلغته فلا بد له من الاستحلال والاستغفار. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/٤۱۰، سعيد، كتاب الكراهية).

ʿAllāmah Ibn Malak (may Allah have mercy on him) has cited in Sharḥ Tuḥfat al-Mulūk that before the backbiting reaches the person concerned, seeking forgiveness is enough, because it is pardoned before the sin has grown severe; but after the backbiting has reached him it is not enough.

وسئل الإمام أبو محمد عمن إذا مات قبل وصول الغيبة إلى المغتاب فيه هل ينفعه توبته؟ قال: نعم، ينفعه فإنه تاب قبل أن يصير الذنب ذنباً لأنه إنما يصير ذنباً إذا بلغت إليه. (شرح تحفة الملوك: ۲/ ۲۰۹۲).

The annotator has raised an objection to this, namely that the correct position is that the sin is committed by the mere act of backbiting. And this is indeed the reality: the sin of backbiting is incurred by the backbiting itself. The scholars have written, however, that as long as the backbiting does not reach that person, seeking forgiveness is enough.

قال في شرح الطريقة المحمدية: كفارة أثم غيبة من اغتبته أيها المكلف! أن تستغفر له

وهذا إذا لم تبلغه الغيبة. (۲/۲۲٦).

A hadith states:

وعن أنس رضي الله عنه عن النبي ﷺ قال: كفارة من اغتبته أن تستغفر له. قال العراقي: ابن أبي الدنيا في الصمت، والحارث بن أبي أسامة في مسنده من حديث أنس رضي الله عنه بسند ضعيف. (تخريج العراقي: ۳/۱۵۰/۳۰۲۵).

That is, the expiation for backbiting is to pray for the forgiveness of the person who was backbitten. Although this report is weak, there are corroborating narrations for it. See: al-Maqāṣid al-Ḥasanah (no. 804).

The scholars have permitted backbiting in the following few cases:

1. when the person is unknown; 2. when the one addressed does not recognise him; 3. when he flaunts his sinfulness openly; 4. when advice is sought; 5. in order to protect Muslims from that person's religious or worldly harm; 6. complaining about an oppressor before someone who is able to put a stop to the oppression; 6. describing the state of affairs in order to obtain a fatwa about some incident; 7. disclosing to a buyer a defect in the goods sold (a male or female slave); 8. identifying a person, such as "the lame one", "the one-eyed one", "the weak-sighted one" and so forth; 9. criticising the transmitters of hadith. There are further cases besides these. ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) mentioned about eleven such cases in Fatāwā Shāmī (6/409, Saʿīd) and then set them out in verse as well. ʿAllāmah al-Ṣanʿānī (may Allah have mercy on him) likewise set these cases out in verse in Subul al-Salām, his commentary on Bulūgh al-Marām. (Subul al-Salām: 4/370; and Iʿānat al-Ṭālibīn: 3/535).

See further: (Tafsīr al-Qurṭubī: 16/337; Iḥyāʾ ʿUlūm al-Dīn: 3/150; and Maʿārif al-Qurʾān).
