### TITLE
The difference between the abode of Islam, the abode of war and the abode of safety

### QUESTION
What is the difference between the abode of Islam, the abode of war and the abode of safety? And does the abode of war exist nowadays or not? Can the countries of America and Europe be called the abode of war or not?

### ANSWER
The abode of Islam is a state in which the Muslims hold such political ascendancy that they are able to enforce all the rulings of Islam.

The abode of war is a state of unbelievers in which the unbelievers enjoy security while the Muslim citizens are deprived of it, and in which the Muslims are unable to exercise their religious rights and acts of worship, or to hold the Friday and Eid prayers and the like, openly.

The abode of safety is a country in which the levers of power are in the hands of non-Muslims but the Muslims are secure, are able to carry out the duty of calling people to the faith, and are able to act upon those rulings of Islam whose enforcement does not require political power.

Keeping the definitions given above in view, it seems difficult to call today's non-Muslim countries the abode of war. Accordingly, most non-Muslim countries will fall within the category of the abode of safety.
