### TITLE
Praying the Friday prayer in the congregational mosque is better

### QUESTION
Does a person receive greater reward for praying the Friday prayer in the congregational mosque, or in a small mosque, or in a prayer hall?

### ANSWER
Praying the Friday prayer in the congregational mosque is better and preferable. Nevertheless, if the Friday prayer is also held in the mosque of one's own neighbourhood, praying it there is valid and correct.

Consider what Mullā ʿAlī al-Qārī (may Allah have mercy on him) wrote in his commentary on Mishkāt under one of the hadiths:

"قال رسول الله ﷺ صلاة الرجل في بيته بصلاة أي تحسب بصلاة واحد وصلا ته في مسجد القبائل بخمس وعشرين صلاة أي بالإضافة إلى صلاة في بيته لا مطلقاً وصلا ته في المسجد الذي يجمع فيه بخمس مائة صلاة أي بالنسبة إلى مسجد الحي". (مرقاة شرح مشكاة: ۲/۲۲۸)

That is, the Prophet ﷺ said that a man's prayer in his house counts as one prayer; his prayer in the mosque of his own locality counts as 25 prayers — that is, in comparison with the prayer in his house, not in an absolute sense; and his prayer in the congregational mosque counts as 500 prayers — that is, in comparison with what has just been mentioned.

ʿAllāmah al-Laknawī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

So if all the people of a city agree to pray the Friday prayer in one congregational mosque, there are many good things in this. First, one is saved from the disagreement of the imams; one gains the reward of praying with a large congregation; agreement and unity among the people grow; the standing of Islam is increased; and whoever works to bring this about will also earn reward. (For the detailed fatwa see: Majmūʿat al-Fatāwā: 1/331, 334, Ārām Bāgh, Karachi)

See also: Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 3/66; Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband: 5/162, Dār al-Ishāʿat; and ʿUmdat al-Fiqh: Kitāb al-Ṣalāh, part two, p. 458, Mujaddidiyyah.
