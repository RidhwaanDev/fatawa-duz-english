### TITLE
Learning to drive from a non-mahram woman

### QUESTION
What is the ruling on learning to drive from a non-mahram woman?

### ANSWER
In the same way, it is not permissible or correct for a man to learn driving from a non-mahram woman, even if she is old. This too involves seclusion with a non-mahram, and without a need recognised by the sharia it is impermissible.

See what Hazrat Mufti Sayyid ʿAbd al-Raḥīm Lājpūrī Sahib (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

In learning from a non-mahram woman, occasions of sitting beside her and talking with her will certainly arise; going unveiled is practically unavoidable; and besides this there are many other evils. الخ ۔ (فتاویٰ رحیمیہ: ٦/٤۲۵، مکتبہ رحیمیہ).
