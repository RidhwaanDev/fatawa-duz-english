### TITLE
A wealthy person using zakat items belonging to a poor person

### QUESTION
Poor people often receive things as zakat: books, utensils, bedding, a bed, bedsheets and so on. Wealthy people who own the nisab come to visit them and use these very things. Is this permissible or not?

### ANSWER
The passage in al-Hidāyah, vol. 3, Kitāb al-Mukātab, shows that it is permissible for a wealthy person to use such things when ownership of them is transferred to him, but not by way of mere permission to use, which is the case described in the question.

Consider what al-Hidāyah states:

وهذا بخلاف ما إذا أباح للغني والهاشمي لأن المباح له يتناوله علي ملك المبيح فلم يتبدل الملك فلا تطيبه. (الهداية: ۳/۳۳۹).

This, however, causes real hardship and difficulty. Suppose a carpet received as zakat is spread out in a man's house and he has to tell his well-off guests, "Please do not sit here, this is zakat property." Apart from anything else, disclosing that something is zakat property is itself felt as a humiliation. On this question, therefore, the commentator on al-Hidāyah, Saʿdī Chalabī (may Allah have mercy on him), inclines towards allowing such use, and this humble servant inclines the same way.

The author of al-Hidāyah has said that there is nothing impure or wrong in the charity itself; what is wrong is the taking of it, and that is why accepting zakat is a humiliation for a Hāshimī and for a wealthy person. Mawlānā Saʿdī Chalabī therefore said that if a wealthy person uses a zakat item in a poor man's house, this ought to be permissible, because he has not taken it into his ownership but has only used it.

قال المصنف رحمه الله: ولا يجوز ذلك للغني من غير حاجة وللهاشمي لزيادة حرمته. أقول: فعلي هذا لو أباح الفقير للغني أو الهاشمي ينبغي أن يطيب لهما عنده، إذ لا أخذ منهماكما لا يخفي.
(تكملة فتح القدير مع حاشية سعدي چلپي: ۹/۲۱٤، دار الفكر).

Furthermore, there is no requirement to disclose that an item is zakat property. This too shows that well-off people may use such things; for if their use were not allowed, it would have been necessary to say that the item is zakat property — and that is not so.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
