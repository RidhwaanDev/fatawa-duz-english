### TITLE
The difference between an irregular marriage and a void marriage

### QUESTION
What is the difference between an irregular marriage (nikāḥ fāsid) and a void marriage (nikāḥ bāṭil)?

### ANSWER
A void marriage is one in which the very object of the contract is absent, whereas in an irregular marriage the object of the contract is present but one of the conditions of the marriage contract is missing. This is what emerges from the examples given by ʿAllāmah Sayyid Aḥmad al-Ṭaḥṭāwī (may Allah have mercy on him), and it also appears to be the substance of the fatwa of Muftī Rashīd Aḥmad. Otherwise there is sharp disagreement among the jurists; ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) has discussed it in several places, and in some of them he has written that there is no difference between the two. Since ʿAllāmah al-Ṭaḥṭāwī's wording on this question is clear, it is his position that has been adopted.

Consider what al-Ṭaḥṭāwī's gloss on al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

وعدة المنكوحة نكاحاً فاسداً كنكاح بغير شهود، فلا عدة في باطل مثاله: تزوج المتزوجة عالماً بذلك. (حاشية الطحطاوي على الدر المختار: ۲/۲۲۱).

From the passage quoted above we learn that a marriage without witnesses is irregular, because the object of the contract is present but a condition of the marriage (two witnesses) is missing; whereas marriage to a woman who is already married is void, because the object of the contract itself is absent.

It is likewise clear from the passages of Majmaʿ al-Fatāwā and al-Mabsūṭ that a marriage between an unbeliever and a Muslim is void and comes under the ruling of fornication, because the object of the contract is absent.

Al-Shāmī states:

ويجب مهر المثل في نكاح الفاسد وهو الذي فقد شرطاً من شرائط الصحة كشهود ومثله تزوج الأختين معاً ونكاح الأخت في عدة الأخت ونكاح المعتدة والخامسة في عدة الرابعة

عن مجمع الفتاوى: نكح كافر مسلمة فولدت منه لا يثبت النسب منه ولا تجب العدة لأنه نكاح باطل. (فتاوى الشامي: ۳/۱۳۲).

Al-Mabsūṭ states:

قال: وإذا تزوج الذمي مسلمة فرق بينهما لقوله تعالى: ﴿ولا تنكحوا المشركين حتى يؤمنوا﴾ وإن أسلم بعد النكاح لم يترك علي نكاحه لأن أصل النكاح كان باطلاً فبالإسلام لا ينقلب صحيحاً. (المبسوط للإمام السرخسي رحمه الله: ۵/٤۵).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

The upshot, as far as this humble servant has understood from studying the texts, is that if the object of the contract simply does not exist — leaving aside any particular quality of the contracting party — then the marriage is void, كنكاح منكوحة الغير مع العلم بأنها متزوجة (as in marrying a woman who is already married to another while knowing that she is married), because two ownerships cannot come together in a single moment. But if the object of the contract does exist and the marriage is forbidden only because of some particular quality of the contracting party or the absence of a condition, then the marriage is irregular, کنکاح المحارم (as in marrying close relatives). Besides being closer to the distinction drawn between a void and an irregular sale, this definition is also supported by the statements of the jurists (may Allah have mercy on them). (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 5/62–64).
