### TITLE
The objection that the bīsī (committee) scheme involves interest, and its answer

### QUESTION
In the villages of India and Pakistan a certain arrangement is well known and widespread among women: the women deposit, say, one thousand rupees each with one woman, and then every month a draw is made. If twenty-four women are contributing, then the first twenty-four thousand goes to one woman, but in future this woman will keep on contributing her money until the twenty-four thousand is made up. There is no interest in this; there is only precedence and delay. Among us some scholars declare it permissible because there is no increase or decrease, while some honoured muftis declare this arrangement impermissible, saying

"كل قرض جر نفعاً فهو ربا"

on the ground that some gained the benefit of receiving a larger sum in one lump because of having lent money — for example, twenty-four women contributed a thousand each and twenty-four thousand was collected, and by the draw one woman received the twenty-four thousand, which she will go on contributing afterwards. What is the ruling of the sacred law on this arrangement? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, an arrangement of this kind is permissible and correct in the sacred law. Several muftis have stated this explicitly: 1. Mawlānā Khālid Sayf Allāh in Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil; 2. Mawlānā Yūsuf Ludhyānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) in Āp kē Masāʾil awr Un kā Ḥal; and 3. permissibility is recorded in Fatāwā Ḥaqqāniyyah as well. Consider the passages:

In Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil it is recorded: its form is that a particular sum is fixed and a number of persons become members of it; they pay the amount every month in the agreed proportion, and the total sum is given each month to one of them by a draw or by mutual agreement. For example, if the committee is of two thousand and ten persons take part, then for ten months each person will deposit two hundred rupees, and each month one of them will receive the lump sum. This form is permissible, because there is no ground for its being invalid. The person who obtains the money of the committee before the completion of the term is in the position of a debtor, and the other members are lenders; the lender grants him a period of grace, and likewise obtains no profit upon it. This is not only permissible but is also a demand of human sympathy and Islamic morals. (Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil 1/372.)

In Fatāwā Ḥaqqāniyyah it is recorded: for a number of persons to collect equal amounts every month in this way and give them to one of them by a draw is mutual assistance and a loan. There is in it no transfer of ownership, one way or the other; rather every participant wishes that the money collected this time should come to him, and so, in order to remove dispute and to keep everyone content, they hold a draw. Where the rights of all the participants are equal, casting lots to please them is permissible. (Fatāwā Ḥaqqāniyyah 6/200.)

In Āp kē Masāʾil awr Un kā Ḥal it is mentioned:

Question: Some people, under the name of a "company", ten men, contribute thirty-two rupees each, and after a month a draw is held and whichever member's name comes out is given six thousand rupees, whereas his deposited amount is nine hundred and sixty rupees. Is this permissible or impermissible? The member whose committee has come out goes on paying thirty-two rupees daily until the six thousand rupees is complete.

Answer: This method of the "company" is a transaction of giving and taking a loan. I for one regard it as permissible. (Āp kē Masāʾil awr Un kā Ḥal 7/375, new edition.)

Concerning

كل قرض جر نفعاً

the jurists have written that the benefit forbidden is one stipulated in the contract; a benefit that is not stipulated is not forbidden. And here there is no benefit at all; there is only precedence and delay, and that too is the result of the draw. It is not stipulated. See what is recorded in the marginal notes on Tabyīn al-Ḥaqāʾiq:

قوله ومن وضع درهماً عند بقال، قال الكرخي رحمه الله في مختصره في كتاب الصرف: وكل قرض جر منفعة لا يجوز، مثل أن يقرض دراهم غلة على أن يعطيه صحاحاً أو يقرض قرضاً على أن يبيع به بيعاً؛ لأنه روي أن كل قرض جر منفعة فهو ربا، وتأويل هذا عندنا أن تكون المنفعة موجبة بعقد القرض مشروطة فيه، وإن كانت غير مشروطة فيه فاستقرض غلة فقضاه صحاحاً من غير أن يشترط عليه جاز، وكذلك لو باعه شيئاً، ولم يكن شرط البيع في أصل العقد جاز ذلك، ولم يكن به بأس إلى هنا لفظ الكرخي في مختصره، وذلك لأن القرض تمليك الشيء بمثله فإذا جر نفعاً صار كأنه استزاد فيه الربا فلا يجوز؛ ولأن القرض تبرع وجر المنفعة يخرجه عن موضعه، وإنما يكره إذا كانت المنفعة مشروطة في العقد، وإذا لم تكن مشروطة فيه يكون المقترض متبرعاً بها فصار كالرجحان الذي دفعه ــ ﷺ ـــ في بدل القرض، وقد روي عن ابن عمر رضي الله عنه أنه كان يستقرض فإذا خرج عطاؤه أعطاه أجود مما أخذ. (حاشية الشلبي على تبيين الحقائق: ٦/۲۹).

See further: (Fiqh al-Muʿāmalāt, "Jadīd Muʿāmalāt kē Sharʿī Aḥkām" 1/183; Muntakhabāt-e Niẓām al-Fatāwā 1/195; Kitāb al-Fatāwā 5/343.)

The objection and its answer:

The objectors say that this is a transaction of a loan in which the lender, by handing over money, has obtained more for the time being — even though afterwards he will pay back the amount received — and that this is made conditional upon the draw: whoever's lot comes out will receive more than the amount he has paid at that time, even though it will be evened out later.

The answer: 1. The gist of the objection is that in the precedence and delay effected by the draw, the one who receives precedence has gained the benefit of a loan. The answer to this is that this arrangement is permissible, and that the money which is collected with one person is not a loan but a trust. In most cases the money of each is not kept separately but is kept mixed together; and when a trust is mixed with the permission of the depositor, then in the view of many jurists it becomes a partnership. So this money became partnership property among those contributing it, from which the partners go on benefiting by means of the draw — just as various people mix their dates together and eat them and benefit from them.

2. If a trust is placed with someone and, with permission, becomes mixed and merged with the property of another, then Imām Abū Ḥanīfah (may Allah have mercy on him) calls it a loan, while the two companions (may Allah have mercy on them) call it a partnership; and many jurists have adopted the position of the two companions. In this matter, on account of the custom and usage of the people, the position of the two companions is indeed preferable. In the Majallat al-Aḥkām al-ʿAdliyyah, which was the law of the Ottoman caliphate, the position of the two companions has been taken. Likewise Tanwīr al-Abṣār, al-Durr al-Mukhtār, the author of al-Baḥr, Ibn al-Humām, the author of Minḥat al-Khāliq, and also Mawlānā Fatḥ Muḥammad in ʿIṭr-e Hidāyah on p. 208, and Mawlānā Aḥsan Nānōtwī in his notes on Kanz al-Daqāʾiq, have adopted the position of the two companions and accepted it as a partnership.

Consider the proofs and references. The ruling on the draw:

When the rights are equal, then a draw for precedence and delay is permissible and correct; but if it is for nullifying a right or for establishing what is false, it is impermissible. See what the author of al-Durr al-Mukhtār says:

ويكتب أساميهم ويقرع لتطيب القلوب فمن خرج اسمه أولاً فله السهم الأول ومن خرج ثانياً فله السهم الثاني إلى أن ينتهي إلى الأخير. وفي الشامي: قوله لتطيب القلوب أشار إلى أن القرعة غير واجبة

الخ. (الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ٦/۲٦۲، سعيد).

وقال في مجمع الأنهر: وإنما يقرع لتطيب القلوب ونفي تهمة الميل عن نفسه فلا يكون ذلك في معني القمار. (مجمع الأنهر: ۳/۳۷۳، ط: بيروت).

In Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ it is recorded:

ومنها أن يقرع بينهم بعد الفراغ من القسمة ويشترط عليهم قبول من خرج سهمه أولاً فله هذا السهم من هذا الجانب من الدار ومن خرج سهمه بعده فله السهم الذي عليه يليه هكذا ثم يقرع بينهم، لا لأن القرعة يتعلق بها حكم بل لتطييب النفوس ولورود السنة بها، ولأن ذلك أنفي للتهمة فكان سنة. (بدائع الصنائع: ۷/۱۹، كتاب القسمة، سعيد).

Many jurists have stated explicitly that by mixing the property with the depositor's permission the trustee becomes a partner. See:

قال في الدر المختار: وكذا لو خلطها المودع بجنسها أو بغيره بماله أو مال آخر بغير إذن المالك بحيث لا تتميز إلا بكلفة كحنطة بشعير ودراهم جياد بزيوف

وإن بإذنه اشتركا شركة أملاك. (الدر المختار: ۵/٦٦۸، سعيد).

In Fatḥ al-Qadīr it is stated:

وإن اتفقا على الخلط ورضيا به، وهو جنس واحد مكيل أو موزون صار عيناً مشتركة. (فتح القدير: ۵/۳۹٤، مكتبة رشيديه كوئته).

وقال في البحر: وإن خلطها بإذنه كان شريكاً له. (البحر الرائق: ۷/۲۷٦، كوئته).

Mawlānā Muḥammad Aḥsan Nānōtwī, in his notes on the Kanz, accepted it as a partnership on the authority of Fatḥ al-Qadīr. (Kanz, note 4, p. 348.)

وللمزيد راجع: (شرح المجلة للشيخ محمد خالد الأتاسي: ۳/۲٦۷، وفتح القدير: ۵/۳۷۸، وحاشية الطحطاوي على الدر المختار: ۲/۵۱۲، ورد المحتار: ٤/۳۰۰، سعيد، والبحر الرائق: ۵/۱٦۷).

وفي رد المحتار: قوله الهلاك على مالكه، فلا يرجع بنصف الهالك على الشريك الآخر حيث بطلت الشركة ولو الهلاك في يد الآخر؛ لأن المال في يده أمانة، بخلاف ما لو هلك بعد الخلط؛ لأنه يهلك على الشركة لعدم التمييز، ط عن الاتقاني. (رد المحتار: ٤/۳۱۵، وكذا في ملتقي الأبحر: ۳٦۹).

وقال في المبسوط: وكل واحد منهما أمين في يده من مال صاحبه واشتراط الضمان على الأمين باطل. (المبسوط: ۱۱/۱۵۷، ط إدارة القرآن).

In Āp kē Masāʾil awr Un kā Ḥal it is recorded:

Question: People's bīsī money, that is, committees, is collected with me. Many people, or my friends, ask for a loan — that is, for paying off a debt, or for treatment in illness, or for paying school and college or examination fees, or under some other head — and I give it to them, and it is duly returned as promised. Is this practice permissible in the sacred law, since the money belongs to others?

Answer: The bīsī money that is collected with you is a trust. If you obtain permission from all the shareholders that you may spend this money, then it is permissible; otherwise not. (Āp kē Masāʾil awr Un kā Ḥal 7/211.)

On account of necessity and common practice the jurists have adopted the position of the two companions (may Allah have mercy on them), and at a time of need this is permissible; the jurists have stated this explicitly. Consider a few references:

In Fatāwā Shāmī it is stated:

لا يعدل عن قول الإمام إلى قولهما أو قول أحدهما إلا لضرورة من ضعف دليل أو تعامل بخلافه كالمزارعة. (فتاوى الشامي: ۱/۳۵۹، ط: سعيد).

See further: (al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq 1/246.)

Moreover, the position of the two companions is not outside the position of the Imām; rather it is the very position of Abū Ḥanīfah (may Allah have mercy on him).

See what ʿAllāmah Shāmī says:

إن أقوال أصحاب الإمام غير خارجة عن مذهبه، فقد نقلوا عنهم أنهم ما قالوا قولاً إلا هو مروي عن الإمام كما أوضحت ذلك في شرح منظومتي في رسم المفتي. (فتاوى الشامي: ٤/۳٦۲، ط: سعيد. وكذا في العقود الدرية في تنقيح الفتاوى الحامدية: ۱/۱۰۹).
