### TITLE
One imam leading two different congregations for the Friday ṣalāh

### QUESTION
In a certain masjid the Friday ṣalāh is performed twice in congregation, and one and the same imam leads on both occasions. In this situation will the ṣalāh of the second congregation be valid or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the view of the Ḥanafīs, the imam's obligation was discharged by his first ṣalāh; the second ṣalāh is now a supererogatory prayer for him, and the following of one who is praying an obligatory prayer behind one who is praying a supererogatory prayer is not valid. Therefore the ṣalāh of the second congregation did not count.

See Al-Hidāyah, in which it is recorded:

و لا يصلي المفترض خلف المتنفل لأن الاقتداء بناء ووصف الفرضية معدوم في حق الإمام فلا يتحقق البناء على المعدوم. (الهداية: ۱/۵۸، ط: المكتبة الإسلامية). (وكذا في الجوهرة النيرة: ۱/۷۳).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
