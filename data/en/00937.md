### TITLE
Zakat on 10 rand together with a quarter ounce of gold

### QUESTION
These days the nisab of silver is very low. If an adult girl has 10 rand and a quarter of an ounce of gold jewellery, is zakat obligatory on her or not?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, if adding the value of the gold to the rand completes the nisab of silver, then zakat is necessary. If it does not complete the nisab, zakat is not necessary.

Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

وتضم

الذهب إلى الفضة قيمة كذا في الكنز حتى لو ملك مائة درهم وخمسة دنانير قيمتها مائة درهم تجب الزكاة عنده. (فتاوى هندية: ۱/۱۷۹، فصل في زكاة الذهب والفضة. وكذا في الهداية: (۱/۱۹٦، فصل في العروض).

Al-Hidāyah states:

ثم قال: يقومها بما هو أنفع للمساكين احتياطا لحق الفقراء، قال: وهذا رواية عن أبي حنيفة رحمه الله. (الهداية: ۱/۱۹۵، فصل في العروض، شركة علمية، والشامي: ۲/۲۹۹، سعيد).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

If a person has five rupees in cash over and above his needs, together with three tolas of gold, zakat becomes obligatory. This is because cash money takes the ruling of silver and gold, and three tolas of gold plus five rupees in cash together come to the value of fifty-two and a half tolas of silver; zakat therefore becomes necessary. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۵/۱٦٦، کتاب الزکوٰۃ) ۔

Working out the nisab when the price of gold and silver keeps changing

Question: In our times the price of gold and silver is always changing. How, then, should a person pay his zakat?

Answer: On the day a person comes to own wealth that reaches the amount of the nisab, the reckoning of the year begins from that day, and when the year is complete he pays the zakat on it. If his gold or silver does not by itself make up a nisab, but adding trade goods and the like to it does make up the nisab of silver, then he pays zakat according to the nisab of silver.

Consider what al-Hidāyah states:

ليس فيما دون مائتي درهم صدقة، لقوله عليه السلام: "ليس فيما دون خمس أواق صدقة" والأوقية أربعون درهماً، فإذا كانت مائتين وحال عليها الحول ففيها خمسة دراهم، لأنه عليه السلام كتب إلى معاذ رضي الله عنه أن خذ من كل مائتي درهم خمسة دراهم ومن كل عشرين مثقالاً من ذهب نصف مثقال. (الهداية: ۱/۱۹٤، باب زكاة المال).

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

وسببه ملك نصاب حولي تام، وفي الشامي: (قوله نصاب) هو ما نصبه الشارع علامة على وجوب الزكاة من المقادير المبينة في الأبواب الآتية، وهذا شرط في غير زكاة الزرع والثمار

. قوله نسبة للحول لحولانه عليه أي دون حولان الحول على النصاب شرط لكونه سبباً وهذا علة للنسبة. (الدر المختار مع الشامي: ۲/۲۵۹، سعيد).

Kitāb al-Fatāwā states:

In zakat the reckoning is made according to the date on which a person first came to own the zakat nisab. Whatever he holds at that time — money kept by him, gold and silver, shares, trade goods, or a debt he expects to recover — is added up, and zakat is paid at the rate of 25 rupees per thousand. Neither his income nor his budget is taken into account; rather, zakat is paid out of whatever zakatable wealth he possesses on that date. (کتاب الفتاوی تیسرا حصہ ص ۲٦۵، نعیمیہ) ۔

Zakat when the husband has nothing but the wife has jewellery

Question: If the husband has no salary at all but the wife has jewellery, is zakat binding? And how is it to be paid?

Answer: In the case asked about, even though the husband has no salary or income, zakat is still binding on the wife, because she is the owner of the jewellery — provided the jewellery reaches the nisab.

Consider what Sunan al-Tirmidhī states:

عن عمرو بن شعيب عن أبيه عن جده، أن امرأتين أتتا رسول الله ﷺ وفي أيديهما سواران من ذهب، فقال لهما: أتؤديان زكاته، فقالتا: لا، فقال لهما رسول الله ﷺ: أتحبان أن يسوركما الله بسوارين من نار، قالتا: لا، قال: فأديا زكاته. (ترمذي شريف: ۱/۱۳۸، باب ما جاء في زكاة الحلي، فيصل).

Al-Hidāyah states:

الزكاة واجبة على الحر العاقل البالغ المسلم إذا بلغ نصاباً ملكاً تامّاً وحال عليه الحول. (الهداية: ۱/۱۸۵).

Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

A woman is the owner of her jewellery and her dowry goods, and the zakat on them is her own responsibility. Since she generally has no cash with which to pay it, she takes it from her husband and pays it, or her husband pays it at her instruction and with her permission. If the husband neither pays it nor gives her the money, then it is necessary for the woman to sell some of her belongings and pay it, because the obligation rests upon her. (کفایت المفتی: ٤/۲٦٦، کتاب الزکاۃ پہلا باب، دار الاشاعت) ۔

Kitāb al-Fatāwā states:

In the Qur'an and the hadith zakat has been declared necessary on gold and silver without any qualification (Sūrat al-Tawbah). Indeed, it is established in the hadiths that Rasūlullāh ﷺ expressly instructed that zakat be paid even on the jewellery that the women Companions (may Allah be pleased with them) were wearing (Tirmidhī). For this reason, in the view of Imam Abū Ḥanīfah (may Allah have mercy on him), zakat is necessary on gold and silver in every case, whether they are in the form of jewellery or not, and whether the jewellery is in use or not. (کتاب الفتاوی: تیسرا حصہ ص: ۲۸۰) ۔
