### TITLE
The ruling on eating shark

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to eat the shark, which eats human beings and other sea creatures, or not — given that it is a man-eater?

### ANSWER
In the Ḥanafī school, of all sea creatures only fish is lawful to eat; it is not permissible or correct to eat any sea creature other than fish. Fish, moreover, is lawful in all its kinds. What is needed, then, is to establish whether the shark is counted among the kinds of fish or not.

The World Book Encyclopaedia describes it as a fish. The passage is in English; here is a summary of it:

The shark is a flesh-eating fish and the most fearsome creature in the whole sea. Scientists have stated that there are about 250 kinds of shark, and that it generally lives in warm water. All kinds of shark are flesh-eaters, and most of them eat fish. The shark is famous for eating human beings, but such incidents have occurred very rarely: in the whole world, out of these 250 kinds only 25 kinds have attacked human beings. The shark is different from other fish. (Summarised from the World Book Encyclopaedia: 17/300).

See further: (Encyclopaedia Britannica: 9/116).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā records:

Question: there is a sea fish which in Arabic is called qirsh, and in al-Munjid the following is written about it:

نوع من السمك يعرف بكلب البحر يقطع الحيوان بأسنانه كما يقطع السيف تخافه جميع دوات البحر.

Besides this, people acquainted with conditions at sea say that this fish menstruates, and secondly that, unlike ordinary fish, it does not lay eggs but gives birth to live young. Is this fish lawful or unlawful? Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā ʿAbd al-Ḥayy (may Allah have mercy on him) has quoted in his fatāwā the position that it is unlawful. I hope you will give us guidance. بینوا توجروا ۔

Answer: in investigations of this kind, both in the sharia and in reason, the word of the experts in the field should be decisive. The zoologists of the modern age state four marks of a fish:

(1) A backbone.

(2) Gills for breathing.

(3) Fins for swimming.

(4) The body temperature rising and falling according to the surroundings.

These marks are present in the qirsh, and this is clear from its form as well. It is therefore lawful; even so, in view of the disagreement, caution is preferable. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 7/391).

Al-Mawrid (an English–Arabic dictionary) records:

(shark)

القرش: سمك مفترس. (المورد، ص: ۱۰٦۵).

The jurists have declared fish, in all its kinds, to be lawful.

See what Fatāwā Qāḍīkhān states:

ولا بأس بسائر أنواع السمك نحو الجريث والمارماهي ولا يؤكل ما في البحر سوى السمك وطير الماء عندنا. (فتاوى قاضيخان على هامش الهندية: ۳/۳۵۷).

Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ states:

ويستوي في حل الأكل جميع أنواع السمك من الجريث والمارماهي وغيرهما لأن ما ذكرنا من الدلائل في إباحة السمك لا يفصل بين سمك وسمك إلا ما خص بدليل وقد روي عن سيدنا على رضي الله عنه وابن عباس رضي الله عنه إباحة الجريث والسمك الذكر ولم ينقل عن غيرهما خلاف ذلك فيكون إجماعاً. (بدائع الصنائع: ۵/۳٦، سعيد).

Al-Nutaf fī al-Fatāwā states:

وأما دواب البحر فإنها محرمة سوى السمك بأجناسها في قول الفقهاء. (النتف في الفتاوى، ص ۱۵۰، حكم دواب البحر، ط: بيروت).

وفي "الفقه الحنفي في ثوبه الجديد" (۵/۲٦۱): قال ابن حجر رحمه الله: ولا خلاف بين العلماء في حل السمك على اختلاف أنواعه.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
