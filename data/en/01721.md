### TITLE
A vow to have someone perform hajj or to send someone out for a chillah

### QUESTION
If a person said, "If Allah Most High gives me a son within a year I will have Zayd perform hajj," or "I will send him out for a chillah," is the vow binding or not?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about the vow is not binding, because a vow takes effect for one's own act and not for the act of another, and because a vow takes effect for an act of worship intended for its own sake, whereas going out for a chillah is not an act of worship intended for its own sake.

Consider what al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

ومن نذر نذرا مطلقاً أو معلقاً بشرط، وكان من جنسه واجب

وهو عبادة مقصودة

ووجد الشرط المعلق به لزم الناذر لحديث "من نذر وسمي فعليه الوفاء بما سمي". وفي الشامي: (قوله: وهو عبادة مقصودة)

وفي البدائع: ومن شروطه أن يكون قربة مقصودة، فلا يصح النذر بعيادة المريض، وتشييع الجنازة، والوضوء، والاغتسال، ودخول المسجد، ومس المصحف، والأذان

(قوله: لزم الناذر) أي لزمه الوفاء به، والمراد أنه يلزمه الوفاء بأصل القربة التي التزمها. (الدر المختار مع الشامي: ۳/۷۳۵، سعيد).

Imdād al-Fatāwā states:

(Question) A person made a vow on this condition, that if Allah Most High granted him a son he would send him to the Noble Kaʿbah after twelve years; but at the time of the vow he was well off, and now poverty has come upon him. What should be done now — is fulfilling the vow obligatory upon him or not?

(Answer) A vow takes effect for one's own act, not for the act of another; so this vow too has not taken effect. (امداد الفتاوی: ۲/۵٦۱) ۔

Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

(Question) Zayd vowed that if a certain matter of his came about he would have someone perform hajj. Zayd's matter has come about; is it now obligatory for Zayd to have the vow carried out?

Answer: A vow of this sort becomes binding in the form "I will myself perform hajj," but in the form "I will have someone perform hajj" it has not become binding. (کفایت المفتی: ۲/۲۵۲) ۔

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

Zayd vowed that if his hand became well he would go out for forty days in tablīgh, that is, with a jamāʿah. Is this vow valid or not, and is fulfilling it obligatory or not? And if it is not obligatory, is it at least permissible?

(Answer) It is a condition for the validity of a vow that the thing vowed be an act of worship intended for its own sake. Tablīgh is not an act of worship intended for its own sake, so this vow has not taken effect; fulfilling it is not obligatory, though it is permissible. (احسن الفتاوی: ۵/٤۹۱) ۔
