### TITLE
The ruling on Amazon Prime

### QUESTION
Amazon is an online store in which trade takes place through the internet. It is like a large market and every kind of item is sold in it. It has a scheme whereby, if a customer pays 99 dollars a month, he will be given the following concessions and facilities:

1. Delivery of small items will be free.
2. A considerable concession will be given on the delivery of large items.
3. Delivery of foodstuffs will take place within a very short time.
4. Certain items will be reserved for these customers only.
5. When there is a discounted price on some item, these customers will be given priority.
6. There will be permission to read one book free of charge each month from the Amazon library — and so on.

This is called Amazon Prime. Is such a transaction permissible or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question, the summary of this contract is that in exchange for 99 dollars a month the above-mentioned concessions and facilities will be given; that is, the favour of the concessions and facilities has been made conditional upon the 99 dollars. This is called a conditional gratuity, and its permissibility is explicitly stated in the hadiths and in the passages of the noble jurists and the elders. On this basis the above-mentioned transaction is permissible and correct. For a detailed account of the evidence, a study of Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā, volume five, is beneficial.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
