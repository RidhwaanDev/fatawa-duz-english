### TITLE
Using ṣadaqat al-fiṭr money to cook food and serve it

### QUESTION
If the money collected for ṣadaqat al-fiṭr is used to have food cooked, and the prisoners in a jail are then seated together and served this food on the day of Eid, is ṣadaqat al-fiṭr thereby discharged or not?

### ANSWER
In ṣadaqat al-fiṭr (the charity of Eid al-Fitr), just as in zakat, transfer of ownership is necessary. When people are seated together and fed, no transfer of ownership takes place; it is merely permission to use the food, since they do not have the right to dispose of it as they please. Ṣadaqat al-fiṭr is therefore not discharged in this way. If, however, the food is placed in each person's own vessel and handed over as his property, then ṣadaqat al-fiṭr is discharged. It should also be borne in mind that some prisoners in a jail own the nisab; giving to them will not discharge it either.

Consider what al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq states:

وأما ركنها فهو نفس الأداء إلى المصرف فهي التمليك كالزكاة فلا تتأدى بطعام الإباحة. (البحر الرائق: ۲/۲۵۲، باب صدقة الفطر، كوئته).

Shāmī states:

واشتراط التمليك فلا تكفي الإباحة كما في البدائع هذا ما ظهر لي، تأمل. (شامي: ۲/۳٦۹، باب صدقة الفطر، سعيد).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

Food given as zakat must be handed over to the deserving person as his own property, so that he understands that this amount belongs to him — whether he eats it, sells it, or feeds it to someone else. When everyone is seated together and fed, this is not the case. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۹/٦۰۲، مبوب و مرتب)۔

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh states:

The essential element of ṣadaqat al-fiṭr is that it be given to the one entitled to receive it, and this giving must be by way of transfer of ownership, as is the case with zakat. It is therefore not discharged by food merely made available for others to eat. (عمدۃ الفقہ: ۳/۱٦۸، مجددیہ)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
