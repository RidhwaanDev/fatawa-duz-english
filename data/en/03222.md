### TITLE
The ruling on paying a fixed percentage of profits as rent along with the monthly rent

### QUESTION
We run a great many retail shops, and our procedure is that we take land from landlords on a fixed rent for three years, under an agreement providing for a seven per cent annual increase in the rent. Along with this the landlords also demand from us a fixed percentage of our business profits. The way this works is that if two per cent of the business profits comes to more than the fixed monthly rent, the excess has to be paid, and if it comes to less, then only the rent has to be paid. In the eyes of the Sharīʿah, is it permissible for us to enter into such a transaction or not? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question, if it is settled at the time of the contract that the rent will be increased by seven per cent annually, that is valid. Likewise, if the demand for a fixed percentage in addition to the rent is also settled by mutual agreement, that too is permissible and valid; whatever amount the landlords then receive will all be a part of the rent.

The original position of the school on this matter is that it is not permissible; but because of the need of the age and because it is generally current among people, and further because — being by mutual consent — it does not lead to dispute, such a transaction is permissible and valid in the Sharīʿah. Consider what is recorded in Islāmī Qānūn-i Ijārah:

"A" lets his house to "B" for a period of five years. The rent for the first year has been fixed at two thousand a month, and it has also been settled that the rent for each following year will be ten per cent more than that of the preceding year: this contract of hire is valid. (Islāmī Qānūn-i Ijārah, p. 276).

في "مجلة الأحكام": يشترط في صحة الإجارة رضا العاقدين. (المادة: ٤٤۸).

As for the rent being made conditional and alternating between two possibilities, then if the rent on both sides is specified, in the view of the two disciples (may Allah have mercy on them) it is valid to make such a contract, and the ruling is given according to that view.

في درر الحكام شرح مجلة الأحكام: ويصح ترديد الأجرة على صورتين أو ثلاث وتسمية أجرة لكل صورة غير أجرة الصورة الأخرى، ويعتبر البيع في جميعها دفعاً للحاجة وبما أن الإجارة بيع منافع فتقاس على بيع العين (مجمع الأنهر). (درر الحكام شرح مجلة الأحكام: ۱/۵۸۰، المادة: ۵۰٦).
وفي شرح المجلة لمحمد خالد الأتاسي: والحكم المذكور فيها، وهو صحة الإجارة مع اعتبار الشرطين هو قولهما، وعند أبي حنيفة رحمه الله يصح الشرط الأول، ولا يصح الشرط الثاني، فلو خالطه في اليوم الأول يجب المسمى في ذلك اليوم اتفاقاً، وإن خالطه في اليوم الثاني، فعندهما وهو الذي مشت عليه المجلة، يجب المسمى فيه. (شرح المجلة: ۲/۵۹۷).

Similarly, in fixing the additional rent as a percentage there is indeed a kind of uncertainty, in that it is not known what amount that additional percentage will come to; but since it is a slight uncertainty and does not lead to dispute, it will have no effect upon the contract.

في شرح المجلة لمحمد خالد الأتاسي: ومن فسادها: جهالة الأجرة

فلم تكن الجهالة مفضية إلى النزاع، والجهالة ليست مانعة لذاتها بل لكونها مفضية إلى النزاع. (شرح المجلة: ۲/۵۳۸، المادة: ٤٦۰).
وفي "إعلاء السنن": الحاصل: أن الجهالة اليسيرة عفو في ما جري به التعامل، لكونها لا تفضي إلى النزاع عادة. (اعلاء السنن: ۱٦/۲۰۲، إدارة القرآن).

Qāmūs al-Fiqh mentions: Nowadays the system of commission has become current in various trades: that is, instead of fixing a salary, a company fixes a percentage for those who sell its goods. In many religious and secular institutions that are run on people's donations, instead of being given a fixed salary the collectors are given a percentage as their wage, and the madrasah authorities regard this as safer for themselves. For this reason these questions demand deep reflection and attention from the scholars of the present age. Among the Ḥanafīs, the shaykhs of Balkh have permitted this in consideration of custom. (Qāmūs al-Fiqh: 1/499).

Nowadays there is another transaction of the same kind, which is described as a participation fund, that is, a share in the employer's profits. In this too the position is the same: the employee takes from the employer, in addition to his salary, some profit calculated as a percentage. Some scholars of the present day hold a contract of this kind to be permissible; the evidence for it has already been given above and may be consulted there.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
