### TITLE
Zakat on land that has been rented out

### QUESTION
If a person has rented out land, who is liable for the zakat?

### ANSWER
Once the rental income from the land reaches the nisab and a year has passed over it, zakat is due from the owner of the land on that income; otherwise not.

Consider what al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

ولا زكاة على مكاتب

ولا ثياب البدن وأثاث المنزل ودور السكني ونحوها وكذا الكتب. قال الشامي: ونحوها أي كثياب البدن الغير المحتاج إليها وكالحوانيت والعقارات. (الدر مع الشامي: ۲/۲۶۴، ۲٦۵، سعيد).

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states:

Zakat is not due on the value of the property. It is due on the rental income, once that reaches the amount of the nisab and a full year passes over it, either on its own or together with other money in hand. (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband: 6/133).

Āp ke Masāʾil states:

I own two houses. I live in one of them myself and the other is rented out. Is the zakat due on the value of the house or on its rent?

Answer: In this case zakat is not due on the value of the house. It is due on its rent, once that reaches the nisab. (Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥal: 3/371).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
