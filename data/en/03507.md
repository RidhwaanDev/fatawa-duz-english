### TITLE
Buying land in the name of a charitable institution with zakat money

### QUESTION
There is a welfare institution which collects zakat money and assists the poor with it. It now intends to buy land with the zakat money, build houses on it and distribute them among the poor. It wishes to adopt one of the two following methods:

(1) The land will be bought in the name of a trust, and the poor person will benefit from that trust; and if the poor person wishes to sell his share, he will sell it to a person of that same trust.

(2) The land will be bought in the name of a company, and the poor person will be a partner in that company; that is, the land will be divided into shares and the poor will be made owners of those shares with full authority, so that whoever wishes to sell may sell — he has complete authority. Now, will the zakat be discharged by the methods described above or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
(1) By the first method the zakat will not be discharged. The reason is that ownership has not been transferred to the poor person; rather the land is the property of the trust, whereas for the discharge of zakat it is necessary to make the poor person the owner.

(2) In the second method the poor person has been made the owner of a share of the land and has been given full authority, and therefore the zakat has been discharged.

See what ʿAllāmah al-Ḥaṣkafī (may Allah have mercy on him) says in al-Durr al-Mukhtār, that for the discharge of zakat transfer of ownership is a condition and merely allowing use is not sufficient:

ويشترط أن يكون الصرف تمليكاً لا إباحة. (الدر المختار: ۲/۳٤٤، سعيد).

قال في البحر: هي تمليك المال من فقير مسلم

بشرط قطع المنفعة عن المملك من كل وجه لله تعالى، لقوله تعالى: ﴿وآتوا الزكاة﴾ [البقرة: ٤۳]، والإيتاء هو التمليك. (البحر الرائق: ۲/۲۱٦، دار المعرفة).

وفي التبيين: قال: وآتوا الزكاة، يقتضي التمليك ولا تتأدى بالإباحة حتى لو كفل يتيماً فأنفق عليه ناوياً للزكاة لا يجزيه. (تبيين الحقائق: ۱/۲۵۱، ملتان).
