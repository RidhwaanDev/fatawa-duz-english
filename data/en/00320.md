### TITLE
Accepting a donation from a non-Muslim for a mosque

### QUESTION
Is it permissible for a Muslim to accept a donation from a non-Muslim for a mosque, a madrasah, or religious gatherings?

### ANSWER
Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

If the building of a mosque or madrasah has been damaged, there is room to accept help. (Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 2/157).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

If in their view building a mosque is an act of worship that earns reward, and there is no other objection, then it is correct in sharia to put their money into the construction of a mosque. What is meant by "building up" in the verse is keeping the mosque in use, its trusteeship and its administration. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 1/487).

ʿUmdat al-Qārī states:

ومن فوائد هذا الحديث جواز الاستعانة بأهل الصنعة فيما يشمل المسلمين نفعة (عمدة القاري: ۳/٤۷۸).

The upshot of these passages is that if a non-Muslim gives a donation for a mosque, a madrasah or some other religious cause, intending by it to gain reward and regarding it as an act of worship, then it is correct in sharia to accept it and to use it — provided there is no fear of discord and he has not given it as a favour that places others under obligation.
