### TITLE
Girls' names and their meanings

### QUESTION
Girls' names and their meanings.

### ANSWER
Letter alif:

Āsiyah: a woman as firm as a pillar; firm and well-grounded in faith. (Tāj al-ʿArūs: 37/79; Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/125; and Miṣbāḥ al-Lughāt, p. 35).

Āṣifah: a plant whose buds are eaten with vinegar or with salt. الاصف: نبت من الفصيلة الكبرية له شوك وورق أخضر وثمره لبي تؤكل براعمه مخللة أو مملحة. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 20; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 23/20).

Arwā: beautiful. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 384; and Miṣbāḥ al-Lughāt, p. 326).

Asmāʾ: one of lofty rank. سما، يسمو، سمواً، علا وارتفع وتطاول. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 452; and Miṣbāḥ al-Lughāt, p. 398).

Umāmah, Umaymah: a leader, a guide; a dear girl. (Tāj al-ʿArūs: 31/230; and Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/135).

Umayyah: a dear or a little girl. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 28).

Arībah: an intelligent, far-sighted girl. (Tāj al-ʿArūs: 2/15).

Ilānah: soft. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1516).

Ilyānah: soft. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1516).

Alīnah Bēgum: this is in origin al-Līnah, which is a fine date; and in Bēgum, bēg means a chief, while the m is the mark of the feminine in the Turkestani language. So Bēgum means an excellent and noble lady. (Ghiyāth al-Lughāt, p. 93).

Letter bāʾ:

Barīrah: the fruit of the pīlū (arāk) tree. ثمر الاراك عامة. (Tāj al-ʿArūs: 10/188).

Buthaynah: a beautiful, soft and delicate girl. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 38; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 34/230).

Barzah: a girl whose good qualities are manifest. امرأة برزة بارزة المحاسن ظاهرتها. (Tāj al-ʿArūs: 15/20).

Barīʿah: beautiful; one who excels others. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 50; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 20/319).

Busrah: like the rising sun. (Tāj al-ʿArūs: 10/174).

Bahiyyah: beautiful, with lovely eyes. (Tāj al-ʿArūs: 37/241; and Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 74).

Bāriʿah: a beautiful, comely and excellent girl. جارية بارعة أي جميلة برع تم في كل شيء فضيلة وجمال. (Tāj al-ʿArūs: 20/317).

Bāṣirah: one who watches over and rears well. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 59).

Bāriqah: one with a radiant face. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/161).

Bilqīs: a woman skilled in reckoning and estimation.

Bakrah: one whose face is bright like the morning. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 67; and Al-Munjid, p. 97).

Balīlah: one who is generous. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 70).

Barāʿat: unrivalled in beauty and comeliness. (Al-Munjid, p. 83; Lisān al-Lisān, p. 78; and Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 50).

Bībī Khānam: bībī means a lady of noble birth, and khānam means a lady of gentle birth. (Lughāt-i Kishwarī, p. 78).

Bānū: the wife of a chief. (Lughāt-i Kishwarī, p. 59).

Bēgum: the wife of a chief. Bēg means a chief, and the m is the mark of the feminine in the Turkestani language. (Lughāt-i Kishwarī, p. 78; and Ghiyāth al-Lughāt, p. 93).

Letter Persian bāʾ:

Parī: a beautiful female jinn. (Lughāt-i Kishwarī, p. 84).

Parwīn: a cluster of several stars. (Lughāt-i Kishwarī, p. 84).

Pashmīnah: soft like wool. (Lughāt-i Saʿīdī, p. 132).

Padam: an aquatic plant which is called nīlūfar (the water lily).

Letter tāʾ:

Taḥiyyah: greeting, supplication and blessing.

Letter thāʾ:

Thuwaylah: one who has a company about her. (Al-Munjid, p. 131; Lisān al-Lisān, p. 157; and Miṣbāḥ al-Lughah, p. 98).

Thubaytah: brave, and firm-footed in the religion. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 93; and Miṣbāḥ al-Lughāt, p. 90).

Thurayyā: a cluster of stars. (Lisān al-Lisān, p. 146; Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 95; and Al-Munjid, p. 123).

Thuwaybah: one who has a small company about her. الجماعۃ من الناس. (Lisān al-Lisān, p. 156; Al-Munjid, p. 130; and Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 102).

Letter jīm:

Juwayriyah: a dear girl; the sun; a boat; a favour of Allah. (Lisān al-ʿArab: 1/140; and Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/254).

Jasrah: brave; one who goes ahead in every task. (Al-Munjid, p. 152; Lisān al-Lisān, p. 186; and Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 122).

Jumāmah: one who brings rest and ease. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 13; and Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 283).

Jumānah: a pearl. الجمان اللؤلؤ. (Al-Munjid, p. 168; Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 137; and Lisān al-Lisān, p. 206).

Jafnah, Jufaynah: one who is generous. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/267).

Letter ḥāʾ:

Ḥawwāʾ: blackness tinged with red. الحمرة تضرب إلى السواد. (Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 5/1854; and Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/397).

Ḥafṣah: a lion cub. (Tāj al-ʿArūs: 17/527).

Ḥārithah: one who prepares for the Hereafter. (Tāj al-ʿArūs: 5/214; and Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 5/216).

Ḥishmat: modest and dignified. (Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 4/1543; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 31/490).

Ḥannah: compassionate. (Miṣbāḥ al-Lughāt, p. 178; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 34/460).

Ḥawrāʾ: a fair-complexioned, beautiful girl. الحوراء من النساء البيضاء. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 206; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 11/100).

Ḥirā: people name a girl Ḥirā with the intention of seeking blessing thereby, for just as Ḥirā is the blessed place of the first revelation, so this girl too is blessed. It is possible that Ḥirā carries the sense of "fitting and suitable," for the distance to that cave was suitable — it was neither too far from Makkah nor too near — and for this reason it was named Ḥirā.

Letter khāʾ:

Khadījah: one cut off from faults and withdrawn from men.

Khawlah: a gazelle; beautiful. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 263; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 28/447).

Khansāʾ: the nilgai. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/479; Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 2/783; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 16/36).

Khaḍībah: verdant and flourishing. (Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 1/109; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 2/369).

Kharīdah: bashful. (Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 2/409; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 8/55).

Khamīlah: a shawl, a woollen wrap. (Miṣbāḥ al-Lughāt, p. 220; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 28/437).

Letter dāl:

Durrah: a pearl. اللؤلؤة العظيمة. (Tāj al-ʿArūs: 11/282).

Diʿāmah: a pillar. (Tāj al-ʿArūs: 32/156).

Dībājah: beautiful and comely, and soft and delicate as silk. (Miṣbāḥ al-Lughāt, p. 228; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 5/546).

Letter dhāl:

Dhunābah: one who follows obediently. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 316).

Dhakiyyah: alert and intelligent. (Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 1/227; and Lisān al-ʿArab: 14/287).

Letter rāʾ:

Ruqayyah: one who rises and advances. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 369).

Ruʿlah: if the rāʾ carries a ḍammah, it means a bouquet of flowers that is placed upon the head in the form of a crown; and if the rāʾ carries a fatḥah, it means a troop and herd of swift horses. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 355).

Rayṭah: a wrap. (Lisān al-ʿArab: 7/307; and Maqāyīs al-Lughah: 2/387).

Rawḍah: a beautiful garden. (Lisān al-ʿArab: 7/162; and Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 794).

Rumayṣāʾ: a star. (Tāj al-ʿArūs: 1/4459; and Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 774).

Rabābah: a chief. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 661; and Al-Munjid, p. 234).

Rabbāniyyah: one devoted to Allah; a nurturer. (Rūḥ al-Maʿānī: 3/208). Some people corrupt it and say Rēbān.

Rabīʿah: spring. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 325).

Rahīfah: soft and delicate. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 378).

Rāʾiʿah: one who fills people with wonder by her beauty or her courage. (Al-Munjid, p. 419; and Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ).

Rayyah: one who quenches thirst. (Al-Munjid, p. 423; and Lisān al-ʿArab: 13/494).

Rīshah: a pen. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/691).

Raghībah: a much-desired girl. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/643; and Lisān al-Lisān, 487).

Rashīqah: a cheerful girl. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/628; and Lisān al-Lisān, p. 488).

Raṣāfah: firm and strong in the religion. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 631; and Lisān al-ʿArab, p. 121).

Rakhīmah: soft and delicate. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/610).

Ramlah: clean and pure like sandstone. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1).

Ramthah: one who sets matters right. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/667; and Lisān al-Lisān, p. 511).

Rūbīnah: one whose face is worth beholding. Rūb means face, and bīn means to see. (Lughāt-i Kishwarī, p. 217).

Rakhshandah: shining and radiant. (Lughāt-i Kishwarī, p. 210).

Rukhsānah: in all likelihood this word is Rakhshānah, which the common people corrupted into Rukhsānah, and its meaning is a girl with a splendid face. Still, if we accept Rukhsānah with a sīn, then its meaning will be this: rukh means face, and sān means the file with which the rust of iron is cleaned; so the sense will be that smiling, cheerful girl at the sight of whom the dross of the heart is cleansed.

Letter zāy:

Zaynab: a fragrant plant. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/719; and Lisān al-Lisān, p. 555).

Zubaydah: pure in religion; in origin zubdah means butter, and butter is the essence of milk and curd. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/569; and Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 387).

Zahrāʾ: one with a bright white face, beautiful and comely. الأزهر: الايبض المستنير، والمرأة الزهراء. (Lisān al-Lisān, p. 558; and Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/722).

Zuhrah: one whose face shines like a star. الزهرة: الكوكب الأبيض والزهرة الحسن البياض. (Lisān al-Lisān, p. 558; and Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/722).

Zahrah: a flower; and a beautiful girl. (Lisān al-Lisān, p. 558).

Zāhirah: white tinged with red, beautiful. والزاهر: الحسن الابيض من الرجال، وقيل: هوالأبيض فيه حمرة. (Lisān al-Lisān, p. 558). Also a plant bearing flowers and buds. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/722).

Zaʿīmah: a chief. (Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 4/1576; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 32/314).

Zakiyyah: pure. (Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 5/1888; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 38/224).

Zulaykhā: a beautiful and comely woman. (Fīrōz al-Lughāt, p. 747).

Zuhayrah: a bud and a flower. (Tāj al-ʿArūs: 11/473; and Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 2/580).

Letter sīn:

Sārah: a keeper of secrets. (Miṣbāḥ al-Lughāt, p. 370; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 12/19).

Saʿdī (Suʿdā): a fragrant plant; a fortunate girl. It is the feminine of the elative asʿad. (Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 2/425; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 8/198).

Sawdah: land of date palms; blessed; wealthy. (Miṣbāḥ al-Lughāt, p. 405; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 8/231).

Sanāʾ: one of lofty rank. السني الرفيع، السناء العلو وارتفاع. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 457).

Sumayyah: one of lofty rank. السمو العلو والرفعة. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 453).

Safānah: a pearl. السفانة: اللؤلؤ. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 434; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 35/195).

Suhaymah: one who obtains a portion and a share, fortunate; one who keeps regard for ties of kinship. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/816; Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 459; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 32/440).

Sunaynah: one who walks upon the Sunnah. Sunaynah is the diminutive of sunnah, and the Sunnah is the highroad of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ upon which the Muslims travel; so Sunaynah will mean the girl who walks upon the Sunnah, who is called Sunaynah by way of emphasis, as in "Zayd is justice."

Salmā: one possessed of safety and well-being. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 446).

Sanā: one whose face is bright like the moon. السنا مقصود ضوء البرق. (Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 5/1901).

Sīmā: a mark and a sign; the sign of the light of faith upon the face. In the Noble Qurʾān it is said: سيماهم في وجوههم من اثر السجود. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/828; and Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, 466).

Sunbulah, Sunbul: a fragrant plant. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/807).

Sabrah: a cool morning. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/739; and Lisān al-Lisān, p. 571).

Sāriyah: the rain and cloud of the night. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/766; and Lisān al-Lisān, p. 597).

Sidrah: the lote tree. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/757; and Lisān al-Lisān, p. 587).

Sōniyah: this is in origin Sōhniyah, which means a beautiful girl; and Sōnī too is in origin Sōhnī, which means beautiful.

Shīr Bānū: the daughter of Yazdajird came into the possession of Ḥaḍrat Ḥusayn (may Allah be pleased with him); he set her free and married her, and from her Zayn al-ʿĀbidīn was born.

Shagufta: the girl whose face is smiling and open.  (Lughāt-i Saʿīdī, p. 458).

Gulbadīn: it is in origin Gul-Badan, which has been corrupted.

Letter lām:

Lubābah: intelligent, alert, chosen. لب كل شيء ولبابه: خالصه واللباب: الخالص من كل شيء، لبابة — to be intelligent. (Lisān al-Lisān: 2/490; and Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1446).

Lubnā: one who gives milk in plenty, one who brings benefit. (Lisān al-Lisān: 2/493; and Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1450).

Liyānah: soft. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1517).

Līnah: a date palm; cheerful; gentle of nature. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1517).

Lamīs: soft and delicate. (Lisān al-Lisān: 2/517).

Luʾluʾah: a pearl. (Lisān al-ʿArab: 1/150; and Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 810).

Labāqah: one possessed of skill. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 813; Lisān al-Lisān: 2/493; and Lisān al-Lisān: 10/327).

Letter mīm:

Muʿādhah: one granted refuge. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1140; Lisān al-Lisān: 2/239; and Lisān al-ʿArab: 3/498).

Maymūnah: blessed. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1916; Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 5/1778; and Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 1067).

Maryam: devoted to worship and to service. (Rūḥ al-Maʿānī: 3/136; and Tāj al-ʿArūs: 32/302).

Māriyah: beautiful; the nilgai. (Lisān al-ʿArab: 15/279).

Miḥjanah: a staff, the support of the weak. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 1/1315).

Musaykah: one who gives support. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1553).

Mulaykah: a dear queen. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1581; and Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 886).

Maymanah: fortunate. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1916; and Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 1066).

Malsāʾ: shining and soft. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1578).

Munīfah: beautiful and of fitting stature. (Lisān al-ʿArab: 9/342; and Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1727).

Muznah: a rain-bearing cloud. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1548; and Lisān al-ʿArab: 13/406).

Māziyah: one of excellence. (Lisān al-ʿArab: 15/279; and Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1548).

Malālah: this is a Pashto word in the sense of "grief-laden," meaning one who carries grief and concern for the religion and the nation.

Malālay: this too is a Pashto word; its simplest rendering is a girl with languid, dreamy eyes.

Marjānah: a precious stone; and the final hāʾ is for indicating the feminine. (Lughāt-i Saʿīdī, p. 717).

Māyirah: one who provides food and provisions. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1595).

Al-Mīrah: one possessed of provisions. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1595).

Letter nūn:

Nabīlah: noble. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 899).

Nasībah: one of lofty lineage. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1639; and Lisān al-ʿArab: 1/756).

Nusaybah: a dear, comely girl. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1639; and Lisān al-ʿArab: 1/756).

Nasīm, Nasīmah: as pleasing as a gentle breeze. (Lisān al-Lisān: 2/614; Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1644; and Lisān al-ʿArab: 12/574).

Nāʾilah: one of bounty. (Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 967; and Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1732).

Nāʿimah: soft and delicate. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1674).

Nāsikah: devoted to worship. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1643).

Najībah: of noble lineage. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1610; and Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 601).

Nasrīn: a kind of flower. (Ghiyāth al-Lughāt, p. 524).

Nīlūfar: an aquatic plant; it is the Arabicised form of nīlūpal. (Ghiyāth al-Lughāt, p. 536; and Lughāt-i Kishwarī, p. 551).

Nadīmah: a companion and friend. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1628; and Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ, p. 911).

Letter wāw:

Wajīhah: honoured. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1817; Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 5/1803; and Lisān al-ʿArab: 13/558).

Waṣīfah: a maidservant. She is called waṣīfah because she describes in praise every quality of her master before him. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1858; Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 3/1189; and Lisān al-ʿArab: 9/357).

Letter hāʾ:

Hājirah: one who forsakes evils and aimless wandering; beautiful. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1741; Lisān al-ʿArab: 5/252; and Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 2/1763).

Huzaylah: slender and beautiful. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1763; Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 4/1504; and Lisān al-ʿArab: 11/697).

Hānī: if it is a boy's name, then one who serves; and if it is a girl's name, then one of bounty and gift. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1783; Lisān al-ʿArab: 1/186; and Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 1/71).

Hindah: one of forbearance and patience. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/784; and Lisān al-ʿArab: 3/439).

Hālah: the halo of the moon. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1789; Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 2/158; and Lisān al-ʿArab: 11/713).

Humā: fortunate. It is the name of a bird concerning which it is well known that if someone comes beneath its shadow, he becomes greatly fortunate and a king. (Lughāt-i Saʿīdī, p. 880).

Letter yāʾ:

Yasīrah: gentle of temperament, one of ease, one of wealth. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1914; Al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 2/730; and Lisān al-ʿArab: 5/296).

Yāfiʿah: of high rank. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1914; and Lisān al-ʿArab: 8/415).

Yāsmīn: fragrant like the jasmine. (Al-Qāmūs al-Waḥīd: 2/1914; and Lisān al-ʿArab: 12/646).
