### TITLE
Saying "You have my permission to marry elsewhere"

### QUESTION
A man said to his wife, "If you want to marry somewhere else, you have my permission." Does a divorce take effect with these words or not? And if it does, which kind? Note that there was no discussion of divorce going on.

### ANSWER
In the case described, if by these words — "If you want to marry somewhere else, you have my permission" — he intended divorce, one irrevocable divorce has taken effect; but if he did not intend divorce, none has taken effect.

Consider what Al-Fatāwā al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah states:

ولو قال: تزوجي ونوى الطلاق أو الثلاث صح، وإن لم ينو شيئاً لم يقع، كذا في العتابية. (الفتاوى الهندية ۱/۳۷٦، باب الكنايات).

It is stated in another place:

وبابتغي الأزواج تقع واحدة بائنة إن نواها، الخ. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۳۷۵، باب الكنايات).

It is also recorded:

اذهبي فتزوجي تقع واحدة إذا نوى. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۳۷٦، باب الكنايات).

This question is dealt with in the new edition of Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband as well, at (۹/۳۸۵، ۹/۳۹۸، ۹/٤۰۳، ۹/٤۳٤، ۹/٤٦۰، ۹/٤٦۹، ۹/٤۸۱، ۹/٤۸۳).

In al-Durr al-Mukhtār, for the words

"اذهبي فتزوجي"

and likewise in al-Fatāwā al-Anqarawiyyah for the same words, it is written that a divorce takes effect without any intention. ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) has rejected this.

Consider what Fatāwā al-Shāmī states:

ويخالفه ما في شرح الجامع الصغير لقاضي خان: ولو قال: اذهبي فتزوجي وقال: لم أنو الطلاق لا يقع شيء، لأن معناه إن أمكنك

ويؤيده ما في الذخيرة: اذهبي فتزوجي لا يقع إلا بالنية، وإن نوى فهي واحدة بائنة، وإن نوى الثلاث فثلاث. (فتاوى الشامي: ۳/۳۱٤، باب الكنايات، سعيد).

Some of our senior scholars have written that with words of this kind a divorce takes effect without any intention when a discussion of divorce is going on. It should be borne in mind that in the case described no such discussion of divorce is present. In the jurists' terminology, a discussion of divorce means that the wife, or someone else on her behalf, demands a divorce, or that a divorce has already been given; and none of that is present in this case, as the question makes plain.

In sum, the husband should be asked whether he intended divorce or not. If he says that he had no such intention and the wife is not satisfied, she may ask him to swear an oath — کذا فی کتب الفقہ ۔ If there was no intention, the husband and wife may live together without renewing the marriage. But if the husband did intend divorce, one irrevocable divorce has taken effect.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
