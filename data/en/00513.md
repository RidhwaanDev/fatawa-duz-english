### TITLE
Tablīgh workers holding congregational prayer at the gathering ground

### QUESTION
What is the sharia ruling on the following matter?

Work on preparing for the gathering begins about six or seven weeks in advance, and many people come from different areas. We normally pray at the gathering ground itself, whether or not there is a mosque nearby. There are several reasons for this:

(1) It is far more convenient and saves a great deal of time.

(2) After every prayer there are discussions and talks of encouragement and instruction, and consultation is held as needed. After Fajr there is the collective recitation of Sūrah Yāsīn, supplication, remembrance of Allah and other regular practices. After most of the prayers, necessary appeals are put before the gathering.

(3) In the evening people come in large numbers — sometimes between 400 and 700 — so arrangements have to be made for prayer, food, drink and accommodation in every respect.

(4) The belongings also need to be guarded. For this reason we perform the Friday prayer at the gathering ground as well, so that a group can be assigned to guard duty at all times.

Recently we were told that performing the Friday prayer at the gathering ground is not in keeping with the sunnah when a mosque is nearby and has room, and that apart from those on guard duty everyone must go to the mosque for the five daily prayers. Is this correct? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case described, the better and more meritorious course is for the congregational prayer to be performed in the mosque. But if, because of some valid excuse, people hold their own congregation, they will still obtain the merit of congregational prayer.

Since the workers of Tablīgh remain occupied with tasks at the gathering ground, and people come there in such numbers that it becomes difficult for everyone to go to the mosque — and some of them have to stay behind to guard the belongings — they may hold their own congregation.

ʿAllāmah Ibn ʿĀbidīn al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) writes:

واختلف العلماء في إقامتها في البيت والأصح أنها كإقامتها في المسجد إلا في الأفضلية. (شامي: ۱/٥٥٤، سعيد)

That is, the scholars differed about holding the congregation at home. The soundest view is that it is like holding it in the mosque, except that the mosque is better and more meritorious.

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

والجماعة سنة مؤكدة للرجال

وأقلها اثنان مع الإمام ولو مميزاً أو ملكاً أو جنياً في المسجد أو غيره.

Al-Ṭaḥṭāwī's gloss on al-Durr states:

فلو صلى في بيته بزوجته أو جاريته أو ولده فقد أتي بفضيلة الجماعة. (الطحطاوي على الدر المختار: ۱/۲٤۰)

That is, the smallest congregation is two people counting the imam, even if the other is a discerning child, an angel or a jinn, and whether it is in the mosque or outside it. So if a man prays in congregation with his wife, his children or his slave-woman, he has obtained the merit of congregational prayer.

ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī writes in Minḥat al-Khāliq:

اختلف العلماء في إقامتها في البيت والأصح أنها كإقامتها في المسجد إلا في الأفضلية وهو ظاهر مذهب الشافعي رحمه الله قلت: ويظهر أن ما سيأتي عن الحلواني مبني على ما مر منه من وجوب الإجابة بالقدم وتقدم أن الظاهر خلافه فلذا صححوا خلاف ما قاله هنا. (منحة الخالق حاشية البحر الرائق: ۱/۳٤٥، كوئتة)

That is, the scholars differed about holding the congregation at home. The correct view is that it is like holding it in the mosque, except in merit, and this is also the position of Imam al-Shāfiʿī (may Allah have mercy on him). Shams al-Aʾimmah al-Ḥulwānī (may Allah have mercy on him) called congregational prayer outside the mosque an innovation, but this rests on his view that walking to the mosque is obligatory, and that is contrary to the apparent position.

In any case, the workers of Tablīgh should try to ensure that some of their companions get to the mosque; still, holding congregational prayer and the Friday prayer at the gathering ground is permissible. It is also clear from the question that the mosque is a kilometre away. The jurists write that if water is a mile away, going to fetch it is waived and a person may perform dry ablution instead. So in such a case going to the mosque is not strictly necessary either.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
