### TITLE
Shaking hands and embracing after a marriage

### QUESTION
After the marriage is concluded, people shake hands and embrace one another. What is the ruling on this in the sharīʿah? And what is the ruling if people do not regard it as a sunnah but only as a means of expressing joy and affection, and they do not censure those who refrain from it?

### ANSWER
It is known from many narrations that shaking hands and embracing in order to express joy and affection is permissible and correct, so it will be permitted on the occasion of a marriage as well. But to regard it as an act of worship and a sunnah is an innovation, as our elders (may Allah have mercy on them) have stated. One sign of regarding something as an act of worship is that those who do not do it are censured and blamed.

For details of the narrations, see: Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā, vol. 2, pp. 590–593, "The chapter on the two ʿĪd prayers".
