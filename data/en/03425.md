### TITLE
The ruling on waiting for the imām after the appointed time of the congregation has passed

### QUESTION
If the appointed time of the congregation has passed and the imām is not present, how long should one wait?

### ANSWER
Some allowance should be made for the imām in beginning the iqāmah. Ḥaḍrat Bilāl (may Allah be pleased with him) used to begin the iqāmah when the Prophet (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) came out. Yes, if the imām is somewhat delayed, the prayer may be begun: on one occasion Ḥaḍrat Bilāl (may Allah be pleased with him) put Ḥaḍrat Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq (may Allah be pleased with him) forward to lead, and on another occasion he put Ḥaḍrat ʿAbd al-Raḥmān ibn ʿAwf (may Allah be pleased with him) forward. However, what is appropriate is that one or two minutes be waited.

عن جابر بن سمرة قال: كان بلال يؤذن ثم يمهل فإذا رأى النبي ﷺ قد خرج أقام الصلاة. (سنن أبي داود، رقم: ۵۳۷، باب في المؤذن ينتظر الإمام).

وعن سهل بن سعد الساعدي رضي الله عنه، أن رسول الله ﷺ ذهب إلى بني عمرو بن عوف ليصلح بينهم فحانت الصلاة فجاء المؤذن إلى أبي بكر فقال: أتصلي للناس فأقيم قال نعم، فصلى أبو بكر (صحيح البخاري: ۱/۹٤، رقم: ٦۷٤، ط: فيصل).

In Kitāb al-Fatāwā it is mentioned:

Question: In the audible prayers, how long should the deputy imām wait for the imām?

Answer: The Messenger of Allah (may Allah bless him and grant him peace) gave the guidance that allowance should be made for the imām in the iqāmah, that is, that the iqāmah be called when the imām arrives. But if the imām does not come at the customary time, the prayer may be begun. Thus, in the very time of the Messenger of Allah (may Allah bless him and grant him peace), on one occasion he was delayed in coming, so Ḥaḍrat Bilāl (may Allah Most High be pleased with him) put Ḥaḍrat Abū Bakr (may Allah Most High be pleased with him) forward, and on another occasion Ḥaḍrat ʿAbd al-Raḥmān ibn ʿAwf (may Allah Most High be pleased with him). In the present age the prayer times in the mosques are fixed; if the imām does not arrive at the appointed time, it is appropriate that one, two or three minutes be waited. If the delay is longer, the deputy imām should lead the prayer, so that it does not become a cause of hardship for the people. (Kitāb al-Fatāwā: 2/301).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
