### TITLE
Zakat on tax money refunded by the government

### QUESTION
There is a charitable institution which collects zakat from people and distributes it among the poor. It generally buys the goods the poor need, and food and drink, and distributes them. The government levies tax on these goods, but since this institution is non-profit the government refunds the tax money to it. The question is this: when the tax on goods purchased with zakat money is refunded, will that money be counted as zakat funds, or will it have the ruling of a voluntary charity given for the sake of Allah? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
The tax that the government took became the property of the government by virtue of the government's power over it. Then, when the government refunded it to the institution, it became a gift from the government to the institution, which will be counted as a voluntary charity. Moreover, taxes nowadays are mostly excessive, and for this reason one may make efforts to have them refunded.

See what ʿAllāmah al-Sarakhsī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

فأما في زماننا إنما يوجد أكثر النوائب بطريق الظلم ومن تمكن من دفع الظلم عن نفسه فذلك خير له. (المبسوط للإمام السرخسي رحمه الله: ۱۰/۳۵، دار الفكر، بيروت).

The government's taking possession is a cause of ownership: when the government has taken possession of some money by way of tax, the government becomes the owner of that money. ʿAllāmah Ẓafar Aḥmad ʿUthmānī (may Allah have mercy on him) writes concerning a Muslim receiving an inheritance from an unbeliever:

يجوز للمسلم أن يرث من الكافر بسبب استيلاء الحكومة الكافرة على مال الكافر أولاً ثم دفعها إلى المسلم بقانونها واستيلاء الكافر سبب للملك عندنا، والله أعلم، أو يقال: استولى المسلم على مال الكافرة بقوة سلطان أهل الحرب وصار تملكاً له بالاستيلاء ودليل التملك بقوة سلطان أهل الحرب ما ذكر في شرح السير.

(امداد الاحکام: ٤/٦۲۸) ۔

وفي الفتاوى الهندية: الباب الخامس في استيلاء الكفار: إذا غلب كفار الترك على كفار الروم فسبوهم وأخذوا أموالهم ملكوها فإن غلبنا على الترك حل لنا ما نجده مما أخذوه

الخ. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۲/۲۲٤). (وكذا في الدر المختار: ٤/۱٦۰، سعيد، والبحر الرائق: ۵/۱۰۲، دار المعرفة، وتبيين الحقائق: ۳/۲٦۰، إمداديه ملتان، وفتح القدير: ٦/۳، ط: دار الفكر).

In Qāmūs al-Fiqh, Mawlānā Khālid Sayf Allāh writes:

Istīlāʾ means to gain the upper hand. In the terminology of jurisprudence it denotes the unbelievers of the Abode of War gaining the upper hand over the Muslims, or over the unbelievers of some other state. In the view of Imām Abū Ḥanīfah (may Allah have mercy on him), if the people of unbelief gain control over someone's property they are held to be its owners. In the same way, if one non-Muslim state seizes the property of another state by force, it will become the owner of it, and it will be correct for Muslims to buy such goods from them; and after buying them they will be held to be their lawful owners. (قاموس الفقہ: ۲/۱۳۱، ۱۳۲) ۔

Ashraf al-Aḥkām, which is the supplement to Imdād al-Fatāwā, records:

He said: In the view of Ḥaḍrat Imām Abū Ḥanīfah (may Allah have mercy on him), an unbeliever's taking possession establishes ownership — quoted from Adab al-Aʿlām, p. 220. (اشرف الاحکام، ص ۲۳۸، میراث کے احکام، ط: ادارہ اسلامیات، لاہور) ۔

The upshot is that once the government became the owner of the tax money and is now giving that money to the institution, this will be regarded as a donation from the government, which has the ruling of a voluntary charity.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
