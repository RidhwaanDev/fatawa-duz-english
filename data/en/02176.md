### TITLE
Two transactions in one in a contract of hire

### QUESTION
Zayd gave cloth to a dyer, telling him to dye it for 50 rupees, and he dyed it; or he gave cloth to a tailor, telling him to sew it for 200 rupees, and he sewed it. On the face of it this transaction is permissible, and it is what people in fact do. But there is a juristic objection to it: since the dye and the thread come from the hired worker, this becomes a case of two transactions in one, because a sale and a hire have been combined — the sale of a substance together with the sale of a benefit is two transactions in one. There also appears to be a further defect in it, namely that a contract of hire is concerned with the consuming of a benefit, whereas here an actual substance is consumed. بینوا توجروا

### ANSWER
The jurists have declared this transaction permissible because of established practice.

Al-Hidāyah states:

وفي الاستحسان يجوز للتعامل فيه فصار كصبغ الثوب وللتعامل جوزنا الاستصناع. (الهداية: ۳/٦۱، باب بيع الفاسد).

Al-ʿInāyah, the commentary on al-Hidāyah, states:

فإن القياس لا يجوز استيجار الصباغ لصبغ الثوب لأن الإجارة عقد على المنافع لا الأعيان وفيه عقد على العين وهو الصبغ لا الصبغ وحده لكن جوز للتعامل جواز الاستصناع. (العناية مع فتح القدير: ٦/۸۵، مكتبه رشيديه).

It is indeed a case of two transactions in one, since along with the hire there is a sale of the dye, and in tailoring a sale of the thread and the materials that go with it; but the hire is the principal matter and the sale is subordinate to it.

See what Hazrat Thānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

Question: Judged by the apparent meaning of "نهي عن صفقة في صفقة", certain dealings appear to be impermissible, although they are extremely common among people of every sort. For example, the repairing of a watch, in which a broken part is taken out and a sound one fitted: the part is a sale and the fitting is a hire. (2) Having a bed made without supplying the cord oneself: here the cord is a sale and the weaving is a hire. (3) Taking water from a water-carrier: once he has drawn the water from the well into his own vessels it becomes his property, so there is a sale of the water and a hire for bringing it, and it is also the sale of something one does not have. (4) Giving jewellery or a ring to be set: the stones are a sale and the setting is a hire — وغیرہ ذلک من المعاملات الرائجۃ.

Answer: On account of established practice — for these dealings are widespread and no one objects to them, which is a kind of consensus — all these transactions are permissible. The general text is thus restricted in part, just as the jurists have allowed this in dyeing and tailoring, where the dye and the thread belong to the craftsman and yet there is a hire as well. و ہذا ظاہر جداً فقط، واللہ اعلم ۔ (امداد الفتاوی: ۳/٦۳، ٦٤).

A detailed discussion of two transactions in one has already been given in the Book of Sales, and may be consulted there.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
