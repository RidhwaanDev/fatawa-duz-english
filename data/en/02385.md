### TITLE
A list of unlawful animals

### QUESTION
"A list of unlawful animals"

### ANSWER
(Land and sea quadrupeds and birds which it is impermissible and unlawful to eat)

For the table of lawful and unlawful animals, use has been made of the following books and treatises:

(1) The treatise of the Mufti of Damascus, Shaykh Maḥmūd Ḥamzāwī. (6) Manḥ al-Jalīl Sharḥ Mukhtaṣar al-Khalīl, vol. 2.

(2) Iʿlāʾ al-Sunan, vol. 17. (7) Al-Mughnī of Ibn Qudāmah al-Ḥanbalī, vol. 11.

(3) Al-Mawsūʿah al-Fiqhiyyah al-Kuwaytiyyah, vol. 5. (8) Al-Fiqh al-Manhajī, vol. 1.

(4) Fatāwā al-Shāmī, vol. 6. (9) Al-Fiqh al-Muyassar.

(5) Al-Nutaf fī al-Fatāwā. (10) Sharḥ al-Muhadhdhab, vol. 9. And some modern treatises.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
