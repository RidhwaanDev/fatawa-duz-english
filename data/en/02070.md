### TITLE
The pension fund

### QUESTION
Government employees receive a monthly stipend as a pension, reckoned at half their salary, after their employment comes to an end. Is it permissible in the light of the sharia to take it or not?

### ANSWER
A pension is merely a gift from the government and a form of assistance, so taking it is permissible and valid.

Consider what Ḥaḍrat Muftī Kifāyat Allāh (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

The pension that an employee receives on retirement from service is permissible. (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 8/97).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

In some departments a pension is given at the end of employment as a reward for good service; this too is not called interest. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 16/394, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

Āp ke Masāʾil states:

A pension has, in one respect, the status of a gift; therefore whatever arrangement is settled between the pensioner and the government is valid, and it is not gambling or a game of chance. (Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥall: 8/294).
