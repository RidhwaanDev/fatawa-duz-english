### TITLE
Making students perform prayers as a punishment

### QUESTION
In some madrasahs students are made to pray 30 or 50 units of optional prayer as a punishment. May prayer be used as a punishment? And will the student receive the reward of that prayer?

### ANSWER
It is correct to have students perform prayers as a punishment in madrasahs, and there is no harm in it. The student will also receive the reward of the prayer, just as the Prophet ﷺ instructed fasting as a means of curbing desire: there is both the reward of the fast and the curbing of desire. Consider what the hadith states:

عن عبد الله بن مسعود رضي الله عنه قال: قال رسول الله ﷺ يا معشر الشباب من استطاع منكم الباءة فليتزوج فإنه أغض للبصر وأحصن للفرج ومن لم يستطع ف عليه بالصوم فإنه له وجاء. (بخاري شريف: ۲/۷٥۸. ومشكاة: ۲/۲٦۷)

Clearly this fast is not kept directly to seek Allah's pleasure but to curb desire so as to avoid unlawful sexual relations. In the same way, having a student pray here is meant to discipline the self and to enforce the rules of the madrasah.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
