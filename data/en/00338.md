### TITLE
Demolishing and rebuilding a mosque that is in a dangerous condition

### QUESTION
I am the trustee of a mosque. For the past year and a half we have been discussing extending the mosque, but now, for several unavoidable reasons, the architect has declared that it must be rebuilt from scratch. The reasons are these:

(1) The basement of the old mosque is unsafe because of water leaking into it, and no guarantee can be given for repairs.

(2) Laying new foundations while keeping the present building will damage it.

(3) If we build on the existing structure we shall exceed the permitted height limit; and the old building is in a poor state in any case.

(4) Rebuilding from scratch will cost less in labour and the like, the work will move faster, and it can carry on even during the times of prayer.

The committee's first worry and concern is this: for these reasons, is it correct and permissible to close the mosque?

### ANSWER
Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

"مسجد مبني أراد رجل أن ينقضه ويبنيه ثانياً أحكم من البناء الأول ليس له ذلك، لأنه لا ولاية له كذا في المضمرات، وفي النوازل: إلا أن يخاف أن ينهدم إن لم يهدم كذا في التتارخانية وتأويله: إذا لم يكن الباني من أهل تلك المحلّة، وأما أهل تلك المحلة فلهم أن يهدموا ويجدّدوا بنائه ويفرشوا الحصير ويعلقوا القناديل، لكن من مال أنفسهم أمّا من مال المسجد فليس لهم ذلك إلا بأمر القاضي كذا في الخلاصة.". فتاوى هندية: ۲/٤۵۷)

This passage shows that if there is a fear that the old building may collapse, or something similar, then there is no harm in pulling it down and putting up a sound mosque. Al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq states:

"وفي البزازية: أرادوا نقض المسجد وبنائه أحكم من الأول أن لم يكن الباني من أهل المحلة ليس لهم ذلك، وإن كان من أهل المحلة لهم ذلك." (البحر الرائق: ۵/۲۵۱)

The upshot is that for the reasons given above the members of the committee may demolish the mosque and build a new mosque in its place, and they may also collect donations for that purpose. If, however, they wish to hang chandeliers and so on for the sake of beauty, they should spend their own money on that, or collect donations specifically for it. They should not put the mosque's funds into decorative items without the permission of those who gave the donations.

If the donors do give permission, then the mosque's funds may be used for decorative items as well.
