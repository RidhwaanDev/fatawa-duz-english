### TITLE
A question concerning the conditional gratuity

### QUESTION
Airlines announce that whoever travels a great deal on their aircraft and accumulates a certain number of miles will be given a free ticket or some other concession. Is it permissible in the Sacred Law to accept this or not?

### ANSWER
This comes under the ruling of a conditional gratuity and is permissible and valid, just as madrasahs make their fees and contributions conditional upon admission — a practice which Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ẓafar Aḥmad Thānawī (may Allah have mercy on him) declared permissible. The details have already been given above and may be consulted there.
