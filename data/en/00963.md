### TITLE
Valuing goods for zakat in wholesale and retail trade

### QUESTION
In one shop some goods are sold at wholesale prices and others at retail prices. At which price should zakat be paid on each?

### ANSWER
In trade goods, zakat depends on the selling price. So for goods sold at retail, the retail price is what counts, and for goods sold at wholesale, the wholesale price is what counts when the zakat is paid.

For detail see: Īḍāḥ al-Nawādir, part two: 44; Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 1/220; and Jadīd Fiqhī Mabāḥith: 7/568.
