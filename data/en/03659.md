### TITLE

The ruling on the excess when a larger sum is received for baggage lost on an aeroplane

### QUESTION

A person's bag was lost by Emirates. In this other person's bag there were two kurtās; then, when the price was returned, that person was given the price of four kurtās. So now is it correct or not to use the price of four kurtās? بینوا بالدلیل ۔

### ANSWER

In the case described, Emirates has the ruling of a common hired worker (ajīr mushtarak), because for a wage it conveys people and goods from one country to another. According to the principle of fiqh, if goods are destroyed in the possession of a common hired worker without any negligence, then no compensation is due; yes, if they are destroyed on account of negligence and carelessness, then compensation is due. Then, if the owner of the bag mentioned in the list only those items which had been destroyed and lost, and the Emirates company paid him an excess amount, then to take it and to use it is permissible and correct. But if the owner of the bag wrote down more items — for example, wrote four kurtās in place of two kurtās — then it is not permissible to take the excess amount; he should give it away in charity. Consider what Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah records:

وحكم الأجير المشترك أن ما هلك في يده من غير صنعه فلا ضمان عليه في قول أبي حنيفة رحمه الله وهو قول زفر والحسن وإنه قياس سواء هلك بأمر يمكن التحرز عنه كالسرقة والغصب أو بأمر لا يمكن التحرز عنه كالحرق الغالب والغارة الغالبة والمكابرة وقال أبو يوسف ومحمد إن هلك بأمر يمكن التحرز عنه فهو ضامن وإن هلك بأمر لا يمكن التحرز عنه فلا ضمان كذا في المحيط، وبعضهم أفتوا بالصلح عملاً بالقولين وبقولهما يفتي اليوم لتغير أحوال الناس وبه يحصل صيانة أموالهم كذا في التبيين. (الفتاوى الهندية: ٤/۵۰۰).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah records: If a garment is lost through the launderer's carelessness, then it is correct to take compensation for it; but if the launderer was helpless and all at once a great deal of water came and, despite his effort, he could not protect it, then no compensation is due from him. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۲/۳٦٤، ط: محمودیہ) ۔
واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
