### TITLE
A second way in which a divorce may be brought into effect

### QUESTION
The difficulties that women are facing nowadays because there is no sharia judge need no description. Sometimes the man is oppressive and does not fulfil his wife's rights: he gives her neither food and maintenance nor a divorce. Moreover, the clash between old and new attitudes to marriage is becoming widespread, and as a result cases in which a divorce is sought are increasing. To deal with these problems, then, could a written undertaking be taken from the man at the time of the marriage by which women would obtain the authority, when the need arises, to bring a divorce into effect upon themselves? Would this be permitted by the sharia or not?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, it is permissible and correct to have an undertaking of this kind drawn up, in which the authority of divorce is placed in the woman's hands, and to make use of it, with due caution, when the need arises.

An undertaking or marriage deed that is simple for ordinary people and beneficial for women is set out below.

(Adapted from Islāmī Qānūn-e Nikāḥ o Ṭalāq by Mawlānā Yaʿqūb Qāsmī Ṣāḥib, published by Jāmiʿah ʿUlūm al-Qurʾān, Jambusar; and likewise in Al-Ḥīlah al-Nājizah, p. 131, Dār al-Ishāʿat, Deoband.)

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

(اختيار نامه) — Deed of Authorisation

I, ____________, of village ____________, district ____________, province ____________,
country ____________, address ____________,

write this deed of authorisation of my own free will, in my full senses, free of any intoxication and without pressure or coercion of any kind.

If, after the marriage, such circumstances should arise with my wife ____________ that I cause her any hurt, or fail to fulfil her rights under the sharia, or cause her any mental distress, then I give authority on my behalf to Mr ____________ that, as soon as he is informed of any kind of distress caused by me to my wife ____________, he may — after consulting a qualified, expert mufti on the matter and acting under his guidance — at that very time, or at any time thereafter, pronounce an irrevocable divorce upon my said wife and separate her from my marriage.

I have read and understood this deed of authorisation, and I sign it in acceptance.

Signature: ____________

Mr ____________ read the above deed of authorisation in our presence and accepted it willingly; of this we are witnesses.

Should the (husband's) visa not be obtained in spite of every effort, or should such a disagreement arise between the two of us as husband and wife that living together becomes difficult and the purpose of the marriage is defeated — should such circumstances arise, then, for the peace of mind of my wife ____________ and her parents, I write the following undertaking in the presence of witnesses, in my full senses, free of any intoxication and without pressure or coercion of any kind, and with my complete consent and free will: that if I do not obtain the ____________ visa, or the purpose of the marriage is defeated because of disagreement between us, and the father of my wife ____________ sincerely feels that, notwithstanding the Qur'anic measures for reconciliation and every kind of effort, there is no way of maintaining the relationship between us and no remedy remains except separation, and my wife demands a divorce, then on my behalf my father-in-law ____________

may pronounce one irrevocable divorce upon my wife ____________ and separate her from my marriage.
