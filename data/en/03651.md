### TITLE

The ruling on taking a membership card from a company and earning points

### QUESTION

Nowadays companies issue a card in the name of the customer by way of membership, on account of which the customer receives special concessions in purchasing goods, and points are also earned on purchases. Then at the end of the year a prize is given through the points, or special concessions are received. But in order to obtain this, it is necessary to purchase goods from that company a number of times during the year. So will it be permissible to obtain such a card and take the concessions or not?

### ANSWER

In the case described, obtaining a card from companies and acquiring conditional points or particular concessions upon it is permissible and correct. In the terminology of fiqh this is called tabarruʿ mashrūṭ (a conditional gratuity).
Consider a few proofs as follows. Muslim Sharīf states:

عن أنس رضي الله عنه أن جاراً لرسول الله ﷺ فارسياً كان طيب المرق فصنع لرسول الله ﷺ ثم جاء يدعوه، فقال: وهذه لعائشة رضي الله تعالى عنها فقال: لا، فقال رسول الله ﷺ: لا، فعاد يدعوه، فقال رسول الله ﷺ: وهذه، قال: لا، قال رسول الله ﷺ: لا، ثم عاد يدعوه، فقال رسول الله ﷺ: وهذه، قال: نعم في الثالثة، فقاما يتدافعان حتى أتيا منزله. (رواه مسلم: ۲/۱۷٦).

The gist of the above ḥadīth is that a man presented an invitation to a meal to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. He ﷺ stipulated the condition of the Mother of the Believers ʿĀʾishah (may Allah be pleased with her) as well; the man refused, and then on the third occasion he gave permission, whereupon he ﷺ went along with ʿĀʾishah (may Allah be pleased with her).
In the same way, Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq (may Allah be pleased with him) purchased a camel saddle from ʿĀzib (may Allah be pleased with him) and requested ʿĀzib (may Allah be pleased with him) to tell his son al-Barāʾ (may Allah be pleased with him) to carry that saddle along with him. ʿĀzib (may Allah be pleased with him) said: No, except on the condition that you relate to us the incident of the Hijrah. So Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq (may Allah be pleased with him) related the incident of the Hijrah and al-Barāʾ (may Allah be pleased with him) carried the saddle. Consider what Bukhārī Sharīf states:

عن البراء رضي الله عنه قال: اشتري أبوبكر رضي الله عنه من عازب رضي الله عنه رحلاً بثلاثة عشر درهماً، فقال أبوبكر رضي الله عنه: لعازب رضي الله عنه مر البراء فليحمل إلى رحلي، فقال عازب: لا، حتى تحدثنا كيف صنعت أنت ورسول الله ﷺ حين خرجتما من مكة والمشركون يطلبونكم، قال: ارتحلنا
الخ. (رواه البخاري ۱/۵۱۵، في مناقب المهاجرين).

From both of these aḥādīth it is learnt that a conditional gratuity is permissible; and tabarruʿ mashrūṭ means to attach a condition to a favour, or to stipulate a condition of benefit for oneself in some transaction.
