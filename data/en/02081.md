### TITLE
Prizes in a fishing competition

### QUESTION
A place has been set up on the sea shore where a fishing competition is held. A fee is taken from those who enter, and prizes are then awarded in order of rank, out of the money collected, to whoever catches the biggest fish. Is this arrangement permissible or not, and will it be correct to take the prize, given that some entrants come away with nothing? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question this arrangement is forbidden and impermissible, because it falls under gambling.

See how the scholars have defined gambling in the following words.

Al-Taʿrīfāt al-Fiqhiyyah states:

القمار مصدر قامر هو كل لعب يشترط فيه غالباً أن يأخذ الغالب شيئاً من المغلوب، وأصله أن يأخذ الواحد من صاحبه شيئاً فشيئاً في اللعب ثم عرفوه بأنه تعليق الملك على الخطر والمال في الجانبين. (التعريفات الفقهية، ص ۱۷۷، وجواهر الفقه: ۲/۲۲۷، وإمداد الفتاوى: ۳/۱٦۱).

Fatāwā Shāmī states:

وسمي القمار قماراً لأن كل واحد من المقامرين ممن يجوز أن يذهب ماله إلى صاحبه، ويجوز أن يستفيد مال صاحبه، وهو حرام بالنص. (رد المحتار: ٦/٤۰۳، كتاب الحظر والإباحة، سعيد).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

Question: The Red Crescent is a Pakistani body that sells tickets for five rupees and three rupees, and every month distributes three hundred thousand and one hundred and fifty thousand rupees in the form of prizes out of the money collected. Is it permissible to buy these tickets and take the prize or not?

Answer: This is a combination of interest and gambling, and is therefore unlawful. (احسن الفتاوی: ۷/۲٦) ۔

Taqrīr Tirmidhī states:

The third form of gharar is

"تعليق التمليك على الخطر"

that is, making the transfer of ownership depend upon an event which may or may not come about. Such a transaction is not sound, and it is called taʿlīq al-tamlīk ʿalā al-khaṭar. It is also called qimār, because in gambling too the payment of money from one side is certain while the return from the other side is not certain but merely possible. Gambling therefore also falls under gharar. (تقریر ترمذی: ۱/۹۷، و اسلام اور جدید معاشی مسائل: ٤/۷٦) ۔

If, however, seating, food and drink and refreshments and the like are arranged for the people there, then the money will be in exchange for those arrangements and will count as an entry fee; and if prizes are then given to those who come first, second and third, there is apparently no harm in it.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
