### TITLE
The ruling on trading in shares on condition of granting a loan

### QUESTION
Shares are to be sold to the members of a company, and every purchaser of shares is obliged, in return for the shares, to pay the company a loan of 320 rand; the company will then pay its members, along with the loan, a further five per cent of the sum by way of profit. Is this transaction permissible or not?

### ANSWER
The first defect in this transaction is that an invalid condition has been attached to it, namely that every purchaser of shares is obliged to give a loan of 320 rand; the jurists have written that this is forbidden. The second defect is that those who deposit money will receive a percentage of that money; this method is also impermissible, because in a muḍārabah one must receive a share of the profit and not a share of the capital sum. If they were to say that the muḍārabah partners will receive 40 per cent of the profits, that would be permissible; then whatever profit is realised, 40 per cent of it would be distributed among all the partners in proportion to their shares. It is also necessary that the company whose shares are being sold should possess some assets or property — for example, a man buys a house and sells shares in it to other people. If at present nothing at all exists and shares are being sold to people, that is not sound. Yes, if equal sums are taken from people and a house is bought with them, and all of them are equal partners in that house, then that is sound; but this is not a sale of shares — it is rather making everybody a partner in the house.

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

ولا بيع بشرط لا يقتضيه العقد ولا يلائمه وفيه نفع لا حدهما. (الدر المختار: ۵/۸۵، سعيد).

Radd al-Muḥtār states:

قوله مثال لما فيه نفع للبائع، ومنه ما لو شرط البائع أن يهبه المشتري شيئاً أو يقرضه. (رد المحتار: ۵/۸۵، مطلب في الشرط الفاسد، سعيد).

Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

ولو باع شيئاً على أن يهب له المشتري أو يتصدق عليه أو يبيع منه شيئاً أو يقرضه كان فاسداً. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۳/۱۳٤، الباب العاشر في الشروط التي تفسد البيع).

Khulāṣat al-Fatāwā states:

ولو كان في الشرط منفعة لأحد المتعاقدين بأن شرط البائع أن يقرض المشتري أو على القلب يفسد العقد. (خلاصة الفتاوى: ۳/۵۰، الفصل الخامس في البيع).

Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ states:

ومنها أن يكون الربح جزء اً شائعاً في الجملة لا معيناً فإن عينا عشرة أو مئة أو نحو ذلك كانت الشركة فاسدة لأن العقد يقتضي تحقق الشركة في الربح والتعيين يقطع الشركة لجواز أن لا يحصل من الربح إلا القدر المعين لا حدهما فلا يتحقق الشركة في الربح. (بدائع الصنائع: ٦/۵۹، كتاب الشركة، فصل في الشرائط العامة، سعيد).

(وكذا في الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ٤/۳۰۵، كتاب الشركة، سعيد).

Jadīd Muʿāmalāt ke Sharʿī Aḥkām states:

In the present age one form of partnership that has become current is this: the owner of a running shop or factory or the like says to his relatives or acquaintances, "Put such-and-such a sum into the business and every month you will receive such-and-such a percentage of profit." The man puts in the money and every month receives the fixed amount of profit. People generally consider this a lawful business, whereas from the point of view of the sharia there are several defects in it.

(1) In any business, to fix a profit upon the capital and pay it comes under the ruling of giving a loan and taking interest upon it, which is plainly unlawful.

(2) In this arrangement the one who puts in the money does not bear the loss in the event of a loss; it is charged to the shopkeeper's account, whereas in the sharia it is necessary for a partnership that there be participation in both profit and loss. This is therefore an invalid partnership; this form of partnership is impermissible, and it is therefore obligatory to avoid it. (جدید معاملات کے شرعی احکام: ۲/۲٦)

Islām awr Jadīd Maʿāshī Masāʾil states:

The rate of profit of each partner must be settled in proportion to the profit actually realised in the business, and not in proportion to the investment made by him. It is not permissible to fix some set amount for a partner, or to settle a rate of profit tied to the capital he has invested. (That is to say, instead of settling that a partner will receive such-and-such a percentage of the actual profits, it is not permissible to settle that he will take such-and-such a percentage of the sum he has invested.) (اسلام اور جدید معاشی مسائل: ۵/۳۱) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
