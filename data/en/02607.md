### TITLE
The ruling on wearing artificial silk

### QUESTION
What is the ruling on wearing silk clothing, given that nowadays silk is artificial?

### ANSWER
It is permissible to wear clothing of artificial silk. Genuine silk, however, is impermissible for men and permissible for women.

قال الإمام النووي رحمه الله: أما المختلط من حرير وغيره فلا يحرم إلا أن يكون الحرير أكثر وزناً، والله أعلم. (الشرح الكامل: ۲/ ۱۸۹، كتاب اللباس والزينة، ط: قديمي).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

Nowadays it is generally artificial silk that is used, and its use is permissible even though it is called silk in common speech. Yes, if it is established by investigation that a particular cloth is genuine silk, then its use will not be permissible for men. (احسن الفتاوی: ۸/٦٦)۔

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

Genuine silk is that which is produced by the silkworm; but an imitation silk also comes from abroad, which is made from something else. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۹/۳۲۷، جامعہ فاروقیہ)۔

Āp ke Masāʾil states:

The cloths that are made of artificial silk are not silk, so wearing and using them is permissible. But if the cloth is of genuine silk, wearing it is not correct. (آپ کے مسائل اور ان کا حل: ۸/۳٦۷، اضافہ شدہ) ۔

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

Nowadays various silk cloths have been invented that have a considerable sheen, but in all likelihood something other than silk is used in them. If this is so, and the sheen is produced in some other way, there is no harm in wearing them. (جدید فقہی مسائل: ۱/۳٤٦)۔

In sum, artificial and manufactured silk is permissible, as is cloth whose warp is of silk. Cloth whose weft is of silk, however, is impermissible for men, and so likewise is cloth on which there is silk patterning of more than four fingers' width.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
