### TITLE
Rejecting the decision of an arbitrator

### QUESTION
Two men accepted a certain Mawlawī Ṣāḥib as arbitrator in a dispute. When the arbitrator gave his decision, one party refused to accept it. Does one party have the right, according to the sharīʿah, to refuse or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, when two men accepted a Mawlānā Ṣāḥib as arbitrator, then so long as he had not given his decision each of them had the right to withdraw; but once the Mawlānā Ṣāḥib has given his decision, neither of the two parties now has any right of withdrawal according to the sharīʿah. Yes, at the time of his being made arbitrator, that is, at the time of the appointment, and at the time of the decision, eligibility for the office of judge is a condition; and it is also necessary that the decision be founded upon evidence and admission, or upon denial.

In our times people commonly make a Mawlānā Ṣāḥib their arbitrator, but when the decision goes against them they refuse to accept it and go to court, so that making someone an arbitrator becomes a mockery. It is therefore better that at the time of the decision a court stamp paper be used bearing the signatures of both parties, so that no room remains afterwards for any kind of refusal. This was the practice of Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Muftī Walī Ḥasan Ṣāḥib Ṭonkī (may Allah have mercy on him).

Consider what Fatḥ al-Qadīr records:

وإذا نفذ حكمه لزمهما لصدور حكمه عن ولاية كاملة عليهما فقط لأنه لا يكون دون الصلح وبعد ما تم الصلح ليس لواحد أن يرجع. (فتح القدير: ۷/۳۱۷، دار الفكر).
وفي الاختيار: حكما رجلاً ليحكم بينهما جاز، ولا يجوز التحكيم فيما يسقط بالشبهة، ويشترط أن يكون من أهل القضاء، وله أن يسمع البينة ويقضي بالنكول والإقرار، فإذا حكم لزمهما، ولكل واحد منهما الرجوع قبل الحكم.

(الاختیار لتعلیل المختار: ۲/۹۹، بروت)۔

(وكذا في البحر الرائق: ۷/۲٦، والدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ۵/٤۲۹، ط: سعيد، والفتاوى الهندية: ۳ /۳۹۷، وتبيين الحقائق: ٤/۱۹۳، ط: إمدادية، ملتان).

Qāḍī Mujāhid al-Islām Qāsimī Ṣāḥib writes in Islāmī ʿAdālat:

The decision of an arbitrator will be enforceable against that party which expressed its agreement to the arbitrator's decision and did not withdraw its agreement before the decision was given. After the decision, neither of the two parties has the option of withdrawing from the arbitrator's decision. (اسلامی عدالت، جلدِ اول ص ۳۳۸، ط: قاضی پبلشرز)۔
