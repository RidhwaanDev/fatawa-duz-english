### TITLE
Reciting Dalāʾil al-Khayrāt as a regular litany

### QUESTION
What is the position on reciting Dalāʾil al-Khayrāt as a regular litany?

### ANSWER
Imdād al-Fatāwā states:

Question: Is it necessary to obtain authorisation in order to recite Dalāʾil al-Khayrāt and to teach it to others? And what is the ruling for someone who recites and teaches it without authorisation and without having obtained a licence?

Answer: It is permissible, but the benefit will not be what it is with authorisation. Even so, if a person recites and teaches it without authorisation, that too will not be without benefit. فقط واللہ اعلم — this humble servant, Rashīd Aḥmad Gangohī.

An explanation of the above answer: benefit is of two kinds — first, reward, and second, the inner state. Reciting without authorisation causes not the slightest reduction in reward; there is, however, a difference in the inner state. This is the detail of what Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā answered. واللہ اعلم — written by Muḥammad Ashraf ʿAlī, may he be pardoned, 2 Muḥarram 1323 AH. (Imdād al-Fatāwā: 3/140)

From the passage quoted above it is clear that reciting Dalāʾil al-Khayrāt as a regular litany is correct.
