### TITLE
The ruling on playing video games

### QUESTION
What is the ruling on playing video games?

### ANSWER
Video games in which there are no pictures of living beings, but rather the game is played with pictures of inanimate objects — for example a game of driving cars, motorcycles, aeroplanes, ships, helicopters and the like, or of making them targets — or in which there are pictures of living beings, but they are so small and indistinct that they cannot be called pictures (for example, the eye, ear, nose and mouth are not distinct in them, but there is only the form of an outline): in both these cases, if this game is played for a momentary refreshment of the temperament, or for sharpness of mind and presence of mind, and with the conditions mentioned earlier, then it is correct.

(2) Those large video games in which the pictures of living beings are distinct and prominent: this game will be impermissible on account of the pictures. In particular, when the conditions mentioned above are not observed, the objectionableness will increase all the more.

Furthermore, if because of video games one feels further exhaustion instead of mental refreshment, and one's studies are affected, and one remains addicted to it night and day, then no kind of game will be correct.

Consider what Ḥaḍrat Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) says in Aḥkām al-Qurʾān:

وحاصل الكلام أن ترويح القلب وتفريحه وكذا تمرين البدن من الاتفاقات المباحة والمصالح البشرية لا تمنعها الشريعة السمحة برأسها، نعم! تمنع الغلو والانهماك فيها بحيث يضر بالمعاش أو المعاد، وهذا هو السر في إباحة بعض الملاهي في بعض الأحيان. (أحكام القرآن: ۳/۱۹٦، إدارة القرآن، كراتشي).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
