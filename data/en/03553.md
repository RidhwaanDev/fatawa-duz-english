### TITLE
The ruling on preparing and distributing food on Shab-i Barāʾat

### QUESTION
Number 6: On the day of the fifteenth of Shaʿbān most women prepare various kinds of dishes, ḥalwā and sweets and distribute them among the children and the relatives. Is this practice established or not?

### ANSWER
Preparing and distributing various dishes on the day of the fifteenth of Shaʿbān is nowhere established. If, however, it is the general practice of these women to prepare and distribute food on other days as well, then it is permissible on the fifteenth of Shaʿbān too. Otherwise, distributing food on account of this particular day will fall under custom and convention, and abandoning it is necessary.

In a hadith of Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim it is stated:

عن عائشة رضي الله تعالى عنها، قالت: قال رسول الله ﷺ: من أحدث في أمرنا هذا ما ليس منه فهو رد. (رواه مسلم، ۱۷۱۸، باب نقض الأحكام الباطلة).

It is recorded in Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah:

To regard as necessary something that is not necessary in the Sharīʿah, and to treat a permissible matter as though it were wājib or sunnah, is not correct. Through this the thing becomes makrūh.

كل مباح يؤدي إلى زعم الجهال سنية أمر أو وجوبه، فهو مكروه كتعيين السورة للصلاة وتعيين القراءة لوقت، كذا في تنقيح الفتاوى الحامدية (۲/۳۳۳)

Indeed, persistence is not permitted even in the case of a recommended act:

الإصرار على المندوب يبلغه إلى حد الكراهة

It is on account of these express statements that the ḥalwā of Shab-i Barāʾat is forbidden. Those gentlemen who describe this prohibition as needless most probably do not have the above statements in mind; otherwise they would not say such a thing without cause. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 5/480, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

Elsewhere he states: To regard it as an ʿīd and to prepare rice ḥalwā with the intention of gaining reward is baseless and wrong. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 5/482, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

It is mentioned in Kifāyat al-Muftī:

Question: Was the ḥalwā of Shab-i Barāʾat an appointed practice among the Companions, the Successors, the Successors of the Successors, the four imams, the hadith scholars and the Muslims of the first era? Have the jurists also mentioned the ḥalwā of Shab-i Barāʾat anywhere? Answer: There is no proof of it from the pious predecessors and the mujtahid imams. (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 2/320).
