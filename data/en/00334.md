### TITLE
Changing the name of a mosque and making a donation conditional on committee membership

### QUESTION
A few years ago a house was bought in (—) with public help and donations, and it was converted into a jamāʿat khānah (prayer hall) and a madrasah, and as a mark of blessing it was named Masjid Imdādiyyah. Since our connection is with the khānqāh of Shaykh Zakariyyā, we placed ourselves, along with the prayer hall and madrasah, under Madrasah Taʿlīm al-Dīn, which is supervised by Ḥaḍrat Ḥāfiẓ ʿAbd al-Raḥmān Miyān Ṣāḥib (may his blessings continue). We then drew up rules for the prayer hall and the madrasah, and a mosque committee was formed from among the worshippers and the people of the neighbourhood. Programmes of spiritual reform and tablīgh are held in our prayer hall from time to time, and tablīgh groups also come and stay there. The number of Muslims in our neighbourhood is growing, so the prayer hall has begun to be too small. We therefore decided to build a new mosque and madrasah on this site. We have started collecting donations for it, and some people have raised objections which we would like to clear up.

(1) Some people say the name of the mosque should be changed — to (—) Muslim Jamāʿat, for instance — and that its rules should be changed as well. Would it be better to keep the old name and rules, or should they be changed?

(2) Since our connection is with the Zakariyyā khānqāh, does that make our mosque and madrasah different from any other mosque and madrasah, or do the two have equal standing?

(3) Some people say that we must tell the donors that we are connected with the Zakariyyā khānqāh, and that otherwise the donation is not permissible and the public should not give it. What is the ruling of the sharia on this?

(4) Some people say they will give a donation on condition that they are included in the mosque committee, and that otherwise they will not give. What is the ruling of the sharia on this?

(5) When everyone here is allowed to hold programmes and everyone is allowed to come to the mosque, is it permissible for a man to oppose this project simply because he is not a member of the committee?

### ANSWER
(1) If all the members of the mosque committee, or the majority of them, want the name changed, there is no harm in changing it. But the old name, Masjid Taʿlīm al-Dīn, is a good name and there is a good omen in it. In my view, therefore, it is better to keep it.

(2) All mosques are equal in being mosques. However, if gatherings for the remembrance of Allah are held in a particular mosque, then "khānqāh mosque" may be used as its name, or joined to its name, so that the work of the khānqāh can be recognised — but this is not necessary.

(3) When collecting donations it is necessary to tell people this much: that a mosque is being built in such-and-such an area. It is not necessary to tell them that it is connected with such-and-such a khānqāh. It is enough to say that it belongs to the Deobandi school. Of course, if someone asks for details, they should be given, and nothing untrue should be said about them.

(4) This rests on the judgement of the committee members. If they consider it unwise to place a particular person on the committee or make him a member, then they should not make him a member and should not take his donation either. But if they consider someone to be of benefit, they should accept his donation and make him a member. In any case, no one should press to be made a committee member. The real work is the service of the religion, whether one becomes a member or not.

(5) When permission is given for all the programmes of the people of truth, then it is not right for anyone to oppose them. Rather, a man should come forward to serve the mosque for the pleasure of Allah, whether he becomes a member of the committee or not.
