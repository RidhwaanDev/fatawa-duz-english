### TITLE
The ruling on giving zakat to a poor Shīʿah

### QUESTION
Here in Australia, Shīʿahs are living in the camps alongside the Muslims, but the local people are unacquainted with their beliefs and do not know whether their beliefs amount to disbelief or not. Given that they are hard-pressed and needy, may zakat money be given to such Shīʿah people, whose beliefs are unknown, or not?

### ANSWER
In the view of some muftis the Shīʿah are disbelievers on account of beliefs that amount to disbelief, so zakat should not be given to them, and if someone does give it to them it will not be discharged. In the view of Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ḥusayn Aḥmad Madanī (may Allah have mercy on him), however, their scholars are disbelievers while the uneducated masses among them are sinners, since they are unacquainted with those beliefs; it will therefore be permissible to give them zakat, provided they do not use it against the Muslims.

قال في الهندية: الرافضي إذا كان يسب الشيخين ويلعنهما والعياذ بالله فهو كافر وإن كان يفضل علياً كرم الله تعالى وجهه على أبي بكر رضي الله تعالى عنه لا يكون كافراً إلا أنه مبتدع

ولو قذف عائشة رضي الله تعالى عنها بالزنا كفر بالله

ويجب إكفار الروافض في قولهم برجعة الأموات إلى الدنيا وبتناسخ الأرواح وبانتقال روح الإلٰه إلى الأئمة وبقولهم في خروج إمام باطن وبتعطيلهم الأمر والنهي إلى أن يخرج الإمام الباطن وبقولهم إن جبريل عليه السلام غلط في الوحي إلى محمد ﷺ دون علي بن أبي طالب رضي الله تعالى عنه وهؤلاء القوم خارجون عن ملة الإسلام وأحكامهم أحكام المرتدين كذا في الظهيرية. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۲/۲٦٤).

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Muftī Rashīd Aḥmad Ludhyānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) says: the Shīʿah and the Qādiyānī are disbelievers — indeed, worse than other disbelievers — and it is not permissible to give zakat to a disbeliever. To give zakat to a Shīʿah or a Qādiyānī is a grave sin and the zakat will not be discharged; in fact it is not permissible to give them charity of any kind. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā 4/280)

Fatāwā Shaykh al-Islām states: Question: is a person who holds the Shīʿī creed a Muslim or a disbeliever?

Answer: whether a Shīʿī is a Muslim or a disbeliever is a question that calls for careful consideration and one on which there is disagreement. Mawlānā ʿAbd al-Shakūr and a great many scholars hold that they are disbelievers; some suspend judgement; and some hold the decisive position that their scholars are disbelievers while the ignorant among them are sinners. Certainly those who believe that the Qurʾan has been altered, who deny the knowledge of Allah Most High or His knowledge of particulars, and who profess badāʾ (that new knowledge arises for Allah) are disbelievers; and so too are those who level slander against Ḥaḍrat al-Ṣiddīqah (may Allah be pleased with her), and so forth. (Fatāwā Shaykh al-Islām, p. 167)
