### TITLE
Trimming the hair of the nose

### QUESTION
In cleaning away the hair of the nose, is it better to pluck it out, or is cutting it with scissors better?

### ANSWER
In the light of the passages of the jurists it appears that it is better to cut the hair inside the nose with scissors. Plucking it out is not advisable, since there is a risk of illness in it. Moreover, whenever the hair is seen to be growing, it should be cleaned away so that it is not visible from outside. See what Fatāwā al-Shāmī states:

ولا ينتف أنفه لان ذلك يورث الأكلة. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/٤۰۷، سعيد). (وكذا في الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳۵۸).
