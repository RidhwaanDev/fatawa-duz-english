### TITLE
The ruling on writing 786 in a letter instead of Bismillāh

### QUESTION
If someone writes 786 in a letter in place of "بسم اللہ الرحمن الرحیم", what is the ruling? Was this the practice in the earliest generations or not? Also, does the numerical value of بسم اللہ الرحمن الرحیم come to 786 or not? People say that it is the numerical value of "Hari Krishna"; kindly write a summary of the calculation according to the abjad reckoning. And what does "Hari Krishna" mean?

### ANSWER
A letter should begin with the pure name of Allah, blessed and exalted, just as Ḥaḍrat Sulaymān (peace be upon him), when he wrote his noble letter to the Queen of Sheba, began it with بسم اللہ الرحمن الرحیم. And the Noble Prophet ﷺ, when he wrote letters inviting the kings of the world to Islam, likewise began them with بسم اللہ, even though there was a fear of disrespect because of their being non-Muslims.

Ḥaḍrat Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him), Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Muḥammad Yūsuf Ludhiyānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) and Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Khālid Sayf Allāh Ṣāḥib have written that 786 is the numerical value of بسم اللہ. However, when the calculation is made by the abjad reckoning, this figure results only if one counts the written letters — that is, if the standing zabar of رحمن and اللہ is not counted; whereas if the pronounced letters are taken into account — that is, if the standing zabar of اللہ and رحمن is counted — the figure increases.

Consider the calculation in the following table:

۲ ٦۰ ٤۰ ۱ ۳۰ ۳۰ ۵ ۱ ۳۰ ۲۰۰ ۸ ٤۰ ۵۰ ۱ ۳۰ ۲۰۰ ۸ ۱۰ ٤۰

The total comes to 786.

The total of "Hari Krishna" also comes to 786. Consider the following table:

ھ ر ی ک ر ش ن الف
۵ ۲۰۰ ۱۰ ۲۰ ۲۰۰ ۳۰۰ ۵۰ ۱

The total comes to 786.

Farhang-e Āṣafiyyah gives the meaning of "Hari Krishna" as follows: Hari — a masculine noun, one of the names of Vishnu; Krishna — God. (٤/۷۱۲، ط: دہلی)۔

Fīrūz al-Lughāt states: Krishan — Kanyā Jī, an incarnation of Shiva; and "incarnation" is given the meaning: in the belief of the Hindus, God's entering into some birth and coming into the world to set creation right. (فیروز اللغات، ص۱۳۵، ۱۰۰۳)۔

See also the following references: (قاموس الفقہ: ۳/٤٦۲، تسمیہ، و آپ کے مسائل اور ان کا حل: ۲/۵۷۱،
و جواہر الفقہ: ۲/۱۸۷، رسالہ: احکام و خواص بسم اللہ، ط: مکتبہ دار العلوم کراچی، و احسن الفتاوی: ۸/۲٤،
و علمی مکاتیب، ص۸۹، جمع و ترتیب مولانا مرغوب احمد لاجپوری بحوالہ صوت القرآن احمد آباد)۔

In Qāmūs al-Fiqh, Mawlānā Khālid Sayf Allāh Ṣāḥib writes:
Nowadays people generally content themselves with writing 786 instead of بسم اللہ. This is not correct. Numbers and figures that people have fixed of their own accord, and which could equally stand for some other sentence, cannot take the place of the original words. Bismillāh should therefore be written out in full. (قاموس الفقہ: ۳/٤٦۲، تسمیہ)۔
