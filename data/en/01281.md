### TITLE
Attaching a condition to the marriage contract

### QUESTION
If a man stipulated before the marriage that the girl would give him a car, what is the ruling on that? And what is the ruling on accepting it?

### ANSWER
In the case in question it is not valid to attach such a condition to a marriage. The condition itself is void, while the marriage is sound and valid. Taking the car comes under the ruling of a bribe, and it should be given back.

Tabyīn al-Ḥaqāʾiq states:

قال رحمه الله تعالى: وما لا يبطل بالشرط الفاسد القرض، والهبة، والصدقة، والنكاح، والطلاق،

هذه كلها لا تبطل بالشروط الفاسدة لما ذكرنا من أن الشروط الفاسدة من باب الربا وأنه يختص بالمبادلة المالية، وهذه العقود ليست بمعاوضة مالية، فلا يؤثر فيها الشروط الفاسدة، ألا ترى أنه عليه الصلاة والسلام أجاز العمري وأبطل شرط المعمر. (تيين الحقائق: ٤/۱۳۳، كتاب البيوع، متفرقات، إمدادية، ملتان).

Niẓām al-Fatāwā states: When the groom's family take something from the bride's family for the marriage, or on the occasion of the marriage, this is what is called tilak. It is a practice and custom of the disbelievers and non-Muslims; in the eyes of the sharia it is impermissible and sinful, and the Noble Qur'an forbids it:

قال الله تعالى: ﴿يا ايها الذين اٰمنوا لا تكونوا كالذين كفروا﴾ (Juzʾ 4)

Taking something in this way amounts to a condition of the marriage, and so becomes unlawful and takes the ruling of a bribe, which the groom and his family are then obliged to return. If the groom or his family do not return it, the bride or her family may take it back themselves. In exactly the same way, if the groom or his family take anything from the bride or her family before the marriage as a condition of the marriage, this too is forbidden in the sharia, takes the ruling of a bribe, and must be returned.

كما صرح به في الشامي: ۲/۳٦۵. (Niẓām al-Fatāwā: 2/217).

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states: Conditions that clash with the binding rulings of the sharia concerning marriage — such as the condition that the wife shall have no dower — are invalid by common agreement. Among these conditions is that the husband should make some financial demand of the woman or of her family. Such conditions are by common agreement of no account and have no effect at all on the marriage: the marriage is contracted, and the conditions are null and void. (Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 2/33).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
