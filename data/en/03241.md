### TITLE
The ruling on a bequest of an unspecified or unknown amount of wealth

### QUESTION
A man made the bequest: "Let some, or a part, of my wealth be given to such-and-such a person." After his death, how much of the wealth is to be given? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question, since the deceased made a bequest of an unspecified and unknown amount, the bequest will rest upon the discretion of the heirs: they may of their own free will give whatever they wish to give.

Consider what al-Fatāwā al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah states:

ومن أوصي لا خر بحظ من ماله أو بشيء من ماله أو بنصيب من ماله أو ببعض من ماله فالبيان إلى الموصي ما دام حياً وإذا مات فالبيان إلى الورثة، كذا في شرح الطحاوي. (الفتاوى الهندية: ٦/۹۸).

Al-Fatāwā al-Sirājiyyah states:

إذا أوصي بجزء من ماله فان الورثة يعطونه ما شاءوا. (الفتاوى السراجية، ص ۵۷۲).

Fatāwā Ḥaqqāniyyah states:

In the Sharīʿah such a bequest is indeed valid; but because of its vagueness, after the death of the testator the determination of it rests upon the discretion of the heirs, who may give the legatee as much as they wish. If, however, the testator himself specified the property during his lifetime, then after his death that very property must be given, provided it does not exceed one third of the deceased's entire estate. (Fatāwā Ḥaqqāniyyah: 6/507).
