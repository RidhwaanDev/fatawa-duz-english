### TITLE
A litany for success in examinations

### QUESTION
The examinations are near. Kindly tell me some litany for success in them.

### ANSWER
In the situation asked about, supplications and litanies will be beneficial and profitable when they go together with sustained effort. If there is neither effort nor understanding, then litanies are like someone who prays for a lawful, righteous child without marrying.

Together with sustained effort, the following litany is beneficial:

Before the examination begins, recite Sūrat al-Aʿlā, or Sūrat al-Qalam, or Sūrat al-Raḥmān, or Sūrat al-Inshirāḥ, and then make supplication for success. Allah willing, success will be attained. (Anwār-e Shamsiyyah, p. 90).

Every morning after the fajr prayer recite "يا عَلِيمُ" 150 times, and on the day of the examination recite it in abundance. (ʿAmaliyyāt wa Taʿwīdhāt awr un ke Sharʿī Aḥkām, from the discourses of Mawlānā Ashraf ʿAlī Thānwī, may Allah have mercy on him, p. 237).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
