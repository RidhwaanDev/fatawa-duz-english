### TITLE
Travelling for Hajj with one's sister's grandson

### QUESTION
May a woman go for Hajj with her sister's grandson or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, since marriage between them is forbidden for ever, it is correct for her to travel for Hajj with him — for this woman is the maternal aunt of his father, and a father's maternal aunt has the same status as one's own maternal aunt.
