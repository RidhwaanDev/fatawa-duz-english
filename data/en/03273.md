### TITLE
Washing clothes in a washing machine

### QUESTION
If a large number of clothes are washed together in a washing machine, pure and impure ones mixed together, will all these clothes become pure or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
Washing machines nowadays are built in such a way that clothes come out of them completely clean and pure. The details are these. In the first stage the machine applies soap to the clothes and loosens the impurity; in the second stage it washes them with water; in the third stage it rinses them clean with fresh water; and then it cleans them thoroughly by rinses and spins. After passing through these stages the clothes become pure: water is used more than three times, and the wringing out is done as well. Consider the evidence and the supporting texts from the books of fiqh on the cleaning of clothes:

قال في الهندية: ثوب نجس غسل في ثلاث جفان أو في واحدة ثلاثاً وعصر في كل مرة طهر لجريان العادة بالغسل هكذا فلولم يطهر لضاق على الناس. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/٤۲).

Fatāwā Tātārkhāniyyah records:

ويجب أن يعلم أن إزالة النجاسة واجبة وإن كانت مرئية بإزالة عينها وأثرها وإن كانت شيئاً يزول أثرها ولا يعتبر فيه العدد وإن كان شيئاً لا يزول أثرها فإزالتها بإزالة عينها ويكون ما بقي من الأثر عفواً وإن كان كثيراً

هذا إذا كانت النجاسة مرئية وإن كانت غير مرئية كالبول والخمر ذكر في الأصل وقال يغسلها ثلاث مرات ويعصر في كل مرة فقد شرط الغسل ثلاث مرات وشرط العصر في كل مرة. (الفتاوى التاتارخانية: ۱/۳۰٦، ط: إدارة القرآن).
وفي حاشية الطحطاوي على مراقي الفلاح: ويطهر محل النجاسة غير المرئية بغسلها ثلاثاً وجوباً

والعصر كل مرة تقديراً لغلبة الظن في استخراجها في ظاهر الرواية وفي رواية يكتفي بالعصر مرة وهو أوفق ووضعه في الماء الجاري يغني عن التثليث والعصر كالإناء إذا وضعه فيه فامتلأ وخرج منه طهر إذا غسله في أوان فهي والمياه متفاوتة فالأولي تطهر وما تصيبه بالغسل ثلاثاً والثانية باثنتين والثالثة بواحدة. (حاشية الطحطاوي على مراقي الفلاح، ص ۱٦۱، باب الأنجاس، ط: قديمي كتب خانه).

Kitāb al-Fatāwā states: If impurity gets on to clothes and it is visible, the way to purify them is that the impurity be thoroughly cleaned off. Where the impurity is not visible, it is necessary that the cloth be washed and wrung out three times, so that one may be satisfied that the impurity has been cleaned away. The later jurists have given this ruling without qualification: whatever the impurity may be, the cloth should be washed and wrung out at least three times, so that one may be thoroughly satisfied that the impurity has come out and the cloth has become clean.

In the light of this principle, the rulings on washing clothes in a washing machine will be as follows:

(a) If all the clothes were pure, then obviously they will remain pure, whether they are given one cycle in the machine or several.

(b) If all the clothes were impure, it will be necessary for them to be washed in three lots of water — that is, when the machine has washed the clothes once and thrown out the water, water should be put in a second time and a third time. The first time, since the aim is also to remove the dirt and grime, the machine is run for a longer period; the second and third times, since the aim is the wringing out, it is enough to run it for a short while so that the water is expelled. (کتاب الفتاوی: ۷/۳٤۳، ط: نعیمیہ دیوبند) ۔

Ahamm Masāʾil jin meṅ Ibtilā ʿĀm Hai states: Ruling: If impure clothes are washed thoroughly in a washing machine and are then put into the spinner — that is, the part of the machine in which clothes are wrung out well by spinning and also become dry to some extent — and a pipe of clean water is fitted above the spinner and it is run long enough for clean water, instead of dirty water, to start coming out of the pipe below, then these clothes will become pure; it is not necessary to wring them out by hand.
