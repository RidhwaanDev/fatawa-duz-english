### TITLE
Wealth lost during the days of sacrifice

### QUESTION
A man had wealth in excess of the niṣāb, but after the days of sacrifice had begun that wealth was destroyed, or stolen, or he gave it away to someone as a gift, so that before the days of sacrifice passed he was no longer an owner of the niṣāb. Is the sacrifice still necessary upon him?

### ANSWER
In the case in question the sacrifice is not necessary upon the man mentioned. (الفتاوی الہندیہ: ۲۹۳، و فتاوی الشامی: ٦/ ۳۱۵، سعید) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
