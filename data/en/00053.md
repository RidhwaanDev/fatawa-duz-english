### TITLE
Hanging a picture of Khomeini outside a mosque

### QUESTION
Is it correct to put up a picture of Khomeini outside the mosque on Friday?

### ANSWER
It is not permissible to keep a picture of any living creature without necessity, and there is no exception to this for anyone: whether it is the picture of a pious man or of anyone else, it is absolutely not allowed. Secondly, one goes to the house of Allah — the mosque — in order to worship, so that our bond with Allah may grow strong; but putting up a picture amounts to saying, "Come here and look at pictures." So displaying a picture is not correct, either in sharia or by reason.
