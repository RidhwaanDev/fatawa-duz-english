### TITLE
Having the hair trimmed

### QUESTION
May the hair be cut to a particular length — that is, complete shaving is not meant, but rather taqṣīr, i.e. shortening it, in such a way that the hair of the whole head becomes completely even and no part of it is left longer or shorter? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
The constant practice of the Prophet ﷺ with regard to hair was to keep it; shaving is established only on the occasion of Hajj and ʿUmrah. On this basis, keeping hair is recommended and preferable. Yes, having the whole head shaved is also permissible, as was the practice of some of the noble Companions (may Allah be pleased with them). And the course lying between the two — for example having the hair shortened in such a way that the hair of the whole head is completely even and is not long in one place and short in another — is also permissible and allowed. Keeping it long in one place and short in another, however, as is the fashion these days, is impermissible.

Consider what Ḥaḍrat Thānawī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

So when shaving the head altogether is permissible, what harm is there in having it trimmed,

للإجماع على تساوي حكم القصر والحلق لشعر الرأس في مثل هذا الحكم وإلي التساوي أشير بقوله تعالي: ﴿محلقين رؤوسكم ومقصرين﴾

واللہ تعالی اعلم ۔ (امداد الفتاوی: ٤/۲۲٤) ۔

In another place Ḥaḍrat (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

For the permissibility of taqṣīr to be restricted to Hajj requires proof. Someone may perhaps entertain the doubt that concerning it the words

يأخذ من كل شعرة قدر الأنملة

have been written. It should be understood that this is a statement of the minimum amount; the intention is not to rule out anything more than it. Accordingly, in Radd al-Muḥtār it is quoted from Badāʾiʿ:

قالوا: يجب أن يزيد في التقصير على قدر الأنملة ۔ الخ

In the same way, the specification of a quarter of the head is a statement of the minimum. Accordingly, in Durr al-Mukhtār it is stated explicitly:

وتقصير الكل مندوب

So that doubt is removed, and there remains no factor distinguishing the one case from the other; hence the permissibility is general. (امداد الفتاویٰ: ٤/۲۲۵، ط: مکتبہ دار العلوم کراچی) ۔

Ḥaḍrat Ibn ʿAbbās (may Allah be pleased with him) reports that Ḥaḍrat Muʿāwiyah (may Allah be pleased with him) said: I trimmed the blessed hair of the Prophet ﷺ with scissors at Marwah. (مسلم، ص ٤۰۸، طبرانی)۔

Benefit: From this it is known that using scissors and trimming or shortening the hair with them is not contrary to the sunnah. But it should be borne in mind that cutting it short in one place and long in another, as is done in English-style haircuts, is impermissible; the hair on every side should be cut evenly. (شمائل کبری، جلدِ دوم، ص ۳۲۸، ط: زمزم)۔

The limits laid down by the sharīʿah concerning hair:

٭ Keeping hair is superior and recommended; with the intention of following the sunnah it is a means of reward.

Then there are three ways of keeping it:

(1) Up to the earlobes; this is called wafrah.

(2) Reaching between the earlobes and the shoulders; this is called limmah.

(3) Reaching the shoulders; this is called jummah. The most superior of these is the first, then the second, then the third, and all three are established from the hadiths. The details have been mentioned above.

٭ Shaving the whole head is permissible. ʿAllāmah al-Ṭībī (may Allah have mercy on him) has called it a sunnah, on account of the constant practice of Ḥaḍrat ʿAlī (may Allah be pleased with him), and Imām al-Ṭaḥāwī (may Allah have mercy on him) also held this view; but shaving part of it and leaving part of it is impermissible.

٭ It is impermissible to imitate and resemble disbelievers and open sinners in the styling of one's hair.

٭ It is impermissible for men to adopt the appearance and likeness of women, and for women to adopt that of men.

٭ If one keeps hair, one should take care to keep it clean and tidy, to oil it, to comb it, and so on.

٭ Excessive affectation and artificiality in the grooming of the hair is forbidden.

٭ Cutting the hair of the whole head evenly is permissible and correct; making it longer in one place and shorter in another is impermissible.

٭ If one keeps hair, tying it in a knot on the head is not correct; in ṣalāh in particular the dislikedness is severe.

٭ It is forbidden for men to keep hair below the shoulders; a prohibition of this has come in the hadith.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
