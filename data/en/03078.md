### TITLE
The ruling on using modern means for tablīgh

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to use the internet and other modern means for tablīgh, or not?

### ANSWER
It is mentioned in Naʾe Masāʾil awr ʿUlamāʾ-e Hind ke Fayṣle:

Every possible effort and striving for the propagation and dissemination of Islam and for its protection and preservation is an important obligation of the Muslim ummah. In accordance with "
وأعدوا لهم ما استطعتم من قوة"
it is necessary, for the discharge of this obligation, to use every possible lawful means and instrument, whether modern or old. Among the modern means of communication and transmission, the use of radio for religious purposes carries no objection, whether that use takes the form of benefiting from its programmes, or of taking practical part in a programme, or of setting up one's own radio station.

Fundamentally, the internet is the most important means of communication of the present age. Its status is that of a means and instrument for conveying one's message to others; and in determining the ruling of the Sharīʿah concerning such means, one must look at the purposes for which those means are being used. The use of means and instruments for lawful purposes is lawful in the Sharīʿah, and for unlawful purposes is unlawful.

In the light of these principles, the view of the participants of the seminar is that the use of the internet, in its capacity as a means and instrument of religious, daʿwah-related and collective welfare in accordance with the Sharīʿah, is permissible and at times necessary. It is also necessary that in the manner of presentation one keep away from evils and from what the Sharīʿah has made unlawful.

One important question concerns the ruling of the Sharīʿah regarding those channels which have been, and are being, set up purely for religious and daʿwah purposes, and which are free and clear of every kind of indecency and immodesty: will it be permissible to benefit from them or not? All the participants of the seminar declare it permissible, while some authorities do not allow it even in these circumstances. (Naʾe Masāʾil awr ʿUlamāʾ-e Hind ke Fayṣle, pp. 151–153).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
