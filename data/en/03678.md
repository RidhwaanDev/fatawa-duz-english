### TITLE
The ruling on taking the wage for a week in which the child did not take milk

### QUESTION
A man appointed a woman at a salary of 4,000 rand to suckle his child. For one week the child was ill, and on account of this the child did not take milk. Will the woman be entitled to the salary for that week or not? The woman is demanding the salary for the whole month, because she was ready and it was the child who could not take it, while the child's father wishes to give the wage for three weeks. What is the ruling now? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question the wet-nurse who suckles the child comes under the ruling of a specific hired worker, and if there has been no transgression or default on the part of a specific hired worker, then she is entitled to the wage. Accordingly, there was no shortcoming on the part of the woman, and therefore the wage for the whole month is due; it is not correct to deduct the salary of one week. Consider: it is recorded in Al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq:

والخاص يستحق الأجر بتسليم نفسه في المدة وإن لم يعمل كمن استوجر شهراً للخدمة أو لرعي الغنم يعني الأجير الخاص يستحق الأجر بتسليم نفسه في المدة عمل أو لم يعمل. (البحر الرائق: ۸/۳۳، دار المعرفة). (وكذا في الدر المختار: ٦/٦۹، سعيد).
