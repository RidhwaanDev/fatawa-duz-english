### TITLE
A doctor taking out insurance

### QUESTION
Is it permissible for a doctor to take out medical insurance — that is, to enter into an insurance arrangement in the sense that if some mistake occurs on his part in an operation or in treatment and the patient suffers harm, he may make it good with the help of the insurance company? For Muslim patients ascribe benefit and harm to the divine decree and bring no claim; but disbelievers, who do not believe in the divine decree, file a claim in the event of a mistake and recover a great deal of money. So, in order to escape this difficulty, may a doctor take out insurance or not?

### ANSWER
Generally, in insurance of this kind money is obtained in exchange for money, and the insurance company also pays an additional sum upon it. This is plain interest, which is forbidden and impermissible. It is therefore binding and necessary upon a Muslim, in his capacity as a Muslim, to keep away from insurance of this kind. Yes, if the insurance company does not pay the money over but instead, in return for that money, has the harm suffered by the patient made good in some large hospital through an experienced specialist doctor, there will be room for it.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
