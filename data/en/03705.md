### TITLE
The ruling on a teacher giving paid tuition to his own students outside class hours

### QUESTION
The teachers of an Islamic school give tuition for a fee to their own students outside school hours. The management has a reservation about this, and some members wish to make a rule that if a teacher gives tuition to his own students he may not charge a fee. The question is: is it permissible to teach in an institution and then to give private tuition for a fee to some of the students of one's own class outside school hours, or not? And will it be permissible to make such a rule or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, if the teachers, on account of private tuition, do not give proper attention to the children during the madrasah hours, then this is a betrayal of their office and will fall under تخونوا أماناتكم[الأنفال: ۲۷]. But if the teachers teach during class hours with full attention and trustworthiness, while some students take tuition in order to gain higher marks, then there is scope for it. To make a rule that they may not teach outside class hours is not correct, unless the teacher, because of teaching outside, falls short during class hours or becomes so tired that teaching becomes difficult for him.

This is because teachers have the status of an ajīr khāṣṣ (a servant hired for a specific term), and if such a hired servant tires himself out during his hours of rest in other unnecessary engagements and thereby falls short in the duties of his office, that will be included in breach of trust. In the Sharīʿah this is not permitted. Consider the following. It is recorded in Sharḥ al-Majallah:

لو استأجر أستاذ لتعليم علم أو صنعة وسميت الأجرة فإن ذكرت انعقدت الإجارة صحيحة علي المدة حتى أن الأستاذ يستحق الأجرة بكونه حاضراً ومهيئاً للتعليم تعلم التلميذ أو لم يتعلم

لأنه لما انعقدت الإجارة صحيحة بتعيين الأجر والمدة استحق الأستاذ الأجرة لمجرد تسليمه نفسه للتعليم

لكونه أجيراً خاصاً

أن الأجير الخاص يستحق الأجرة إذا كان في مدة الإجارة حاضراً للعمل ولا يشترط عمله بالفعل. (شرح المجلة لسليم رستم باز: ۱/۳۰۵، المادة: ۵٦۸).

قال في الدر المختار: والثاني وهو الأجير الخاص ويسمي أجير وحد وهو من يعمل لواحد عملاً موقتاً بالتخصيص ويستحق الأجر بتسليم نفسه في المدة وإن لم يعمل كمن استوجر شهراً للخدمة. (الدر المختار: ٦/٦۹، سعيد، ومثله في الهداية: ۳/۳۱۰).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

When times have been fixed for a teacher, then during those times he is an ajīr khāṣṣ, and during those times it is not permissible for him to take on other work for hire. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah:).

It is mentioned in Āp ke Masāʾil aur un kā Ḥal:

Nowadays a general epidemic has spread through the country: most teachers of schools and colleges give tuition; they take a salary from the government and also take a fee from the children whom they teach privately. Taking tuition fees is permissible, but not giving attention to the children in the place of education is a sin. (Āp ke Masāʾil aur un kā Ḥal: 7/235).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
