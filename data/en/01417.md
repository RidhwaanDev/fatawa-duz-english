### TITLE
Performing ṭawāf in the masʿā because of crowding

### QUESTION
Because of the crowds, people often find themselves on the roof of the Masjid al-Ḥarām in the masʿā (the course of the saʿy between Ṣafā and Marwah) and complete part of their ṭawāf there. Will the ṭawāf be valid if it is performed in the masʿā or not? It should also be borne in mind that the masʿā lies outside the Masjid al-Ḥarām.

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, it is a condition of every kind of ṭawāf that it be made around the Kaʿbah from within the Masjid al-Ḥarām, even if it is on the roof of the Masjid al-Ḥarām. So if ṭawāf is performed outside the Masjid al-Ḥarām, it does not count as ṭawāf; and by the agreement of the scholars the masʿā lies outside the Masjid al-Ḥarām. Ṭawāf performed in the masʿā is therefore not valid.

Consider what Sharḥ al-Lubāb states:

فصل في مكان الطواف: (مكانه حول البيت لا فيه) أي لا في داخله (داخل المسجد) أي سواء كان قريباً من البيت أو بعيداً عنه بعد أن يكون في المسجد (ويجوز) الطواف (في المسجد) أي في جميع أجزاء ه (ولو من وراء السواري) الأسطوانات (وزمزم) وكذا المقامات (ولو طاف على سطح المسجد ولو مرتفعاً عن البيت) أي من جدرانه كما صرح به صاحب الغاية (جاز) لأن حقيقة البيت هو الفضاء الشامل لما فوق البناء من الهوى

ولو طاف خارج المسجد، فمع وجود الجدران لا يصح إجماعاً، وأما إذا كانت جدرانه منهدمة فكذا عند عامة العلماء. (شرح اللباب، ص۱٦۵).

ʿAṣr-i Ḥāḍir ke Pēchīdah Masāʾil kā Sharʿī Ḥall states:

This subject was considered at a session of al-Majmaʿ al-Fiqhī al-Islāmī, and it was decided by majority opinion that even after the place of saʿy has been brought within the building of the Masjid al-Ḥarām it does not take the ruling of a mosque, and the rulings of a mosque do not apply to it, since it is itself an independent mashʿar (a site of the rites of Allah). Allah Most High says:

إن الصفا والمروة من شعائر الله فمن حج البيت أو اعتمر فلا جناح عليه أن يطوف بهما. [سورئه بقره: ۵۸]

The majority of the jurists, among them the four imams, hold this same view. Praying in the masʿā while following the imam of the Masjid al-Ḥarām is permissible in the same way as it is permissible in any other clean place; and it is permissible for a menstruating woman or a person in a state of major impurity to remain in the masʿā and to perform the saʿy there, although purity is recommended in the saʿy as well. (ʿAṣr-i Ḥāḍir ke Pēchīdah Masāʾil kā Ḥall, p. 264).

Tuḥfah-yi Ḥujjāj states:

If a person passed through the masʿā in all the circuits of the ṭawāf al-ziyārah, or in more than three of them, he must make that ṭawāf up. If he did not make it up by the 12th, and performed neither the farewell ṭawāf nor any other voluntary ṭawāf, one expiatory sacrifice will become necessary because of the delay, and he will still have to make up the ṭawāf.

If he is unable to perform this ṭawāf in his lifetime, it will be obligatory upon him before his death to make a bequest for a badanah, that is, the sacrifice of a camel. (Masāʾil-i Ḥajj o ʿUmrah, p. 396).

Moreover, as long as the ṭawāf al-ziyārah remains outstanding, intimacy with one's wife also remains forbidden. If he had intercourse with her several times in a single sitting, one sacrifice will be necessary; and for intercourse in separate sittings a separate sacrifice will be necessary for each act. (Zubdah, p. 375).

(Note: if, however, he supposed himself to be free of iḥrām and committed various violations in various sittings, only a single sacrifice will be necessary.

قال في غنية الناسك: وإذا اختلف جنس الجناية تعذر التداخل إلا إذا فعلها على قصد رفض الإحرام، فإن المحرم إذا نوى رفض الإحرام، فجعل يصنع ما يصنعه الحلال من لبس الثياب والتطيب والحلق والجماع وقتل الصيد، فعليه دم بجميع ما ارتكب. (غنية الناسك، ص۱۲۹، باب الجنايات).)

If he performed four circuits correctly inside the Masjid al-Ḥarām and passed through the masʿā in three circuits or fewer, the ṭawāf al-ziyārah is discharged, but a charitable payment must be given for each such circuit. (Zubdat al-Manāsik with ʿUmdat al-Manāsik, p. 375). (Derived from Tuḥfah-yi Ḥujjāj, p. 32).

For more, see: (ʿUmdat al-Fiqh: 4/168, and Ghunyat al-Nāsik, pp. 57–58, Bāb fī Māhiyyat al-Ṭawāf).
