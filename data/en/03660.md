### TITLE

The ruling on reserving the copyright of CDs

### QUESTION

Will the copyright of CDs be protected by reserving it, or not? Also, if there is no permission from the company's people to copy it, what is the ruling on copying it for one's own use or for selling?

### ANSWER

The right of invention is a right which the Sharīʿah has acknowledged: that when a person has invented something, he is entitled to protect it and to sell it or to take profits upon it. If every Tom, Dick and Harry were given permission to copy it, to sell it and to take profits from it, then the one who bore the toil and hardship would be deprived of the fruit of his labour. Yes, if a person wishes to copy it for his own use, there is scope for that, provided there is no intention of selling it and acquiring profit.
In the noble ḥadīth it is stated: "من سبق إلى ما لم يسبقه إليه مسلم فهو له ". (رواه البيهقي في سننه الكبرى: ٦/۱٤۲، كتاب إحياء الموات، وأبو داود: ۲/٤۳۷، باب في إقطاع الأرضين).

The commentator of al-Bukhārī, Ibn Baṭṭāl al-Qurṭubī (may Allah have mercy on him), writes that in those rights in which people at large are equal, if someone takes precedence in them, then the one who took precedence will have the greater right:
من سبق إلى المواضع عند العالم أو المسجد أو غيره مما حقوق الناس فيه متساوية أنه أحق به. (شرح صحيح البخاري: ٦/۷٦).

Moreover, Sharḥ al-Majallah has declared it permissible to take a consideration for abstract rights. Consider what Sharḥ al-Majallah records:

قال: وعلى ما ذكره من جواز الاعتياض عن الحقوق المجردة بمال، ينبغي أن يجوز الاعتياض عن حق التعلي، وعن حق الشرب، وعن حق المسبل بمال، لأن هذه الحقوق لم تثبت لا صحابها لا جل دفع الضرر عنهم، بل تثبت لهم ابتداء بحق شرعي الخ. (شرح المجلة لمحمد خالد الأتاسي: ۲/۱۲۱).
