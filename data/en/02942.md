### TITLE
A litany for success and prosperity

### QUESTION
Kindly tell me some supplication or litany by which success and prosperity may be attained, and by which every kind of success and wealth may be granted. بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question, reciting the following few litanies is beneficial for success and prosperity:

٭ Recite
يا مغني
(one thousand one hundred times). (انوار شمسیہ: ۸۹)۔

٭ Reciting Sūrat Quraysh (11 times) is for expansion in sustenance; while reciting
الذي أطعمهم من جوع
one should keep expansion in sustenance in mind. (انوار شمسیہ: ۹۲)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
