### TITLE
Taking oxygen while fasting

### QUESTION
Will the fast be broken by giving a patient oxygen while he is fasting, or not — seeing that oxygen is only air, and there is no vapour or moisture or anything of the kind in it? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
Oxygen is an inseparable part of the survival of human life; a person takes in oxygen with every breath. Sometimes, however, this is not enough for people who suffer from asthma, so artificial oxygen (which is clean, pure air) is given so that life may be sustained and relief may be had from the distress of asthma. The passing of clean, pure air into the body does not break the fast. Consider what ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

والذي ذكره المحققون أن معنى الفطر وصول ما فيه صلاح البدن إلى الجوف أعم من كونه غذاء أو دواء. (فتاوى الشامي: ۲/٤۱۰، سعيد).

ʿAllāmah Abū Bakr al-Kāsānī (may Allah have mercy on him) says in Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ:

وما وصل إلى الجوف أو إلى الدماغ عن المخارق الأصلية كالأنف والأذن والدبر بأن يستعط أو احتقن أو أقطر في أذنه فوصل إلى الجوف أو إلى الدماغ فسد صومه، أما إذا وصل إلى الجوف فلا شك فيه لوجود الأكل من الصورة، وكذا إذا وصل إلى الدماغ لأنه له منفذ إلى الجوف فكان بمنزلة زاوية من زوايا الجوف. (بدائع الصنائع: ۲/۹۳، سعيد).

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states: If there is no medicine along with the oxygen, the fast should not break, because this is breathing, and taking in air by way of the breath neither breaks the fast nor is it called eating and drinking. (1/188).

Kitāb al-Masāʾil states: If a person breathes by means of oxygen while fasting, the fast is not broken by it, because oxygen is merely clean, pure air, and its passing into the body does not break the fast. (Kitāb al-Masāʾil: 2/154).

The upshot is that if the oxygen is such that some vapour and moisture comes out with the air and enters the nose, then it does break the fast and the fast will become invalid; but if only clean, pure air comes out without any moisture, then it does not break the fast and the fast will not become invalid.

See further: (Rozē kē Masāʾil kā Insāʾiklopīḍiyā, appendix, p. 9, and ʿIlm-i Tashrīḥ al-Abdān: 110, 118).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
