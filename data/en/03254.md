### TITLE
The ruling on inheritance when a non-Muslim government declares a man the killer of his parents

### QUESTION
People spread a great many rumours about a certain man. He spent some time in prison, and applied to the government of the day for acquittal, but the application was rejected. The government then, purely on the strength of circumstantial evidence, ruled that he was the murderer of his parents and imposed a sentence of 22 years' imprisonment. Since it is now well-nigh impossible to establish the true state of affairs, is this man to be excluded from his parents' inheritance on the basis of the court's verdict, or not? He himself denies the killing and says that his mother killed his father — that is, the wife killed her husband — and that the wife then took her own life. بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the pure Sharīʿah, in order to establish a killing there must either be two witnesses or an admission by the accused; without a proof recognised by the Sharīʿah, a killing is not established. In the case in question the man has neither made an admission nor are there two witnesses against him; the court declared the son a killer purely on the strength of circumstantial evidence, and in the eyes of the Sharīʿah such evidence is on the face of it insufficient to establish a killing. Further, the jurists have held that the cause of exclusion from inheritance is a killing on account of which retaliation or expiation becomes due; and in the case in question the non-Muslim government has not carried out any sentence of retaliation. The said man is therefore entitled to his parents' inheritance.

قال العلامة السرخسي رحمه الله:

وحاصل المسألة على أربعة أوجه: أحدها إذا عاين قتله والثاني: إذا أقر عنده أنه قتله فهذا ومعاينة القتل سواء لأن الإقرار موجب بنفسه حتى لا يملك المقر الرجوع عن إقراره فهذا ومعاينة السبب سواء والثالث: أن يقيم البينة بأنه قتل أباه فيقضي له القاضي بالقود فهو في سعة من قتله لأن قضاء القاضي ملزم فيثبت به السبب المطلق لاستيفاء القود له والرابع أن يشهد عنده شاهدا عدل أن هذا الرجل قتل أباه فليس له أن يقتله بشهادة لأن الشهادة لا توجب الحق مالم يتصل بها قضاء القاضي فلا يتقرر عنده السبب المطلق لاستيفاء القود بمجرد الشهادة مالم ينضم إليه القضاء

الخ. (المبسوط: ۱۰/۳۱۲، ط: بيروت). وينظر: (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۷٦۷، سعيد).

From the passage above it emerges that, to establish a killing, two upright eyewitnesses must give evidence before the judge and the judge must give judgement upon it, or else the killer must himself admit the killing. Apart from these, a killing is not established by bare circumstantial evidence. And since the son is not established to be the killer of his parents, he will not be excluded from the inheritance either.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
