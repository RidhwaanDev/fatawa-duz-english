### TITLE
Salary for the days a teacher is away for Hajj

### QUESTION
A teacher wants to go for the obligatory Hajj. Is he entitled to his salary for those days or not?

### ANSWER
If there is an agreement with the teacher, or a rule of the madrasah on the matter, then that should be acted upon. Otherwise the practice of other madrasahs should be followed. Here with us the practice is to grant leave with pay for the obligatory Hajj.

Fatāwā al-Shāmī states:

أما لو شرط شرطاً تبع كحضور الدرس أياماً معلومة في كل جمعة فلا يستحق المعلوم إلا من باشر خصوصاً إذا قال: من غاب عن الدرس قطع معلومة فيجب اتباعه. (فتاوى الشامية: ٤/٤۱۹، سعيد).

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states:

However, it is correct to pay them for holidays and days of leave in keeping with what is known and customary; and in this matter the imam, the muezzin and the other employees of an endowment are all alike. (ʿAzīz al-Fatāwā: vols. 5 and 6, 258).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
