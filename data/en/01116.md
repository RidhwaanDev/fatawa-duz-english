### TITLE
The meaning of "لا بأس" in ʿĀlamgīrī and other works

### QUESTION
In ʿĀlamgīrī and other works the expression

"لا بأس"

is used, and this expression is used of what is contrary to what is preferable. It would therefore appear that the matter is contrary to what is preferable, that is, disliked. What is the answer to this?

### ANSWER
In the wording of ʿĀlamgīrī and other works, "لا بأس" does not mean that a thing is disliked or contrary to what is preferable. In many places "لا بأس" is used even of acts that are recommended. ʿAllāmah Ibn Nujaym (may Allah have mercy on him) and ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) have stated this explicitly in a number of places.

Consider what Shāmī states:

كلمة "لا بأس" قد تستعمل في المندوب، كما في البحر من الجنائز والجهاد. (شامي: ۲/۱۸۰، سعيد. و۱/۱۱۹، سعيد. والبحر الرائق: ۵/۱۹۲، كوئته).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
