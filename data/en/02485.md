### TITLE
The ruling on placing the fingers in the ears during the adhān

### QUESTION
When calling the adhān in the ears of a newborn, should the caller place his fingers in his own ears or not?

### ANSWER
Placing the fingers in the ears at the time of the adhān is established, whereas it is not established for the iqāmah; for this reason the fingers are placed in the ears in the former and are not placed in the latter. In the same way, placing the fingers in the ears when calling the adhān in the ears of a newborn is not established either, and so they should not be placed there.

Consider what Taqrīrāt al-Rāfiʿī states:

قال السندي: فيرفع المولود عند الولادة على يديه مستقبل القبلة ويؤذن في أذنه اليمني ويقيم في اليسرى. (التحرير المختار: ۱/٤۵، سعيد).

From the passage of ʿAllāmah al-Sindhī (may Allah have mercy on him) it is learnt that both hands will be occupied in holding up the child — that is, placing them in the ears is not recommended.

Ḥaḍrat Muftī Maḥmūd Ḥasan Gangohī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

The words of the adhān and the iqāmah are sufficient; there is no need to place the fingers in the ears. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۵/٤۵٤، جامعہ فاروقیہ) ۔

Furthermore, raising the voice is not intended here either, and so there is no need to place the fingers in the ears. Yes, since placing the fingers in the ears is among the etiquettes of the adhān, there is scope for it.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
