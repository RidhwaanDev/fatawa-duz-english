### TITLE
Holding the Eid prayer more than once in a mosque

### QUESTION
In Europe and America it is sometimes difficult to hold the Eid prayer all at one time, so the Eid prayer is held in the mosque several times, one after the other. Is doing this correct or not? Does it make a difference whether it is a mosque or somewhere else?

### ANSWER
In Europe and America the Eid prayer is generally held in three kinds of places:

(1) A prayer hall in which the five daily prayers, the Friday prayer and so on are performed, but which was not bought with the intention of its being a mosque in the legal sense — that is, the land has not been made an endowment — and where people simply come, pray at their own times, and leave.

The ruling here is that repeating the congregation in it is not disliked.

(2) An Eid prayer ground, that is, an open field in which the Eid prayer is performed; or, in some places, a large hall rented for the Eid prayer. The ruling here is the same: repeating the congregation is not disliked.

(3) A mosque in the legal sense. The general ruling here is that a second congregation is disliked; but according to Imam Abū Yūsuf (may Allah have mercy on him) it is permissible without any dislike provided the form of the first congregation is changed.

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

ويكره تكرار الجماعة بأذان وإقامة في مسجد محلة لا في مسجد طريق ومسجد لا إمام له ولا مؤذن. وفي الشامية: عبارته في الخزائن

أو كان مسجد طريق جاز إجماعاً كما في مسجد ليس له إمام ولا مؤذن ويصلي الناس فيه فوجاً فوجاً والمراد بمسجد المحلة ماله إمام وجماعة معلومون كما في الدرر وغيرها. (الدر المختار مع الشامي: ۱/۵۵۳، سعيد).

It also states:

وقدمنا في باب الأذان عن آخر شرح المنية عن أبي يوسف رحمه الله أنه إذا لم تكن الجماعة على الهيئة الأولى لا تكره، وإلا تكره وهو الصحيح، وبالعدول عن المحراب تختلف الهيئة، كذا في البزازية، انتهى، وفي التاتارخانية: عن الولوالجية: وبه نأخذ. (شامي: ۱/۵۵۳، سعيد).

Sharḥ Munyat al-Muṣallī states:

وأما من حيث جواز التعدد وعدمه فالأولى هو الاحتياط لأن الخلاف فيه أقوي إذا الجمعة جامعة للجماعات (وهكذا العيد) ولم تكن في زمن السلف تصلي إلا في موضع واحد من المصر وكون الصحيح جواز التعدد للضرورة للفتوي لا يمنع شرعية الاحتياط للتقوي. (شرح منية المصلي: ۵۵۲، سهيل).

Khulāṣah Bihishtī Zewar states:

In the same way, a second congregation for any obligatory prayer is strictly disliked when these four conditions are met:

(1) The mosque is a neighbourhood mosque and not one on a public thoroughfare. A neighbourhood mosque has been defined as one that has a fixed imam and a fixed body of worshippers.

(2) The first congregation was held with the call to prayer and the second call given aloud.

(3) The first congregation was performed by the people who live in that neighbourhood and who have charge of running the mosque.

(4) The second congregation is performed in the same form and manner as the first. This fourth condition is according to Imam Abū Yūsuf (may Allah have mercy on him); that is, if the second congregation is not performed in the same form as the first, then it will not be disliked. (Dīn kī Bātēṅ: 110).

Muʿallim al-Fiqh states:

Question: After the Friday congregation has finished, ten or fifteen men arrive. Should they pray the Friday prayer with the sermon in the same mosque, or should they pray the noon prayer in congregation?

Answer: Since holding more than one Friday congregation is permissible according to the sound position, and since a person on whom the Friday prayer is obligatory may not pray the noon prayer that day, they should pray the Friday prayer in congregation with a sermon. There is no harm in their doing so in the same mosque, though it is better that it be in another mosque. (Muʿallim al-Fiqh, the Urdu translation of Majmūʿat al-Fatāwā: 372).

However, in "الوصية الإخوانية في حكم الجماعة الثانية" Muftī Rashīd Aḥmad (may Allah have mercy on him) gave preference to the view that it is not permissible, and he cited the following report:

عن خرشة بن الحر أن عمر رضي الله عنه كان يكره أن يصلي بعد صلاة الجمعة مثلها" (رواه الطحاوي بسند صحيح: ۱/۲۳٤، في باب التطوع بعد الجمعة كيف هو )

This shows that praying a second time in the same form is disliked.

Then, at the end of the same treatise, he writes:

The second congregation that has now become common practice involves the following evils:

(1) It goes against the way of the Prophet ﷺ and the noble Companions.

(2) It shrinks the congregation and makes people take it lightly.

(3) It causes slackness about joining the original congregation and leads to that becoming a habit.

(4) It is a form of division and a cause of it — especially in the case of the Eid prayer, which is one of the emblems of the religion and a display of the unity and agreement of all Muslims. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 3/325, 328, "الوصية الإخوانية في الحكم الجماعة الثانية").

In short: where there is genuine hardship — for example, the mosque is small and the gathering is large and no other arrangement can be made — it is permissible to hold the Eid prayer more than once, whether in a mosque or at an Eid prayer ground or elsewhere. But holding the prayer every hour merely out of laxity and laziness, as happens in some mosques in Europe, is not correct.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
