### TITLE
Testimony about the moon sighting that goes against the government's ruling

### QUESTION
In the northern province of Pakistan, witnesses sometimes come forward in the last days of Shaʿbān or Ramadan and say that they saw the moon a day before everyone else. Will the testimony of such witnesses be accepted in the sharia or not?

### ANSWER
In the northern province of Pakistan some scholars and members of the public have taken the matter of the moon into their own hands and act against the committee appointed by the government. This is not right. What they ought to do is to close down their own committees altogether and rely on the committee set up by the government. The answer to the question itself is that if these witnesses have come from far away, such testimony is accepted; otherwise it is not.

Khulāṣat al-Fatāwā states:

وإذا شهد الشهود على هلال رمضان في اليوم التاسع والعشرين أنهم رأوا هلال رمضان قبل صومهم بيوم إن كانوا في هذا المصر ينبغي أن لا تقبل شهادتهم لأنهم تركوا الحسبة وإن جاءوا من مكان بعيد جازت شهادتهم لانتفاء التهمة. (خلاصة الفتاوى: ۱/۲۵۰). (وكذا في البحر الرائق: ۲/۲۸۷، دار المعرفة، وفتح القدير: ۲/۳۲۳، ط: دار الفكر، وفتاوى الشامي: ۲/۳۸۵، ط: سعيد).

In our own times, however, with the abundance of means of travel, the excuse of having come from far away ought not to be accepted, and a judge should not admit such testimony.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
