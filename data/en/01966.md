### TITLE
Trading in saddles made from snakeskin

### QUESTION
It appears from the statements of the jurists that the skin of a snake does not become pure by tanning, because it cannot bear tanning. But if in the present age it is tanned by means of chemicals, will it become pure or not? And will trading in a saddle made from it, and using it, be permissible or not?

See the method of tanning by modern chemicals:

In our times snakeskin is commonly used for making a saddle. The method is described as follows: chemicals are put into it and it is tanned and dried; then further chemicals are put into it and it is softened and made supple, whereby all the moisture in the skin is removed. Then, if it is put into water again, no effect of that moisture appears — that is, all the moisture has been removed by these chemicals. بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
When the jurists state that the skin of a snake does not become pure even by tanning, they give as the reason that it cannot bear tanning. But if tanning is achieved by the modern method, by means of chemicals, then, in view of the general wording of the noble hadith, the ruling of purity will be given. Trading in things made from it, and using them, will therefore also be permissible and valid.

See the noble hadith, which states:

"كل إهاب دبغ فقد طهر".

Al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq states:

وقوله كل إهاب يتناول كل جلد يحتمل الدباغة. (البحر الرائق: ۱/۹۹، كوئته).

Fatḥ al-Qadīr states:

وكل إهاب دبغ فقد طهر يتناول كل جلد يحتمل الدباغة لا مالا يحتمله. (فتح القدير: ۱/۹۲، دار الفكر).

In Qāmūs al-Fiqh, Mawlānā Khālid Sayf Allāh Ṣāḥib says:

The Hanafis have generally held that the skin of a snake, a mouse and the like cannot be put to use either, since tanning them is not possible. But since in the present age it has become possible to tan the skins of small land creatures as well, it is clear that they too will be fit for use after tanning. Indeed it is related from Imām Muḥammad (may Allah have mercy on him) that if the bladder of a dead goat is tanned and made fit for use, it too will become pure. (قاموس الفقہ: ۳/٤۰۵) ۔
