### TITLE
Three divorces by saying "divorce, divorce, divorce"

### QUESTION
In the course of a quarrel between a husband and wife, the husband said to his wife, "Divorce, divorce, divorce — are you happy now? Will you keep quiet now?" Afterwards the husband says that he only spoke this way in order to silence her. What is the ruling of the pure Sharīʿah in this case?

### ANSWER
In the case described three divorces have taken effect, because explicit words of divorce need no intention, and divorce also takes effect when spoken in play or in jest. His plea that he was only silencing her will therefore not be accepted. If, however, he had taken witnesses beforehand — for instance, if he had said in advance, "I am going to speak in this way, but my intention is not to divorce her, only to quieten my wife" — then he is believed and no divorce takes effect.

Radd al-Muḥtār states:

وأما الهازل فيقع طلاقه قضاءً وديانة لأنه قصد السبب عالماً بأنه سبب فرتب الشرع حكمه عليه أراده أولم يرده. (فتاوى الشامي: ۳/۲۵۰، سعيد. وكذا في الهداية: ۲/۳۵۹، باب إيقاع الطلاق).

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

وإن قال تعمدته تخويفاً لم يصدق قضاءً إلا إذا أشهد عليه قبله وبه يفتي. وفي الطحطاوي: قوله تعمدته تخويفاً ولم يكن من قصدي الطلاق، قوله إلا إذا أشهد عليه قبله أي قبل التكلم بأن قال: امرأتي طلبت مني الطلاق وأناْ لا أطلق فأقول هذا، بحر. (الدر المختار مع حاشية الطحطاوي: ۲/۱۱۲، باب الصريح).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

The word "divorce" was uttered three times, so the ruling will be given that three divorces have taken effect. As for his not having intended divorce, the fact is that the word "divorce" is explicit for pronouncing divorce and stands in no need of intention:

"ولا يفتقر إلى نية لأنه صريح فيه لغلبة الاستعمال"

۔

(الهداية: ۲/۳۵۹، باب إيقاع الطلاق).

In the same way, a plea of frightening or threatening her is not acceptable: "وإن قال: تعمدتہ ". For divorce to take effect, however, attribution is necessary — that is, the divorce must be related to the wife by naming her, or by pointing to her, or by addressing her. An explicit attribution is not required; an implicit attribution established by the surrounding indications, by the discussion in progress and by the evidence of the situation is sufficient. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۸/۳۱۳، مبوب و مرتب) ۔
