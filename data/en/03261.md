### TITLE
Inheritance of a compulsory pension fund

### QUESTION
A woman has died. She was a teacher in a government school, and every month a sum was deducted from her salary as a pension fund. The government now wishes to pay her heirs from that pension fund on these terms: out of one third, the children and the husband will each receive an equal share, and in addition the husband will continue to receive a monthly sum out of the remaining two thirds for as long as he lives. How is this pension fund money to be divided among the heirs? The deceased is survived by her parents, her husband, four sons and two daughters. بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case described, the pension money that the government pays will be counted as part of the deceased's estate and will be divided, together with her other property, among all the heirs according to the shares fixed by the sharia. Whoever the government nominates does not thereby become the lawful owner of this money, for it is a right belonging to the deceased, and the rights of a deceased person are likewise included in the estate. See what Niẓām al-Fatāwā records:

Question: Zayd was the salaried managing director of his company, and under the company's regulations a portion of his salary was deducted and accumulated every month. He also nominated his eldest son as the heir to this accumulated money. What is the position of this in the sharia? الجواب: The same ruling applies to the fund money. The person whom the deceased nominated as heir under the law will not be the only one entitled to it; rather all the sharia heirs will be entitled to it according to the sharia distribution. Furthermore, as for the amount over and above what was deducted from his salary — that is, what the department adds from its own side, or gives as an increase — it is not necessary to give it away in charity. This additional amount too is counted as part of the employee's estate and will be divided, like the original amount, among all the sharia heirs according to the sharia distribution. (منتخباتِ نظام الفتاوی، جلدِ دوم، ص ۲۷۰، ۲۷۲، ط: قاضی پبلشرز)۔

Majmūʿah Qawānīn Islāmī states:

In matters such as a provident fund, life insurance and a cooperative society, if a person nominates someone else for his money in accordance with their regulations, then whether the person nominated is an heir or not, his position is neither that of a legatee nor that of a donee; it is only that of a trustee. The money deposited and deducted by the person who made the nomination will therefore be divided, after his death, among his heirs according to the shares fixed by the sharia. (مجموعہ قوانین اسلامی، ص ۲۵۵، دفعہ ۱۰)۔

A pension fund is a right of the deceased, and it is established from the hadiths that inheritance runs in rights as well. For example, if a Muslim kills another Muslim by mistake and blood money becomes due, that money is a right of the deceased and inheritance runs in it. Likewise, if someone kills a Muslim deliberately and a settlement is reached with the heirs of the victim, the sum agreed upon in the settlement is one of the rights of the deceased, and inheritance runs in it too. Consider the following evidence:

أخرج الإمام مسلم عن أبي هريرة رضي الله عنه قال: اقتتلت امرأتان من هذيل فرمت إحداهما الأخرى بحجر فقتلتها وما في بطنها فاختصموا إلى رسول الله ﷺ فقضي رسول الله ﷺ أن دية جنينها غرة عبد أو وليدة وقضي بدية المرأة على عاقلتها وورثها ولدها ومن معهم

الخ. (صحيح مسلم، رقم: ۱٦۸۱، باب دية الجنين).

Fatāwā al-Shāmī states:

أن الإرث يجري في الأعيان المالية، أما الحقوق فمنها ما يورث كحق حبس المبيع وحبس الرهن، ومنها ما لا يورث كحق الشفعة وخيار الشرط. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۷٦۲، سعيد، وكذا ٤/٤۳۵، سعيد).

Al-Sharīfiyyah Sharḥ al-Sirājiyyah states:

واعلم أن دية المقتول خطأ كسائر أمواله حتى يقضي منها ديونه وتنفذ وصاياه ويرثها كل من يرث سائر أمواله، وقال مالك: لا يرث الزوجان من الدية لانقطاع الزوجية بالموت ولا وجوب للدية إلا بعده، ولنا أنه ﷺ أمر بتوريث امرأة أشيم الضبابي من عقد زوجها، وقال الزهري: كان قتل أشيم خطأ، وكذا يثبت عندنا حق الزوجين في القصاص لقوله ﷺ: من ترك مالاً أو حقاً فلورثته، ولا ننكر أن القصاص حقه؛ لأنه بدل نفسه فيستحق جميع الورثة بحسب إرثهم كالدية

هكذا ذكره الإمام السرخسي في شرح كتاب الديات. (الشريفية شرح السراجية، ص ٤۹، ط: الرياض).
وللاستزادة انظر: (حاشية الطحطاوي على الدر المختار: ٤/۳٦۵، ومجمع الأنهر: ۲/٤۰۱، وفتاوى الشامي: ٤/۵۱۷، سعيد).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

Question: The deceased was an employee of a railway workshop. Under the name of a provident fund, a part of his salary was deducted and accumulated every month. In the event of the employee's death, is it given to the person whom he has nominated? Answer: The money deducted from the deceased's salary and accumulated is likewise part of his estate. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۲۰/۲٤٤، جامعہ فاروقیہ)۔

In sum: out of the deceased's entire estate, including the pension fund, her debts, if she had any, will first be paid; then, if she made a bequest, it will be carried out from one third of what remains; then the remainder will be divided into 24 shares, of which the husband will be given 6, the father 4, the mother 4, each son 2 and each daughter 1.

Some muftis have written the contrary. See what Fatāwā Ḥaqqāniyyah records: Question: Respected Mufti Sahib, a man here has died who was employed in the army. He had put his pension in his wife's name, stipulating that after his death his pension should be given to his widow. Now, after his death, his other heirs are claiming a share of the pension. Do the other heirs also have a share in the pension besides the widow, or not?

الجواب: A pension allowance is not owned property; it is a grant from the government. On that basis this allowance is excluded from the distribution of the estate: the government may give it to whomever it wishes and in whatever amount it wishes. In the case in question, since the deceased's papers are in his widow's name and the government too is content with that, this pension is the right of the widow alone.

لما قال الشيخ أشرف علي التهانوي رحمه الله:

Since inheritance runs in property that is owned, and this allowance is purely a favour and kindness on the part of the government which is not owned without taking possession of it, inheritance will not run in the allowance received hereafter; the government has the right to distribute it in whatever way it wishes. الخ (امداد الفتاوی: ۳٤۲، کتاب الفرائض)۔ (فتاوی حقانیہ: ٦/۵٤۱، و امداد الفتاوی: ٤/۳٤۲، و احسن الفتاوی: ۹/۳۰۲)۔

But in the light of the evidence we have written above that a pension is a right of the deceased, and that inheritance therefore runs in it. In our view the position that inheritance runs in it is the stronger one.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
