### TITLE
A sale to a non-Muslim in which a corrupting condition is laid down

### QUESTION
A gentleman bought a church for a girls' madrasah. Certain conditions were laid down in the sale. One condition, for instance, was that they may make whatever changes they wish inside the church, but its appearance from the outside must remain exactly as it is, because it is a very old church and, being an ancient monument, there must be no change in its outward form. A further condition laid down was that within its grounds they may build a high wall around the grave of its founder, so that nothing inside may be seen. Because of the importance of this church the buyer accepted these conditions, so that a house of unbelief might become a house of Islam. On account of the importance of the church and the objections of some Christian groups, the matter found its way into some newspapers, whereupon a mufti issued a fatwa that, because of the corrupting conditions, the contract is invalid and must be revoked. A fatwa from your Dār al-Iftāʾ is requested on this question.

### ANSWER
The law of the pure sharia is indeed that a contract becomes invalid on account of corrupting conditions; but this law applies to dealings among Muslims themselves. If the contract is with a non-Muslim, the contract does not become invalid on account of such a condition. In Imdād al-Aḥkām, Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ẓafar Aḥmad ʿUthmānī (may Allah have mercy on him) wrote, in answer to a question about selling before taking possession, that selling before taking possession is impermissible, because it is the sale of something non-existent, or the sale of what one has not taken possession of, and both are invalid; but if the buyer is an unbeliever, there is no objection to making such a sale with him — such a transaction should not be made with a Muslim. (امداد الاحکام: ۳/٤۱۰) ۔

In another place he says: There is no harm in selling musical instruments to unbelievers:

وفي بيعه أي المزمار مع الكفار لم تقم الحرمة بالعين ولا بالفعل فإن الكفار ليسوا مخاطبين بحرمة الغناء ولا هو حرام في الأديان كلها
۔ (امداد الاحکام: ۳/۳۸٤) ۔
