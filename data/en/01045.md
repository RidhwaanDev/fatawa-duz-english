### TITLE
Spending zakat money contrary to what the donors specified

### QUESTION
Someone gave zakat money for people affected by an earthquake, and for various reasons the money could not be spent there. Is it now permissible to use this zakat money somewhere else or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question it is not permissible to spend the zakat money in another place; the permission of the owners is necessary. Yes, if the money is the zakat of a number of different people, and it is difficult to inform them all, and it is also impossible to spend it on the purpose specified, then it will be permissible to spend it elsewhere on a valid recipient of zakat.

Consider what al-Fiqh al-Ḥanafī wa Adillatuh states:

أما إذا عين الغني الفقير للوكيل لم يجز له أن يدفع الزكاة إلى غيره. (الفقه الحنفي وأدلته: ۱/۳٤۰، بيروت).

Al-Shāmī states:

وهنا الوكيل إنما يستفيد التصرف من المؤكل وقد أمره بالدفع إلى فلان فلا يملك الدفع إلى غيره. (شامي: ۲/۲٦۹، سعيد).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

An agent is a trustee; he has no right to act contrary to the instruction of the one who appointed him. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۹/٤۹۵، مبوب و مرتب) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
