### TITLE
Whether a full year must pass on the wealth that makes sacrifice necessary

### QUESTION
Is the passing of a full year a condition for the niṣāb (the minimum amount of wealth) of the sacrifice, or not?

### ANSWER
The passing of a full year is not a condition for the niṣāb of the sacrifice. Rather, at whatever moment during the days of sacrifice a sane, adult, resident Muslim comes to own the niṣāb of the sacrifice, the sacrifice becomes necessary upon him. (فتاوی شامی: ٦/۳۱۲، سعید) ۔
