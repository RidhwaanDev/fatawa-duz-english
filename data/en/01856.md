### TITLE
Teachers acting contrary to the conditions laid down by their madrasah

### QUESTION
Some trustees appointed two teachers at their madrasah on the condition that they would teach at the madrasah in the afternoon only and would do no other work in the morning, so that they could teach in the afternoon with their full attention. The teachers accepted the condition, and on that basis the trustees increased their salary by 35%. After that, these teachers began teaching somewhere else in the morning. Is what they are doing correct or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, it is impermissible to act contrary to the conditions of the madrasah after having accepted them; the conditions of the madrasah must be kept to as far as possible. Both teachers are therefore at fault for breaking them, and the trustees now have the right either to stop them from teaching in the morning or to reduce their salary. If, however, there is no failing in their teaching in the afternoon and they give themselves to their teaching with keen interest and hard work, then the trustees should give them permission to teach in the morning.

Allah says:

﴿يا أيها الذين آمنوا أوفوا بالعقود﴾. (سورة المائدة، الآية: ۱).

O you who believe, fulfil your undertakings.

Imam Abū Bakr al-Jaṣṣāṣ al-Rāzī (may Allah have mercy on him), commenting on this verse, has related from Ḥaḍrat ʿAbd Allāh ibn ʿAbbās (may Allah be pleased with him), Mujāhid, Ibn Jurayj, Abū ʿUbaydah and a number of others that what is meant by ʿuqūd is ʿuhūd, that is, agreements and undertakings. It is plain that lawful conditions too are of the nature of an undertaking. Al-Jaṣṣāṣ himself goes on to write:

وكذلك كل شرط شرطه إنسان على نفسه في شيء يعمله في المستقبل فهو عقد.

Whatever condition a person imposes upon himself with regard to acts he is to perform in the future is a contract. Then, shedding light on what this verse requires, he says:

وهو عموم في إيجاب الوفاء بجميع ما يشترط الإنسان على نفسه مالم تقم دلالة تخصصه.

Whatever conditions a person imposes upon himself, this verse makes their fulfilment and completion obligatory, unless there is some evidence calling for an exception to be made.

The Noble Qur'an has expressed the same idea in another place in these words:

وأوفوا بالعهد.

Fulfil the covenant of Allah.

The commentator al-Qurṭubī (may Allah have mercy on him) writes in explanation of it:

لفظ عام لجميع ما يعقد باللسان ويلتزمه الإنسان من بيع أو صلة أو مواثقة في أمر موافق للديانة.

A contract covers everything that is settled by word of mouth and that a person makes binding upon himself, whether it be a sale, or the keeping of family ties, or an agreement in any matter that accords with the faith. (مستفاد از جدید فقہی مسائل: ۳/۳۷) ۔

Furthermore, since they accepted the condition, the teachers are exclusively hired employees (ajīr khāṣṣ), and it is not correct for such an employee to work elsewhere.

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

والثاني وهو الأجير الخاص ويسمي أجير وحد وهو يعمل لواحد عملاً موقتاً بالتخصيص ويستحق الأجر بتسليم نفسه في المدة وإن لم يعمل كمن استوجر شهراً للخدمة أو شهراً لرعي الغنم المسمى بأجر مسمي، بخلاف ما لو آجر المدة بأن استأجره للرعي شهراً حيث يكون مشتركاً إلا إذا شرط أن لا يخدم غيره ولا يرعي لغيره فيكون خاصاً وتحقيقه في الدرر: وليس للخاص أن يعمل لغيره ولو عمل نقص من أجرته بقدر ما عمل. (قوله وتحقيقه في الدرر) ونصه اعلم أن الأجير للخدمة أو لرعي الغنم إنما يكون أجيراً خاصاً إذا شرط عليه إلا يخدم غيره أو لا يرعي لغيره أو ذكر المدة أولا. (قوله وليس للخاص أن يعمل لغيره) بل ولا أن يصلي النافلة. (الدر المختار مع الشامي: ٦/٦۹، ۷۰).
