### TITLE
Establishing the minimum dower

### QUESTION
What should the minimum dower be? What is its amount? Is it established by any hadith or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, the minimum amount of the mahr (dower) is 10 dirhams; less than this is not permissible. This limit is established by hadith, and by modern weights it comes to 30 grams 618 milligrams. For the details of the modern calculation, see Kitāb al-Zakāh, chapter one.

Consider what Fatḥ al-Qadīr states:

رواه ابن أبي حاتم من حديث جابر رضي الله عنه عن عمرو بن عبد الله الأودي بسنده، ثم أوجدنا بعض أصحابنا صورة السند عن الحافظ قاضي القضاة العسقلاني رحمه الله الشهير بابن حجر. قال ابن أبي حاتم: حدثنا عمرو بن عبد الله الأودي حدثنا وكيع عن عباد بن منصور قال: حدثنا القاسم بن محمد قال: سمعت جابراً يقول: قال سمعت رسول الله ﷺ يقول: "ولا مهر أقل من عشرة" من الحديث الطويل، قال الحافظ: إنه بهذا الإسناد حسن ولا أقل منه. (فتح القدير: ۳/۲۹۲، باب الكفاءة، دار الفكر).

This narration is sound, as Ḥāfiẓ Ibn Ḥajar (may Allah have mercy on him) stated, because its chain does not contain weak narrators such as Mubashshir ibn ʿUbayd. As for the well-known narration of al-Bayhaqī and others, two of the narrators in its chain are open to criticism: (1) Mubashshir ibn ʿUbayd and (2) Ḥajjāj ibn Arṭāh. That report is therefore extremely weak, although most scholars have graded it sound on account of its several routes of transmission. Other reports also support it. For details see: Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā, volume one, Abwāb al-Ḥadīth: p. 362.

The jurists too have argued from the above hadith and have not allowed a dower of less than ten dirhams.

Consider what al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

أقله عشرة دراهم لحديث البيهقي وغيره "لا مهر أقل من عشرة دراهم" وفي الشامي: قوله لحديث البيهقي وغيره، رواه البيهقي بسند ضعيف ورواه ابن أبي حاتم وقال الحافظ ابن حجر: إنه بهذا الإسناد حسن. (الدر المختار مع الشامي: ۳/۱۰۱، باب المهر، سعيد. والبحر الرائق: ۳/۱٤۲، كوئته).

Īḍāḥ al-Masāʾil states:

In the sacred law of Islam the minimum dower is ten dirhams; fixing a dower at less than this will not be valid. Ten dirhams amount to two tolas and seven and a half māshahs of silver, which by present-day grams comes to 30 grams 618 milligrams; and by a tola of ten grams it comes to 3 tolas and 618 milligrams of silver. (Īḍāḥ al-Masāʾil: 129).

Majmūʿah Qawānīn Islāmī states:

The minimum amount of dower is ten dirhams, whose weight is two tolas and seven and a half māshahs (30 grams 618 milligrams) of silver. The minimum will therefore be, in every age and every country, whatever amount by the weights of that age and that country will obtain two tolas and seven and a half māshahs (30 grams 618 milligrams) of silver. There is no upper limit. (Majmūʿah Qawānīn Islāmī: 101, clause 129).

In Aḥsan al-Fatāwā (5/32) the amount of ten dirhams is recorded as 34 grams by modern reckoning, but scholars in general have adopted the first view.
