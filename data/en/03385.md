### TITLE
A follower returning from standing for the third rakʿah to recite the tashahhud

### QUESTION
A follower forgot to recite the tashahhud with the imam in the first sitting, and the imam stood up for the third rakʿah and the follower stood up with him. Should he now return to recite the tashahhud, or should he follow the imam? Explain and be rewarded.

### ANSWER
Regarding one who has omitted the tashahhud returning and reciting it, some commentators on the standard texts and some authors of fatwa collections have written that the follower should return and recite the tashahhud, and they have based this on the position that following the imam is obligatory. But ʿAllāmah ʿAlāʾ al-Dīn al-Ḥaṣkafī (may Allah have mercy on him), quoting al-Nahr, stated that following the imam is necessary in what is necessary and obligatory in what is obligatory. On the basis of this point, ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) raised a difficulty against the follower returning and reciting the tashahhud: since the tashahhud is only necessary, how can following the imam in it be obligatory? Moreover, the standing has already begun, and that is obligatory, so how can one turn from something obligatory to something merely necessary? Muftī Marghūb Aḥmad Lājpūrī (may Allah have mercy on him), the elder, also adopted this position, that one should not return; and this position is in keeping with the details of the juristic rulings, while the claim that following the imam is obligatory in every case is open to question.

See: al-Nahr al-Fāʾiq states that following the imam is necessary in what is necessary and obligatory in what is obligatory.

قال في النهر: والذي ينبغي أن يقال: إنها واجبة في الواجب فرض في الفرض. (النهر الفائق: ۱/۳۲۷، ط: قديمي).

ʿAllāmah al-Ḥaṣkafī (may Allah have mercy on him) has also written the same, quoting al-Nahr:
قلت: وفيه كلام، والظاهر أنها واجبة في الواجب فرض في الفرض، نهر. (فتاوى الشامي: ۲/۸۵، سعيد).

Because of this statement of ʿAllāmah al-Ḥaṣkafī (may Allah have mercy on him), ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) expressed a difficulty about one who has omitted the tashahhud returning to it. See:
قوله والظاهر أنها واجبة

قلت: وعلى ما استظهره الشارح تبعاً للنهر يشكل العود إلى قراءة التشهد بعد التلبس بالقيام الفرض مع إمامه، فتأمل. (فتاوى الشامي: ۲/۸۵، سعيد).

ʿAllāmah al-Rāfiʿī (may Allah have mercy on him), however, gave the following answer to ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī's difficulty:
قوله يشكل العود إلى قراءة التشهد، يدفع بأنه بعوده إلى قراءة التشهد كان متابعاً لإمامه فيه ثم يتابع الإمام في القيام فلم يكن فيه ترك المتابعة. (تقريرات الرافعي: ۲/۱۰۱، سعيد).

But ʿAllāmah al-Rāfiʿī's point is hard to follow, for the standing is obligatory while the tashahhud is only necessary, so how can one leave the obligatory standing and turn to the necessary tashahhud? And then, the claim that following the imam is obligatory without qualification is itself open to question.

ʿAllāmah al-Ṭaḥṭāwī (may Allah have mercy on him) has also agreed with the author of al-Nahr. See:
قوله لحكم المتابعة، هي واجبة في الواجب فريضة في الفرض كما استظهره صاحب النهر. (حاشية الطحطاوي على مراقي الفلاح، ص ٤٦٦، ط: قديمي).

The upshot is that in the view of ʿAllāmah al-Ḥaṣkafī (may Allah have mercy on him), ʿAllāmah al-Ṭaḥṭāwī (may Allah have mercy on him) and the author of al-Nahr, following the imam is not obligatory without qualification; and ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) too stated this explicitly in his discussion of the necessary elements of prayer. See:

أقول: الذي يظهر أنهم أرادوا بالفرض الواجب، وكون المتابعة فرضاً في الفرض لا يصح على إطلاقه لما صرحوا به من أن المسبوق لو قام قبل قعود إمامه قدر التشهد في آخر الصلاة، تصح صلاته إن قرأ ما تجوز به الصلاة بعد قعود الإمام قدر التشهد وإلا لا، مع أنه لم يتابع في القعدة الأخيرة، فلو كانت المتابعة فرضاً في الفرض مطلقاً لبطلت صلاته مطلقاً، نعم تكون المتابعة فرضاً، بمعنى أن يأتي بالفرض مع إمامه أو بعده. (فتاوى الشامي: ۱/ ٤۷۱، سعيد).

From the above passage it is clear that in the view of ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) following the imam is not obligatory without qualification.

The rulings that indicate that the follower should not return are as follows:

(1) The jurists have written that if a person forgets the first sitting and stands up for the third rakʿah, he will not now leave the standing and return to the sitting; he will complete the prayer and perform the prostration of forgetfulness. See what is recorded in Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ:

ولو ترك القعدة الأولى من ذوات الأربع وقام إلى الثالثة فإن استتم قائماً لا يعود لما روي عن النبي ﷺ أنه قام من الثانية إلى الثالثة ولم يقعد فسبحوا به فلم يعد

ولأن القيام فريضة والقعدة الأولى واجبة فلا يترك الفرض لمكان الواجب. (بدائع الصنائع: ۱/۱۷۱، سعيد).

(2) The jurists have written that if a person forgets to recite the supplication of qunūt in the witr prayer and goes into the bowing, he will not leave the bowing and return to the qunūt, because its proper place has passed. The same applies here.

قال في الدر المختار: ولو نسيه أي القنوت ثم تذكره في الركوع لا يقنت فيه لفوات محله، ولا يعود إلى القيام في الأصح لأن فيه رفض الفرض للواجب. (الدر المختار: ۲/۹، سعيد). (وكذا في حاشية الطحطاوي على مراقي الفلاح).

(3) If a person forgets the Eid takbīrs while standing in the Eid prayer and goes into the bowing, the jurists write that he will not leave the imam's bowing and return to the standing for the takbīrs. Likewise, if a person joins the imam late, he will say the takbīrs in the bowing; and if the imam raises his head, he will leave the takbīrs and follow the imam. So here too one should not leave the standing with the imam in order to perform the necessary — or sunnah — tashahhud.

See what is stated in al-Jawharah al-Nayyirah:

ولو رفع الإمام رأسه بعد ما أدى بعض التكبيرات فإنه يرفع رأسه ويتابع الإمام ويسقط عنه باقي التكبيرات؛ لأن متابعة الإمام واجبة. (الجوهرة النيرة: ۱/۳۷۱، باب صلاة العيدين).
وزاد في فتح القدير: لأنه إن أتى به في الركوع لزم ترك المتابعة المفروضة للواجب. (فتح القدير: ۲/۷۸، دار الفكر). (وهكذا في الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱۵۱، وفتاوى الشامي: ۲/۱۷٤، سعيد، ومراقي الفلاح، ص ۲۱٦، ط: بيروت).

When here the necessary takbīrs drop away on account of following the imam, then the necessary tashahhud drops away on account of the obligatory standing with all the more reason.

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Marghūb Ṣāḥib Lājpūrī (may Allah have mercy on him), the elder, our esteemed forefather, likewise wrote that the follower will not return but will follow the imam. His worthy grandson, Mawlānā Marghūb Ṣāḥib Lājpūrī (may his shade endure), however, after quoting this question from Marghūb al-Fatāwā in ʿIlmī Makātīb, attributed an oversight to his grandfather. But on the basis of the references quoted above, in our view the ruling of Marghūb al-Fatāwā is sound and correct, and attributing an oversight to the author of the fatwa collection is open to question. Accordingly the full account is reproduced below from ʿIlmī Makātīb:

My respected and honoured colleague Mawlānā Muftī ʿAbd al-Qayyūm Ṣāḥib زید مجدہ. السلام علیکم و رحمۃ اللہ وبرکاتہ ۔ Your esteemed letter reached me. In it you raised difficulties about certain fatwas in Marghūb al-Fatāwā, volume two. This writer reconsidered those places and felt that your objections are correct in some places, and corrections have accordingly been made there. In some places, however, I am not yet satisfied; I am giving those further thought, and I have also referred them to certain scholars and muftis whose replies I am awaiting. Later I shall be able to reach a decision; whatever I feel satisfied about and whatever has the support of the senior scholars, I shall let stand.

1. You raised a difficulty about this fatwa of Ḥaḍrat's: Q: A follower forgot to recite the tashahhud in the first sitting and stood up with the imam for the third rakʿah. What should he do now? Should he leave off following the imam and then sit and recite the tashahhud, or should he remain standing in following the imam?

A: حامداً و مصلیاً، الجواب و باللہ التوفیق: If a follower forgot to recite the tashahhud in the first sitting and remembered it in the third rakʿah, he should not now leave off following the imam, nor should he leave the imam and return to the sitting for the sake of the tashahhud. Yes, if he was still seated in the sitting and remembered that he had not recited the tashahhud, then he should recite it; and even if the imam stands up, the follower should not stand up with him but should recite the tashahhud and then stand. But once he has stood up with the imam for the third rakʿah, he should not now sit again to recite the tashahhud, nor should he leave off following the imam. In such a case the follower's prayer will be valid, though with dislikedness. فقط، واللہ تعالی اعلم و علمہٗ اتم و احکم ۔

The difficulty raised was that this particular ruling ("but once he has stood up with the imam for the third rakʿah, he should not now sit again to recite the tashahhud") conflicts with the ruling in al-Kabīrī. This writer therefore looked into it and made the following correction in the footnote:

It seems that an oversight occurred on Ḥaḍrat's part (may Allah have mercy on him) in this matter, for al-Kabīrī states the opposite:
المقتدي إذا نسي التشهد في القعدة الأولى فذكر بعد ما قام، عليه أن يعود ويتشهد. (كبيري، ص ٤۵۹، فصل في سجود السهو).

After that he quoted the passage of ʿUmdat al-Fiqh (2/217). (علمی مکاتیب، ص ۳۳٤) ۔
