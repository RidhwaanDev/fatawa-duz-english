### TITLE

The ruling on a partnership in goods

### QUESTION

If partners form a partnership in goods instead of cash — for example, Zayd has cloth and ʿUmar has books, and they conclude a partnership between themselves on the condition of half the profit each — is this permissible or not?

### ANSWER

The jurists say that for a sharikat al-mufāwaḍah and a sharikat al-ʿinān, the capital that the partners put together must be of the category of cash: it must consist of gold, silver, current coinage or the like. A partnership in goods will not be sound or valid.
Sharḥ al-Majallah states:

كون رأس المال من قبيل النقود شرط لصحة شركة الأموال سواء كانت مفاوضة أو عناناً فلا تجوز بالعروض والمكيل والموزون عندنا، لأنه يودي إلى ربح مالم يضمن لأنه إذا باع كل واحد منهما رأس ماله وتفاضل الثمنان، فما يستحقه أحدهما من الزيادة في مال صاحبه ربح مالم يملك ومالم يضمن. (شرح المجلة، للأتاسي، ٤/۲٦۱، المادة: ۱۳۳۸).

Mawlānā Ẓafar Aḥmad ʿUthmānī (may Allah have mercy on him), however, gave a fatwa in Imdād al-Aḥkām permitting a partnership in trade goods. Accordingly, on account of the widespread need, the fatwa in this question is given on the position of the other imāms, and the partnership described, as well as partnership in trade goods, is declared permissible. (امداد الاحکام: ۳/٤٤۵).
Ḥakīm al-Ummat Mawlānā Ashraf ʿAlī Thānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) mentioned in "امداد الفتاوی" that although the noble jurists differ over partnership in trade goods, the fatwa may be given on the school of Imām Mālik (may Allah have mercy on him) on account of the needs of the present age and the widespread need, since this can be of greater help in resolving the difficulties and hardships of the modern era. (شرکت اور مضاربت عصر حاضر میں، ص۲۵٤، ادارۃ المعارف کراچی) ۔
