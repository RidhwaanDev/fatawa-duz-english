### TITLE
The ruling on putting up the announcement of a programme on the mosque's notice board

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to put up on the mosque's notice board a sheet announcing a game or an election, or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, an announcement in which there is the propagation and spreading of the religion may correctly be put up; likewise an announcement in which there is benefit for the people may also properly be put up. But one ought to keep away from putting up announcements of mere idle sport, amusement and nonsense. Although the mosque's notice board is outside the legally defined mosque, it is nevertheless necessary to protect the precincts of the mosque from vain things. Consider what is stated in the noble Ibn Mājah:

عن واثلة بن الأسقع أن النبي ﷺ قال: جنبوا مساجدكم صبيانكم ومجانينكم وشراركم وبيعكم وخصوماتكم ورفع أصواتكم وإقامة حدودكم وسل سيوفكم، واتخذوا على أبوابها المطاهر وجمروها في الجمع. (رواه ابن ماجه، رقم: ۷۵۰).

قال في الزوائد: إسناده ضعيف، فإن الحارث بن نبهان متفق على ضعفه.

Yes, if it is beneficial from the religious or worldly point of view, then there is permission for it — just as the Abyssinians played a game in the courtyard outside the Prophet's Mosque.

Consider what is stated in the noble Bukhārī:

عن عروة بن الزبير أن عائشة قالت: لقد رأيت رسول الله ﷺ يوماً على باب حجرتي والحبشة يلعبون في المسجد ورسول الله ﷺ يسترني بردائه انظر إلى لعبهم. (رقم: ٤۵٤٤۱۱).

قال المهلب: المسجد موضوع لا مر جماعة المسلمين وكل ما كان من الأعمال التي تجمع منفعة الدين وأهله واللعب بالحراب من تدريب الجوارح على معاني الحروب. (عمدة القاري: ۷/۷۵).

In Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil it is said:

Nowadays certain calendars are published in which the prayer times, religious articles, aḥādīth and portions of verses are entered, and in which, for the sake of economic ease in their printing, commercial advertisements are also entered, the quantity of which is very small in comparison with the other matter. It is permissible to hang such calendars in mosques, because the real purpose of these calendars is invitation to the religion and its propagation, while the advertisements have a subordinate status and therefore will not be taken into account. (Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 1/154).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
