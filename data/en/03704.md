### TITLE
A way of gifting a house to one's wife during one's lifetime

### QUESTION
1. A man wishes to gift his house to his wife during his lifetime, but there are movable articles and goods in it which he wishes to exclude from the gift. What form should this take in the Sharīʿah?

2. And if he wishes to gift part of a piece of land that is capable of division, but does not wish to divide it, what form should that take?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, the easy way for the gift to be complete is that the husband should sell the house to his wife for a nominal price, excluding the goods, and should then waive the price. In this way the house will become the wife's and the goods will remain the husband's.

2. The easy way for this too is that he should sell half the land to the donee for a nominal price and then waive the price. Moreover, in order to block any future denial, it is better to have the house or the land transferred into the donee's name.

A second way is that of deposit: he should hand the goods over to the wife as a deposit and vacate the place, then gift the house and give the wife possession of it, and the gift will be complete. Consider the following. It is mentioned in Fatāwā Hindiyyah:

ولو وهب داراً فيها متاع الواهب وسلم الدار إليه أو سلمها مع المتاع لم تصح والحيلة فيه أن يودع المتاع أولاً عند الموهوب له ويخلي بينه وبينه ثم يسلم الدار إليه فتصح الهبة فيها. (الفتاوى الهندية: ٤/۳۸۰).
(وكذا في فتاوى الشامي: ۸/٤۳۹، سعيد، and Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā: 5/601).
