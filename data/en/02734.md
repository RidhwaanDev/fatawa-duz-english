### TITLE
Wearing a clip in the hair

### QUESTION
In our times some women wear a large clip in the hair to make it look fuller. It is also a kind of fashion that is widespread in the Arab lands, and they do not even wear a scarf over it. Does this fall under the hadith "كأسنمة البخت" and so become forbidden and impermissible, or is it correct to do?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, if the clip is large, and it sits on the upper part of the head, and it gives the appearance of a camel's hump, it will fall under the prohibition in the hadith and so be forbidden. But if the clip is a small one and she gathers the hair into a bun at the nape and fastens it there, there is no harm in it. All the same, deliberate imitation of corrupt and disbelieving women should be avoided.

See what ʿAllāmah al-Nawawī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

ومعني رؤوسهن كأسنمة البخت أن يكبرنها ويعظمنها بلف عمامة أو عصابة أو نحوها. (شرح صحيح مسلم: ۱٤/۱۱۰، دار إحياء التراث العربي).

وفيه: قال وهي ضفر غدائر وشدها إلى فوق وجمعها في وسط الرأس فتصير كأسنمة البخت قال: وهذا يدل على أن المراد بالتشبيه بأسنمة البخت إنما هو لارتفاع الغدائر فوق رؤوسهن وجمع عقائصها هناك

الخ. (شرح مسلم: ۱۷/۱۹۱، ط: بيروت).

Takmilat Fatḥ al-Mulhim states:

قلت: قد ظهرت في عصرنا نساء يعقدن شعورهن المسترسلة على أقفيتهن أو في أوساط رؤوسهن بما يشابه سنام البعير سواء بسواء، كأن النبي ﷺ شبه رؤوسهن بأسنمة البخت. (تكملة فتح الملهم: ٤/۲۰۱).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

It is permissible for women to wear a clip in the hair, provided it is not impure and is not a badge of the disbelievers or of corrupt people; the basic ruling is permissibility, and the prohibition rests on the grounds mentioned. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۹/٤٤۱، جامعہ فاروقیہ) ۔

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā records:

It is not permissible for women to gather the hair and tie it in a bun on top of the head; a severe warning has come in the hadith about it, namely that such women will not so much as smell the fragrance of Paradise. Apart from this, other ways are permissible, provided that no man outside the forbidden degrees sees it and that there is no imitation of the disbelievers. The hair must be strictly covered. Tying a bun at the nape is permissible; indeed, it is better during prayer, because it makes it easier to keep the hair covered. (احسن الفتاویٰ: ۸/۷۵) ۔

واللہ اعلم ۔
