### TITLE
Giving a copy of the Qur'an to a non-Muslim

### QUESTION
May we give a non-Muslim a copy of the Noble Qur'an with a translation if he asks us for it? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question, giving a non-Muslim a translated copy of the Noble Qur'an is permissible and correct subject to the following two conditions: (1) there should be no fear of disrespect or dishonour on the part of the non-Muslim; (2) he should observe purity — if he is in a state of major impurity he should perform a bath, and if he has already bathed he should be told to perform ablution. Consider what Fatāwā Hindiyyah states:

قال أبو حنيفة رحمه الله: أعَلِّمُ النصراني الفقه والقرآنَ لعله يهتدي ولا يمس المصحف وإن اغتسل ثم مس لا بأس كذا في الملتقط. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳۲۳).

(وكذا في الأشباه والنظائر: ۱/۱٦۸، والدر المختار: ۱/۱۷۷، سعيد، والبحر الرائق: ۸/۲۰۳).

Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ states:

وروي عن أبي يوسف رحمه الله أنه لا يترك الكافر أن يمس المصحف لان الكافر نجس فيجب تنزيه المصحف عن مسه. وقال محمد: لا بأس به إذا اغتسل لان المانع هو الحدث وقد زال بالغسل وإنما بقي نجاسة اعتقاده وذلك في قلبه لا في يده. (بدائع الصنائع: ۱/۳۷، سعيد).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah records:

If the non-Muslim has reverence for the Glorious Qur'an in his heart, and one is satisfied on his part that he will not treat it with disrespect, then it is permissible to give him the Glorious Qur'an; it may be that he is granted guidance. He should, however, be instructed that this is the sacred speech of Allah, and that to touch it in a state of impurity is contrary to its reverence; so if he is in a state of major impurity he should study it after performing a bath, and otherwise after performing ablution. The method of ablution and of the bath should also be explained to him. Through this, reverence for the Glorious Qur'an will be created in his heart, if Allah wills. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: 2/151، دیوبند)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
