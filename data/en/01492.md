### TITLE
Annulment obtained after two divorces makes the woman irrevocably divorced

### QUESTION
A man gave his wife one divorce on each of two separate occasions and afterwards took her back. Later, for a number of reasons, the Jamʿiyyat annulled the marriage, since the husband would not pronounce divorce. The wife now wishes to enter into marriage with her former husband again. Is this permissible without ḥalālah or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, the Jamʿiyyat's annulment came about because of the husband's failings. For that reason this annulment carries the ruling of an irrevocable divorce and, taken together with the two earlier divorces, assumes the ruling of three divorces. It will therefore not be correct for the woman to enter into marriage with her former husband without ḥalālah (an intervening valid marriage to another man which is consummated and then comes to an end).

Mukhtaṣar al-Qudūrī states:

والفرقة تطليقة بائنة. (قدوري: ۲۱۹، كتاب النكاح).

Al-Hidāyah states:

لأن فعل القاضي أضيف إلى فعل الزوج فكأنه طلقها بنفسه. (الهداية: ۲/٤۲۱).

Radd al-Muḥtār states:

قوله فرق الحاكم وهو طلاق البائن. (فتاوى الشامي: ۳/۷۰، سعيد).

Al-Ḥīlat al-Nājizah states:

In those cases in which the judge gives the woman the option, the ruling is that separation can be effected only if the woman seeks it in that same sitting, and not otherwise. So if, when given the option, the woman states in that same sitting that she wishes to be separated from this husband, the judge should tell her husband to divorce her. If the husband thereupon pronounces divorce, an irrevocable divorce takes effect. If he refuses to pronounce it, the judge should himself effect the separation — by saying, for example, "I have separated you from his marriage" — and in the Sharīʿah this separation too stands in the place of an irrevocable divorce. (الحیلۃ الناجزۃ: ۸۲) ۔

In another place it states:

If a Sharīʿah council reaches agreement in a case and effects a separation, its decision stands in the place of a judge's decision, and the separation and the like are valid. (الحیلۃ الناجزۃ: ۷٤) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
