### TITLE
Prefixing Muḥammad to every name

### QUESTION
My name is Muḥammad Yaʿqūb and my son's name is Sulṭān Muḥammad. Some Arabs object to this, saying that a name ought to be a single name, whereas these have become two names. What is the answer to this? And is it correct to give a compound name of this kind or not?

### ANSWER
Among the non-Arabs it is customary to give compound names, in which one part is the name proper while the other is added as a title, to create a distinction and as a good omen. For instance, Muḥammad will be the name proper and Yaʿqūb will be for distinction, by way of a good omen: may Allah Most High grant that his righteous progeny continue. In the same way, in Sulṭān Muḥammad, Muḥammad is the name and Sulṭān is a title given as a good omen, that Allah may grant him ascendancy.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
