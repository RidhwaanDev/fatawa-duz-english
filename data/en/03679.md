### TITLE
The ruling on online drop-shipping trade

### QUESTION
The procedure of drop-shipping is that a person advertises the goods of a company by means of his website. When customers pay the price, this person sends the price and the buyer's order on to the company; the company delivers the item the buyer wants to his house and also pays the person who brought the customer his commission. Now the question is: is it permissible to take such a commission? Furthermore, it sometimes happens that the required item is not available at the company and it is also difficult to supply it within a suitable time; so will it be permissible to give him a coupon, or is it necessary to return his money?

### ANSWER
In the case in question this person, who is the intermediary between the company and the buyer, comes under the ruling of an agent and a broker, and it is permissible and correct for a broker to take a wage or commission. But if the required item is not found with the company, then this is the sale of something non-existent, which is void; therefore it is necessary to return the price to the buyer. Yes, if the buyer is content to take a coupon, then giving a coupon is also correct. Consider: it is recorded in Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ:

وأما الذي يرجع إلى المعقود عليه فأنواع منها: أن يكون موجوداً فلا ينعقد بيع المعدوم، وماله خطر العدم. (بدائع الصنائع: ۵/۱۳۸، سعيد).

Majallat al-Aḥkām al-ʿAdliyyah records:

المادة: بيع المعدوم باطل فيبطل بيع ثمرة لم تبرز أصلاً. (مجلة الأحكام ص ٤۲).

The wage of brokerage is permissible and correct. For the details, consider: (فتاوی دار العلوم زکریا: جلد، ۵) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
