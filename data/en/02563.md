### TITLE
Bone marrow transplantation

### QUESTION
What do the scholars of religion and the muftis of the firm Sharia say about the following matter:

Is a transplant of the marrow inside a bone permissible or not?

### ANSWER
The opinions of the scholars differ regarding benefiting from human organs and transplanting them. However, in a case of severe necessity there is room to act upon the view of those who permit it.
