### TITLE
The ruling on receiving the amount of the road fund with an increase

### QUESTION
After an accident on the road, a road fund is received from the government, and the High Court gives a decision for the payment of the amount. Along with this it is also stipulated that if it is not paid on time, the amount will be paid with a further two and a half per cent interest added to it. Now the question is that, after the order of the court, the road accident fund people pay the amount after six months and therefore also pay interest on it; so will it be permissible to take the additional amount or not?

2. The second question is that when zakāh is paid on this amount, which date will be taken into account: the date on which the court gave its decision, or the day on which the money is received? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
The amount that is received from the road accident fund, after the decision of the court, falls within the category of an entitlement; ownership of it is not established. Therefore whatever additional amount is given under the name of interest is an initial gratuity and is not interest, even though the government designates it as interest; taking it is permissible and correct.

2. The counting of the year will begin after the amount is received. The example of the road accident fund is the provident fund, which the scholars have counted as a weak debt — that is, zakāh should be paid on it after taking possession of it, and not before that.

Interest is that in which, in a financial exchange, an increase without any counter-value is stipulated as a condition. Consider how the jurists write the definition of interest:

قال في الهداية: الربا هو الفضل المستحق لاحد المتعاقدين في المعاوضة الخالي عن عوض شرط فيه. (الهداية: ۳/۷۸).

Al-ʿInāyah Sharḥ al-Hidāyah states:

الربا في اللغة هو الزيادة

وفي الاصطلاح هو الفضل الخالي عن العوض المشروط في البيع. (العناية: ۳/۷). (وكذا في الدر المختار: ۵/۱٦۸، سعيد).

Muʿjam Lughat al-Fuqahāʾ mentions:

الربا كل زيادة مشروطة في العقد خالية عن عوض مشروع. (معجم لغة الفقهاء، ص ۲۱۸). (وكذا في التعريفات الفقهية، ص ۱۰۲).

Muntakhabāt-e Niẓām al-Fatāwā records:

Whatever passages the jurists have transmitted concerning the meaning of ribā in the Sharīʿah, the upshot of them all is that there should be a transaction of an exchange contract in the goods subject to ribā, and that in that very contract there should be an increase without counter-value in favour of one of the parties — that is, in which the gain is stipulated as a condition in the contract of the transaction. (منتخباتِ نظام الفتاوی: ۱/۱۹۵) ۔

The upshot is that interest is that in which an increase without counter-value is stipulated as a condition in an exchange contract, whereas in the case before us it is not so; this is an initial gratuity, and taking it is permissible and correct. It is like the provident fund: zakāh is to be paid after it has been received, and not before that. The scholars have counted it as a weak debt.

Consider the ruling on a weak debt:

قال في فتح القدير: فنقول: قسم أبو حنيفة رحمه الله الدين إلى ثلاثة أقسام: قوي وهو بدل القرض ومال التجارة، ومتوسط: وهو بدل ما ليس للتجارة كثمن ثياب البذلة وعبد الخدمة ودار السكني، وضعيف: وهو بدل ما ليس بمال كالمهر والوصية وبدل الخلع والصلح عن دم العمد والدية وبدل الكتابة والسعاية

وفي الضعيف لا تجب ما لم يقبض نصاباً ويحول الحول بعد القبض عليه. (فتح القدير: ۲/۱٦۷، ط: دار الفكر).

Kifāyat al-Muftī records:

The amount which is given by the department on the provident fund, and likewise the amount which is added under the name of interest to the total of the two amounts — all this money is permissible. It is not interest in the Sharīʿah, even though the department designates it by the name of interest. And the ruling for paying zakāh on all these sums is that their zakāh should be paid upon receipt of the money; payment is not binding before it is received. (کفایت المفتی: ۸/۹۷، دار الاشاعت) ۔

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband mentions:

Some money is deducted from the salary of employees, and then, with some amount added to it, it is given to the employees at the time of the termination of their service. This is regarded as a government award. Zakāh for the past years is not obligatory upon it; for the future, after it has been received, when a year has passed over the niṣāb, at that time it will be binding to pay it. (فتاوی دار العلوم دیوبند: ٦/۳۳۱، ط: مدلل و مکمل)،

For further detail, kindly consult Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā, volume three, p. 151, under "Provident".
