### TITLE
The ruling on eating prawns

### QUESTION
I have heard different things about the prawn from different scholars. Some say that it is unlawful, some say that it is entirely lawful and wholesome, and most say that the prawn is disliked. My respectful request is that you point out some middle course of safety, and so earn a great reward.

### ANSWER
There is a difference of opinion among the muftis of the present age, and the real basis of the difference is whether or not it is a fish. Those who counted it among the kinds of fish gave a fatwa of lawfulness, and those who did not count it among the kinds of fish gave a fatwa that it is not lawful. Then the grounds on which it is or is not reckoned among the kinds of fish themselves differ: the lexicographers count it among the kinds of fish, while modern scientists exclude it from the definition of a fish, because zoologists have mentioned three things in the definition of a fish:

(1) A backbone. (2) Gills for breathing. (3) Fins for swimming.

And these things are not found in the prawn.

See what Takmilah Fatḥ al-Mulhim states:

لكن خبراء علم الحيوان اليوم لا يعتبرونه سمكاً ويذكرونه كنوع مستقل، ويقولون أنه من أسرة السرطان دون السمك، وتعريف السمك عند علماء الحيوان، على ما ذكر في دائرة المعارف البريطانية (۹/۳۰۵، ط: ۱۹۵۰ م): هو حيوان ذو عمود فقري، يعيش في الماء ويسبح بعواماته، ويتنفس بغلصمته". (تكملة فتح الملهم: ۳/۵۱۳، مسألة الروبيان).

With this definition in view, Muftī Rashīd Aḥmad Ludhyānwī Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) wrote a treatise arguing that it is not lawful. See: (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 391–399).

Keeping in view the statements of the lexicographers and common usage, Muftī ʿAbd al-Salām Chāṭgāmī Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) wrote a treatise on the lawfulness and permissibility of the prawn. See: (Jawāhir al-Fatāwā: 3/462–503, published by Islāmī Kutub Khānah, Karachi).

Besides these two, the muftis of the coastal regions have written that it is permissible, while the muftis of the non-coastal regions have written that it is impermissible, or that it is disliked. The texts of all of them are omitted here for fear of undue length.

In sum, in view of

"خير الأمور أوسطها"

and the leaving aside of doubtful matters, and

"دع ما يريبك إلى ما لا يريبك"

and in view of the disagreement among the muftis, scholars and students should refrain from eating prawns. Allah Most High has created countless things to eat; let them eat those. As for those, whether of the general public or of the learned, who do eat it, they should not be censured, and they should not be called eaters of the unlawful.

In Arabic, besides rūbiyān, it is also called saraṭān al-baḥr and jarād al-baḥr, which negates its being a fish. In al-Mawrid (903) it is given as barghūth al-baḥr, al-irbiyān, prawn. Moreover, the prawn has existed in the sea from the beginning and its shape is not the shape of a fish. Had it been lawful by common agreement, then — just as the jurists expressly stated the lawfulness of the jirrīth and the eel, so that any doubt of prohibition might be removed and no one might think it unlawful — they would likewise have made an express statement about the rūbiyān, so that any doubt about its prohibition might be removed. But they did not do so. There is therefore safety and well-being in keeping away from eating it.

The practice of the senior elders (may Allah have mercy on them) concerning the prawn:

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ḥusayn Aḥmad Madanī (may Allah have mercy on him) was once offered fried prawns by someone at a station while travelling. Ḥaḍrat asked, "Brother, what is this?" The man who had brought it said, "This is prawn fish." Ḥaḍrat Madanī (may Allah have mercy on him) said, "Brother, do fish have feet? This one has feet."

Ḥaḍrat Gangohī (may Allah have mercy on him) also wrote that the prawn is among the insects of dry land and is a water creature that is not a fish; and apart from fish, all water creatures are impermissible in the view of the Ḥanafīs. (Fatāwā Rashīdiyyah, p. 537).

Ḥaḍrat Muftī Kifāyatullāh Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) says: in my view it does not fall within the kinds of fish; nevertheless, because of the disagreement of the scholars, I do not care to be strict about it. (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 9/138, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

Ḥaḍrat Thānwī (may Allah have mercy on him), relying upon the researches of Damīrī the Shāfiʿī, said: at present this humble one is entirely at ease about its being a fish,

ولعل الله يحدث بعد ذلك أمراً

(Imdād al-Fatāwā: 4/104).

Concerning Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Khalīl Aḥmad Sahāranpūrī (may Allah have mercy on him), Mawlānā ʿĀshiq Ilāhī Ṣāḥib has written in Tadhkirat al-Khalīl that his opinion about the prawn was this: the name "fish" has come to be attached to it, but in reality it is not a fish, for it has no gills. He therefore regarded it as a water creature and would not give a fatwa of lawfulness. (Tadhkirat al-Khalīl, p. 293, Kutub Khānah Ishāʿat al-ʿUlūm).

In sum, this is a disputed question, and caution lies in avoiding eating it. But those who do eat it should not be treated with harshness and severity, nor should they be censured, because from the point of view of the sharia it is not unlawful, and there is room for eating it too.

See what Muftī Taqī Ṣāḥib says:

فمن أخذ بحقيقة الإربيان حسب علم الحيوان قال بمنع أكله عند الحنفية، ومن أخذ بعرف أهل العرب قال بجوازه، وربما يرجح هذا القول بأن المعهود من الشريعة في أمثال هذه المسائل الرجوع إلى العرف المتفاهم بين الناس، دون التدقيق في الأبحاث النظرية، فلا ينبغي التشديد في مسألة الاربيان عند الإفتاء، وإن اختلاف الفقهاء يورث التخفيف كما تقرر في محله، غير أن الاجتناب عن أكله أحوط وأولي وأحري، والله سبحانه وتعالى أعلم. (تكملة فتح الملهم: ۳/۵۱٤، مسألة الروبيان، وفقهي مقالات، جلد: ۳).
