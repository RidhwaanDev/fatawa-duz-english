### TITLE
The ruling on the loan money when the creditor is untraceable

### QUESTION
A person took a loan from another; it was a large sum, and now he wishes to return it, but there is no trace whatever of the creditor and contact is not possible by any means. So what should now be done with this money? Is it correct to give it to some charitable institution or not? How long should one wait? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question, when no clue is found even after searching and making inquiries about the creditor, it should be given to his Sharʿī heirs; but if, after waiting a long while, no trace of the heirs either is found, then it may be given to some reliable charitable institution — indeed, if it is distributed among the poor and the destitute that will be better. But this much should be borne in mind: that if the creditor comes and approves the ṣadaqah, then well and good; otherwise the entire amount will have to be returned to him, and the debtor will receive the reward of the ṣadaqah.

Consider: it is mentioned in Durr al-Mukhtār:

عليه ديون ومظالم جهل أربابها وأيس من عليه ذلك، من معرفتهم فعليه التصدق بقدرها من ماله

هذا مذهب أصحابنا. وفي رد المحتار: قوله جهل أربابها، يشمل ورثتهم فلو علمهم لزمه الدفع إليهم لأن الدين صار حقهم

وإن لم يجد المديون ولا وارثه صاحب الدين ولا وارثه فتصدق أو وارثه عن صاحب الدين بري في الآخرة. قوله كمن في يده عروض لا يعلم مستحقيها، يشمل ما إذا كانت لقطة علم حكمها، وإن كانت غيرها فالظاهر وجوب التصدق بأعيانها أيضاً، قوله سقط عنه المطالبة، كأنه والله تعالى أعلم لأنه بمنزلة المال الضائع والفقراء مصرفه عند جهل أربابه، وبالتوبة يسقط أثم الإقدام على الظلم. (فتاوى الشامي: ٤/۲۸۳، سعيد). وكذا في مجمع الأنهر في شرح ملتقى الأبحر: ۲/۵۳۱، ط: بيروت).

Fatāwā Dīniyyah records:

Those people whose loan is still outstanding have passed away, so it is necessary to search out their heirs and, after investigation, to convey this money to them, because the right to this money now belongs to them. If, even after much searching, no knowledge of their heirs is obtained, then if that money is given in ṣadaqah on behalf of the deceased to a masjid or among the poor, that will suffice; however, if after this the heirs become known, it will be necessary to pay them a second time. (فتاوی دینیہ: ٤/٤۹۲، مکتبہ راندیر) ۔

Ashraf al-Aḥkām records: The ruling of a found article is that when ascertaining the owner becomes impossible, it should be given in some good cause; accordingly, I give it to the madrasah. (اشرف الاحکام، ص ۱۹۷، بحوالہ حسن العزیز: ۳/٦) ۔
