### TITLE
Refraining from eating on Eid for one who is not offering a sacrifice

### QUESTION
If a person does not wish to offer a sacrifice at Eid al-Adha, is it recommended for him to eat nothing from the morning of the day of Eid, or not? And if on the day of Eid he eats something from someone else's sacrifice, will he have acted upon this recommendation or not?

### ANSWER
The scholars differ over the above question. Caution, however, lies in this: that for those who do not have the means to offer a sacrifice, or who have their sacrifice carried out somewhere else, refraining is better as well.

See what Fatāwā Shāmī states:

قوله ويندب تأخير أكله عنهما أي يندب الإمساك عما يفطر الصائم من صبحه إلى أن يصلي وإن لم يضح في الأصح

قوله في الأصح، وقيل لا يستحب التأخير في حق من لم يضح، بحر. (فتاوى الشامي: ۲/۱۷٦، سعيد).

وفي حاشية اللامع للشيخ زكريا رحمه الله: والتعليل ببداية الأكل من أضحيته يؤيد تقييد الندب بمن له أضحية والتعليل بموافقة المساكين أو اتباع فعله ﷺ أو إطلاق لفظ الصوم على هذا اليوم في بعض الروايات يؤيد العموم. (حاشية اللامع: ۲/۳۹).

Tabyīn al-Ḥaqāʾiq states:

(وهي أحكام الأضحى)

(لكن يؤخر الأكل عنها) لما روي أنه عليه الصلاة والسلام كان لا يطعم في يوم الأضحى حتى يرجع فيأكل من أضحيته. وقيل هذا في حق من يضحي ليأكل من أضحيته أولاً أما في حق غيره فلا. (تبيين الحقائق: ۱/۲۲٦، إمدادية، ملتان).

For further detail see:

(البحر الرائق: ۲/۱٦۳، ومجمع الأنهر: ۱/۱۷٤، التراث، وحاشية الطحطاوي على الدر المختار: ۱/۳۵۵، وحاشية الطحطاوي مراقي الفلاح، ص ۵۳٦، قديمي، وفتاوى محموديه: ۱۷/٤۸۵، فاروقيه، وأحسن الفتاوى: ۷/۵۲۰)

Some miscellaneous questions relating to the sacrifice

Question: What is the ruling on sacrificing an animal that has mange?

Answer: If the effect of the mange has not reached the flesh, its sacrifice is permissible; and the same detail applies in the case of a skin disease. (Ḥāshiyat al-Ṭaḥṭāwī ʿalā al-Durr: ٤/۱٦۵, Tabyīn al-Ḥaqāʾiq: ٦/۵, Imdādiyyah, Multān, Fatāwā Qāḍīkhān: ۳/۳۵۳, Fatāwā Bayyināt: ٤/۵۷۲).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: What is the ruling on slaughtering the sacrifice at night?

Answer: Offering the sacrifice on the night of the eleventh and the twelfth of Dhū al-Ḥijjah is permissible, but it is disliked because of the possibility that the veins may not be cut properly. If there is good electric light, then it will not be disliked. (Fatāwā al-Shāmī: ٦/۳۲۰, Saʿīd, Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ: ۵/٦۰, Saʿīd, ʿĀlamgīrī: ۵/۲۹٦).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: Is it correct to have someone else slaughter the sacrifice?

Answer: It is better to slaughter it oneself. If a man does not know how, or is not used to it, or has not the heart for it, he may have someone else slaughter it; but it is better for him to be present there. (al-Durr al-Mukhtār maʿa Radd al-Muḥtār: ٦/۳۲۸, Saʿīd, Tabyīn al-Ḥaqāʾiq: ٦/۹, Imdādiyyah, Multān, Takmilat al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq: ۸/۱۷۹, Quetta, ʿĀlamgīrī: ۵/۳۰۰).

If he remembers, it is better to recite the following supplication before slaughtering:

"إني وجهت وجهي للذي فطر السموات والأرض حنيفاً وما أناْ من المشركين".

"إن صلاتي ونسكي ومحياي ومماتي لله رب العلمين، لا شريك له وبذلك أمرت وأناْ من المسلمين". اللهم منك ولك.

Then he should say "بسم الله، والله أكبر" and slaughter, and after slaughtering he should read this supplication:

"اللهم تقبل مني كما تقبلت من حبيبك محمد وخليلك إبراهيم عليهما الصلاة والسلام".

For the evidences see the following books of hadith and jurisprudence: (Abū Dāwūd Sharīf, no. ۲۷۹۵, Ibn Mājah, no. ۳۱۲۱, Musnad Aḥmad, no. ۳/۳۷۵, Sunan al-Dārimī, no. ۱۹٤٦, Ṣaḥīḥ Ibn Khuzaymah, Muslim Sharīf: ۲/۱۵٦, ed. Qadīmī, Mustadrak Ḥākim, Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ: ۵/۷۹, Saʿīd, al-Jawharah al-Nayyirah: ۲/۲۸٦).

Question: A man bought an animal for sacrifice, or took a share in one, and then he died. What is the ruling on the animal?

Answer: If he died before the tenth of Dhū al-Ḥijjah, the sacrifice has lapsed for him. Now, if all the heirs wish, they may slaughter that animal to convey the reward to the deceased. (Tuḥfat al-Fuqahāʾ: ۳/۸۲, Fatāwā al-Shāmī: ٦/۳۲٦, Saʿīd, Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ: ۵/۷۲, Saʿīd, al-Mabsūṭ: ۱۲/۱۲).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: May the meat of the sacrifice be given to a non-Muslim or not?

Answer: It may be given, but giving it to poor Muslims is preferable and better. (Iʿlāʾ al-Sunan: ۱۷/۲۵۸, Ṭaḥṭāwī ʿalā al-Durr: ٤/۱٦٦, ʿĀlamgīrī: ۵/۳۰۰).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: What is the ruling if a man cooks the meat of the sacrifice and sells it?

Answer: To do this is not permissible at all, and if he has done it he must give its price away in charity. (Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ: ۵/۸۱, Saʿīd, Khulāṣat al-Fatāwā: ٤/۳۲۲, al-Jawharah al-Nayyirah: ۲/۲۸٦, Iʿlāʾ al-Sunan: ۱۷/۲۵۸).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: May the hide of the sacrifice be given to a wealthy person?

Answer: It may be given. (Hidāyah: ٤/٤۵۰, Fatāwā al-Shāmī: ٦/۲۳۷, Saʿīd, Fatāwā Bazzāziyyah: ٦/۲۹٤).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: If five men made the intention of sacrifice in a large animal, and one man kept two shares for an ʿaqīqah, is that permissible or not?

Answer: It is permissible. (Fatāwā al-Shāmī: ٦/۳۲٦, Saʿīd, Tabyīn al-Ḥaqāʾiq: ٦/۷, Imdādiyyah).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: Is it permissible to sacrifice an animal whose testicles have been cut off, or not?

Answer: This too is a kind of castrated animal, and its sacrifice is permissible. (Hidāyah: ٤/٤۴۸, Badāʾiʿ: ۵/۸۰, Saʿīd).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: If a ram or a male goat was born on the day of Eid al-Adha, may it be sacrificed at Eid al-Adha the following year or not?

Answer: If it was born on the tenth of Dhū al-Ḥijjah at sunset, then on the tenth of Dhū al-Ḥijjah its sacrifice is not permissible before the hour of its birth, and on the eleventh of Dhū al-Ḥijjah it may be sacrificed. In short, what counts is the completion of the year; the two teeth are not what counts. If, however, people are in the habit of lying, then out of caution the two teeth may be taken into account.

The upshot is that if an animal was born at nine in the morning on the tenth of Dhū al-Ḥijjah at Eid al-Adha, then after nine o'clock its sacrifice is permissible. (Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ: ۵/۷۰, Saʿīd, ʿĀlamgīrī: ۵/۲۹۷, Hidāyah: ٤/٤۴۹, Fatāwā Qāḍīkhān: ۳/۳۴۸, Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥal: ۵/٤۳۸).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: An animal's teeth have fallen out. Is its sacrifice permissible or not?

Answer: If all its teeth have fallen out and it cannot eat grass, it is impermissible; but if most of them are sound and it can eat fodder, the sacrifice is permissible. (Hidāyah: ٤/٤۴۸, Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ: ۵/۷۵, Saʿīd, Tabyīn al-Ḥaqāʾiq: ٦/٦, Imdādiyyah, Multān, Khulāṣat al-Fatāwā: ٤/۳۲۰, al-Fatāwā al-Bazzāziyyah: ٦/۲۹۳, Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: ۷/۵۱٤).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: If an animal's ears are completely cut off, is its sacrifice permissible or not?

Answer: If they are cut off right at the root, or a third or more has been cut off, the sacrifice is impermissible. (Abū Dāwūd Sharīf: ۲/۳۲, Ibn Mājah Sharīf, p. ۲۲۷, Hidāyah: ٤/٤۴۷, Mabsūṭ: ۱۲/۱۵, ʿĀlamgīrī: ۵/۲۹۸, Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ: ۵/۷۵, Saʿīd, al-Jawharah al-Nayyirah: ۲/۲۸٤).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: In a city or town the Eid prayer has been performed in one place, but the person offering the sacrifice has not yet performed it. Is his sacrifice permissible or not?

Answer: Once the prayer has been performed in one place, the sacrifice is permissible. (al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah: ۵/۲۹٦, al-Fatāwā al-Tātārkhāniyyah: ۱۷/٤۱۹, al-Muḥīṭ al-Burhānī: ٦/٤۷۵).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: In our village an accident occurred on the day of Eid al-Adha in which several people died, and because of it the Eid prayer could not be performed. Is the sacrifice permissible without the Eid prayer or not? It should be borne in mind that the prayer was not performed anywhere in the village.

Answer: On the day of Eid al-Adha the sacrifice may be offered after midday, and the prayer may be performed the next day; that is, once the time of the prayer has ended, the sacrifice may be offered. Moreover, in the case in question the sacrifice may also be offered on the second day before the prayer. Furthermore, in a small village where the Eid prayer is not necessary, the sacrifice may be offered on the tenth of Dhū al-Ḥijjah as soon as dawn breaks. (Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ: ۵/۷۳, Saʿīd, Tabyīn al-Ḥaqāʾiq: ٦/٤, Imdādiyyah, Fatāwā Qāḍīkhān: ۳/۳۴۵, Fatāwā al-Shāmī: ٦/۳۱۸, Saʿīd).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: If two or three men are partners in a large animal, is the sacrifice correct or not?

Answer: It is correct, because no one's share is less than a seventh. If a share is less than a seventh it is not permissible. For example, a man died leaving three sons and a wife, and the only thing in the estate is a cow. If they all made the intention of sacrifice in the jointly owned cow, it would not be sound, because the widow's share in it is an eighth. (Fatāwā al-Shāmī: ٦/۳۱٦, Saʿīd, Fatāwā Qāḍīkhān ʿalā hāmish al-Hindiyyah: ۳/۳۵۱, Ḥāshiyat al-Ṭaḥṭāwī ʿalā al-Durr al-Mukhtār: ٤/۱٦۱, Khulāṣat al-Fatāwā: ٤/۳۱۵, Sharḥ al-ʿInāyah ʿalā hāmish Takmilat Fatḥ al-Qadīr: ۹/۵۱۰, Dār al-Fikr).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: Is it permissible to sacrifice an animal that has a tumour or not?

Answer: It is permissible. (Fatāwā Qāḍīkhān ʿalā hāmish al-Hindiyyah: ۳/۳۵۳, Fatāwā al-Shāmī: ٦/۳۲۳, Saʿīd, Tabyīn al-Ḥaqāʾiq: ٦/۵, ed. Imdādiyyah, Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: ۵/٤۰۹).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: What is the ruling if an animal has no tongue, or more than a third of it has been cut off?

Answer: It is not permissible, because a cow takes its fodder with its tongue. In a goat this is not a defect, because it takes its fodder with its teeth. (Khulāṣat al-Fatāwā: ٤/۳۲۰, Ḥāshiyat al-Ṭaḥṭāwī ʿalā al-Durr al-Mukhtār: ٤/۱٦۵, Fatāwā Hindiyyah: ۵/۲۹۸, Fatāwā Tātārkhāniyyah: ۱۷/ ٤۲۸, Maktabah Zakariyyā Deoband).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: Seven men bought seven rams and each slaughtered one without putting any mark on it. Has the sacrifice been fulfilled or not?

Answer: Yes, the sacrifice of all of them has been fulfilled. (al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah: ۵/۳۰٦, Imdād al-Fatāwā: ۳/۵٦۲).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: A man bought an animal with the intention of sacrifice and afterwards, for some reason, sold it and bought another. Is this permissible or not?

Answer: There is room for it; but if the second animal is of lesser value, he should give the difference in price away in charity. (Fatāwā al-Shāmī: ٦/۳۲٦, Saʿīd, Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ: ۵/۷۸, Saʿīd, Takmilat Fatḥ al-Qadīr: ۹/۵۲۰, Dār al-Fikr, Fatāwā Qāḍīkhān ʿalā hāmish al-Hindiyyah: ۳/۳٤۷, Tabyīn al-Ḥaqāʾiq: ٦/۱۰, Imdādiyyah).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: A deceased person made a bequest for a sacrifice, and the heirs carried out the bequest and offered the sacrifice out of a third of the deceased's wealth. What is now the ruling on the meat?

Answer: It is necessary to give the whole of the meat away in charity. (Sharḥ Manẓūmat Ibn Wahbān: ۲/۱٤۸, Fatāwā Shāmī: ٦/۳۳۵, Saʿīd, Iʿlāʾ al-Sunan: ۱۷/۲٦۹, Khulāṣat al-Fatāwā: ٤/۳۲۲).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: If a sacrifice was offered to convey the reward to one deceased person or to several, what is the ruling on the meat of that sacrifice?

Answer: The meat of this sacrifice does not have to be given away in charity. He may eat it himself or give it to others; that is, its ruling is like that of the sacrifice of one's own animal. (Iʿlāʾ al-Sunan: ۱۷/۲٦۹, Fatāwā al-Shāmī: ٦/۳۳۵, Saʿīd, Fatāwā Qāḍīkhān ʿalā hāmish al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah: ۳/۳۵۲, Khulāṣat al-Fatāwā: ٤/۳۲۲).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: If a man sacrificed a whole cow, is one share the necessary sacrifice and the rest voluntary, or is the whole cow counted as the necessary sacrifice?

Answer: The whole cow is counted as the necessary sacrifice. The reward of what is necessary and obligatory is greater than that of what is voluntary, so he will receive greater reward. (al-Durr al-Mukhtār maʿa Radd al-Muḥtār: ٦/۳۳۳, Saʿīd, Ḥāshiyat al-Ṭaḥṭāwī ʿalā al-Durr al-Mukhtār: ٤/۱٦۸, Fatāwā Qāḍīkhān ʿalā hāmish al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah: ۳/۳۵۰).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: A man has not offered a sacrifice for several years, although he was wealthy. How should he now make up for it?

Answer: He should give the price of the sacrifices of the past years, and repent sincerely from the heart. (Fatāwā al-Shāmī: ٦/ ۳۲۰, Saʿīd, Tabyīn al-Ḥaqāʾiq: ٦/۵, Imdādiyyah, Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ: ۵/٦۸, Saʿīd).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: A man drew off the milk of his sacrificial animal after buying it. What should he do with it?

Answer: He should give it away in charity; and if he sheared the wool or hair, the same ruling applies to that. However, if he buys the grass and other fodder himself and feeds it to the animal, then there is room for him to use it. (Khulāṣat al-Fatāwā: ٤/۳۲۱, Tabyīn al-Ḥaqāʾiq: ٦/۹, Fatāwā al-Shāmī: ٦/ ۳۲۲, Saʿīd, Fatāwā Qāḍīkhān ʿalā hāmish al-Hindiyyah: ۳/۳۵٤).
