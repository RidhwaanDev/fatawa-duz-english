### TITLE
Cosmetic surgery on the body

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to have surgery done to beautify parts of the human body, such as the body itself or the face, or not? This surgery also covers defects that arise from an accident, from birth, or from old age. Is it permissible to use a laser to soften the skin? And if a breast has been removed because of cancer, what is the ruling on grafting flesh and muscle or putting something else in its place? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
Alterations to the human body which in one's own estimation are merely a matter of beautification are not correct. Removing defects, however, is permissible. For example, it is permissible to have a tooth fitted in place of a broken one, since this is the removal of a defect; and in the same way it is correct to straighten protruding teeth, and to have an artificial nose or ear fitted so that the defect is removed. But creating a gap between the teeth is not correct, because that is beauty only in the person's own imagination — an imagined beauty, one might call it, with no basis in reality.

Consider the following. Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

The view of Islam is that the body is a trust from Allah and its form is a manifestation of Allah's creating, so that no self-devised alteration of it is correct without some need recognised by the sharia or by nature. It is for this reason that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ declared the fitting of artificial hair, and creating a gap between the teeth for the sake of beauty, to be impermissible, deserving of a curse and an alteration of Allah's creation. It is therefore clear that an operation of this kind, or any alteration of the body, purely for the sake of adornment and fashion, is certainly not correct — as is done nowadays with the nose, the breasts and so on.

A hadith accordingly states:

لعن الله الواشمات والمستوشمات والمتنمصات. (صحيح البخاري).

The curse of Allah is upon women who tattoo and women who have themselves tattooed, and women who pluck out hair.

It is also narrated from Abū Rayḥānah (may Allah be pleased with him):

أن رسول الله ﷺ حرم الوشر. (سنن النسائي: ۲/۲۸۱).

The Prophet ﷺ forbade filing the teeth to sharpen them.

Yes, if a person has an extra limb contrary to normal nature — for example, six fingers instead of five — it may be removed by an operation:

إذا أراد الرجل أن يقطع أصبعاً زائدة أو شيئاً آخر قال نصير رحمه الله: إن كان الغالب على من قطع مثل ذلك الهلاك فإنه لا يفعل وإن كان الغالب هو النجاة فهو في سعة من ذلك. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳٦۰).

(جدید فقہی مسائل: ۱/۳۱۲)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
