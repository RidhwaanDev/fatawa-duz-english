### TITLE
Paying zakat with banknotes

### QUESTION
Is zakat discharged when it is paid with banknotes or not? Some people say that zakat is not discharged until the notes have been exchanged for gold or silver. Is that correct?

### ANSWER
In earlier times there was some difference of opinion among the scholars. Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ashraf ʿAlī Thānawī (may Allah have mercy on him), Ḥaḍrat Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ (may Allah have mercy on him) and many other scholars held that a banknote is no more than a document, having the status of a certificate of debt.

In the present age, however, there is virtual agreement that the note has now taken on the character of value in its own right — that is, the note is itself wealth and a price, not merely a certificate or document. Zakat is therefore due on notes; and if a person holds the nisab, paying zakat with them is valid too. The zakat is discharged the moment the poor person is made owner of them.

Wahbah al-Zuḥaylī, a researcher of the modern age, says:

والحق وجوب الزكاة فيها (الأوراق النقدية) لأنها أصبحت هي أثمان الأشياء، وامتنع التعامل بالذهب. (الفقه الإسلامي وأدلته: ۲/۷۷۲، زكاة الأوراق النقدية، دار الفكر).

An article of the same kind has appeared in the monthly al-ʿAṣr, published from Peshawar. Consider the following:

Banknotes have themselves become a customary medium of price, so there is no doubt about paying zakat by means of notes. Indeed, it is regarded as paid at once, and this is the stronger view.

It further states:

In the present age it has become an obvious fact that paper notes have become a customary medium of price, for human society has accepted them as the means of exchange and as the measure of purchasing power. Otherwise, in themselves, they are nothing more than scraps of paper. (ماہنامہ "العصرــ" جامعہ عثمانیہ پش اور ، ص: ۳۰ ۔ ۳۱، ستمبر۲۰۰۷؁ء شعبان ۱٤۲۸؁هـ).
