### TITLE
The ruling on a non-Muslim making a bequest of half his wealth to his Muslim friend

### QUESTION
A Muslim had a friendship with a non-Muslim. Before dying, the disbelieving friend wrote a bequest of half his wealth to his Muslim friend. According to the law of the government, the Muslim is entitled to this half of the wealth; but some scholars told him that the same law applies to a non-Muslim as applies to a Muslim. Al-Hidāyah states:

ولو أوصي الذمي بأكثر من الثلث أو لبعض ورثته لا يجوز اعتباراً بالمسلمين لأنهم التزموا أحكام الإسلام فيما يرجع إلى المعاملات، الخ. (الهداية: ٤/٦۹۰)

Now the matter to be asked about is this: is it correct for the Muslim friend to take half the wealth of the disbeliever or not, given that legally he can take it? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question, since Islamic rulings are not in force in our country, the non-Muslim government has nothing to do with the text of Al-Hidāyah. The meaning of the text of Al-Hidāyah is that in the abode of Islam, where Islamic rulings are in force, the non-Muslims there have bound themselves to Islamic law, and on that basis they will be subject to Islamic law, and a Muslim judge will not enforce a bequest of more than one third. But the present situation is such that it is neither the abode of Islam nor is there any enforcement of Islamic rulings. Therefore, according to the law of the non-Muslim government, the Muslim may take half the wealth of his disbelieving friend. It is as though the government first took the disbeliever's wealth into its own custody and then, on its own part, made the Muslim its owner in accordance with the bequest; so by the force of the law the Muslim has become the owner of the non-Muslim's wealth.

The jurists have stated separate rulings for the enemy alien under safe conduct and for the protected non-Muslim subject. Note what Al-Hidāyah states:

وإذا دخل الحربي دارنا بأمان فأوصي لمسلم أو ذمي بماله كله جاز لأن امتناع الوصية بما زاد على الثلث لحق الورثة ولهذا ينفذ بإجازتهم وليس لورثته حق مرعي لكونهم في دار الحرب إذ هم أموات في حقنا

ولو أعتق عبده عند الموت أو دبر عبده في دار الإسلام فذلك صحيح منه من غير اعتبار الثلث لما بينا. (الهداية: ٤/٦۹۰، باب وصية الذمي، ط: شركت علمية).

After this the author of Al-Hidāyah (may Allah have mercy on him) stated the question of the bequest of a protected non-Muslim subject, which is mentioned in the question, and he wrote its reason as follows:

لأنهم التزموا أحكام الإسلام،

A non-Muslim under treaty and a protected subject who is resident in the abode of Islam has bound himself to Islamic rulings in his dealings; for this reason judgment will be given in accordance with the Islamic law of inheritance.

But in our time, in this country, there is neither Islamic law nor any enforcement of Islamic rulings; with regard to religion everyone is free, which in modern terminology is expressed as the abode of safety.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
