### TITLE
Sacrifice after a defect has disappeared

### QUESTION
If an animal was defective at the time of purchase and its defect then disappeared, is its sacrifice valid for a wealthy person or not?

### ANSWER
Once the defect has disappeared, the sacrifice of that animal will be valid even for a wealthy person. (Fatāwā ʿĀlamgīriyyah: 5/298; and Fatāwā Qāḍīkhān on the margin of al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah: 3/353).
