### TITLE
Applying henna on the occasion of a wedding

### QUESTION
What is the ruling on applying henna on the occasion of a wedding? Moreover, customs and conventions are adhered to in it, women gather together to apply it, and there is dancing, singing and music as well. What is the ruling on this in the sharīʿah?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, it is recommended for women to apply henna individually on the occasion of a wedding or other happy event. But adhering to customs and conventions, and evils such as singing and music, are impermissible, and it is necessary to abandon all of them.

Consider what Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states: It is recommended for women to apply henna; but the henna custom of today, in which a great gathering of other women also assembles, is a combination of several evils, so it is necessary to avoid it. Women may apply henna on their own. (احسن الفتاوی: ۸/۱٦۰)۔

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

It is correct for women to apply henna — indeed it is something particular to them that they apply it to their hands and feet. It is not correct for men to adopt a resemblance to them. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۱/۲۱٤، مبوب و مرتب)۔

Ḥalāl o Ḥarām states:

Since greater latitude has been allowed to women in the matter of adornment and beautification, they may also apply henna, both to their hands and to their feet. (حلال و حرام: ۲۰۹، از مولانا خالد سیف اللہ صاحب)۔
