### TITLE
Whether flying over one's own city by aeroplane makes one a resident

### QUESTION
If a person travelled by aeroplane from Cape Town to Johannesburg, and this person is a resident of Johannesburg, and the aeroplane flew over Johannesburg and then landed at the airport, has this person become a resident by flying over his own city, or is he still a traveller? Explain and be rewarded.

### ANSWER
It appears from the statements of the jurists that travel status is cancelled by entering one's own city, and flying over a city is not called entering it. For instance, if a person flies over the city of Durban, it will not be said that so-and-so entered Durban; rather this is called entering its airspace. Furthermore, the jurists call passing over a miqāt, or passing level with a miqāt, being "in line with" it (muḥādhāt); they do not call it entering the miqāt. In the same way, passing overhead in an aeroplane is being in line with a city, not entering it — and travel status is cancelled only by entering one's own city. See what the jurists write, that entering the city cancels travel status:

قال في الخلاصة: وإنما يصير المسافر مقيماً إما بدخوله مصراً له فيه أهل أو بأن بدا له العود إليه بعد ما خرج

وسواء دخل مصره بنية الاختيار أو لقضاء حاجته والخروج بعد ذلك صار مقيماً حين دخلها. (خلاصة الفتاوى: ۱/۱۹۹). (وكذا في بدائع الصنائع: ۱/۱۰۳، سعيد، والفتاوى التاتارخانية: ۲/۵۱۳، والفتاوى السراجية، ص ۷۹).

Consider these statements of the jurists regarding being in line with the miqāt:

قال في الشامية: أنه لا يتصور عدم المحاذاة، أي لأن المواقيت تعم جهات مكة كلها فلا بد من محاذاة أحدها. (فتاوى الشامي: ۲/٤۷٦، سعيد).
قال في لباب المناسك مع شرحه: وأعيان هذه، أي المواقيت فقط (ليس بشرط) ولهذا يصح الإحرام قبلها، بل الواجب عينها أو حذوها، أي محاذاتها ومقابلتها. (لباب المناسك مع شرحه وإرشاد الساري، ص ۹۰، ط: بيروت).

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil records:
As for where one should enter the state of consecration when travelling by aeroplane, this too should be judged by the same analogy. The better course is to put on the garments of consecration and begin the journey in them. It is also permissible, however, to enter the state of consecration during the journey, but this must be done by the last point that comes level with one of the stations of consecration, after which there will be no further place that comes level with a station. And if one passes directly over a station itself, and the journey after that station is towards the sacred precinct, then it will be necessary to enter the state of consecration right there. (جدید فقہی مسائل: ۱/۱۳۸) ۔
