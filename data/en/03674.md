### TITLE
The ruling on obtaining a club card and benefiting from it

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to obtain a club card and to benefit from it, or not? A club card too is like a points card, which is obtained from a particular shop, Klicks, and points are received on making purchases from that shop. Kindly inform us of the Sharʿī ruling and thereby deserve a great reward.

### ANSWER
Obtaining a club card and benefiting from it is permissible and correct. It comes under the ruling of a conditional gratuity — that is, the shopkeeper has attached the condition of purchase to the gift, and this is permissible so long as the goods are being sold at the ordinary price and a higher price is not being charged on account of the gift. Some proofs concerning a conditional gratuity have been mentioned earlier. Consider some further material: Imdād al-Aḥkām states:

It was asked whether it is permissible in the Sharīʿah or not that, under the agreement mentioned, the purchaser who keeps buying goods continuously throughout the year should be given a concession of, say, two or three rupees per hundred at the completion of the year, while one who buys even once during the year from another place should be given nothing at all.

الجواب: This agreement is permissible, because the commission per hundred which is given to the purchaser at the completion of the year is a gratuity on the part of the seller and is not a binding right of the purchaser; and to make a gratuity conditional upon some condition is permissible. (امداد الاحکام: ۳/ ٦۰٦) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
