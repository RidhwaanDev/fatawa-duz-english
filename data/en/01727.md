### TITLE
A vow conditional on having a child when the child is born of adultery

### QUESTION
A married woman made a vow that if she had a child she would perform Umrah with the child. The doctors' finding, however, was that she could not have a child by her husband. She then committed adultery, and a child was born to her as a result of it. Is this woman now required to perform Umrah or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question the woman must fulfil her vow by performing Umrah, because she made her vow conditional on having a child; a child was born, and the child's lineage is established. A hadith states:

"الولد للفراش وللعاهر الحجر"

So once the condition on which the vow was suspended has come about, fulfilling the vow becomes binding. The woman has, of course, sinned by this shameful act, and she must repent of it.

Consider what al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

ثم إن المعلق فيه تفصيل، فإن علقه بشرط يريده كإن قدم غائبي أو شفي مريضي، يوفي وجوبا إن وجد الشرط. وفي الشامي: (قوله: إن المعلق) اعلم أن المذكور في كتب ظاهر الرواية أن المعلق يجب الوفاء به مطلقاً: أي سواء كان الشرط مما يريد كونه أي يطلب حصوله كإن شفي الله مريضي أو لا كإن كلمت زيداً. (الدر المختار مع فتاوى الشامي: ۳/۷۳۸ أحكام النذر، سعيد).
