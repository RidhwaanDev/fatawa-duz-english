### TITLE
The ruling on returning goods on their expiry (Expire) and recovering the money from the company

### QUESTION
A man has a drinks shop; he buys drinks from a company and sells them. When new drinks arrive at the company, the company gives the shopkeeper two thousand bottles: one thousand for a price and one thousand free. Then, upon their expiry (Expire), the company takes the drinks back and returns the price. Recently the company gave him two thousand bottles, one thousand for a price and one thousand free; then, because of their expiry (Expire), the shopkeeper returned 1,500, and the company returned the price of 1,500. So is it permissible to take the price of the five hundred which were free, or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question the company's giving of one thousand drinks free will be counted as an initial gratuity and gift, and the owner has also taken possession of them; therefore these became the shopkeeper's property. Upon their expiry (Expire) the shopkeeper returned 1,500; so with regard to one thousand a rescission took place, which is at the original price and is therefore sound and correct. As for the price of the remaining five hundred which the company paid, this too is of the category of a gift and a gratuity, because to all appearances the company knew beforehand that it had given one thousand free and even so paid their price. This is therefore a gratuity and a gift, and it is permissible and correct to receive this gift. However, if the company does not know, then deception should not be resorted to; rather it should be told. Consider: it is in Muslim Sharīf:

عن أبي هريرة رضي الله عنه أن رسول الله ﷺ قال: من حمل السلاح علينا فليس منا ومن غشنا فليس منا. (رواه مسلم، رقم: ۱۰۱).

Al-Lubāb fī Sharḥ al-Kitāb mentions:

الهبة لغة: التبرع والتفضل بما ينفع الموهوب مطلقاً، وشرعاً تمليك عين بلا عوض. (اللباب في شرح الكتاب: ۱/۲۱۹).

Fatḥ al-Qadīr states:

الهبة في الشريعة فهي تمليك المال بلا عوض. (فتح القدير: ۱۹/٤۵۷).

The company's making a conditional gift to its purchaser is of the category of a conditional gratuity, and this is permissible. The proofs for it have already been mentioned earlier and may be consulted there. And with regard to the one thousand bottles there is a rescission, and a rescission is correct with the agreement of both parties at the original price. Consider: it is recorded in Al-Hidāyah:

الإقالة جائز ة في البيع بثمل الأول، لقول عليه الصلاة والسلام: من أقال نادماً بيعته أقال الله عثرته يوم القيامة، ولأن العقد حقهما فيملكانه رفعه دفعاً لحاجتهما، فإن شرطا أكثر منه أو أقل فالشرط باطل ويرد مثل الثمن الأول. (الهداية: ۳/۱۰۲، ط: بشري).

Fatāwā ʿĀlamgīrī records:

وشرط صحة الإقالة رضاء المتقائلين. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۳/۱۵۷).

قال في شرح المجلة: للعاقدين أن يتقايلا البيع برضاهما بعد انعقاد فالرضاء شرط في الإقالة كما في سائر العقود وهي لا تصح إلا من العاقدين أو ورثتهما. (شرح المجلة لسليم رستم باز: ۱/۹۲). وكذا في الدر المختار: ۵/۱۲۱، سعيد).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
