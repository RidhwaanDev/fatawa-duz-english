### TITLE
Spending donations collected for one madrasah on another madrasah

### QUESTION
A man collected a donation from someone for a particular madrasah. Now another madrasah is in need. May he spend that donation on the other madrasah or not? Note that no receipt was issued.

### ANSWER
If the collector stated explicitly at the time of collecting that he was collecting for a particular madrasah, then, for as long as that madrasah needs the donation, or will need it in the near future, it is not correct to give it to another madrasah. If, however, that madrasah has no need of the money at present and will have no need of it in the near future either, while another madrasah is in severe need, then in that case some jurists have permitted it to be spent on the other madrasah. The safer course, though, is that if the donor can be contacted, his permission should be obtained and the money then spent on the other madrasah.

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

The money must be spent on the very purpose for which it was given. If it is spent elsewhere, liability to make it good arises, because the trustee is a trustee and an agent and has no right to spend it contrary to the donor's express instruction. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۵/٤۷٤، فاروقیہ) ۔

It is also written there that if the donors agree to it and have no objection, then acting in this way is permissible in the sharia. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۵/٤۷٤، فاروقیہ) ۔

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states:

As for those who have given their property or cash to a mosque: if at the time of the endowment they laid down the condition that whatever money is surplus to the mosque's expenses should be spent on some Islamic madrasah or on some other charitable purpose, then this surplus money may be spent on a madrasah, even if the endowers are not willing for it at that time. Likewise, if the endowers stipulated at the time of the endowment that they would retain the right to change the purpose to which that property and money are put, then too the endowers may at their discretion spend it elsewhere, whether on a madrasah or on something else. But if neither of these two conditions was made at the time of the endowment, then it is in no way permissible to spend the mosque's surplus money on a madrasah. If some other mosque is in need of donations, however, a ruling of permissibility may be given on account of the severity of the need, علی رأی المتأخرین — and that only when the mosque whose money it is has no need of it at the time, no such need is to be expected of it in the foreseeable future, and there is a danger that the surplus money that has accumulated will go to waste. (امداد المفتین: ۲/۷٦۸، دار الاشاعت) ۔
