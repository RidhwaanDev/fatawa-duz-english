### TITLE
Ruling where the spouses differ over whether the ṭalāq was conditional or immediate

### QUESTION
A quarrel and disagreement arose between a husband and wife, and in the course of it the husband said, "When I leave here shortly, I will leave you with two ṭalāqs when I go." The wife's account is that his words were, "I am leaving you with two ṭalāqs." The husband then set out on a journey four or five days later. What has to be determined now is whose account is to be accepted, and whether a ṭalāq has taken effect or not.

### ANSWER
In the case in question the husband claims a suspended ṭalāq while the woman states that it was immediate. It is therefore for the woman to produce two witnesses; if she cannot produce two witnesses, the husband's word will be accepted on oath. If the woman does produce witnesses, two revocable ṭalāqs have taken effect, and the husband has the right to take her back before the ʿiddah ends, while after the ʿiddah he may contract the marriage afresh with the woman's consent, without any ḥalālah. If the woman is unable to produce witnesses, the husband's word will be accepted on oath and the ṭalāq will be treated as suspended; and in the case of such a suspension no ṭalāq has taken effect, because the husband said that he was leaving at once, whereas he did not leave then but set out four or five days later.

Radd al-Muḥtār states:

(فإن اختلفا في وجود الشرط فالقول له مع اليمين إلا إذا برهنت) قوله في وجود الشرط أي أصلاً أو تحققاً كما في شرح المجمع: أي اختلفا في وجود أصل التعليق بالشرط، أوفي تحقق الشرط بعد التعليق، وفي البزازية: ادعي الاستثناء أو الشرط فالقول له، ثم قال: وذكر النسفي: ادعي الزوج الاستثناء وأنكرت فالقول لها ولا يصدق بلا بينة، وإن ادعي تعليق الطلاق بالشرط وادعت الإرسال فالقول له. (فتاوى الشامي: ۳/۳۵٦، مطلب اختلاف الزوجين في وجود الشرط، سعيد).

Al-Hidāyah states:

وإن اختلفا في الشرط فالقول قول الزوج إلا أن تقيم المرأة البينة لأنه متمسك بالأصل وهو عدم الشرط ولأنه منكر وقوع الطلاق وزوال الملك والمرأة تدعيه. (الهداية: ۲/۳۸٦).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states: In a dispute over the suspension of a ṭalāq and over the fulfilment of the condition, the burden of proof lies on the wife; otherwise the husband's word will be accepted on oath. (احسن الفتاوی: ۵/۱۹٦) ۔
