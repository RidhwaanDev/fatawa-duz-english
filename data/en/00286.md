### TITLE
The position of Imam Muḥammad on a question of menstruation

### QUESTION
A woman bled for two days, then the bleeding stopped for ten days, and then on the eleventh day it began again; this is counted as continuous bleeding. Al-Hidāyah states: إذا كان اقل من خمسة عشر يوماً لا يفصل وهو كله كالدم المتوالي لا نه طهر فاسد.

Women sometimes face this situation during the Hajj and in Ramadan, and it causes them great difficulty and hardship. Is there any solution to it in the books of fiqh?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, according to Imam Muḥammad (may Allah have mercy on him) the two days are irregular bleeding and the ten days are purity. As for the bleeding that followed, if it continued for three days it is menstruation, and if it was less than three days it is irregular bleeding. Some jurists have issued rulings on the basis of Imam Muḥammad's view.

وفي الوجيز: "الفتوي على قول محمد"

Because of the difficulties that arise in fasting and in the Hajj, a ruling may be given on the basis of Imam Muḥammad's view.

The report of Imam Ḥasan ibn Ziyād (may Allah have mercy on him), which he took from Imam Abū Ḥanīfah (may Allah have mercy on him), states that when a purity of three days comes between two bleedings, it counts as genuine purity. In his view it is not even necessary for the days of bleeding to be greater than the days of purity or equal to them.

كما في شرح النقاية للملا علي قاري رحمه الله: وقد روى الحسن بن زياد عن أبي حنيفة رحمه الله أن الطهر المتخلل بين الدمين إذا كان دون ثلاثة أيام لا يصير فاصلاً (شرح النقاية ۱/۸۳).

ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) has written, in the discussion of the colours of menstrual blood, that for the sake of ease a ruling may be given on the basis of any of the views mentioned.

وفي المعراج عن فخر الأئمة: لو افتي مفتٍ بشيء من هذه الأقوال في مواضع الضرورة طلباً للتيسير كان حسناً. (فتاوى الشامي: ۱/۲۸۹).

In my view, therefore, the muftis should also give thought to the position of Ḥasan ibn Ziyād (may Allah have mercy on him).
