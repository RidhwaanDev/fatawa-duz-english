### TITLE
The meaning of the hadith "لا عدوى ولا طيرة ولا هامة ولا صفر"

### QUESTION
In the well-known hadith "لا عدوي ولا طيرة ولا هامّة ولا صفر" (صحيح البخاري: ۲/۸۵۰، مع فتح الباري: ۱۰/۱۹۵) I cannot understand what لا عدوي means, since it is certain, or at least highly likely, that some diseases are contagious, and the Prophet ﷺ himself said "فرّ من المجذوم فرارك من الأسد". (صحيح البخاري: ۲/۸۵۰، مع فتح الباري: ۱۰ /۱۹۵).

### ANSWER
The commentators of hadith have given many answers to this. Our teacher Abū Ghuddah, in his notes on al-Mawḍūʿāt al-Ṣaghīr, has also given it the meaning "do not pass disease on to others", in the way that ﴿لا رفث ولا فسوق﴾ means "do not speak obscenely and do not disobey". But this meaning does not seem convincing, because after the words لا عدوي a Companion asked about a mangy camel that often makes other camels mangy, and the Prophet ﷺ did not answer, "You have missed the point; it means do not bring a sick camel near a healthy one." He said instead: who made the first camel sick and mangy?

In any case, the simple meaning of لا عدوی is this. If a disease is only doubtfully or probably contagious, then its passing on is not certain. And if its passing on is certain, then what is denied is that the effect belongs to the disease itself — that is, if a disease does spread, it does not carry through the air any power of its own to infect; rather Allah has placed that effect in it. It is like ﴿وما رميت إذ رميت﴾, which means that when you threw, you were not the one who placed the effect in the throw; Allah placed it there. For whenever the working of something is not outwardly intelligible, it is ascribed to Allah.

واللہ اعلم
