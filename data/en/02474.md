### TITLE
The period within which the ʿaqīqah may be performed

### QUESTION
If the ʿaqīqah could not be performed on the seventh day, until when may it be performed? What is its period?

### ANSWER
Performing the ʿaqīqah on the seventh day is a sunnah and recommended. If it cannot be done on the seventh day, then the ʿaqīqah should be performed on the fourteenth or the twenty-first day; and in the same way it is better, right up until puberty, to observe the number seven. If one acts contrary to this, the recommended manner will not be discharged, although the ʿaqīqah itself will be fulfilled. The ʿaqīqah is a sunnah and a recommended act, and it is therefore commendable to discharge it in the recommended manner.

Consider what the noble hadith states:

عن سمرة رضي الله عنه قال: قال رسول الله ﷺ: الغلام مرتهن بعقيقته يذبح عنه يوم السابع، ويسمي ويحلق رأسه. (رواه الترمذي: ۱/۲۷۸، وقال: هذا حديث حسن صحيح، والعمل على هذا عند أهل العلم؛ يستحبون ان يذبح عن الغلام العقيقة يوم السابع، فان لم يتهيأ يوم السابع فيوم الرابع عشر، فان لم يتهيأ عق عنه يوم حاد وعشرين).
(وأيضا رواه أبو داود: ۲/۳٦، والبيهقي: ۹/۳۰۳).

In some narrations mention is made of the seventh, the fourteenth and the twenty-first day, although there is some discussion regarding that narration. Consider what al-Bayhaqī states:

عن عبد الله بن بريدة عن أبيه عن النبي ﷺ قال: العقيقة تذبح لسبع ولأربع عشرة ولإحدى وعشرين. (رواه البيهقي في السنن الكبري: ۹/ ۳۰۳، باب ما جاء في وقت العقيقة).
وكذا رواه الطبراني في الأوسط (رقم: ٤۸۸۲) وفي الصغير (رقم ۷۲۳). وقال المناوي في التيسير بشرح الجامع الصغير (۲/۳۰۱): ضعيف. قال الهيثمي في المجمع (٤/۵۹): وفيه إسماعيل بن مسلم المكي، وهو ضعيف لكثرة غلطه ووهمه.

ʿAzīz al-Fatāwā states:

The sunnah and recommended course is that the ʿaqīqah be performed on the seventh day, that the hair be shaved on that same day, and that on that same day, along with the shaving of the hair, a goat be slaughtered. If it cannot be done on the seventh day, then the ʿaqīqah should be performed on the fourteenth or the twenty-first day. If one acts contrary to this, the recommended manner will not be discharged; and the ʿaqīqah is itself a recommended act, so it ought to be discharged in the recommended manner. If one performs it without regard for the seventh day, the ʿaqīqah will be fulfilled, but the recommended manner will not be discharged. (فتاوی دار العلوم دیوبند: ۱/٦۸۱، دار الاشاعت)۔

Mawlānā Khālid Sayfullāh Ṣāḥib says:

The ʿaqīqah should be performed on the seventh day from the birth of the child; likewise, if it is performed even before the seventh day, that is sufficient. Up to what age may the ʿaqīqah be performed? On this question the view of Imām al-Shāfiʿī (may Allah have mercy on him) and Imām Aḥmad (may Allah have mercy on him) is that it may be performed at any time before puberty (شرح المہذب: ۸/۳۳۰، و المغنی: ۸/٦٤٦). In the view of Imām Mālik (may Allah have mercy on him), if one has not managed to perform the ʿaqīqah by the seventh day, then no scope for the ʿaqīqah now remains (سبل السلام: ٤/۱٤۲۹). If one has not managed the ʿaqīqah on the seventh day, then it should be performed on the fourteenth, and otherwise on the twenty-first day. On this subject Ḥaḍrat Buraydah (may Allah be pleased with him) has related a narration from the Prophet ﷺ, the soundness of which is doubtful with the scholars of hadith (مجمع الزوائد: ٤/۵۹); support for it is also found in a statement of Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah (may Allah be pleased with her). (حلال و حرام، ص ٤۵۳)۔

Mā lā budda minhu states:

مسئلہ بعدِ ولادت ہفتم روز یا چہار دہم یا بست و یکم بہمیں حساب
یا بعد ہفت ماہ یا ہفت سال عقیقہ باید کرد الغرض رعایتِ عددِ ہفت بہتر است۔
(ما لا بد منہ، رسالہ احکامِ عقیقہ، ص ۲۲۰)۔

Translation: After the birth, the ʿaqīqah should be performed on the seventh day, or the fourteenth, or the twenty-first, and by the same reckoning after seven months or seven years. In short, observing the number seven is better.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
