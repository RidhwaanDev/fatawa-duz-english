### TITLE
The ruling on eating the spinal cord

### QUESTION
What is the ruling on eating the spinal cord, which is well known among people — is it unlawful, or disliked, or permitted, or contrary to what is preferable? Removing it from small animals or from chickens is also a cause of trouble. Kindly guide us to the ruling of the sharia.

### ANSWER
Eating the spinal cord is disliked, and the preferred position is that the dislike involved is mild.

See what al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

كره من الشاة الحياء والخصية والغدة والمثانة والمرارة والدم المسفوح والذكر ونخاع الصلب كذا في الكنز. (الفتاوى الهندية: ٦/٤٤۵).

Kanz al-Daqāʾiq states:

كره من الشاة: الحياء [ الفرج ] والخصية، والغدة، المثانة، والمرارة، والدم المسفوح، والذكر.

وفي تعليقات الكنز للشيخ سائد بكداش: قال: لورود الأثر في النهي عن هذه الأشياء، ولاستخباث النفوس السليمة لها، وأما الدم: فقال أبو حنيفة رحمه الله بحرمته لثبوت النص في تحريمه.

تنبيه: جاء ت في ص ۲۱۷ من نسخة الكنز المطبوعة في الهند سنة ۱۲۹٤ هــ زيادة جملة على هذه السبع، وهي: "ونخاع الصلب"، وهو حكم تفردت به هذه النسخة دون غيرها من النسخ الخطية والمطبوعة، ولم يذكر هذا الفرع أيضاً في الكنز مع تتمة الطوري ۸/۵۵۳، ولا في تفريعات الطوري، وكذلك في بقية الشروح، ينظر: تبيين الحقائق ٦/۲۲٦، فتح المعين لا بي السعود ۳/۵٦۰، كشف الحقائق ۲/۳۳۸، وكذلك لم يذكر في الدر المختار، ولا في فروع ابن عابدين عليه ٦/۷٤۹.

في حين أن علماء الهند في الفتاوى الهندية (العالمكيرية) ٦/٤٤۵ اعتماداً منهم على هذه المطبوعة الهندية من الكنز قرروا حكماً شرعياً وهو: كراهة أكل نخاع الصلب، حيث كتبوا في الفتاوى: "والنخاع الصلب، وكذا في الهندية". وكتب مصحح الفتاوى الهندية معلقاً في الحاشية: قوله: والنخاع الصلب، ولم أجد ذ لك في عبارة الكنز، ولا في شرحه التبيين. (تعليقات الشيخ سائد بكداش على الكنز، ص ٦۹٤، مسائل شتي، ط: دار البشائر الإسلامية).

In the Kanz printed by Imdādiyyah of Multān the mention of nukhāʿ al-ṣulb is present (p. 496), but its annotator, Mawlānā Ḥabīb al-Raḥmān Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him), also rejected it. Consider the passage:

قوله ونخاع الصلب المراد به ما يقال في الهندية "حرام مغز" واعلم أن قوله ونخاع الصلب لم يوجد في جميع الشروح من المتن وإنما هو في النسخ المطبوعة ولم يذكر صاحب الدر المختار أيضاً فيما عدوه من الأشياء المكروهة ولم يأخذه في نظم الذي ذكر نقلته من ملا مسكين أيضاً بل اكتفي فيه على سبعة فافهم. "حبيب الرحمن".

(حاشية كنز الدقائق، ص ٤۹٦، ط: إمدادية ملتان، پاكستان).

In sum, the mention of nukhāʿ al-ṣulb is found in the Indian copies of Kanz al-Daqāʾiq, but it is not found in any other copy, whether manuscript or printed. It is also mentioned in ʿĀlamgīrī with a reference to the Kanz, but in the footnote it has been made clear that this word is not present in the Kanz or in its commentary al-Tabyīn. The passage of the Kanz has therefore become doubtful, and according to the research of Sāʾid Bakdāsh it falls away altogether; so the dislike of nukhāʿ al-ṣulb has become doubtful. If, however, it is written down as disliked in other books of the school, then dislike will be established — but even then strict dislike is difficult to establish, and only mild dislike can be proved. Accordingly ʿAllāmah Sayyid Aḥmad Ṭaḥṭāwī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

(قوله والدم المسفوح) أي السائل من العروق أما الدم الباقي في العروق بعد الذبح لا يكره كما في المفتاح وزيد نخاع الصلب. (حاشية الطحطاوي على الدر المختار: ٤/۳٦۰، ط: كوئته).

So if the nukhāʿ al-ṣulb can be removed easily, it should be removed; but if it is difficult to remove in the case of small animals, then removing it is not necessary.

See what ʿAllāmah ʿAbd al-Ḥayy Lakhnawī (may Allah have mercy on him) writes in Majmūʿat al-Fatāwā:

Question: is the spinal cord strictly disliked or mildly disliked? If it is strictly disliked, is it then necessary to remove the spinal cord of every animal, small or large — goat, chicken, sparrow, pigeon and so on — or only of large animals?

Answer: in Niṣāb al-Iḥtisāb and Maṭālib al-Muʾminīn and elsewhere it is written down as disliked, and from the plain sense of the books it appears that its dislike is mild, not strict. So for those animals in which removing the spinal cord is difficult, removing it is not necessary. (Muʿallim al-Fiqh, the Urdu translation of Majmūʿat al-Fatāwā, vol. 2, p. 321, published at Ārām Bāgh, Karachi).

Consider the passage of Niṣāb al-Iḥtisāb:

وأما ما يكره فهو عشرة: الغدة، والقبل، والدبر، والذكر، والخصيان، والمرارة، والمثانة، ونخاع الصلب

الخ. (نصاب الاحتساب، الباب الثامن والخمسون في الاحتساب على الطباخ، ص ۳۷۵).

The statements of the senior elders (may Allah have mercy on them) also differ on this matter. Consider the following passages from a number of fatwa collections:

Ḥaḍrat Muftī Kifāyatullāh Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) says: eating the testicles is disliked; the kidneys are permissible; the spinal cord is neither unlawful nor disliked — the poor thing has simply been given a bad name for nothing. (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 8/262, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

The author of Badhl al-Majhūd, Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Khalīl Aḥmad Sahāranpūrī (may Allah have mercy on him), says: the prohibition of the nukhāʿ has never come before my eyes in the books of fiqh. (Fatāwā Maẓāhir ʿUlūm, p. 299).

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ẓafar Aḥmad ʿUthmānī (may Allah have mercy on him) says: no proof has been established for the prohibition of the spinal cord, and it is not included among the seven parts of the sheep which they have held to be disliked; so eating it is lawful. (Imdād al-Aḥkām: 4/312).

In answer to another query he writes: we have not found in the books of fiqh any explicit statement that the spinal cord is unlawful. If you have seen an explicit statement, kindly write out the passage of the book together with the page, volume and chapter reference and inform us. (Imdād al-Aḥkām: 4/312).

Ḥaḍrat Muftī Maḥmūd Ḥasan Gangohī (may Allah have mercy on him) has written that it is forbidden, citing al-Ṭaḥṭāwī. See:

Question: what is the ruling on eating the spinal cord? Answer: it is forbidden — al-Ṭaḥṭāwī: 4/360. In the notes to it the passage of Kanz al-Daqāʾiq has also been presented as evidence. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 17/298, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Rashīd Aḥmad Gangohī (may Allah have mercy on him) has likewise written in Fatāwā Rashīdiyyah that it is forbidden. See: seven things of a lawful animal are forbidden to eat — the male organ, the female private part, the bladder, the glands, the spinal cord, which lies within the vertebrae of the back. All the remaining parts he has written down as lawful. (Fatāwā Rashīdiyyah, p. 587, published at Lahore).

Imdād al-Muftīn states:

Question: is it correct to eat the spinal cord of a lawful animal or not? The books of Ḥanafī fiqh write that seven things of a lawful animal are unlawful, and among them there is nowhere any mention of the prohibition of the spinal cord; yet in the Fatāwā Rashīdiyyah of Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Gangohī (may Allah have mercy on him) it is declared unlawful, though without any reference. A reference is therefore requested from the scholars, for the satisfaction of the heart.

Answer: in spite of much searching, this humble one too has found no reference for it. And so on.

In the footnote it is recorded: later the respected Mawlānā Saʿīd Aḥmad Ṣāḥib, Mufti of Maẓāhir ʿUlūm Sahāranpūr (may Allah have mercy on him), looked into it and pointed out its reference. It is exactly as follows:

صرح به الطحطاوي على الدر حيث قال: وزيد نخاع الصلب. (Imdād al-Muftīn, with the author's own footnote: 2/805, published by Dār al-Ishāʿat).

In sum, only the passage of al-Ṭaḥṭāwī is explicit on this matter, but its manner of expression shows that the spinal cord was included among the disliked things at a later stage, since no explicit statement about it is mentioned in the general books of fiqh, where only seven things are counted among the disliked. In any case, because of the passage of al-Ṭaḥṭāwī, the position of mild dislike is sound, while the position of strict dislike is open to question.

Since the meaning of ḥarām maghz is not obvious, its definition is quoted here from the books of lexicography.

نخع: بالفتح والكسر والضم عرق أبيض في داخل العنق، ينقاد في فقار الصلب حتى يبلغ عجب الذنب وهو يسقي العظام. (لسان العرب: ۸/۳٤۸، وتاج العروس: ۵/۵۲۰).

وفي المغرب: نخع: النخاع؛ خيط أبيض في جوف عظم الرقبة يمتد إلى الصلب. (المغرب: ۲/۲۹۳).

The substance of these definitions is that the nukhāʿ is a white cord which runs from inside the neck, passes through the back and reaches as far as the root of the tail.
