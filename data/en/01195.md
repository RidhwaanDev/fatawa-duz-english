### TITLE
A mutamattiʿ who travels to Madinah after his ʿumrah and then performs ʿumrah on returning

### QUESTION
A man goes for ḥajj and performs ḥajj tamattuʿ. After reaching Makkah he performs his ʿumrah, and then he travels to Madinah. May this person perform a second ʿumrah when he returns from Madinah to Makkah, or not? That is, will this be counted as tamattuʿ upon tamattuʿ or not?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, there is scope for performing a second ʿumrah on the return from Madinah, and it will not be counted as tamattuʿ upon tamattuʿ. It is better, however, to enter iḥrām for the ḥajj alone.

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

A person who has performed ʿumrah during the months of ḥajj, has thereafter presented himself in Madinah, and will then perform ḥajj that same year and return home, is a mutamattiʿ according to Imām Abū Ḥanīfah. Imām Abū Ḥanīfah forbids such a person, once he has performed one ʿumrah, to set out from Madinah and perform another ʿumrah before the ḥajj. According to the two companions, his tamattuʿ became void by his going to Madinah, so that if he now performs another ʿumrah his tamattuʿ will be valid. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۰/۳۹۳، مبوب و مرتب).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

If an āfāqī, after performing ʿumrah in the months of ḥajj, travels off to Madinah or Jeddah or somewhere similar, then at the time of his return it is better for him to enter iḥrām for ḥajj ifrād. According to Imām Abū Ḥanīfah (may Allah have mercy on him), tamattuʿ is not invalidated by travelling to a place other than one's home town, while according to the two companions (may Allah have mercy on them) it is invalidated. Since the first ʿumrah did not count as the ʿumrah of tamattuʿ, he may now begin afresh and perform either tamattuʿ or qirān, whichever he wishes. According to the position of Imām Abū Ḥanīfah (may Allah have mercy on him) he has the status of a Makkan, so he cannot perform qirān; his ʿumrah of tamattuʿ, however, remains, and the permissibility of a second ʿumrah after it and before the ḥajj is disputed. The most balanced view is that it is permissible from outside the sacred precincts (indeed it is permissible even from Makkah). There is therefore no real harm in entering iḥrām for ʿumrah on returning from Madinah; even so, in order to avoid the disagreement it is better to enter iḥrām for the ḥajj alone. (احسن الفتاوی: ٤/۵۱٤).
