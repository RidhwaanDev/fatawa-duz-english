### TITLE
Paying ṣadaqat al-fiṭr late

### QUESTION
A man gave me a cheque so that I could pay his ṣadaqat al-fiṭr, but I forgot about it. I only remembered a few days ago, and then paid it. Has this man's ṣadaqat al-fiṭr been discharged or not?

### ANSWER
The writings of the jurists make it clear that there are three positions on paying ṣadaqat al-fiṭr late. (1) The position of the standard texts and of the ẓāhir al-riwāyah is that ṣadaqat al-fiṭr is discharged whenever it is paid during a person's lifetime, and that this counts as payment in time, because it is obligatory in the same way as zakat. It is recommended, though, that it be paid on the day of Eid al-Fitr before going out to the Eid prayer ground. The author of Badāʾiʿ, ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī and others have given this position preference, and the fatwa is accordingly given on it.

(2) According to Imam al-Ḥasan ibn Ziyād (may Allah have mercy on him) it lapses once the day of Eid al-Fitr has passed, just as the sacrifice lapses once the days of sacrifice have passed — that is, the shedding of blood lapses. Ibn al-Humām (may Allah have mercy on him) has said that this position is not correct.

(3) Ibn al-Humām (may Allah have mercy on him) held that the payment of ṣadaqat al-fiṭr is not free of any time limit but is tied to a particular time, because of the words of the Prophet ﷺ:

"اغنوهم في هذا اليوم عن المسألة"

So if a person gives it after the day of Eid al-Fitr, it is a make-up payment. ʿAllāmah Ibn Nujaym al-Miṣrī (may Allah have mercy on him) also agreed with Ibn al-Humām (may Allah have mercy on him). ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) said that this third position falls outside the school.
