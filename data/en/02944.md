### TITLE
A litany to stop the hair of the head from falling out

### QUESTION
For some time now my hair has been falling out heavily. I am still young, and half the hair on my head has fallen out. Kindly give me some supplication or litany by which the hair that has fallen out may grow back and the hair that remains may be preserved.

### ANSWER
Recite the following supplication three times every morning and evening. The supplication is:

"بِسْمِ الله الذِي لا يضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهٖ شَيْئٌ فِي الأرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَائِ وَهو السَمِيعُ العَلِيم".
(انوارِ شمسیہ، ص ۸۹)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
