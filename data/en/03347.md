### TITLE
The ruling on correcting the qiblah of a mosque that is out of alignment

### QUESTION
The direction of the qiblah of a certain prayer hall is out of alignment by about 45 degrees, and the scholars say that if the deviation is up to 45 degrees the prayer is valid, and beyond that it invalidates it. But there is a deviation in the qiblah: so should it be put right, or should it be left as it is, seeing that leaving it means keeping the mistake permanently in place? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
It is preferable and better that the direction of the qiblah of a mosque or prayer hall should be completely correct and accurate, with no deviation at all, so that the prayer may be performed towards the entirely correct direction of the qiblah. On this basis, it is better to put it right.

ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him), in his marginalia on al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq, has recorded one view concerning the direction of the qiblah which allows latitude, and, after mentioning a second and more cautious view, has declared the first to be permissible and the second to be recommended — that is, acting upon the more cautious view is recommended — and, on the authority of Majmūʿ al-Nawāzil, he has declared this to be the preferred position. Consider the passage of Minḥat al-Khāliq:

في منية المصلي عن أمالي الفتاوى حد القبلة في بلادنا يعني سمرقند ما بين المغربين مغرب الشتاء ومغرب الصيف فإن صلى إلى جهة خرجت من المغربين فسدت صلاته، قال شارحها ابن أمير حاج: وذكر هذه العبارة في الملتقط مع زيادة وهي: وقال أبو منصور: ينظر إلى أقصر يوم في الشتاء وإلى أطول يوم في الصيف فيعرف مغربيهما ثم يترك الثلثين عن يمينه والثلث عن يساره ويصلي فيما بين ذلك وهذا استحباب والأول للجواز، ومشى علي الأول الرستغفني وجعل في مجموع النوازل ما ذكره أبو منصور هو المختار. (منحة الخالق حاشية البحر الرائق: ۱/۲۸۵، ط: كوئته).

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband records:

The scholars of the Ummah and the jurists of the religious community have, one and all and without disagreement, stated explicitly that the direction of the qiblah does not rest upon observational instruments and mathematical calculations; rather, in this matter it is sufficient to follow and to conform to the ancient mosques, which are founded upon customary indications and signs, even though, according to the rules of astronomy, there may be some deviation in them from the true direction. In this there is room, from the basic rule, up to nearly a quarter of a circle (90 degrees) — that is, up to an eighth of a circle on either side (45 degrees each way) — and, on the basis of caution, up to what lies between the two points of sunset, that is, 24 degrees on each side, 48 degrees in all.

لما في حاشية البحر للشامي بعد نقل القولين: وهذا استحباب والأول للجواز. (۱/۲۸۵)،

On this basis, even with both the deviations mentioned in the question, the prayer will be valid. But it is preferable that this mosque should be put right in conformity with the other ancient mosques, whether they be in this settlement or in the surrounding neighbourhood. Written by: Ashraf ʿAlī, may he be pardoned. (فتاوی دار العلوم دیوبند، امداد المفتین: ۲/۳٦۲، ط: دار الاشاعت) ۔

Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥall states:

If the prayer niche is twenty degrees out of alignment with the qiblah, then it is better for the niche to be put right, so that the worshippers may face the correct direction of the qiblah without deviation. So long as the niche is not put right, there is room for a deviation of up to twenty degrees; and the prayers that have already been performed were valid. (آپ کے مسائل اور ان کا حل: ۳/۳٤۳، مکتبہ لدھیانوی) ۔

Jawāhir al-Fiqh states: As for those mosques which accord with the direction of the qiblah, whether they be new or old, there is no doubt at all about the prayers in them being in accordance with the direction of the qiblah. And as for those mosques whose qiblahs are not in the correct direction, if they are within forty-five degrees, the prayer will still be valid; but it is necessary to correct their qiblah direction according to the rule. Yet even if prayers are performed in them in their present state, they will still be valid. (جواہر الفقہ: ۲/٤۲۰) ۔
