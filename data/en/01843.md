### TITLE
Taking someone else's shoes from the mosque

### QUESTION
In a mosque, or in the two Ḥarams, someone's shoes were exchanged: another person took his shoes and left his own behind. Is it now all right to wear the other person's shoes?

### ANSWER
If it occurs to the person that the owner of the shoes would in all likelihood be happy for them to be used, then wearing them is permissible and correct; otherwise he should avoid doing so.

Consider what al-Sirājiyyah states:

قوم أصابوا مذبوحاً في طريق البادية وقد وقع في قلبه أن صاحبه قد فعل إباحة للناس لا بأس بأكله. (الفتاوى السراجية: ۳۰۷).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
