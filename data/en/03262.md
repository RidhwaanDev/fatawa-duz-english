### TITLE
Whether children born of fornication inherit from their father

### QUESTION
A man married a woman and three children were born to them. He then formed an illicit relationship with his wife's younger sister, as a result of which two or three children were born. He afterwards divorced his wife and married the sister. He has now died: will these children born of fornication receive a share of the inheritance or not? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case described, the children born of fornication will receive no share from their father; they will, however, inherit from their mother. See what al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq records:

ويرث ولد الزنا واللعان بجهة الأم فقط، لأن نسبه من جهة الأب منقطع فلا يرث به ومن جهة الأم ثابت فيرث به أمه وأخته من الأم بالفرض لا غير وكذا ترثه أمه فرضاً لا غير. (تكملة البحر الرائق: ۸/۵۰۲).

و فتاوی دار العلوم زکریا، جلد چہارم، ص ۳۳۱ ۔ ط: زمزم)۔
