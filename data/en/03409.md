### TITLE
The ruling on prostration when a verse of prostration is heard over a mobile phone during a conversation

### QUESTION
I was speaking with my mother on the mobile phone. My brother was sitting near her reciting the Qurʾān, and he recited a verse of prostration which I heard over the mobile. Now is the prostration of recitation obligatory upon me or not?

### ANSWER
A conversation on a mobile phone is the speaker's voice directly; it is not like the sound of a cassette. The prostration of recitation will therefore be obligatory upon the listener, just as the sound of a loudspeaker has the status of the original voice.
