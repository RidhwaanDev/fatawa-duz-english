### TITLE
Fixing a monthly profit in a murābaḥah sale

### QUESTION
Zayd bought a machine from ʿUmar for one thousand. ʿUmar sold it in this way: the cost price is one thousand rand, but since Zayd will pay the price in instalments he will take a profit of two hundred rand a month. So, for example, on the basis of five months of instalments from January to the end of May, the price came to two thousand, and he sold the machine for two thousand on deferred terms. Along with this he also said that if the buyer pays the whole price after two months, the price will become fourteen hundred. Is this arrangement permissible or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described this contract is in reality a murābaḥah contract, and in a murābaḥah sale it is permissible to take a profit of two hundred rand a month. So if payment is made after five months, the price will come to two thousand; and if he paid after two months, or the buyer died, then it comes to fourteen hundred rand.

See what Islām awr Jadīd Maʿāshī Masāʾil states:

If it is a murābaḥah sale in which the seller expressly states the increase he is making in the price in return for the term, then the fatwa of the later Ḥanafī scholars concerning it is that if the debtor pays his debt before the appointed term, or dies before the appointed term arrives, then in that case the seller will take only as much of the price as corresponds to the days that have already passed, and he will have to forgo the price corresponding to the days that remain until the appointed term. The basis of this fatwa of the later Ḥanafīs is perhaps this: that although a term does not by itself have the capacity to be a counter-value, it is nevertheless permissible to fix some price against it in a subordinate and incidental way — just as the sale of the calf in a cow's womb is not permissible in its own right, yet it is permissible to increase the price of the cow on account of that calf. Thus the sale of a number of things is not permissible in its own right, yet at times it is permissible to take a counter-value for them incidentally. (Islām awr Jadīd Maʿāshī Masāʾil, 3/118, 120, in summary).

See what al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

قضي المديون الدين المؤجل قبل حلول الأجل أو مات فحل بموته فأخذ من تركته لا يأخذ من المرابحة التي جرت بينهما إلا بقدر ما مضي من الأيام، وهو جواب المتأخرين، قنية، وبه أفتي المرحوم أبو السعود آفندي مفتي الروم، وعلله بالرفق للجانبين. وفي الشامية: قوله لا يأخذ من المرابحة، صورته اشتري شيئاً بعشرة نقداً وباعه لا خر بعشرين إلى أجل هو عشرة أشهر، فإذا قضاه بعد تمام خمسة أو مات بعدها يأخذ خمسة ويترك خمسة، أقول: والظاهر أن مثله ما لو أقرضه وباعه سلعة بثمن معلوم وأجل ذلك، فيحسب له من ثمن السلعة بقدر ما مضى فقط تأمل. (الدر المختار مع فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۷۵۷، قبيل كتاب الفرائض، سعيد).

Shāmī states:

(قوله وعلله) أي علله الحانوتي بالتباعد عن شبهة الربا لا نها في باب الربا ملحقة بالحقيقة، وجهه أن الربح في مقابلة الأجل، لأن الأجل وإن لم يكن مالاً، ولا يقابله شيء من الثمن لكن اعتبروه مالاً في المرابحة إذا ذكر الأجل بمقابلة زيادة الثمن، فلو أخذ كل الثمن قبل الحلول كان أخذه بلا عوض. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۷۵۷، قبيل كتاب الفرائض، سعيد).
