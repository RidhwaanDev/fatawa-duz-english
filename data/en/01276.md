### TITLE
A woman conducting the marriage ceremony

### QUESTION
A Palestinian man married a Tunisian woman at the municipal office in Tunisia, a place that resembles a church and a court. The marriage was conducted by a woman magistrate in the presence of the woman's father and brother. Note that the woman magistrate was a Muslim, and that the bride herself was present at the marriage gathering. Is this marriage valid or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question the person who conducted the marriage was a Muslim woman, and she conducted it in the presence of two witnesses, so the marriage is valid. For when an adult woman is able to make the offer and acceptance herself, she may equally appoint another woman as her agent.

Al-Shāmī states:

(قوله يجعل عاقداً حكماً) لأن الوكيل في النكاح سفير ومعبر ينقل عبارة الموكل، فإذا كان الموكل حاضراً كان مباشراً لأن العبارة تنتقل إليه وهو في المجلس. (فتاوى الشامي: ۳/۲٤، سعيد).

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states: A marriage conducted through a woman acting as agent is valid, so long as the offer and acceptance took place in the presence of two witnesses. (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm: 7/98, Mudallal wa Mukammal; Niẓām al-Fatāwā: 2/211).
