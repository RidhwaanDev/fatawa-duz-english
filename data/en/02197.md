### TITLE
The ruling on the Ramadan salary of a teacher who resigns in Shaʿbān

### QUESTION
If a teacher resigns in Shaʿbān or Ramadan, will he receive the Ramadan salary or not?

### ANSWER
A madrasah's engagement is for a year, but by custom what is meant is the academic year, and that is understood to run to the end of Shaʿbān. The engagement will therefore be up to the end of Shaʿbān. If, however, a condition to that effect has been laid down, or it is the custom, that the madrasah pays the Ramadan salary when it releases a teacher, then the salary ought to be paid. Apart from custom, the holiday is for the sake of rest, so that the teacher may gain peace of mind for the coming year; it is therefore reasonable to attach Ramadan to the coming year. Hence the academic year will run to the end of Shaʿbān.

Sharḥ al-Majallah states:

وفي مجمع الحقائق: العادة المطردة تنزل منزلة الشرط، حتى لو باع التاجر في السوق شيئاً بثمن، ولم يصرحا بحلول ولا تأجيل، وكان المتعارف فيما بينهم أن البائع يأخذ من الثمن كل جمعة قدراً معلوماً، انصرف البيع إليه بلا بيان، لأن المعروف عرفاً كالمشروط شرطاً. (شرح المجلة لمحمد الأتاسي: ۱/۹۵، المادة: ٤۱).

Al-Ashbāh wa al-Naẓāʾir states:

واعلم أن اعتبار العادة والعرف يرجع إليه في الفقه في مسائل كثيرة، حتى جعلوا ذلك أصلاً، فقالوا في الأصول في باب ما تترك به الحقيقة: تترك الحقيقة بدلالة الاستعمال والعادة، هكذا ذكر فخر الإسلام

ومنها: البطالة في المدارس كأيام الأعياد ويوم عاشوراء وشهر رمضان في درس الفقه لم أرها صريحة في كلامهم، والمسألة على وجهين، فإن كانت مشروطة لم يسقط من المعلوم شيء، وإلا فينبغي أن يلحق ببطالة القاضي. وقد اختلفوا في أخذ القاضي ما رتب له من بيت المال في يوم بطالته، فقال في المحيط: إنه يأخذ في يوم البطالة، لأنه يستريح لليوم الثاني، وقيل: لا يأخذ، انتهى. وفي المنية: القاضي يستحق الكفاية من بيت المال في يوم البطالة في الأصح، واختاره في منظومة ابن وهبان، وقال إنه الأظهر (منظومة ابن وهبان: ۱/۲۸۸، فصل من كتاب أدب القاضي) فينبغي أن يكون كذلك في المدارس، لأن يوم البطالة للاستراحة، وفي الحقيقة يكون للمطالعة والتحرير عند ذي الهمة. (الأشباه والنظائر: ۱/۲٦۸، ۲۷۲، القاعدة السادسة العادة محكمة).

كذا في رد المحتار: ٤/۳۷۲، مطلب في استحقاق القاضي والمدرس الوظيفة في يوم البطالة، سعيد).

Imdād al-Aḥkām states:

Since this teacher did not come and work in the month of Shawwāl — whether the cause of his not working came from his own side or from the committee's, but in any case his not working is established — he is not entitled to the Ramadan salary.

لأن شرط الاستحقاق هو العمل في شوال ولم يوجد، وإذا فات الشرط فات المشروط بخلاف ما إذا لم يعمل بحصول الرخصة والإذن من المتولي فإنه في حكم العمل كما لا يخفي. والله أعلم

(Imdād al-Aḥkām: 3/574).

Imdād al-Fatāwā states:

The salary is indeed for the days of work, but the period of the holiday is attached to the days of work as something subordinate to them, so that the teacher may rest and then be able to work during the days of work. If he is relieved of his post at the end of Shaʿbān he will not receive the salary; and if he is not relieved, he will receive the salary at the end of Ramadan, provided he has also worked in Shawwāl. (Imdād al-Fatāwā: 3/348).

Fatāwā ʿUthmānī states:

Making the salary for the month of Ramadan (the days of vacation and rest) conditional upon returning at the beginning of Shawwāl no longer invalidates the contract, because this condition has now become well known. Shaykh al-Muḥaddithīn Ḥaḍrat Sahāranpūrī (may his secret be sanctified) himself stated this explicitly in his adjudication in "Fatāwā Khalīliyyah", saying that if it is treated as a well-known condition, then in all these cases the teacher will be entitled to the full salary in the disputed case. (Fatāwā Khalīliyyah: 1/248).

That this condition is well known is clear for several reasons, which are as follows:

(a) That this condition is well known in the religious madrasahs needs no demonstration, since almost all madrasahs act upon it.

(b) This condition is also well known among the jurists, because entitlement to the salary for the days of vacation exists precisely so that, after resting, the teacher may work again with greater vigour. If there is no intention of working after the days of vacation, how can there be an entitlement to the salary for the days of vacation?

(c) This condition is also entirely in keeping with safeguarding endowment funds and spending them on the most correct purpose, because in madrasahs the zakat money and donations are a trust belonging to the donors or to the students. Caution with this trust requires that the money should not be given to anyone without work or without a return. There must therefore either be work actually present, as in the days of work, or work reasonably expected, as in the days of rest when there is an intention to work in future; otherwise the permissibility of the wage will be open to question. — From Muftī Maḥmūd Ashraf ʿUthmānī (may his shade endure).

A fatwa from Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Muftī Muḥammad Taqī ʿUthmānī (may his blessings endure):

Both are permissible: making work in Shawwāl a condition upon which the salary for the Ramadan vacation depends, and not making it so. The permissibility of not making it a condition is obvious, since its upshot is that the one month's salary fixed in the contract of hire without work is, as it were, an additional wage for the total work of the whole year, which is known, and is permissible by the agreement of both parties. As for the permissibility of making it a condition, one reason may be the very one indicated in the answer of Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Khalīl Aḥmad (may his secret be sanctified), and another reason may be that the salary for the days of vacation has been made a condition for the renewal of the contract — that is, if the contract is renewed in Shawwāl for the coming year, the salary for the days of vacation will also be paid, and otherwise not. It is as though in this case the contract of hire came to an end with Shaʿbān, and at the time of renewing the contract in Shawwāl the salary for the days of vacation was given as an addition along with Shawwāl. (Fatāwā ʿUthmānī: 3/368, 373, 374).

Niẓām al-Fatāwā states:

It is correct that the Ramadan vacation is for the sake of rest, so that after resting the work may be carried on with fresh energy, as is customary in madrasahs generally; and on this basis teachers and employees who are not returning the following year have no entitlement to the Ramadan salary. (Niẓām al-Fatāwā: 1/332).
