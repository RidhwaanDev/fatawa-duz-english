### TITLE
The ruling on bringing stones or ash from a volcano

### QUESTION
In various places in the world volcanoes erupt, and from there fire flows like a channel. Some people bring stones or ash and the like from there. Is it correct to do this or not? Some people say that this is a place of punishment and that nothing should be taken from here. What is the ruling of the Sharīʿah?

### ANSWER
A volcano is one of the wonders of the power of Allah Most High; it is not the punishment of the punished settlements. The punishments that were sent down upon the nations were punishments that destroyed the people; a volcano is not like that. The land around Madīnah al-Munawwarah has been expressed by the term "lābah". In al-Bukhārī it occurs: "حُرِّمَ ما بين لا بتي المدينة على لساني".

And in the lexicon, "ḥarrah" and "lābah" are the terms for land with congealed black stones after a volcano:

In the above passage "barākīn" is the plural of "burkān", and "burkān" is the term for a volcano.

And the lands in the surroundings of Madīnah al-Munawwarah are of this very kind; despite this the Prophet ﷺ did not forbid their use.

Furthermore, it is a principle of fiqh: "الأصل في الأشياء الإباحة" — that is, the original ruling concerning things is that they are permissible and lawful to use. Who knows how many metals and minerals come out of the earth and come into use; everyone gathers the wood of the forest. In the same way it is permissible to put the stones of a volcano to use; all these are originally permissible.

Volcanoes are of several kinds:

(1) One kind is that in which only fire comes out in the form of a flowing channel. This kind is mostly not harmful, and this kind is found more on a mountain of a beautiful island of the Pacific Ocean, and is also found on the mountain of Réunion. And after this kind of volcano there is greater greenery and verdure. And in the congealed channels of fire of a volcano a kind of flower appears.

(2) The second kind is that in which a kind of matter erupts; the atmosphere becomes polluted by its gases and stone particles, and often it is so abundant that even the flight of aircraft becomes difficult.

Furthermore, by the will of Allah Most High a volcano sometimes proves harmful; thus up to today about two hundred thousand people have lost their lives from its eruptions.

The benefits of a volcano: (1) Sulphur (that is, brimstone) is extracted from its stones, which is used in countless things. (2) By means of it thermal power and electricity are generated. (3) Its stones contain very valuable minerals (such as gold, silver, copper, brass and so forth). (4) Its stones possess many special properties and are used in construction. (5) Some of the beautiful and lovely scenes of the world and its natural gardens are indebted to it.

The above details are derived from the following books: (الموسوعة العربية العالمية اول واضخم عمل من نوعه وحجمه ومنهجه في تاريخ الثقافة العربية الإسلامية. وعمل موسوعي ضخم اعتمد في بعض أجزاءه على النسخة الدولية من دائرة المعارف، world Book international، تحت مادة "بركان").

In Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah it occurs:

Question: Réunion is a mountainous region, and here there is a volcano which is spread over a great distance. Near it lie many burnt stones which are light in weight and are used in many places. In the graveyard of Saint-Louis, so that there be no mud on the pathway used for coming and going, they put these stones there. Some people raise an objection to this, saying that these stones are like the stones of hell. What is the ruling of the Sharīʿah in this connection? (2) Many Muslims here, regarding it as a fashion, have a small volcano built in their houses using the stones mentioned in the question above. What is the ruling of the Sharīʿah concerning this? Kindly explain and be rewarded.

The answer: (1) These stones may be laid to pave the pathway on which people walk inside the graveyard; to lay them inside a grave and on top of a grave is forbidden. (2) It is permissible, they may build it; but it is better to avoid superfluities. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۵/٤۸۳، ط: مکتبۃ الاحسان، دیوبند)۔
