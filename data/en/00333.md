### TITLE
Paying madrasah teachers out of donations collected for the mosque

### QUESTION
What do the honourable scholars say about this matter: here in Venda, one hundred rand is collected every month from each shop in the name of the mosque. Can the teachers who teach in the madrasah be paid their salaries out of this money? Please explain the ruling of the sharia and guide us.

### ANSWER
It is not correct to pay the salaries of the madrasah teachers out of money that is collected for the mosque. What may be done is this: send a written notice to those from whom the donations are collected, stating that their donation will be spent on the madrasah and its teachers as well as on the mosque, and have them sign it. Alternatively, speak to them about it directly, or make an announcement in the mosque. After that, the money may be spent on the madrasah as well as on the mosque. If it has been spent in this way until now without their permission, then obtain the donors' permission for it, and Allah will accept that expenditure too.
