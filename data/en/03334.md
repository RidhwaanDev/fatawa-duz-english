### TITLE
The validity of the fajr prayer when the sun rises during it

### QUESTION
The ruling on the validity of the prayer when the sun rises during the fajr prayer.

### ANSWER
Fatāwā Ḥaqqāniyyah records:

The sunnah method is that supplication in the prayer should be made in the Arabic language only. If supplication is made in some other language, the prayer will not be free of dislikedness. (Fatāwā Ḥaqqāniyyah 3/209; Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā 2/397, Bombay edition)

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
