### TITLE
Staying at the house of the bereaved for three days after the burial

### QUESTION
Is it permissible or not to stay for some days at the house of the deceased after the burial, in order to console the family of the deceased or to assist them in other matters? Some people stay for three days; what is the proof for this?

### ANSWER
Offering condolence to the family of the deceased, whether after the burial or before it, is a sunnah, but staying for three days is disliked and not commendable. There is, however, latitude for those relatives who have travelled from far away and for whom it would be difficult to return the same day; for them to stay so that they may console the family of the deceased and help with the other work is correct.

Fatāwā Shāmī records:

وفي الإمداد: وقال كثير من متأخري أئمتنا: يكره الاجتماع عند صاحب الميت، ويكره له الجلوس في بيته حتى يأتي إليه من يعزي، بل إذا فرغ ورجع الناس من الدفن، فليتفرقوا، ويشتغل الناس بأمورهم، وصاحب البيت بأمره. (فتاوى الشامي: ۲/۲٤۱، سعيد).

(وكذا في حاشية الطحطاوي، ص ٦۱٦، قديمي).

وفي موضع آخر: روى الإمام أحمد وابن ماجه بإسناد صحيح عن جرير بن عبد الله رضي الله عنه قال: كنا نعد الاجتماع إلى أهل الميت، وصنعهم الطعام من النياحة. (فتاوى الشامي: ۲/۲٤۰).

Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

The custom that has grown up of eating a meal of hospitality at the house of the bereaved must certainly be given up. Only those relatives and kin of the bereaved who have come from distant places and cannot return the same day, or whose staying is necessary for the consolation of the bereaved, may eat food at the house of the deceased, and there is no harm in that. All the rest of those who come to offer condolence should return to their own homes; they should neither stay at the house of the deceased nor eat a meal of hospitality there. (کفایت المفتی: ٦/۵۵، جامعہ فاروقیہ) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
