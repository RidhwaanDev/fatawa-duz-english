### TITLE
Putting the sacrificial animal to work

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to use the sacrificial animal for carrying loads or for any other work, or not?

### ANSWER
It is not correct to place a load on the sacrificial animal, to use it for carrying goods, to ride it, or to hire it out; rather, it is not sound or correct to put the sacrificial animal to any work at all. (در مختار: ٦/۳۲۹، سعید، و لسان الحکام، ص ۳۸۸) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
