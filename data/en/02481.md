### TITLE
The ruling on breaking the bones of the meat of the ʿaqīqah

### QUESTION
After the animal of the ʿaqīqah has been slaughtered, is it disliked to break its bones, or is it permissible? What do the jurists say on this subject?

### ANSWER
After the animal of the ʿaqīqah has been slaughtered, breaking its bones is permissible and lawful.

Consider what Fatāwā al-Shāmī states:

وهي شاة تصلح للأضحية تذبح للذكر والأنثى سواء فرق لحمها نيئاً أو طبخه بحموضة أو بدونها مع كسر عظمها أولا. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۳۳٦، سعيد).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

The common people regard the breaking of the bones as impermissible; this belief is mistaken, and the scholars have refuted it. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 17/527, Fārūqiyyah).

Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

Breaking the bones is permissible. Some people have forbidden the breaking of the bones, but there is no evidence for this prohibition. (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 8/241, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

Imdād al-Fatāwā states:

Breaking the bones of the animal of the ʿaqīqah is correct; whatever is of consequence among the conditions and rulings of the sacrifice is of consequence in the ʿaqīqah as well. (Imdād al-Fatāwā: 3/620).

However, on account of the hadiths, some scholars have declared it commendable not to break the bones.

Consider what Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd states:

حدثنا محمد بن العلاء، حدثنا حفص، حدثنا جعفر عن أبيه، أن النبي ﷺ قال في العقيقة التي عقتها فاطمة رضي الله عنه عن الحسن والحسين: "أن تبعثوا إلى القابلة منها برجل، وكلوا وأطعموا ولا تكسروا منها عظماً". (رواه أبو داود في مراسيله، ص ۲۷۸، رقم ۳۷۹، باب في العقيقة).
قال شعيب الأرناؤوط: رجاله ثقات رجال الشيخين غير جعفر، وهو ابن محمد بن على بن الحسين فإنه من رجال مسلم. ورواه البيهقي (۹/۳۰۲) من طريق أبي داود. (تعليق الشيخ شعيب على مراسيل أبي داود، ص ۲۷۹).

See further: (مستدرك حاكم: ٤/۲۹۱/۷۵۹۵، كتاب الذبائح، ومسند إسحاق ابن راهويه: ۳/٦۹۲).

Iʿlāʾ al-Sunan states:

وفيه [شرح المهذب] أيضاً: يستحب أن تفصل أعضاء ه ولا يكسر شيء من عظامها، فإن كسر فهو خلاف الأولي، وبالجملة فلا تقطع إلا من المفاصل، وعن عطاء كانوا يستحبون أن لا يكسر لها عظم. (اعلاء السنن: ۱۷/۱۲۱).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

There is no contradiction between permissibility and being contrary to what is preferable. In Kifāyat al-Muftī the evidence for a prohibition has been denied — that is, there is no evidence for calling the breaking of bones forbidden and impermissible; its being contrary to what is preferable has not been denied. The upshot is that the directive not to break the bones which occurs in the narrations is not one of obligation but of recommendation. Since the common people have given it the rank of an obligation and regard the breaking of the bones as impermissible and forbidden, and this belief is mistaken and in need of correction, the scholars have refuted it. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 7/537).

Imdād al-Muftīn states:

Some scholars consider it commendable that the bones should not be broken, but rather collected in one place and buried. Imām Mālik (may Allah have mercy on him), however, says that just as the ruling for ordinary sacrifices is that the bones are broken, so too is the ruling for the ʿaqīqah; there is no difference.

كما في تحفة المودود بأحكام المولود: قول مالك رحمه الله تكسر عظامها ويطعم منها الجيران، ص ۲۷

No explicit statement on this matter has been seen narrated from al-Imām al-Aʿẓam Abū Ḥanīfah (may Allah have mercy on him); but this much is mentioned in the Ḥanafī books, that in its general rulings the ʿaqīqah is like the sacrifice. (Imdād al-Muftīn, vol. 2, p. 803, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

What is contained in the narration transmitted is not a directive of obligation but something recommended by way of a good omen. If it is kept within these limits it is correct; but if it is given the rank of an obligation, then dislikedness will enter into it. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 17/527, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

The upshot is that no explicit, sound, marfūʿ narration is available concerning not breaking the bones. The narration of Marāsīl Abī Dāwūd is mursal, though sound in its chain; and the narration of al-Mustadrak of al-Ḥākim (4/291) is weak on account of two defects: (1) discontinuity in the chain; (2) irregularity and interpolation.

This narration is also mentioned in the Musnad of Isḥāq ibn Rāhawayh (3/692), but in his notes upon it Dr. ʿAbd al-Ghafūr ʿAbd al-Ḥaqq Ḥusayn, discussing it, has declared it weak.

A few questions relating to the ʿaqīqah, with their answers:

Question: What is the ruling on the ʿaqīqah, and how many animals are slaughtered for it?

Answer: Performing the ʿaqīqah is recommended, and through its blessing calamities and afflictions are averted from the child. In the ʿaqīqah there are two goats or two rams, or two shares in a large animal, for a boy; and one goat or one ram, or one share in a cow, for a girl. If one has not the means for two on behalf of a boy, then one goat or one ram is also correct. The ʿaqīqah should be performed on the seventh day from the birth of the child. (Iʿlāʾ al-Sunan: 17/114; and Awjaz al-Masālik: 1/169).

Question: If the ʿaqīqah cannot be performed on the seventh day, when should it be performed?

Answer: Regard should be had for the seventh day; so if the child was born on a Sunday, it should be performed on the Saturday. (Badhl al-Majhūd: 9/609; Tanqīḥ al-Fatāwā al-Ḥāmidiyyah: 2/233; and al-Majmūʿ Sharḥ al-Muhadhdhab: 8/431, Dār al-Fikr).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: What sort of animal should the animal of the ʿaqīqah be?

Answer: Whatever animal is valid for the sacrifice is valid for the ʿaqīqah too, and whatever is invalid for the sacrifice is invalid for the ʿaqīqah too. In the distribution of the meat as well, the practice of the sacrifice is recommended: one portion for oneself, one portion for relatives and friends, and one for the poor and needy. The rulings concerning its hide are also the same as those of the sacrifice. (Radd al-Muḥtār: 6/336, Saʿīd; and Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 17/513, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: Is it impermissible to break the bones of the ʿaqīqah?

Answer: It is permissible. (Fatāwā al-Shāmī: 6/336, Saʿīd; Tuḥfat al-Mawdūd, p. 52, Riyāḍ; Imdād al-Fatāwā: 3/620; Kifāyat al-Muftī: 8/241; and Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 7/537).

Objection: In this age the common people do not regard the breaking of the bones as impermissible; so, in the light of the reports and the mursal hadith, should it not be recommended not to break them?

Answer: The answer to this, as it appears, is that in the best of ages it was customary to roast the animal whole, and so, by way of a good omen, not breaking the bones was a commendable practice. Nowadays this custom is as good as non-existent, and therefore breaking the bones is now correct on account of need, so that cooking may be made easy.

Question: May a person perform his own ʿaqīqah?

Answer: Yes, he may. (Iʿlāʾ al-Sunan: 17/121; Tuḥfat al-Mawdūd, p. 56; and Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥal: 5/488).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: If a child dies before the ʿaqīqah, is there an ʿaqīqah for him?

Answer: There is not. Yes, one may perform it by way of charity and in the hope of intercession. (Sharḥ al-Muhadhdhab: 8/448, Dār al-Fikr; Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 5/432; and Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 17/529, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

Question: What should be recited when slaughtering the animal of the ʿaqīqah?

Answer: One should recite this supplication:

اللهم هذه عقيقة ابني (say the boy's name)
دمها بدمه، ولحمها بلحمه، وعظمها بعظمه، وجلدها بجلده، وشعرها بشعره، اللهم اجعلها فداء لبني من النار". (تنقيح الفتاوى الحامدية: ۲/۲۳۳، وكتاب الفتاوى: ٤/۱۷۸).

If it is the ʿaqīqah of a girl, then one should recite this supplication:

اللهم هذه عقيقة بنتي (say the girl's name)
دمها بدمها، ولحمها بلحمها، عظمها بعظمها، وجلدها بجلدها، وشعرها بشعرها، اللهم اجعلها فداء لبنتي من النار". (تنقيح الفتاوى الحامدية: ۲/۲۳۳، وكتاب الفتاوى: ٤/۱۷۸).

After that one should recite this supplication:

إني وجهت وجهي للذي فطر السموات والأرض حنيفاً وما أناْ من المشركين، لا شريك له، وبذلك أمرت وأناْ من المسلمين". (Abū Dāwūd Sharīf, no. 2795; and Ibn Mājah, no. 3121).

After that one should recite:

"اللهم منك ولك"

Then one should slaughter while saying:

"بسم الله الله أكبر"

(Sunan al-Kubrā of al-Bayhaqī: 9/303).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: What is the ruling on performing the ʿaqīqah during the days of sacrifice?

Answer: It is permissible and correct. If any of the days of sacrifice happens to be the seventh day, then the ʿaqīqah may be joined with the sacrifice; for example, two shares for a boy and the remaining five shares for the sacrifice. (Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ: 5/72, Saʿīd; Radd al-Muḥtār: 6/326, Saʿīd; Ḥāshiyat al-Ṭaḥṭāwī ʿalā al-Durr al-Mukhtār: 4/166; and Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 17/515, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: Who should perform the child's ʿaqīqah?

Answer: The father, or the person upon whom the maintenance of the child is obligatory. (Muwaṭṭaʾ of Imām Mālik, may Allah have mercy on him, pp. 1047 and 1050; Iʿlāʾ al-Sunan: 17/127; and Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 5/433).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: Is it permissible to use the meat of the ʿaqīqah at a wedding function or not?

Answer: It is permissible; however, if there is some custom of give and take, one should keep clear of the suspicion of a return payment. (Ḥāshiyat al-Ṭaḥṭāwī ʿalā al-Durr al-Mukhtār: 4/166; Iʿlāʾ al-Sunan: 17/126; Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 5/434; and Kifāyat al-Muftī: 8/242, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: What is the ruling on serving the meat of the ʿaqīqah at the walīmah?

Answer: It is correct. (Iʿlāʾ al-Sunan: 17/127; Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ: 5/72, Saʿīd; Radd al-Muḥtār: 6/326, Saʿīd; Tuḥfat al-Mawdūd, p. 55; and Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 17/518, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: Is it permissible to slaughter a large animal for the ʿaqīqah or not?

Answer: It is permissible and correct. (Iʿlāʾ al-Sunan: 17/117; Awjaz al-Masālik: 10/191; Fatāwā ʿUlamāʾ Balad al-Ḥarām, p. 1022; and Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 17/524).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: Is it permissible to perform the ʿaqīqah of seven children together in one large animal or not?

Answer: It is permissible. (ʿĀlamgīriyyah: 5/304; Tuḥfat al-Mawdūd, p. 65; Imdād al-Aḥkām: 4/228; and Kifāyat al-Muftī: 8/240, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم

Question: Where should the ʿaqīqah be performed?

Answer: Wherever the child is present. (Iʿlāʾ al-Sunan: 17/126; Badhl al-Majhūd: 9/612; Kifāyat al-Muftī: 8/241, Dār al-Ishāʿat; and Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 17/529, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

But these days the system of the telephone and the like is available; so if one slaughters the animal in India and, after informing them, the child's hair is shaved in South Africa, this too is permissible.
