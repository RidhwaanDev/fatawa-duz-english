### TITLE
The four boundaries of the beard and its ruling in the four schools

### QUESTION
What is the limit of the beard? Do the hairs below the cheek and the jawline fall within the limit of the beard, or is it permissible to cut them? Is it permissible to cut the hair below the round bone of the face, that is, below the cheek, or not? Nowadays students and some other people have taken up this practice, and the custom is spreading widely: they leave only a narrow strip and trim the line far below the round bone. Is this in accordance with the sunnah in sharia, or against the sunnah? And what is the ruling on cutting the hair above the chin and below the lower lip, which is called rīsh-i bacha? I hope you will oblige me with a reasoned answer.

### ANSWER
واللہ الموفق للصواب:

The pure sharia has made it obligatory and binding upon men to keep a beard, and its minimum limit, in length and in breadth, is one fist. That is, it is impermissible to reduce the beard to less than a fist's length. This is the agreed and accepted ruling of the four imams. Anyone who has his beard cut before it reaches a fist's length, or who keeps it shorter than that, is a sinner and a transgressor.

See what Bukhārī Sharīf states:

عن ابن عمر رضي الله عنه عن النبي ﷺ قال: خالفوا المشركين، وفروا اللحى وأحفوا الشوارب. (رواه البخاري: ۲/۸۷۵، باب تقليم الأظفار، ط: فيصل).

Muslim Sharīf states:

عن أبي هريرة رضي الله عنه قال: قال رسول الله ﷺ: جزوا الشوارب وأرخوا اللحى، خالفوا المجوس. (رواه مسلم: ۱/۱۲۹، باب خصال الفطرة، فيصل).

ʿAllāmah al-ʿAynī (may Allah have mercy on him) has written that opposing the polytheists lies in letting the beard grow, because the polytheists used to keep their beards short.

See what ʿUmdat al-Qārī states:

لأنهم كانوا يقصرون لحاهم ومنهم من كان يحلقها. (عمدة القاري: ۱۵/۹۰، دار الحديث، ملتان).
