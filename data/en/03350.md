### TITLE
The ruling on a cigarette smoker leading the prayer

### QUESTION
A certain scholar has the habit of smoking cigarettes. Is such a scholar entitled to lead the prayer? Will the prayer be valid behind him? And what is the ruling on appointing him as a permanent imam? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the sight of the Sacred Law, cigarette smoking is an ugly and undesirable thing, and smoking it is disliked. Its harm encompasses both religion and worldly life; the angels are caused distress by it; and it is utterly contrary to the dignity of a scholar. For this reason a scholar should repent sincerely and abandon this ugly habit. For the rest, it is disliked for a cigarette smoker to lead the prayer — yes, the prayer behind him will be valid, but appointing him as a permanent imam is not free from dislikedness.

In cigarette smoking there is also the wasting of wealth and extravagance, and this is forbidden. Consider:

قال الله تعالى: ﴿وكلوا واشربوا ولا تسرفوا﴾ [الأعراف: ۳۱].

وقال تعالى: ﴿ولا تبذر تبذيراً، إن المبذرين كانوا إخوان الشياطين وكان الشيطان لربه كفوراً﴾ [الإسراء: ۲٦، ۲۷].

وقال تعالى: ﴿ولا تلقوا بأيديكم إلى التهلكة﴾ [البقرة: ۱۹۵].

قال رسول الله ﷺ: إن الله حرم عليكم عقوق الأمهات ومنعاً وهات ووأد البنات وكره لكم قيل وقال وكثرة السؤال وإضاعة المال. (رواه البخاري: ۲/۵۹۷۵).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah records:

The prayer behind an imam who smokes tobacco is also valid; but to come into the mosque with a foul-smelling mouth is disliked to the degree of prohibition. He should therefore clean his mouth thoroughly with the tooth-stick during his ablution and then come into the mosque; otherwise the angels too will be caused distress. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ٦/۸۳، جامعہ فاروقیہ) ۔
