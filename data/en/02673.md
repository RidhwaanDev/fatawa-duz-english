### TITLE
The ruling on platinum jewellery

### QUESTION
Is it permissible for a woman to wear platinum jewellery or not?

### ANSWER
It is permissible and correct for women to wear jewellery of gold, silver and other metals. Platinum is a precious kind of iron that is bright like silver, which is why it is also called white gold; so wearing jewellery of it is permissible and correct as well. However, one should avoid wearing a ring of it, because it is not established that it is gold or silver, and for women only a gold or silver ring is permissible; a ring of any other metal is disliked for women too. See what Bihishtī Zewar states:

It is permissible for women to wear jewellery. Jewellery made of things other than gold and silver is also correct — such as brass, gilt, pewter and the like — but a ring of anything other than gold and silver is not correct. (Bihishtī Zewar: 3/278, Dār al-Ishāʿat)

Fatāwā Qāḍīkhān states:

ولا بأس لهن بلبس الديباج والحرير والذهب والفضة واللؤلؤ. (فتاوى قاضيخان على هامش الفتاوى الهندية: ۳/٤۱۳، باب ما يكره من الثياب والحلي والزينة ومالا يكره).

وفي الهندية: ولا بأس للنساء بتعليق الخرز في شعورهن من صفر أو نحاس أو شبه أو حديد ونحوها للزينة والسوار منها. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳۵۹).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
