### TITLE
The background of the blessings and manifestations of the noble House of Allah

### QUESTION
What is the Kaʿbah of Allah? And what are the blessings of the Hajj of the House of Allah?

### ANSWER
The greatest centre of Allah's manifestations in this world, the focal point of the manifestations of the Holy Court, is called Bayt Allāh al-Ḥarām and the Kaʿbah of Allah. It is the treasury of eternal mercy, the cradle of forgiveness and mercy, and the divinely appointed banqueting place of those who travel in the spiritual realm, where every person draws benefit according to his own capacity. For this reason every person who has the means has been commanded to present himself at this most sacred place at least once in his life; and presenting oneself at this court once in a lifetime, for one who has the means, is what is called the Hajj of the House of Allah, which is the fifth pillar of the religion of Islam and is counted among the most important of the symbols of Allah. Through it, from the lights and blessings of the centre of mercy and of manifestations, further brightness and polish are produced in the light of faith, and a share is granted of the splendours of eternal mercy. For the Muslims of every region of the world, this magnificent assembly of those who gain the means of forgiveness through weeping and lamentation, and of those who come with sincerity, repentance and turning back to Allah, becomes a means of blessings of every kind. Then at every step there are the splendours of the sanctity and honour of the symbols of Allah and the memorials of those brought near to His court: here the radiance of the Black Stone, there the magnetic attraction of the Station of Ibrāhīm; here the lights and blessings of Ṣafā and Marwah, there the manifestations of the valley of ʿArafāt, and elsewhere the lights of Muzdalifah and Minā. In short, at every step there are promises of forgiveness and mercy. This is why, even in today's turbulent age of materialism, a true longing for the Hajj of the House of Allah is present in thousands upon thousands of hearts: any heart in which even a little of the light of faith is present is restless for the Hajj of the House of Allah. It is plain that the real purpose of these acts of worship is the pleasure of Allah, the making of provision for the hereafter, and becoming deserving of the blessings of the hereafter; yet such is the comprehensiveness of the noble Sacred Law of Islam that worldly benefits too have been joined to the religious ones. The finest minds of the Muslim world, the experts in thought and politics, the men of righteousness and God-consciousness, the masters of spiritual allegiance and guidance, the scholars and the hadith masters — in short, people of every class and every temperament, common folk and elite, the men of wealth and the men of power — all of them come together in the greatest of assemblies, the like of which is not to be found anywhere in the world. What a wonderful arrangement it is! A person of any taste and any school of thought can find there the means of satisfying his own need and purpose. Where else could such precious opportunities be available — the soul-stirring scenes of the unity of the Muslim world, the reassuring proposals for the difficulties of the Muslim world, and the openings for teaching and training and for giving and receiving benefit? In any case, whether it be the organisation of trade and economy or the treasures of knowledge and spiritual insight, the questions of spiritual discipleship and politics or the dream of the unity of the world, the means of fulfilling all of them are present here. In two phrases of the Noble Qur'an all of this has been gathered up. (1)

وإذ جعلنا البيت مثابة للناس وأمناً. (البقرة ع ۱۵)

And when We made the House of the Kaʿbah a place of assembly for the people and a place of safety. (Translation of Shaykh al-Hind, may Allah have mercy on him) (2)

ليشهدوا منافع لهم. (الحج ع ٤)

So that they may reach the places of their benefit. (The same).

Reflection on the noble verses of the Noble Qur'an shows that the House of Allah is the means by which the world stands and the universe endures. As long as this House of Allah remains in the world, the world will remain standing; and when Allah, glorified be His majesty, wills to bring this world to an end, this Kaʿbah will be laid waste. It is as though the secret of the world's continuance lies in the Kaʿbah and the House of Allah: it is the centre of the world, and once this centre comes to an end the process of the world's passing away will begin. Then, just as the secret of the outward continuance of the world lies in the continuance of Bayt Allāh al-Ḥarām, in exactly the same way the chain of the Lord's spiritual guidance is also established from this House of Allah. It seems that the outpouring of heavenly guidance and light comes from the court of the mighty Throne upon this mighty House, and that in the world this House alone has been made the fountainhead of guidance and the spring of direction. Allah Most High says:

﴿إن أول بيت وضع للناس للذي ببكة مباركاً وهدي للعالمين﴾ (آل عمران ع ۱۰)

Indeed the first House appointed for the people is the one that is in Makkah, blessed and a guidance for the people of the world. (Translation of Shaykh al-Hind, may Allah have mercy on him).

Facing al-Bayt al-Maʿmūr — which is on the seventh heaven and is the place of the angels' circumambulation — Allah Most High made this spot on earth the centre of His manifestations from the very day of eternity, and through the ages of human history the House of Allah continued to be built. The circuits, prayers, supplications and cries of love and longing of the noble angels, the great prophets and those brought near to the divine court made it such a "spot of light" that the intellect stands amazed. This is why the lovers are never sated either with circling it or with gazing upon it. (Looking at it is an act of worship.) It is this same inner meaning of the "House of Allah" that led to its being made the qiblah of the prayers, and so being made still further the point and centre of the manifestations of the Holy Court. The people of insight know that wherever a single righteous servant brought near to Allah appears in his splendour, there the mercy of Allah and the divine lights turn towards him. So what limit can there be to the blessings of a place that is the centre of the supplications, acts of worship, glorifications and manifestations of the angels, the messengers and prophets, and those brought near to the divine court! What is to be said of the blessings of so sacred a place, where those who hope for the perfect mercy of Allah gather from every corner of the world, repenting of their sins, and are now absorbed with rapture in weeping and lamentation and now prostrate with their heads upon the ground! At this sacred spot, according to the hadiths of the Prophet ﷺ, one hundred and twenty mercies descend every day: sixty for those making ṭawāf, forty for those praying, and twenty for those visiting it and gazing upon it. How, then, can its magnetic attraction be measured? And what is to be said of the good fortune of those blessed souls who are gladdened by all three kinds of worship! (Excerpts from the monthly Bayyināt, Muḥarram al-Ḥarām 1387 AH, by Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Muḥammad Yūsuf Banūrī, may his secret be sanctified).

Allah's turning towards the Kaʿbah is greater than towards any other place; so those who have eyes know and see that the divine manifestations upon the Kaʿbah really are very great. It is this that is meant by His turning towards it, and those very manifestations are the spirit of the Kaʿbah and the reality of the Kaʿbah. This is why the prayer is valid even on the roof of the outward Kaʿbah: for although at that moment the form of the Kaʿbah is not before one, the reality of the Kaʿbah — that is, the manifestation — is before one.

Muslims in reality face the divine manifestation; they do not face the walls of the Kaʿbah. But since not every person perceives the divine manifestation, Allah Most High has fixed the boundary of this particular spot upon which His manifestation is greater than upon other places. So this building exists only so that the place of that greatest manifestation may be identified; the building itself is not intended for its own sake. Accordingly, the fact that the prayer would not be suspended if the building were demolished, and that the prayer is valid on the roof of the Kaʿbah, is proof of this. The jurists have grasped this secret, and that is why they say that the qiblah is whatever lies in line with the Kaʿbah, up to the sky and below it down to the lowest layers of the earth.

The building of the Kaʿbah and this place are clothed with the divine manifestation, and because of this clothing blessing has come into them as well. In the view of the people of the subtle spiritual faculties, this same manifestation is the meaning of

﴿الرحمن علي العرش استوي﴾

that is, the manifestation of all-mercifulness takes place upon the Throne. It does not at all mean that Allah Most High is seated upon the Throne; the Throne cannot be a place for Allah Most High, because the Throne is limited while the divine Essence is unlimited, and the limited can in no way be a place for the unlimited. So the meaning of

استوي علي العرش

is simply that the manifestation of Allah Most High, in respect of the attribute of all-mercifulness, takes place upon it. It is for this reason that He said

﴿الرحمن علي العرش استوي﴾

and did not say

"الله علي العرش استوي"

because "Allāh" is a proper name of the Essence while "al-Raḥmān" is a name of an attribute. From this it becomes known that the Throne is not the seat of the Essence but the locus of manifestation of the attribute of mercy, in that the manifestation of mercy there is greater than in other places. This is the secret of facing the qiblah. (Excerpts from Maḥāsin-e Islām, p. 6, by Ḥaḍrat Ḥakīm al-Ummah, may his secret be sanctified).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
