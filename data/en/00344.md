### TITLE
Informing subordinates of the decisions of the consultative council

### QUESTION
Is it necessary for the trustees to tell those working under them about the decisions the shūrā (consultative council) has taken, and the reasons for them, or not?

### ANSWER
To keep clear of any accusation, and as a matter of caution, those working under them should be told, so that no suspicion of any kind arises. The Prophet ﷺ once went out with the Mother of the Believers, Ḥaḍrat Ṣafiyyah, and told the people who came upon him that Ṣafiyyah was with him, so that those who saw him would not fall into suspicion. In this the community was taught caution and a way of guarding against suspicion. However, if those working under them express their satisfaction and make it clear that they are content even without being told, then there is no harm in not telling them.
