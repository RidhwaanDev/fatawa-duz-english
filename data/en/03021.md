### TITLE
The ruling on attending the birthday of a non-Muslim teacher

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to attend the birthday of a non-Muslim teacher at an Islamic school and to sing the birthday song, or not? Kindly explain and be rewarded.

### ANSWER
On the occasion of a non-Muslim's birthday one should make this supplication: that Allah prolong his life and health and grant him guidance. As for celebrating a birthday, that is the practice of the non-Muslims. The noble Companions and the Successors did not celebrate the day of the birth of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, nor did they ever make arrangements for it; later people invented these things, which are no proof. Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ Ṣāḥib (raḥimahullāh) writes in Aḥkām al-Qurʾān:

قوله تعالى: ﴿لا ينهاكم الله عن الذين لم يقاتلوكم في الدين﴾

وبالجملة في الآية دليل على جواز البر والإحسان إلى الكافر إذا لم يكن محارباً لكن يشترط أن لا يقصد تعظيمه وإكرامه وفي التعبير بعدم النهي إشارة إلى أنه مأذون فيه فقط لا أنه مرغوب فيه. (أحكام القرآن: ۵/٤۵، إدارة القرآن).

Shaykh al-Islām Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Shabbīr Aḥmad ʿUthmānī (raḥimahullāh) says in Tafsīr ʿUthmānī:

In Makkah there were also some people who themselves did not become Muslims yet harboured no obstinacy or hostility towards those who did: they neither fought them over the matter of religion, nor became helpers of the oppressors in persecuting and expelling them. Islam does not forbid dealing with disbelievers of this kind with kindness and good character. When they deal with you gently and tolerantly, justice demands that you too treat them well and show the world how lofty the standard of Islamic morals is. The teaching of Islam is not that, if one nation of disbelievers is at war with the Muslims, you should start driving all disbelievers with the same stick without distinction; to do so would be contrary to wisdom and justice. It is necessary that a distinction be made between women, men, children, the aged and the young, and between the hostile and the peaceable, according to their circumstances — some detail of which has already passed in the notes on Sūrat al-Māʾidah and Āl ʿImrān. (تفسیر عثمانی، ص ۷۲۹).

Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

To supplicate for the long life of a non-Muslim with the intention that God may perhaps grant him guidance and that in his future life he may become illumined and enlightened by the light of Islam is permissible. (کفایت المفتی: ۹/۵٦، ط: دار الاشاعت).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
