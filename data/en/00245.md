### TITLE
The ritual bath when a patch is stuck to part of the body

### QUESTION
A woman has applied a greasy patch (contraceptive patch) to part of her body, which will remain stuck to her body for a week; after that week she will change it. During this period, will her obligatory bath be valid without removing the patch from her body, or not?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, if the woman did not remove this patch at the time of the obligatory bath and the area beneath it stayed dry, then the obligatory bath has not been performed, and in the sharia her prayer will not be accepted. Wiping over the patch will not be enough for her either, because wiping is for cases of pressing necessity and for a wound, and here there is no such particular necessity.

Consider what Fatāwā al-Shāmī states:

قوله بخلاف نحو عجين نعم ذكر الخلاف في شرح المنية في العجين واستظهر المنع لأن فيه لزوجة وصلابة تمنع نفوذ الماء ومفاده عدم الجواز إذا علم أنه لم يصل الماء تحته، قال في الحلية: وهو أثبت. قوله إن صلبا صرح به في شرح المنية وقال لا متناع نفوذ الماء مع عدم الضرورة والحرج. (فتاوى الشامي: ۱/۱۵٤، سعيد).
