### TITLE
Paying a deceased person's debt out of a pension fund

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to pay a deceased person's debt out of the money received from a pension fund or not?

### ANSWER
The money that is received from a pension fund may be used, and it is permissible and correct to pay the deceased's debt and other such dues out of it.

Consider what Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

The pension that an employee receives when he retires from service is permissible. (کفایت المفتی: ۸/۹٤) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
