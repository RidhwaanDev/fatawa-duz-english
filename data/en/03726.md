### TITLE
The ruling on binary options trading

### QUESTION
Is binary options trading permissible or not?

### ANSWER
The procedure of binary options trading is that the investor, with regard to capital that he owns, makes a prediction together with an agent as to whether the value of these assets will rise or fall within a fixed time (sometimes 60 seconds). Then, once the period has elapsed, it is seen whether his prediction was correct or wrong. If it was correct, he receives his capital together with an additional amount; otherwise he loses his capital as well, and the company with which he entered into this transaction takes possession of that asset. This method is current in cash dealings too. In a great many countries a transaction of this kind is prohibited, and in the view of the specialists in the field it is a new form of gambling; indeed the very meaning of "binary" is just this — win or lose, with no middle course.

If this transaction is examined from the point of view of the sharīʿah, it is a combination of both interest and gambling: that is, if the investor's prediction is correct he receives more, and if it is wrong he loses his entire capital. Both interest and gambling are unlawful by decisive text; hence such a transaction is impermissible and unlawful, and abstaining from it altogether is binding and necessary.

[البقرة: ۲۷۵]،
وقال تعالى: ﴿يا أيها الذين آمنوا إنما الخمر والميسر والأنصاب والأزلام رجس من عمل الشيطان فاجتنبوه لعلكم تفلحون﴾ [المائدة: ۹۰].
وقال في الهداية: الربا هو الفضل المستحق لا حد المتعاقدين في المعاوضة الخالي عن عوض شرط فيه. (الهداية: ۳/۷۸).
قال في الدر المختار: وسمي القمار قماراً لأن كل واحد من المقامرين ممن يجوز أن يذهب ماله إلى صاحبه، ويجوز أن يستفيد مال صاحبه وهو حرام بالنص، ولا كذلك إذا شرط من جانب واحد لأن الزيادة والنقصان لا تمكن فيهما بل في أحدهما تمكن الزيادة، وفي الآخر الانتقاض فقط فلا تكون مقامرة لأنها مفاعلة عنه، زيلعي. (الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ٦/٤۰۳، سعيد).
في التعريفات الفقهية: القمار مصدر قامر هو كل لعب يشترط فيه غالباً أن يأخذ الغالب شيئاً من المغلوب وأصله أن يأخذ الواحد من صاحبه شيئاً فشيئاً في اللعب ثم عرفوه بأنه تعليق الملك على الخطر والمال في الجانبين. (التعريفات الفقهية، ص: ۱۷۷).
(وكذا في جواهر الفقه: ۲/۲۲۷، وإمداد الفتاوى: ۳/۱٦۱).

Islām awr Jadīd Maʿāshī Masāʾil records:

One form of gharar, which the noble jurists have expressed as "suspending transfer of ownership upon a risk", is to make the transfer of ownership in contracts of exchange conditional upon some risk. "Risk" means some future event which may equally happen or not happen; to make the transfer of ownership dependent upon such an event — that is, "if this event occurs, I have made you the owner of such-and-such a thing of mine" — is called gambling.

Gambling, that is, qimār or maysir, is where payment is certain from one side and doubtful from the other. This is gambling; it is the suspending of transfer of ownership upon a risk, and it is one branch of gharar, which is unlawful. (اسلام اور جدید معاشی مسائل: ٤/۷٦) ۔
