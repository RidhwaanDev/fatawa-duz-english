### TITLE
Entering iḥrām for ʿumrah from within the Ḥaram

### QUESTION
A man entered iḥrām for ʿumrah in Makkah Mukarramah, since he was resident in Makkah Mukarramah. He completed the ʿumrah and came out of iḥrām. What is now due from him? I have heard that he ought to have gone outside the Ḥaram.

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, it was binding upon the man to go outside the Ḥaram, to Tanʿīm or some other place, and to enter iḥrām there. He did not do so but completed the ʿumrah, and therefore one expiatory sacrifice became due from him, one that is to be offered specifically within the Ḥaram.

قال ابن الهمام في فتح القدير: وإذ أحرم المكي للعمرة من الحرم فعليه دم إن لم يعد إلى ميقاته على ما عرف. (فتح القدير: ۳/۱۱٤، دار الفكر).

(وكذا في البحر الرائق: ۳/۵۰، وفتاوى الشامي: ۲/٤۷۹، سعيد، وبدائع الصنائع: ۲/۱٦۷، سعيد).

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh states:

So if a person living in Makkah Mukarramah or within the boundaries of the Ḥaram enters iḥrām for ʿumrah from the ground of the Ḥaram, an expiatory sacrifice will be due from him, because he has abandoned his mīqāt, even though these mawāqīt are established by consensus. (ʿUmdat al-Fiqh: 4/96, the mīqāt of the people of the Ḥaram).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
