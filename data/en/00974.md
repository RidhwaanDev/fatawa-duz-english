### TITLE
Zakat on a provident fund

### QUESTION
Is zakat due on a provident fund or not?

### ANSWER
The money that the government deducts from a salary falls under the heading of an entitlement, not of ownership, so there is no zakat on it before it is received. The increase that the government pays on that money is not ribā (interest), because ribā means a stipulated increase on money one already owns, whereas this money is not owned by the employee. If, on the other hand, a person deposits his own money in a bank and takes a stipulated profit on it, that is ribā and is unlawful.

Consider what Fatḥ al-Qadīr states:

فنقول قسم أبو حنيفة رحمه الله الدين على ثلاثة أقسام: إلى قوله

وضعيف وهو بدل ما ليس بمال كالمهر والوصية وغيره إلى قوله

وفي الضعيف لا تجب ما لم يقبض نصاباً ويحول عليه الحول بعد القبض عليه. (فتح القدير: ۲/۱٦۷، كتاب الزكاة، دار الفكر).

Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ states:

وأما دين الوسط فما وجب له بدلاً عن مال ليس للتجارة (إلى قوله) وفيه روايتان عنه وروى ابن سماعة عن أبي يوسف رحمه الله عن أبي حنيفة رحمه الله أنه لا زكاة فيه حتى يقبض المائتين ويحول عليه الحول من وقت القبض وهو أصح الروايتين عنه الخ. (بدائع الصنائع: ۲/۱۰، سعيد).

Jadīd Fiqhī Mabāḥith states:

Where a part of the salary of employees of government departments and other private bodies is deducted under the name of a fund and held on deposit, and is kept safe with a further increase added to it, and at retirement both the original sum and the increase are handed over to the employee, the position is this. By common agreement the fund money does not fall within the category of a strong debt, and the stronger view is that it falls within the category of a weak debt, since the employee has not yet taken possession of the money and so it has not entered his ownership. For the same reason the added amount is not placed in the category of interest. And in the case of a weak debt it is agreed that, once possession is taken, zakat for past years is not due. So zakat for the past will not be due on a provident fund. (Imdād al-Fatāwā: 2/49; Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 3/51; Kifāyat al-Muftī: 4/288 and 246; Jawāhir al-Fatāwā: 1/385; Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 5/147; ʿAzīz al-Fatāwā: 368).

أما دين الوسط فما وجب له بدلاً عن مال ليس للتجارة (إلى قوله) وفيه روايتان عنه

الخ. بدائع: ۲/۱۰، منحة الخالق: ۲/۲۰۷، ومثله في الشامي: ۲/۳۰٦ و مثله في مجمع الأنهر: ۱/۱۹۵.

(Jadīd Fiqhī Mabāḥith: 6/290, Idārat al-Qurʾān al-ʿUlūm al-Islāmiyyah).

Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

The money that the department pays into the provident fund, and likewise the amount added under the name of interest on the total of the two sums — all of this money is lawful. In the sharia it is not interest, even though the department calls it interest. The rule for paying zakat on all these sums is that their zakat is to be paid after the money has been received; paying zakat before it is received is not required. (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 8/97, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states:

Some money is deducted from the salaries of employees, and then a further sum is added to it and handed to them when their service ends. This is regarded as a government award. Zakat on it for the past years is not due. In future, once it has been received, zakat must be paid when a year has passed over the nisab.

وعند قبض مائتين مع حولان الحول بعده أي بعد القبض من دين ضعيف وهو بدل غير مال كمهر ودية وبدل كتابة. الدر المختار: ۲/٤۹، باب زكاة المال، سعيد.

(Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband: 6/331, Mudallal wa Mukammal).
