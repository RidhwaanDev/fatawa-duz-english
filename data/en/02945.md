### TITLE
A litany for safety from accidents

### QUESTION
What should one recite before leaving the house in order to be preserved from accidents and mishaps? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
To be preserved from accidents and mishaps, recite these supplications:

(1) When leaving the house, recite this supplication:

بِسْمِ اللَّه، تَوَكلْتُ على اللَّه، لا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّة إِلَّا بِاللَّه.

(2)
بِسْمِ الله الَّذِي لا يضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهٖ شَيْئٌ فِي الأرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَائِ وَهو السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيم.
(ابو داود، رقم: ۵۰۸۸، و ابن ماجہ، رقم: ۳۸٦۹، و الترمذی، رقم: ۳۳۸۸، و قال: حدیث حسن صحیح)۔

Ḥaḍrat Abān ibn ʿUthmān narrates from Ḥaḍrat ʿUthmān al-Ghanī (may Allah be pleased with him) that … whoever recites this supplication three times every morning and evening, nothing will be able to harm him.

(3) Whoever recites the following words ten times in the morning and in the evening, Allah Most High will record ten good deeds for him, will erase ten sins from his record of deeds, he will receive the reward of freeing a slave, and he will remain protected that day and that night from calamities and from all that is unpleasant. Those words are:

"لَا إِله إلا اللَّه وَحْدَه لا شَرِيك لَه لَه الْمُلْك وَلَه الْحَمْدُ وَهوَ على كلِّ شَيْئٍ قَدِيرٌ". (أخرجه الترمذي: ۲/۱۹٤، وابن ماجه، ص: ۲۸٤).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
