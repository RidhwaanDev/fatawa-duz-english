### TITLE
The ruling on short selling

### QUESTION
Is short selling permissible or not?

### ANSWER
A short sale is in reality a name for the sale of what one does not own: that is, the seller sells shares that are not yet in his ownership, but he expects that after the deal is struck he will hand these shares over to the buyer.

Short selling is therefore not permissible.

Jāmiʿ al-Tirmidhī states:

حدثنا قتيبة ثنا هشيم عن أبي بشر عن يوسف بن مالك عن حكيم بن حزام رضي الله عنه قال: سألت رسول الله ﷺ فقلت: يأتيني الرجل فيسألني من البيع ما ليس عندي أبتاع له من السوق ثم أبيعه قال: لا تبع ما ليس عندك.
(ترمذی شریف: ۱/۲۳۳، باب ما جاء فی کراہیۃ بیع ما لیس عندہ) ۔

A brief explanation of this matter has already been given under the question of buying and selling shares.

Yes — if only a promise to sell is made, without an actual sale being concluded, and the sale is contracted after the goods or shares have been obtained, then this form is permissible and valid.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
