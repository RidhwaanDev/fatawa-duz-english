### TITLE
Resting the hands on the knees when going down into prostration

### QUESTION
When going down into prostration, should one rest one's hands on one's knees or not?

### ANSWER
No explicit ruling on this question has been found. The unbroken practice of the Muslim community, however, is to do so, and it is also easier; for this reason resting the hands on the knees when going down into prostration appears to be the better course. The books of fiqh do state that when going down into prostration one places the knees on the ground first and then the hands, and as evidence they cite "حديث النهي عن البروك كبروك الإبل", a hadith found in Jāmiʿ al-Tirmidhī. That can only be done easily when the hands are resting on the knees.

Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

إذا أراد السجود وضع أولاً ما كان أقرب إلى الأرض فيضع ركبتيه أولاً ثم يديه ثم أنفه ثم جبهته. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۷٥)

It also states:

ويكره وضع اليد قبل الركبتين إذا سجد. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱۰۷، الفصل الثاني فيما يكره في الصلاة، بلوچستان)

Bahishtī Zēwar states:

Then, saying the takbīr, he should go down into prostration with both hands resting on his knees. (بہشتی زیور، گیا رواں حصہ ص ۸۸٤، فرض نماز کے بعض مسائل)

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

I have not seen this particular point stated explicitly in any book. The established practice is that people place their hands on their thighs and knees — that is, they support themselves on them — as they move from the standing position after bowing down into prostration, just as they support themselves on their thighs and knees when rising from prostration.

ويمكن أن يشم رائحة الاستدلال من حديث "استعينوا بالركب" الجامع الصغير.
(فتاوی محمودیہ: ۵/٦۱۸، نماز کی سنتوں کا بیان، جامعہ فاروقیہ)

Saying "اللہ اکبر", he should go down into prostration with both hands resting on his knees. (رسول آ کرم ﷺ کا طریقہ نماز: ص ۲۲۳، از قلم مفتی جمیل احمد نذیری) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
