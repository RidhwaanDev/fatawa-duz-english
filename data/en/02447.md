### TITLE
The last part of the time is what counts for the sacrifice

### QUESTION
If a man comes to own enough wealth to make the sacrifice necessary on the evening of the twelfth of Dhū al-Ḥijjah, does the sacrifice become necessary upon him or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question the sacrifice has become necessary upon this man. If, however, he had already offered a sacrifice earlier, then that first one is enough. (فتاوی الشامی: ٦/۳۱٦ ۔ سعید، و الفتاوی الہندیۃ: ۵/ ۲۹۲) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
