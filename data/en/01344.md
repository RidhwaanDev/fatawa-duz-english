### TITLE
Spacing children by three or four years without any excuse

### QUESTION
Nowadays most married couples make their own personal plans; for example, they consider a gap of three or four years between children necessary, without any medical constraint. Is this permissible or not, especially when no fear about livelihood is in mind either?

### ANSWER
Many people think: why place the burden of children upon themselves right away, when a few years of free living may be enjoyed first? And so they adopt measures to prevent pregnancy. Outwardly this aim is not one that is contrary to the sharīʿah, so in the light of the general principles permission for it appears to exist; but since it takes the form of turning away from children, it is not free of unseemliness. (Baḥth o Naẓar: 2/353, "Birth control from the Islamic point of view", under the supervision of Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Qāḍī Mujāhid al-Islām Qāsimī, may Allah have mercy on him.)
