### TITLE
Contracting a marriage by text message on a mobile phone

### QUESTION
An Indian man married a Filipina convert to Islam in the following way. From India the man sent a text message making the offer of marriage in front of two witnesses. From the Philippines the woman sent a text message accepting the marriage in front of two witnesses. Was a marriage contracted in this way or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question the marriage of the man mentioned has not been contracted. The reason is that the sitting in which a marriage is contracted must be one and the same: the offer and the acceptance must be made in the presence of two witnesses, and the witnesses must hear both of them in that same sitting. On this basis the marriage should be performed again. The simplest way is for the woman to appoint an agent, and for the agent, on the girl's behalf, to carry out the offer and acceptance at the marriage gathering in the presence of two witnesses.

See what Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ states:

فأما إذا كان أحدهما غائباً لم ينعقد حتى لو قالت امرأة بحضرة شاهدين: زوجت نفسي من فلان وهو غائب فبلغه الخبر فقال: قبلت أو قال رجل بحضرة شاهدين تزوجت فلانة وهي غائبة فبلغها الخبر فقالت: زوجت نفسي منه لم يجز وإن كان القبول بحضرة ذينك الشاهدين، وهذا قول أبي حنيفة رحمه الله ومحمد رحمه الله. (بدائع الصنائع: ۲/۲۳۲، سعيد).
