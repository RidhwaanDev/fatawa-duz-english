### TITLE
Paying off a loan with interest money

### QUESTION
Some people buy blankets and the like with interest money and distribute them among those entitled to receive them. On one occasion no interest money was available, so they took a loan from the lillāh (general charity) funds, bought blankets and so on with it, and then, when interest money came in, they repaid the loan with it. Is this method permissible or not?

### ANSWER
The basic rule regarding interest money is that it should be returned to its real owner; but if the owner is not known, it should be given away in charity to the poor and needy without any intention of earning reward. Some scholars, however, have ruled it permissible to pay off a loan of this kind with interest money as well, so when there is a need there is room to act on their position. Even so, spending it after first employing the device of transferring ownership is preferable and is the more cautious course.

Al-Sunan al-Kubrā of al-Bayhaqī states:

عن عبد الله بن مسعود رضي الله عنه أنه اشتري جارية فذهب صاحبها فتصدق بثمنها وقال: اللهم عن صاحبها فإن كره فلي وعلى الغرم. (رواه البيهقي في سننه الكبرى: ٦/۱۸۸، كتاب اللقطة، دار الفكر، بيروت).

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

فيجب رد عين الربا لو قائماً. (الدر المختار: ۵/۱٦۹، سعيد).

Al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq states:

ويردونه على أربابه إن عرفوهم وإلا يتصدقوا به لأن سبيل الكسب الخبيث التصدق إذا تعذر الرد. (البحر الرائق: ۸/۲۰۱، فصل في البيع، كتاب الكراهية، كوئته).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

Question: I am employed at a madrasah, and the salary I am paid comes out of the bank's interest money. What I wish to know is whether I should take this interest money or stop taking it. What is the way out?

Answer: A salary should not be taken from interest money; that is not permissible. The way for you to be paid a salary is for those people to take a loan from someone and pay you your salary from it, and then to repay the loan with that money. This is the only form in which there is room for it. (Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 3/173).

Mufti Kifāyatullāh says:

Interest money that has been received may be spent on the needy and the like, on paying off the debts of Muslims who are in debt, on education — whether primary or religious — and on every kind of public welfare work. (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 8/59).

Niẓām al-Fatāwā states:

Once the device of transferring ownership to an entitled recipient has been used, it will be correct to use the money even for cleaning a mosque's lavatory and the like. The device of transferring ownership is that the whole of the interest money is given to poor and needy people who are entitled to charity, so that they become its owners, and the recipient then gives it to the mosque as a donation of his own.

وقدمنا أن الحيلة أن يتصدق على الفقير، ثم يأمره بفعل هذه الأشياء. (الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ۲/۳٤۵، كتاب الزكاة، باب المصرف، سعيد). (Niẓām al-Fatāwā: 1/199).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
