### TITLE
Making amends after someone not entitled to zakat has used zakat money

### QUESTION
Someone gave zakat money to a man who was not entitled to zakat, and even so he used the money. Now the man is deeply ashamed. What is the way for him to make amends? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
It was not permissible for a man of means to take zakat money, and when he then used it himself he became sinful. The way to make amends now is that he should repent and seek forgiveness for this act of his, and give the equivalent amount in charity, or return it to the giver. But since returning it carries the risk of gossip, ill feeling and suspicion, he should give that amount in charity to a poor person. Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

باب المصرف، أي مصرف الزكاة والعشر:

ويشترط أن يكون الصرف (تمليكاً) لا إباحة كما مر (لا) يصرف

(و) لا إلى (غني) يملك قدر نصاب فارغ عن حاجته الأصلية من أي مال، فكان كمن له نصاب سائمة لا تساوي مئة درهم

وفي رد المحتار: ولا يسترد في الولد والغني وهل يطيب له؟ فيه خلاف، وإذا لم يطب، قيل يتصدق، وقيل يرد على المعطي

(الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ۲/۳۵۳، سعيد).

Fatāwā Qāsimiyyah states: Allah Most High has made zakat obligatory in order to meet the needs of the poor and the destitute — not so that someone's business may be helped along by means of zakat and those entitled to it be deprived of it. Allah Most High says in the Noble Qur'an that only the poor and the destitute are entitled to zakat:

﴿إِنَّمَا الصَّدَقَاتُ لِلْفُقَرَاءِ وَالمَسَاكينِ ﴾ (التوبة: ٦۰).

Moreover, in the noble hadith a person who is fit and healthy and has the power to earn is strictly forbidden to take zakat money:

لا يحل الصدقة لغني ولا لذي مرة سوى. (سنن الترمذي: ٦۵۸، ۱/۱٤۲، باب ما جاء في فضل الصدقة).

(فتاوی قاسمیہ: ۱۰/٦۷۹) ۔

Islāmī Fiqh states: However, if the recipient comes to know that this money or grain was from ʿushr or zakat, he should return it. (اسلامی فقہ: ۱/٤۷۱) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
