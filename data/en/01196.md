### TITLE
Some questions concerning tamattuʿ

### QUESTION
What do the scholars of the religion say about this matter: an āfāqī went to Makkah during the months of ḥajj and performed ʿumrah. After completing the ʿumrah he travelled to Madinah. On his return he performed a second ʿumrah, and then entered iḥrām for ḥajj from Makkah.

(1) Is his tamattuʿ valid or not?

(2) Is the dam of tamattuʿ due from him or not?

(3) Is any compensatory dam (dam-e jabr) due from him or not?

(4) Was the tamattuʿ constituted by the first ʿumrah or by the second?

(5) Is it valid for an āfāqī to perform more than one ʿumrah during the months of ḥajj?

(6) If he entered iḥrām for the ḥajj alone from Madinah, will the tamattuʿ be valid or not?

(7) Will any compensatory dam be due from him or not?

(8) What is the position regarding an āfāqī pilgrim going outside the mīqāt during the months of ḥajj?

(9) Which of these two ways is the better one? (The questioner: Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Shabbīr Aḥmad, principal of this madrasah.)

The Jurist of the Ummah, Ḥaḍrat Mufti Maḥmūd Ḥasan Gangohī (may Allah have mercy on him), was kind enough to give the answers. They are as follows:

### ANSWER
If a person performed ʿumrah during the months of ḥajj and then travelled to Madinah, and after returning from there came in iḥrām for the ḥajj alone, his tamattuʿ will be valid. This is according to Imām Abū Ḥanīfah, unlike the two companions, in whose view the first tamattuʿ became void. Yes, if he then came from Madinah in iḥrām for ʿumrah and afterwards performed the ḥajj, in their view the tamattuʿ would be valid; but according to Imām Abū Ḥanīfah he should not do so. This passage has been taken from footnote 1 of Mawlānā Shabbīr Muhammad's marginal notes in Muʿallim al-Ḥujjāj, p. 218.

From this passage the answers to all your questions emerge, either explicitly or by implication. Now take them one by one:

(1) According to the two companions his tamattuʿ is valid.

(2) In their view the dam of tamattuʿ is obligatory.

(3) Because he went outside the mīqāt, his first tamattuʿ became void, and this requires that a compensatory dam be obligatory upon him. "This is according to the position of the two companions (may Allah have mercy on them); according to the position of Imām Abū Ḥanīfah (may Allah have mercy on him) no compensatory dam will be due, and the fatwa is upon this."

(4) The tamattuʿ was constituted by the second ʿumrah.

(5) There is a difference of opinion on this; the question is mentioned in Muʿallim al-Ḥujjāj, p. 221. (That is, a mutamattiʿ may perform a second ʿumrah after the first one, before the ḥajj — Muʿallim al-Ḥujjāj: p. 256, Idārat al-Qurʾān — and in footnote 1 the disagreement has been recorded, as has already been mentioned.)

(6) According to Imām Abū Ḥanīfah his tamattuʿ will be fulfilled.

(7) No compensatory dam is obligatory upon him. "This is the position of Imām Abū Ḥanīfah."

(8) It is improper.

(9) According to Imām Abū Ḥanīfah the better way is that he come from Madinah in iḥrām for the ḥajj alone.

In acts of worship, where there is a difference of opinion, the fatwa is given according to the statement of Imām Abū Ḥanīfah. فقط واللہ سبحانہ و تعالیٰ اعلم
