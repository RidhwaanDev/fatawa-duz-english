### TITLE
A lean ram or sheep under a year old is not valid

### QUESTION
If a lean, weak ram or sheep is less than a full year old, will the sacrifice be valid or not?

### ANSWER
If a sheep or a ram is so plump that it looks like a full year old, then six months is enough; but if it is not that plump, then one less than a full year old is not permissible. (فتاوی الشامی: ٦/ ۳۲۲) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
