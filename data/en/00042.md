### TITLE
Al-Walīd ibn ʿUqbah (may Allah be pleased with him) was a Companion

### QUESTION
Was al-Walīd ibn ʿUqbah a Companion or not? Is there any disagreement about his being a Companion? If there is, please explain that as well.

### ANSWER
By common agreement al-Walīd ibn ʿUqbah is a Companion. He accepted Islam at the conquest of Mecca. He was a very brave fighter, generous, and a poet.

Al-Walīd ibn ʿUqbah was the grandson of the Prophet's ﷺ paternal aunt through her daughter, and the half-brother of ʿUthmān (may Allah be pleased with him) on his mother's side. He was brought up under ʿUthmān's care. He had two and a half years in which to benefit from the Prophet's ﷺ company, and he held high office in the time of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, of Abū Bakr (may Allah be pleased with him) and of ʿUmar (may Allah be pleased with him). Imam al-Dhahabī says:

وكان مع فسقه ـ والله يسامحه ـ سخياً ممدحاً شاعراً شجاعاً قائماً بأمر الجهاد. (سير أعلام النبلاء: ۳/٤۱۵).

We say that he was not the sinner; the sinners were those who brought him the false report.

Ibn al-Athīr says:

وأقام عليها خمس سنين هو من أحب الناس إلى أهلها. (الكامل: ۳/۸۳، ذكر عزل سعد عن الكوفة وولاية الوليد).

In another place he says:

وأنه كان محبوباً إلى الناس فبقي كذلك خمس سنين وليس لداره باب. (الكامل، ذكر عزل الوليد عن الكوفة وولاية سعيد).

Ibn Jarīr al-Ṭabarī has written the same. And when he was removed from office, the historians wrote:

ولقد تفجع عليه الأحرار والمماليك" (تاريخ الأمم والملوك: ۲/٦۱۲، ط: بيروت، ومختصر تاريخ مدينة دمشق: ۸/۸٦).

That is, free men and slaves alike grieved at his dismissal. In al-Walīd's place ʿUthmān (may Allah be pleased with him) appointed Saʿīd ibn al-ʿĀṣ as governor of Kufa, but people kept saying:

يا ويلتا عزل الوليد وجاءنا مجوعاً سعيد

ينقص في الصاع ولا يزيد فجوع الإماء والعبيد

(تاريخ الأمم والملوك: ۲/٦۱۲، ط: بيروت، ومختصر تاريخ مدينة دمشق: ۸/۸٦).

That is: Saʿīd ibn al-ʿĀṣ has come and starved us; alas that al-Walīd was dismissed.

We quote here a summary of his merits from Mawlānā Bashīr Aḥmad Ḥiṣārī's book ʿUthmān Dhū al-Nūrayn (p. 274):

"He was forbearing by nature, large-hearted, brave and capable (al-Istīʿāb). He was exceedingly generous and dear to the people, and in five years of governorship his standing with them never declined. Al-Shaʿbī was once sitting with a grandson of al-Walīd ibn ʿUqbah when Muḥammad mentioned the bravery of Maslamah ibn ʿAbd al-Malik, the famous conqueror and commander of the Umayyads. Al-Shaʿbī (may Allah have mercy on him) said: if you had seen al-Walīd ibn ʿUqbah's rule and his campaigns, you would not have spoken of anyone else — and this continued until he was removed from his post."
