### TITLE
Zakat on money deposited in a bank

### QUESTION
Is zakat due on money deposited in a bank or not?

### ANSWER
Zakat is due on money deposited in a bank, because the owner deposited it of his own choice and the money is still his property. So once a year has passed over it, zakat is due.

Consider what Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

Whether money is kept in a current account at a bank or placed in a fixed deposit, zakat is due on the deposited sum in either case. For zakat to be due, ownership and possession are required. Ownership of money deposited in a bank plainly belongs to the depositor. Possession, on the face of it, is not his; but since he placed the money in the bank of his own will and choice, the bank acts as the real owner's agent in holding it, and in this way the depositor's own possession is also established, indirectly. It is for this reason that the jurists have ruled that zakat is due on wealth left with someone for safekeeping, since the trustee's possession is the owner's possession. So zakat is due on every deposited sum that the owner has deposited by his own choice and consent. (Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 1/216, Nuʿaymiyyah).

See also: Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm: 6/134; Imdād al-Aḥkām: 2/25; and Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 2/14.
