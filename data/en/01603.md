### TITLE
The waiting period after intercourse entered into by mistake

### QUESTION
A man had intercourse with another man's wife, thinking she was his own wife, and only afterwards learnt that she was not. Will the waiting period be binding on that woman or not? Likewise, what is the ruling on the waiting period in a defective marriage? And what is the ruling regarding a child born from such a union?

### ANSWER
In the case described — intercourse by mistake, and a defective marriage in which intercourse has taken place — the waiting period is binding and necessary in both situations. The waiting period is three complete menstrual cycles. If she does not menstruate because she is too young or too old, the waiting period is three lunar months, and if she is pregnant the waiting period lasts until she gives birth. The child is not illegitimate; its lineage is sound, and it belongs to the man who had intercourse by mistake.

Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

إذا دخل الرجل بالمرأة على وجه شبهة أو نكاح فاسد فعليه المهر وعليها العدة ثلاث حيض إن كانت حرة

فإن كانت لا تحيض من صغر أو كبر فعدة الحرة ثلاثة أشهر. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۵۲۷، وكذا في بدائع الصنائع: ۳/۱۹۲، سعيد. والدر المختار: ۳/۵۰۱، سعيد).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
