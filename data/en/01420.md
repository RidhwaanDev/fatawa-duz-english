### TITLE
Looking at the Sacred House during ṭawāf

### QUESTION
What is the position of looking towards the Sacred House while performing ṭawāf? Is it permissible or impermissible?

### ANSWER
Raising one's eyes and looking towards the Sacred House while performing ṭawāf is contrary to proper etiquette; it is not impermissible or forbidden. The etiquette for one performing ṭawāf is rather that he should keep his gaze lowered, so that presence of heart may be attained in the ṭawāf. Consider what Ghunyat al-Nāsik states:

وينبغي أن لا يجاوز بصره محل مشيه كالمصلي لا يجاوز بصره محل سجوده لأنه الأدب الذي يحصل به اجتماع القلب. (غنية الناسك، ص ٦۵، فصل في مستحبات الطواف).

Tuḥfah-yi Ḥujjāj, quoting Majmūʿah-yi Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Karachi (2/256), states:

Turning one's face and gaze towards the Kaʿbah during ṭawāf is neither forbidden nor disliked; it is, however, contrary to etiquette. The etiquette is that the one performing ṭawāf should keep his gaze on the ground where he is walking, so that his attention is not drawn this way and that and the ṭawāf is performed with an undivided mind. (Tuḥfah-yi Ḥujjāj, p. 37). (Likewise in Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 4/548).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
