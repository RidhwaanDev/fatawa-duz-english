### TITLE
Fornication with the wife's sister and prohibition by affinity

### QUESTION
There are two sisters, both of them married. The younger sister's husband is now involved with the elder sister in unlawful acts, and matters have even reached the point of fornication. In such a situation, does his own wife leave his marriage or not? And if she does leave the marriage, what should he do now? Please give a satisfying answer in the light of the sacred law; it would be a great kindness.

### ANSWER
In the case described, the younger sister's husband becoming involved with the elder sister and committing fornication with her is impermissible — indeed forbidden. He must repent of this evil act. He should have no dealings or contact whatever with the elder sister, and it is necessary that he keep away from her. Nevertheless, no effect falls upon either sister's marriage in the sacred law, and each sister remains in her husband's marriage as before.

Consider what al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

وفي الخلاصة: وطئ أخت امرأته لا تحرم عليه امرأته. وفي الشامي: هذا محترز التقييد بالأصول والفروع وقوله: لا تحرم أي لا تثبت حرمة المصاهرة، فالمعني: لا تحرم حرمة مؤبدة. (الدر المختار مع الشامي: ۳/۳٤، فصل في المحرمات، سعيد).

Al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq states:

أراد بحرمة المصاهرة الحرمات الأربع حرمة المرأة على أصول الزاني وفروعه نسباً ورضاعاً وحرمة أصولها وفروعها على الزاني نسباً ورضاعاً كما في الوطئ الحلال ويحل لا صول الزاني وفروعه أصول المزني بها وفروعها. (البحر الرائق: ۳/۱۰۱، فصل في المحرمات، كوئته).

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states:

By committing fornication with his wife's sister, his wife has not become unlawful to him, because no ground of prohibition is found in this. (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm: 7/644, 328, Mudallal wa Mukammal; and likewise Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 11/409, Mubawwab wa Murattab)

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
