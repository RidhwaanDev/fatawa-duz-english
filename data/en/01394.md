### TITLE
Breaking an expiatory fast in the mistaken belief that the sun had set

### QUESTION
A man had deliberately broken a fast of Ramadan, and he then began keeping the consecutive fasts of expiation. One day, before the call to the maghrib prayer, he happened to hear the sound of ṣalāt and salām being recited from a Barelwī mosque, and mistaking it for the call to prayer he broke his fast, although the sun had not yet set. Must this man now begin the fasts again or not? That is, is his continuity broken or not?

### ANSWER
ʿAllāmah al-Rāfiʿī (may Allah have mercy on him) has related from Qāḍīkhān that if a person thought the sun had set when in reality it had not, the continuity is not lost. Consider these words of ʿAllāmah al-Rāfiʿī (may Allah have mercy on him):

لكن قد صحح قاضيخان في شرح الجامع الصغير سقوط الكفارة في المسألتين وشبههما بمن أفطر وأكبر ظنه أن الشمس غربت ثم ظهر عدمه. (التحرير المختار علي رد المحتار: ۲/۱٤۹، سعيد).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
