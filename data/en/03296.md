### TITLE
The ruling on repeating prayers in which the head was not wiped during ablution

### QUESTION
After reaching puberty, a girl never wiped her head whenever she performed ablution for prayer; she did not know that wiping the head is also obligatory. Later she went to study at a madrasah and learnt of it. Is she now required to repeat all the prayers she has performed since reaching puberty, or not?

### ANSWER
In our time, a Muslim's being unaware of the obligations of Islam is not an acceptable excuse, because acquiring knowledge of the fundamentals of Islam after reaching puberty is a matter of the greatest importance and necessity. On that basis, in the case in question it is binding and necessary to make up all the prayers from puberty onwards. Consider what al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah records:

طلب العلم فريضة بقدر الشرائع وما يحتاج إليه لا مر لا بد منه من أحكام الوضوء والصلاة وسائر الشرائع ولأمور معاشه وما وراء ذلك ليس بفرض. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۳۷۷).

التيسير بشرح الجامع الصغير states:

طلب العلم فريضة على كل مسلم، وهو العلم الذي لا يعذر المكلف في الجهل به والعلم ستة أقسام فرض كفاية إذا قام به البعض سقط الحرج عن الكل وإلا أثم الكل وفرض عين وهو ما يحتاجه المكلف في الفرض كوضوء وصلاة وصوم

(التيسير: ۱/۳۲۹، ط: الرياض).

مجمع الأنهر في شرح ملتقى الأبحر states:

وفي البزازية: طلب العلم والفقه

وهو أقسام فرض وهو مقدار ما يحتاج إليه لا قامة الفرائض ومعرفة الحق والباطل والحلال والحرام. (مجمع الأنهر: ٤/۱۸٤، ط: بيروت).

Qāmūs al-Fiqh states:

As far as the ruling in this world is concerned, ignorance takes several forms. The first case is that a person fails to carry out something the sharia has commanded. His ignorance will not excuse him from that obligation; rather he must make good the omission. And since he did not carry out the command at all, he will also be deprived of its reward. For example, there was enough impurity on a person's clothing or body that prayer is not valid with it, and he unknowingly performed the prayer with that impurity on him and only learnt of it afterwards: making the prayer up then becomes necessary. (Qāmūs al-Fiqh: 3/168)

See further: (Nūr al-Anwār, p. 299; Uṣūl al-Bazdawī, p. 338, Karachi; and Kashf al-Asrār: 4/457, Beirut).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
