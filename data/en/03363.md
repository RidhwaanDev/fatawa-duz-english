### TITLE
Joining a witr congregation with the intention of tarāwīḥ

### QUESTION
A man reached the mosque at a time when the witr prayer was being performed in congregation. He thought it was the tarāwīḥ prayer, so he joined with the intention of tarāwīḥ. When the imam stood up for the third rakʿah, he realised that this was the witr prayer. Has this man's witr prayer been performed or not? Note that he had already performed the obligatory prayer.

### ANSWER
This man's witr prayer has not been performed. He should therefore add one more rakʿah, so that these four rakʿahs become a voluntary prayer, and he should perform the witr again. See what is mentioned in Sharḥ al-Munyah:

اقتدى به على ظن أن الإمام يصلي التراويح فإذا هو في الوتر يتمه معه ويضم رابعة ولو أفسدها لا شيء عليه.
(غنية المتملي في شرح منية المصلي، ص: ٤۱۰، ط: سهيل اكيڈمي).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:
In the case in question, after the imam gives the salām he should add a fourth rakʿah and complete the prayer, and these four rakʿahs will become a voluntary prayer. The witr will still remain due from him and he will have to perform it. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۳/۳۰۹) ۔
