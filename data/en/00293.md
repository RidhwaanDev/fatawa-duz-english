### TITLE
An important question about menstruation

### QUESTION
A woman menstruated for three days, then remained pure for four days, and then bled again for three days. What we need to know is: according to Imam Muḥammad (may Allah have mercy on him), how many of her days are days of purity and how many are days of menstruation?

### ANSWER
In this case, if the two periods of bleeding are added together they come to six days in all, and adding the four days of purity makes ten days altogether. Both of the conditions of Imam Muḥammad's position (may Allah have mercy on him) are therefore met. Those conditions are these: if the period of purity is shorter than the period of bleeding, and the whole falls within the maximum period of menstruation, then the intervening purity is counted as menstruation. So in the case in question, all ten days count as menstruation.
