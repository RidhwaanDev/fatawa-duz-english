### TITLE
Zakat on a poultry farm and a fish pond

### QUESTION
Is zakat due on a poultry farm and a fish pond or not?

### ANSWER
No zakat is due on the land of a poultry farm or a fish pond, or on the equipment and other things that go with them. But if the chickens and the fish were bought with the intention of selling them, then once a year has passed zakat is obligatory on their value.

Consider what Īḍāḥ al-Masāʾil states:

Zakat is not obligatory on the value of the poultry farm and the pond themselves. If, when the fish and their young were bought and put into the pond, the intention was to sell them, then zakat is due on their value. If some of the fish escape from the pond in a flood or the like, no zakat is due on those; zakat is due on the ones that remain. (Īḍāḥ al-Masāʾil: 107).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

There is no zakat on the land, the building or the equipment of a poultry farm or a fish pond. If the hens and chicks were bought with the intention of selling the birds themselves, zakat is obligatory on their value; but if the intention is to sell their eggs and their young rather than the birds, there is no zakat. If fish or their young were bought and put into the pond, zakat is obligatory on their value; otherwise not. In every case zakat is due on the income of the poultry farm and the pond. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 4/300).
