### TITLE
Zakat collected for a clinic that is then lost

### QUESTION
There is a clinic which has a zakat fund. Medicines are bought from it and distributed among poor patients. If zakat is taken for this purpose and is then lost while in the agent's hands, has the zakat been discharged or not?

### ANSWER
As a rule, those who benefit from a hospital are the people of the surrounding area. Just as in the case of a madrasah it is the students of that madrasah who are meant, so here it is the poor patients of the hospital who are meant. The zakat is therefore discharged and does not have to be paid again. This case is exactly like the case of the madrasah collector, and the evidence for it has already been given.
