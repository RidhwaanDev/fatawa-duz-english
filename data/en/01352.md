### TITLE
The ruling on zakat being due on the funds of charitable institutions and associations

### QUESTION
People have formed an association in which money is collected, and out of this money the shrouding and burial of the deceased and other charitable works are carried out. The money reaches thousands, or even hundreds of thousands. Once a year has passed, will zakat be due on this money or not? And is zakat obligatory on charitable institutions or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, the funds of the association and of charitable institutions have already left the ownership of the donors. An association and other charitable institutions are a legal person, yet no particular individual owns them. That is precisely why, when one of the members of the association passes away, no share of this money is returned to the deceased's heirs — that is to say, it does not form part of his estate. Zakat will therefore not be due or binding on funds of this kind.

Consider what is recorded in Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ:

وأما الشرائط التي ترجع إلى المال فمنها الملك فلا تجب الزكاة في سوائم الوقف والخيل المسبلة لعدم الملك وهذا لأن في الزكاة تمليكاً والتمليك في غير الملك لا يتصور. (بدائع الصنائع: ۲/۹، سعيد). (وكذا في الدر المختار: ۲/۲۷۷، سعيد).

وقال في المبسوط: قال الشيخ الإمام الأجل

الزكاة لا تجب إلا باعتبار الملك والمالك ولهذا لا تجب في سوائم الوقف ولا في سوائم المكاتب. (المبسوط للإمام السرخسي: ۳/۹٤، ط: بيروت).

وفي الموسوعة الفقهية الكويتية: الشرط الأول: كون المال مملوكاً لمعين: فلا زكاة فيما ليس له مالك معين، ومن هنا ذهب الحنفية إلى أن الزكاة لا تجب في سوائم الوقف، والخيل المسبلة، لأنها غير مملوكة. قالوا: لأن في الزكاة تمليكاً، والتمليك في غير الملك لا يتصور. (الموسوعة: ۲۳/۲۳٦، ط: الكويت).

Kifāyat al-Muftī states: money belonging to a locality that is the common money of the community and has been collected to be of use to people, or money belonging to a mosque, is not subject to zakat. (Kifāyat al-Muftī 4/265, Dār al-Ishāʿat)

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states: wealth that has no particular owner, but is owned instead by a mosque, a madrasah or some other institution, is not subject to zakat. (Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil 2/50)

See further: (Jadīd Fiqhī Mabāḥith 6/20, 21)

In the Hanafi school zakat is not due upon a legal person. Consider what Dars-e Tirmidhī states: no zakat is payable on jointly held wealth in its capacity as a legal person. (Dars-e Tirmidhī 2/425)

See further: (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā 1/432)

For a detailed treatment of legal personality, see: (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā, volume four, pp. 660–666)
