### TITLE
The objection to the narrations of the Ṣaḥīḥayn concerning the consummation of the marriage of Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhā), and the answer

### QUESTION
Objection: Some "enlightened" people raise an objection against the narrations of the Ṣaḥīḥayn "which have come concerning the marriage and the consummation of the marriage of Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah (raḍiyallāhu taʿālā ʿanhā)", namely that their narrator is Hishām from ʿUrwah — that is, these narrations were transmitted from ʿUrwah by Hishām, and Hishām's narrations of his Madīnan period are sound while his narrations of his Iraqi period are not sound, for confusion and mixing occurred in them because his memory had deteriorated?

### ANSWER
First of all one should bear in mind that it is not only Hishām who transmits from Ḥaḍrat ʿUrwah; rather, besides Hishām, those who narrate from him are ʿAbdullāh al-Madanī, Imām al-Zuhrī al-Madanī and ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿUrwah. Then there are a number of persons who transmit from Hishām: Abū Usāmah, Ismāʿīl ibn Zakariyyā, ʿAbdah, ʿAbd al-Raḥmān, ʿAlī ibn Mushir, Sufyān, ʿAbdullāh ibn Muḥammad ibn Yaḥyā al-Madanī, Wuhayb ibn Khālid, Wakīʿ, Abū Muʿāwiyah and Ḥammād ibn Zayd.

Furthermore, it is not only ʿUrwah who narrates from Ḥaḍrat ʿĀʾishah (raḍiyallāhu taʿālā ʿanhā); rather there are several further persons, who are as follows: Abū ʿUbaydah, al-Aswad, Abū Salamah ibn ʿAbd al-Raḥmān, Jābir ibn Zayd, al-Qāsim ibn Muḥammad, Yaḥyā ibn ʿAbd al-Raḥmān, Ibn Abī Mulaykah and ʿAbd al-Malik ibn ʿUmayr, and others.

Even if, for the sake of argument, the narrations of Hishām's Iraqi period were conceded to be confused, even then Madīnan narrators too have transmitted this hadith from Hishām — thus there is ʿAbdullāh ibn Muḥammad ibn Yaḥyā al-Madanī [and those who transmitted this hadith from ʿUrwah besides Hishām are Abū al-Zinād ʿAbdullāh and Imām al-Zuhrī, both of whom are Madīnan].
