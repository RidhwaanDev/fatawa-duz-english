### TITLE
Attending the mosque for congregational prayer with a bag collecting bodily waste

### QUESTION
A man is ill, and a bag has been fitted to his abdomen into which waste — the kind normally passed through the back passage — collects by means of a tube. What is the ruling on this man coming to the mosque and praying with the congregation? Does this count as bringing filth into the mosque or not? And does it make any difference whether there is a bad smell or not?

### ANSWER
In ordinary circumstances it is strictly disliked to bring foul-smelling things or filth into the mosque, especially when there is a risk of soiling the mosque. But in the case of a person who is legally excused, as in the case asked about, this bag takes the same ruling as his own stomach and belly. So if there is no smell, the bag is concealed, and it causes no revulsion to others, it is permissible and correct for him to go to the mosque. Filth passing into the bag does break the ablution; but since this man is legally excused and filth keeps coming out of his stomach continuously, his ablution will break with the expiry of the prayer time, unless something else occurs that breaks it.

It is established that in the time of the Prophet ﷺ a woman with chronic bleeding kept iʿtikāf (spiritual retreat) in the mosque. Filth was present with her, but it was concealed and there was no smell, so the Prophet ﷺ did not object.

Consider what Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī states:

عن عائشة رضي الله تعالى عنها قالت: اعتكفت مع رسول الله ﷺ امرأة من أزواجه مستحاضة فكانت تري الحمرة والصفرة فربما وضعنا الطست تحتها وهي تصلي. (رواه البخاري: ۱/۲۷۳/۱۹۹۱، باب اعتكاف المستحاضة، فيصل)

Commenting on this hadith, ʿAllāmah al-ʿAynī (may Allah have mercy on him) says that as long as the clothing and the mosque are not soiled there is no problem, and that the same applies to anyone in the same position as a woman with chronic bleeding — that is, those who are legally excused: they too are permitted to enter the mosque and to keep iʿtikāf.

ʿUmdat al-Qārī states:

ومما يستنبط منه: جواز اعتكاف المستحاضة، وجواز صلاتها لأن حالها حال الطاهرات وإنها تضع الطست لئلا يصيب ثوبها أو المسجد وأن دم الاستحاضة رقيق ليس كدم الحيض، ويلحق بالمستحاضة ما في معناها كمن به سلس البول والمذي والودي ومن به جرح يسيل في جواز الاعتكاف. (عمدة القاري: ۳/۱۳۰، كتاب الحيض، باب الاعتكاف للمستحاضة، دار الحديث، ملتان).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
