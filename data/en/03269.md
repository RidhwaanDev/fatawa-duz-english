### TITLE
The cost of dividing jointly held estate property

### QUESTION
Some time ago a man died. His estate includes a great deal of wealth, land, properties and houses. The division among the heirs was carried out through lawyers, and three hundred thousand rand was spent on the process. The question now is this: will the expense deducted from the estate be borne in proportion to the heirs' shares, or will it be divided by the number of heirs? For example, if the deceased leaves three sons, two daughters and a wife, will the expense of three hundred thousand rand be divided equally among these six persons, so that each bears 50,000; or will it be borne in proportion to their shares, so that the wife bears one eighth, and the sons and daughters bear it
للذكر مثل حظ الأنثيين
? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the case described, the cost of dividing the jointly held property, three hundred thousand rand, will be borne equally by the six persons.

See what al-Hidāyah records:

فإن لم يفعل نصب قاسماً بالأجر معناه بأجر على المتقاسمين، لأن النفع لهم على الخصوص، ويقدر أجر مثله كي لا يتحكم بالزيادة

وأجرة القسمة على عدد الرؤوس عند أبي حنيفة رحمه الله قال أبو يوسف رحمه الله ومحمد رحمه الله: على قدر الأنصباء. (الهداية: ٤/٤۱۱، كتاب القسمة).

Majmaʿ al-Anhur fī Sharḥ Multaqā al-Abḥur states:

وهو أي أجر المثل على عدد الرؤوس أي رؤوس المتقاسمين عند الإمام لأن تمييز الأقل من الأكثر كتمييز الأكثر من الأقل في المشقة وعندهما على قدر السهام لأنه مؤنة الملك فيقدر بقدره. وبهامشه قال: والأول الصحيح لأن المعقود عليه التمييز لا غير كما في المضمرات وغيرها. (مجمع الأنهر مع الدر المنتقي: ۲/٤۸۹، ط: دار إحياء التراث).

The author of al-Qawl al-Rājiḥ writes:

القول الراجح هو قول الإمام أبي حنيفة، قال العلامة الحصكفي وهو أي ما يقدر على عدد الرؤوس عنده وعندهما على قدر السهام والأول الصحيح. (القول الراجح: ۲/۲۷۷).

Al-Fatāwā al-Sirājiyyah states:

أجرة القسام على عدد الرؤوس لا على عدد الأنصباء. (الفتاوى السراجية، ٤٦۱، زمزم).

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā states:

All the co-owners will share equally in the cost of having the land valued, because there is a benefit in it for all of them: each of them will come to know the value of the land. And just as the divider's fee falls equally upon all the co-owners, so too the cost of the valuation will fall equally upon all the co-owners. (فتاوی دار العلوم زکریا: ۵/۸۱۰، ط: بمبئی)۔

Note: Over the past ten or fifteen years, land in some provinces of India, and in Gujarat in particular, has become extremely valuable. Those Gujarati families who have settled abroad are selling their land; and since these families have been away for something like a hundred or two hundred years, the price of a property, once it is sold, has to be divided not among ten or fifteen people but among hundreds. Countless questions of this kind concerning inheritance have come to our Dār al-Iftāʾ, and — praise be to Allah — the Dār al-Iftāʾ has resolved them. If all these questions and answers were published they would make up a volume in themselves. Since there is no particular benefit for scholars and students in these charts and cases, these questions and answers have not been included in the eighth volume; a few important questions and answers relating to inheritance have, however, been included in this publication.
