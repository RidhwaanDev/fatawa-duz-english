### TITLE
The ruling on slaughtering a bird whose head has been severed

### QUESTION
A man shot a bird with a gun without reciting Bismillāh. The bird's neck was cut through, with only a little remaining; he then recited Bismillāh over it and drew the knife across it. Will this slaughter be valid in the sharīʿah or not?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, if the place of slaughter — that is, the four vessels of the throat — was still intact, and he cut all four after reciting Bismillāh, then the slaughtered animal is lawful. Otherwise this slaughter will be of no account and the animal will be unlawful.

Fatāwā al-Shāmī states:

شاة قطع الذئب أوداجها وهي حية لا تذكي لفوات محل الذبح، ولو انتزع رأسها وهي حية تحل بالذبح بين اللبة واللحيين. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۳۰۸، سعيد).
(وكذا في الفتاوى البزازية على هامش الهندية: ٦/۳۰۸، والفتاوى الهندية: ۵/۲۹۱).
وفي فتاوى قاضيخان: قوله عليه الصلاة والسلام: الذكاة ما بين اللبة واللحيين"، والذكاة الكاملة فري الأوداج الأربعة وهي الحلقوم والمري والعرقان اللذان بينهما الحلقوم والمري

وإن علم حياتها وقت الذبح أكلت على كل حال. (فتاوى قاضيخان على هامش الفتاوى الهندية: ۳/۳٦٦).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

Question: A bird was struck by a bullet in such a way that its neck was carried clean away and no trace of the head remained. It was slaughtered at the part of the neck, and some blood came out of it. Is this slaughter valid in the sharīʿah or not?

Answer: If the vessels mentioned above were still intact — that is, if that portion of the neck in which these vessels lie was still there — and it was then slaughtered, the slaughter is valid, even though the head no longer remained. If these vessels were no longer there — that is, if not even that portion of the neck in which these vessels lie remained — then the slaughter is not valid. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۷/۲۸٤، جامعہ فاروقیہ).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

If the hen is alive and so much of the neck remains that it can be slaughtered, then it is correct to slaughter it and eat it; but it is not correct to eat the severed head. And when the whole neck has been broken off along with the head, and no portion of the requisite measure for slaughtering remains, then there is no way of slaughtering it and eating it is unlawful. (فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۵/۳٦٦، مکتبۃ الاحسان، دیوبند).
