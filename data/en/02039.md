### TITLE
Giving interest money to support a political party

### QUESTION
An election was held here in Barbados, and a Muslim organisation wished to support one of the parties in it with financial assistance. To support that party the organisation collected donations from Muslim brothers and sisters, and it also collected interest money from Muslims. The question now is whether it is permissible for a Muslim to collect interest money for the sake of a political party. If it is not permissible, what is the ruling on the money that has already been collected and handed over?

### ANSWER
The scholars differ over spending interest money on public welfare, as has been mentioned earlier. In the case in question, however, the general interest of the Muslims in a non-Muslim country is bound up with this financial support, and the government is a non-Muslim one; so if interest money is collected and given as support in this way, and the Muslims do not use it themselves, there should be room for it.

Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

Question: May interest money be spent on religious education, on public welfare, and on poor Muslims?

Answer: Interest money that has been received may be spent on the needy and the like, on paying off the debts of Muslims who are in debt, on education — whether primary or religious — and on every kind of public welfare work. (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 8/68).

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

Concerning bank interest it should be remembered that it may neither be spent on oneself nor given away as charity; apart from these it may be used in other ways — for instance, to meet the individual need of some needy person, or to carry out a work of collective benefit such as digging a well. This money should not be put into mosques and madrasahs, but the scholars have permitted its use for building lavatories. (Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil, vol. 1, p. 253).

See further: (Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 2/90; and Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 7/16).

In sum, since a great many scholars regard transferring the money into the ownership of the poor as necessary, spending it on work of this kind after first transferring ownership to a poor person takes one out of the disagreement, and it will then be correct by the agreement of all.
