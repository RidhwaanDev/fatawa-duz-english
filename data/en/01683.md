### TITLE
Talking during marital intercourse

### QUESTION
What is the ruling on talking during intercourse? Is it disliked or permissible?

### ANSWER
In the case in question it is not disliked for a husband and wife to talk to each other during intercourse. Talking to someone else, however, is disliked.

Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ (may Allah have mercy on him) writes in Imdād al-Muftīn:

Speaking during intercourse is disliked,

كما في الدر المختار: ويكره الكلام في المسجد وخلف الجنازة وفي الخلاء وفي حالة الجماع،

but this is where one speaks to someone else; there is no harm in speaking to one's own wife. (امداد المفتین جلد دوم: ۱۰۳۲، کتاب الحظر و الاباحۃ) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
