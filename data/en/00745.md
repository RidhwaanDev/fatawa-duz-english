### TITLE
A traveller who prays in full behind a resident and then repeats the prayer after it is invalidated

### QUESTION
If a traveller prayed four rakʿahs behind a resident and the imam's prayer became invalid, then when the traveller prays his prayer again, will he pray four rakʿahs or two?

### ANSWER
In the case described, since the imam's prayer became invalid, the traveller will pray two rakʿahs if he prays on his own. Consider what Shāmī states:

ولو أفسده صلي ركعتين لزوال المغير. (شامي: ۲/۱۳۰، سعيد)

Al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

وإن اقتدى مسافر بمقيم أتم أربعاً وإن أفسده يصلي ركعتين. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱٤۲، باب صلاة المسافر. وكذا في بدائع الصنائع: ۱/۹۳، سعيد)

ʿUmdat al-Fiqh states:

A traveller may follow a resident imam within the time of the prayer. So if a traveller follows a resident imam within the time, he prays the full four rakʿahs in order to keep with the imam. If he then invalidates that prayer, or it becomes invalid for some reason, and he now prays alone or follows a traveller, he prays two rakʿahs, because the reason that had made four necessary is gone. But if he again follows a resident, he prays four. (عمدۃ الفقه: ۲/٤۲۲، مسافر کی نماز کا بیان، کراچی ۔ و کذا فی فتاوی رحیمیہ: ۵/۱۳) ۔
