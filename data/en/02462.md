### TITLE
Sacrificing an animal whose teats have dried up

### QUESTION
If an animal's teats have dried up on account of disease, is the sacrifice of such an animal valid or not?

### ANSWER
If, on account of disease, one teat of a sheep or goat has dried up, or two teats of a cow, buffalo or she-camel have dried up, the sacrifice is not permissible. (شامی: ٦/۳۲۵، سعید، و لسان الحکام، ص ۳۸۹) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
