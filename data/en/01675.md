### TITLE
Whether the costs of childbirth fall upon the husband

### QUESTION
The jurists have written that the wife's medical treatment is not the husband's responsibility. But what is the ruling on the expenses of childbirth, which is not the woman's own act alone, since the husband has a share in it too?

### ANSWER
In the case in question it is not settled that the wife's medical treatment is not the husband's responsibility. The jurists wrote what they did on the basis of the custom and usage of their own time, since in their day this was not considered a basic necessity. But in view of present-day conditions and custom, medical treatment has become a basic necessity, and so it ought to fall upon the husband. Some detail on this has already been given under "باب النفقات".

As for the expenses of pregnancy and childbirth, the jurists have written on this as well: if the husband calls the midwife, the husband is to bear the cost; if the woman calls her, the woman is to bear it; and if the midwife came of her own accord, then according to some the husband bears it and according to others the woman. ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him), however, gave preference to the view that the husband should bear it, because most of the benefit goes to the child, and the child's maintenance and other needs are the father's responsibility.

In our own time the expenses of childbirth too have become a basic necessity, and so in every case the husband will bear all of these costs. Even so, the woman should decide with an eye to her husband's circumstances. If the husband's financial position is not very comfortable and the woman is from a well-off family, and her parents gladly bear the cost, there is no harm in that — indeed it is worthy of the highest praise.

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

وفيه أجرة القابلة على من استأجرها من زوجة وزوج ولو جاء ت بلا استئجار قيل عليه وقيل عليها. وفي الشامي: قوله قيل عليه عبارة البحر من الخلاصة: فلقائل أن يقول عليه لأنه مؤنة الجماع ولقائل أن يقول عليها كأجرة الطبيب وكذا ذكر غيره ومقتضاه أنه قياس ذو وجهين لم يجزم أحد من المشايخ بأحدهما خلاف ما يفهمه كلام الشارح ويظهر لي ترجيح الأول لأن نفع القابلة معظمه يعود إلى الولد فيكون على أبيه تأمل. (الدر المختار مع فتاوى الشامي: ۳/۵۸۰، باب النفقات. وكذا في البحر الرائق: ٤/۱۷۷، وفتح القدير: ٤/۳۸۷، ط: دار الفكر).
