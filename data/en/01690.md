### TITLE
A husband preventing his wife from observing the veil

### QUESTION
A woman, māshāʾAllāh, wishes to act upon the commands of the sharia and to cover her face completely, but her husband does not want this and forbids her, telling her to go out with him without a veil. What should the woman do?

### ANSWER
In the case in question a woman's veiling is required and desired by the sharia, and the husband's forbidding it is an order contrary to the sharia — something the pure sharia does not allow: "لا طاعۃ لمخلوق في معصیۃ الخالق". So the woman should not cast the law of the sharia behind her back. While acting upon the sharia she should try to explain the matter to her husband and encourage him, but she should not be obstinate or defiant with him, should not treat him badly, and should not fall short in fulfilling his rights. Allah will soften her husband's heart and he will come round to the veil that the sharia requires. In our times the best course is for her to acquaint her husband with the work of tablīgh and send him out with a group for a while; when he returns, God willing, he will himself tell her to observe the veil.

قال الله تعالى: "وإذا سألتموهن متاعاً فاسألوهن من وراء حجاب". (سورة الأحزاب: الآية: ۵۳).

If the noble verse just quoted did not require the covering of the face, what need would there be for "من وراء حجاب"? There would then have been no harm in her coming before them.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
