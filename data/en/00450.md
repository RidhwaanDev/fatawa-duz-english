### TITLE
Making the supplication after tarāwīḥ and before the witr

### QUESTION
In our mosque during the blessed month of Ramadan the collective supplication is made after the witr, and some of the worshippers are busy with voluntary prayers at that time. Is it necessary to wait for them?

### ANSWER
The practice of our senior scholars is to make the supplication after the tarāwīḥ and before the witr, not after the witr. Supplication is also answered after the completion of the Qur'an. So if you make the supplication after the tarāwīḥ instead of after the witr, that will be better: everyone will be kept content, and there will be no need to wait and no inconvenience. Even so, if someone does not take part in the supplication, he should not be reproached or blamed, and disputing about it should be avoided.

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states:

It is correct and recommended to make supplication once the tarāwīḥ is completed, and this has been the practice of the earlier and later generations. A further supplication after the witr is then not necessary; once is enough — that is, after the tarāwīḥ is enough. (فتاوی دار العلوم دیوبند: ٤/۲٥۳)

It also states:

The correct position is that supplication after the completion of the Qur'an, and after the tarāwīḥ prayer at all times, is a sunnah and recommended; and a hadith states that this is a time when supplications are answered. This is why the practice of our senior scholars and shaykhs is to supplicate after the tarāwīḥ and after the completion of the Qur'an. (دار العلوم دیوبند: ٤/۲۷۱) ۔
