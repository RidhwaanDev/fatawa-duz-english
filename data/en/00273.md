### TITLE
Performing dry ablution on the wall of a bus

### QUESTION
A man is travelling by bus and the time for prayer arrives. There is no arrangement for ablution on the bus, and the bus does not stop. May this man perform dry ablution on the wall of the bus or not?

### ANSWER
If there is dust on the wall of the bus or the like, he may perform dry ablution on it; otherwise he may not.

Al-Hidāyah states:

ويجوز التيمم عند أبي حنيفة ومحمد بكل ما كان من جنس الأرض من التراب والرمل والحجر والجص وكذا يجوز بالغبار. (الهداية: ۱/۵۱، باب التيمم).

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

The walls of trains, buses and the like are usually made of iron, wood or plastic, and dry ablution may not be performed on them. During a journey, however, dust normally settles on them, and in the view of Imam Abū Ḥanīfah (may Allah have mercy on him) dry ablution may be performed on dust as well. (Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 1/98).
