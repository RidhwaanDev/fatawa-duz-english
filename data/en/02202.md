### TITLE
The ruling on working for a bank

### QUESTION
A man is employed at a bank in South Africa. Is such employment permissible or not? And is the salary lawful or unlawful? Is there any kind of permissible employment to be found in a bank or not? And if a business is one hundred per cent interest-based, what then is the ruling on working there? بینوا بالتفصیل توجروا باجر جزیل

### ANSWER
Employment in a bank that is connected purely with interest-based accounting and dealings is not permissible, and the salary from it is not lawful, because it is assistance in interest, and according to the noble hadith no kind of assistance in interest is permissible. Those departments of a bank that are not connected with interest, however — for instance work as a peon, a cook, a driver or a sweeper in a bank — are permissible and correct, and the salary too is lawful.

Some scholars are of the view that nowadays banks handle imports and exports and take payment of electricity and telephone bills and the like, over and above interest-based dealings; that is, lawful business is also carried on there, so that employment connected with such business ought to be permissible, provided one is not involved in interest-based dealings. Even so, it is preferable to keep away.

See what the noble hadith states:

عن جابر رضي الله عنه قال: لعن رسول الله ﷺ آكل الربا وموكله وكاتبه وشاهديه وقال هم سواء. (رواه مسلم: كتاب المساقات والمزارعة، باب الربا، ۲/۲۷).

Imdād al-Muftīn states:

In the hadith the curse has been pronounced upon those who assist in interest-based dealings as well. (Imdād al-Muftīn: 2/703).

Fatāwā Bayyināt states:

It is not permissible to take up employment in a bank, and likewise it is not permissible to take a bank's salary. The reason is that interest-based business is carried on in a bank, and to be employed in a bank is to become a helper in interest-based business, and to take a salary from it is to take a salary from interest — whereas the Prophet ﷺ cursed those who take interest, those who pay it, those who record it and those who witness it, together with all who assist in it, and said that they are all equal in the sin. "Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim" states:

عن جابر رضي الله عنه قال: لعن رسول الله ﷺ آكل الربا وموكله وكاتبه وشاهديه وقال هم سواء. (رواه مسلم: كتاب المساقات والمزارعة، باب الربا، ۲/۲۷).

It is narrated from Ḥaḍrat Jābir (may Allah be pleased with him) that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ cursed the one who consumes interest, the one who feeds it to others, the one who records it and its witnesses, and said that they are all equal in the sin. (Fatāwā Bayyināt: 4/71, "The ruling on the salaries of bank employees").

Niẓām al-Fatāwā states:

It is permissible to be employed in a bank for work that is itself permissible; not every kind of employment there is impermissible. (Niẓām al-Fatāwā: 1/193).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

When every dealing in a bank is an interest-based transaction, then the sharia gives no permission to accept employment there. The noble hadith states:

عن جابر رضي الله عنه قال: لعن رسول الله ﷺ آكل الربا وموكله وكاتبه وشاهديه وقال هم سواء.

This establishes that assisting in sinful work and taking any kind of part in it is not permissible but sinful.

Allah Most High says:

﴿ولا تعاونوا على الإثم والعدوان﴾،

Translation: And do not assist one another in sin and in wrongdoing and transgression, and keep fearing Allah; indeed Allah is severe in punishment. (Sūrat al-Māʾidah). (Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 9/289).

Islām awr Jadīd Maʿāshī Masāʾil states:

Question: The employees of which departments of a bank have lawful income?

Answer: The income of all those departments in which one has to do the work of interest is impermissible. Dealing in interest, or recording it, or bearing witness to it, or assisting in the matter in any way whatsoever, is all impermissible. As for dealings that have no connection with interest — for instance a man sitting as a cashier to whom someone brings a cheque and hands it over, or a driver or a peon — to that extent there is room. (Islām awr Jadīd Maʿāshī Masāʾil: 4/135; and likewise in Fatāwā Ḥaqqāniyyah: 6/258).

Kitāb al-Fatāwā states:

Those bank employees who do the accounting, the writing and reading, and the transacting fall within that group; they are directly assisting in disobedience, and so this employment is not permissible for them. Lower-grade employment, however — for instance a sweeper, a watchman and the like, who have no connection with the business and its dealings and are appointed to guard the building itself and similar duties — is permissible. (Kitāb al-Fatāwā: 5/392).

See further: (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 7/307, 310, Dār al-Ishāʿat; and Fatāwā ʿUthmānī: 3/395).
