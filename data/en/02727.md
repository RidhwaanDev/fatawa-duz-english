### TITLE
The charge of deception in attaching artificial hair

### QUESTION
Some people say that attaching artificial hair involves confusion and deception. What is the answer to this? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
With real human hair there is a danger of falsification and disguise; with plastic hair, cords and the like that danger is slight. This is why the jurists, wherever they mention wool and similar things, say nothing about falsification. It shows that since cords, wool and so on are not hair as such, the possibility of deception is small. Yes, if someone uses them for the very purpose of deceiving, it will be forbidden and impermissible. Ordinarily artificial hair is put on for adornment or to cover a defect. Even so, deliberate imitation of disbelieving and sinful women should be avoided.

See what ʿAllāmah al-Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

قوله سواء كان شعرها أو شعر غيرها، لما فيه من التزوير

وإنما الرخصة في غير شعر بني آدم تتخذه المرأة لتزيد في قرونها، وهو مروي عن أبي يوسف رحمه الله، وفي الخانية: ولا بأس للمرأة أن تجعل في قرونها وذوائبها شيئاً من الوبر. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۳۷۳، سعيد).
