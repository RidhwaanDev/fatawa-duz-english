### TITLE
Taking another person's help in ablution

### QUESTION
If someone wants help from another person in his ablution and tells him to pour the water or to wash his hands and feet for him, what is the ruling?

### ANSWER
There is room for taking another person's help in ablution to the extent of pouring water; but for another person to wash one's hands and feet without a valid excuse is disliked. Consider the following:

وحاصله أن الاستعانة في الوضوء إن كانت بصب الماء أو استقائه أو إحضاره فلا كراهة بها أصلاً ولو يطلبه وإن كانت بالغسل والمسح، فتكره بلا عذر، ولذا قال في التتارخانية: ومن الآداب: أن يقوم بأمر الوضوء بنفسه ولو استعان بغيره جاز بعد ألا يكون الغاسل غيره، بل يغسل بنفسه. (فتاوى الشامي: ۱/۱۲۷).

Seeking help is of three kinds. (1) Seeking help in having water brought is permissible. (2) Seeking help in having water poured is contrary to what is preferable, unless there is a valid excuse. (3) Seeking help such that the other person pours the water and rubs the limbs is disliked if there is no valid excuse.
