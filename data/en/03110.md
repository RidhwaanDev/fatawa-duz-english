### TITLE
The ruling on studying actuarial science

### QUESTION
Nowadays there is a discipline in the universities which is called actuarial science. Is it permissible in the Sharīʿah to learn this science or not? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
By actuarial science is meant the knowledge of mathematics and statistics by which help is gained in economics and finance. The full scope of these sciences cannot be gathered into any one subject; rather the term is applied to the combination of mathematics, statistics, economics and finance which is named actuarial science. (summarised from the website Wikipedia.org)

If no unlawful act is involved in acting upon these sciences, then there is no harm in acting upon them, and there is no harm in learning them either, so that one may distinguish between the lawful and the unlawful and keep away from the unlawful.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
