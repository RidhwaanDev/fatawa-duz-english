### TITLE
A woman's sacrifice paid for out of her husband's wealth

### QUESTION
A woman is an owner of the niṣāb, but she has no cash in hand. If she takes money from her husband and offers the sacrifice with it, will her sacrifice be discharged or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question the woman's sacrifice will be discharged. (فتاوی الشامی: ٦/ ۳۱۵) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
