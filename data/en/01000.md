### TITLE
How a woman with only jewellery is to pay her zakat

### QUESTION
A woman is divorced. All she owns is jewellery, which exceeds the nisab for zakat, and she has no money besides it. How is she to pay her zakat?

### ANSWER
In the case described, the woman owns jewellery in excess of the nisab, so she is obliged to pay zakat. If she has no cash, she may pay a little at a time each month, and then there is no need for her to sell the jewellery.

Consider what al-Tirmidhī states:

عن عمرو بن شعيب عن أبيه عن جده، أن امرأتين أتتا رسول الله ﷺ وفي أيديهما سواران من ذهب، فقال لهما: أتؤديان زكاته فقالتا: لا، فقال لهما رسول الله ﷺ: أتحبان أن يسوركما الله بسوارين من نار، قالتا: لا، قال: فأديا زكاته. (ترمذي شريف: ۱/۱۳۸، باب ما جاء في زكاة الحلي، فيصل).

Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

A woman owns her jewellery and her trousseau, and the zakat on it is due from her. Since she generally has no money with which to pay it, she takes it from her husband and pays it, or her husband pays it at her instruction and with her permission. If the husband neither pays it nor gives her the money, the woman is obliged to sell her belongings and pay it, because the obligation rests on her. (کفایت المفتی: ٤/۲٦٦، کتاب الزکاۃ، پہلا باب، دار الاشاعت).

Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband states:

Jewellery that a wife owns and has in her possession, and that amounts to the nisab, is her own liability for zakat. If her husband pays it on her behalf as a favour, or she takes the money from him and pays it, or she pays it out of the allowance her husband gives her, that is permissible. And if she has nothing at all, then the woman will have to pay the zakat out of that very jewellery. (فتاوی دار العلوم دیوبند: ٦/۲۸۵، مدلل و مکمل، دار الاشاعت).

In another place it states: if no other means of paying the zakat is available, then this must certainly be done — a portion of the jewellery equal to the zakat is to be given as zakat, for this is an obligation laid down by Allah. (فتاوی دار العلوم دیوبند: ٦/۱۰۹، مدلل و مکمل، دار الاشاعت).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

Zakat is discharged even when it is paid a little at a time. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۹/٤٦٦، مبوب و مرتب جامعہ فاروقیہ).

Āp ke Masāʾil awr un kā Ḥal states:

The wife should either save from her personal allowance and pay the zakat, or give a portion of the jewellery as zakat. (آپ کے مسائل اور ان کا حل: ۳/۳٤۵، مکتبہ لدھیانوی).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
