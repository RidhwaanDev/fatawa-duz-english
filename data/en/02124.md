### TITLE
Not paying the government the full amount of tax

### QUESTION
Very often traders collect tax from their customers but do not pay the full amount over to the government. Is this tax a trust? Is it necessary to hand it over or not?

### ANSWER
When traders collect it from their customers they are acting as agents of the government, and the tax money is in their hands as a trust. It is necessary and obligatory to pay it to the government; otherwise it will be a breach of trust. Allah Most High says:

﴿يا أيها الذين آمنوا لا تخونوا الله والرسول وتخونوا أماناتكم﴾.

al-Fatāwā al-Hindiyyah states:

وأما صفتها أي صفة الوكالة فإنها من العقود الجائزة الغير اللازمة

ومنه أنه أمين فيما في يده كالمودع. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۳/۵٦۷).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
