### TITLE
The wisdom behind the ṭawāf of the House of Allah and the saʿy between Ṣafā and Marwah

### QUESTION
I was taking part in the Hajj when a gentleman with an English education began asking what wisdom there is in circling the Kaʿbah and in the saʿy between Ṣafā and Marwah. What answer should be given to such people?

### ANSWER
The prophets (peace be upon them) — and in particular Ibrāhīm (peace be upon him) and Muḥammad ﷺ — performed the ṭawāf and the saʿy, and to follow them and to resemble them is a very great blessing and a means of obtaining lights and outpourings of grace. People in this world take pride and delight in resembling famous sportsmen; will resembling and following the prophets (may blessings and peace be upon them) not be a means of obtaining blessings? It certainly will. The noble verse:

﴿ومن يطع الرسول فقد أطاع الله﴾. (سورة النساء)

and the noble hadith:

من تشبه بقوم فهو منهم". (أبو داود).

should be kept before us. Furthermore, the noble House of Allah is the House of Allah and the emblem of the religion. In circling it there is an indication that the earth and the world are round — as if to say: I pledge to travel round the whole earth to spread the religion and to establish its authority. Keeping before me the words

﴿وما أرسلناك إلا كافة للناس بشيراً ونذيراً﴾

I shall keep proclaiming the religion. And the saʿy between Ṣafā and Marwah is a memorial of Hājar's (may Allah be pleased with her) search for sustenance, in which there is an indication that I shall keep moving from one place to another in search of sustenance — yet it does not involve going round in a circle as the ṭawāf does, which shows that in seeking sustenance there is no need for excessive exertion or for scouring the whole world, whereas striving for the religion should encompass the whole earth. It should be remembered that the saʿy is also an act of religion which brings reward and recompense, and it is obligatory.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
