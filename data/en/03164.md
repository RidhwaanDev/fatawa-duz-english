### TITLE
Investigation of the report of Ḥaḍrat Sahl ibn ʿAbd Allāh (may Allah be pleased with him)

### QUESTION
Investigation of the report of Ḥaḍrat Sahl ibn ʿAbd Allāh (may Allah be pleased with him).

### ANSWER
ʿAllāmah al-Sakhāwī (may Allah have mercy on him) has related the report of Ḥaḍrat Sahl ibn ʿAbd Allāh (may Allah be pleased with him) on the authority of Ibn Bashkuwāl; accordingly we quote it from Ibn Bashkuwāl's book

"القربة"

Consider it:

قال ابن بشكوال: قال شيخنا أبو القاسم: وروينا عن سهل بن عبد الله: من قال في يوم الجمعة بعد العصر: "اللهم صلِّ على محمد النبي الأمي وعلي آله وسلم، ثمانين مرة غفرت له ذنوب ثمانين سنة". (القربة لابن بشكوال، ص ۱۱٤، رقم: ۱۱٤).

Between the first report — that is, the above-mentioned report of Ḥaḍrat Abū Hurayrah (may Allah be pleased with him) — and this second report there are three differences.

(a) In the first report the words

قبل أن يقوم من مكانه"

are present, whereas in the second they are not.

(b) In the first report there is

وسلم تسليماً"

and in the second

"تسليماً"

is not there.

(c) In the first report there is

"كتبت له عبادة ثمانين سنة"

whereas in the second report these words are not present.

Furthermore, Ibn Bashkuwāl (may Allah have mercy on him) has not mentioned any chain for this hadith; he has quoted it only on the authority of his own teacher Abū al-Qāsim, whereas between Shaykh Abū al-Qāsim Aḥmad ibn Taqī (died 532 AH) and Sahl ibn ʿAbd Allāh (may Allah be pleased with him) there are wide deserts — a long distance. And along with the chain being broken, this report is also stopped at Ḥaḍrat Sahl ibn ʿAbd Allāh رضي الله عنه himself.

Therefore, without knowing the condition of the chain and of its men, it is difficult to say anything about this report; and to say that ʿAllāmah al-Sakhāwī (may Allah have mercy on him) was a very great man and held a high rank in the science of criticism and endorsement, or that Shaykh Ibn Bashkuwāl (may Allah have mercy on him) was a very great man, or that his teacher Abū al-Qāsim was a great man — this is not enough, in terms of the science of the principles of hadith, to establish a hadith.

لأنه لا يشفي العليل ولا يروي الغليل.

The reason is that a great many hadith scholars have compiled books of hadith, and all these hadith scholars were great, godly, trustworthy and precise men in whose learning and practice there was no deficiency; yet in some of their books fabricated reports are also included. However, on this subject another hadith is narrated, which al-Khaṭīb al-Baghdādī mentioned in his world-famous book Tārīkh Baghdād. Consider it:

قال الخطيب البغدادي: وهب بن داود بن سليمان أبو القاسم المخرمي حدث عن إسماعيل بن علية روي عنه محمد بن جعفر المطيري وكان ضريراً ولم يكن ثقة. أخبرنا أبو طالب عمر بن إبراهيم الفقيه حدثنا عمر بن إبراهيم المقرئ حدثنا محمد بن جعفر المطيري حدثنا وهب بن داود بن سليمان الضرير حدثنا إسماعيل بن إبراهيم حدثنا عبد العزيز بن صهيب عن أنس بن مالك رضي الله عنه قال: كنت واقفاً بين يدي رسول الله ﷺ فقال: من صلي على يوم الجمعة ثمانين مرة غفر الله له ذنوب ثمانين عاماً فقيل له كيف الصلاة عليك يا رسول الله قال: تقول: "اللهم صلِّ على محمد عبدك ونبيك ورسولك النبي الأمي"، وتعقد واحداً. (تاريخ بغداد: ۱۳/٤۸۹/۷۳۲٦).

This hadith is also narrated from Ḥaḍrat Abū Hurayrah (may Allah be pleased with him), and Imam al-Dāraquṭnī quoted it in his book al-Afrād; but its chain is weak, the investigation of which has already passed above.

However, in al-Khaṭīb al-Baghdādī's report only one narrator, Wahb ibn Dāwūd, was described by al-Khaṭīb al-Baghdādī as

لم يكن ثقة"

Although Shaykh al-Albānī has called it fabricated, Shaykh al-Albānī's statement does not appeal to our understanding, because there is in its chain no liar or forger of hadith but only a single weak narrator, and to declare it fabricated on that account is very great daring.

The upshot of the discussion is that the above-mentioned hadith of Ḥaḍrat Anas (may Allah be pleased with him) is sound in comparison with the other hadiths and may be acted upon. Yes, in it there is no restriction to Friday after ʿaṣr, so it may be recited at any time during the whole day.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم بالصواب۔
