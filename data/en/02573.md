### TITLE
Aborting a pregnancy resulting from fornication

### QUESTION
An unmarried girl mistakenly committed fornication with a boy and became pregnant, and five months have now passed over the pregnancy. She now wishes to have the pregnancy aborted in order to escape disgrace. Is she permitted to do so or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
Among the jurists there is a difference of opinion over aborting a pregnancy before four months, but at the session of the Fiqh Academy it was said to be impermissible according to the sound position; and after four months it is forbidden and impermissible by consensus. In this there is no difference between a child of fornication and any other child.

Note what Shāmī states:

وفي الذخيرة: لو أرادت إلقاء الماء بعد وصوله إلى الرحم قالوا: إن مضت مدة ينفخ فيه الروح لا يباح لها وقبله اختلف المشايخ فيه والنفخ مقدر بمائة وعشرين يوماً بالحديث. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۳۷٤، سعيد).

Jadīd Fiqhī Mabāḥith states:

If the pregnancy is the result of fornication by consent (then absolutely), or (where the fornication was by force) four months have passed over the pregnancy, then in these situations an abortion is not permitted. (جدید فقہی مباحث: ۱/۳۷۳، ط: ادارۃ القرآن) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
