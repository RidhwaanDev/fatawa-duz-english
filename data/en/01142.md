### TITLE
A woman travelling for Hajj with her son-in-law

### QUESTION
May a woman travel for Hajj with her son-in-law or not?

### ANSWER
In the case described, the woman may travel for Hajj with her son-in-law. But if there is a fear of temptation she should not go; and in any case being alone together and the like must be avoided.

قال الله تعالى: ﴿حرمت عليكم

وأمهات نسائكم﴾.

وعن ابن عباس رضي الله تعالى عنه قال: حرم من النسب سبع ومن الصهر سبع ثم قرأ حرمت عليكم أمها تكم. (رواه البخاري، مشكاة: ۲/۲۷۵).

وفي الشامي: فصل في المحرمات

وأمهات الزوجات. (فتاوى الشامية: ۳/۲۸، فصل في المحرمات، سعيد).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

A son-in-law is a maḥram for his mother-in-law; marriage between the two is forbidden for ever. Al-Fatāwā al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah states:

القسم الثاني المحرمات بالصهرية: وهي أربع فرق الأولى أمهات الزوجات. (ʿĀlamgīrī)

But these are days of temptation, and caution is needed in dealings between in-laws, especially when they are young. (Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 8/287; likewise in Muʿallim al-Ḥujjāj: p. 95; and ʿAzīz al-Fatāwā: 3, 4/75).

Īḍāḥ al-Masāʾil states:

A woman may go for Hajj with her husband, her full brother, her paternal uncle, her maternal uncle, her foster brother, or her son-in-law; and she may also go for Hajj with her son-in-law after her daughter's death. (Īḍāḥ al-Masāʾil: p. 123, Nuʿaymiyyah).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
