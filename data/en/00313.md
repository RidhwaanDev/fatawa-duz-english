### TITLE
Using spirit to clean a watch or similar items

### QUESTION
Is it correct to use spirit for cleaning a watch or anything else, given that spirit is the essence of alcohol?

### ANSWER
Although spirit is impure and its use should be avoided, there is room for leniency because it has become so widespread. Furthermore, in the view of the two Shaykhs, Imam Abū Ḥanīfah and Imam Abū Yūsuf (may Allah have mercy on them), it is impure only when it has been obtained from grapes, raisins or dates, and research shows that spirit is not obtained nowadays from grapes, raisins or dates. According to the position of the two Shaykhs, therefore, it is pure. It is true that, because of the corruption of the times, the position of Imam Muḥammad (may Allah have mercy on him) was adopted for fatwa; but today, taking account of the need for medical treatment and of how widespread the matter has become, the fatwa is given according to the position of the two Shaykhs. The statements of other scholars are given below.

Imdād al-Fatāwā states:

If spirit has not been obtained from grapes, raisins, fresh dates or dried dates, there is room for leniency, للاختلاف; otherwise there is no room, للاتفاق. (Imdād al-Fatāwā: 1/137).

Niẓām al-Fatāwā states:

Spirit need not belong to the same class as the four intoxicants that are impure in themselves; it is also made from molasses and the like. So until it is established by sharia evidence that a particular spirit is the essence or the dregs of those very intoxicants, we cannot call that spirit impure, nor can we call it impermissible and unlawful to use things into which such spirit has been put. In the same way, we cannot call it impermissible and unlawful to clean utensils with it or to add it to medicines. This is the formal ruling; piety is a separate matter. (Niẓām al-Fatāwā, p. 361)

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā states:

If spirit has been obtained from grapes, raisins or dates, it is impure by agreement. If it has been made from something else, it is pure in the view of the two Shaykhs (may Allah have mercy on them) and impure in the view of Imam Muḥammad (may Allah have mercy on him). Research has shown that grapes and dates are not used nowadays to produce spirit and alcohol, so according to the position of the two Shaykhs it is pure. The jurists (may Allah have mercy on them) did adopt Imam Muḥammad's position for fatwa because of the corruption of the times; but today, in view of the need for medical treatment and of how widespread the matter has become, the ruling of purity is given according to the position of the two Shaykhs. In any case, on the principles of the school the position of the two Shaykhs carries preference. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 2/95).

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

Spirit is also used in certain things that are in constant use and that are very hard to avoid in our time — for instance, clothing dyes, ink, dyed cloth and the like. Using these is also correct: first, because their use has become general and a situation of widespread affliction has arisen, which is a cause for leniency in juristic rulings,

فمن القواعد الشرعية المتفق عليها "أن الامر اذا ضاق اتسع". الأشباه والنظائر لا بن نجيم ص ۸٤.

(Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 1/107)

In short, if the spirit comes from something other than the four prohibited intoxicating drinks, it is correct to use it for cleaning a watch or other items; even so, caution is the better course.
