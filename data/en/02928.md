### TITLE
The ruling on seeking a means of approach through a drawing of the blessed sandal

### QUESTION
What is the ruling on seeking a means of approach through a drawing of the blessed sandal? Kindly favour us with an answer in the light of the noble hadiths. بینوا توجروا

### ANSWER
To seek blessings from the sacred relics left behind by the noble Prophet ﷺ is not merely permissible; it is recommended, and it is a mark of love and devotion towards the Prophet ﷺ, a feeling that must be present in abundance in the heart of every Muslim. In the matter of a picture or drawing of the blessed sandal, however, no hadith with a chain of transmission has come before our eyes. Nor is it certain that such a picture corresponds a hundred per cent to the original. Furthermore, seeking a means of approach through the original is reasonable, whereas seeking it through a picture is not. On this basis, seeking a means of approach through a drawing of the blessed sandal is not correct.

Consider what Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

Question: Is the miracle of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ — namely that beneath the blessed foot of the Seal of Prophethood ﷺ a stone turned to wax and the impression of his blessed foot came upon the stone — established anywhere in the Six Books, or in other books of hadith, or in any other reliable or unreliable books, or not?

Answer (from the Deputy Muftī of Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband): As for the mark that certain people carry about nowadays, saying that it is the impression and mark of the blessed foot of the Prophet ﷺ, this claim of theirs is not correct; this mark is artificial and fabricated. فقط واللہ اعلم. Masʿūd Aḥmad, Deputy Muftī, Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband.

Answer (from Ḥaḍrat Shaykh al-Islām Mawlānā al-Sayyid Ḥusayn Aḥmad Madanī, may his secret be sanctified): The miracles of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ are very many and exceedingly great. Beside them, a stone becoming like wax and the impression of a foot falling upon it carries no weight at all; that is a slight matter. But to this day this miracle has not come before my eyes in any book of hadith or of biography. واللہ اعلم, Ḥusayn Aḥmad غفر لہ.

Answer (from Ḥaḍrat the Grand Muftī, may Allah have mercy on him): Although it is not far-fetched that the mark of the blessed foot of the Prophet ﷺ should have been imprinted upon a stone by way of a miracle, some chain of transmission and report is needed in order to accept it, and no such chain or report has come to notice. Muḥammad Kifāyatullāh کان اللہ لہ. (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 1/117, published by Dār al-Ishāʿat).

In another place he states: To attribute anything to the Prophet ﷺ without proof and without a firm chain of transmission is unlawful. Likewise, to treat even genuine relics with the degree of exaggeration mentioned in the question is impermissible and an innovation. And if someone forbids honouring and revering these relics on the ground that they are unproven, then to accuse him of insulting the Prophet ﷺ is oppression and ignorance. (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 1/359, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

Ḥaḍrat Ḥakīm al-Ummat (may Allah have mercy on him) wrote a treatise on this question entitled "Nayl al-Shifāʾ bi-Naʿl al-Muṣṭafā", which appears in Zād al-Saʿīd from p. 47 to p. 53. In it he set out an exhortation and encouragement to the Muslims to seek blessings and a means of approach through a drawing of the blessed sandal, and an incitement to publicise and circulate the drawing. Ḥaḍrat Muftī Muḥammad Kifāyatullāh Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him) then expressed a view against this, and a correspondence continued between the two of them until Ḥaḍrat Ḥakīm al-Ummat (may Allah have mercy on him), having regard to the common people's overstepping of the limits and their exaggeration, withdrew his encouragement to seek blessings and a means of approach through it and his exhortation to publicise and circulate it. A summary of this is set down in Kifāyat al-Muftī, volume two, from p. 91 to p. 99. Whoever has a desire for it may study Nayl al-Shifāʾ, Zād al-Saʿīd, Kifāyat al-Muftī, Bawādir al-Nawādir and the like. Keeping clear of undue length, we content ourselves here with a single fatwa.
