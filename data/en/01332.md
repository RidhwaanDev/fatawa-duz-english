### TITLE
Holding a marriage to a Christian woman in the masjid

### QUESTION
A man wishes to marry a Christian girl, and the girl is not prepared to accept Islam. Is this marriage disliked, or is it entirely permissible? And if it is disliked, may the committee give permission when the bridegroom's family insist on holding this marriage in the masjid?

### ANSWER
The details concerning marriage to a woman of the People of the Book — that is, a Jewish or Christian woman — have already been given. In short, it is no less than disliked. Now, since this marriage is disliked, the masjid should be kept free of this disliked act. Deliberately sitting in the masjid even for permissible affairs is disliked; burning impure oil in the masjid is disliked; bringing in children and the insane is disliked. So this disliked marriage too should be held somewhere else, and if the committee refuses permission, it is entirely justified in doing so.
