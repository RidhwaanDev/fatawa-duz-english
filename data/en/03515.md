### TITLE

Zakat on the price of produce after its ʿushr has been paid

### QUESTION

In Rabīʿ al-Thānī a man paid the ʿushr on his crop as the sharīʿah requires, and afterwards he sold the crop for three hundred thousand rupees. Then, when paying zakat in the blessed month of Ramadan, will he have to pay zakat on those three hundred thousand rupees or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER

Once the ʿushr on a crop has been paid, no second ʿushr is due on it, nor is zakat due on it. Yes, after it has been sold, the money must be added to his other wealth that has reached the threshold, and zakat paid on it when the year passes. So the man in question will, when paying zakat on his other wealth in the blessed month of Ramadan, pay zakat on the three hundred thousand rupees too.

Consider what al-Jawharah al-Nayyirah states:

قوله ومن كان له نصاب فاستفاد في أثناء الحول مالاً من جنسه ضمه إلى ماله وزكاه، سواء كان المستفاد من نمائه أو لا وبأي وجه استفاده ضمه سواء كان بميراث أو هبة أو غير ذلك وشرط كونه من جنسه. (الجوهرة النيرة: ۱/۱۲۰، ط: الخيرية). (وكذا في بدائع الصنائع: ۲/۱٤، والفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱۷۵، وتبيين الحقائق: ۱/۲۷۲، إمداديه، وفتح القدير: ۲/۱۹۵، دار الفكر).

Aḥsan al-Fatāwā records:

The ʿushr on produce is one obligation in its own right, and if the produce is sold, zakat on the money obtained from it is a separate obligation.

لأن الحقين لم يتعلقا بمحل واحد فإن العشر حق الأرض النامية بالخارج حقيقة وزكاة النقود حق المال النامي تقديراً

قال ابن نجيم رحمه الله في زكاة مال التجارة إذا دخل من أرضه حنطة تبلغ قيمتها قيمة نصاب ونوى أن يمسكها ويبيعها فأمسكها حولاً لا تجب فيها الزكاة. (البحر الرائق: ۲/۲۰۹)،

In the passage quoted, the reason given for zakat not being due is that the wheat is not merchandise. This establishes that in the case of selling the wheat, zakat will be obligatory on the money, لأنها متعينة للتجارة خلقة. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 4/360.)

Āp ke Masāʾil aur un kā Ḥal states:

Once ʿushr has been paid, so long as the produce is not sold, neither ʿushr nor zakat is due on it. And when, after paying the ʿushr, the crop is sold, then zakat will be due on the money obtained from it when a year passes over it; or, if the man was already in possession of the threshold amount, then when the year is complete over his existing threshold, he will at that time pay zakat on this money too. (Āp ke Masāʾil aur un kā Ḥal: 5/183.)

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
