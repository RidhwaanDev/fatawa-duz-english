### TITLE
The age at which a boy may act as a mahram

### QUESTION
From what age does a boy become fit to act as a mahram for his mother? And is any age fixed under the government's law for obtaining an Umrah visa? بینوا توجروا

### ANSWER
There is a difference of opinion as to whether, in the matter of travel, a boy close to puberty is like an adult or not. One view, however, is that he is like an adult, and given how much travel is needed today this view may be adopted. See: the jurists say that a murāhiq (a boy close to puberty) is like an adult.

قال في الدر المختار: ومع زوج أو محرم

بالغ قيد لهما كما في النهر بحثاً عاقل والمراهق كبالغ جوهرة. (الدر المختار: ۲/٤٦٤، كتاب الحج، سعيد). وكذا في الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۲۱۹).
قال في تقريرات الرافعي: قول المصنف (والمراهق كبالغ) جعله الرحمتى كصبي لأنه محتاج إلى من يدفع عنه ولذا كان للأب منعه عن حجة الإسلام فكيف يصلح لحمايتها وفي المحيطين والبدائع الذي لم يحتلم لا عبرة له لكن ما في الجوهرة موافق لما في الخلاصة والبزازية، سندي. (التحرير المختار على الدر المختار: ۲/۱۵۷، سعيد).

ʿAllāmah Sayyid Ahmad al-Ṭaḥṭāwī (may Allah have mercy on him) says in Ḥāshiyat al-Ṭaḥṭāwī ʿalā Marāqī al-Falāḥ:

قوله بالغ، المراهق كالبالغ. (حاشية الطحطاوي على مراقي الفلاح، ص ۷۲۸، قديمي، وكذا في غمز عيون البصائر شرح الأشباه والنظائر: ۱/٤۷٦، ط: إدارة القرآن).

In ʿUmdat al-Fiqh, Hazrat Maulānā Sayyid Zawwār Husain Sahib says:

The ruling on a murāhiq (a boy close to puberty) is like that of an adult, as stated in al-Jawharah — so travelling with him is permissible. Al-Raḥmatī (may Allah have mercy on him) placed him under the ruling of a minor boy, because he himself needs someone to defend him; and it is for this very reason that his father has the right to stop him from performing the obligatory Hajj. So how can he be capable of protecting a woman? In the two Muḥīṭs and in al-Badāʾiʿ it is stated that travelling with a boy who has not yet had a wet dream — that is, has not reached puberty — is not to be relied upon. But what is in al-Jawharah agrees with what is in al-Khulāṣah and al-Bazzāziyyah, so this is what is chosen for the fatwa: that he comes under the ruling of an adult and travelling with him is permissible. (Author.) (عمدۃ الفقہ: ٤/۵۳، کتاب الحج).

(2) If the boy is close to puberty he can act as a mahram, and the sharia allows it. Yes, provided it does not conflict with the visa law, they may travel for Hajj and Umrah, since breaking the law is an offence in the eyes of the government, on account of which one has to face difficulties; and it is extremely important for a person to protect his honour and good name.

Some muftis have fixed the age of a boy close to puberty at 12 years. See: (احسن الفتاوی: ۸/۳۰).
