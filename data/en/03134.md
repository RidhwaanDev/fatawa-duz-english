### TITLE
The Prophet's ﷺ dress was Abrahamic

### QUESTION
It is generally said that Muslims should wear the kurtā, and this warning is given: do not wear the dress of the disbelievers, otherwise you will be counted among them. Then it is stated that the kurtā is the dress of the Muslims and the suit and tie is that of the disbelievers. Now the question is: in the time of the Prophet ﷺ, when the noble Companions wore the kurtā, what did the disbelievers wear? Did they wear a suit and tie, or did all of them wear the kurtā — in which case how was a distinction made? And if the dress of all of them was the kurtā, then did the noble Companions — we seek refuge with Allah — dress like the disbelievers? A clarification is requested. Kindly explain and be rewarded.

### ANSWER
In the case in question, the dress that was used in the time of the Prophet ﷺ was the dress of Arabia, which had come down in the religion of Ibrāhīm (ʿalayhis salām), just as certain other customs too were shared between the Muslims and the non-Muslims. Such things have been expressed in the hadith by the term "fiṭrah", that is, the ancient way of the prophets (ʿalayhimus salām) — for example: circumcision, rinsing the mouth, drawing water into the nose, cleaning oneself after relieving oneself, trimming the nails, letting the beard grow, clipping the moustache, making a parting in the hair of the head, using the tooth-stick, and so on.

أخرج الإمام مسلم في صحيحه (رقم: ۲٦۱) بسنده عن عائشة رضي الله تعالى عنها قالت: قال رسول الله ﷺ: عشر من الفطرة قص الشارب وإعفاء اللحية والسواك واستنشاق الماء وقص الأظفار وغسل البراجم ونتف الإبط وحلق العانة وانتقاص الماء قال زكريا: قال مصعب: ونسيت العاشرة إلا أن تكون المضمضة زاد قتيبة قال وكيع: انتقاص الماء يعني: الاستنجاء.

قال الإمام النووي رحمه الله: وأما الفطرة فقد اختلف في المراد بها هنا فقال سليمان الخطابي ذهب أكثر العلماء إلى أنها السنة وكذا ذكره جماعة غير الخطابي قالوا: ومعناه أنها من سنن الأنبياء صلوات الله وسلامه عليهم.

Now when Muslims adopt these matters, it is not called resemblance; rather this is the old way of Arabia which our Sharīʿah retained. For example, Muslims wear a shirt, and in the Noble Qur'an too the shirt of Ḥaḍrat Yūsuf (ʿalayhis salām) is mentioned: "اذهبوا بقميصي هذا فألقوه على وجه أبي يأت بصيراً". [يوسف: ۹۳].

As for those who wear trousers, the mention of the trousers of Ḥaḍrat Ibrāhīm (ʿalayhis salām) is present in the narrations. So in the time of the Prophet ﷺ the Muslims and the non-Muslims almost all wore one and the same dress, which was the ancient dress of Arabia. Yes, some people, or some women, wore immodest clothing, which the Messenger of Allah ﷺ disliked. It comes in the hadith: "عن أم سلمة قالت: استيقظ النبي ﷺ ذات ليلة فقال: سبحان الله! ماذا أنزل الليلة من الفتن وماذا فتح من الخزائن أيقظوا صواحب الحجر فرب كاسية في الدنيا عارية في الآخرة". (رواه البخاري، رقم: ۱۱۵، باب العلم والعظة بالليل).

Ḥaḍrat Umm Salamah (raḍiyallāhu taʿālā ʿanhā) says: The Noble Prophet ﷺ awoke one night and said: Glory be to Allah! How many trials have descended this night, and how many treasures have been opened! Awaken the noble wives. Many a woman clothed in this world will be naked in the hereafter — that is, whoever has worn tight or flimsy clothing will be naked in the hereafter.

Therefore, to call the noble Companions (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhum) people who resembled the disbelievers is wrong. Moreover, in the time of the noble Companions (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhum) there was no particular mark of the Muslims in dress either; for instance, no difference is established between the beard of Abū Jahl and the beard of the noble Companions (raḍiyallāhu ʿanhum) in general. But later on dress became of two kinds: some dress is the dress of the righteous Muslims and some is the dress of the non-Muslims, in which no regard is paid to modesty and covering. So a Muslim should adopt the dress of the righteous and should avoid the dress of the wrong sort of people. And if a Muslim adopts a mark which is a religious sign of the non-Muslims, such as the cross and the sacred thread, then its use is entirely impermissible. Yes, since the use of trousers and shirt is very widespread among the disbelievers and non-Muslims, and in it too there is not much regard for covering the private parts, one should avoid it.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
