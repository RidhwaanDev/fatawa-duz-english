### TITLE
The inverted prayer (ṣalāt al-maʿkūs) and its ruling

### QUESTION
Some people say: "Perform the inverted prayer." So what is the inverted prayer called such? And what is its ruling? بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In Tarbiyat al-Ṭālibīn, the Jurist of the Ummah, Ḥaḍrat Muftī Maḥmūd Ḥasan Ṣāḥib (may Allah have mercy on him), writes:

The inverted prayer means that some people tie a rope to the ceiling, bind their feet in it, and hang upside down and complete their regular litany in that state. This is the inverted prayer; do not adopt it. The qawāriʿ are those verses in which there is mention of the Resurrection, of the world's shattering and bursting apart, and of being presented before God, and in which there is a warning for the disobedient — such as Sūrat al-Qāriʿah. (Tarbiyat al-Ṭālibīn, p. 117).

In al-Qawl al-Jamīl, Ḥaḍrat Shāh Walī Allāh, the ḥadīth scholar of Delhi (may Allah have mercy on him), says:

وللچشتية صلاة تسمى صلاة المعكوس، لم نجد من السنة ولا أقوال الفقهاء ما نشدها به فلذلك حذفناها والعلم عند الله.
(شفاء العليل ترجمه القول الجميل، ص ۸٦، ط: رحمانيه).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
