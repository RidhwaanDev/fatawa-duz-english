### TITLE
An objection concerning the marriage to the wife of an adopted son, and its answer

### QUESTION
Why did the Noble Prophet ﷺ marry the wife of his adopted son? Some non-Muslims raise this as an objection; what is a good answer by which a non-Muslim may be made to understand? بینوا بالدلیل ۔

### ANSWER
The simple answer for making a non-Muslim understand is this: can any person in the world have two real fathers? That is utterly impossible. How then could Ḥaḍrat Zayd have both Ḥārithah and the Holy Prophet ﷺ as his father? Moreover, the thing that would truly be objectionable is that another man's son should be made one's own son; what objection can there be in calling another man's son the son of that other man?

As for the substantive answer, it is this: it was a custom of the age of ignorance that if a man called someone his son, that person was regarded as his real son; he was given all the same rights that were given to a real son, and he was made a sharer in inheritance and the like. Hence, if an adopted son were to become a real son, lineage would be thrown into confusion, he would become a sharer in the inheritance, and through him other relatives too would become sharers in it, and on that account there would be injustice and oppression towards the real heirs. On this basis Allah Most High abolished this evil custom of the age of ignorance through the practice of His Prophet, so that no one should be left with any right of objection.

Guidance was also given thereby to the point that the permissibility or prohibition of marriage is not connected with adoption, but with lineage and blood relationship and with fosterage and affinity by marriage. To take on the support of an orphan is purely of the nature of kindness and benevolence.

Consider what is written in Tafsīr Ḥaqqānī:

The guidance of the people rests upon the greatness of the Prophet (upon him be peace), and therefore [Allah] repels certain of the reproaches which the opponents used to level against the Prophet ﷺ. Among these reproaches was one concerning his marriage with Ḥaḍrat Zaynab, the wife of Ḥaḍrat Zayd, which the Prophet ﷺ contracted after Zayd had divorced her and her waiting period had passed, on account of a heavenly command and a divine purpose.

And O Prophet, why did We give her to you in marriage?

لكي لا يكون على المؤمنين حرج في أزواج أدعيائهم إذا قضوا منهن وطراً وكان أمر الله مفعولاً

— that is, so that no impediment should remain for the Muslims in marrying the wives of their adopted sons once they have divorced them. In other words, this woman is not among those forbidden in Islam, and it is only because of a custom that people regard her as forbidden; let this custom and this fabrication of the age of ignorance be wiped out. In reality, great attacks are launched against people when such customs are broken. In our country marrying the widow of a paternal uncle or of a maternal uncle is considered highly disgraceful; and if someone does so in order to break the custom, then see what sort of calumnies are heaped upon him and what attacks are made. He says: this was a matter that was bound to come about, for God willed the effacement of that custom.

﴿مَا كَانَ عَلَى النَّبِيِّ مِنْ حَرَجٍ فِيمَا فَرَضَ اللَّهُ لَهُ﴾

— If someone raises the doubt: why was this custom made to be effaced through the Prophet, when it could have been broken through the marriage of someone else? In answer to this He says: what fault is there upon the Prophet, and what constraint or impediment, in his carrying out the matter which Allah has ordained for him, or which He has commanded him to do?

سنة الله في الذين خلوا من قبل

— Among the prophets and the righteous of former times too this same practice of Allah has continued: that He has the customs of ignorance broken at their hands and appoints them to break them, because it is the work of these men of God to become the target of blame. And whose practice is this breaking of customs?

﴿الَّذِينَ يُبَلِّغُونَ رِسَالَاتِ اللَّهِ وَيَخْشَوْنَهُ وَلَا يَخْشَوْنَ أَحَدًا إِلَّا اللَّهَ﴾

— It is the practice of those who convey the commands of Allah and fear none but Allah; they pay no heed to anyone's taunts, reproaches and abuse.

وكفى بالله حسيباً

— And Allah suffices as a reckoner: from those who taunt these pure-hearted men He will certainly take an account and call them to answer. (تفسیر حقانی: ٤/۱۲، ۱۳، میر محمد کتب خانہ، کراچی) ۔

Tafsīr Jawāhir al-Qurʾān states:

It was the will of Allah Most High that this custom of ignorance should be abolished through the Prophet's own practice; and so Allah Most High admonished him that he was fearing the objection of the people, whereas he ought to fear none but Allah.

When Zayd divorced Zaynab, We gave her to you in marriage and thereby broke this custom of ignorance, so that no impediment or constraint should remain in marrying the divorced wives of adopted sons. Allah Most High, glorified be His majesty, has made marriage to the divorced wife of an adopted son lawful; therefore no blame attaches to you for marrying the divorced wife of Zayd. (تفسیر جواہر القرآن: ۳/۹٤۰، از افادات: حضرت مولانا حسین علی رحمه الله، ط: کتب خانہ رشیدیہ، راول پنڈی) ۔

Maʿārif al-Qurʾān states:

A man's real son is the one born of his seed. How can it be possible for an adopted son to become a son in reality? It is rationally impossible for a person to have two fathers in reality. Merely calling someone one's son with the tongue does not make him a son in reality; and were an adopted son to become a son in reality, lineage would be thrown into confusion, he would compete with the real son in inheritance, and other relatives too would become sharers in the inheritance. In this way, taking someone as a son is plain injustice and oppression towards the real relatives. (معارف القرآن: ٦/۲۲۵ از مولانا محمد ادریس صاحب کاندھلوی رحمه الله، مکتبہ عثمانیہ) ۔

Tafsīr ʿUthmānī states:

لكي لا يكون على المؤمنين حرج في أزواج أدعيائهم

That is, the purpose of giving her to you in marriage was precisely this: that this false notion of the age of ignorance should be uprooted entirely from people's hearts, and that no constraint or obstruction should remain in this matter in the future. (تفسیر عثمانی، ص ۵٦۳، ط: مجمع الملک فہد) ۔

Kitāb al-Fatāwā records: The relationship of parents and children is not established merely by supposing it and saying it; rather it is a natural and innate relationship. Therefore, merely because a man has brought up some girl and that girl regards him as a parent, she will not become unlawful for the children of the man who brought her up, and marriage between the two of them will be valid. (کتاب الفتاوی: ٤/۳٤۰) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
