### TITLE
Zakat on a pension fund

### QUESTION
Is zakat due on a pension fund or not? And if it is, when should it be paid — when the whole sum is received, or every year?

### ANSWER
A pension fund is a gift from the government, and in a gift ownership does not pass before possession is taken. So zakat becomes due once the money has been received and a year has passed over it. Alternatively, if the person already has other money in hand, the pension money is added to it, and when the year is complete the zakat on the pension money is paid along with it.

Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

The pension an employee receives on retiring from service is lawful, since it is a kind of grant and support from the government. (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 8/97, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

The ruling on a pension fund is exactly the same as the ruling on a provident fund, and the references for it have already been given.
