### TITLE
Forfeiting money paid when a booking is cancelled

### QUESTION
A man books a wedding hall for a Sunday and pays part of the amount in advance. If for some reason he then cancels one or two days beforehand, part of the amount he has paid is forfeited. Is it correct to do this?

### ANSWER
Forfeiting the money resembles a sale with an earnest deposit (bayʿ al-ʿurbūn), and that is impermissible. If, however, the owners of the wedding hall fix a certain fee for the booking and that sum is non-refundable — say they fix a hundred rand for the booking, and the paperwork, telephone costs, office expenses and so on are met from it — then this sum will not be returned, just as an admission fee for a child is non-refundable.

A detailed discussion of the sale with an earnest deposit has already been given in the Book of Sales, and may be consulted there.

For the evidences that an admission fee is permissible, see: (امداد الاحکام: ۳/۵٦٦، ٦۰٦ ۔ و امداد الفتاوی: ۳/٤۰۲).
