### TITLE
Transplanting the cornea of the eye

### QUESTION
I am a woman whose eyesight has almost gone, and the doctors say that if I do not have treatment I will become completely blind. They say that in order to regain my sight I will have to undergo a corneal graft.

A brief description of this treatment is as follows:

It is a transplant operation in which an organ of one person — the cornea of the eye — is inserted into another person's eye.

This cornea is taken from the eye of someone who has made a bequest that after his death his cornea should be used for someone else. After the death of the one who made the bequest, the cornea is kept for a set period in an eye bank, where it is monitored.

Before it is transplanted into the patient, the cornea is thoroughly examined to see whether it is sound and beneficial, and whether transplanting it into the patient's eye will transmit any kind of disease.

I request you to inform me of the ruling of the Sharia on this — whether treatment of this kind is permitted or not. بینوا توجروا۔

### ANSWER
In the view of some scholars and muftis there is room for it, and you may act upon their verdict. The detailed evidence of both parties may be seen in the detailed fatwa given earlier. Making a bequest of this kind, however, is not permissible or correct.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
