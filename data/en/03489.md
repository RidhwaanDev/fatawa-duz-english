### TITLE
The ruling on the funeral prayer over bodies torn to pieces in accidents

### QUESTION
In today's accidents it sometimes happens that the body of the deceased is torn to pieces and cannot be identified, and the limbs too are scattered. Is it permissible to gather all of them together and perform a collective funeral prayer or not, so that the heirs may be consoled?

### ANSWER
If, in accidents, the greater part of the body of the deceased, or half of it together with the head, has been recovered, then it is correct to gather all the martyrs and perform the funeral prayer with the intention of praying over them, so that everyone may be consoled. But if the greater part of the body of any of these martyrs has not been recovered whole, then the funeral prayer lapses; and if someone does perform it, it will be a funeral prayer in the customary sense, not a funeral prayer in the Sharīʿah sense. Consider what is in Fatāwā Hindiyyah:

وإن وجد نصفه من غير الرأس أو وجد نصفه مشقوقاً طولاً فإنه لا يغسل ولا يصلى عليه ويلف في خرقة ويدفن فيها. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱۵۹).

In al-Durr al-Mukhtār, ʿAllāmah al-Ḥaṣkafī (may Allah have mercy on him) writes:

وجد رأس آدمي، أو أحد شقيه لا يغسل ولا يصلى عليه بل يدفن إلا أن يوجد أكثر من نصفه ولو بلا رأس. (الدر المختار: ۲/۱۹۹، سعيد).

For more, see: (al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq: 2/305, published by Rashīdiyyah; al-Fatāwā al-Tātārkhāniyyah: 2/178; Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ: 1/302, Saʿīd; Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 8/665, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah; Kifāyat al-Muftī: 5/369; and Fatāwā Ḥaqqāniyyah: 3/440).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
