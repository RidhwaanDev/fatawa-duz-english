### TITLE
The ruling on reducing one's tax by means of a zakat receipt

### QUESTION
When zakat is paid to an institution or a society, that institution or society issues a receipt. If we present this receipt against government tax, one per cent of the tax is deducted. The question is this: is it correct to have the government's income tax reduced by means of a zakat receipt, or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
The taxes levied by some governments are in most cases oppressive, so it is permissible to adopt measures to lessen such a tax and to have it reduced. Since income tax is imposed by the government unjustly, it is correct to have it reduced by presenting a zakat receipt. As for taxes that are legitimately due and whose benefit reaches us in one form or another, it is not permissible to have such a tax reduced by presenting a zakat receipt. Consider what ʿAllāmah al-Sarakhsī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

فأما في زماننا إنما يوجد أكثر النوائب بطريق الظلم ومن تمكن من دفع الظلم عن نفسه فذلك خير له

(المبسوط للإمام السرخسي: ۱۰/۳۵، ط: دار الفكر، بيروت).

(وكذا في فتاوى الشامي: ۲/۳۳٦، سعيد، و۵/۳۳۲، سعيد، وفتح القدير: ۷/۲۳۳، وشرح العناية: ۷/۲۲۲، وحاشية الطحطاوي علي الدر: ۱/۳۹۰).

Income tax is a tax that is not legitimately due. Consider what Mawlānā Khālid Sayfullāh writes:

Income tax is not legitimately due, and it is collected to an unwarranted extent. So if a person is compelled to keep money in a bank under a scheme that yields interest, and interest is received, income tax may be paid out of that interest, provided that the bank or institution is itself a government one.

House tax is levied in return for the municipal amenities that the government provides to the public. It is a lawful and equitable tax whose benefit returns to the taxpayer, so if interest money were used to pay it, that would amount to benefiting from interest. (Kitāb al-Fatāwā 5/318, 319)

It thus emerges that having a tax which is not legitimately due reduced is correct; but since the benefit of a legitimate tax returns to the taxpayer, having it reduced by presenting a zakat receipt is impermissible, because it would fall under benefiting from zakat, and that is not allowed.

For further detail see: (Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā, volume five, pp. 400–403)
