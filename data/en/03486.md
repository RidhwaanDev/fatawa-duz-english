### TITLE
The ruling on making the intention after the act when conveying reward

### QUESTION
For conveying reward, is it necessary to make the intention before the act, or may one make the intention after performing the act as well? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
For conveying reward it is not necessary to make the intention at the time of the act; rather one may make the intention of conveying reward after performing the act as well. Consider what is in Fatāwā Shāmī:

والظاهر أنه لا فرق بين أن ينوي به عند الفعل للغير أو يفعله لنفسه بعد ذلك يجعل ثوابه لغيره لإطلاق كلامهم، وأنه لا فرق بين الفرض والنفل. (فتاوى الشامي: ۲/۲٤۳، سعيد).

It is recorded in al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq:

لا فرق بين أن ينوي به عند الفعل للغير أو يفعله لنفسه ثم بعد ذلك يجعل ثوابه لغيره لإطلاق كلامه. (البحر الرائق: ۳/٦٤، دار المعرفة).

It is mentioned in Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah:

If a good deed — recitation of the Noble Qurʾān, supererogatory prayers, or charity — is performed with the intention that Allah Most High should convey its reward to such-and-such a person, that too is sufficient; and if it is done without that intention, then afterwards one should make this supplication: "O Allah, convey its reward to such-and-such a person." (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 9/203, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
