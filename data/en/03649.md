### TITLE

The ruling on a consignment sale

### QUESTION

A certain trade is run on the basis of consignment sale. Its procedure is that, along with an ordinary sale, the company grants certain purchasers the option that if they are unable to sell the goods after purchasing them, they may return those goods to the company. For the company this return of the goods is reckoned to come under iqālah. Can there be any scope in the Sharīʿah for a sale of this kind? Or will it fall under a sale with a condition and so be impermissible? Nowadays most of the large companies in the world are engaged in this, and on account of it purchasers are more inclined towards them; many Muslim traders too have adopted this method.

### ANSWER

In the case described, the correct manner of this transaction is that the condition of return should not be stipulated within the sale; rather, after the contract of sale has been concluded, a promise should be made and entered in the documents as well. A transaction of this kind will then be sound and correct, and this condition will have the status of a promise.
Its parallel is bayʿ bi'l-wafāʾ, in which iqālah is certain, and many scholars have given a fatwā of permissibility for that sale — the details of which may be seen in Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā (volume 5). In the light of that, the above-mentioned form will be permissible with even greater reason, since in it iqālah is only a possibility and not a certainty.
Concerning bayʿ bi'l-wafāʾ, Sharḥ al-Niqāyah has written; consider:

وإن ذكر البيع من شرط ثم ذكر الشرط على وجه الميعاد جاز البيع ويلزمه الوفاء بالميعاد لأن المواعيد قد تكون لا زمة، قال رسول الله ﷺ: العدة دين فيجعل هذا الميعاد لا زماً لحاجة الناس إليه. (شرح النقاية: ۳/۲۵۳، ط: بيروت).
وقال في تبيين الحقائق: وإن ذكر البيع من غير شرط ثم ذكرا الشرط على الوجه المعتاد جاز البيع ويلزمه الوفاء بالميعاد لأن المواعيد قد تكون لا زمة قال عليه الصلاة والسلام: العدة دين. فيجعل هذا الميعاد لا زماً لحاجة الناس إليه. وقال جلال الدين في حواشي الهداية: صورته أن يقول البائع للمشتري بعت منك هذا العين بألف على أني لو دفعت إليك ثمنك تدفع العين إلى ثم قال: ويسمي هذا بيع الوفاء. (تبيين الحقائق: ۵/۱۸٤، ط: إمداديه). (وكذا في فتاوى قاضيخان: ۲/۱٦۵، ومجمع الضمانات: ۱/۵۳۰، وفتح القدير: ٦/٤٤۵، ط: دار الفكر، والعناية شرح الهداية بهامش فتح القدير: ٦/٤٤٤).

Imdād al-Aḥkām states:
And one form of bayʿ bi'l-wafāʾ is that the offer and acceptance of sale and purchase took place and there was no condition of return or the like within the offer and acceptance; rather the condition of return and so forth is made after the offer and acceptance. This is permissible by agreement, لخلو العقد عن الشرط; and when there is no condition immediately joined to the verbal offer and acceptance, then writing the condition immediately in the deed of sale will not bring about prohibition,
لأن الأصل في العقود القول والكتابة وثيقة، والله تعالى أعلم.
(امداد الاحکام: ۳/٤۳۵، کراچی) ۔
In another place it is written:
Since there is a difference of opinion among the imāms regarding the impermissibility of a sale with a condition — thus in the view of Imām al-Shāfiʿī (may Allah have mercy on him) a sale with a condition is permissible in certain forms, and this is also the view of Ibn Abī Laylā and Ibn Shubrumah, that a sale with a condition is permissible — the later jurists have therefore declared bayʿ bi'l-wafāʾ permissible on account of need. (امداد الاحکام: ۳/٤۲۹، کراچی) ۔
واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
