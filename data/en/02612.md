### TITLE
The ruling on wearing tight clothing in front of women

### QUESTION
Is it permissible for a Muslim woman to wear tight, close-fitting clothing that shows the shape of her body in front of another Muslim woman, or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, it is not permissible or correct for a Muslim woman to wear clothing so close-fitting that the shape of her limbs is apparent.

The purpose of clothing is to conceal the shape and form of the parts of the body that must be covered. Allah Most High says:

﴿لباساً يواري سوآتكم﴾ (سورۂ اعراف: ۳٦)۔

Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

Those parts of the human body whose exposure a person naturally considers bad and shameful are called sawʾah. The meaning is: We have sent down for your welfare and success a clothing with which you may conceal your parts of shame. (معارف القرآن: ۳/۵۳۳)۔

Clothing that does not conceal the shameful and private parts of the body, or through whose tightness their shape is visible, will therefore be impermissible.

A warning has come in the hadith concerning such women. The hadith and the necessary explanation of it have been given above; reference may be made to that place.

Further, it does not befit a Muslim woman to wear before any woman clothing that displays the shape of her body. ʿAllāmah Shāmī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

لا يحل للمسلمة أن تنكشف بين يدي يهودية أو نصرانية أو مشركة إلا أن تكون أمة لها كما في السراج، ونصاب الاحتساب، ولا تنبغي للمرأة الصالحة أن تنظر إليها المرأة الفاجرة لأنها تصفها عند الرجال، فلا تضع جلبابها ولا خمارها كما في السراج. (فتاوى الشامي: ٦/۳۷۱، سعيد).

Mawlānā Muḥammad Yūsuf Ludhyānwī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

It is not permissible for women to wear cloth so fine that the body can be seen through it. It has been said of such women in the hadith that they will be deprived even of the fragrance of Paradise. (آپ کے مسائل اور ان کا حل: ۸/۳٦۸)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
