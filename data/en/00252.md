### TITLE
Blood that comes out when an injection is given

### QUESTION
If a patient is given an injection and a little blood comes out with it, does the ablution break or not?

### ANSWER
A small amount of the body's blood gets onto the needle when an injection is given. Blood leaving the body in such a quantity does not break the ablution, because the amount is too small to flow. In line with the statements of the jurists, if blood comes out of the body and is wiped away, and the amount is so small that it would not have flowed even if it had not been wiped, the ablution is not broken.

Al-Fatāwā al-ʿĀlamgīriyyah states:

إذا خرج من الجرح دم قليل فمسحه ثم خرج أيضاً ومسحه فإن كان الدم بحال لو ترك ما قد مسح منه فسال انتقض وضوءه وإن كان لا يسيل لا ينقض. (الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۱۱).

That is: if a little blood comes out of a wound and a person wipes it away, and then more comes out and he wipes that away too, then if the blood taken together is of such an amount that it would have flowed had the wiped blood been left alone, the ablution is broken; otherwise it is not.
