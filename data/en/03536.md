### TITLE
The ruling on a fast when an ultrasound is done while fasting

### QUESTION
Is it correct to have a vaginal ultrasound done while fasting, or not? Will the fast be broken by it or not? Also, if it is done during the fasts of an expiation, will the unbroken sequence be affected or not? And can not knowing that the fast has been broken serve as an excuse in the middle of the fasts of an expiation? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In a vaginal ultrasound a kind of instrument is inserted into the woman's private part. There is a balloon fitted over this instrument, and a kind of gel is applied over the balloon. The purpose of inserting this instrument is that by means of it the condition of the child in the woman's womb may be fully known — whether the child is safe and sound or whether there is some defect in it. Sometimes this procedure is also carried out in order to find out whether the child is male or female. This instrument makes the condition of the child in the mother's womb entirely clear.

In the case asked about, if someone had this procedure carried out while fasting because of a severe necessity, there will be no sin; the fast, however, will be broken. This is because gel is applied to the instrument that is used in it, and the instrument is then placed in the woman's inner private part, and the jurists have stated plainly that if any moist thing is inserted into the inner private part the fast will be broken. Therefore, if this procedure is carried out in the middle of the fasts of an expiation, then, since the fast is broken by it, the unbroken sequence will also be affected and sixty fasts will have to be kept afresh. Moreover, not knowing that the fast has been broken is not an excuse. Consider what is stated in Marāqī al-Falāḥ:

(أو أدخل أصبعه مبلولة بماء أو دهن في دبره) أو استنجى فوصل الماء إلى داخل دبره أو فرجها الداخل بالمبالغة فيه والحد الفاصل الذي يتعلق بالوصول إليه الفساد قدر الحقنة وقلما يكون ذلك. (مراقي الفلاح، ص ۲۵۲).

Al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq states:

وكذا لو أدخل أصبعه في إسته أو أدخلت المرأة في فرجها هو المختار إلا إذا كانت الأصبع مبتلة بالماء أو الدهن فحينئذ يفسد لوصول الماء أو الدهن. (البحر الرائق: ۲/ ۳۰۰، ط: دار المعرفة).

Al-Nutaf fī al-Fatāwā states:

قال وإن اختار الصوم فيصوم شهرين متتابعين فإن أفطر يوماً من غير عذر استقبل الصوم وإن كان عذر فلا يستقبل في قول أبي عبد الله والعذر ما ذكرناه فوق ذلك وفي قول أبي حنيفة وأصحابه لا عذر لأحد في ترك الاستقبال إذا أفطر يوماً إلا المرأة تحيض فإنها تبني على إذا طهرت فحسب لأنها لا يمكنها أن تصوم شهرين متتابعين من غير حيض. (النتف في الفتاوى، ص ۱۰٤، دار الكتب العلمية بيروت).

Mufaṭṭirāt-e-Ṣawm awr ʿAṣr-e-Ḥāḍir kē Baʿḍ Masāʾil states: Since the instruments which doctors use for diagnosis and investigation are taken out again after being inserted into the private part, and are not left there, the fast will not be broken by them, provided that no medicine or water or the like has been applied to these instruments. (Mufaṭṭirāt-e-Ṣawm awr ʿAṣr-e-Ḥāḍir kē Baʿḍ Masāʾil, p. 20, Masīḥ al-ʿUlūm, Bangalore).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states: If a moist finger reaches as far as the place of an enema, the fast will be broken. (15/186, Maḥmūdiyyah edition).

In another place it says:

Question: A man kept fifty-nine fasts of an expiation without a break. On the day he was to keep the sixtieth fast he fell ill. Should he keep sixty fasts afresh, or should he keep only one fast after regaining his health?

Answer: He should keep sixty fasts afresh without a break. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 15/204, Maktabah Maḥmūdiyyah).
