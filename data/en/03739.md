### TITLE
The ruling on the rent of a liquor shop on a property

### QUESTION
A person bought a property in which there was already a liquor shop. Now, until the contract of lease comes to an end, the liquor seller cannot be removed from there; yes, after the contract of lease ends he will vacate it. Will it be permissible to collect and use the rent of this shop during this period or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
In the case in question, if the one selling the liquor is a Muslim, then the rent will be disliked to the degree of prohibition and it will be obligatory to give it away in charity; and if he is a non-Muslim, then it will be disliked in the lesser sense — using it is permitted, though giving it away in charity is superior and better.

Consider what is recorded in al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq:

وإجارة بيت ليتخذ بيت نار أو بيعة أو كنيسة أو يباع فيه خمر بالسواد، يعني جاز إجارة البيت لكافر ليتخذ معبداً أو بيت نار للمجوس أو يباع فيه خمر في السواد وهذا قول الإمام، وقالا: يكره كل ذلك لقوله تعالى: وتعاونوا على البر والتقوى ولا تعاونوا على الإثم والعدوان. (تكملة البحر: ۸/۲۳۰).

It is mentioned in Aḥsan al-Fatāwā:

In this servant's view there is a difference between leasing to a disbeliever and leasing to a Muslim. The passages of the jurists (may Allah Most High have mercy on them) relate to the disbeliever, and in that case, both by transmitted evidence and by reasoning, the preferred view appears to be that it is disliked in the lesser sense; whereas in leasing to a Muslim the preponderant view appears to be that it is disliked to the degree of prohibition. And Allah knows best. (Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 6/456).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
