### TITLE
The ruling of the sharia on smoking the hookah

### QUESTION
Is smoking the hookah permissible in the sharia or not? If it is permissible, is it so with dislike or without dislike? بینوا توجروا

### ANSWER
About four hundred years ago the unbelievers invented the hookah, after which it became very widespread. In former times the hookah was regarded as a treatment for certain illnesses, but in our own time people use it out of fondness, as a fashion. Accordingly the ruling on it is this: if it is intoxicating — as some of its kinds are intoxicating — then its use is impermissible; but if it is not intoxicating, then its use will be disliked. If someone is addicted to it, it is binding upon him to clean his mouth thoroughly before going to the masjid or to a gathering and the like, so that human beings and the angels are not troubled by its stench.

It is stated in the noble hadith: Ḥaḍrat Jābir (may Allah be pleased with him) narrates that the Noble Prophet ﷺ said: whoever eats anything of this foul-smelling plant should not come near our masjid, for the angels are troubled by that which troubles human beings.

عن جابر رضي الله عنه قال: قال رسول الله ﷺ: من أكل من هذه الشجرة المنتنة فلا يقربن مسجد نا، فإن الملائكة تتأذي مما يتأذى منه الإنس. (رواه مسلم: ۱/۲۰۹).

Fatāwā Shāmī states:

قال الإمام العيني رحمه الله في شرحه على صحيح البخاري، قلت: علة النهي أذى الملائكة وأذي المسلمين، ولا يختص بمسجده عليه الصلاة والسلام، بل الكل سواء لرواية مساجدنا بالجمع، خلافاً لمن شذ، ويلحق بما نص عليه في الحديث كل ماله رائحة كريهة مأكولاً أو غيره

(فتاوى الشامي: ۱/٦٦۱، أحكام المساجد، سعيد).

Nevertheless, to make a habit of smoking the hookah is severely disliked, because the following harms lie in it:

(1) It is injurious to the lungs and to the other organs of the body.

(2) A dullness comes over a person's senses.

(3) A stench is produced in the mouth.

(4) There is a resemblance to the people of the Fire in it, since smoke issues from the mouth and the nose.

(5) Those addicted to the hookah do not regard it as an evil, and thereby the aversion to evil is erased from the heart.

(6) Those addicted to the hookah become habituated to evil and filth, and then no cleanliness is left in their lives, whereas cleanliness is an emblem of Islam.

(7) Generally those who fall into it are negligent and slack in their obligatory duties and so forth; at the very least a shortfall and defect certainly comes into their worldly duties.

(8) It involves the waste of wealth.

(Derived from Imdād al-Fatāwā 4/97–98, and Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah 18/386, 389, 392, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states: the hookah in which impure or intoxicating substances are consumed is unlawful by agreement; there is no disagreement about it at all. (Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah 5/470, Deoband edition).

Further harms of smoking the hookah:

A professor of the University of Virginia states that every danger there is in smoking cigarettes is present in smoking the shīshah as well.

The department of health of Britain states that tobacco smoked through a shīshah is many times more dangerous than a cigarette.

A few further harms of smoking the hookah are set down below:

It contains such poisonous substances that there is a severe danger of cancer of the mouth, cancer of the lung and diseases of the heart.

The gums become weak.

A decline occurs in a man's virility.

Several people use a single pipe, and because of this there is a danger of the spread of diseases such as T.B. and herpes.

The smoke that issues from the shīshah contains many poisonous substances, for example: lesenic, lead, cobalt.

In the case of prolonged use, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease can occur.

On investigation it also came to light that these days such flavours (which are said to contain tobacco) are also made in which there is no tobacco at all. If this is correct, and the ingredients used are lawful, and there is no harm in them either, then the use of such a flavour will be contrary to what is preferable.

The upshot is that the use of a flavour that contains tobacco and is harmful is disliked to the degree of prohibition; but as for a flavour in which there is no tobacco at all but rather other permissible and lawful substances, its use will be contrary to what is preferable.
