### TITLE
Giving a live animal in charity during the days of sacrifice

### QUESTION
If someone gives a live animal away in charity during the days of sacrifice, does the obligation of the sacrifice fall away from him or not?

### ANSWER
For the obligation of the sacrifice to fall away during the days of sacrifice, the slaughtering of the animal is necessary. The sacrifice will not be discharged by giving the animal away alive in charity, because the sacrifice is an act of worship in its own right, while charity and almsgiving are a separate act that brings reward. It is obvious that performing one act of worship does not discharge another independent act of worship. Therefore the sacrifice will not be discharged by giving charity and alms, just as neither the Hajj nor the prayer is discharged by paying charity or zakat. (فتاوی الشامی ٦/ ۳۱۳، ط: سعید) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
