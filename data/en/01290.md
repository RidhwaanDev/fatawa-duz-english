### TITLE
Two brothers marrying a mother and her daughter

### QUESTION
Zayd and ʿAmr are full brothers, and both want to marry into the same household: Zayd wishes to marry the mother and ʿAmr the daughter. Is it permissible to marry in this way or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, marrying in this way is sound and valid. It is lawful, coming under the noble verse

﴿وأحل لكم ما وراء ذلكم﴾

and no ground of prohibition exists here.

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states: If the daughter married Zayd's brother and the daughter's mother married Zayd, both marriages are valid. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 11/281, Mubawwab wa Murattab).

See further: Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband: 7/176, Mudallal wa Mukammal; Imdād al-Aḥkām: 2/251.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
