### TITLE

Holding a second Friday congregation in a mosque because of a valid excuse

### QUESTION

At a place in Russia, a great many people missed the Friday prayer because of heavy rain, since there was no arrangement of a courtyard or the like outside the mosque. People normally used to pray in the lanes and on the roads, but because of the heavy rain they had to stand there and could not pray. What should they do? Should they pray Zuhr, or may they hold a second Friday prayer in the mosque? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER

In the Ḥanafī school the jurists have written that in ordinary circumstances a second congregation in a mosque is disliked, but it is allowed when there is a valid excuse. So, in the case in question, if the Friday prayer has already been performed in the other mosques of that town as well, it is correct, on account of the excuse, to hold the Friday prayer again in that same mosque. It is not correct for those resident in a city to pray Zuhr instead.

Consider what is recorded in Fatāwā Shāmī:

قوله ولا على من حال بينه وبينها مطر وطين، أشار بالحيلولة إلى أن المراد المطر الكثير كما قيده به في صلاة الجمعة وكذا الطين، وفي الحلية وعن أبي يوسف رحمه الله سألت أبا حنيفة رحمه الله عن الجماعة في طين وردغة فقال: لا أحب تركها، وقال محمد في الموطأ: الحديث رخصة يعني قوله: إذ ابتلت النعال فالصلاة في الرحال والنعال هنا الأراضي الصلاب، وفي شرح الزاهدي عن شرح التمرتاشي واختلف في كون الأمطار والثلوج والأرحال والبرد الشديد عذراً وعن أبي حنيفة رحمه الله إن اشتد التأذي يعذر، قال الحسن: أفادت هذه الرواية أن الجمعة والجماعة في ذلك سواء ليس على ما ظنه البعض أن ذلك عذر في الجماعة لأنها سنة لا في الجمعة لأنها من آكد الفرائض. (فتاوى الشامي: ۱/۵۵۵، سعيد).

Khayr al-Fatāwā records:

Question: Fifteen or twenty men arrived on a Friday at a time when the Friday prayer had already been performed. Should these people pray the Friday prayer with a sermon in congregation in that mosque, or should they perform Zuhr in congregation?

Answer: Since holding more than one Friday prayer is permissible according to the correct view, and since a person upon whom the Friday prayer is obligatory may not pray Zuhr on a Friday, these people should perform the Friday prayer, that is, the congregation together with the sermon. There is no harm if this takes place in that same mosque, though it is preferable that it be in another mosque. Fatāwā ʿAbd al-Ḥayy, p. 310. (Khayr al-Fatāwā: 3/95, published by Imdādiyyah, Multan.)

Kitāb al-Fatāwā records:

A great many people come to our mosque for the Friday prayer. When the mosque is full, some people — usually more than seventy — perform the Friday prayer on the roof of the mosque, under the open sky. Last Friday it kept raining. In such a case, should the people upstairs perform the prayer while getting soaked in the rain, or should they form a second congregation? Please explain clearly how the Friday prayer is to be performed in such a situation.

Answer: In the situation you have described, the first thing to try is that, instead of the roof of the mosque, the second Friday congregation be held in another mosque if there is one, or in some other covered place besides the mosque. But if this is difficult, then there is room, on account of the excuse, to hold a second congregation, because in particular circumstances the jurists have permitted repeating the congregation. (Kitāb al-Fatāwā: 3/81, published by Zamzam.)

Kitāb al-Masāʾil records:

If the worshippers are many and no other place is available for the congregation, then because of the severity of the rain there is room to repeat the congregation in one and the same mosque. فقط واللہ تعالی اعلم۔ (Kitāb al-Masāʾil: 1/422, published by Nuʿaymiyyah, Deoband.)
