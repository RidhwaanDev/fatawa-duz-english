### TITLE
Giving a contraceptive injection to a non-Muslim woman

### QUESTION
Can a Muslim doctor give a contraceptive injection to a non-Muslim woman or not?

### ANSWER
There is no harm in giving a contraceptive injection to a non-Muslim woman.

Note what Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

Question: Zayd is a physician. Non-Muslims come to Zayd asking for medicines to prevent conception. May Zayd give them such a medicine or not?

الجواب حامداً و مصلیاً: It is sound,

وهو ظاهر لا يخفي.
