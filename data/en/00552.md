### TITLE
A latecomer who says the opening takbīr as the imam gives the salām

### QUESTION
A man joined the prayer just as the imam was giving the salām, or a little before that but the imam gave the salām before he had sat down for the final sitting. Was his following of the imam valid or not? And if it was not valid, should he break his intention or carry on with the prayer he has already begun?

### ANSWER
If the latecomer said the opening takbīr (takbīr taḥrīmah) before the imam's salām, his following of the imam is valid. But if the imam had already given the salām to one side and only then did the man say the opening takbīr, his following is not valid; he should now say a fresh opening takbīr and pray on his own. Fatāwā Shāmī states:

(قوله وتنقضي قدوة بالأول) أي بالسلام الأول، قال في التجنيس: الإمام إذا فرغ من صلاته فلما قال: السلام جاء رجل واقتدي به قبل أن يقول عليكم لا يصير داخلاً في صلاته. (شامي: ۱/٤٦۸ واجبات الصلاة، سعيد. وكذا في حاشية الطحطاوي على مراقي الفلاح: ۲٥۱، فصل في بيان واجبات الصلاة، قديمي)

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:

وإذا فسد الاقتداء بأي وجه كان لا يمنع شروعه في صلاة نفسه لأنه قصد المشاركة وهي غير صلاة الانفراد على الصحيح، محيط، وادعي في البحر أنه المذهب. (الدر المختار: ۱/ ٥۸۲، باب الإمامة، سعيد)

Badāʾiʿ al-Ṣanāʾiʿ states:

الاقتداء عبارة عن المتابعة والشركة فيقتضي المساواة. (بدائع الصنائع: ۱/۱۲۸، سعيد)

Kifāyat al-Muftī states:

When a latecomer says the opening takbīr before the imam's salām, intending to join the imam's prayer, he has entered the imam's prayer. For the following to be valid it is enough that he say the opening takbīr with the intention of following the imam; saying the takbīr with that intention is by itself what makes the following valid. So if the imam gives the salām before the follower has sat down, the follower should complete the prayer with that same takbīr in the way a latecomer does. (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 3/438, Kitāb al-Ṣalāh, Dār al-Ishāʿat)

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

Following the imam after he has said the word al-salām: such following is not valid. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 6/547, Bāb al-Masbūq wa al-Lāḥiq, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah)

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

If a man has said the opening takbīr before the imam gives the salām, he counts as having joined the congregation, and there is no need for him to repeat the opening takbīr.

But if the imam has said the word al-salām and has not yet said ʿalaykum, and someone follows him at that point, this following does not count; he should say the opening takbīr again and begin his prayer afresh. This is on the authority of Shāmī. (Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 1/205; for further explanation: 5/135)

See also: Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 3/270, Bāb al-Imāmah wa al-Jamāʿah; Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Deoband: 3/69, Bāb al-Jamāʿah, Mudallal wa Mukammal, Dār al-Ishāʿat; and Imdād al-Aḥkām: 1/549, Faṣl fī al-Masbūq wa al-Lāḥiq, Maktabah Dār al-ʿUlūm Karachi.
