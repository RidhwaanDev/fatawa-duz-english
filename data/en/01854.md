### TITLE
Spending money given to a madrasah for one purpose on another purpose

### QUESTION
If money comes to a madrasah under one heading, may it be spent under another heading or not? For instance, if some money has come under the heading of books, may it be spent on building work, or the other way round — given that both headings are voluntary charity?

### ANSWER
In the case in question it is not correct or permissible to spend money given to a madrasah under one heading on another heading, because the terms on which the principal has appointed his agent must be kept in view and acted upon. So when a person has given money for books, it is not correct to spend it under another heading.

Consider the following passage from Fatāwā Shāmī:

الوكيل إنما يستفيد التصرف من الموكل وقد أمره بالدفع إلى فلان فلا يملك الدفع إلى غيره، كما لو أوصي لزيد بكذا ليس للوصي الدفع إلى غيره فتأمل. (فتاوى الشامي: ۲/۲٦٦، سعيد).

When someone gives a sum of money to a madrasah under a particular heading, then, since the principal of the madrasah is the donor's agent, and the agent must spend it under the heading the donor has specified, it follows that when the donor has given money under the heading of books, spending it on building work is a breach of trust and a breach of promise.

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

The money must be spent on the very purpose for which it was given. If it is spent elsewhere, liability to make it good arises, because the trustee is a trustee and an agent and has no right to spend it contrary to the donor's express instruction. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۵/٤۷٤، فاروقیہ ۔ امداد الفتاوی: ۳/۳۱۵، ۳۱٦) ۔

It is also written there that if the donors agree to it and have no objection, then acting in this way is permissible in the sharia. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۵/٤۷٤، فاروقیہ) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
