### TITLE
Cutting the hair of young girls

### QUESTION
Is it permissible to have young girls' hair cut, or not? And if it is permissible, up to what age may it be cut?

### ANSWER
Muftī Walī Ḥasan (may Allah have mercy on him) used to say that the aim in cutting young girls' hair is to make the hair grow; if it is cut with that aim, it is permissible and correct. If, however, it is cut in imitation of other nations, it is impermissible. Mawlānā Ẓafar Aḥmad ʿUthmānī (may Allah have mercy on him) permitted girls' hair to be cut up to the age of nine.

Nowadays, though, people generally have their daughters' hair cut in imitation of Western culture, and this is impermissible; they ought to give it up. The mark of it is that the hair is not left even all round but is left short in some places and long in others, and in the light of the hadith this is strictly disliked.

The hadith contains a prohibition against shaving off part of the hair of the head when it is done as a fashion. See:

روي البخاري بسنده عن ابن عمر رضي الله عنه أن رسول الله ﷺ نهى عن القزع

(رقم: ۵۹۲۱، باب القزع) ۔

Fatāwā Bayyināt states:

It is not permissible to have the hair cut of girls who have reached puberty or are close to it. As for girls who are still small and are not near puberty, it is permissible to have their hair cut for the sake of their appearance or for some other lawful purpose; even so, one should avoid deliberate imitation of disbelievers and open sinners. (فتاوی بینات: ٤/٤۰٦)۔

Imdād al-Aḥkām states:

قال الطحطاوي في حاشيته على مراقي الفلاح قال في السراج: الصغير جداً لا تكون له عورة ولا بأس بالنظر إليها ومسها، (ص ۱۳۹). وفي الدر: لا عورة للصغير جداً ثم مادام لم يشته فقبل ودبر ثم تغلظ إلى عشر سنين ثم كبالغ. قال الشامي رحمه الله: قوله الصغير جداً قال: وفسره شيخنا بابن أربع فما دونها ولم أدر لمن عزاه، وحد الاشتهاء يعتبر بحال كل صبي وصبية فإذا بلغ حد الشهوة وقدره بعضهم بسبع وبعضهم بتسع وسيأتي في باب الإمامة تصحيح عدم اعتباره بالسن بل المعتبر أن تصلح للجماع بأن تكون عبلة ضخيمة فله حكم البالغين فيجب على الولي أن يأمره بستر العورة هذا ما علمته من كلام الشامي. (۱/٤۲۳).

These particulars show that, where covering the nakedness is concerned, a girl who has not yet reached puberty is counted as though she were mature at nine or ten years of age — and, if she is well grown and desire has begun to be felt towards her, even before that. Since it is not permissible for a mature woman to shave her head without a strong excuse, the same ruling will apply to a girl who has reached puberty. The proper course is that once a girl turns nine her head should not be shaved, even if she is not yet an object of desire, for that is the settled term of her puberty, الخ۔ (امداد الاحکام: ٤/۳٤۱، لڑکی کا سر منڈانا کس عمر تک جائز ہے)۔

Majmūʿah Fatāwā barā-e Khawātīn states:

Cutting the hair of small girls as a fashion is likewise forbidden. (Muftī ʿAbd al-Raḥīm Lājpūrī). (مجموعہ فتاوی: ۲/۵۳۱، مرتب مولانا مفتی ثناء اللہ محمود صاحب، ط: دار الاشاعت)۔
