### TITLE
Making a habit of praying Fajr at home

### QUESTION
Am I sinful for not praying the Fajr prayer in the mosque? I start my work at ten and work until eight. I become very tired and do not go to the mosque for the Fajr prayer, because I am very lazy and do not want to cut down on my sleep, so that my work should not suffer. My question is: am I sinful? Will I still get the same reward as praying in the mosque? Explain and be rewarded.

### ANSWER
To leave the congregation in the mosque without a valid excuse recognised by the Sharīʿah is to deprive oneself of an immense reward. Laziness and tiredness are no excuse for abandoning the congregation in the mosque. So, after repenting and seeking forgiveness, make a point from now on of performing the Fajr prayer in congregation in the mosque every day. Consider the merit of prayer in congregation:

عن عبد الله بن عمر رضي الله عنه أن رسول الله ﷺ قال: صلاة الجماعة تفضل صلاة الفذ بسبع وعشرين درجة. (صحيح البخاري: ۱/۱۳۱، ٦٤۵).

Prayer in congregation is twenty-seven degrees better than praying alone.

Other merits of praying in congregation in the mosque are mentioned in the hadiths as well. For example: seven people will be under the shade of the Throne, among them those whose hearts are attached to the mosque, so that when they leave it they are already thinking of returning. The incident of those two Companions is well known: after praying in the mosque, in the intense darkness light shone out from their staffs. Whoever goes to the mosque morning and evening, Allah will prepare hospitality for him in Paradise. When a person sets out for the mosque with the sole intention of prayer, his rank is raised and his sins are wiped away; and when he has finished praying, the angels pray for mercy for him. All these merits are mentioned in the books of hadith. See further: (فتاوی دار العلوم زکریا، جلد دوم، ص ۲۹۷، ط: بمبئی) ۔

Consider the warning against deliberately abandoning congregational prayer without an excuse:

عن أبي هريرة رضي الله تعالى عنه أن رسول الله ﷺ قال: والذي نفسي بيده لقد هممت أن آمر بحطب فيحطب ثم آمر بالصلاة فيؤذن لها ثم آمر رجلاً فيؤم الناس ثم أخالف إلى رجال فأحرق عليهم بيوتهم والذي نفسي بيده لو يعلم أحدهم أنه يجد عرقاً سميناً أو مرماتين حسنتين لشهد العشاء. (صحيح البخاري: ۹/۸۲/۷۲۲٤).

In this hadith the Noble Prophet ﷺ expressed the intention of burning down the houses of those who pray at home without an excuse. This is a most alarming warning.

Al-Durr al-Mukhtār states:
و الجماعة سنة مؤكدة للرجال، قال الزاهدي: أرادوا بالتأكيد الوجوب وقيل: واجبة وعليه العامة، فتسن أو تجب، ثمرته تظهر في الإثم بتركها مرة، على الرجال العقلاء البالغين الأحرار القادرين على الصلاة بالجماعة من غير حرج. (الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ۱/۵۵۲، سعيد).
وفي البحر: وصرح في المحيط: بأنه لا يرخص لا حد في تركها بغير عذر حتى لو تركها أهل مصر يؤمرون بها فإن ائتمروا وإلا يحل مقاتلتهم. (البحر الرائق: ۱/۳٦۵، دار المعرفة، وكذا في الفتاوى الهندية: ۱/۸۲).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah records:
To leave the prayer in the mosque without a valid excuse recognised by the Sharīʿah and to pray at home instead is a very great loss and an abandonment of one of the great emblems of Islam. There is a severe warning against it in the noble hadith. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ٦/٤۰۸، جامعہ فاروقیہ) ۔

Imdād al-Aḥkām states: Praying in congregation is an emphasised sunnah, close to necessary; so to leave it without an excuse is a sin, and to make a habit of leaving the congregation is open disobedience — such a person is a transgressor in the eyes of the Sharīʿah. (امداد الاحکام: ۱/۵۲٤) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
