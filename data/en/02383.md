### TITLE
The ruling on eating crayfish

### QUESTION
What is the ruling regarding cray fish (Cray fish)? Is it permissible to eat it or not?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, since the cray fish (Cray fish) is not a fish, eating it is not permissible or correct according to the Ḥanafī school. Consider the following explanation about this creature:

In Arabic this creature is called jarād al-baḥr and jarād al-nahr. (Al-Mawrid: 288).

Consider the following summary of the passage recorded in the World Book Encyclopaedia:

It is like the lobster (lobster), and is also called spiny lobster. In Urdu it is called daryāʾī ṭiḍḍī, the river locust. It is a shelled creature that lives in fresh water. It is found in every continent except Africa and Antarctica. The front part of its body is rigid and motionless, while the rear part moves. It has six legs: with the two front legs it does the work of grasping, and the remaining four legs it uses for walking. At the beginning of the night, when this creature comes out of its hole onto the shore, it is very brisk. This creature is in the habit of boring holes, and because of this the flood-retaining embankment of a river dyke is sometimes destroyed. In Europe it is counted among the fine foods. (Summarised from the World Book Encyclopaedia: 4/901).

In the Ḥanafī school, of all water creatures only fish is lawful. Commenting on the verse of the Noble Qur'an:

﴿وأحل لك صيد البحر وطعامه﴾

Imām Abū Bakr Jaṣṣāṣ Rāzī (may Allah have mercy on him) says:

أنه أراد السمك خاصة دون ما سو اهـ. (أحكام القرآن: ٤/۱٤۷، إحياء التراث).

Tuḥfat al-Mulūk records:

ولا يحل من حيوان الماء، إلا أنواع السمك كلها. (تحفة الملوك، ص ۲۱٤، فصل ما يحرم أكله من الحيوانات، ط: دار البشائر الإسلامية).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
