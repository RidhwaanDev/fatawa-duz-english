### TITLE
The ruling on charging an entrance fee for a game farm

### QUESTION
Hunters go to a hunting farm which contains a place for hunting as well as other scenery. The owner of the farm charges an entrance fee for it. Is it permissible to take this fee or not?

### ANSWER
People derive enjoyment in the farm both from the hunting and from the other scenery, and this is also the prevailing custom. Therefore, in the case asked about, it is permitted to take an entrance fee for two reasons.

The first reason: if the period and the rent are known, we shall include it under hire.

Consider the following. Sharḥ al-Majallah states:

يشترط في الإجارة أن تكون المنفعة معلومة بوجه يكون مانعاً للمنازعة. (شرح المجلة: ۲/۵۳۲، للشيخ محمد خالد الأتاسي).

خلاصة الفتاوى
states:

عقد الإجارة لا يجوز إلا أن يبين البدل من الجانبين جميعاً أما بيان المنفعة فبإحدى معان ثلاثة بيان الوقت وهو الأجل وبيان العمل وبيان المكان. (خلاصة الفتاوى: ۳/۱۰۳).

The second reason for permissibility: if it is not accepted as a hire contract, then taking the fee will be permissible by way of a conditional voluntary contribution.

Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Ẓafar Aḥmad ʿUthmānī (raḥimahullāh) has reckoned an entrance fee as a conditional voluntary contribution and as a donation. Consider the following.

When a question was asked concerning the admission fee at a madrasah, he replied: It is permissible, because this is not a wage but a donation, and a condition is permissible in a donation, since no compulsion arises thereby: whoever does not accept the condition has the option of not enrolling.

ودليله أنه ﷺ قال لمن أضافه وعائشة رضي الله تعالى عنها قال: لا قال: فلا إذن، حتى قال في الثالثة: وعائشة، قال: فنعم.

(Imdād al-Aḥkām: 3/606.)

For further details see: Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā, volume five, Bāb al-Ijārah and Bāb al-Hibah, "conditional voluntary contribution".

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
