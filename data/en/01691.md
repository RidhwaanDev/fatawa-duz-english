### TITLE
Leaving one's wife to spend a year in the work of tablīgh

### QUESTION
It is common for people to spend a year in the work of tablīgh after completing their studies, whether they are married or unmarried. If a man is married, is it permissible for him to leave his wife and children and spend a year in this way or not? And does it make any difference whether the wife agrees or not?

### ANSWER
In the case in question, going out for four months or for a year depends upon the agreement of both husband and wife. In most cases the matter is settled between the two families before the marriage — that the young man will go out for a year immediately after the marriage — and the marriage is contracted on that basis, the wife moving into his home a year later.

If she has already moved in, however, and the two have begun living together, then the wife's consent is necessary for him to go out for the year. So if the wife is willing and there is no fear of any temptation or trouble, there is no harm in his spending the year; indeed it is of the greatest benefit.

ʿAllāmah al-Ḥaṣkafī (may Allah have mercy on him) said that intimacy is necessary from time to time as a matter of conscience, and that a man should not let the period of īlāʾ — that is, four months — pass without intimacy, unless it is with his wife's consent, in which case there is no harm.

يجب ديانة أحياناً ولا يبلغ مدة الإيلاء إلا برضاها. (الدر المختار: ۳/۲۰۳، باب القسم).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

If the woman is able to bear it and the husband goes home once during the year with her permission, then, God willing, he will not be sinful; otherwise he will be guilty of failing to fulfil her right. He should not remain away for more than four months. کذا في رد المحتار. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۸/۵۹۲، مبوب و مرتب) ۔

In another place it is written:

If the woman is young, she should not be left for more than four months, since there is a fear of temptation. If she can be patient and there is no fear of temptation, then there is no objection even to a year. (فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۸/۵۹۲، مبوب و مرتب) ۔

It is also written:

The wife has rights beyond food and maintenance — what arrangement has been made for those? If she is young and cannot master her feelings, her right will be lost. If she does have control of herself and has gladly given permission for so long a period, and there is no danger of any sin, then it is allowed; otherwise he should come to her once in every four months.

و یجب دیانۃ أحیاناً، و لا یبلغ مدۃ الإیلاء إلا برضاہا ۔

(فتاوی محمودیہ: ۱۸/۵۹٤، مبوب و مرتب) ۔

Besides this, we may cite a parallel from the statements of the jurists: they allow the wife of an impotent man a respite of one year, and the wife of a missing man four years, even though over such long periods there could likewise be a fear of temptation.

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
