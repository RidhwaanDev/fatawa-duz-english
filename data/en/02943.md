### TITLE
A litany for bodily strength and vigour

### QUESTION
Can you tell me some litany or supplication by which I may become physically strong and robust?

### ANSWER
For bodily strength and vigour, reciting the following litany is beneficial.

٭ Recite
يا الله. يا سلام. يا قوي.
(313 times). (انوار شمسیہ، ص ۸۸)۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
