### TITLE

Drinking water during or after a meal

### QUESTION

Is it established from the Messenger of Allah ﷺ that he drank water during or after a meal, or not? Doctors say that water should be drunk half an hour after eating. If a person acts upon this, will he be said to be acting contrary to the sunnah or not?

### ANSWER

What the doctors say — that water should be drunk half an hour after eating — is not contrary to the sunnah, because the Messenger of Allah ﷺ did not drink water immediately after eating. Indeed, the writers on his life have counted drinking water immediately after eating among the things that are disliked from a medical point of view. Yes, drinking a little water is not contrary to this.

Consider what ʿAllāmah Ibn al-Qayyim says in Zād al-Maʿād:

ولم يكن من هديه أن يشرب على طعامه فيفسده، ولا سيما إن كان الماء حاراً أو بارداً، فإنه رديء جداً

ويكره شرب الماء عقيب الرياضة، والتعب، وعقيب الجماع، وعقيب الطعام وقبله، وعقيب الفاكهة، وإن كان الشرب عقيب بعضها أسهل من بعض

فهذا كله مناف لحفظ الصحة، ولا اعتبار بالعوائد، فإنها طبائع ثوان

وأما هديه في الشراب، فمن أكمل هدي يحفظ به الصحة. (زاد المعاد: ٤/۲۲٤، ط: مؤسسة الرسالة).

Shamāʾil Kubrā states:

The Prophet ﷺ did not drink water (immediately) after eating. (Madārij, p. 17.)

Drinking water immediately after eating is harmful to the stomach and to digestion, so water should be drunk after a little while. (Shamāʾil Kubrā: 1/128, published by Zamzam Publishers.)

Uswah-i Rasūl-i Akram states:

Drinking water after eating is not the sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ, especially if the water is hot or very cold, because both of these are extremely harmful. He did not consider it good to drink water after exercise when one is tired, after eating food or fruit, or after intercourse or a bath. (Uswah-i Rasūl-i Akram, p. 133, published by Dār al-Ishāʿat.)
