### TITLE
The ruling on wearing jewellery bearing the sign of the cross

### QUESTION
Is it permissible for a Muslim woman to wear a piece of jewellery or a necklace that carries a cross, which is the particular religious symbol of the Christians? It is worn only for adornment. بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
The Christians believe that Ḥaḍrat ʿĪsā (peace and blessings be upon our Prophet and upon him) was wrongfully crucified and killed, and for this reason they made the cross the emblem of their false religion. Allah has declared this belief false in the Noble Qur'an. Therefore, if a Muslim adopts the sign of the cross out of respect for the Christian religion, this is disbelief; and if he simply uses it as an ornament, even then it is not permissible or correct. See what Muṣannaf Ibn Abī Shaybah states:

عن عائشة رضي الله عنه قالت: إنا لانلبس الثياب التي فيها الصليب. وعن أبي جحاف قال: سألت أبا جعفر عن تابوت لي فيه تماثيل فقال: حدثني من رأي عمر رضي الله عنه يحرق ثوباً فيه صليب، ينزع الصليب منه. وعن ابن عون، عن محمد؛ أن النبي ﷺ رأي على بعض أزواجه ستراً فيه صليب، فأمر به فقضب. (مصنف ابن أبي شيبة: ۸/۱۹٦، كتاب اللباس).

وقال ابن ملك: الصليب في اصطلاح النصارى خشبة مثلثة يدعون أن عيسى عليه الصلاة والسلام صلب على خشبة مثلثة على تلك الصورة وقد يكون فيه صورة المسيح. (عون المعبود: ۱۱/۳۰٦، ط: بيروت).

وفي رد المحتار: أقول: والظاهر أنه يلحق به الصليب وإن لم يكن تمثال ذي روح لان فيه تشبها بالنصارى ويكره التشبه بهم في المذموم وإن لم يقصده كما مر. (فتاوى الشامي: ۱/٦٤۸، سعيد).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

At one time the necktie was an emblem of the Christians, and at that time the ruling on it was severe as well. Now non-Christians also use it widely, so the ruling on it has been eased: it will not be called polytheism or forbidden, though it is still not free of dislikedness — in some places the dislikedness will be strong and in others slight. Where its use has become general, its prohibition will not be pressed. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 19/289, arranged and classified edition).

Some scholars, however, have declared it impermissible on the ground that it is a religious emblem of the Christians.

See what is recorded in the notes and marginalia of Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah:

Although the use of the necktie has become common among Muslims too, it is still a part of Western dress. Even if it is not counted as Western dress, it is in any case the dress of the sinful and the immoral, so it will be declared forbidden on account of resembling the sinful. Secondly, people of piety do not care for this dress either, because it is contrary to the dress of the scholars and the righteous. Thirdly, apart from this there is a further defect in the necktie: by it the Christians point to their belief in the "cross of ʿĪsā (peace be upon him)" — that is, that Ḥaḍrat ʿĪsā (peace be upon him) was crucified — which is against the explicit text of the Qur'an. So, along with resembling the disbelievers, wearing it is also impermissible because it is a religious memorial and a religious emblem of the Christians. (Some scholars, however, take issue with its being a sign of the cross and a religious emblem.)

وعن ابن عمر رضي الله تعالى عنهما قال: قال رسول الله ﷺ: "من تشبه بقوم" أي من شبه نفسه بالكفار مثلاً في اللباس وغيره أو بالفساق أو الفجار أو بأهل التصوف والصلحاء الأبرار، "فهو منهم" في الإثم والخير، قال الطيبي رحمه الله: هذا عام في الخَلق والخُلق والشعار ولما كان الشعار أظهر في التشبه

(مرقاة المفاتيح، كتاب اللباس، ۷/۱۵۵، رشيدية). (Drawn from the marginalia of Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 19/289, arranged and classified edition).
