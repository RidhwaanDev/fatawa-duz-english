### TITLE
A doctor taking his fee from a medical-aid company

### QUESTION
Is medical aid permissible or not? And if it is not permissible, what is the ruling on doctors taking their fees and the like from the medical-aid company?

### ANSWER
In the case asked about, the form of medical aid in which one receives treatment from the medical-aid company in exchange for one's money, and money is not paid over directly, is permissible and valid. The doctors who treat the patient may also take their fees from the medical-aid company; there is no harm in it, since they are taking the wage for their own work. Yes, if most of the sources of the medical-aid company's income are unlawful, it is necessary to keep away from it.

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states:

The fee that a doctor takes for an examination is permissible, and he may spend it on anything. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 16/391, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah).
