### TITLE
The ruling on wiping over socks that have been made waterproof with a spray

### QUESTION
I know the ruling that there are three conditions for wiping over leather socks: 1. that water does not seep through them; 2. that they stay in place of themselves without being fastened; 3. that it is possible to walk continuously in them. I also know that it is permissible to wipe over Seal Skinz. But what about ordinary cotton socks, through which water does seep? If such socks are made waterproof by means of a spray, will it be permissible to wipe over them or not? This spray keeps water from penetrating for up to 24 hours. بینوا توجروا

### ANSWER
It is clear from the statements of the jurists that, for wiping to be valid, the socks themselves must be made in such a way that the conditions mentioned are found in them; the conditions must not be met by means of something external. Ordinary socks may therefore not be wiped over, and a spray is an external thing, so it is of no account. Consider what al-Durr al-Mukhtār records:

شرط مسحه ثلاثة أمور الأول كونه ساتر محل فرض الغسل القدم مع الكعب أو يكون نقصانه أقل من الخرق المانع

والثاني كونه مشغولاً بالرجل ليمنع سراية الحدث

والثالث كونه مما يمكن متابعة المشي المعتاد فيه فرسخاً فأكثر

(الدر المختار: ۱/۲٦۱، ۲٦٤، سعيد).

Imdād al-Fattāḥ states:

والشرط الخامس: استمساكهما على الرجلين من غير شد لثخانته

لأن الرقيق لا يجوز المسح عليه اتفاقاً، لعدم صلاحيته لقطع المسافة. والشرط السادس: منعهما وصول الماء إلى الجسد فلا يشفان الماء لثخانتهما وصلابتهما

وإنما يجوز المسح على الموقين عندنا إذا كانا صالحين للمسح باستقلالهما كالثخين. (إمداد الفتاح، ص ۱۳۵، ط: بيروت).

Tadhkirat al-Rashīd states:

A sock made of cloth thick enough that it does not absorb water and does not slip down while walking is acceptable, and wiping over it is permissible. (Tadhkirat al-Rashīd, p. 136, Idārah-e-Islāmiyyāt, Karachi)

Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah states:

If the socks are not of leather but of cotton or wool, then the jurists have laid down these conditions for the permissibility of wiping over them: first, that they be so thick, heavy and firm that it is possible to walk three miles wearing them alone; secondly, that they stay up on the calf without being tied (because the material is thick); thirdly, that water neither seeps through them nor soaks through them to reach the foot. If cotton or woollen socks are of this kind, it is permissible to wipe over them, because such socks then come under the ruling of leather socks. (Fatāwā Raḥīmiyyah: 2/341)

See further: (Niẓām al-Fatāwā: 1/42; Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 5/196, Jāmiʿah Fārūqiyyah; Fatāwā Farīdiyyah: 2/104; and Fatāwā Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā: 1/721, Zamzam).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
