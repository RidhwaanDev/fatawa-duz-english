### TITLE
The ruling on using ﴿هَٰؤُلَاءِ بَنَاتِي هُنَّ أَطْهَرُ لَكُمْ﴾ as an argument for an evil act

### QUESTION
Nowadays some misguided people say: (1) In the time of Lūṭ (peace be upon him) sodomy was unlawful because they used to do it by force; so if there is mutual consent there is room for it — we seek refuge in Allah. (2) Concerning هؤلاء بناتي هن أطهر لكم they say that marrying the girls is a matter of greater purity as compared with sodomy, and that there is room for sodomy too — we seek refuge in Allah. What is the answer to this satanic question?

### ANSWER
Outwardly the people of Lūṭ used to commit sodomy by consent, because in the Noble Quran the words of "coming to" are used about these people, and in the Quran and the hadiths the word "coming to" is used for a consensual act. Thus concerning the people of Lūṭ this verse is mentioned in the Noble Quran: وتأتون في ناديكم المنكر — "and you commit evil in your gatherings." Here it is quite plain that those people used to commit these evils by consent. "Coming to" means doing something with consent; doing it by force is called compulsion or coercion, and in this evil act the word of coercion is nowhere mentioned regarding the people of Lūṭ. And even supposing that in that age they used to do it by force, that does not mean that it is then permissible if there is consent — just as, if in some country the government has made it binding and necessary to pay interest or to take out insurance, that does not mean

And the meaning of هؤلاء بناتي is "the daughters of my people," that is, marry the daughters of the people; and هن أطهر لكم has two meanings: 1. Either the comparative noun is in the sense of the verb, as in أصحاب الجنة خير مقاماً وأحسن مقيلاً — the place of the people of Paradise is good, and against it Hell is evil; or as in الله أعلى وأجل — Allah Most High is most exalted and most majestic. This is the meaning intended here; it does not mean that Allah Most High is more majestic and greater and that the idols are great too.

2. Or أطهر means "cleaner," that is, the place of intercourse in women is very clean, whereas the rear passage of men is a place of filth and impurity and excrement. So why do you regard a place of filth as a place of pleasure?
