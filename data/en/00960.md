### TITLE
Zakat on key money paid to a landlord

### QUESTION
A tenant pays the landlord a lump sum in advance. Once a year has passed over that money, who is liable for its zakat?

### ANSWER
In the case described, the zakat will be due from the landlord.

Consider what Īḍāḥ al-Nawādir states:

The lump sum that a tenant pays in advance to the owner of a house or shop becomes the owner's property, and its zakat is likewise due from the owner. It is not due from the tenant, because the tenant does not have full ownership of that money.

إذا عجل الأجرة لا يملك الاسترداد. شامي: ٦/۱۰.

(Īḍāḥ al-Nawādir, part two, p. 10).

Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil states:

The advance payment passes into the landlord's ownership, so it is the landlord who must pay zakat on it. Ibn al-Humām (may Allah have mercy on him) writes:

و أما زكاة الأجرة المعجلة عن سنين في الإجارة الطويلة التي يفعلها بعض الناس عقوداً ويشترطون الخيار ثلاثة أيام في رأس كل شهر فتجب علي الآجر لأنه ملكها بالقبض. (فتح القدير: ۲/۱۲۱).

So zakat on rent paid in advance is due from the landlord. (Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil: 1/218).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
