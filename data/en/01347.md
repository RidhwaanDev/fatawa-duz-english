### TITLE
Temporary birth control for a student

### QUESTION
Zayd is a student and is married. He wishes to adopt some form of temporary birth control until he completes his studies. Is this permissible? He says that he considers having children during his studies harmful both for himself and for his education; and, moreover, that at present he has no income apart from what he receives from his father. In these circumstances, is there any room for him to adopt some temporary form of birth control or not?

### ANSWER
It is possible that some means of birth control may come about from Allah's side and Zayd's wish be fulfilled. Even so, if this wish of Zayd's is not fulfilled by Allah Most High, then birth control with the intention of avoiding the question of where the child will get his food from is not correct. Moreover, marrying during one's studies and bringing a wife into the home is not held to be harmful to one's education (even though experience has shown it to be harmful), so the poor child will all the more certainly not be harmful.

The scholars have written on birth control in considerable detail. If more is desired, one should refer to the treatises that have been written on this question, for example: Jadīd Fiqhī Masāʾil, "Family planning and Islam", pp. 92–147; Aḥsan al-Fatāwā: 8/347–353, "Birth control and abortion"; Ḍabṭ-e Wilādat kī ʿAqlī o Sharʿī Ḥaythiyyat, by Muftī Muḥammad Shafīʿ (may Allah have mercy on him); and Baḥth o Naẓar, "Birth control from the Islamic point of view", under the supervision of Ḥaḍrat Mawlānā Qāḍī Mujāhid al-Islām Qāsimī.
