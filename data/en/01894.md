### TITLE
Giving a discount for early payment in a credit sale

### QUESTION
A cloth business is carried on in a certain shop in which the cloth is sold on credit, but the term is not specified. The buyer is given a receipt on which it is written that if you pay within one month you will receive such-and-such a discount, and if you pay within two months you will receive such-and-such a discount. Is this transaction correct in the sharia or not?

### ANSWER
(1) In this transaction it is necessary to write a final date together with the price — that is, that this is the price, and it is to be paid within, say, three months.

Sharḥ al-Majallah states:

يلزم أن تكون المدة معلومة في البيع بالتأجيل والتقسيط، لأن جهالته تفضي إلى النزاع فالبائع يطالب في مدة قريبة، والمشتري يأباها، فيفسد البيع (بحر). (شرح المجلة لمحمد خالد الأتاسي، ۲/۱٦۷).

(2) The discounts allowed for payment in the meantime should be by way of a promise, namely: "We promise, if Allah Most High wills, that on payment within one month we shall give such-and-such a discount, and on payment within two months such-and-such a discount." And since the buyer does not sign the receipt, this is a promise from one side; it is not a settlement.

The definition of a settlement is:

"عقد يرفع النزاع". (الدر المختار: ۸/۲۱٦، سعيد).

Here no contract is to be found, because the buyer neither said anything nor wrote anything; rather it is a promise from one side only.

(3) If it were held to be a settlement, then settling a deferred debt by immediate payment, and reducing the debt, has been declared permissible in Sharḥ al-Kāfī of al-Isbījābī.

وذكر في شرح الكافي للاسبيجابي: جواز هذا الصلح مطلقاً على قياس قول أبي يوسف رحمه الله لأنه إحسان من المديون في القضاء بالتعجيل وإحسان من صاحب الدين في الاقتضاء بحط بعض حقه. (تكملة رد المحتار، لمحمد علاء الدين الشامي، ۸/۲۵۳، فصل في دعوي الدين).

That is, in Sharḥ al-Kāfī this settlement has been declared permissible in accordance with the view of Imām Abū Yūsuf (may Allah have mercy on him), because the debtor does a favour by paying early, and the creditor does a favour in collecting his debt by remitting part of his right.

It is mentioned in Sharḥ ʿUqūd Rasm al-Muftī that in judicial matters the verdict should be given according to the view of Imām Abū Yūsuf (may Allah have mercy on him), since, having held the office of chief justice, he had practical experience of such matters. (شرح عقود رسم المفتی، ص ۲۹).

The merit of showing kindness both when paying a debt and when collecting it has also been mentioned in the noble hadith. See what Mishkāt Sharīf states:

قال: وخياركم من إذا كان عليه الدين أحسن القضاء وإن كان له أجمل في الطلب. (مشكاة المصابيح: ۲/٤۳۷، باب الامر بالمعروف).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
