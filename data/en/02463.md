### TITLE
Sacrificing an animal with an amputated foot

### QUESTION
If an animal's foot has been cut off, is its sacrifice permissible or not?

### ANSWER
The sacrifice of an animal whose foot has been cut off is not permissible (شامی) ۔ And an animal that is so lame that it walks on only three feet and cannot place the fourth on the ground — or can place it but cannot walk with its weight upon it — is not permissible for sacrifice; but if it can walk resting on the fourth foot, it is permissible. (فتاوی الشامی: ٦/۳۲۳، سعید) ۔

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
