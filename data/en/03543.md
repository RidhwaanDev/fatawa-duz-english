### TITLE
A person in iʿtikāf in the two Ḥarams going out for food and other needs

### QUESTION
In the two Ḥarams there is no permission to eat food or to take it in, nor may one take in a large amount of belongings. So is it correct to go to some nearby restaurant in order to eat, or not? بینوا توجروا ۔

### ANSWER
The jurists have given permission to go out for a natural need, and food is included among the natural needs. However, if there is someone to bring the food, he should have it brought and eat it in the courtyard of the mosque; and if there is no one, he should go quickly to a nearby restaurant, eat, and return to the mosque without wasting any time. No defect will enter the sunnah iʿtikāf on account of this. For the rest, it is not correct to go out into the markets merely for diversion. Consider what ʿUmdat al-Qārī states:

وذكر أنه يخرج للأكل والشرب بعد الغروب. (عمدة القاري: باب الحائض ترجل المعتكف).

قال في البحر الرائق: وفي الفتاوى الظهيرية: وقيل: يخرج بعد الغروب للأكل والشرب، وينبغي حمله على ما إذا لم يجد من يأتي له به فحينئذٍ يكون من الحوائج الضرورية كالبول والغائط. (البحر الرائق: ۲/۳۰۳، ط: كوئته).

(وكذا في الدر المختار مع رد المحتار: ۲/٤٤۹، سعيد، باب الاعتكاف، والنهر الفائق؛ ۲/٤۷، دار الكتب العلمية، وحاشية الطحطاوي على مراقي الفلاح، ص، باب الاعتكاف).

Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah states: There is permission to take the pre-dawn meal and to break the fast in the mosque, so he should not go outside for this. If there is no one to bring it, he may go and fetch it from his house. (Fatāwā Maḥmūdiyyah: 15/268, Maktabah Maḥmūdiyyah).

Kifāyat al-Muftī states: If there is no one to bring the food to the mosque, he should go to fetch the food and, having taken it, should return at once. (Kifāyat al-Muftī: 4/246, Dār al-Ishāʿat).

Kitāb al-Masāʾil states: In the two noble Ḥarams there is no permission to bring food inside. Therefore, if those who are granted the blessing of iʿtikāf there in the blessed month of Ramaḍān go to a nearby restaurant after Maghrib and eat, or go out into the outer courtyard and eat, there is no harm in it, because this is included among the natural needs. However, they should not sit on there after eating; rather, having finished, they should come back into the mosque at once. (Kitāb al-Masāʾil: 2/185, Kutub Khānah Nuʿaymiyyah).

واللہ سبحانہ و تعالی اعلم
